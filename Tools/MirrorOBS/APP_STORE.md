**Description:**
Mirror OBS puts your iPhone's whole screen into OBS Studio as a browser
source, live, over Wi-Fi. Demo an app, play a game on stage, or share your
phone in a stream or a church service, without a cable or a capture card.

How it works
• On the OBS computer, open OBS Sharing at 7twenty8studio.com and get a room code.
• Type the room code in Mirror OBS and tap Start Broadcast.
• Add this iPhone's link to OBS as a Browser source. Done.

What you get
• Your whole screen, in any app, at up to 1600 pixels and 30 frames a second.
• Straight from your iPhone to OBS (peer to peer); nothing is recorded or stored.
• Several iPhones in one room, each with its own link, plus a host page and an OBS dock to see them all.
• A source that fills an iPhone-shaped box edge to edge.
• No account, no ads, no tracking.

Pricing
Try everything free for 7 days. The trial never charges you. After that,
unlock Mirror OBS with a single purchase. No subscription.

Mirror OBS works with OBS Studio. It isn't made by, endorsed by or
affiliated with the OBS Project.

**Review notes:**
Mirror OBS sends the iPhone's screen (ReplayKit broadcast extension) to a
web page that OBS Studio shows as a browser source. To try it without OBS:
open https://www.7twenty8studio.com/services/obs-sharing on a computer,
note the room code it shows, type it in the app under Room, tap Start
Broadcast, then Start Broadcast in the system sheet. The iPhone appears on
the web page within a few seconds. The 7-Day Trial is a free
non-consumable, as guideline 3.1.1 describes for a trial before a one-time
unlock; the unlock is a non-consumable.

**In-app purchases:**
