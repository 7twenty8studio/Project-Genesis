### Elpalet (topic 1612)
- name note: The glossary gives Elifelet (Eliphelet), but this topic is the separate spelling Elpalet (RV1909 1 Cr 14:5), and its heading says it is also called ELIFELET.

### En-rogel (topic 1658)
- name note: The glossary’s “Rogel” drops the En- that the RV1909 keeps in the place name (Jos 15:7; 18:16, En-rogel).

### En-tappuah (topic 1661)
- name note: The glossary’s “Tapua” is the town; this topic is the spring En-tapua (RV1909 Jos 17:7), and its heading names Tapua separately.
