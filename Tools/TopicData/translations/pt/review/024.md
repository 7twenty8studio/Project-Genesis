### Zizah (topic 5299)
- name note: This topic is the Gershonite of 1 Chr 23:11, whom Almeida (ACF) calls Zizá; the glossary's Zina is his other spelling in 1 Chr 23:10, which is the separate topic Zina just before (whose heading says "Called ZIZAH"). Keeping Zizá keeps the two topics apart.
