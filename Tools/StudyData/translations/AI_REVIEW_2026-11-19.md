# AI review of the Easton and Nave's translations (19 November 2026)

This was an AI review, made with Claude. No qualified human reviewer has signed off on these translations. A Spanish or Portuguese
reviewer with biblical training should still read them; the open questions below are a good place to start.

## What was read
- Easton's Bible Dictionary: all 2,781 entries (44 batches), Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, each read whole against the English.
- Nave's Topical Bible: all 29,826 items (5,319 topic names and 24,507 headings, 24 batches), both languages, against the English.
- Looked for: meaning, omissions and additions, grammar and agreement, terminology (NOTES.md choices), names, leftover English,
  Spanish in the Portuguese or the reverse. Scripture quoted from the RV1909 / Bíblia Livre was left verbatim.

## What changed
- 360 corrections in the batches (182 Easton, 178 Nave's; 221 Spanish, 139 Portuguese), listed at the end.
- Spanish consistency, outside quotations: Absalón -> Absalom (123, the app's and the RV1909's name), Quedorlaomer -> Quédorlaomer (5).
- Seven fixes inside translated quotations that aren't Scripture (Easton quoting Stalker, Geikie, Porter, Boscawen and others): Rebekah 2401 es,
  Pharaoh 2331 pt, Paul 2479 es (Éfeso), Solomon 2762 pt, Rahab 969 es, Straight 1112 pt, Syria 1122 pt.
- App names that the aligner had wrong, pinned in manual.json (`ids`) and es.json / pt.json, so Explore matches: es Quelub (929, 930),
  Mehir (1982), Gamul (1278), Dara (976), Dibri (1010), Palal (2268), Reaía (2398), Selef (2602), Seber (2576), Secanías (2581),
  Oded (2236, 2237); pt Dara, Dibri, Odede (2236, 2237), Noadias (2209). Nave's topics with those names carry a `name_note`.
- Feminine participles under the fixed phrase "Sent as a judgment" / "Forbidden as food" carry a `phrase_note`.
- All checks pass afterwards: bios.py check and naves.py check (es, pt), test_bios.py. Study.sqlite and Topics.sqlite rebuilt.
