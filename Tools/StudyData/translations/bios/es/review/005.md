# Quotations to review: es batch 005

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Cain (person 533)
- unsure (Gen 4:3): “in process of time” → “andando el tiempo”
- partial (Gen 4:4): “firstlings of his flock and of the fat,” → “de los primogénitos de sus ovejas, y de su grosura.”
- partial (Gen 4:3): “of the fruit of the ground.” → “del fruto de la tierra.”
- wording (Heb 11:4): “more excellent” → “mayor”
- partial (Gen 4:16): “land of Nod” → “tierra de Nod”
- partial (Gen 4:16): “east of Eden,” → “al oriente de Edén,”
