# Quotations to review: es batch 033

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Giloh (place 479)
- wording (2Sam 15:12): “the Gilonite” → “el Gilonita”

### Golgotha (place 487)
- partial (Mark 15:22): “the place of a skull” → “Lugar de la Calavera”
- wording (John 19:41): “garden” → “huerto”

### Gomorrah (place 489)
- partial (2Pet 2:6): “ensample unto those that after should live ungodly” → “ejemplo á los que habían de vivir sin temor y reverencia de Dios”

### Goshen (place 490)
- partial (Gen 45:10): “the land of Goshen” → “la tierra de Gosén”
- wording (Gen 45:10): “Goshen” → “Gosén”
- unsure (Gen 47:11): “the land of Rameses” → “la tierra de Rameses”
- partial (Gen 47:6): “the best of the land” → “lo mejor de la tierra”

### Gozan (place 492)

### Hachilah (place 500)

### Hamath (place 516)

### Helkath-hazzurim (place 559)

### Hermon (place 564)
