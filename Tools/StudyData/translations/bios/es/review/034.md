# Quotations to review: es batch 034

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Hethlon (place 567)
- partial (Ezek 47:15): “way of Hethlon” → “camino de Hethlon”
- unsure (Num 34:8): “entrance of Hamath.” → “entrada de Hamat.”

### Hormah (place 580)
- loose (Num 14:44, Num 14:45): “smote and discomfited them even unto Hormah” → “hiriéronlos y derrotáronlos, persiguiéndolos hasta Horma”
- loose (Num 21:1): “fought against them, and took some of them prisoners.” → “peleó con Israel, y tomó de él presa.”
- wording (Josh 12:14): “Hormah” → “Horma”

### Lebanon (place 585)
- wording (Josh 11:16): “the valley,” → “el valle,”
- unsure (2Kings 14:25): “the entering of Hamath” → “la entrada de Hamat”

### Jericho (place 634)
- wording (Josh 6:17): “accursed” → “anatema”
- wording (Lev 27:28): “devoted” → “consagrado”
- partial (Josh 6:24): “only the silver, and the gold, and the vessels of brass and of iron” → “la plata, y el oro, y los vasos de metal y de hierro”
- loose (Josh 6:23, Josh 6:24): “put into the treasury of the house of Jehovah” → “pusieron en el tesoro de la casa de Jehová”
- partial (Josh 6:25): “and her father’s household, and all that she had,” → “y á la casa de su padre, y á todo lo que ella tenía”
- partial (Ezra 2:34): “Children of Jericho” → “Los hijos de Jericó”
