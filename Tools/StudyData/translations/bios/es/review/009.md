# Quotations to review: es batch 009

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### David (person 994)
- partial (1Sam 16:13): “the Spirit of the Lord came upon David from that day forward,” → “desde aquel día en adelante el espíritu de Jehová tomó á David,”
- partial (1Sam 16:14): “the Spirit of the Lord departed from Saul” → “el espíritu de Jehová se apartó de Saúl”
- unsure (1Sam 17:40): “out of the brook,” → “del arroyo,”
- loose (2Sam 23:14, 2Sam 23:15): “Oh that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Bethlehem;” → “¡Quién me diera á beber del agua de la cisterna de Beth-lehem;”
- loose (1Sam 22:17, 1Sam 22:18): “persons who wore a linen ephod” → “varones que vestían ephod de lino”
- loose (1Sam 23:18, 1Sam 23:19): “in the hill Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon,” → “en el collado de Hachîla que está á la mano derecha del desierto,”
- partial (2Sam 1:17): “lamentation over Saul and over Jonathan his son” → “endechó David á Saúl y á Jonathán su hijo”
- wording (2Sam 1:18): “The Bow,” → “El arco,”
- partial (2Sam 1:18): “Behold, it is written in the book of Jasher” → “He aquí que está escrito en el libro del derecho”
- partial (2Sam 18:33): “went up to the chamber over the gate, and wept” → “subióse á la sala de la puerta, y lloró”
- partial (2Sam 18:33): “Would God I had died for thee, O Absalom, my son, my son!” → “¡Quién me diera que muriera yo en lugar de ti, Absalom, hijo mío, hijo mío!”
- partial (1Chr 22:5): “exceeding magnifical, of fame and of glory throughout all countries” → “magnífica por excelencia, para nombre y honra en todas las tierras”
- partial (1Kings 2:10): “and was buried in the city of David.” → “y fué sepultado en la ciudad de David.”
