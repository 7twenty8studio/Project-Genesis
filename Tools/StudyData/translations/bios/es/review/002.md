# Quotations to review: es batch 002

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Abraham (person 58)
- partial (Heb 11:8): “not knowing whither he went” → “sin saber dónde iba”
- partial (Gen 12:2): “I will make of thee a great nation,” → “haré de ti una nación grande,”
- partial (Gen 13:2): “in cattle, in silver, and in gold” → “en ganado, en plata y oro”
- unsure (Gen 15:1): “The word of the Lord” → “La palabra de Jehová”
- unsure (Num 23:17): “came to him” → “vino a él”
- unsure (Gen 19:28): “smoke of a furnace” → “humo de un horno”
- partial (Judg 6:3): “children of the east” → “los orientales”
- partial (James 2:23): “the friend of God” → “amigo de Dios”
- wording (Gal 3:9): “faithful Abraham” → “el creyente Abraham”
- partial (Rom 4:16): “the father of us all” → “padre de todos nosotros”
