# Quotations to review: pt batch 002

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Abraham (person 58)
- partial (Heb 11:8): “not knowing whither he went” → “saiu sem que soubesse aonde ia”
- partial (Gen 12:2): “I will make of thee a great nation,” → “farei de ti uma grande nação”
- partial (Gen 13:2): “in cattle, in silver, and in gold” → “em gado, em prata e ouro”
- unsure (Gen 15:1): “The word of the Lord” → “A palavra do Senhor”
- unsure (Num 23:17): “came to him” → “veio a ele”
- unsure (Gen 19:28): “smoke of a furnace” → “fumaça de um forno”
- partial (Judg 6:3): “children of the east” → “os orientais”
- partial (James 2:23): “the friend of God” → “amigo de Deus”
- wording (Gal 3:9): “faithful Abraham” → “o crente Abraão”
- partial (Rom 4:16): “the father of us all” → “Pai de todos nós”

### Absalom (person 59)
- partial (2Sam 18:33): “O my son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! would God I had died for thee, O Absalom, my son, my son!” → “Filho meu Absalão, filho meu, filho meu Absalão! Quem me dera que morresse eu em lugar de ti, Absalão, filho meu, filho meu!”

### Adam (person 78)
- loose (Gen 1:27): “God created man [Heb., Adam] in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.” → “E criou Deus o ser humano à sua imagem, à imagem de Deus o criou; macho e fêmea os criou.”
- partial (Gen 2:17): “Of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil thou shalt not eat of it; for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.” → “da árvore do conhecimento do bem e do mal não comerás dela; porque no dia que dela comeres, morrerás.”
- partial (Gen 2:23): “This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.” → “Esta é agora osso de meus ossos, e carne de minha carne: esta será chamada Mulher, porque do homem foi tomada.”
- partial (Acts 17:26): “hath made of one blood all nations of men for to dwell on all the face of the earth” → “fez toda nação humana, para habitarem sobre a face da terra”
