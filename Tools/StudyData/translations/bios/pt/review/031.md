# Quotations to review: pt batch 031

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Egypt (place 362)
- wording (Isa 19:6): “defence” → “defesa”
- unsure (Num 13:29): “the land of the south” → “a terra do sul”
- partial (Ex 1:8): “which knew not Joseph” → “que não conhecia José”
- unsure (Gen 45:10): “the land of Goshen” → “a terra de Gósen”
