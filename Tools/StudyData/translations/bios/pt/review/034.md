# Quotations to review: pt batch 034

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Hethlon (place 567)
- partial (Ezek 47:15): “way of Hethlon” → “caminho de Hetlom”
- unsure (Num 34:8): “entrance of Hamath.” → “entrada de Hamate.”

### Hormah (place 580)
- loose (Num 14:44, Num 14:45): “smote and discomfited them even unto Hormah” → “os feriram e os derrotaram, perseguindo-os até Hormá”
- loose (Num 21:1): “fought against them, and took some of them prisoners.” → “lutou contra Israel, e dele levou alguns como prisioneiros.”
- wording (Josh 12:14): “Hormah” → “Hormá”

### Lebanon (place 585)
- wording (Josh 11:16): “the valley,” → “o vale,”
- unsure (2Kings 14:25): “the entering of Hamath” → “a entrada de Hamate”

### Jericho (place 634)
- wording (Josh 6:17): “accursed” → “anátema”
- wording (Lev 27:28): “devoted” → “consagrado”
- partial (Josh 6:24): “only the silver, and the gold, and the vessels of brass and of iron” → “a prata, e o ouro, e os vasos de bronze e de ferro”
- loose (Josh 6:23, Josh 6:24): “put into the treasury of the house of Jehovah” → “puseram no tesouro da casa do SENHOR”
- partial (Josh 6:25): “and her father’s household, and all that she had,” → “e à casa de seu pai, e a tudo o que ela tinha,”
- partial (Ezra 2:34): “Children of Jericho” → “de Jericó”
