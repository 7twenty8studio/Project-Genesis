# Quotations to review: pt batch 033

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Giloh (place 479)
- wording (2Sam 15:12): “the Gilonite” → “o gilonita”

### Golgotha (place 487)
- partial (Mark 15:22): “the place of a skull” → “o lugar da caveira”
- wording (John 19:41): “garden” → “jardim”

### Gomorrah (place 489)
- partial (2Pet 2:6): “ensample unto those that after should live ungodly” → “exemplo para os que vivessem impiamente”

### Goshen (place 490)

### Gozan (place 492)
