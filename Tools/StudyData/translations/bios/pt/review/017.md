# Quotations to review: pt batch 017

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

### Moses (person 2108)
- unsure (Gen 47:6): “best of the land” → “melhor da terra”
- partial (Ex 1:7): “the land was filled with them” → “a terra encheu-se deles”
- partial (Ex 1:14): “all their service, wherein they made them serve, was with rigour” → “em todo o seu serviço, ao qual os obrigavam com rigor”
- loose (Ex 1:12, Ex 1:13): “the more the Egyptians afflicted them, the more they multiplied and grew” → “quanto mais os oprimiam, mais se multiplicavam e cresciam”
- unsure (Ex 1:20): “the people multiplied” → “o povo se multiplicou”
- loose (Ex 2:5, Ex 2:6): “saw the child; and behold the child wept.” → “viu ao menino; e eis que o menino chorava.”
- partial (Ex 2:9): “Take this child away, and nurse it for me, and I will give thee thy wages.” → “Leva este menino, e cria-o para mim, e eu te o pagarei.”
- wording (Ex 2:10): “Moses” → “Moisés”
- wording (Ex 2:11): “brethren.” → “irmãos.”
- partial (Acts 7:22): “learned in all the wisdom of the Egyptians” → “instruído em toda a sabedoria dos egípcios”
- loose (Ex 2:10, Ex 2:11): “went out unto his brethren, and looked upon their burdens” → “saiu a seus irmãos, e viu suas cargas”
- partial (Ex 2:15): “sought to slay Moses” → “procurou matar a Moisés”
- partial (Ex 3:11): “bring forth the children of Israel” → “tire do Egito aos filhos de Israel,”
- loose (Deut 34:1): “the mountain of Nebo (q.v.), to the top of Pisgah, that is over against Jericho” → “monte Nebo, ao cume de Pisga, que está em frente de Jericó”
- loose (Deut 34:2, Deut 34:3): “Jehovah shewed him all the land of Gilead, unto Dan, and all Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim, and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah, unto the utmost sea, and the south, and the plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, unto Zoar” → “e a todo Naftali, e a terra de Efraim e de Manassés, toda a terra de Judá até o mar mais distante … a parte sul, e a campina, o vale de Jericó, cidade das palmeiras, até Zoar”
- partial (Deut 34:6): “in a valley in the land of Moab, over against Beth-peor” → “no vale, em terra de Moabe, em frente de Bete-Peor”
- partial (Deut 33:1): “Moses the man of God” → “Moisés homem de Deus”
- partial (Heb 11:27): “he endured as seeing him who is invisible.” → “perseverou como que vendo aquele que é invisível.”
- partial (Deut 34:10, Deut 34:11, Deut 34:12): “There arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses, whom the Lord knew face to face, in all the signs and the wonders, which the Lord sent him to do in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to all his land, and in all that mighty hand, and in all the great terror which Moses shewed in the sight of all Israel” → “Nunca mais se levantou profeta em Israel como Moisés, a quem haja conhecido o SENHOR face a face; Em todos os sinais e prodígios que lhe enviou o SENHOR a fazer em terra do Egito a Faraó, e a todos os seus servos, e a toda sua terra; E em toda aquela mão poderosa, e em todo o grande espanto que Moisés causou à vista de todo Israel”
