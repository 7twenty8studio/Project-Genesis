# Quotations to review: pt batch 005

Partial and loose quotations use the Bible's words verbatim; wording ones are Easton commenting on a KJV rendering; unsure ones were found in a verse he doesn't cite and were translated.

- wording (Heb 11:4): “more excellent” → “melhor”
