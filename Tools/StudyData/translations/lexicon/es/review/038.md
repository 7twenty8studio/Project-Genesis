# To review: es lexicon batch 038

### G1373 δίψος (thirst)
- note: The source omits the Hebrew word after 'for'; kept as it is.

### G1380 δοκέω (to think)
- note: Source 'Schmidt, with 17' is a garbled 'Schmidt, c. 17' (chapter 17), rendered 'c 17'. 'LXXx' (Trench's § lxxx) kept as written.

### G1384 δόκιμος (tested)
- note: Source 'seel.' (twice) is a garbled 'sel.' (selection/reading), kept as written; 'א with a vid' is a garbled 'א c a vid' (corrector, apparently), rendered 'c a vid'.

### G1386 δόλιος (deceitful)
- note: The source omits the Hebrew word after 'for'; kept as it is.

### G1387 δολιόω (to deceive)
- note: Source 'imperfect 3rd of person(s) pl.' is a garbled 'impf. 3 pers. pl.', rendered as such.

### G1393 Δορκάς (Dorcas)
- note: Gloss 'Dorcas' is the Reina-Valera spelling, the same as the English.

### G1400 δοῦλον (slave)
- note: This entry is from Mounce's dictionary, not Abbott-Smith; its English reference abbreviations (Mt., Phil., Lk., Acts, Jn., 1Cor.…) are kept as codes.

### G1406 δραχμή (drachma)
- note: The source's word order here is garbled ('for in בֶּקַע, שֶׁקֶל Ezra for'); kept as it is.

### G1411 δύναμις (power)
- note: Source '(with)' is a garbled '(c)' (the third subsection), rendered '(c)'.

### G1424 δυσμή (west)
- note: Source 'accusative to Thayer' is a garbled 'acc. to' (according to): rendered 'según'.

### G1438 ἑαυτοῦ (themself)
- note: Source 'pi.' after the dative and accusative is a typo for 'pl.', rendered 'pl.'.

### G1456 ἐγκαίνια (Feast|Festival of Dedication)
- note: Source 'HSjrj' is a garbled transliteration of the Hebrew word, kept as written.

### G1457 ἐγκαινίζω (to inaugurate)
- note: Source '(Br-m pi.)' and '(-^jn)' are garbled transliterations of the Hebrew words, kept as written.

### G1473 ἐγώ (I/we)
- note: Source '(with)' is a garbled '(c)' (the third subsection), rendered '(c)'. The source's 'I.' on its own line is the pronoun's meaning ('I'), rendered 'yo.'.
