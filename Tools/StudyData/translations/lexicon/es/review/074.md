# To review: es lexicon batch 074

### G6081 συμπίπτω (be downcast)
- note: Source 'with part' read as 'with part.' (participle). 'et' (Latin, in 'accusative et infinitive') kept.

### G6083 σύνοιδα (be conscious of)
- note: Lemma 'ύν-οιδα' (σύν-οιδα missing its σ) kept as in the source; 'prop. reflex.' read as 'pron. reflex.' (reflexive pronoun).

### G6084 Τιτίος (Titius)
- note: Not in the RV1909 (TR: 'uno llamado Justo', Hechos 18:7): Ticio, the usual Spanish form; Justo as the RV1909 spells it.

### G6095 Ἀρνὶ (Arni)
- note: Names as the RV1909 spells them (Rut 4:19; 1 Crónicas 2:9, 18, 21, 24; 'Ashur' modernized to Asur). Arní and Admín (not in the RV1909, whose Lucas 3:33 has Aram) in the usual Spanish form.

### G6632 ἀφυστερέω (to withhold)
- note: Source '(Ne, i.e.;' is a misread 'l.c.' (the passage cited), rendered 'l. con' like 'Sir, l.with' before it.

### G9315 συνεπιτίθημι (to join in making an attack)
- note: Source garble '̀ 6 6̀ το' (a misencoded 'to') kept as written.
