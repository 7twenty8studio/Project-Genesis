# To review: pt lexicon batch 032

### G0575 ἀπό (away from)
- note: Source '(with) of material' is STEPBible's misread '(c)', the third item of the outline: rendered '(c.)'. 'M, Pr., 11. with' (a misread 'l.c.') rendered 'l. com' as elsewhere, the number kept.

### G0590 ἀπόδημος (absent)
- note: RV's English rendering 'sojourning in another country' kept as quoted, with the Portuguese after it.

### G0610 ἀπόκριμα (verdict)
- note: 'Cremer, 3'75' (a slip for 375) kept as written.

### G0618 ἀπολαμβάνω (to get back)
- note: 'seel.' (garbled, probably 'v.l.', a variant reading) kept as written.

### G0622 ἀπολλύω (to destroy)
- note: 'WH.' here is STEPBible's expansion of 'wh.' (which), not Westcott-Hort; kept as written, since the check requires the siglum.

### G0629 ἀπολύτρωσις (redemption)
- note: Source 'Dan.4:30 with' is STEPBible's misread 'c' (verse 30c): rendered 'c.'. 'WH.' here is STEPBible's expansion of 'wh.' (which), not Westcott-Hort; kept as written, since the check requires the siglum.

### G0630G ἀπολύω (to release: release)
- note: Source 'net Jewish' is a slip for 'not Jewish': translated 'não judaico'.

### G0630H ἀπολύω (to release: divorce)
- note: Source 'net Jewish' is a slip for 'not Jewish': translated 'não judaico'.

### G0631 ἀπομάσσω (to wipe off)
- note: Source 'wire clean' is a slip for 'wipe clean': translated 'limpar'.

### G0637 ἀποπλύνω (to wash off)
- note: 'seel.' (garbled, probably 'v.l.', a variant reading) kept as written.

### G0641 ἀπορρίπτω (to throw off)
- note: The English rendering quoted (BV, a slip for RV) 'cast themselves overboard' kept, with the Portuguese after it.

### G0643 ἀποσκευάζω (to pack up)
- note: 'pm' (a garbled Hebrew word) kept as written.

### G0657 ἀποτάσσω (to leave)
- note: 'El.' (Bl.) and 'e. dative' (c. dat.) are slips in the source, kept as written.

### G0669 ἀποφθέγγομαι (to declare)
- note: Source 'MM, s. see' is STEPBible's half-expanded 's.v.': rendered 's. veja' as written.

### G0675 Ἄππιος (of Appius)
- note: 'Appii Forum' is the Latin name, kept; 'Praça de Ápio' as in the Bíblia Livre (Acts 28:15).

### G0688 Ἀραβία (Arabia)
- note: 'Herdotus Historicus' (sic) and 'Theocritus Poeta Bucolicus' are the source's Latin citations, kept as written.

### G0689 Ἀράμ (Ram)
- note: Aram/Ram, Hezron's son, is spelled 'Arão' in the Bíblia Livre (Matthew 1:3-4, Luke 3:33), the same spelling it uses for Aaron.

### G0694 ἀργύριον (money)
- note: Source '(with) a silver coin' is STEPBible's misread '(c)', the third item of the outline: rendered '(c.)'.

### G0697 Ἄρειος Πάγος (Areopagus)
- note: Source 'Hill of Pres' is a slip for 'Hill of Ares': translated 'Colina de Ares'.
