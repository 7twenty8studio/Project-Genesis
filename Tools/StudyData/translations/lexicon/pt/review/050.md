### G2596 κατά (according to)
- note: Source '(with)' (three times: the (c) of I.1, II.1 and II.3) is a garbled '(c)': rendered '(c.)'. Source 'l.with' is a misread 'l. c.': rendered 'l. com'. 'He 6:13,16' and 'Act.2:1o' copied as in the source.
