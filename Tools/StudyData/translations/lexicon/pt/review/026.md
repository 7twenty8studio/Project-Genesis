# To review: pt lexicon batch 026

### H7984 שִׁלְטוֹן (governor)
- note: Source carries STEPBible's editorial note glued to the Hebrew ('lemma ... missing vowel, corrected to'); translated as 'lema ... vogal ausente, corrigido para', Hebrew kept as is.

### H8043 שִׁמְאָם (Shimeam)
- note: Shimam (Shimeam) is Simeia in the Bíblia Livre (1 Crônicas 9:38).

### H8078 שִׁמָּמוֹן (horror)
- note: Gloss: the Portuguese word is spelled as in English (horror).

### H8149 שְׂנִיר (Senir)
- note: Strong's two spellings (Shenir or Senir) are both Senir in Almeida / Bíblia Livre.

### H8169 שַׁעַלְבִים (Shaalbim)
- note: Strong's two spellings (Shaalbim or Shaalabbin) are both Saalbim in Almeida / Bíblia Livre.

### H8186A שַׁעֲרוּרָה (horror)

### H8186B שַׁעֲרִירִיָּה (horror)

### H8195 שְׁפוֹ, שְׁפִי (Shepho)
- note: Strong's two spellings (Shepho or Shephi) are both Sefô in Almeida / Bíblia Livre.

### H8197A שְׁפוּפָם (Shephupham)
- note: Sefufã as in Almeida; the Bíblia Livre reads Sufã in Números 26:39.

### H8197B שְׁפוּפָן (Shephuphan)
- note: Sefufã as in Almeida (1 Crônicas 8:5); the Bíblia Livre reads Sefufá.
