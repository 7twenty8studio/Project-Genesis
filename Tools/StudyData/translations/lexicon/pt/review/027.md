# To review: pt lexicon batch 027

### H8303 שִׂרְיֹן (Sirion)
- note: Strong's two spellings (Shirjon or Sirjon) are both Siriom in Almeida / Bíblia Livre.

### H8338 שִׁשֵּׁא (to lead)
- note: Source derivation is cut off ('a primitive root; apparently,'); translated as is.

### H8471 תַּחְפַּנְחֵס (Tahpanhes)
- note: Strong's three spellings (Tachpanches, Techaphneches or Tachpenes) are all Tafnes in Almeida / Bíblia Livre; the Hebrew forms stay in the derivation.

### H8483 תַּחְתִּים חׇדְשִׁי (Kadesh)
- note: Gloss 'Kadesh' (STEPBible) translated as Cades; the definition's Tachtim-Chodshi is Tatim-Hodsi (2 Samuel 24:6; the Bíblia Livre has 'terra baixa de Hodsi').

### H8484 תִּיכוֹן (middle)
- note: Definition: the Portuguese word is spelled as in English (central).

### H8501 תָּכָךְ (deceitful)
- note: Source derivation is cut off ('from an unused root meaning'); translated as is.

### H8556B תִּמְנַת סֶ֫רַח (Timnath-serah)
- note: Timnath-serah (gloss) is Timnate-Sera in Almeida; the Bíblia Livre reads Timnate-Heres in Josué 19:50 too. Definition's Timnath-Cheres is Timnate-Heres.

### H8567 תָּנָה (to recount)
- note: Source derivation reads 'identical with through' (number missing in the source); translated as is.

### H8590 תַּעֲנָךְ (Taanach)
- note: Strong's two spellings (Taanak or Tanak) are both Taanaque in Almeida / Bíblia Livre.

### H8606 תִּפְלֶ֫צֶת (terror)
- note: Gloss: the Portuguese word is spelled as in English (terror).
