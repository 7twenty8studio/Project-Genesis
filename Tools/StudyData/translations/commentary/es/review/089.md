# To review: es commentary batch 089

### 3686 2Cor 6:11-18 By affection for them, And by earnest concern, that they might have no fellowship with unbelievers and idolaters.
- flag: The English has a typo, "they must expect must distress" (for "much distress"); translated as "mucha angustia".
- flag: Unlisted echoes of 2 Cor 6:14 ("unequally yoked"), Gen 2:18 ("help meet"), 2 Cor 6:17 ("come out... separate") and 2 Cor 6:18 ("sons and daughters of the Almighty") translated; "this present evil world" (Gal 1:4) given as the RV1909's "este presente siglo malo" in Henry's own wording; "ayuda idónea" (Gen 2:18 RV1909) used for "help meet".

### 3687 2Cor 7:1-4 An exhortation to holiness, and the whole church entreated to bear affection to the apostle.
- quoted (2Cor 7:1): “cleanse ourselves from all filthiness” → “de toda inmundicia de carne y de espíritu”
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909's "limpiémonos" can't stand after Henry's "we must", so "debemos limpiarnos" is translated and the rest ("de toda inmundicia de carne y de espíritu") quoted.

### 3688 2Cor 7:5-11 He rejoiced in their sorrowing to repentance.
- quoted (2Cor 7:10): “the sorrow of the world” → “el dolor del siglo”
- flag: Henry's "sorrow" is rendered "dolor" throughout to match the RV1909 of 2 Cor 7:10 ("el dolor que es según Dios... el dolor del siglo"); "this sorrow of a godly sort" given as "este dolor que es según Dios", echoing the RV1909.
- flag: Unlisted echo of 2 Cor 7:5 ("fightings without... fears within") translated in Henry's wording (RV1909: "de fuera, cuestiones; de dentro, temores").

### 3691 2Cor 8:7-9 Enforces this by their gifts, and by the love and grace of Christ.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Heb 11:6 (cited by Henry): "sin fe es imposible agradar" is the RV1909 verbatim; the RV1909's "á Dios" is written "a Dios".

### 3694 2Cor 9:1-5 The reason for sending Titus to collect their alms.
- flag: "adorn the doctrine of God their Saviour in all things" (Titus 2:10) translated in Henry's wording ("their" for the verse's "our"; RV1909: "adornen en todo la doctrina de nuestro Salvador Dios").

### 3695 2Cor 9:6-15 The Corinthians to be liberal and cheerful, The apostle thanks God for his unspeakable gift.
- quoted (2Cor 9:8): “is able to make all grace abound” → “Poderoso es Dios para hacer que abunde … toda gracia”
- quoted (Acts 20:35): “more blessed to give than to receive” → “más bienaventurada cosa es dar que recibir”
- flag: Unlisted echo of Prov 11:24 ("scatter, and yet increase... withhold more than is meet, and it tends to poverty") translated in Henry's wording.
- flag: "pertaining to life and godliness" (2 Pet 1:3) translated.

### 3696 2Cor 10:1-6 The apostle states his authority with meekness and humility.
- quoted (2Cor 10:4): “the weapons of our warfare are” → “las armas de nuestra milicia”
- flag: "mighty through God" (2 Cor 10:4) rendered "poderosos en Dios", as in the RV1909, but not marked as a quotation (Henry applies it to "means", not weapons).

### 3697 2Cor 10:7-11 Reasons with the Corinthians.
- flag: The English is garbled: "We must not think that none outward appearance" (words seem to be missing, perhaps "none [are Christians who lack an imposing] outward appearance"); translated word for word as "No debemos pensar que ninguno apariencia exterior", keeping the gap.

### 3698 2Cor 10:12-18 Seeks the glory of God, and to be approved of him.
- flag: "that honour which cometh from God only" (John 5:44) translated in Henry's wording (RV1909: "la gloria que de sólo Dios viene").

### 3700 2Cor 11:5-15 Shows that he had freely preached the gospel.
- flag: "hijos de desobediencia" (Eph 2:2) and "obreros fraudulentos" (2 Cor 11:13) are RV1909 wording for Henry's unlisted echoes "children of disobedience" and "deceitful workers"; not marked as quotations (two-word phrases).

### 3702 2Cor 11:22-33 He gives an account of his labours, cares, sufferings, dangers, and deliverances.
- quoted (Rom 15:6): “the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “Padre de nuestro Señor Jesucristo”

### 3703 2Cor 12:1-6 The apostle's revelations.
- quoted (1Pet 5:1): “of the glory that shall be revealed” → “la gloria que ha de ser revelada”

### 3704 2Cor 12:7-10 Which were improved to his spiritual advantage.
- quoted (Rom 16:20): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “la gracia del Señor nuestro Jesucristo”
- flag: "thorn in the flesh" / "messenger of Satan" (2 Cor 12:7) rendered with the RV1909's words ("aguijón en la carne", "mensajero de Satanás"); "His strength is made perfect in our weakness" (2 Cor 12:9) given with the RV1909's nouns ("potencia... flaqueza") in Henry's person; "continue instant in prayer" (Rom 12:12) translated.

### 3705 2Cor 12:11-21 The signs of an apostle were in him, His purpose of making them a visit; but he expresses his fear lest he should have to be severe with some.
- flag: "These vast verses" in the English looks like a slip for "These last verses"; translated "Estos últimos versículos" (reviewer).

### 3707 2Cor 13:7-10 He prays for their reformation.
- flag: "dejen de hacer lo malo y aprendan á hacer bien" echoes Isa 1:16-17 (RV1909 "dejad de hacer lo malo: Aprended á hacer bien"), unlisted; given in Henry's person, not marked as a quotation.
- flag: The English begins its last sentence with a lower-case "let"; capitalised in the translation ("Oremos").

### 3709 Gal 1:1-5 The apostle Paul asserts his apostolic character against such as lessened it.
- quoted (Gal 1:4): “gave himself for our sins” → “se dió á sí mismo por nuestros pecados”

- flag: Henry here draws silently on Luther's Galatians ("the infinite greatness of the price", "our weak nature starts back"); translated from Henry's English.
- flag: The English says "It would bring him that is whole, and not him that has need of a physician" (echoing Matt 9:12); translated as written.

- quoted (Gal 2:17): “Christ the minister of sin” → “Cristo ministro de pecado”

### 3716 Gal 2:20-21
