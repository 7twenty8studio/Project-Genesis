# To review: es commentary batch 101

### 4008 Rev 8:7-13 Four sound them.
- quoted (Rev 8:7): “there followed hail and fire mingled with blood” → “fué hecho granizo y fuego, mezclado con sangre”
- quoted (Rev 8:8): “a great mountain, burning with fire, was cast into the sea; and the third part of the sea became blood” → “como un grande monte ardiendo con fuego fué lanzado en la mar; y la tercera parte de la mar se tornó en sangre”
- quoted (Rev 8:10): “The third angel sounded, and there fell” → “el tercer ángel tocó la trompeta, y cayó del cielo”
- quoted (Deut 4:19): “the sun, and the moon, and the stars” → “el sol y la luna y las estrellas”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”
- flag: "sounded" follows the RV1909's "tocó la trompeta" for all four angels; quote 2: the RV1909's "una grande estrella" is shortened to Henry's "a star" outside the quoted run.

### 4010 Rev 9:13-21 The sixth trumpet is followed by the loosing of four angels bound in the great river Euphrates.
- flag: sensitive: Henry identifies the sixth trumpet with the power of the Turks and calls the religion they brought (Islam) "poisonous and ruinous"; he also speaks of "idolatry" in the eastern church.

### 4011 Rev 10:1-7 The Angel of the covenant presents a little open book, which is followed with seven thunders.
- quoted (Rev 10:1): “A rainbow was upon his head” → “el arco celeste sobre su cabeza”
- flag: quote 0: Henry's "A rainbow was upon his head" is given as the RV1909's phrase without a verb ("El arco celeste sobre su cabeza:"), since the RV1909 has none there.

### 4014 Rev 11:3-13 Two witnesses prophesy is sackcloth.
- quoted (Rev 11:11): “The Spirit of life from God” → “el espíritu de vida enviado de Dios”
- flag: source typo in the title: "Two witnesses prophesy is sackcloth" read as "in sackcloth".
- flag: source typo: "the witnesses are not a present exposed" read as "at present".
- flag: "in sackcloth" follows the RV1909's "vestidos de sacos" (Rev 11:3) as "vestidos de saco".
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909 writes "espíritu" in lower case where Henry has "The Spirit of life"; kept verbatim.
- flag: sensitive: "antichrist" and "the antichristian empire" here are, for Henry, the papal power (cf. the following sections).

### 4015 Rev 11:14-19 Under the seventh trumpet, all antichristian powers are to be destroyed and there will be a glorious state of Christ's kingdom upon earth.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ps 65:5 ("by terrible things in righteousness"), given in the RV1909's words "con tremendas cosas, en justicia".
- flag: sensitive: Henry sets "the reign of antichrist" (traditions and decrees, the Scriptures locked up from the people) against the Reformation, i.e. the Roman Catholic Church before the Reformation.

### 4016 Rev 12:1-6 A description of the church of Christ and of Satan, under the figures of a woman and of a great red dragon.
- quoted (Rev 12:1): “her head a crown of twelve stars” → “sobre su cabeza una corona de doce estrellas”
- quoted (Rev 12:4): “third part of the stars” → “la tercera parte de las estrellas”
- flag: unlisted quotations of Rev 12:1 ("clothed with the sun" -> "vestida del sol"; "The moon was under her feet" -> "La luna debajo de sus pies"), given in the RV1909's words.
- flag: sensitive: Henry identifies the dragon's agents with "the pagan empire of Rome".

### 4017 Rev 12:7-11 Michael and his angels fight against the devil and his angels, who are defeated.
- quoted (Rev 12:11): “by the blood of the Lamb” → “por la sangre del Cordero”
- quoted (Rev 12:11): “By the word of their testimony” → “Por la palabra de su testimonio”
- quoted (Rev 12:11): “they loved not their lives” → “no han amado sus vidas”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2Cor 10:4 ("mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds"), given in the RV1909's words "en Dios para la destrucción de fortalezas".
- flag: sensitive: "Pagan idolatry, which was the worship of devils" (Henry on the heathen religion of the Roman empire).

### 4019 Rev 13:1-10 A wild beast rises out of the sea, to whom the dragon gives his power.
- quoted (Rev 13:8): “in the book of life” → “en el libro de la vida”
- flag: sensitive: Henry calls the healing of the beast's wound "introducing popish idolatry, the same in substance" as pagan idolatry (Roman Catholicism).

### 4020 Rev 13:11-18 Another beast, which has two horns like a lamb, but speaks as a dragon.
- quoted (Rev 13:14): “make an image to the beast” → “la imagen de la bestia”
- quoted (Rev 13:12): “whose deadly wound was healed” → “cuya llaga de muerte fué curada”
- quoted (Rev 13:15): “worship the image of the beast” → “la imagen de la bestia”
- quoted (Rev 13:15): “the image of the beast” → “la imagen de la bestia”
- quoted (Rev 13:18): “The number of the beast” → “El número de la bestia”
- flag: quotes 0 and 2: Henry's infinitives ("To make an image to the beast", "To worship the image of the beast") are his own; only "la imagen de la bestia" is the RV1909's run (the RV1909 has "hagan", "adoraren").
- flag: sensitive: the second beast, "a spiritual dominion, professing to be derived from Christ", with "lying wonders, pretended miracles", is for Henry the papacy.

### 4022 Rev 14:6-13 Three angels; one proclaiming the everlasting gospel; another, the downfal of Babylon; and a third, the dreadful wrath of God on the worshippers of the beast. The blessedness of those who die in the Lord.
- flag: unlisted quotations of Job 3:17 ("there the wicked cease from troubling, there the weary are at rest" -> "allí los impíos dejan el perturbar, y allí descansan los de cansadas fuerzas") and Rev 14:13 ("die in the Lord" -> "mueren en el Señor"; "Their works follow them" -> "Sus obras con ellos siguen"), given in the RV1909's words.
- flag: Henry speaks of "four proclamations" though the title names three angels; translated as he wrote it.
- flag: sensitive: Henry reads the angels' proclamations as the progress of the Reformation against "antichrist" (the papacy).

### 4024 Rev 15:1-4 A song of praise is sung by the church.
- quoted (Rev 15:2): “a sea of glass mingled with fire” → “un mar de vidrio mezclado con fuego”
- flag: "the Lord God Almighty" and "the King of saints" follow the RV1909's Rev 15:3 ("Señor Dios Todopoderoso", "Rey de los santos").

### 4025 Rev 15:5-8 Seven angels with the seven plagues; and to them one of the living creatures gives seven golden vials full of the wrath of God.
- quoted (Rev 15:6): “their breasts girded with golden girdles” → “ceñidos alrededor de los pechos con bandas de oro”
- flag: "clothed with pure and white linen" is given in the RV1909's words of Rev 15:6 ("vestidos de un lino limpio y blanco"), unlisted; "vials" are "copas" in the RV1909's Rev 15:7.
- flag: "living creatures" (RV1909 "animales") translated "seres vivientes".

### 4028 Rev 16:12-16 The sixth on the great river Euphrates.
- flag: sensitive: Henry links "the Turkish power" with "idolatry", speaks of "the return of the Jews", and identifies mystical Babylon with Rome.

### 4030 Rev 17:1-6

- flag: sensitive: Henry identifies the harlot Babylon with "papal Rome", gaining rule "by crafty and politic management, with all kinds of deceit", and charges her with shedding "oceans of Christian blood".

### 4031 Rev 17:7-14 And interprets the mystery of the woman, and the beast that had seven heads and ten horns.
- quoted (1Tim 6:15): “the King of kings, and Lord of lords” → “Rey de reyes, y Señor de señores”

- flag: source typo: "This beast was seven heads" read as "had seven heads".
- flag: sensitive: Henry identifies the beast's seat with Rome and says it is "directed by the papacy" and "sets up idolatry again".

### 4032 Rev 17:15-18

### 4033 Rev 18:1-8 Another angel from heaven proclaims the fall of mystical Babylon.

### 4034 Rev 18:9-19 The lamentations over her.
- quoted (Rev 18:9): “The kings of the earth” → “Los reyes de la tierra”
- translated, not quoted (Rev 18:14; Henry changes Rev 18:14's "thy soul" to "their souls"; the RV1909's "los frutos del deseo de tu alma se apartaron de ti" can't stand in his sentence.): “lusted after, are departed from”
- flag: sensitive: the merchants "trafficked for her indulgences, pardons, and honours"; "the Roman antichrist" (the Roman Catholic Church).
- flag: "riches make to themselves wings and fly away" echoes Prov 23:5 (RV1909 "hacerse han alas ... y volarán al cielo"); translated as Henry's sentence.

### 4035 Rev 18:20-24 The church called upon to rejoice in her utter ruin.
- flag: sensitive: "The apostles, who are honoured and daily worshipped at Rome in an idolatrous manner" (Roman Catholic veneration of saints).

### 4036 Rev 19:1-10 The church in heaven and that on earth triumph, and praise the Lord for his righteous judgments.
- quoted (Rev 19:8): “in fine linen, clean and white” → “de lino fino, limpio y brillante”
- translated, not quoted (Rev 19:9; Henry's noun phrase in apposition ("the promises of the gospel, the true sayings of God"); the RV1909's sentence "Estas palabras de Dios son verdaderas" can't stand there.): “the true sayings of God”
- quoted (Rev 19:9): “These are the true sayings of God” → “Estas palabras de Dios son verdaderas”
- flag: quote 0: Henry's "clean and white" (KJV) is "limpio y brillante" in the RV1909's Rev 19:8.
- flag: "millennium" note in parentheses is Henry's own, kept.
- flag: sensitive: "those who worship the elements of bread and wine, and saints, and angels" (Roman Catholic Mass and veneration of saints) and those who deny Christ's deity (Socinians/Unitarians) "stand convicted of idolatry".

### 4037 Rev 19:11-21 A vision of Christ going forth to destroy the beast and his armies.
- translated, not quoted (Rev 19:16; the RV1909 prints Rev 19:16's title in capitals as an inscription ("REY DE REYES Y SEÑOR DE SEÑORES"); in Henry's sentence it is given in ordinary type, same words.): “King of kings, and Lord of lords”
- flag: "The Word of God" (Henry's quotation marks) is "EL VERBO DE DIOS" in the RV1909's Rev 19:13; given as "El Verbo de Dios", and "this Word" as "este Verbo".
