# To review: es commentary batch 075

### 3243 Luke 11:29-36 Christ reproves the Jews.
- flag: "pray without ceasing" (1 Ts 5:17, RV1909 "Orad sin cesar") is worked into Henry's sentence as "orar sin cesar"; Lc 11:35 is adapted ("in us") and translated.

### 3245 Luke 12:1-12 Christ reproves the interpreters of the law.
- quoted (Luke 12:8): “before the angels of God” → “delante de los ángeles de Dios”

### 3246 Luke 12:13-21 A caution against covetousness, The parable of the rich man.
- quoted (Luke 12:20): “Thy soul shall be required” → “Vuelven á pedir tu alma”
- flag: The English has "wordly dominion" (typo for "worldly"); translated "mundano".
- flag: "the folly of carnal worldling while they live" (singular noun, plural pronoun); translated as plural "los mundanos carnales".
- flag: "Thy soul shall be required" is given with the RV1909's "Vuelven á pedir tu alma" (Lc 12:20); Henry's following "God shall require it" is kept as "Dios la pedirá".

### 3247 Luke 12:22-40 Worldly care reproved.
- quoted (Luke 12:36): “men that wait for their lord” → “hombres que esperan cuando su señor ha de volver”
- quoted (Luke 12:40): “Be ye therefore ready also” → “Vosotros pues también, estad apercibidos”
- quoted (Luke 12:39): “what hour the thief would come” → “á qué hora había de venir el ladrón”
- flag: "Christ is our Master": "Master" translated "Señor" to match "servants"/"lord" in the parable.
- flag: "this little flock" (Lc 12:32) given with the RV1909's "manada pequeña" (unlisted).

### 3248 Luke 12:41-53 Watchfulness enforced.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 12:50; Henry puts Lc 12:50 in the third person ("Christ had a baptism to be baptized with"); the RV1909's first-person "me es necesario ser bautizado" cannot stand in his sentence): “a baptism to be baptized”
- flag: Mt 10:36 (unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "los enemigos del hombre serán los de su casa", for "a man's own household may be his foes".

### 3249 Luke 12:54-59 A warning to be reconciled to God.
- flag: 2 Co 5:19 (unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "Dios estaba en Cristo reconciliando el mundo á sí".

### 3250 Luke 13:1-5 Christ exhorts to repentance from the case of the Galileans and others.
- quoted (Matt 4:17): “repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand” → “Arrepentíos, que el reino de los cielos se ha acercado”

### 3251 Luke 13:6-9 Parable of the barren fig-tree.
- flag: sensitive: Henry applies the barren fig-tree "in the first place" to "the nation and people of the Jews".

### 3252 Luke 13:10-17 The infirm woman strengthened.
- flag: Henry moves from "the sabbath day" to "the Lord's day" (Sunday) in the last sentence; translated as he wrote it ("el día del Señor").

### 3253 Luke 13:18-22 The parables of the mustard seed, and leaven.
- flag: Is 60:22 (unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "el pequeño será por mil", for "the little one becomes a thousand".

### 3254 Luke 13:23-30 Exhortation to enter at the strait gate.
- quoted (Luke 13:24): “Strive to enter in at the strait gate” → “Porfiad á entrar por la puerta angosta”
- quoted (Luke 13:24): “enter in at the strait gate” → “entrar por la puerta angosta”
- flag: Title: "the strait gate" rendered "la puerta estrecha" in the heading; in the text the RV1909's "la puerta angosta" is used.
- flag: "Are there few that shall be saved?" (Lc 13:23) given in the RV1909's words, "¿son pocos los que se salvan?" (unlisted). Lc 13:30 is worded differently in the RV1909 and is translated from Henry.

### 3255 Luke 13:31-35 Christ's reproof to Herod, and to the people of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Luke 13:35): “in the name of the Lord” → “en nombre del Señor”

### 3256 Luke 14:1-6 Christ heals a man on the sabbath.
- flag: The source's first two paragraphs ("The necessity of consideration and self-denial." and "(25-35)") are the heading of section 3259 (Lc 14:25-35) misplaced into this one; translated as they stand, paragraphs kept.

### 3257 Luke 14:7-14 He teaches humility.
- flag: Unlisted quotations in the RV1909's words: Pr 29:23 ("la soberbia del hombre le abate") and Pr 15:33 ("delante de la honra está la humildad").

### 3258 Luke 14:15-24 Parable of the great supper.
- flag: sensitive: "This reproves the Jewish nation for their neglect of the offers of Christ's grace."

### 3259 Luke 14:25-35 
- flag: This section has no title in the source; its heading appears misplaced at the start of 3256.
- flag: Sal 90:11 adapted by Henry ("his anger"); the RV1909's "quién conoce la fortaleza de" is followed, with "su ira" for "tu ira".

### 3261 Luke 15:11-16 The prodigal son, his wickedness and distress.
- translated, not quoted (Rom 13:14; the RV1909 of Ro 13:14 reads "no hagáis caso de la carne en sus deseos", which would lose Henry's "make provision for the flesh"): “provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof”
- flag: The English has "a state constant discontent" (missing "of"); translated "un estado de constante descontento".
- flag: "A sinful state is of departure" (missing "a state") translated "un estado de alejamiento".

### 3262 Luke 15:17-24 His repentance and pardon.
- flag: "coming to himself" (Lc 15:17) given with the RV1909's "volviendo en sí" as "vuelve en sí".

### 3263 Luke 15:25-32 The elder brother offended.
- flag: sensitive: "The Jews, in general, showed the same spirit towards the converted Gentiles".

### 3264 Luke 16:1-12 The parable of the unjust steward.
- flag: "the children of light" given with the RV1909's "los hijos de luz" (Lc 16:8). "rich toward God" (Lc 12:21, RV1909 "rico en Dios") translated "ricos para con Dios".

### 3265 Luke 16:13-18 Christ reproves the hypocrisy of the covetous Pharisees.
- flag: "Ye cannot serve God and the world" adapts Lc 16:13 (RV1909 "No podéis servir á Dios y á las riquezas"); Henry puts "the world" for mammon, so translated.

### 3266 Luke 16:19-31 The rich man and Lazarus.
- quoted (2Pet 1:19): “sure word of prophecy” → “palabra profética más permanente”
- flag: Lc 16:23 (unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "alzó sus ojos, estando en los tormentos"; Is 8:20 is followed with "a la ley y al testimonio".
- flag: sensitive: Henry rejects "praying to saints departed" (a Roman Catholic practice) as resting on "the mistake of a damned sinner".

### 3267 Luke 17:1-10 Ten lepers cleansed.
- flag: The titles of 3267 and 3268 appear swapped in the source: this text (Lc 17:1-10) is about offences, faith and humility, while 3268 (Lc 17:11-19) is about the ten lepers. Titles translated as given.

### 3268 Luke 17:11-19 To avoid offences, To pray for increase of faith, Humility taught.
- flag: See 3267: this title belongs to the previous section.

### 3269 Luke 17:20-37 Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Luke 17:30): “Thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed” → “Como esto será el día en que el Hijo del hombre se manifestará”
- flag: sensitive: "When Christ came to destroy the Jewish nation by the Roman armies".

### 3271 Luke 18:9-14 The Pharisee and the publican.
- quoted (Luke 18:9): “trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and despised others” → “confiaban de sí como justos, y menospreciaban á los otros”
- quoted (Luke 18:13): “God be merciful to me a sinner” → “Dios, sé propicio á mí pecador”
- flag: Lc 18:14 (unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "descendió á su casa justificado".

### 3272 Luke 18:15-17 Children brought to Christ.
- flag: "The promise is to us, and to our seed" adapts Hch 2:39 (RV1909 "para vosotros es la promesa, y para vuestros hijos"); Henry changes the person, so translated.

### 3273 Luke 18:18-30 The ruler hindered by his riches.
- flag: The English has "wordly gain" (typo for "worldly"); translated "mundana".

### 3274 Luke 18:31-34 Christ foreshows his death.
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “The Spirit of Christ” → “El Espíritu de Cristo”
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “the glory that should follow” → “las glorias después de ellas”
- flag: 1 P 1:11: "his sufferings" given as "sus aflicciones" so that the RV1909's "las glorias después de ellas" (the glories after them) can follow.

### 3276 Luke 19:1-10 The conversion of Zaccheus.
- quoted (Luke 19:10): “to seek and to save” → “á buscar y á salvar”
- flag: Henry's "to seek and to save it" (the lost world): the RV1909's "á buscar y á salvar" is quoted; "it" is left implicit.

### 3277 Luke 19:11-27 The parable of the nobleman and his servants.
- quoted (1Cor 12:7): “The manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal” → “Á cada uno le es dada manifestación del Espíritu para provecho”
- flag: 1 P 4:10 (Henry's quotation, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "Cada uno según el don que ha recibido, adminístrelo á los otros".
- flag: "the pound" rendered "la mina", the RV1909's word in Lc 19:13.

### 3278 Luke 19:28-40 Christ enters Jerusalem.
- flag: Mt 3:9 (unlisted) followed in the RV1909's words, "puede Dios despertar hijos á Abraham", with Henry's "out of stones" ("aun de las piedras").

### 3281 Luke 20:9-19 The parable of the vineyard and husbandmen.
- flag: sensitive: "the Jews who murdered the prophets and crucified Christ".
