### 1096 1Chr 2:1-55 Genealogies.
- flag: 'dwell alone, and not be reckoned among the nations' echoes Nm 23:9 (RV1909 'habitará confiado, y no será contado entre las gentes'), translated.

### 1098 1Chr 4:1-43 Genealogies.
- quoted (1Chr 4:9): “Jabez was more honourable than his brethren” → “Jabes fué más ilustre que sus hermanos”
- quoted (1Chr 4:10): “Oh that thou wouldest bless me” → “Oh si me dieras bendición”
- flag: 1 Cr 4:9: the RV1909's 'más ilustre' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'more honourable'.
- flag: 'bless him indeed' rests on the KJV's 'bless me indeed'; the RV1909 has only 'me dieras bendición'. 'enlarge his coast' translated 'ensanchara su término' after the RV1909's 'ensancharas mi término'.
- flag: 'a Jabez indeed, a man of sorrow' plays on the name (1 Cr 4:9, RV1909 'Por cuanto le parí en dolor'); kept as 'un hombre de dolor'.
- flag: 'his ear is not now heavy' echoes Is 59:1 (RV1909 'ni hase agravado su oído'), translated.

### 1101 1Chr 7:1-40 Genealogies.

### 1103 1Chr 9:1-44 Genealogies.

### 1107 1Chr 12:1-22 Those who came to David at Ziklag.
