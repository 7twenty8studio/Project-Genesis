# To review: es commentary batch 093

### 3806 1Thess 5:12-15 He directs to several particular duties.
- flag: Source slip "Whatever man do to us" read as "Whatever men do to us".
- flag: unlisted echo of 1 Thess 5:14: RV1909 "sufridos para con todos" used (as "ser sufridos ... para con todos").

### 3807 1Thess 5:16-22 
- translated, not quoted (Eccl 11:8; Henry turns Eccl 11:8 ("si el hombre viviere muchos años, y en todos ellos hubiere gozado alegría") into an infinitive after "expect"; the RV1909's conditional forms can't stand there.): “live many years, and rejoice in them all”
- quoted (Matt 3:11): “with the Holy Ghost and with fire” → “en Espíritu Santo y en fuego”
- translated, not quoted (1Thess 5:21; Henry turns the imperative "retened lo bueno" into an infinitive ("to hold fast that which is good"); rendered "retener lo bueno".): “hold fast that which is good”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Thess 5:19: RV1909 "No apaguéis el Espíritu" used verbatim; "Orad sin cesar" / "Dad gracias en todo" (5:17-18) echoed in Henry's own sentence.
- flag: "abstain from ... whatever looks like sin" rests on the KJV's "all appearance of evil" (1 Thess 5:22); the RV1909 has "toda especie de mal".

### 3808 1Thess 5:23-28 And concludes with prayer, greetings, and a blessing.
- quoted (2Cor 8:9): “know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “la gracia de nuestro Señor Jesucristo”
- flag: "transplanted" (Henry's word for translating Scripture into other tongues) rendered "traducida".
- flag: sensitive: "The word of God should not be kept in an unknown tongue" is aimed at the Roman Catholic practice of the Latin Scriptures, though no church is named.

### intro 2Thess 
- translated, not quoted (2Thess 3:18; Not a quotation: Henry's own opening sentence only happens to match the KJV's subscription to 2 Thessalonians.): “The second epistle to the Thessalonians was written”

### 3809 2Thess 1:1-4 The apostle blesses God for the growing state of the love and patience of the Thessalonians.
- quoted (Prov 4:18): “The path of the just is as the shining light” → “la senda de los justos es como la luz de la aurora”
- quoted (Prov 4:18): “more and more unto the perfect day” → “que va en aumento hasta que el día es perfecto”
- flag: Quotes 0-1 (Prov 4:18): the RV1909's opening "Mas" is kept; KJV "shining light" is "la luz de la aurora" in the RV1909.
- flag: "faith works by love" (Gal 5:6) translated; the RV1909 has "la fe que obra por la caridad".

### 3810 2Thess 1:5-10 And encourages them to persevere under all their sufferings for Christ, considering his coming at the great day of account.
- quoted (2Thess 1:8): “obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “obedecen al evangelio de nuestro Señor Jesucristo”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Joel 1:15 (or Isa 13:6): RV1909 "destrucción por el Todopoderoso" used
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ps 90:11: RV1909 "quién conoce la fortaleza de tu ira" adapted to "de su ira" (Henry: "his anger")
- flag: "those that know not God" (2 Thess 1:8) rendered with the RV1909's past tense "no conocieron a Dios".

### 3811 2Thess 1:11-12 
- translated, not quoted (2Thess 1:11; The RV1909 renders 2 Thess 1:11 "hincha de bondad todo buen intento", with no "good pleasure of his goodness"; Henry's point (that it is called grace) needs his wording.): “the good pleasure of his goodness”
- quoted (2Thess 1:11): “the work of faith with power” → “obra de fe con potencia”
- flag: "This is to their doing every other good work" is elliptical in the English; rendered "Esto es para que hagan toda otra buena obra".

### 3812 2Thess 2:1-4 Cautions against the error that the time of Christ's coming was just at hand. There would first be a general apostasy from the faith, and a revealing of the antichristian man of sin.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2 Thess 2:3: RV1909 "hombre de pecado" and "hijo de perdición" used; "falling away" rendered "apostasía" as in the RV1909.
- flag: sensitive: Henry's reading of the "man of sin" points ahead to the papacy (made explicit in section 3813).

### 3813 2Thess 2:5-12 His destruction, and that of those who obey him.
- quoted (2Thess 2:4): “himself above all that is called God, or that is worshipped” → “contra todo lo que se llama Dios, ó que se adora”
- quoted (2Thess 2:8): “with the brightness of his coming” → “con el resplandor de su venida”
- flag: sensitive: Henry identifies the man of sin with "popery", "the Romish church" and "the Romish popes" ("papismo", "la iglesia romana", "los papas romanos"); translated in full.
- flag: unlisted quotations of 2 Thess 2:7, 2:9: RV1909 "misterio de iniquidad" and "milagros mentirosos" used.
- flag: Quote 0: KJV "exalteth himself above" is "levantándose contra" in the RV1909, used as "se ha levantado contra".

### 3814 2Thess 2:13-15 The security of the Thessalonians from apostacy; an exhortation to stedfastness, and prayer for them.
- quoted (Rom 11:5): “there is a remnant according to the election of grace” → “han quedado reliquias por la elección de gracia”
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909's "reliquias" (remnant) kept as in Rom 11:5.
- flag: unlisted quotations: Jer 31:3 "amor eterno", 2 Thess 2:13 "fe de la verdad" and 2 Thess 2:15 "estad firmes" (as "Estemos firmes") from the RV1909.
- flag: sensitive: "reject all additions, and vain traditions" is aimed against Roman Catholic tradition, though no church is named.

### 3815 2Thess 2:16-17

- flag: unlisted quotation of 2 Thess 2:16: RV1909 "buena esperanza por gracia" used verbatim

### 3816 2Thess 3:1-5 The apostle expresses confidence in the Thessalonians, and prays for them.
- flag: unlisted quotations of 2 Thess 3:2 and 3:5: RV1909 "hombres importunos y malos" and "el amor de Dios, y en la paciencia de Cristo" (as "el amor de Dios y la paciencia de Cristo") used.
- flag: The last sentence repeats "that we may be enabled, without reserve" and its second half ("enabled ... to the love of God") is incomplete in the English (KJV 2 Thess 3:5 "direct your hearts into the love of God"); translated as it stands, repetition kept.

### 3817 2Thess 3:6-15 He charges them to withdraw from disorderly walkers, particularly from the lazy and busybodies.

- flag: Henry's "always, or in every thing" / "by all means" rest on the KJV's "always by all means" (2 Thess 3:16); the RV1909 has "siempre en toda manera".

### 3819 1Tim 1:1-4 The apostle salutes Timothy.

### 3820 1Tim 1:5-11 The design of the law as given by Moses.

### 3821 1Tim 1:12-17 Of his own conversion and call to the apostleship.
- quoted (1Tim 1:15): “This is a faithful saying” → “Palabra fiel”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Tim 1:15: RV1909 "vino al mundo" and "para salvar á los pecadores" used around Henry's inserted "willingly and purposely".
- flag: "extreme to mark what was amiss" echoes Ps 130:3 KJV; the RV1909 there ("si mirares á los pecados") is different, so translated.

### 3822 1Tim 1:18-20 The obligation to maintain faith and a good conscience.
- flag: "the Captain of our salvation" (Heb 2:10 KJV): the RV1909 has "el autor de la salud de ellos"; Henry's title translated "el Capitán de nuestra salvación".
- flag: unlisted echo of 1 Tim 1:19: RV1909 "hicieron naufragio en la fe" (as "hacer naufragio también en la fe").

### 3823 1Tim 2:1-7 Prayer to be made for all persons, since the grace of the gospel makes no difference of ranks or stations.

- quoted (2Tim 3:7): “come to the knowledge of the truth” → “llegar al conocimiento de la verdad”
- flag: "godliness" and "honesty" (1 Tim 2:2 KJV): the RV1909 has "piedad y honestidad", matching Henry's two words.

### 3824 1Tim 2:8-15 How men and women ought to behave, both in their religious and common life.

- flag: Henry's "in child-bearing, or with child-bearing" weighs the KJV's preposition (1 Tim 2:15 "saved in childbearing"); the RV1909 has "se salvará engendrando hijos".
- flag: "sobriety" (KJV 1 Tim 2:15) rendered "modestia", as in the RV1909 ("con modestia").
- flag: unlisted echo of 1 Tim 2:8: RV1909 "en todo lugar" and "sin ira" in Henry's sentence.

### 3827 1Tim 3:14-16 The reason of writing about these, and other church affairs.
