# To review: es commentary batch 014

### 436 Num 15:1-21 The law of the meat-offering and the drink-offering, The stranger under the same law.
- quoted (Num 15:2): “which I give unto you” → “Cuando hubiereis entrado en la tierra de vuestras habitaciones, que yo os doy”
- flag: 'meat-offering' (KJV) rendered 'ofrenda de presente' as the RV1909 calls it (Nm 15:4).

### 437 Num 15:22-29 The sacrifice for the sin of ignorance.
- quoted (Luke 23:34): “Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do” → “Padre, perdónalos, porque no saben lo que hacen”

### 438 Num 15:30-36 The punishment of presumption, The sabbath-breaker stoned.
- quoted (Num 15:31): “the word of the Lord” → “la palabra de Jehová”
- flag: 'presumption' / 'presumptuous sinners' rendered 'soberbia' / 'pecadores soberbios' (RV1909 Nm 15:30 'con soberbia').

### 439 Num 15:37-41 The law for fringes on garment.
- quoted (Num 15:38): “the borders of their garments” → “los remates de sus vestidos”
- translated, not quoted (2Pet 3:1; the RV1909 says 'despierto con exhortación vuestro limpio entendimiento' (first person, 'exhortation'); it can't stand as Henry's 'stir up their minds by way of remembrance' about the fringes.): “minds by way of remembrance”
- quoted (Num 15:40): “Be holy unto your God” → “santos á vuestro Dios”
- quoted (Num 15:41): “I am the Lord your God” → “Yo Jehová vuestro Dios”
- flag: RV1909 Nm 15:38 calls the fringes 'pezuelos (franjas)'; Henry's own 'fringes' rendered 'flecos'.

### 440 Num 16:1-11 The rebellion of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram Korah contends for the priesthood.
- flag: 'The heart of the wise studies to answer' echoes Pr 15:28 ('the heart of the righteous'; RV1909 'El corazón del justo piensa para responder'); not a verbatim quotation, translated.
- flag: title as in the source, two headings run together ('Abiram Korah').
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 442 Num 16:16-22 The glory of the Lord appears, The intercession of Moses and Aaron.
- quoted (Lev 9:23): “glory of the Lord” → “gloria de Jehová”

### 443 Num 16:23-34 The earth swallows up Dathan and Abiram.
- flag: 'come out from among them, and be separate' echoes 2 Co 6:17 (RV1909 'salid de en medio de ellos, y apartaos'); Henry changes the person, translated.
- flag: 'the things that accompany salvation' echoes He 6:9 (RV1909 'más cercanas á salud'); translated as Henry's phrase.

### 444 Num 16:35-40 The company of Korah consumed.
- quoted (Num 16:35): “from the Lord, and consumed the two hundred and fifty men that offered incense” → “de Jehová, y consumió los doscientos y cincuenta hombres que ofrecían el sahumerio”
- quoted (Prov 15:8): “The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord” → “El sacrificio de los impíos es abominación á Jehová”
- translated, not quoted (Deut 17:13; Henry turns Dt 17:13 into a purpose clause about 'others' ('oirá, y temerá, y no se ensoberbecerán más' in the RV1909); the verbs had to change mood, translated with the RV1909's words.): “hear and fear, and do no more presumptuously”

### 445 Num 16:41-50 The people murmur A plague sent.
- quoted (Num 16:41, Num 16:42): “the people of the Lord” → “pueblo de Jehová”
- quoted (Num 16:48): “between the dead and the living” → “entre los muertos y los vivos”
- quoted (Rom 5:8): “us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us” → “su caridad para con nosotros, porque siendo aún pecadores, Cristo murió por nosotros”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Col 1:14 / Ef 1:7 ('redemption through his blood, even the remission of sins'): RV1909 'redención por su sangre, la remisión de pecados' used.

### 447 Num 17:8-13 Aaron's rod buds, and is kept for a memorial.
- quoted (Isa 11:1): “a rod out of the stem of Jesse” → “una vara del tronco de Isaí”
- quoted (Num 17:12): “Behold, we die, we perish, we all perish” → “He aquí nosotros somos muertos, perdidos somos, todos nosotros somos perdidos”
- flag: first two sentences run together in the source ('While all the other rods remained as they were. Aaron's rod became…'); joined into one sentence.
- flag: Henry's '!' after the people's cry dropped because the RV1909's words end with a full stop in the verse; check placement.

### 448 Num 18:1-7 The charge of the priests and Levites.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ro 11:20 ('Be not high-minded, but fear'): RV1909 'No te ensoberbezcas, antes teme' used.

### 449 Num 18:8-19 The priests' portion.
- flag: unlisted quotations: 1 Ti 4:8 (RV1909 'tiene promesa de esta vida presente') and Gá 6:7 (RV1909 'Dios no puede ser burlado') used.
- flag: 'Scandalous maintenance makes scandalous ministers': 'scandalous' in the old sense of shamefully meagre; rendered 'mezquino' both times to keep the wordplay.

### 450 Num 18:20-32 The Levites' portion.
- translated, not quoted (Num 18:32; Henry applies the verse to 'us' ('so as to bear no sin by reason of them'); the RV1909's second-person 'no llevaréis por ello pecado' quoted in full right after.): “bear no sin by reason”
- quoted (Num 18:32): “Ye shall bear no sin by reason of it, when ye have heaved” → “hubiereis ofrecido de ello lo mejor suyo, no llevaréis por ello pecado”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2 Ts 3:10: RV1909 'si alguno no quisiere trabajar, tampoco coma' used.
- flag: quote 1: Henry puts 'Ye shall bear no sin' first; the RV1909's clauses kept in its own order ('cuando… no llevaréis por ello pecado').

### 451 Num 19:1-10 The ashes of a heifer.
- flag: 'heifer' rendered 'vaca' as in the RV1909 (Nm 19:2, 'una vaca bermeja'); 'a purification for sin' is the KJV of Nm 19:9 (RV1909 'es una expiación'), translated as Henry's words.

### 452 Num 19:11-22 Used to purify the unclean.
- flag: unlisted quotations used from the RV1909: 2 Ti 1:10 ('á la luz la vida y la inmortalidad'), Jn 7:38 ('ríos de agua viva'), 1 Co 15:33 ('las malas conversaciones'); 'death is the wages of sin' (Ro 6:23) kept in Henry's word order ('la muerte es la paga del pecado').

### 453 Num 20:1-13 The people come to Zin, They murmur for water, Moses directed to smite the rock, The infirmity of Moses and Aaron.
- quoted (Num 20:2): “There was no water for the congregation” → “agua para la congregación”
- flag: 'Must we fetch water?' (KJV Nm 20:10): RV1909 '¿os hemos de hacer salir aguas de esta peña?' used, shortened as Henry quotes it.
- flag: 'spake unadvisedly with his lips' is the KJV of Sal 106:33; the RV1909 says it differently ('como lo expresó con sus labios'), so translated.
- flag: unlisted 1 Co 1:30: RV1909 'sabiduría, y justificación, y santificación, y redención' used (KJV 'righteousness').

### 454 Num 20:14-21 The Israelites are refused a passage through Edom.
- quoted (Gen 27:41): “Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing” → “aborreció Esaú á Jacob por la bendición”

### 455 Num 20:22-29 Aaron reigns the priest's office to Eleazar, and dies in mount Hor.
- flag: title typo in the source: 'reigns' for 'resigns'; translated as 'entrega'.
- flag: unlisted quotations used from the RV1909: Nm 20:24 ('reunido á sus pueblos', 'las aguas de la rencilla') and He 7:19 ('la introducción de mejor esperanza').

### 457 Num 21:4-9 The people murmuring, are plagued with fiery serpents, They repenting, are healed through the brazen serpent.
- quoted (John 3:14): “as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness” → “como Moisés levantó la serpiente en el desierto”
- quoted (John 12:34): “the Son of man must be lifted up” → “el Hijo del hombre sea levantado”
- quoted (John 3:15): “whosoever believeth in him, should not perish” → “en él creyere, no se pierda”
- flag: quote 1 is listed as Jn 12:34; Henry is quoting Jn 3:14 (the RV1909 has the same words there).
- flag: 'light bread' (KJV Nm 21:5): RV1909 'pan tan liviano' used; Henry applies it to the word of God, so the pronoun is feminine ('la llame').
- flag: unlisted Mt 8:25: RV1909 'Señor, sálvanos, que perecemos' used.
- flag: 'bites like a serpent, and stings like an adder' echoes Pr 23:32 (RV1909 'como serpiente morderá, y como basilisco dará dolor'); Henry changes the tense, translated.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 458 Num 21:10-20 Further journeys of the Israelites.
- quoted (Isa 12:3): “draw water out of the wells of salvation” → “aguas con gozo de las fuentes de la salud”
- flag: "They set forward" (KJV Nm 21:10): RV1909 'partieron' used.
- flag: 'Vaheb in Suphah' is in the KJV of Nm 21:14 but not in the RV1909 ('Lo que hizo en el mar Bermejo'); kept as Henry names it ('Vaheb en Sufa').
- flag: 'fountain of living waters' (Jer 2:13) and 'rivers of living waters' (Jn 7:38): RV1909 'fuente de agua viva', 'ríos de agua viva'.
- flag: RV1909 Is 12:3 says 'fuentes de la salud' (KJV 'wells of salvation').

### 459 Num 21:21-35 Sihon and Og overcome, Their land possessed.
- flag: unlisted Zac 4:10: RV1909 'el día de las pequeñeces' used.
- flag: 'more than conquerors' (Ro 8:37; RV1909 'hacemos más que vencer'): Henry changes the tense and adds 'over every enemy', translated.

### 460 Num 22:1-14 Balak's fear of Israel, He sends for Balaam.
- flag: 'the curse shall not come upon us if there is not a cause' paraphrases Pr 26:2 (RV1909 'la maldición sin causa nunca vendrá'); translated.
- flag: 'the wages of unrighteousness' (2 P 2:15): RV1909 'el premio de la maldad' used; Nm 22:7 calls it 'las dádivas de adivinación'.

### 461 Num 22:15-21 Balaam goes to Balak.
- flag: 'Shall we then be unwilling to do what is right? God forbid!': the second sentence answers the question as stated (we must not be unwilling); rendered '¡De ninguna manera!'.
- flag: 'many call God theirs… not truly because not only his' (Balaam's 'the LORD my God', Nm 22:18): compressed in the source; translated closely.

### 462 Num 22:22-35 The opposition to Balaam by the way.
- quoted (Eccl 8:11): “fully set in them to do evil” → “lleno para hacer mal”
- quoted (Num 22:28): “The Lord opened the mouth of the ass” → “Jehová abrió la boca al asna”
- flag: 'I have sinned' (Nm 22:34): RV1909 'He pecado' used.

### 463 Num 22:36-41 Balaam and Balak meet.
- flag: unlisted Mt 6:9, 13: RV1909 'Padre nuestro que estás en los cielos' and 'no nos metas en tentación' used, joined with '…' (two verses).

### 464 Num 23:1-10 Balak's sacrifice, Balaam pronounces a blessing instead of a curse.
- quoted (Num 23:10): “die the death of the righteous” → “de la muerte de los rectos”
- flag: 'Happy in their last end': RV1909 Nm 23:10 'postrimería' echoed ('Dichosos en su postrimería').
- flag: 'the righteous' rendered 'los rectos' where Henry echoes Nm 23:10 (the RV1909's word), 'los justos' elsewhere.

### 465 Num 23:11-30 Balak's disappointment, and second sacrifice, Balaam again blesses Israel.
- flag: 'many devices in man's heart, God's counsels shall stand' paraphrases Pr 19:21 (RV1909 'Muchos pensamientos hay en el corazón del hombre; mas el consejo de Jehová permanecerá'); translated with its words where they fit.
- flag: unlisted Hab 2:3: RV1909 'al fin hablará, y no mentirá' used.
- flag: 'There was sin in Jacob' answers Nm 23:21 (KJV 'He hath not beheld iniquity in Jacob'; RV1909 'No ha notado iniquidad en Jacob').

### 466 Num 24:1-9 Balaam, leaving divinations, prophesies the happiness of Israel.

- flag: unlisted Pr 12:26 ('more excellent than his neighbour'): RV1909 'hace ventaja á su prójimo' used.
- flag: 'bold as a lion' (KJV Pr 28:1): RV1909 'confiado como un leoncillo' keeps Henry's point (bold when at rest).

- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.
