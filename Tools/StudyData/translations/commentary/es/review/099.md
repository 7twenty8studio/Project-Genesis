# To review: es commentary batch 099

### 3962 1John 3:3-10 The purifying influence of the hope of seeing Christ, and the danger of pretending to this, and living in sin.
- quoted (1John 3:7): “He that doeth righteousness is righteous” → “el que hace justicia, es justo”
- quoted (Rom 15:19): “by the power of the Spirit of God” → “en virtud del Espíritu de Dios”
- quoted (1John 3:10): “the children of the devil” → “los hijos del diablo”
- quoted (1John 3:10): “In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil” → “En esto son manifiestos los hijos de Dios, y los hijos del diablo”
- flag: "righteousness" / "righteous": the RV1909 has "justicia" / "justo", used throughout.

### 3963 1John 3:11-15 Love to the brethren is the character of real Christians.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1John 3:14 ("We know that we are passed from death to life"), given in the RV1909's words.

### 3966 1John 4:1-6 Believers cautioned against giving heed to every one that pretends to the Spirit.
- quoted (1John 4:1): “try the spirits whether they are of God” → “los espíritus si son de Dios”
- flag: quote 0: Henry's infinitive "try" is rendered "probar"; the RV1909's imperative "probad" is left outside the quoted run.

### 3967 1John 4:7-13 Brotherly love enforced.
- quoted (1John 4:10): “not that we loved God, but that he” → “no que nosotros hayamos amado á Dios, sino que él”

### 3968 1John 4:14-21 
- quoted (1John 4:14): “The Father sent the Son” → “el Padre ha enviado al Hijo”
- quoted (1John 4:15): “whosoever shall confess that Jesus is the Son of God, God dwelleth in him, and he in God” → “cualquiera que confesare que Jesús es el Hijo de Dios, Dios está en él, y él en Dios”

### 3969 1John 5:1-5 Brotherly love is the effect of the new birth, which makes obedience to all God's commandments pleasant.
- translated, not quoted (1John 5:2; Henry's own sentence ("cannot truly love the children of God"); the RV1909's first-person "amamos" can't stand there.): “love the children of God”

### 3970 1John 5:6-8 Reference to witnesses agreeing to prove that Jesus, the Son of God, is the true Messiah.
- quoted (Eph 5:25, Eph 5:26, Eph 5:27): “loved the church, and gave himself for it, that he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word; that he might present it to himself a glorious church” → “amó á la iglesia, y se entregó á sí mismo por ella … santificarla limpiándola en el lavacro del agua por la palabra … presentársela gloriosa para sí”
- flag: "whether this passage be cited or not" refers to the disputed 1John 5:7 (the three heavenly witnesses), which the RV1909 includes ("el Padre, el Verbo, y el Espíritu Santo"); "The Word" rendered "El Verbo" accordingly.
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909's "una iglesia" after "gloriosa para sí" is left out, since Henry's quotation ends at "a glorious church" and the RV1909 continues the clause.

### 3971 1John 5:9-12 The satisfaction the believer has about Christ, and eternal life through him.
- quoted (1John 5:10): “not the record that God gave of his Son” → “no ha creído en el testimonio que Dios ha testificado de su Hijo”

### 3972 1John 5:13-17 The assurance of God's hearing and answering prayer.
- quoted (1John 5:13): “believe on the name of the Son of God” → “en el nombre del Hijo de Dios”
- quoted (1Cor 15:58): “Always abounding in the work of the Lord” → “creciendo en la obra del Señor siempre”
- quoted (1Cor 15:58): “labour is not in vain in the Lord” → “trabajo en el Señor no es vano”
- flag: Henry says "We ought to pray for others, as well as for ourselves" twice in this section; both are translated.
- flag: quote 2: Henry turns 1Cor 15:58's "your labour" into "our labour" ("nuestro trabajo"); the quoted run begins after it.

### 3973 1John 5:18-21 The happy condition of true believers, and a charge to renounce all idolatry.
- quoted (1John 5:20): “know that the Son of God is come” → “que el Hijo de Dios es venido”
- quoted (1Pet 1:5): “kept by the power of God, through faith, unto” → “guardados en la virtud de Dios por fe, para alcanzar”
- quoted (1Pet 5:11): “be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen” → “sea gloria é imperio para siempre. Amén”

### 3974 2John 1:1-3 The apostle salutes the elect lady and her children.
- flag: source typo: "And old disciple is honourable" read as "An old disciple is honourable".
- flag: "for those already rich in grace, need continual forgiveness" is ungrammatical in the English; rendered as "even those already rich in grace need continual forgiveness".

### 3976 2John 1:7-11 Cautions them against deceivers.

- quoted (2John 1:9): “not in the doctrine of Christ” → “no persevera en la doctrina de Cristo”

### 3979 3John 1:9-12 Cautions him against siding with Diotrephes, who was a turbulent spirit; but recommends Demetrius as a man of excellent character.
- flag: "he that doeth good ... is born of God": Henry adds "born" to 3John 11 (RV1909 "El que hace bien es de Dios"); translated as his own words.

### 3980 3John 1:13-14 He hopes soon to see Gaius.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 3John 1:14 ("Peace be to you"), given in the RV1909's words "Paz sea contigo" (singular, as there).

### 3981 Jude 1:1-4 The apostle exhorts to stedfastness in the faith.
- flag: Henry's "common salvation" and "contend earnestly for the faith" echo Jude 3 (RV1909 "la común salud", "contendáis eficazmente por la fe"); kept as his own words ("común salvación", "contender ardientemente").

### 3982 Jude 1:5-7 The danger of being infected by false professors, and the dreadful punishment which shall be inflicted on them and their followers.
- quoted (Jude 1:6): “the judgment of the great day” → “el juicio del gran día”
- quoted (1Pet 2:11): “war against the soul” → “los deseos carnales que batallan contra el alma”
- quoted (Job 15:16): “drinketh iniquity like water” → “que bebe la iniquidad como agua”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ps 4:4 ("Stand in awe, and sin not"), given in the RV1909's words "temblad, y no pequéis" (KJV "stand in awe").

### 3983 Jude 1:8-16 An awful description of these seducers and their deplorable end.
- quoted (Rom 13:1): “the powers that be, are ordained of God” → “las que son, de Dios son ordenadas”
- quoted (Jude 1:9): “about the body of Moses” → “sobre el cuerpo de Moisés”
- quoted (Jude 1:13): “the blackness of darkness for ever” → “la oscuridad de las tinieblas”
- flag: unlisted quotations of Jude 12 ("Twice dead" -> "Dos veces muertos") and Jude 14 ("the Lord cometh" -> "el Señor es venido"), given in the RV1909's words.
- flag: the word "ungodly" Henry points to is "impíos" (with "impiedad", "impíamente") in the RV1909's Jude 15.
- flag: quote 0: "the powers that be" — the RV1909 has "las que son" with "potestades" earlier in Rom 13:1, so "en cuanto a las potestades" is added outside the quotation to carry the sense.
- flag: quote 2: the RV1909 puts "eternalmente" before "la oscuridad de las tinieblas"; Henry's "for ever" follows as his own "para siempre".

### 3984 Jude 1:17-23 Believers cautioned against being surprised at such deceivers arising among them.
- quoted (Jude 1:20): “praying in the Holy Ghost” → “orando por el Espíritu Santo”
- flag: Henry's first "pray in the Holy Ghost" is his own sentence ("oramos en el Espíritu Santo"); the second, the quotation, follows the RV1909's "orando por el Espíritu Santo".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jude 23 ("Others save with fear"), given in the RV1909's words "Haced salvos á los otros por temor".

### 3985 Jude 1:24-25 The epistle ends with an encouraging doxology, or words of praise.
- translated, not quoted (Jude 1:24; Henry changes Jude 24's "you" to "us"; the RV1909's "guardaros ... presentaros" becomes "guardarnos sin caída, y presentarnos".): “from falling, and to present”
- quoted (Jude 1:24): “faultless before the presence of his glory” → “delante de su gloria irreprensibles”
- quoted (Jude 1:24): “before the presence of his glory” → “delante de su gloria”
- quoted (Jude 1:25): “be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now” → “sea gloria y magnificencia, imperio y potencia, ahora”
- flag: quote 3: Henry's "both now and for ever" ends as his own "y para siempre"; the RV1909 has "ahora y en todos los siglos".
