# To review: es commentary batch 007

### 184 Gen 48:8-22 Jacob blesses Joseph's sons.
- quoted (Gen 48:9): “They are my sons whom God” → “Son mis hijos, que Dios me ha dado”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 48:11: 'Dios me ha hecho ver también tu simiente' (RV1909, Henry's 'God hath showed me thy seed').
- flag: Echoes of Gn 48:16 ('redeems from all evil'; RV1909 'me liberta de todo mal'), 1 Co 1:27 ('lo flaco del mundo') and 1 S 2:8 ('levanta del polvo al pobre') in Henry's own syntax.

### 186 Gen 49:3-7 Reuben, Simeon, Levi.
- flag: KJV-dependent: 'unstable as water' (Gn 49:4 KJV) is 'Corriente como las aguas' in the RV1909; Henry's point rests on 'unstable', so translated 'inestable como el agua'.
- flag: unlisted quotations of Gn 49:7, RV1909: 'Maldito su furor' (KJV 'Cursed be their anger') and 'yo los apartaré' (KJV 'I will divide them').
- flag: 'Our soul is our honour' follows Gn 49:6 (RV1909 'mi alma ... mi honra').

### 187 Gen 49:8-12 Judah.
- quoted (Isa 55:1): “wine and milk, without money and without price” → “sin dinero y sin precio, vino y leche”
- flag: 'Shiloh' (Gn 49:10, the Messianic title; RV1909 'Shiloh') written 'Siloh', not the glossary's 'Silo' (the town); please check.
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909: Jn 15:1 ('la vid verdadera') and Jn 6:55 ('verdadera bebida'); 'his brethren shall praise him' (Gn 49:8) translated.

### 188 Gen 49:13-18 Zebulun, Issachar, Dn
- quoted (Gen 49:18): “I have waited for thy salvation, O Lord” → “Tu salud esperé, oh Jehová”
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909: Gn 49:13 ('será para puerto de navíos') and Gn 49:15 ('que la tierra era deleitosa').
- flag: Source oddity: 'dwell at the heaven of the sea' is 'haven'; rendered 'junto al puerto del mar'.
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909 says 'salud' (old sense 'salvation'); Henry's following 'salvation' rendered 'salvación'. 'to whom the gathering of the people shall be' echoes Gn 49:10 (RV1909 'á él se congregarán los pueblos').
- flag: Title: source has 'Dn' (abbreviation slip for Dan).

### 189 Gen 49:19-21 Gad, Asher, Naphtali.
- quoted (Gen 49:21): “Naphtali, is a hind let loose” → “Nephtalí, sierva dejada”
- flag: Quote 0 keeps the RV1909's 'Nephtalí' and its 'sierva' (a hind); elsewhere 'Neftalí' per the glossary.
- flag: 'with goodly words' echoes Gn 49:21 (RV1909 'dichos hermosos'); translated.

### 190 Gen 49:22-27 Joseph and Benjamin.
- quoted (Gen 49:27): “shall ravin as a wolf” → “Benjamín, lobo arrebatador”
- flag: KJV-dependent: 'who was separated from his brethren' (Gn 49:26 KJV 'separate') is 'del Nazareo de sus hermanos' in the RV1909; Henry's quotation translated, his quotation marks kept.
- flag: Quote 0: Henry's 'He shall ravin as a wolf' (KJV) is the RV1909's verbless 'Benjamín, lobo arrebatador'.

### 191 Gen 49:28-33 Jacob's charge respecting his burial, His death.

### 193 Gen 50:7-14 His funeral.
- flag: 'a grievous mourning' (Gn 50:11 KJV) is 'Llanto grande' in the RV1909; translated in Henry's sentence.

### 194 Gen 50:15-21 Joseph's brethren crave his pardon, He comforts them.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 50:21: 'les habló al corazón' (RV1909, KJV 'spake kindly unto them').

### 195 Gen 50:22-26 Joseph's direction concerning his bones, His death.
- quoted (Ps 73:26): “My flesh and my heart faileth, but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever” → “Mi carne y mi corazón desfallecen: mas la roca de mi corazón y mi porción es Dios para siempre”
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909: He 11:22 ('dió mandamiento acerca de sus huesos') and Hch 20:24 ('acabe mi carrera con gozo', rendered 'acabar la carrera con gozo').
- flag: Source oddity: 'How happy to set our early' (a word missing, 'set out early'); rendered 'emprender temprano'.
- flag: Quote 0 (Sal 73:26): the RV1909 says 'la roca de mi corazón' where the KJV has 'the strength of my heart'.

### 197 Ex 1:8-14 The children of Israel increase in Egypt after the death of Joseph.
- translated, not quoted (Ex 1:10; The RV1909's 'se vaya de la tierra' is in Pharaoh's own words, singular and present subjunctive; Henry reports it in the plural past.): “get them up out of the land”
- quoted (Ex 1:11): “to afflict them with their burdens” → “que los molestasen con sus cargas”
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909: Is 2:22 ('Dejaos del hombre', Henry's 'Cease from man'), Ex 1:14 ('amargaron su vida'), Sal 2:1 ('piensan vanidad', rendered 'pensar vanidad') and He 6:10 ('el trabajo de amor').
- flag: Echoes of Gn 5:29 ('This same shall comfort us'; the RV1909 has 'Este nos aliviará'), Sal 132:14 ('Este es mi reposo', RV1909) and Pr 28:1 in Henry's own syntax.
- flag: The 'not unrighteous ... to forget our work' sentence keeps Henry's compressed syntax of He 6:10 (God is not unrighteous to forget).

### 199 Ex 2:1-4 Moses is born, and exposed on the river.

### 200 Ex 2:5-10 He is found, and brought up by Pharaoh's daughter.

### 201 Ex 2:11-15 Moses slays an Egyptian, and flees to Midian.

### 202 Ex 2:16-22 Moses marries the daughter of Jethro.

### 203 Ex 2:23-25 God hears the Israelites.
- quoted (Ex 2:25): “looked upon the children of Israel” → “Miró Dios á los hijos de Israel”
- quoted (Matt 11:28): “Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest” → “Venid á mí todos los que estáis trabajados y cargados, que yo os haré descansar”
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909: Ex 2:24 ('Oyó Dios el gemido de ellos') and Sal 46:1 ('nuestro pronto auxilio en las tribulaciones').
- flag: KJV-dependent: 'He had respect unto them' (Ex 2:25 KJV) is 'reconociólos Dios' in the RV1909; translated 'Los tuvo en cuenta'.

### 204 Ex 3:1-6 God appears to Moses in a burning bush.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 20:37; In the RV1909 Lc 20:37 opens with a subjunctive clause ('Y que los muertos hayan de resucitar'); after Henry's 'prueba que' the indicative is needed.): “the dead are raised”
- quoted (Ex 3:6): “afraid to look upon God” → “miedo de mirar á Dios”
- flag: 'Jeshurun' as in the RV1909 (Dt 33:5).
- flag: 'Moisés cubrió su rostro' is also the RV1909's wording of Ex 3:6.

### 205 Ex 3:7-10 God sends Moses to deliver Israel.
- flag: Source oddity: the reference '(Ex 3:11-15)' at the end points to the next section; kept as in the English.

### 206 Ex 3:11-15 The name Jehovah.
- quoted (Ex 3:12): “Certainly I will be with thee” → “yo seré contigo”
- quoted (Ex 3:14): “I AM hath sent me unto you” → “YO SOY me ha enviado á vosotros”
- quoted (Ex 3:15): “The Lord God of your fathers” → “Jehová, el Dios de vuestros padres … me ha enviado á vosotros”

- flag: Quote 0: Henry's 'Certainly' kept outside the RV1909's 'yo seré contigo' (Ex 3:12).
