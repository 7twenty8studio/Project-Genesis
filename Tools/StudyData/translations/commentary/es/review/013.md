# To review: es commentary batch 013

### 400 Num 3:40-51 
- quoted (Acts 15:18): “Known unto God are all his works” → “Conocidas son á Dios desde el siglo todas sus obras”
- flag: Hch 15:18: the RV1909's 'desde el siglo' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'from the beginning of the world', which he gives as 'beforehand'.
- flag: Echoes of He 12:23 ('the church of the first-born'; RV1909 'la congregación de los primogénitos') and 1 P 1:18-19 ('la sangre preciosa') translated in Henry's syntax.

### 402 Num 4:4-20 The duties of the Kohathites.
- flag: Echo of He 4:16 ('Lleguémonos pues confiadamente al trono de la gracia', RV1909) in Henry's syntax.

### 403 Num 4:21-33 The duties of the Gershonites and Merarites.
- flag: Echoes of Flp 3:21 (RV1909 'el cuerpo de nuestra bajeza… el cuerpo de su gloria') and 1 Ts 4:17 ('así estaremos siempre con el Señor') translated in Henry's syntax.

### 405 Num 5:1-10 The unclean to be removed out of the camp, Restitution to be made for trespasses.
- quoted (James 3:17): “from above is first pure, then peaceable” → “la sabiduría que es de lo alto, primeramente es pura, después pacífica”
- quoted (Num 5:6): “a trespass against the Lord” → “prevaricación contra Jehová”

### 406 Num 5:11-31 The trial of jealousy.
- quoted (2Sam 2:26): “will be bitterness in the latter end” → “al cabo se sigue amargura”
- quoted (Rom 2:16): “judge the secrets of men” → “juzgará el Señor lo encubierto de los hombres”
- flag: unlisted quotation of He 13:4 ('á los fornicarios y á los adúlteros juzgará Dios', RV1909).
- flag: Ro 2:16: the RV1909's 'el Señor' (KJV 'God') kept inside the quotation.
- flag: Source lacks the period after '2'; added. Echo of Jer 2:19 ('an evil and a bitter thing') translated.

### 407 Num 6:1-21 The law concerning the Nazarites.
- translated, not quoted (Judg 13:14; Henry gives the Nazarite rule of Nm 6:3 in the plural ('They must not drink…'); the listed Jue 13:14 is said of Samson's mother in the singular ('no beberá vino ni sidra').): “drink wine or strong drink, nor eat”
- flag: Henry writes 'Nazarite(s)'; rendered 'nazareo(s)' (the RV1909 has 'nazareo' / 'nazareato', Nm 6:2-5).
- flag: Source typos: 'fleshy lusts' (fleshly) and a comma for a period before 'Let us not forget'.
- flag: 'holy to the Lord' (Nm 6:8, RV1909 'santo á Jehová') put in the plural as Henry does.

### 408 Num 6:22-27 The form of blessing the people.
- quoted (2Sam 6:18): “the people in the name of the Lord” → “al pueblo en el nombre de Jehová”
- translated, not quoted (Rom 16:20; Henry cites 2 Co 13:14, whose RV1909 words ('la gracia del Señor Jesucristo') are used; the listed Ro 16:20 reads 'del Señor nuestro Jesucristo'.): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ”
- quoted (2Cor 13:14): “the communion of the Holy Ghost” → “la participación del Espíritu Santo”
- flag: 2 Co 13:14: the RV1909 has 'el amor de Dios' where Henry says 'the love of the Father'; Henry's wording kept.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 2 Co 13:14 ('la gracia del Señor Jesucristo', RV1909), answered as quote 1.

### 409 Num 7:1-9 The offerings of the princes at the dedication of the tabernacle.

### 410 Num 7:10-89 The offerings of the princes at the dedication of the altar.

### 411 Num 8:1-4 The lamps of the sanctuary.
- quoted (2Pet 1:19): “in a dark place” → “una antorcha que alumbra en lugar oscuro”
- flag: 2 P 1:19: the RV1909's 'antorcha que alumbra' for Henry's 'light shining'.

### 412 Num 8:5-26 Consecration of the Levites, and their service.
- translated, not quoted (Isa 52:11; Henry turns Is 52:11's command ('limpiaos los que lleváis los vasos de Jehová') into a third-person statement; translated with the RV1909's 'los vasos de Jehová'.): “clean that bear the vessels of the Lord”
- quoted (Num 8:7): “water of purifying upon them” → “sobre ellos el agua de la expiación”
- quoted (Num 8:15): “to do the service of the tabernacle” → “á ministrar en el tabernáculo del testimonio”
- flag: Nm 8:7: the RV1909 says 'el agua de la expiación' where the KJV has 'water of purifying'.

### 413 Num 9:1-14 Of the Passover.
- quoted (Gal 6:7): “Be not deceived: God is not mocked” → “No os engañéis: Dios no puede ser burlado”
- flag: 'a sabbath or a sacrament' rendered 'un día del Señor o de un sacramento' (the Christian sabbath, per the glossary rule).
- flag: Gá 6:7: the RV1909's 'no puede ser burlado' for 'is not mocked'.

### 414 Num 9:15-23 The removals of the Israelites.
- quoted (Num 9:18): “As long as the cloud” → “Todos los días que la nube estaba sobre el tabernáculo”
- quoted (Num 9:17): “When the cloud was taken up” → “Según que se alzaba la nube”
- quoted (Num 9:18): “At the commandment of the Lord” → “Al mandato de Jehová”
- quoted (James 1:21): “word, which is able to save your souls” → “recibid con mansedumbre la palabra ingerida, la cual puede hacer salvas vuestras almas”
- flag: Stg 1:21 kept in the RV1909's plural ('recibid… vuestras almas'), as Henry's KJV has 'your souls' after his singular 'Lay the BIBLE to heart'.
- flag: 'the earthly house of this tabernacle' (2 Co 5:1 KJV) translated; the RV1909 reads 'la casa terrestre de nuestra habitación'.
- flag: Pr 3:6 echoed in Henry's syntax with the RV1909's 'enderezará… veredas'.

### 416 Num 10:11-28 The Israelites remove from Sinai to Paran.
- quoted (Num 10:13): “took their journey according to the commandment of the Lord” → “al dicho de Jehová”
- quoted (Num 10:12): “out of the wilderness of Sinai” → “del desierto de Sinaí”
- quoted (Num 10:12): “rested in the wilderness of Paran” → “en el desierto de Parán”
- flag: Nm 10:12: the RV1909 says the cloud rested ('paró la nube'); Henry's 'they… rested' kept, with only 'en el desierto de Parán' quoted.

### 418 Num 10:33-36 The blessing pronounced by Moses.
- quoted (Num 10:35): “when the ark set forward” → “en moviendo el arca”
- quoted (Num 10:35): “let thine enemies be scattered” → “y sean disipados tus enemigos”
- quoted (Deut 33:29): “Happy art thou, O Israel! who is like unto thee, O people” → “Bienaventurado tú, oh Israel, ¿quién como tú, pueblo”
- flag: Nm 10:35: Henry's quotation omits 'Jehová'; kept as he gives it ('Levántate, y sean disipados tus enemigos').
- flag: Dt 33:29: Henry's exclamations ('Happy art thou, O Israel! … O people!') follow the RV1909's punctuation, the verse cut after 'pueblo'.
- flag: 'that God would cause his people to rest' paraphrases Nm 10:36 (RV1909 'Vuelve, Jehová, á los millares de millares de Israel'); translated.

### 419 Num 11:1-3 The burning at Taberah.
- flag: 'Taberah' rendered 'Taberá' (RV1909 'Taberah', Nm 11:3, modernized); not in the batch glossary for Spanish.

### 420 Num 11:4-9 The people lust for flesh, and loathe the manna.

### 421 Num 11:10-15 Moses complains of his charge.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mt 6:13 ('no nos metas en tentación', RV1909).

### 422 Num 11:16-23 Elders appointed to divide the charge. Flesh meat promised.
- quoted (Num 11:23): “whether my word shall come to pass” → “ahora verás si te sucede mi dicho, ó no”
- flag: Source numbers the points 'I.' and '2.'; kept as given.
- flag: Ro 4:18 ('against hope to believe in hope') given in the RV1909's words, 'en esperanza contra esperanza' (unlisted).

### 423 Num 11:24-30 The Spirit rests on the elders.
- quoted (Num 11:26): “went not out unto the tabernacle” → “no habían salido al tabernáculo”
- quoted (Num 11:26, Num 11:27): “in the camp, and there” → “en el campo”
- quoted (Num 11:29): “all the Lord's people were prophets” → “todo el pueblo de Jehová fuesen profetas”
- quoted (Num 11:29): “would put his Spirit upon” → “que Jehová pusiera su espíritu sobre”
- flag: Nm 11:29: the RV1909 writes 'espíritu' in lower case and 'Jehová' for Henry's 'he'; kept verbatim.
- flag: unlisted quotations of 2 P 1:21 ('siendo inspirados del Espíritu Santo') and Jn 3:8 ('de donde quiere'), RV1909; Lc 14:11 echoed and translated.

### 424 Num 11:31-35 Quails are given.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jn 6:27 ('la comida que perece… la comida que á vida eterna permanece', RV1909).

### 425 Num 12:1-9 God rebukes the murmuring of Aaron and Miriam.
- quoted (Matt 11:11, Matt 11:12): “he that is least in the kingdom of heaven, is greater than he” → “el que es muy más pequeño en el reino de los cielos, mayor es que él”
- quoted (2Pet 2:10): “are not afraid to speak evil of dignities” → “no temen decir mal de las potestades superiores”
- flag: 2 P 2:10: the RV1909 has 'las potestades superiores' where the KJV has 'dignities'.
- flag: 'Miriam' rendered 'María', as the RV1909 and the glossary have her.

### 426 Num 12:10-16 Miriam struck with leprosy, and healed at the prayer of Moses.

### 427 Num 13:1-20 Twelve men sent to search the land of Canaan, Their instructions.
- flag: 'Be of good courage' (Nm 13:20) given in the RV1909's word, 'Esforzaos' (unlisted).
- flag: 'We walk by sight not by faith' inverts 2 Co 5:7 on purpose; translated.

### 429 Num 13:26-33 Their account of the land.

### 430 Num 14:1-4 The people murmur at the account of the spies.

### 431 Num 14:5-10 Joshua and Caleb labour to still the people.
- flag: Nm 14:9 ('their defence is departed from them') given with the RV1909's 'su amparo se ha apartado de ellos', in Henry's past tense ('se había apartado').

### 433 Num 14:20-35 The murmurers forbidden to enter the promised land.
- translated, not quoted (Num 14:34; Henry explains the KJV's 'my breach of promise' (its causes and consequences); the RV1909's 'conoceréis mi castigo' would lose his point.): “Ye shall know my breach of promise”
- quoted (Num 14:31): “said should be a prey, them will I bring” → “dijisteis que serían por presa, yo los introduciré”
- flag: Nm 14:34: Henry's comment rests on the KJV's 'breach of promise'; the RV1909 reads 'conoceréis mi castigo'.
- flag: 'loving kindness' rendered 'misericordia'.

### 434 Num 14:36-39 Death of the evil spies.
- quoted (Num 14:36): “a slander upon the land” → “desacreditando aquel país”
- flag: 'murmurers made mourners' rendered 'los murmuradores son hechos dolientes'; the English wordplay is lost.
