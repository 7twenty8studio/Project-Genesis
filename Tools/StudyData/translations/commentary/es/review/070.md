# To review: es commentary batch 070

### 3068 Matt 20:20-28 The ambition of James and John.
- flag: 'cup' (Mt 20:22): the RV1909 has 'vaso'; Henry's 'cup' rendered 'copa' throughout his explanation.
- flag: 'a cup of trembling' (Is 51:17, 22): RV1909 'cáliz de aturdimiento'; Henry's phrase translated 'copa de temblor'.
- flag: 'for unto us it is given' (Fil 1:29): given with the RV1909's 'á vosotros es concedido' (unlisted quotation of Phil 1:29; the RV1909's person, where Henry has 'us').
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 20:28 ('rescate por muchos'), RV1909 words.

### 3069 Matt 20:29-34 Jesus gives sight to two blind men near Jericho.
- quoted (2Pet 3:18): “grow in grace and in the knowledge” → “en la gracia y conocimiento de nuestro Señor y Salvador Jesucristo”
- flag: quote 0: 'grow in grace and in the knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ' given as 'crecer' + the RV1909's run 'en la gracia y conocimiento de nuestro Señor y Salvador Jesucristo' (its 'creced' is an imperative); the RV1909 has 'nuestro Señor' where the KJV has 'our Lord'.

### 3070 Matt 21:1-11 Christ enters Jerusalem.
- quoted (Matt 21:9): “Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord” → “¡Bendito el que viene en el nombre del Señor!”
- quoted (Matt 21:10): “was come into Jerusalem all the city was moved” → “entrando él en Jerusalem, toda la ciudad se alborotó”
- flag: The English is garbled here: the heading of a later section ('The barren fig-tree cursed. (18-22)') stands at the start of this section's text; translated and kept where it is.
- flag: Henry plays on 'moved' ('all the city was moved; some… were moved with joy… moved with envy'); the RV1909 has 'se alborotó', so the play is lost: 'se conmovieron' for the rest.
- flag: 'Jerusalem' kept as the RV1909 spells it inside quote 1; 'Jerusalén' elsewhere. 'the Consolation of Israel' rendered 'la consolación de Israel' after Lk 2:25 (RV1909).
- flag: 'Crucify him' rendered 'Crucifícale' (the RV1909 has 'Sea crucificado' in Mt 27:22-23); not a quotation.

### 3071 Matt 21:12-17 He drives out those who profaned the temple.
- flag: 'the glory of the latter house… greater than the glory of the former' after Hag 2:9 (RV1909 'La gloria de aquesta casa postrera será mayor que la de la primera'); rendered after it, not marked as a quotation.
- flag: 'den of thieves' / 'house of prayer' rendered with the RV1909's words in Mt 21:13 ('cueva de ladrones', 'casa de oración').

### 3072 Matt 21:18-22 
- flag: This section has no title in the English.
- flag: sensitive: 'This represents the state of the nation and people of the Jews… nothing but leaves… blindness and hardness grew upon them, till they were undone, and their place and nation rooted up. The Lord was righteous in it.'

### 3074 Matt 21:28-32 The parable of the two sons.
- flag: 'more plausible in their disobedience' read as 'more specious, of better appearance' ('más aparentes').

### 3075 Matt 21:33-46 The parable of the wicked husbandmen.
- flag: sensitive: 'This parable plainly sets forth the sin and ruin of the Jewish nation' and 'he who was rejected by the Jews, was embraced by the Gentiles'.
- flag: 'restraining the remainders of wrath… making that which breaks out redound to his praise' alludes to Sal 76:10; translated as Henry words it.

### 3076 Matt 22:1-14 The parable of the marriage feast.
- quoted (Matt 22:11): “had not on a wedding-garment” → “no vestido de boda”
- flag: sensitive: 'The guests first invited were the Jews' and 'The utter ruin coming upon the Jewish church and nation, is here represented'.
- flag: 'Take him away' (Mt 22:13; RV1909 'tomadle') rendered 'Llevadle'; 'They were careless' (Mt 22:5) rendered with the RV1909's 'No se cuidaron' (unlisted quotation of Matt 22:5); 'utter darkness' rendered 'las tinieblas de afuera' after Mt 22:13.
- flag: 'There is enough and to spare' rendered 'Hay de sobra, y aún sobra' (Henry's emphatic phrase).
- flag: 'Making light of Christ' after Mt 22:5 (KJV 'made light of it'); the RV1909 has 'no se cuidaron', so 'Tener en poco' is a translation of Henry.

### 3077 Matt 22:15-22 The Pharisees question Jesus as to the tribute.
- quoted (Matt 22:16): “their disciples with the Herodians” → “los discípulos de ellos, con los Herodianos”
- flag: 'his kingdom is not of this world' after Jn 18:36 (RV1909 'Mi reino no es de este mundo'); fitted as 'su reino no es de este mundo', not marked as a quotation.

### 3078 Matt 22:23-33 The question of the Sadducees as to the resurrection.
- flag: English typo 'reveled' read as 'revealed' ('reveladas').
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Cor 15:20 ('primicias de los que durmieron'), RV1909 words.

### 3079 Matt 22:34-40 The substance of the commandments.
- quoted (Matt 22:38, Matt 22:39): “is the first and great commandment” → “el primero y el grande mandamiento”
- flag: 'not in word and tongue only' after 1 Jn 3:18 (RV1909 'no amemos de palabra ni de lengua'); rendered 'no de palabra ni de lengua solamente', not marked as a quotation.

### 3080 Matt 22:41-46 Jesus questions the Pharisees.
- flag: Henry's quoted '"What think we of Christ?"' changes the person of Mt 22:42 (RV1909 '¿Qué os parece del Cristo?'); rendered '¿Qué nos parece del Cristo?' after it, keeping his 'we'.
- flag: 'God manifested in the flesh' after 1 Ti 3:16 (RV1909 'Dios ha sido manifestado en carne'); rendered 'Dios manifestado en carne'.

### 3081 Matt 23:1-12 Jesus reproves the scribes and Pharisees.
- quoted (Gal 6:6): “him that is taught in the word” → “el que es enseñado en la palabra”
- flag: 'four paragraphs of the law' rendered 'cuatro pasajes de la ley'; 'phylacteries' 'filacterias' and 'fringes' 'flecos', as in the RV1909's Mt 23:5.

### 3082 Matt 23:13-33 Crimes of the Pharisees.
- translated, not quoted (Matt 5:20; The RV1909's run is 'la de los escribas y de los Fariseos', which depends on 'vuestra justicia' in Mt 5:20; Henry's own sentence about their righteousness needs the noun, so it is translated): “The righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees”
- flag: 'strain… a gnat… swallowing a camel' rendered with the RV1909's words in Mt 23:24 ('colar el mosquito', 'tragar el camello').
- flag: 'Gain being their godliness' alludes to 1 Ti 6:5; translated as Henry words it.

### 3083 Matt 23:34-39 The guilt of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Matt 23:37): “her chickens under her wings” → “sus pollos debajo de las alas”
- flag: sensitive: 'The present dispersion and unbelief of the Jews… Jerusalem and her children had a large share of guilt, and their punishment has been signal.'
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909 has 'debajo de las alas' where Henry (KJV) has 'under her wings'.

### 3084 Matt 24:1-3 Christ foretells the destruction of the temple.
- quoted (Matt 24:2): “See ye not all these things” → “¿Veis todo esto?”

### 3085 Matt 24:4-28 The troubles before the destruction of Jerusalem.
- quoted (2Thess 2:1): “the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “la venida de nuestro Señor Jesucristo”
- flag: sensitive: 'From the time that the Jews rejected Christ… the sword never departed from them' and 'the ruin coming upon the people of the Jews'; also 'When a people, by their sin, make themselves as loathsome carcasses…'.
- flag: English tense slip 'When we looked forward' read as 'When we look forward' ('Cuando miramos adelante').
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909 words: Matt 24:8 ('principio de dolores'), Matt 24:22 ('por causa de los escogidos aquellos días'), 2 Pet 1:10 ('hacer firme nuestra vocación y elección', fitted to 'nuestra').
- flag: 'the sabbath day' here is the Jewish sabbath of Mt 24:20 applied generally: 'el día de sábado' (no Christian-sabbath note).

### 3086 Matt 24:29-41 Christ foretells other signs and miseries, to the end of the world.
- quoted (Matt 24:30): “they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds” → “verán al Hijo del hombre que vendrá sobre las nubes”
- quoted (Matt 24:39): “knew not, until the flood came” → “no conocieron hasta que vino el diluvio”
- flag: sensitive: 'the Jews should never cease to be a distinct people… Judah shall never cease to exist as a distinct people' and the judgment 'hastening upon the nation and people of the Jews'.
- flag: Judah: Henry means the Jewish people; rendered 'Judá' (glossary).
- flag: unlisted quotation of Luke 2:34 ('señal á la que será contradicho', fitted as 'por señal a la que sería contradicho'), RV1909 wording in Henry's tense; 'those who sow in those tears shall shortly reap in joy' after Sal 126:5 ('sembraron con lágrimas, con regocijo segarán'), fitted, not marked.
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909 has 'que vendrá sobre las nubes' where Henry (KJV) has 'coming in the clouds'.

### 3087 Matt 24:42-51 Exhortations to watchfulness.
- quoted (Col 1:12): “to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light” → “aptos para participar de la suerte de los santos en luz”
- flag: quote 0: 'inheritance' is 'suerte' in the RV1909 (Col 1:12); the run includes its 'aptos para', which renders Henry's 'fit to be'.

### 3088 Matt 25:1-13 The parable of the ten virgins.
- quoted (Matt 25:5): “They all slumbered and slept” → “cabecearon todas, y se durmieron”
- quoted (Ps 23:4): “the valley of the shadow of death” → “valle de sombra de muerte”
- quoted (Prov 23:17): “in the fear of the Lord all the day long” → “en el temor de Jehová todo tiempo”
- flag: 'foolish' virgins rendered 'fatuas', the RV1909's word in Mt 25:2.
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909 words: Matt 25:6 ('salid á recibirle'), Matt 25:10 ('se cerró la puerta'), Matt 25:13 ('Velad, pues').
- flag: 'Those who care not to live the life, yet would die the death of the righteous' alludes to Nm 23:10; translated.
- flag: 'undone… undone for ever': Henry's wordplay rendered 'queda sin hacer… queda perdido para siempre'.

### 3089 Matt 25:14-30 The parable of the talents.
- quoted (1Cor 12:7): “The manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal” → “le es dada manifestación del Espíritu para provecho”
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909's 'á cada uno' written 'A cada uno' (modern spelling at the head of the sentence), so the answered run starts at 'le es dada'.
- flag: 'the love of Christ constrains him… to Him that died for him, and rose again' after 2 Co 5:14-15; translated as Henry words it.
- flag: 'the things that belong to their peace hid from their eyes' after Lc 19:42 (RV1909 'lo que toca á tu paz… está encubierto de tus ojos'); rendered after it.

### 3090 Matt 25:31-46 The judgment.
- quoted (Matt 25:34): “from the foundation of the world” → “desde la fundación del mundo”
- flag: 'saints and sinners, holy and unholy' gives 'santos… santos' in Spanish ('santos y pecadores, santos e impíos'); a reviewer may prefer 'santos y profanos' for the second pair.
- flag: 'life and death, good and evil, the blessing and the curse' after Dt 30:15, 19; translated in Henry's order.

### 3092 Matt 26:6-13 Christ anointed at Bethany.
- flag: Henry identifies the woman of Mt 26:7 as Mary (of Bethany, Jn 12:3); kept.

### 3093 Matt 26:14-16 Judas bargains to betray Christ.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Tim 6:10 ('el amor del dinero', 'la raíz de todos los males'), RV1909 words; Henry's 'all evil' is 'todos los males' in the RV1909.
- flag: 'What did Judas want?' read as 'lack' ('¿Qué le faltaba a Judas?').

### 3094 Matt 26:17-25 The Passover.
- quoted (Matt 26:19): “The disciples did as Jesus had appointed” → “los discípulos hicieron como Jesús les mandó”
- flag: quote 0: 'had appointed' is 'les mandó' in the RV1909.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Cor 5:7 ('nuestra pascua, que es Cristo, fué sacrificada por nosotros'), RV1909 words; 'not to be high-minded, but to fear' after Rom 11:20 ('No te ensoberbezcas, antes teme'), fitted.
