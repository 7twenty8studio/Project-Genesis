- flag: "the second death" here follows the RV1909's Rev 20:6 "la segunda muerte" (Rev 20:14 has "la muerte segunda").

### 4041 Rev 20:11-15 The last and general resurrection.
- quoted (Rev 20:12): “the dead, small and great” → “los muertos, grandes y pequeños”
- quoted (Rev 20:14): “This is the second death” → “Esta es la muerte segunda”
- flag: quote 0: Henry's "small and great" (KJV order) is "grandes y pequeños" in the RV1909's Rev 20:12; the RV1909's order is used.
- flag: "Who shall dwell with devouring flames?" blends Isa 33:14 (RV1909 "¿quién de nosotros habitará con las llamas eternas?"); Henry's own wording is translated.
