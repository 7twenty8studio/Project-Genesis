# To review: es commentary batch 043

### 1635 Ps 107:17-22 In sickness.
- flag: 'He sends his word, and heals' echoes Sal 107:20 (RV1909 'Envió su palabra, y curólos'); Henry's present tense, translated.

### 1639 Ps 109:6-20 He prophesies their destruction.
- flag: 'Go, ye cursed' (Henry's own quotation marks) is his short form of Mt 25:41 (KJV 'Depart from me, ye cursed'; RV1909 'Apartaos de mí, malditos'); translated as he wrote it, marks kept.

### 1640 Ps 109:21-31 Prayers and praises.
- quoted (1Pet 4:19): “suffer according to the will of God, commit the keeping of their souls to him” → “los que son afligidos según la voluntad de Dios, encomiéndenle sus almas”
- flag: Source has a stray hyphen in 'thy-mercy'; translated 'tu misericordia'.
- flag: 1 P 4:19: Henry's 'commit the keeping of their souls to him' rendered by the RV1909's 'encomiéndenle sus almas'.

### 1641 Ps 110:1-7 Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 110:7): “he shall drink of the brook in the way” → “del arroyo beberá en el camino”
- flag: Source reads 'the power of the world' (evidently 'word'); translated 'el poder de la palabra'.
- flag: Source typo 'the people of Christs'; translated 'el pueblo de Cristo'.
- flag: Melchizedek written 'Melquisedec' (RV1909 Sal 110:4 'Melchîsedech'); not in the glossary.
- flag: 'the power of God unto salvation' (Ro 1:16; RV1909 'potencia de Dios para salud') translated with 'salvación' in Henry's question.

### 1642 Ps 111:1-10 The Lord is to be praised for his works.
- quoted (Ps 111:2): “the works of the Lord” → “las obras de Jehová”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 111:2; Henry's own sentence about the works as one ('All the works of the Lord are spoken of as one'), not v. 2's 'are great'.): “the works of the Lord are”
- quoted (Ps 111:5, Ps 111:6): “He will ever be mindful of his covenant; he” → “para siempre se acordará de su pacto”
- quoted (Ps 111:9): “He sent redemption unto his people” → “Redención ha enviado á su pueblo”
- flag: Sal 111:7: Henry glosses the KJV's 'verity, or truth' and 'judgment, or righteous'; the RV1909 has 'verdad y juicio', so 'verity, or truth' is rendered once as 'verdad'.
- flag: 'of a good understanding' echoes Sal 111:10 (RV1909 'buen entendimiento').

### 1643 Ps 112:1-10 The blessedness of the righteous.
- quoted (Ps 112:10): “The desire of the wicked shall perish” → “Perecerá el deseo de los impíos”
- flag: 'everlasting remembrance' (Sal 112:6) rendered with the RV1909's 'memoria eterna'; 'evil tidings' (Sal 112:7, RV1909 'mala fama') translated 'malas noticias'.

### 1644 Ps 113:1-9 An exhortation to praise God.
- quoted (Ps 113:3): “the Lord's name is to be praised” → “sea alabado el nombre de Jehová”
- quoted (Rev 15:4): “all nations shall come and worship before” → “todas las naciones vendrán, y adorarán delante de”
- quoted (Neh 9:5): “is exalted above all blessing and praise” → “alto sobre toda bendición y alabanza”
- quoted (Ps 113:5): “Who is like unto the Lord our God” → “¿Quién como Jehová nuestro Dios”
- flag: Ap 15:4: the RV1909 has 'delante de ti'; Henry's 'before him' kept as 'delante de él' after the quoted run.
- flag: 'seek and save those that were lost' echoes Lc 19:10 (RV1909 'buscar y á salvar lo que se había perdido'); translated.

### 1645 Ps 114:1-8 An exhortation to fear God.
- quoted (Ps 114:7): “At the presence of the Lord” → “A la presencia del Señor”
- flag: 'that rock was Christ' echoes 1 Co 10:4 (RV1909 'la piedra era Cristo'); translated with 'roca' to keep Henry's Rock imagery.
- flag: 'What ailed…' echoes the KJV of Sal 114:5 ('What ailed thee, O thou sea'; RV1909 '¿Qué tuviste, oh mar…?'), rendered '¿Qué tuvieron…?'.

### 1646 Ps 115:1-8 Glory to be ascribed to God.
- flag: Source begins with a fragment of the title, in lower case ('by trusting in him and praising him.'), then '(9-18)'; kept and translated, paragraphs as given.
- flag: Source typo 'they mercy and truth' read as 'thy'; translated 'tu misericordia y tu verdad'.
- flag: sensitive: 'The heathen gods are senseless things' (idols of the nations), translated 'Los dioses de los paganos'.
- flag: 'works of men's hands' rendered with the RV1909's words of Sal 115:4 ('obra de manos de hombres').

### 1648 Ps 116:1-9 The psalmist declares his love to the Lord.
- flag: 'It is of his mercies that we are not consumed' echoes Lm 3:22 (RV1909 'Es por la misericordia de Jehová que no somos consumidos'); Henry's 'his', translated.
- flag: 'labour and are heavy laden… rest to their souls' adapts Mt 11:28-29 to the third person (RV1909 'trabajados y cargados', 'descanso para vuestras almas'); translated.

### 1649 Ps 116:10-19 His desire to be thankful.
- quoted (Ps 116:13): “I will take the cup of salvation” → “Tomaré la copa de la salud”
- quoted (Ps 116:16): “truly I am thy servant” → “yo soy tu siervo”
- quoted (Ps 116:16): “the son of thine handmaid” → “hijo de tu sierva”
- quoted (Ps 116:16): “thou hast loosed my bonds” → “rompiste mis prisiones”
- flag: Sal 116:13: the RV1909's 'la copa de la salud' (salud = salvation) is used, and Henry's later 'cup of salvation' / 'spiritual health' kept as 'copa de salud' / 'salud espiritual' to keep his wordplay.
- flag: Sal 116:16: Henry's 'loosed my bonds' rendered with the RV1909's 'rompiste mis prisiones', and 'The bonds thou hast loosed' as 'Las prisiones que rompiste'.

### 1651 Ps 118:1-18 It is good to trust in the Lord.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 53:10 (RV1909 'la voluntad de Jehová será en su mano prosperada'); also its 'quebrantarlo, sujetándole á padecimiento' echoed in Henry's 'bruised him, and put him to grief', translated.
- flag: 'what man can do unto us' echoes Sal 118:6 (RV1909 'lo que me pueda hacer el hombre'); 'by his stripes' Is 53:5 ('por su llaga'); both translated in Henry's person.

### 1652 Ps 118:19-29 The coming of Christ in his kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 118:22): “the Stone which the builders refused” → “Piedra que desecharon los edificadores”
- quoted (Ps 118:24): “We will rejoice and be glad” → “Nos gozaremos y alegraremos”
- flag: Source reads 'The duty which the Lord has made, brings light with it' (evidently 'The day', Sal 118:24, 27); translated 'El día que hizo el Señor'.
- flag: 'the Lord's day' and 'Sabbath days' here mean the Christian Sunday (Christ's resurrection); kept as 'el día del Señor' / 'Los días de sábado' as Henry wrote them, without the bracketed note (he doesn't say 'Christian sabbath').
- flag: 'glad tidings of great joy to all people' echoes Lc 2:10-11 (RV1909 'nuevas de gran gozo, que será para todo el pueblo', 'CRISTO el Señor'); translated.

### 1653 Ps 119:1-8 
- flag: Source garbled and repeated: 'The tempter would make men think they are at them out of those ways.' (a broken line) stands before the full sentence; both translated, the broken one word for word.
- flag: Source typo 'the temper will be too hard for them' read as 'tempter'; translated 'el tentador'.
- flag: Henry's ten words follow the KJV's terms; the RV1909 in Sal 119 uses ley, caminos, testimonios, mandamientos, palabra/dichos, juicios, justicia, estatutos, verdad, sometimes for different Hebrew words.

### 1654 Ps 119:9-16 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ef 3:8 (RV1909 'las inescrutables riquezas de Cristo').

### 1656 Ps 119:25-32 
- flag: 'The way of lying' rendered 'El camino de mentira', the RV1909's words in Sal 119:29.

### 1657 Ps 119:33-40 
- quoted (James 4:4): “the friendship of the world is enmity with God” → “la amistad del mundo es enemistad con Dios”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sal 119:37 (RV1909 'avívame en tu camino').
- flag: 'causing us to walk in his statutes' echoes Ez 36:27 (RV1909 'haré que andéis en mis mandamientos'); translated.

### 1659 Ps 119:49-56 
- flag: 'absent from the body, and present with the Lord' echoes 2 Co 5:8 (RV1909 'partir del cuerpo, y estar presentes al Señor'); translated.

### 1661 Ps 119:65-72 
- flag: Source typo 'percepts' read as 'precepts'; translated 'preceptos'.

### 1663 Ps 119:81-88 
- flag: 'a leathern bottle… in the smoke' (Sal 119:83) rendered with the RV1909's 'odre' ('como el odre al humo').

### 1664 Ps 119:89-96 
- flag: Source typo 'which one sees and end of' read as 'an end of' (Sal 119:96); translated.
- flag: 'The Lord will keep the man in peace, whose mind is stayed on him' echoes Is 26:3 (RV1909 'cuyo pensamiento en ti persevera'), adapted by Henry to 'him'; translated.
- flag: 'the flower of the grass' echoes 1 P 1:24 (RV1909 'como la flor de la hierba').
