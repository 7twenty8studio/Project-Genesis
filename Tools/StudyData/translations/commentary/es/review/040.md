# To review: es commentary batch 040

### 1518 Ps 59:8-17 He foresees their destruction.
- flag: Sal 59:9-10, 16 echoed in Henry's own syntax ('mi defensa', 'el Dios de mi misericordia', 'refugio en el día de mi angustia', RV1909); translated in his plural.

### 1520 Ps 60:6-12 He entreats God to carry on and complete their victories.
- flag: 'the strong city' given with the RV1909's 'ciudad fortalecida' (Sal 60:9).

### 1521 Ps 61:1-4 David seeks God upon former experience.
- quoted (Ps 61:2): “rock that is higher than” → “la peña más alta que”
- flag: Sal 61:2: the RV1909's 'peña' kept in the quotation and in 'Esta peña es Cristo'; the glossary's 'Roca' used for Henry's capitalized 'Rock'.

### 1522 Ps 61:5-8 He vows to serve God.
- quoted (Luke 1:32, Luke 1:33): “shall give unto him the throne of his father David” → “le dará el Señor Dios el trono de David su padre”
- quoted (Luke 1:33): “of his kingdom there shall be no end” → “de su reino no habrá fin”
- flag: Lc 1:32-33: Henry's 'the Lord' given with the RV1909's 'el Señor Dios'. Jn 1:17 ('la gracia y la verdad por Jesucristo') echoed.

### 1523 Ps 62:1-7 David's confidence in God.
- quoted (Ps 62:2): “my Rock and my salvation” → “mi fuerte, y mi salud”
- flag: Sal 62:2: Henry's 'my Rock and my salvation' given with the RV1909's 'mi fuerte, y mi salud' (salud = salvation), so the word 'Rock' is not seen here.

### 1524 Ps 62:8-12 No trust to be put in worldly things.
- quoted (Ps 62:8): “trust in him at all times” → “en él en todo tiempo”
- flag: Sal 62:8: the RV1909 has 'Esperad en él en todo tiempo'; given as 'esperar en él en todo tiempo' in Henry's 'We must so trust', the run 'en él en todo tiempo' kept. 'pouring out our hearts' and 'lighter than vanity' follow Sal 62:8-9 (RV1909 'derramad … vuestro corazón', 'menos que la vanidad').
- flag: Source 'lay out wants and our wishes' read as 'lay out our wants and our wishes'; lower-case 'the good counsel' after a full stop capitalized.

### 1525 Ps 63:1-2 David's desire toward God.
- quoted (Ps 63:1): “Early will I seek thee” → “levantaréme á ti de mañana”
- quoted (Rev 21:6): “the Fountain of the water of life” → “fuente del agua de vida”
- flag: Sal 63:1: Henry's 'Early will I seek thee' given with the RV1909's 'Levantaréme á ti de mañana' (no word 'seek').

### 1526 Ps 63:3-6 His satisfaction in God.
- flag: Sal 63:3, 5 ('mejor es tu misericordia que la vida', 'labios de júbilo', RV1909) and Is 51:11 ('el dolor y el gemido huirán') used; 'loving-kindness' rendered 'misericordia'. Sal 17:15 echoed.

### 1527 Ps 63:7-11 His dependence upon God, and assurance of safety.
- flag: 'follow hard after God' follows the KJV of Sal 63:8; the RV1909 has 'Está mi alma apegada á ti'. Sal 126:5 ('Los que sembraron con lágrimas, con regocijo segarán') echoed in Henry's third person, translated.

### 1528 Ps 64:1-6 Prayer for deliverance.
- flag: Stg 3:5 ('la lengua es un miembro pequeño, y se gloría de grandes cosas', RV1909) echoed, translated; 'a present help' given with Sal 46:1's 'pronto auxilio' as in batch 039.

### 1529 Ps 64:7-10 The destruction of the wicked, encouragement to the righteous.
- quoted (Ps 64:10): “The righteous shall be glad in the Lord” → “Alegraráse el justo en Jehová”
- flag: Sal 109:17 ('Amó la maldición, y vínole', RV1909) and Is 45:25 ('en Jehová será justificada y se gloriará toda la generación de Israel') echoed in Henry's words; translated.

### 1530 Ps 65:1-5 God is to be praised in the kingdom of grace.
- flag: 'Praise is silent unto thee' follows the KJV's margin of Sal 65:1; the RV1909 has 'A TI es plácida la alabanza'. Translated. Is 55:1 ('sin dinero y sin precio', RV1909) used.

### 1531 Ps 65:6-13 In the kingdom of providence.
- quoted (Ps 65:8): “of the morning and evening” → “de la mañana y de la tarde”
- quoted (Ps 65:12): “upon the pastures of the wilderness” → “sobre las estancias del desierto”
- flag: Sal 65:8 ('the outgoings of the morning and evening'): the RV1909 has 'las salidas de la mañana y de la tarde'. Sal 65:9, 11 ('el río de Dios, lleno de aguas', 'destilan grosura') and Mal 4:2 ('el Sol de justicia') used in Henry's sentences.
- flag: Sal 104:24 adapted by Henry ('in loving-kindness hast thou made them all' for 'in wisdom'); translated. Stg 1:17 echoed, translated.

### 1533 Ps 66:8-12 For his favour to his church.
- flag: Hch 14:22 ('por muchas tribulaciones entremos en el reino de Dios', RV1909) echoed in Henry's singular.

### 1534 Ps 66:13-20 And the psalmist's praise for his experience of God's goodness.
- quoted (Prov 15:8): “the sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination” → “sacrificio de los impíos es abominación á Jehová”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 66:20; Henry turns Sal 66:20 into the first person plural ('our prayer… from us'); the RV1909's 'mi oración, ni de mí su misericordia' cannot stand, so it is translated after its wording.): “prayer nor his mercy from”
- flag: Source 'gross practice, Sin, regarded' read as two sentences ('…gross practice. Sin, regarded…'). Pr 15:8: Henry's 'an abomination of the Lord' given with the RV1909's 'abominación á Jehová'.
- flag: 'Sin, regarded in the heart' echoes Sal 66:18 (RV1909 'Si en mi corazón hubiese yo mirado á la iniquidad'); 2 Co 1:12 echoed.

### 1535 Ps 67:1-7 A prayer for the enlargement of Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 67:7): “the ends of the earth” → “los fines de la tierra”
- flag: sensitive: 'the conversion of the heathen' / 'make known to the heathen' ('los paganos').
- flag: Source typo 'shall became' read as 'shall become'; 'accompany the word the Holy Ghost' read as 'accompany the word with the Holy Ghost'.
- flag: Sal 67:1 ('Dios tenga misericordia de nosotros', RV1909 'DIOS tenga…'), Pr 14:34 ('la justicia engrandece la nación') used; Ap 11:15 echoed as Henry words it ('the kingdom of the Lord'), translated.

### 1537 Ps 68:7-14 The wonderful works God wrought for his people.
- quoted (Ps 68:13): “dove, covered with silver, and her feathers” → “las alas de la paloma cubierta de plata, y sus plumas”
- flag: Sal 68:13: Henry's 'among the kilns' explains the KJV's 'lien among the pots'; the RV1909 has 'entre los tiestos'. 'kilns' translated 'hornos'. His 'as gold' (KJV 'with yellow gold'; RV1909 'con amarillez de oro') translated 'como oro'.

### 1538 Ps 68:15-21 The presence of God in his church.
- quoted (Ps 68:18): “the Lord God might dwell among them” → “para que habite entre ellos JAH Dios”
- flag: Sal 68:18: Henry's 'the Lord God' given with the RV1909's 'JAH Dios'. Ef 4:8 ('dió dones á los hombres', RV1909) echoed; Sal 68:19 'load us' (KJV 'daily loadeth us'; RV1909 'nos colma de beneficios') given as 'nos colma'.

### 1541 Ps 68:32-35 The glory and grace of God.
- quoted (Ps 68:35): “holy places. The God of Israel” → “el Dios de Israel”
- quoted (Ps 68:35): “strength and power unto his people” → “da fortaleza y vigor á su pueblo”
- flag: Sal 68:35: 'holy places' is Henry's own phrase ('sus santuarios', RV1909 'tus santuarios'); the quotation starts at 'el Dios de Israel, él da fortaleza y vigor á su pueblo', the RV1909's words, with its 'él'. Fil 4:13 echoed.

### 1542 Ps 69:1-12 David complains of great distress.
- flag: Sal 69:4: 'he restored that which he took not away' (KJV) translated in Henry's third person; the RV1909 has 'he venido pues á pagar lo que no he tomado'. 'hated wrongfully' given as 'sin causa' after the RV1909's 'me aborrecen sin causa'.

### 1543 Ps 69:13-21 And begs for succour.
- flag: Hch 5:41 ('tenidos por dignos de padecer afrenta por el Nombre', RV1909) and Sal 116:13 ('la copa de la salud') used in Henry's sentences; Job 16:2 ('consoladores molestos sois todos vosotros') given in his third person ('son todos ellos'). 2 Co 1:3 echoed.

### 1544 Ps 69:22-29 He declares the judgments of God.
- flag: sensitive: 'the judgments of God upon the unbelieving Jews'.
- flag: Is 53:3 ('varón de dolores', RV1909) used; Mt 8:20 and Jn 3:19 echoed, translated. 'set us up on high' follows the KJV of Sal 69:29 (RV1909 'me defenderá').

### 1545 Ps 69:30-36 He concludes with joy and praise.
- quoted (Ps 69:36): “love his name shall dwell” → “los que aman su nombre habitarán”
- quoted (Rom 8:32): “He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things” → “El que aun á su propio Hijo no perdonó, antes le entregó por todos nosotros, ¿cómo no nos dará también con él todas las cosas”
- flag: Ro 8:32: the RV1909 has no word for Henry's (KJV) 'freely'. 'Restorer of the ancient places to dwell in' combines Is 58:12 (RV1909 'restaurador de calzadas para habitar') with 'los desiertos antiguos'; translated. Ro 11:26 ('quitará de Jacob la impiedad') echoed.

### 1546 Ps 70:1-5 The speedy destruction of the wicked, and the preservation of the godly.
- flag: sensitive: 'his Jewish, heathen, and antichristian enemies'.

### 1547 Ps 71:1-13 Prayers that God would deliver and save.
- flag: Sal 71:9 ('No me deseches en el tiempo de la vejez; cuando mi fuerza se acabare, no me desampares', RV1909) and 71:7 ('mi refugio fuerte') echoed in Henry's third person; translated.

### 1548 Ps 71:14-24 Believing praises.
- flag: 'Not on a sabbath only': Henry means the day of worship; rendered 'sábado' per INSTRUCTIONS, without the Christian-sabbath note (he does not say Christian sabbath here).
- flag: Sal 71:15: 'the numbers thereof' given with the RV1909's 'el número de ellas' (RV1909 'aunque no sé'); Ap 5:12 ('El Cordero que fué inmolado', RV1909) used inside Henry's sentence.

### 1550 Ps 72:2-17 He passes into a prophecy of the glories of his reign, and of Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 72:6): “come down like rain upon the mown grass” → “descenderá como la lluvia sobre la hierba cortada”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 72:17; Sal 72:17 in the RV1909 ('benditas serán en él todas las gentes: llamarlo han bienaventurado') splits Henry's sentence and gives no place for his capitalized 'HIM'; translated, keeping his emphasis.): “All nations shall call HIM blessed”
- flag: Sal 72:6: Henry's 'not on that cut down' contrasts 'mown grass' with what is left growing; the RV1909's 'hierba cortada' makes the contrast read awkwardly; translated as he says it.
- flag: Sal 72:7: 'abundance of peace' given with the RV1909's 'muchedumbre de paz'.
