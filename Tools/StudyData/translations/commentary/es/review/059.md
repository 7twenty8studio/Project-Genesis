# To review: es commentary batch 059

### 2684 Ezek 7:16-22 
- quoted (Prov 11:4): “Riches profit not in the day of wrath” → “No aprovecharán las riquezas en el día de la ira”

### 2685 Ezek 7:23-27 
- translated, not quoted (Luke 10:42; RV1909 Lc 10:42 'la cual no le será quitada' speaks of María ('le'); Henry applies it to us without a person): “good part which shall not be taken away”

### 2688 Ezek 8:13-18 
- translated, not quoted (Ezek 8:17; RV1909 Ez 8:17 reads 'ponen hedor á mis narices'; Henry explains the KJV's branch): “lo, they put the branch to their nose”
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's 'they put the branch to their nose'; RV1909 Ez 8:17 reads 'he aquí que ponen hedor á mis narices' (a stench to my nostrils), so the branch is translated, inside Henry's own quotation marks.

### 2689 Ezek 9:1-4 
- flag: 'has an interest in heaven, and in whom saints on earth have an interest' is a play on 'interest'; rendered 'tiene influencia en el cielo, y en quien los santos ... tienen parte'.

### 2690 Ezek 9:5-11 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jn 18:9 (KJV 'Of them which thou gavest me have I lost none'; Henry's 'Of all that thou hast given me, I have lost none' is closer to Jn 6:39): RV1909 Jn 18:9 words used, 'De los que me diste, ninguno de ellos perdí'.

### 2692 Ezek 10:8-22 
- quoted (Isa 55:6): “the Lord while he may be found” → “á Jehová mientras puede ser hallado”
- flag: 'to call on him while he is near' (rest of Is 55:6, RV1909 'llamadle en tanto que está cercano') is translated, since the imperative can't stand in Henry's sentence.

### 2694 Ezek 11:14-21 
- translated, not quoted (Ezek 11:19; RV1909 Ez 11:19 'darles he un corazón' is God's first person; Henry reports it in the third person): “will give them one heart”
- quoted (2Cor 5:17): “If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature” → “si alguno está en Cristo, nueva criatura es”

### 2695 Ezek 11:22-25 
- quoted (1Sam 12:22): “the Lord will not forsake his people” → “Jehová no desamparará á su pueblo”

### 2700 Ezek 13:10-16 
- flag: 'daubed the wall' (KJV 'daub it with untempered morter'); RV1909 Ez 13:10 'la recubrían con lodo suelto'; rendered 'recubrieron'.

### 2704 Ezek 15:1-8 
- quoted (Ezek 15:4): “it is cast into the fire” → “es puesto en el fuego”
- flag: RV1909 'es puesto' is masculine (el palo de la vid); kept verbatim after 'vid'.

### 2705 Ezek 16:1-58 
- flag: 'an exposed infant' rendered 'una niña expuesta', since the parable's infant is female (Jerusalem).

### 2706 Ezek 16:59-63 
- flag: 'will keep him by his power, through faith unto salvation' echoes 1 P 1:5 (RV1909 'guardados en la virtud de Dios por fe, para alcanzar la salud'); translated, in Henry's own words.

### 2707 Ezek 17:1-10 
- flag: Source outline 'to which an explanation is added' starts in lower case after a full stop; kept as given.

### 2709 Ezek 17:22-24 
- flag: Source 'which has borne so long, so large a sway' read as 'has borne sway'.

### 2710 Ezek 18:1-20 
- quoted (Ezek 18:4): “The soul that sinneth it shall die” → “El alma que pecare, esa morirá”

### 2711 Ezek 18:21-29 
- quoted (Ezek 18:28): “he shall surely live” → “de cierto vivirá”
- flag: Henry cites 'Ezek 18:26,27' for the fulness of pardon, though those verses speak of the righteous turning away and the wicked turning back; kept as given.

### 2712 Ezek 18:30-32 
- quoted (Ezek 18:31): “a new heart and a new spirit” → “corazón nuevo y espíritu nuevo”

### 2714 Ezek 19:10-14 
- flag: 'one Branch of the vine' (Christ) rendered 'un Renuevo', the RV1909's word for the messianic Branch (Jer 23:5); 'a strong rod for the sceptre of those that rule' echoes Ez 19:11; translated.

### 2715 Ezek 20:1-9 
- quoted (Ezek 20:1): “The elders of Israel” → “los ancianos de Israel”
- flag: Source paragraph begins with a stray '. ' (perhaps a lost heading); the stray full stop is left out, nothing else.

### 2716 Ezek 20:10-26 
- flag: Source paragraph begins with a stray '. ' (perhaps a lost heading); the stray full stop is left out, nothing else.
- flag: 'the Lord that makes us holy' echoes Ez 20:12 (RV1909 'yo soy Jehová que los santifico'); translated as Henry's own sentence.

### 2717 Ezek 20:27-32 
- flag: sensitive: 'the heathen' rendered 'los paganos'.

### 2718 Ezek 20:33-44 
- flag: Source writes 'the jews' in lower case; rendered 'los judíos'.

### 2720 Ezek 21:1-17 
- quoted (Ezek 21:19): “of the king of Babylon” → “del rey de Babilonia”

### 2721 Ezek 21:18-27 
- flag: 'Him whose right it is' (Ez 21:27): RV1909 'aquel cuyo es el derecho' used (unlisted quotation of Ezek 21:27).

### 2723 Ezek 22:1-16 
- quoted (Ezek 22:2): “judge the bloody city” → “á la ciudad derramadora de sangre”
- flag: 'the city of bloods' is the KJV margin's literal Hebrew; translated 'la ciudad de las sangres'.

### 2724 Ezek 22:17-22 
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”
- flag: 'these light and momentary afflictions' echoes 2 Co 4:17 (RV1909 'lo que al presente es momentáneo y leve de nuestra tribulación'); translated.

### 2726 Ezek 23:1-49 
- flag: Aholah / Aholibah have no es glossary entry; RV1909 Ez 23:4 spelling 'Ahola' / 'Aholiba' used.

### 2728 Ezek 24:15-27 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 1 Ts 4:13 (KJV 'sorrow not, even as others which have no hope'): RV1909 words 'como los otros que no tienen esperanza' used.

### 2729 Ezek 25:1-7 
- flag: Source paragraph begins with a stray '. ' (perhaps a lost heading); the stray full stop is left out, nothing else.

### 2730 Ezek 25:8-17 
- flag: Source 'Though one event seem to the righteous and wicked' is missing a verb ('seem to happen to', Ec 9:2); translated as 'parezca acontecer'.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ro 2:5: RV1909 words 'atesoras para ti mismo ira para el día de la ira' adapted ('atesoran para sí mismos ira para el día de la ira'), since Henry changes the person.

### 2732 Ezek 26:15-21 
- flag: 'vanity and vexation' (Ec 1:14, KJV 'vanity and vexation of spirit') rendered with RV1909's 'vanidad y aflicción de espíritu'.

### 2733 Ezek 27:1-25 
- flag: Unlisted quotations: Hch 24:16 'conciencia sin remordimiento' and Dt 8:18 'el poder para hacer las riquezas' (RV1909 words used).

### 2734 Ezek 27:26-36 
- quoted (Matt 6:33): “first the kingdom of God and his righteousness” → “primeramente el reino de Dios y su justicia”
- flag: 'have the God of Jacob for their Help, and whose hope is in the Lord their God' echoes Sal 146:5 (RV1909 'en cuya ayuda es el Dios de Jacob, cuya esperanza es en Jehová su Dios'); translated in Henry's construction.

### 2735 Ezek 28:1-19

### 2736 Ezek 28:20-26

### 2737 Ezek 29:1-16

- flag: Henry's 'the basest of the kingdoms' (KJV) is given in RV1909's words 'en comparación de los otros reinos será humilde' (Ez 29:15), slightly milder than the KJV superlative.
