# To review: es commentary batch 035

### 1364 Job 32:15-22 He speaks without partiality.
- flag: The English reads “set the wrath Lord always before us”, apparently a slip; rendered “al Señor de la ira”, keeping both words.

### 1368 Job 33:19-28 God sends afflictions for good.
- quoted (Job 33:27): “perverted that which was right” → “pervertí lo recto”
- flag: “So foolish have I been and ignorant” echoes Ps 73:22 (KJV “So foolish was I, and ignorant”); the RV1909 reads “Mas yo era ignorante, y no entendía”, so Henry's words are translated, not quoted.
- flag: Unlisted echo of Matt 20:28 (“dio su vida en rescate por muchos”), which happens to match the RV1909's “para dar su vida en rescate por muchos”.

### 1369 Job 33:29-33 Elihu entreats Job's attention.
- flag: The English has a word missing: “kept back from the we shall bless”; probably “the pit” (Job 33:30). Supplied in brackets as “[abismo]”; the odd “at least” is read as “at last” (“al fin”), the evident sense.

### 1370 Job 34:1-9 Elihu accuses Job of charging God with injustice.
- flag: “He that say, I have cleansed my hands in vain” (grammar slip “say” translated as “dice”) paraphrases Ps 73:13; the RV1909 reads “en vano he limpiado mi corazón, y lavado mis manos en inocencia”, so Henry's form is translated, not quoted.

### 1371 Job 34:10-15 God cannot be unjust.
- quoted (Prov 24:12): “render to every man according to his works” → “dará al hombre según sus obras”
- flag: Henry's “to every man” is narrower in the RV1909 (Prov 24:12 “dará al hombre según sus obras”).
- flag: “God cannot do wickedness, nor the Almighty commit wrong” paraphrases Job 34:10 (RV1909 “Lejos esté de Dios la impiedad, y del Omnipotente la iniquidad”); translated, echoing its words.

### 1373 Job 34:31-37 Elihu reproves Job.
- quoted (Ps 145:17): “is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works” → “Justo es Jehová en todos sus caminos, y misericordioso en todas sus obras”
- flag: The English reads “Let will only oblige him”, apparently a slip for “Elihu will only”; rendered “Eliú solo le obligará”.
- flag: Ps 145:17: the KJV has “holy in all his works”, the RV1909 “misericordioso en todas sus obras”; the RV1909 is quoted.

### 1375 Job 35:9-13 Why those who cry out under afflictions are not regarded.
- flag: Unlisted echo of Job 35:10 (RV1909 “que da canciones en la noche”): “da canciones en la noche” uses its words.
- flag: The English reads “This he knew not how to reconcile the justice of God”, with “with” missing; rendered as meant (“conciliar esto con”).

### 1376 Job 35:14-16 Elihu reproves Job's impatience.
- flag: Unlisted echo of Job 35:16 (RV1909 “Job abrió su boca vanamente, y multiplica palabras sin sabiduría”): “abrió su boca vanamente” quoted; Henry's past tense kept in “había multiplicado palabras sin sabiduría”.

### 1377 Job 36:1-4 Elihu desires Job's attention.
- flag: The English repeats itself (“It pointed out to Job the true reason of those trials with which he had been pointed out to Job the true reason of those trials with which he had been visited”); the repetition is translated as it stands, not dropped.

### 1380 Job 36:24-33 The wonders in the works of creation.
- flag: The English has a word missing: “cause the Lord to his face”; probably “to hide his face”. Supplied in brackets as “[esconda]”.

### 1381 Job 37:1-13 Elihu observes the power of God.
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”

### 1382 Job 37:14-20 Job required to explain the works of nature.
- quoted (1Cor 13:10): “when that which is perfect is come” → “cuando venga lo que es perfecto”

### 1383 Job 37:21-24 God is great, and is to be feared.
- quoted (Job 37:22): “With God is terrible majesty” → “En Dios hay una majestad terrible”

### 1386 Job 38:12-24 Concerning the light and darkness.
- quoted (Job 38:14): “as clay to the seal” → “como lodo bajo de sello”
- quoted (Job 38:18): “the breadth of the earth” → “las anchuras de la tierra”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Luke 1:78-79, given in the RV1909's words (“Por las entrañas de misericordia de nuestro Dios … nos visitó de lo alto el Oriente, para dar luz á los que habitan en tinieblas”); “Day-spring” = RV1909 “el Oriente”, “sit” = “habitan”.
- flag: “perceive not the breadth of the earth”: the RV1909 (Job 38:18) has “considerado … las anchuras”; Henry's verb is kept (“no percibimos”).

### 1388 Job 39:1-30 God inquires of Job concerning several animals.
- quoted (Jer 49:16): “I will bring thee down from thence, saith the Lord” → “de allí te haré descender, dice Jehová”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jer 8:6, given in the RV1909's words (“Cada cual se volvió á su carrera, como caballo que arremete con ímpetu á la batalla”); Henry has the present “turneth”.
- flag: “a heart”: Henry means a willing heart; rendered “un corazón dispuesto” (one word added for sense).
- flag: “wild ass” = RV1909 “asno montés”, “unicorn” = RV1909 “unicornio” (Job 39:5, 9).

### 1389 Job 40:1-5 Job humbles himself to God.
- quoted (Job 40:2): “Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him” → “Es sabiduría contender con el Omnipotente”
- quoted (Ps 29:4): “the voice of the Lord is powerful” → “voz de Jehová con potencia”
- quoted (John 16:13): “the Spirit of truth is come, he” → “viniere aquel Espíritu de verdad, él”
- flag: Job 40:2: the KJV asks “Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him?”, the RV1909 “¿Es sabiduría contender con el Omnipotente?” (its Job 40 numbers differ); the RV1909's question is quoted.
- flag: “he shall convince” is John 16:8 (RV1909 “redargüirá”); Henry's verb is translated “convencerá” after the RV1909's words of John 16:13.

### 1391 Job 40:15-24 God's power shown in Behemoth.
- flag: “Behemoth” rendered “Behemot” (RV1909 “behemoth”, Job 40:10 in its numbering).

### 1392 Job 41:1-34 Concerning Leviathan.
- flag: “Leviathan” rendered “Leviatán” (RV1909 “leviathán”).

### 1393 Job 42:1-6 Job humbly submits unto God.
- quoted (2Cor 3:18): “into the same image” → “en la misma semejanza”
- flag: 2 Cor 3:18: KJV “image”, RV1909 “semejanza”; the RV1909 is quoted.

### 1394 Job 42:7-9 Job intercedes for his friends.
- quoted (Matt 25:21): “Well done, thou good and faithful servant” → “Bien, buen siervo y fiel”
- flag: The English has a comma where a sentence ends (“reconcile them, Those who differ”); kept as it stands.
- flag: “he spake of God that which was right” echoes Job 42:7 (RV1909 “no habéis hablado por mí lo recto, como mi siervo Job”); translated, echoing “lo recto”.

### 1395 Job 42:10-17 His renewed prosperity.
- quoted (Prov 4:18): “more and more unto the perfect day” → “va en aumento hasta que el día es perfecto”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Prov 10:22 in the RV1909's words (“La bendición de Jehová es la que enriquece”).
- flag: Henry's wordplay “warm devotions … warm disputes” rendered “devociones fervientes … disputas acaloradas”.

### intro Ps 
- flag: “mediating on his truths” is a typo for “meditating”; rendered “meditando”.
- flag: “sound speech which cannot be condemned” echoes Titus 2:8; translated.

### 1396 Ps 1:1-3 The holiness and happiness of a godly man.
- flag: Henry's “night and day” (Ps 1:2) rendered with the RV1909's order “de día y de noche”.

### 1397 Ps 1:4-6 The sinfulness and misery of a wicked man, The ground and reason of both.
- quoted (Ps 1:4): “are like the chaff which the wind” → “como el tamo que arrebata el viento”
- flag: “The ungodly are not so, ver. 4” quotes Ps 1:4; given in the RV1909's words (“No así los malos”), which say “malos” where Henry's KJV has “ungodly” (rendered “impíos” elsewhere).
- flag: Unlisted echoes in the RV1909's words: Matt 3:12 (“cuyo aventador en su mano está, y que aventará su era”; KJV “thoroughly purge”) and 2 Cor 5:17 (“He aquí todas son hechas nuevas”, “nueva criatura”).
- flag: Section title in the English joins two titles with a comma (“…a wicked man, The ground…”); rendered with a semicolon.

### 1398 Ps 2:1-6 Threatenings against the enemies of Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Eph 1:22): “Head over all things to the church” → “cabeza sobre todas las cosas á la iglesia”
- flag: Ps 2:3 “bands … cords”: the RV1909 has “coyundas … cuerdas”; Henry's own phrase is translated (“ataduras … cuerdas”).

### 1399 Ps 2:7-9 Promise to Christ as the Head of this kingdom.
- quoted (Heb 5:5): “said unto him, Thou art my Son” → “Tú eres mi Hijo”
- flag: A stray apostrophe ends “my Sovereign'” in the English; left out.
- flag: Unlisted echo of Heb 7:25 in the RV1909's words (“puede … salvar eternamente”).
- flag: sensitive: “the heathen” (Ps 2:8) rendered “las naciones paganas”.

### 1400 Ps 2:10-12 Counsel to all, to espouse its interests.
- flag: Henry plays on Ps 2:12 “perish from the way” (RV1909 “perezcáis en el camino”): “in the way … and from the way” rendered “en el camino … y fuera del camino”.

### 1401 Ps 3:1-3 David complains to God of his enemies, and confides in God.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ps 3:3 in the RV1909's words (“escudo alrededor de mí”; KJV “a shield for me”).
- flag: “all shall work for good to them” echoes Rom 8:28 (RV1909 “todas las cosas les ayudan á bien”); translated in Henry's future tense.
