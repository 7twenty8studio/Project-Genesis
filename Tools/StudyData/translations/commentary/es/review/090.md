# To review: es commentary batch 090

### 3718 Gal 3:6-14 This doctrine established from the example of Abraham.
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “redeemed us from the curse of the law; being made” → “Cristo nos redimió de la maldición de la ley”
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “made a curse for us” → “hecho por nosotros maldición”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry's run ends "being made" (sin, for us); the RV1909's "hecho" goes with "maldición" (quote 1), so only "Cristo nos redimió de la maldición de la ley" is quoted there.
- flag: "he spared not his own Son" (Rom 8:32) translated.

### 3719 Gal 3:15-18 From the covenant of promises, which the law could not disannul.
- flag: "Rightly to divide the word of truth" (2 Tim 2:15) translated (RV1909: "que traza bien la palabra de verdad"); the rest of the sentence follows Luther's Galatians, as Henry does.

### 3720 Gal 3:19-22 The law was a school master to lead them to Christ.
- quoted (Gal 3:19): “wherefore then serveth the law” → “de qué sirve la ley”
- quoted (Gal 3:19): “the hand of a mediator” → “la mano de un mediador”
- quoted (Gal 3:22): “the promise by faith of Jesus Christ, might be given” → “la promesa fuese dada á los creyentes por la fe de Jesucristo”
- flag: Title: KJV "schoolmaster" (Gal 3:24) is "ayo" in the RV1909; the title uses "ayo".
- flag: Quote 2: the RV1909's word order ("dada á los creyentes por la fe de Jesucristo") is kept, so Henry's "to those that believe" falls inside the quotation.

### 3721 Gal 3:23-25 
- translated, not quoted (Gal 3:24; Henry changes the RV1909's "fuésemos" (we) to "they"; translated "fuesen justificados por la fe".): “might be justified by faith”
- flag: "as the word properly signifies, a servant, to lead to Christ" explains the Greek behind the KJV's "schoolmaster" (Gal 3:24); the RV1909 has "ayo", which is that very servant.
- flag: The English misspells "wordly" (worldly); translated "mundanos".

### 3722 Gal 3:26-29 Under the gospel state true believers are all one in Christ.
- quoted (Rom 8:9): “if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none” → “si alguno no tiene el Espíritu de Cristo, el tal no es de él”

### 3724 Gal 4:8-11 The happy change made in the Gentile believers.
- flag: "outward church" rendered "la iglesia visible".

### 3725 Gal 4:12-18 The apostle reasons against following false teachers.
- quoted (Gal 4:18): “always in a good thing” → “Bueno es ser celosos en bien siempre”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry's whole clause "It is good to be zealous always in a good thing" is given as the RV1909's "Bueno es ser celosos en bien siempre".

### 3728 Gal 4:28-31 
- quoted (Gal 4:31): “then, brethren, we are not children” → “De manera, hermanos, que no somos hijos de la sierva, mas de la libre”

### 3729 Gal 5:1-6 An earnest exhortation to stand fast in the liberty of the gospel.
- quoted (Gal 5:5): “through the Spirit, wait for the hope of righteousness by faith” → “por el Espíritu … la esperanza de la justicia por la fe”
- flag: The source's text opens with the second half of a two-part heading ("And to walk in the Spirit... (16-26)"), apparently merged in from the next section's title; translated and the paragraph breaks kept.
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909's "esperamos" becomes "esperan" after Henry's "those who"; the two runs around it are quoted.

### 3731 Gal 5:13-15 To take heed of indulging a sinful temper.
- quoted (Gal 5:14): “all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself” → “toda la ley en aquesta sola palabra se cumple: Amarás á tu prójimo como á ti mismo”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 1 Tim 6:3 (cited by Henry): "doctrina que es conforme á la piedad" is the RV1909 verbatim.
- flag: "biting and devouring one another" (Gal 5:15) translated with the RV1909's verbs ("morderse y comerse").

### 3732 Gal 5:16-26 
- quoted (Gal 5:19): “The works of the flesh are” → “manifiestas son las obras de la carne”
- quoted (Gal 5:19): “the works of the flesh” → “las obras de la carne”
- flag: "mortify the deeds of the body" (Rom 8:13) translated literally ("las obras del cuerpo"), not quoted; "cease to do evil... learn to do well" (Isa 1:16-17) translated.

### 3733 Gal 6:1-5 Exhortations to meekness, gentleness, and humility.
- quoted (Gal 6:2): “fulfil the law of Christ” → “la ley de Cristo”
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909's imperative "cumplid así" can't stand in Henry's "So we shall fulfil"; "Así cumpliremos" is translated and "la ley de Cristo" quoted. "sobrellevar los unos las cargas de los otros" echoes the RV1909 of Gal 6:2 in Henry's infinitive.
- flag: "No man can pay a ransom for his brother" (Ps 49:7) translated.

### 3734 Gal 6:6-11 To kindness towards all men, especially believers.
- translated, not quoted (Gal 6:8; Henry's plural subject ("those who... shall reap") needs "segarán" for the RV1909's "segará"; translated "del Espíritu segarán vida eterna", otherwise the RV1909's words.): “shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting”
- flag: "he will not be mocked" (Gal 6:7) translated (RV1909: "Dios no puede ser burlado").

### 3735 Gal 6:12-15 The Galatians guarded against the judaizing teachers.
- quoted (Eph 2:10): “created in Christ Jesus unto good works” → “criados en Cristo Jesús para buenas obras”

### 3736 Gal 6:16-18 A solemn blessing.
- quoted (Gal 6:16): “walk according to this rule” → “conforme á esta regla”
- quoted (Gal 6:17): “body the marks of the Lord Jesus” → “las marcas del Señor Jesús”
- quoted (Gal 6:18): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “la gracia de nuestro Señor Jesucristo”
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's "his body" for the RV1909's "mi cuerpo"; "en su cuerpo" is translated and "las marcas del Señor Jesús" quoted.

### 3737 Eph 1:1-2 A salutation, and an account of saving blessings, as prepared in God's eternal election, as purchased by Christ's blood.
- quoted (2John 1:3): “from God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ” → “de Dios Padre, y del Señor Jesucristo”

### 3738 Eph 1:3-8 
- quoted (Eph 1:4): “before the foundation of the world” → “antes de la fundación del mundo”
- quoted (Gal 3:26): “children of God by faith in Christ Jesus” → “hijos de Dios por la fe en Cristo Jesús”
- flag: "spared not his own Son" (Rom 8:32) translated.

### 3739 Eph 1:9-14 And as conveyed in effectual calling: this is applied to the believing Jews, and to the believing Gentiles.
- quoted (Eph 1:9): “according to his good pleasure” → “según su beneplácito”
- quoted (Eph 1:14): “unto the praise of his glory” → “para alabanza de su gloria”

### 3740 Eph 1:15-23 The apostle thanks God for their faith and love, and prays for the continuance of their knowledge and hope, with respect to the heavenly inheritance, and to God's powerful working in them.
- flag: The English mixes tenses ("if we dispute less, and prayed more"); translated as one conditional ("si disputáramos menos, y oráramos más").

### 3741 Eph 2:1-10 The riches of God's grace towards men, shown from their deplorable state by nature, and the happy change Divine grace makes in them.
- translated, not quoted (Eph 2:1; Henry makes it general ("A man dead in trespasses and sins"); the RV1909's "muertos en vuestros delitos y pecados" addresses the readers, so it is translated "muerto en delitos y pecados".): “dead in trespasses and sins”
- quoted (Eph 2:9): “not of works, lest any man should boast” → “por obras, para que nadie se gloríe”
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's verb ("are not of works") gives "no son" before the RV1909's "por obras, para que nadie se gloríe".

### 3742 Eph 2:11-13 The Ephesians called to reflect on their state of heathenism.
- quoted (Eph 2:12): “of promise, having no hope” → “de la promesa, sin esperanza”

### 3746 Eph 3:8-12 Also the noble purposes answered by it.
- quoted (Eph 3:8): “the unsearchable riches of Christ” → “las inescrutables riquezas de Cristo”
