# To review: es commentary batch 069

### 3037 Matt 13:31-43 
- flag: This section has no title in the English.
- flag: 'the parable of the seed sown' here means the mustard seed (Mt 13:31-32); translated as Henry words it.
- flag: 'alters the property even of the members of the body' read as 'alters the character (use) of the members' ('altera la condición aun de los miembros del cuerpo').

### 3038 Matt 13:44-52 The parables of the hidden treasure, the pearl of great price, the net cast into the sea, and the householder.
- flag: 'householder' rendered 'padre de familia', the RV1909's word in Mt 13:52.

### 3039 Matt 13:53-58 Jesus is again rejected at Nazareth.
- quoted (Matt 13:55): “Is not this the carpenter's son” → “¿No es éste el hijo del carpintero”
- quoted (Matt 13:58): “He did not many mighty works there, because of their unbelief” → “no hizo allí muchas maravillas, á causa de la incredulidad de ellos”
- flag: English slip 'to those who have repulsed them' (them = his offers) rendered 'a los que lo han rechazado'.

### 3042 Matt 14:22-33 Jesus walks upon the sea.
- quoted (Heb 11:6): “to God, must believe” → “es menester que el que á Dios se allega, crea”
- flag: quote 0: Henry's 'He that comes to God, must believe' given with the RV1909's run 'es menester que el que á Dios se allega, crea' (one word longer than Henry's run, needed for the sense).
- flag: 'a very present help' (Sal 46:1): RV1909 'nuestro pronto auxilio'; rendered 'un pronto auxilio' after it, not marked as a listed quotation.

### 3043 Matt 14:34-36 Jesus healing the sick.
- quoted (Mark 1:32): “They brought unto him all that were diseased” → “traían á él todos los que tenían mal”
- quoted (Matt 14:36): “As many as touched, were made perfectly whole” → “todos los que tocaron, quedaron sanos”
- flag: quote 1: Henry's 'perfectly whole' follows the KJV; the RV1909 has only 'quedaron sanos', so 'por completo' is added outside the quotation to keep Henry's word.

### 3044 Matt 15:1-9 Jesus discourses about human traditions.
- flag: Henry's quoted '"In vain do they worship me"' (Mt 15:9, not listed) given with the RV1909's words, 'En vano me honran' (unlisted quotation of Matt 15:9).
- flag: 'the fifth commandment' kept as Henry numbers it (honour thy father and mother).

### 3045 Matt 15:10-20 He warns against things which really defile.
- flag: 'desperately wicked' (Jer 17:9): the RV1909 has 'Engañoso… y perverso'; Henry's phrase translated, not quoted.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Zech 13:1 ('para el pecado y la inmundicia'), RV1909 words; 'Fountain' rendered 'Manantial' as in earlier batches.

### 3046 Matt 15:21-28 He heals the daughter of a Syrophenician woman.
- quoted (Isa 52:10): “the ends of the earth shall see” → “los términos de la tierra verán”
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909 continues 'la salud del Dios nuestro'; Henry's 'his salvation' rendered 'su salvación' outside the quotation.
- flag: 'though he seems ready to slay us, yet to trust in him' alludes to Job 13:15; translated as Henry words it.
- flag: 'He spake, and it was done' (Sal 33:9; RV1909 'él dijo, y fué hecho') rendered 'Él habló, y fue hecho', not marked as a listed quotation.
- flag: 'Of all graces… Christ honours faith most' translated literally; the second clause reads 'Cristo es el que más honra a la fe' (of all graces, faith is the one Christ honours most).

### 3047 Matt 15:29-39 Jesus heals the sick, and miraculously feeds four thousand.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 15:37 ('Comieron todos, y se hartaron'), RV1909 words; 'Lord, increase our faith' after Luke 17:5 ('Auméntanos la fe'), fitted into Henry's prayer.

### 3048 Matt 16:1-4 The Pharisees and Sadducees ask a sign.
- flag: The English is garbled here: the heading of a later section ('The necessity of self-denial. (24-28)') stands at the start of this section's text; translated and kept where it is.

### 3049 Matt 16:5-12 Jesus cautions against the doctrine of the Pharisees.
- flag: 'Then understood they' (Mt 16:12, RV1909 'Entonces entendieron'); the RV1909's opening words used, not marked as a listed quotation.

### 3050 Matt 16:13-20 Peter's testimony that Jesus was the Christ.
- flag: Henry explains the English word 'rock' in Mt 16:18; the RV1909 has 'sobre esta piedra edificaré mi iglesia' ('piedra', not 'roca'); his explanation translated with 'roca'.
- flag: sensitive: 'Nothing can be more wrong than to suppose that Christ meant the person of Peter was the rock… all pretensions of any man, either to absolve or retain men's sins, are blasphemous and absurd' (aimed at Roman Catholic teaching on Peter, the pope and priestly absolution).
- flag: 'None can forgive sins but God only' after Mk 2:7 (RV1909 '¿Quién puede perdonar pecados, sino solo Dios?'); rendered as a statement, not marked as a quotation.

### 3051 Matt 16:21-23 Christ foretells his sufferings, and rebukes Peter.
- flag: 'savour… the things of man… of God' (Mt 16:23): the RV1909 has 'no entiendes lo que es de Dios sino lo que es de los hombres'; Henry's 'savour' rendered 'saben más a', not quoted.

### 3052 Matt 16:24-28 
- flag: This section has no title in the English.
- flag: Henry's quoted '"Let him deny himself"' and '"Let him take up his cross"' (Mt 16:24, not listed) given with the RV1909's words 'Niéguese á sí mismo' and 'Tome su cruz' (unlisted quotation of Matt 16:24).

### 3053 Matt 17:1-13 The transfiguration of Christ.
- quoted (John 1:14): “glory, as of the only begotten of the Father” → “gloria como del unigénito del Padre”
- translated, not quoted (Matt 17:5; The RV1909 run 'una nube de luz que los cubrió' has no main verb (it follows 'he aquí'); Henry's statement needs one, so 'una nube de luz los cubrió'): “a bright cloud overshadowed them”
- flag: quote 1: translated 'una nube de luz los cubrió', close to the RV1909's 'una nube de luz que los cubrió' (relative clause after 'he aquí').
- flag: 'In this he knew not what he said' after Lk 9:33 (RV1909 'no sabiendo lo que se decía'); translated, not quoted.

### 3054 Matt 17:14-21 Jesus casts out a dumb and deaf spirit.
- flag: 'Do we wonder… from the fall!' is a question ending with an exclamation mark in the English; kept as '¿…!'.

### 3056 Matt 17:24-27 He works a miracle to pay the tribute money.
- quoted (Phil 4:19): “need, according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus” → “conforme á sus riquezas en gloria en Cristo Jesús”
- flag: quote 0: 'our God shall supply all our need' rendered after the RV1909 ('suplirá todo lo que os falta') in the first person, 'todo lo que nos falta', outside the quoted run; the run 'conforme á sus riquezas en gloria en Cristo Jesús' is verbatim.

### 3057 Matt 18:1-6 The importance of humility.
- flag: 'renewed in the spirit of our minds' after Ef 4:23 (RV1909 'renovaros en el espíritu de vuestra mente'); rendered 'renovados… en el espíritu de nuestra mente', not marked as a quotation.

### 3058 Matt 18:7-14 Caution against offences.
- flag: Henry's two sentences after Rom 8:13 are in the first person plural ('If we live after the flesh…'); translated in his person, not quoted (the RV1909 has the second person: 'Si viviereis conforme á la carne, moriréis…').
- flag: unlisted quotation of Luke 19:10 ('á buscar y á salvar'), RV1909 words.

### 3060 Matt 18:21-35 Conduct towards brethren, The parable of the unmerciful servant.
- flag: Title joins two headings with a comma in the English ('…brethren, The parable…'); split with a period.
- flag: 'the wages of sin' (Ro 6:23, RV1909 'la paga del pecado') used in Henry's sentence, not marked as a listed quotation.

### 3061 Matt 19:1-2 Jesus enters Judea.
- flag: 'the Sun of Righteousness… with healing in his wings' after Mal 4:2 (RV1909 'el Sol de justicia, y en sus alas traerá salud'); 'Sol de justicia' kept, 'salud' used for 'healing' as the RV1909 does; not marked as a listed quotation.

### 3063 Matt 19:13-15 Young children brought to Jesus.
- flag: 'he will in no wise cast out' after Jn 6:37 (RV1909 'no le hecho fuera'); translated, not quoted.

### 3065 Matt 19:23-30 The recompence of Christ's followers.
- quoted (Matt 19:25): “Who then can be saved” → “¿Quién pues podrá ser salvo”
- quoted (Matt 19:28): “when the Son of man shall sit” → “cuando se sentará el Hijo del hombre”
- quoted (Matt 19:28): “the throne of his glory” → “el trono de su gloria”
- flag: 'Who can be saved? say the disciples' repeats the question in Henry's own sentence; rendered '¿Quién podrá ser salvo?' (the RV1909's words without 'pues').
- flag: 'We have forsaken all' after Mt 19:27 (RV1909 'nosotros hemos dejado todo'); rendered 'Hemos dejado todo', not marked as a listed quotation.
- flag: 'the gate… a strait gate' (Mt 7:13, RV1909 'la puerta estrecha') rendered 'una puerta estrecha'; 'according to what a man hath' (2 Co 8:12) translated.

### 3066 Matt 20:1-16 The parable of the labourers in the vineyard.
- quoted (Matt 20:16): “the last shall be first, and the first last” → “los primeros serán postreros, y los postreros primeros”
- flag: quote 0: Henry's 'the last shall be first, and the first last' (KJV Mt 19:30 order) given with the RV1909's Mt 20:16, which has the reverse order ('los primeros serán postreros, y los postreros primeros'); same saying, order follows the verse listed.
- flag: 'The Roman penny was sevenpence halfpenny in our money': 'penny' is 'denario' in the RV1909 (Mt 20:2); rendered 'El denario romano equivalía a siete peniques y medio de nuestra moneda' (Henry's English money kept).
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909 words: Luke 17:10 ('siervos inútiles somos', fitted as 'somos siervos inútiles'), Matt 20:7 ('Nadie nos ha ajustado'), 2 Cor 6:2 ('ahora el tiempo aceptable'); 'agree… for their penny' after Mt 20:13 ('te concertaste… por un denario').
- flag: 'market-place' rendered 'plaza', the RV1909's word in Mt 20:3.
