# To review: es commentary batch 051

### 2356 Isa 10:5-19 
- quoted (1Sam 6:20): “Who is able to stand before this” → “Quién podrá estar delante de”
- flag: Henry's 'Jehovah' kept as 'Jehová'; 'a standard-bearer fainteth' rendered in his words (RV1909 Is 10:18: 'como abanderado en derrota').

### 2357 Isa 10:20-34 
- quoted (Isa 10:27): “because of the anointing” → “por causa de la unción”

### 2358 Isa 11:1-9 
- quoted (Isa 11:1): “out of the stem of Jesse” → “del tronco de Isaí”
- quoted (Isa 11:4): “the meek of the earth” → “los mansos de la tierra”
- quoted (Rom 8:35): “Who shall separate us from the love of Christ” → “¿Quién nos apartará del amor de Cristo?”
- flag: 'Rod' and 'Branch' (Is 11:1) rendered with RV1909's words 'Vara' and 'Vástago'.
- flag: The last sentence's English is garbled ('And this blessed power there have been witnesses…', a word such as 'of' missing); translated as 'de este bendito poder ha habido testigos'.

### 2359 Isa 11:10-16 
- quoted (Jude 1:21): “the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life” → “la misericordia de nuestro Señor Jesucristo, para vida eterna”

### 2360 Isa 12:1-6 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mt 25:34 at the end: RV1909 'Venid, benditos de mi Padre, heredad el reino preparado para vosotros desde la fundación del mundo' used verbatim (Henry's 'receive' and 'beginning' follow his memory, KJV 'inherit', 'foundation').
- flag: 'strength' and 'song' (Is 12:2) as RV1909 'fortaleza', 'canción'; 'wells of salvation' (Is 12:3) translated 'fuentes de salvación' (RV1909: 'las fuentes de la salud').

### 2362 Isa 13:6-18 
- quoted (Isa 13:10): “shall not give their light, the sun shall be darkened” → “no derramarán su lumbre; y el sol se oscurecerá”
- quoted (Isa 13:9): “with wrath and fierce anger” → “de saña y ardor de ira”

### 2363 Isa 13:19-22 
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”
- quoted (Heb 12:28): “grace whereby we may serve God acceptably with reverence and godly fear” → “retengamos la gracia por la cual sirvamos á Dios agradándole con temor y reverencia”
- flag: RV1909 He 12:28 has 'temor y reverencia' where Henry (KJV) has 'reverence and godly fear'.

### 2364 Isa 14:1-23 
- quoted (1Pet 1:16): “Be ye holy, for I am holy” → “Sed santos, porque yo soy santo”
- quoted (Isa 14:14): “be like the Most High” → “semejante al Altísimo”
- quoted (Isa 14:23): “with the besom of destruction” → “con escobas de destrucción”
- flag: RV1909 Is 14:23 has the plural 'escobas de destrucción' for KJV 'the besom of destruction'; Henry's own 'besom of reformation' is rendered 'la escoba de la reforma'.
- flag: Unlisted echoes: Dn 12:2 'vergüenza y confusión perpetua' (KJV 'shame and everlasting contempt') used verbatim; Is 14:20 'la simiente de los malignos' (RV1909: 'No será nombrada para siempre la simiente de los malignos') with the rest in Henry's words; Lc 14:11 'He who exalts himself shall be abased' translated.

### 2365 Isa 14:24-27 
- translated, not quoted (Isa 14:27; Henry makes it a statement ('his hand is stretched out to execute this purpose'); RV1909's 'Y su mano extendida, ¿quién la hará tornar?' doesn't fit his sentence.): “his hand is stretched out”
- flag: Henry's 'The Lord of hosts' (Is 14:27) rendered 'Jehová de los ejércitos', as RV1909 has it.

### 2367 Isa 15:1-9 
- flag: The English runs 'it is foretold, Is. 15:1. That their chief cities…'; joined into one sentence. Henry's bare '5.' (for Is 15:5) kept as given.

### 2368 Isa 16:1-5 
- flag: 'Break off thy sins by righteousness, it may lengthen thy quiet' is Dn 4:27 (KJV wording); translated, since RV1909 reads differently ('redime tus pecados con justicia … una prolongación de tu tranquilidad').
- flag: Henry's 'the great Ruler' rendered 'el gran Señor' (Is 16:1 'the ruler of the land', RV1909 'al señor de la tierra').

### 2370 Isa 17:1-11 
- quoted (Isa 17:7): “the Holy One of Israel” → “Santo de Israel”
- flag: Echo of Is 17:10 ('the God of our salvation … the Rock of our strength') in Henry's plural; translated (RV1909: 'el Dios de tu salud … la roca de tu fortaleza'); 'pleasant plants' and 'slips' follow RV1909's 'plantas hermosas' and 'sarmiento extraño'.

### 2371 Isa 17:12-14 
- quoted (Isa 17:13): “like a rolling thing, before the whirlwind” → “como el polvo delante del torbellino”
- flag: RV1909 Is 17:13 has 'el polvo' where the KJV (and Henry) has 'a rolling thing'.

### 2372 Isa 18:1-7 
- quoted (Matt 16:27): “reward every man according to his works” → “pagará á cada uno conforme á sus obras”
- flag: Echoes of 2 Co 4:9 ('cast down, yet not deserted, not destroyed') and Ec 12:14 ('bring every work into judgment') translated in Henry's wording.

### 2373 Isa 19:1-17 
- quoted (Isa 19:1): “shall come into Egypt” → “entrará en Egipto”

### 2374 Isa 19:18-25 
- quoted (Matt 12:34): “out of the abundance of the heart the mouth” → “de la abundancia del corazón habla la boca”
- flag: Henry's quoted 'In that day' (KJV Is 19:18) translated 'En aquel día'; RV1909 there reads 'En aquel tiempo'.

### 2375 Isa 20:1-6 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jer 23:6 ('the Lord our Righteousness'): RV1909 'JEHOVÁ, JUSTICIA NUESTRA' used verbatim, as in batch 050.

### 2377 Isa 21:11-12 
- flag: The English text begins with a stray ',12' (from the range '11,12'); kept as it stands so the numbers match.
- flag: 'What tidings of the night?' echoes Is 21:11 (RV1909: 'Guarda, ¿qué de la noche?'); translated. 'Inquire, return, come' echoes Is 21:12; rendered with RV1909's 'preguntad; volved, venid' (unlisted).

### 2378 Isa 21:13-17

- quoted (1Sam 15:29): “the Strength of Israel will not lie” → “el Vencedor de Israel no mentirá”
- flag: RV1909 1 S 15:29 calls God 'el Vencedor de Israel' where the KJV (and Henry) has 'the Strength of Israel'.

### 2379 Isa 22:1-7

### 2381 Isa 22:15-25 
- quoted (Isa 22:23): “a nail in a sure place” → “clavo en lugar firme”
- quoted (Isa 22:23): “as a nail in a sure place” → “como clavo en lugar firme”
- flag: The English runs 'security; what vanity is all earthly grandeur…!' in one sentence; split at the semicolon.
- flag: Unlisted echoes of Ap 3:7-8: 'la llave de David', 'una puerta abierta … la cual ninguno puede cerrar' and 'cierra, y ninguno abre' (Henry 'when he shutteth none can open') from RV1909, fitted to Henry's sentence.

### 2383 Isa 23:15-18 
- quoted (Isa 23:17): “The Lord will visit Tyre” → “visitará Jehová á Tiro”
- flag: The English repeats itself ('The love of worldly wealth is spiritual idolatry; and covetousness is spiritual idolatry'); translated as it stands.
- flag: 'holiness to the Lord' echoes KJV Is 23:18; RV1909 there reads 'será consagrada á Jehová'; translated 'santidad al Señor'.

### 2384 Isa 24:1-12 
- translated, not quoted (Job 14:1; Henry puts Job's words in the plural ('the children of men … are but of few days'); RV1909's 'corto de días, y harto de sinsabores' is singular.): “of few days, and full of trouble”
- flag: The English has a comma before a capital ('full of trouble, See the power…'); a full stop used.
- flag: 'vanity and vexation of spirit' follows RV1909 Ec 1:14 'vanidad y aflicción de espíritu'; 'strong drink' rendered 'sidra' as RV1909 Is 24:9.

### 2385 Isa 24:13-15 
- translated, not quoted (Isa 24:15; RV1909 Is 24:15 reads 'en los valles' where the KJV has 'in the fires'; Henry's point is the fires (he gives 'Or, in the valleys' as the other reading).): “the Lord in the fires”
- flag: Henry follows KJV Is 24:15 'glorify ye the Lord in the fires' and mentions the alternative 'in the valleys', which is RV1909's reading.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 2 Ti 2:19 ('The Lord knows those that are his'): RV1909 'Conoce el Señor á los que son suyos' used verbatim.

### 2386 Isa 24:16-23 
- quoted (Isa 24:20): “shall be removed like a cottage” → “será removida como una choza”
- quoted (Isa 24:20): “It shall fall and not rise again” → “Caerá, y nunca más se levantará”
- translated, not quoted (Isa 24:21; Henry's 'The high ones' (KJV Is 24:21 'the host of the high ones') is his own phrase here; RV1909 reads 'el ejército sublime en lo alto'.): “The high ones, that are”
- flag: 'the Sun of Righteousness' rendered 'el Sol de justicia' as RV1909 Mal 4:2; 'judge nothing before the time' (1 Co 4:5) translated.
