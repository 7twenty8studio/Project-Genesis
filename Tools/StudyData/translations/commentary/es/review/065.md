# To review: es commentary batch 065

### 2927 Zech 1:1-6 An exhortation to repentance.
- quoted (Zech 1:4): “Turn ye now from your evil ways, and from your evil doings” → “Volveos ahora de vuestros malos caminos, y de vuestras malas obras”
- flag: 'not one jot or title': the English has 'title', evidently for 'tittle' (Mt 5:18); translated as 'tilde'.

### 2928 Zech 1:7-17 A vision of the ministry of angels.
- quoted (Matt 13:11): “the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven” → “los misterios del reino de los cielos”
- quoted (Zech 1:13): “The Lord answered the Angel” → “Jehová respondió”
- quoted (Zech 1:13): “good words and comfortable words” → “buenas palabras, palabras consolatorias”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Zac 1:11: 'The earth sat still, and was at rest' given in the RV1909's words, 'toda la tierra está reposada y quieta' (present tense, 'toda' as in the RV1909).
- flag: 'red horse' (Zac 1:8): RV1909 'caballo bermejo'; Henry's own 'red' translated 'rojo'.
- flag: sensitive: 'the New Testament Babylon' (in Henry's Protestant usage, usually papal Rome) translated literally.

### 2929 Zech 1:18-21 The security of the Jews and the destruction of their enemies.
- flag: 'four workmen' (Zac 1:20, KJV 'carpenters'): translated 'cuatro artesanos'; the RV1909 has 'cuatro carpinteros'.

### 2930 Zech 2:1-5 The prosperity of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Zech 2:1): “with a measuring line in his hand” → “en su mano un cordel de medir”
- quoted (Zech 2:5): “a Wall of fire round” → “muro de fuego en derredor”

### 2931 Zech 2:6-9 The Jews called to return to their own land.
- quoted (Gen 19:17): “Escape for thy life, look not behind thee” → “Escapa por tu vida; no mires tras ti”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Zac 2:8: 'He that touches you, touches the apple of his eye' given in the RV1909's words, 'el que os toca, toca á la niña de su ojo'.

### 2932 Zech 2:10-13 A promise of God's presence.
- translated, not quoted (Isa 26:21; The RV1909 (Is 26:21) says 'visitar la maldad del morador de la tierra'; Henry's 'to punish the inhabitants … for their sins' is his own sentence and is translated.): “to punish the inhabitants of the earth”

### 2933 Zech 3:1-5 The restoration of the church.
- quoted (Zech 3:2): “a brand plucked out of the fire” → “tizón arrebatado del incendio”
- quoted (1Cor 6:11): “in the name of the Lord Jesus, and by the Spirit of our God” → “en el nombre del Señor Jesús, y por el Espíritu de nuestro Dios”
- translated, not quoted (Rev 19:8; The RV1909 (Ap 19:8) has 'las justificaciones de los santos'; Henry's point (righteousness 'both imputed and implanted') needs 'la justicia de los santos'.): “The righteousness of saints”
- quoted (Rev 19:8): “fine linen, clean and white” → “lino fino, limpio y brillante”
- flag: 'fine linen, clean and white' (Ap 19:8): RV1909 'lino fino, limpio y brillante' used ('brillante' for KJV 'white').
- flag: 'the bride, the Lamb's wife' (Ap 21:9) translated 'la esposa, la mujer del Cordero'.

### 2934 Zech 3:6-10 A promise concerning the Messiah.
- flag: 'the Branch' (Zac 3:8): RV1909 'el Pimpollo'; Henry's title translated 'el Renuevo' (and 'el Renuevo de justicia', cf. Jer 23:5; 33:15).

### 2936 Zech 4:8-10 Further encouragement.
- quoted (Zech 4:10): “the eyes of the Lord” → “los ojos de Jehová”
- flag: Source oddity: the English ends with a bare '(Zec 4:11-14)', apparently the next section's outline reference; kept as '(Zac 4:11-14)'.

### 2938 Zech 5:1-4 The vision of a flying roll.
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “us from the curse of the law, being” → “de la maldición de la ley … hecho por nosotros maldición”
- flag: 'flee for refuge to lay hold on the hope set before us' (Heb 6:18) translated; RV1909 'los que nos acogemos á trabarnos de la esperanza propuesta'.

### 2939 Zech 5:5-11 The vision of a woman and an ephah.
- quoted (Zech 5:7): “in the midst of the ephah” → “en medio de aquel epha”
- flag: 'ephah': Henry's own word given as 'efa'; the RV1909 quotation keeps its spelling 'epha'.
- flag: 'Blindness is happened unto Israel' (Ro 11:25): RV1909 has 'el endurecimiento en parte ha acontecido en Israel'; Henry's 'blindness' kept ('La ceguera').
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ro 2:5: 'ira para el día de la ira' in the RV1909's words.
- flag: sensitive: the Jewish nation's condemnation and long dispersion for crucifying Christ (whole section).

### 2940 Zech 6:1-8 The vision of the chariots.
- translated, not quoted (Zech 6:5; The RV1909 (Zac 6:5) has 'los cuatro vientos de los cielos'; Henry's explanation turns on 'spirits' (impulses upon the spirits of men), so his words are translated.): “four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from”
- quoted (Num 16:22): “the God of the spirits of all flesh” → “Dios de los espíritus de toda carne”
- flag: 'four spirits of the heavens' (Zac 6:5): RV1909 'cuatro vientos'; Henry's KJV 'spirits' kept because his explanation depends on it.
- flag: 'mountains of brass' (Zac 6:1): RV1909 'montes … de metal'; translated 'montes de bronce'.

### 2941 Zech 6:9-15 Joshua, the high priest, crowned as a type of Christ.
- translated, not quoted (Zech 6:11; The RV1909 (Zac 6:11) reads 'en la cabeza del gran sacerdote Josué'; Henry's short 'upon the head of Joshua' is his own narration and is translated.): “upon the head of Joshua”
- flag: 'the counsel of peace' (Zac 6:13): matches the RV1909's 'consejo de paz'.
- flag: 'an exceeding weight of glory' (2 Co 4:17) translated 'un inmenso peso de gloria'; RV1909 'un sobremanera alto y eterno peso de gloria'.

### 2944 Zech 8:1-8 The restoration of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Matt 19:26): “With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible” → “Para con los hombres imposible es esto; mas para con Dios todo es posible”
- flag: 'a city of truth and a mountain of holiness' (Zac 8:3): RV1909 'Ciudad de Verdad … Monte de Santidad'; given in lowercase as Henry's own phrase.

### 2945 Zech 8:9-17 The people encouraged by promises of God's favour, and exhorted to holiness.
- flag: 'speak every man peace with his neighbour': Zac 8:16 and Ef 4:25 say 'truth' (RV1909 'Hablad verdad cada cual con su prójimo'); Henry's 'peace' translated as written ('paz').

### 2946 Zech 8:18-23 The Jews in the latter days.
- quoted (Col 3:15): “let the peace of God rule in your hearts” → “la paz de Dios gobierne en vuestros corazones”
- flag: Henry's address in this section is given with 'vosotros' to match the RV1909 quotation of Col 3:15.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 2 Co 3:2: 'sabidas y leídas de todos los hombres' in the RV1909's words.
- flag: 'heathen neighbours' translated 'vecinos paganos'.

### 2947 Zech 9:1-8 God's defence of his church.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Cnt 2:2: 'como el lirio entre las espinas' in the RV1909's words.
- flag: 'no exactor shall pass by her any more' (Zac 9:8, KJV 'oppressor'): translated 'ningún opresor'; RV1909 'no pasará más sobre ellos angustiador'.

### 2948 Zech 9:9-17 Christ's coming and his kingdom.
- quoted (Zech 9:17): “How great is his goodness” → “cuánta es su bondad”
- flag: Zac 9:11-12 phrases in the RV1909's words: 'aljibe … en que no hay agua' and 'presos de esperanza' (unlisted quotations).
- flag: 'Ac 2' (a chapter, no verse) given as 'Hch 2'.
- flag: 'jewels of his crown' (Zac 9:16, RV1909 'piedras de corona') translated 'joyas de su corona'.
- flag: 'received among the heathen' translated 'entre los paganos'.

### 2949 Zech 10:1-5 Blessings to be sought from the Lord.
- translated, not quoted (Zech 10:1; The RV1909 (Zac 10:1) has 'Jehová hará relámpagos, y os dará lluvia abundante'; Henry restates it in the conditional with the KJV's 'bright clouds'.): “make bright clouds, and give”
- flag: 'bright clouds' (Zac 10:1): RV1909 'relámpagos'; Henry's KJV image translated 'nubes resplandecientes'.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 45:19: 'En vano me buscáis' in the RV1909's words (Henry: 'Seek ye me in vain').

### 2950 Zech 10:6-12 God will restore his people.
- quoted (Col 3:17): “do all in the name of the Lord Jesus” → “todo en el nombre del Señor Jesús”

### 2951 Zech 11:1-3 Destruction to come upon the Jews.
- flag: sensitive: the destruction of the Jewish church and nation foretold as judgment.

### 2952 Zech 11:4-14 The Lord's dealing with the Jews.
- flag: 'Beauty' and 'Bands' (Zac 11:7): the RV1909's names for the staves, 'Suavidad' and 'Ataduras', used.
- flag: 'cast it to the potter' (Zac 11:13, KJV 'potter'): RV1909 reads 'al tesorero'; Henry's 'potter' kept ('al alfarero').
- flag: sensitive: the Jewish church and nation called 'wretchedly corrupt and degenerate' and given over to judgment.

### 2953 Zech 11:15-17 The emblem and curse of a foolish shepherd.
- flag: 'the idol shepherd' (Zac 11:17): RV1909 'el pastor de nada'; Henry's KJV term translated literally 'el pastor ídolo'.

### 2954 Zech 12:1-8 Punishment of the enemies of Judah.
- quoted (Zech 12:6): “on the right hand and on the left” → “á diestra y á siniestra”
- flag: 'as fire among wood, as a torch in a sheaf' (Zac 12:6) translated from Henry's wording; RV1909 'como un brasero de fuego en leña, y como una hacha de fuego en gavillas'.

### 2955 Zech 12:9-14 Repentance and sorrow of the Jews.
- flag: 'the Spirit of supplications' (Zac 12:10): RV1909 'espíritu de gracia y de oración'; Henry's KJV 'supplications' translated 'súplicas'.

### 2956 Zech 13:1-6 The Fountain for the remission of sins, The conviction of the false prophets.
- quoted (Isa 64:6): “We are all as an unclean thing” → “Todos nosotros somos como suciedad”
- quoted (Prov 27:6): “faithful are the wounds of a friend” → “fieles son las heridas del que ama”
- flag: 'fountain' (Zac 13:1): RV1909 'manantial abierto'; used throughout for Henry's 'fountain'.
- flag: Title joins two headings with a comma in the English ('…sins, The conviction…'); split with a period.
- flag: 'Even the atoning blood' (a fragment in the English) rendered 'Es decir, la sangre expiatoria'.
- flag: 'wounds of a friend' (Pr 27:6): RV1909 'del que ama'; quoted as such.
- flag: sensitive: 'the unbelieving nation of Israel' and the fountain closed to it.
