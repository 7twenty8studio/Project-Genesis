# To review: es commentary batch 019

### 639 Josh 8:30-35 The law read on Ebal and Gerizim.
- translated, not quoted (Josh 8:33; a common phrase in Henry's own sentence ('confirmed the covenant of the Lord'), not a quotation of Jos 8:33's 'el arca del pacto de Jehová'.): “the covenant of the Lord”
- flag: 'De 11; 27' (Deuteronomy chapters 11 and 27) kept as 'Dt 11; 27'.

### 641 Josh 9:3-13 The Gibeonites apply for peace.
- quoted (2Cor 2:16): “savour of life unto life” → “olor de vida para vida”
- quoted (2Cor 2:16): “savour of death unto death” → “olor de muerte para muerte”
- quoted (Josh 9:6): “come from a far country” → “Nosotros venimos de tierra muy lejana”
- flag: source garbled: 'We must not do evil that good may themselves to the God of Israel, we have reason…' has lost words (probably 'that good may come. Had they submitted themselves…'); translated as it stands, broken.
- flag: source garbled: 'they were led to say it made of skins, and their clothes' has lost words (probably 'of their bread, their wine-bottles made of skins…'); translated as it stands.

### 642 Josh 9:14-21 They obtain peace, but are soon detected.
- quoted (Prov 12:19): “A lying tongue is but for a moment” → “la lengua de mentira … por un momento”

### 643 Josh 9:22-27 The Gibeonites are to be bondmen.
- quoted (Josh 9:25): “seemeth good and right unto thee” → “Henos aquí en tu mano: lo que te pareciere bueno y recto hacer de nosotros, hazlo”
- quoted (Ps 27:4): “in the house of the Lord all the days” → “en la casa de Jehová todos los días”
- flag: Q0 quotes Jos 9:25 from 'henos aquí en tu mano' as Henry does ('We are in thy hand, do unto us as seemeth good and right unto thee').
- flag: 'him that cometh to Him, he will in nowise cast out' (Jn 6:37) changed to the third person by Henry; translated (RV1909 'al que á mí viene, no le hecho fuera').

### 645 Josh 10:7-14 Joshua succours Gibeon, The sun and moon stand still.
- quoted (Gen 18:14): “Is any thing too hard for the Lord” → “¿Hay para Dios alguna cosa difícil?”
- flag: Q0: KJV 'too hard for the Lord'; RV1909 Gn 18:14 has 'para Dios'.
- flag: 'Behold the works of the Lord… What nation is there so great…' echoes Ps 46:8 and Dt 4:7 loosely; translated, as the RV1909 wording differs.

### 646 Josh 10:15-27 The kings are taken, their armies defeated, and they are put to death.
- quoted (Josh 10:21): “None moved his tongue against any of the children of Israel” → “No hubo quien moviese su lengua contra los hijos de Israel”
- flag: Q0: the RV1909 has no word for the KJV's 'any' ('against any of the children of Israel').
- flag: source oddity: 'Refuges of lies will but secure for God's judgment' seems to lack an object (them); translated as it stands.

### 647 Josh 10:28-43 Seven other kings defeated and slain.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 8:31 ('if he be for us, who can be against us?'): RV1909 'Si Dios por nosotros, ¿quién contra nosotros?' used.

### 650 Josh 11:15-23 All that country subdued, The Anakims cut off.
- quoted (Josh 11:23): “The land rested from war” → “Y la tierra reposó de guerra”
- flag: title: 'Anakims' rendered 'anaceos' (the RV1909's 'Anaceos', Jos 11:21).

### 652 Josh 12:7-24 The kings whom Joshua smote.
- flag: Henry's remark that the land is 'in our day' among the most barren in the world reflects his own time (c. 1700); kept as written.

### 653 Josh 13:1-6 Bounds of the land not yet conquered.
- quoted (Josh 13:1): “old and stricken in years” → “viejo, entrado en días”
- flag: source typos 'God promise' and 'through Joshua was old' read as 'God promised' and 'though Joshua was old'.

### 654 Josh 13:7-33 Inheritance of Reuben.
- quoted (Josh 13:33): “the tribe of Levi Moses gave” → “la tribu de Leví no dió Moisés heredad”
- quoted (Josh 13:14): “the Lord God of Israel” → “Jehová Dios de Israel”

### 656 Josh 14:6-15 Caleb obtains Hebron.
- quoted (Josh 14:14): “he wholly followed the Lord God of Israel” → “cumplió siguiendo á Jehová Dios de Israel”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Josh 14:12 ('Give me this mountain', Henry's quotation marks): RV1909 'Dame… este monte' used.
- flag: source typo 'let Israel knows' read as 'know'.
- flag: Q0: KJV 'wholly followed' is 'cumplió siguiendo' in the RV1909.

### 658 Josh 15:13-19 Caleb's portion, His daughter's blessing.
- flag: KJV 'a south land' (Jos 15:19) is 'tierra de secadal' (dry land) in the RV1909; Henry's 'south land' translated 'tierra del sur'. 'upper and nether springs' given as the RV1909's 'fuentes de arriba… de abajo'.

### 659 Josh 15:20-63 The cities of Judah.
- quoted (Micah 5:2): “little among the thousands of Judah” → “pequeña para ser en los millares de Judá”

### 661 Josh 17:1-6 The lot of Manasseh.
- quoted (Col 1:12): “the inheritance of the saints in light” → “la suerte de los santos en luz”
- translated, not quoted (Josh 17:4; Henry's own prayer echoes Jos 17:4 ('give us an inheritance among') but with 'thy saints'; the RV1909's 'nos diese herencia entre nuestros hermanos' can't stand there.): “give us an inheritance among”
- flag: Q0: KJV 'inheritance of the saints in light' is 'suerte' (lot) in the RV1909 (Col 1:12).

### 663 Josh 17:14-18 Joseph desires a larger portion.
- flag: source starts a sentence lowercase ('if we belong to Him'); capitalized.

### 664 Josh 18:1-1 The tabernacle set up at Shiloh.
- flag: Henry links Shiloh to the 'Shiloh' of Gn 49:10 (KJV); the RV1909 there also reads 'Shiloh', while the town is 'Silo' in Spanish, so the link is less visible.

### 666 Josh 18:11-28 The boundaries of Benjamin.
- quoted (1Sam 3:18): “It is the Lord; let him do what seemeth him good” → “Jehová es; haga lo que bien le pareciere”

### 667 Josh 19:1-9 The lot of Simeon.
- flag: 'Love seeketh not her own, doth not behave unseemly' (1 Co 13:4-5): the RV1909 says 'caridad… no busca lo suyo'; translated, 'no busca lo suyo' matching it.

### 669 Josh 19:17-51 The lot of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Dn
- flag: title: source 'Dn' is a slip for 'Dan' (the tribe); translated 'Dan'.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 15:3 ('Christ pleased not himself'): RV1909 'Cristo no se agradó á sí mismo' used.

### 671 Josh 20:7-9 The cities appointed as refuges.
- quoted (Josh 20:8): “on the other side Jordan” → “de la otra parte del Jordán”
- quoted (Isa 38:22): “go up to the house of the Lord” → “subir á la casa de Jehová”
- quoted (Ps 18:30): “to all those that trust in him” → “todos los que en él esperan”
- flag: the name meanings follow Henry; the RV1909 spells two of these cities 'Ramoth' and 'Gaulón' (Jos 20:8), the app's names 'Ramot' and 'Golán' used.
- flag: Q2: KJV 'trust in him' is 'esperan' in the RV1909 (Sal 18:30).

### 672 Josh 21:1-8 Cities for the Levites.
- quoted (1Cor 9:14): “they which preach the gospel should live” → “los que anuncian el evangelio”
- flag: Q0: only 'los que anuncian el evangelio' is verbatim; the RV1909's 'que vivan del evangelio' recast as 'deben vivir del evangelio' to keep Henry's 'should live'.

### 674 Josh 21:43-45 God gave the land and rest to the Israelites, according to his promise.
- quoted (Josh 21:43, Josh 21:44): “they possessed it, and dwelt therein” → “poseyéronla, y habitaron en ella”
- quoted (Josh 21:44): “There stood not a man” → “ninguno de todos los enemigos les paró delante”
- quoted (Josh 21:45): “of any good thing, which the Lord had spoken” → “No faltó palabra de todas la buenas que habló Jehová á la casa de Israel”
- flag: Q2: 'todas la buenas' is the RV1909's own wording (also Jos 23:14), kept verbatim; KJV 'There failed not aught of any good thing' is 'No faltó palabra' (no word failed) there.
- flag: Q1: KJV 'There stood not a man before them' is 'ninguno de todos los enemigos les paró delante' in the RV1909.

### 676 Josh 22:10-20 They build an altar of testimony, The congregation offended thereat.
- quoted (Lev 19:17): “suffer sin upon him” → “sobre él pecado”
- flag: Q0: the RV1909's 'no consentirás sobre él pecado' (Lv 19:17) kept as 'consintamos sobre él pecado'; only 'sobre él pecado' verbatim.

### 677 Josh 22:21-29 The answer of the Reubenites.
- quoted (Heb 4:12): “the thoughts and intents of the heart” → “los pensamientos y las intenciones del corazón”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Josh 22:22 ('God knows it', Henry's quotation marks): RV1909 'él sabe' used; KJV 'he knoweth'.
- flag: 'a part in him' (their children having a part in the Lord, Jos 22:25) kept as 'partícipes en él'.

### 678 Josh 22:30-34 The children of Israel satisfied.
- quoted (Eph 4:3): “to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace” → “guardar la unidad del Espíritu en el vínculo de la paz”
- flag: 'ED' kept in Henry's capitals; the RV1909 spells the altar's name 'Ed' (Jos 22:34).

### 680 Josh 23:11-16 Joshua warns the people of idolatry.
- translated, not quoted (Josh 23:11; Henry makes Jos 23:11's clause an imperative; the RV1909's 'que améis á Jehová vuestro Dios' (subjunctive after 'cuidad') can't stand as a command.): “Love the Lord your God”
- translated, not quoted (1Kings 15:5; the listed verse (1 R 15:5) is a chance match; Henry echoes Ps 23:6 ('goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life'), turned into the third person, so the RV1909's first person can't stand.): “him all the days of his life”
- flag: Q1 is listed with the wrong verse (1 Kings 15:5); Henry's phrase echoes Ps 23:6.
