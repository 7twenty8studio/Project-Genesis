# To review: es commentary batch 023

### 814 1Sam 17:1-11 Goliath's challenge.
- flag: The English opens with a stray fragment ('and goes to meet him.') and an outline marker '(40-47)' that belong to another section; translated as given, nothing dropped.

### 815 1Sam 17:12-30 David comes to the camp.
- flag: Echoes of 1 P 3:9 ('maldición por maldición', RV1909) and Pr 15:1 ('La blanda respuesta quita la ira') translated in Henry's syntax (he changes the tense).

### 816 1Sam 17:31-39 David undertakes to fight Goliath.
- flag: 'good words' read as good tidings ('buenas noticias').
- flag: Echoes of 1 Co 1:27 ('lo necio del mundo… lo flaco del mundo', RV1909), Jn 10:11 ('su vida da por las ovejas'), Ef 6:11 ('toda la armadura de Dios') and Fil 2:5 ('este sentir que hubo también en Cristo') worked into Henry's syntax.

### 818 1Sam 17:48-58 He kills Goliath.
- quoted (James 4:7): “resist the devil, and he will flee from you” → “resistid al diablo, y de vosotros huirá”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of He 3:12: 'corazón malo de incredulidad' (RV1909).
- flag: Echoes of Jer 9:23, Stg 4:6 and Job 9:4 translated in Henry's own wording (RV1909: 'ni en su valentía se alabe el valiente'; '¿quién se endureció contra él, y quedó en paz?').

### 819 1Sam 18:1-5 Jonathan's friendship for David.
- flag: 'un corazón y un alma' is the RV1909's wording of Hch 4:32 (Henry: 'one heart and one soul'), unlisted.

### 821 1Sam 18:12-30 Saul's fear of David.
- quoted (Heb 12:3): “Consider Him that endured” → “Reducid pues á vuestro pensamiento á aquel que sufrió”
- quoted (Heb 12:3): “contradiction of sinners against himself, lest ye” → “tal contradicción de pecadores contra sí mismo, porque no os fatiguéis”
- quoted (Heb 12:3): “faint in your minds” → “en vuestros ánimos desmayando”
- flag: He 12:3 quoted whole from the RV1909, as Henry quotes the whole verse; the RV1909 has 'pues' where Henry's 'Consider' has none.

### 823 1Sam 19:11-24 David flees to Samuel.
- quoted (John 4:14): “a well of water springing up into everlasting life” → “una fuente de agua que salte para vida eterna”
- flag: 'con propósito de corazón' follows Hch 11:23 (RV1909 'en el propósito del corazón'); Henry's 'full purpose' is the KJV's.

### 824 1Sam 20:1-10 David consults Jonathan.
- quoted (1Sam 20:3): “there is but a step between” → “apenas hay un paso entre”
- flag: Echoes of Hch 14:22 ('por muchas tribulaciones', RV1909), Hch 13:36 and Jn 15:7 ('pedid todo lo que quisiereis, y os será hecho', which Henry shortens) translated in Henry's syntax.

### 826 1Sam 20:24-34 Saul, missing David, seeks to kill Jonathan.
- flag: 'waved' read as 'waived' (renounced).

### 827 1Sam 20:35-42 Jonathan takes leave of David.
- flag: Echoes of 1 Ts 4:13 and Ap 7:17 translated in Henry's syntax.

### 830 1Sam 22:1-5 David at Adullam, Many resort to him.
- quoted (Ps 37:23): “The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord” → “Por Jehová son ordenados los pasos del hombre”
- flag: Sal 37:23: the RV1909 has 'del hombre' where Henry (KJV) has 'of a good man'.

### 831 1Sam 22:6-19 Saul destroys the priests of Nob.
- flag: Echo of 1 S 3:19 ('no dejó caer á tierra ninguna de sus palabras', RV1909) translated in Henry's syntax.

### 834 1Sam 23:7-13 God warns him to escape from Keilah.
- translated, not quoted (Prov 24:12; Henry's infinitive ('how to render to every man') depends on 'knows'; the RV1909's 'dará al hombre según sus obras' (Pr 24:12) can't stand in that construction.): “render to every man according to his works”
- flag: Echo of Sal 109:4-5 / 35:12 ('mal por bien', 'En pago de mi amor me han sido adversarios', RV1909) put into Henry's indirect speech.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 2 P 2:9: 'librar de tentación á los píos' (RV1909).
- flag: 'Trae acá la Biblia' echoes David's 'Trae el ephod' (1 S 23:9), which Henry adapts.

### 836 1Sam 23:19-29 He is rescued from Saul by an invasion of the Philistines.
- translated, not quoted (Isa 28:29; The RV1909 of Is 28:29 ('para hacer maravilloso el consejo y engrandecer la sabiduría') says something different from Henry's description of God.): “is wonderful in counsel and excellent in working”
- quoted (Isa 7:9): “If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established” → “Si vosotros no creyereis, de cierto no permaneceréis”

### 837 1Sam 24:1-7 David spares Saul's life.
- flag: Echo of Ro 12:21 ('No seas vencido de lo malo; mas vence con el bien el mal', RV1909) in Henry's infinitives.

### 839 1Sam 24:16-22 Saul acknowledges his fault.
- quoted (Ps 37:6): “righteousness as the light” → “justicia como la luz”
- flag: Unlisted quotations: 'la sinagoga de Satanás' (Ap 3:9) and 'raíz de amargura' (He 12:15), RV1909.

### 841 1Sam 25:2-11 David's request; Nabal's churlish refusal.
- flag: The English has a stray comma after 'want!' ('suffers want!, David'); kept as given.
- flag: 'Nabal, "A fool"': the Hebrew name's meaning, translated 'Necio'.

### 842 1Sam 25:12-17 David's intention to destroy Nabal.
- flag: Echoes of Lc 6:35 ('benigno para con los ingratos y malos', RV1909) and Mt 6:13 ('no nos metas en tentación') in Henry's wording.

### 843 1Sam 25:18-31 Abigail takes a present to David.
- flag: 'atoned for' here means made amends for, not the atonement: rendered 'reparó'.

### 844 1Sam 25:32-44 He is pacified, Nabal dies.
- quoted (Prov 14:13): “the end of that mirth is heaviness” → “el término de la alegría es congoja”
- quoted (1Sam 25:38): “the Lord smote Nabal, that he died” → “Jehová hirió á Nabal, y murió”
- flag: 'pesado como una piedra' echoes 1 S 25:37 (RV1909 'quedóse como una piedra'), translated in Henry's wording.
- flag: Pr 14:13: the RV1909 has 'el término de la alegría' where Henry (KJV) has 'the end of that mirth'.

### 846 1Sam 26:13-20 David exhorts Saul.
- quoted (1Sam 26:19): “stirred thee up against me” → “te incita contra mí”
- quoted (1Sam 26:19): “let Him accept an offering” → “acepte un sacrificio”
- flag: 1 S 26:19: the RV1909 has the present 'te incita' where Henry (KJV) has the past 'stirred'; the RV1909's 'sacrificio' stands for his 'offering'.

### 847 1Sam 26:21-25 Saul acknowledges his sin.
- flag: The English has a slip, 'founded in an cemented', read as 'founded in and cemented by'.
- flag: 'obran neciamente y yerran en gran manera' echoes Saul's words in 1 S 26:21 (RV1909 'yo he hecho neciamente, y he errado en gran manera'), put in Henry's third person.

### 848 1Sam 27:1-7 David retires to Gath.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Lc 17:5: 'auméntanos la fe' (RV1909).
- flag: Echoes of He 12:1 ('the sin which doth so easily beset') and 2 Co 7:5 (RV1909 'de fuera, cuestiones; de dentro, temores') translated in Henry's wording.

### 849 1Sam 27:8-12 David deceives Achish.
- flag: 'the devoted nations': nations under the ban (herem), rendered 'consagradas al anatema'.

### 850 1Sam 28:1-6 Achish puts confidence in David, Saul's fear.
- quoted (1Sam 28:3): “those that had familiar spirits” → “los encantadores y adivinos”
- flag: 1 S 28:3: the RV1909 has 'los encantadores y adivinos' for the KJV's 'those that had familiar spirits, and the wizards'; Henry quotes only the first part.
- flag: Unlisted echoes: 'un verdadero Israelita' (Jn 1:47), 'los hijos de desobediencia' (Ef 2:2), RV1909; 'ánimo doble e inconstante' translated (Stg 1:8).
- flag: The English has a stray comma in 'zealous against, sin'; ignored.

### 851 1Sam 28:7-19 Saul consults a witch at Endor.
- flag: Henry's own quotation marks around 'Bring me up Samuel' (1 S 28:11) and 'Thou and thy sons shall be with me' (1 S 28:19) kept; translated, since he paraphrases the KJV (RV1909: 'Hazme venir á Samuel'; 'mañana seréis conmigo, tú y tus hijos').

### 853 1Sam 29:1-5 David objected to by the Philistines.

### 856 1Sam 30:7-15 David overtakes the Amalekites.
- flag: Echo of Pr 3:6 ('Reconócelo en todos tus caminos', RV1909) translated in Henry's syntax.

- flag: Unlisted quotations: 'Paz y seguridad' (1 Ts 5:3) and 'la compañía de los muertos' (Pr 21:16), RV1909; 'alejan de sí el día malo' echoes Am 6:3 (RV1909 'dilatáis el día malo').
