# To review: es commentary batch 017

### 550 Deut 17:14-20 The choice of a king, His duties.
- quoted (Deut 17:19): “read therein all the days of his life” → “leerá en él todos los días de su vida”
- flag: Q0: the RV1909's 'leerá en él' (the copy of the law); 'a copy of the Scriptures' rendered 'un ejemplar' so the masculine 'él' agrees.

### 552 Deut 18:9-14 The abominations of the Canaanites to be avoided.
- quoted (Deut 18:9): “to do after the abominations” → “hacer según las abominaciones”

### 553 Deut 18:15-22 Christ the great Prophet.
- quoted (John 8:12): “the Light of the world” → “la luz del mundo”
- flag: source reads 'He is the World by whom God speaks to us' (Joh 1:1), evidently a slip for 'Word'; translated 'el Verbo' (RV1909's word in Jn 1:1).
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 11:3 ("He that should come", Henry's quotation marks): RV1909 'aquél que había de venir' used.

### 557 Deut 20:1-9 Exhortation and proclamation respecting those who went to war.
- quoted (Ps 110:3): “Thy people shall be willing” → “Tu pueblo serálo de buena voluntad”
- quoted (Deut 20:8): “as well as his heart” → “como su corazón”
- flag: Is 8:12 paraphrased by Henry ('fear not with the fear of them that are afraid'); RV1909 reads differently ('ni temáis lo que temen'), so translated, not quoted.

### 559 Deut 21:1-9 The expiation of uncertain murder.
- quoted (Eph 5:11): “fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness” → “con las obras infructuosas de las tinieblas”
- flag: source oddity: the text opens with the heading of the next section ('The first-born not to be disinherited…', with '(15-17)'); kept as it stands.
- flag: Q0: Henry's 'we have fellowship with' rendered 'comunicamos con', after the RV1909's 'no comuniquéis con' (Ef 5:11); only 'con las obras infructuosas de las tinieblas' is verbatim.

### 562 Deut 21:18-21 A stubborn son to be stoned.

### 563 Deut 21:22-23 Malefactors not to be left hanging all night.

- flag: Q0: RV1909 'maldición de Dios es el colgado' (curse of God) vs KJV 'accursed of God'; Henry's later 'being accursed of God' translated 'ser maldito de Dios'.

### 564 Deut 22:1-4 Of humanity towards brethren.

### 566 Deut 22:13-30 Against impurity.
