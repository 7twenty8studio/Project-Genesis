# To review: es commentary batch 048

### 2211 Prov 28:13-13 
- quoted (Rom 5:21): “through righteousness unto eternal life, by Jesus Christ our Lord” → “por la justicia para vida eterna por Jesucristo Señor nuestro”
- flag: Ro 5:21 speaks of grace ('la gracia reine'); Henry applies it to 'mercy', kept.

### 2213 Prov 28:15-15 
- quoted (Prov 28:15): “a roaring lion, and a ranging bear” → “León rugiente y oso hambriento”
- flag: KJV 'ranging bear'; the RV1909 has 'oso hambriento' (hungry bear), its words kept.

### 2224 Prov 28:26-26 
- flag: 'deceitful above all things' is Jer 17:9; RV1909 words used: 'engañoso … más que todas las cosas' (unlisted; 'es' moved).

### 2227 Prov 29:1-1 
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”

### 2236 Prov 29:10-10 
- flag: 'hated of all men' echoes Mt 10:22; RV1909 words used: 'aborrecidos de todos' (unlisted). 'their salvation' read as the bloodthirsty's salvation (KJV Pr 29:10 'the just seek his soul').

### 2244 Prov 29:18-18 
- flag: Henry builds on the KJV 'Where there is no vision' (Pr 29:18) and 'open vision' (1 S 3:1); the RV1909 has 'Sin profecía' in Pr 29:18, so his 'open vision' ('visión manifiesta', RV1909 1 S 3:1) no longer echoes the verse here.

### 2254 Prov 30:1-6 
- flag: 'wanting a righteousness' keeps Henry's 'a' (read as 'lacking righteousness').

### 2255 Prov 30:7-9 
- flag: 'Food convenient' is the KJV of Pr 30:8; the RV1909 has 'el pan que he menester', so Henry's catchword is translated. 'supply all our need' echoes Flp 4:19 (RV1909 'suplirá todo lo que os falta'), adapted to 'nos'.

### 2258 Prov 30:15-17 
- flag: Henry's quoted "Give, give" (KJV Pr 30:15) set with the RV1909's 'Trae, trae' (unlisted quotation).

### 2262 Prov 30:29-33 
- flag: 'follow peace with all men' echoes He 12:14; RV1909 words used: 'Seguid la paz con todos' as 'sigamos la paz con todos' (unlisted).

### 2263 Prov 31:1-9 An exhortation to king Lemuel to take heed of sin, and to do duties.
- flag: 'Every creature of God is good' is 1 Ti 4:4 (KJV); the RV1909 has 'todo lo que Dios crió es bueno', so Henry's wording is translated. 'women' here: 'vile women' (Pr 31:3).

### 2264 Prov 31:10-31 The description of a virtuous woman.
- quoted (Prov 31:27): “well to the ways of her household” → “Considera los caminos de su casa”
- flag: sensitive: Henry on the wife's place ('willing that he rule over her', 'women's business'); translated as written.

### intro Eccl 
- flag: Henry's quoted "vanity and vexation of spirit" set in the RV1909's words, 'vanidad y aflicción de espíritu' (Ec 1:14; 2:11; unlisted here).

### 2265 Eccl 1:1-3 
- translated, not quoted (Eccl 1:2; Henry writes it in capitals; the RV1909's words 'vanidad de vanidades, todo vanidad' are used, but in his capitals, which the verbatim check can't match.): “VANITY OF VANITIES, ALL IS VANITY”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ec 1:3 ('What profit has a man of all his labour?'); RV1909 words used: '¿Qué provecho tiene el hombre de todo su trabajo'. 'broken … cisterns', 'Fountain of living water' echo Jer 2:13 (RV1909 'cisternas rotas', 'fuente de agua viva').

### 2269 Eccl 2:1-11 
- quoted (Eccl 2:11): “All was vanity and vexation of spirit” → “vanidad y aflicción de espíritu”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Ti 6:8 ('Having food and raiment, let us therewith be content'); RV1909 words used: 'teniendo sustento y con qué cubrirnos, seamos contentos con esto'.

### 2270 Eccl 2:12-17 
- quoted (Heb 12:23): “the spirits of just men made perfect” → “los espíritus de los justos hechos perfectos”
- quoted (Eccl 2:11): “vanity and vexation of spirit” → “vanidad y aflicción de espíritu”

### 2273 Eccl 3:11-15 
- quoted (Rom 8:28): “all things work together for good to them that love” → “á los que á Dios aman, todas las cosas les ayudan á bien”
- quoted (Eccl 3:14): “men should fear before him” → “para que delante de él teman los hombres”
- flag: Ro 8:28: Henry's 'to them that love him' rendered with the RV1909's 'á los que á Dios aman'. Last sentence echoes 1 Co 10:13 in Henry's own syntax; translated.

### 2274 Eccl 3:16-22 
- quoted (Eccl 3:21): “the spirit of the beast” → “el espíritu del animal”

### 2277 Eccl 4:7-8 
- flag: The source text opens with a stray ',8' (left from the outline reference 'Eccl. 4:7,8'); kept as given so the numbers match.
- flag: Henry's quoted 2 Co 8:9 ("though he was rich, for our sakes became poor") translated in his first person plural; the RV1909 has 'por amor de vosotros se hizo pobre, siendo rico'.

### 2278 Eccl 4:9-12 
- flag: 'a threefold cord' (Ec 4:12) given in the RV1909's words, 'cordón de tres dobleces' (unlisted).

### 2279 Eccl 4:13-16 
- quoted (Eccl 4:16): “is vanity and vexation of spirit” → “vanidad y aflicción de espíritu”

### 2280 Eccl 5:1-3 
- flag: Outline reference '(Eccl. 5:9-7)' in the source is garbled (should be 5:9-17); kept as given.
- flag: 'the sacrifice of fools' (Ec 5:1) in the RV1909's words, 'el sacrificio de los necios' (unlisted).

### 2281 Eccl 5:4-8 
- quoted (Eccl 5:8): “if thou seest the oppression of the poor” → “violencias de pobres”

### 2282 Eccl 5:9-17 
- flag: 'laboured for the wind' (Ec 5:16) translated in Henry's syntax; the RV1909 has 'trabajar al viento', echoed.

### 2283 Eccl 5:18-20 
- quoted (John 6:27): “perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life” → “Trabajad no por la comida que perece, mas por la comida que á vida eterna permanece”

### 2285 Eccl 6:7-12 
- quoted (Eccl 6:9): “Better is the sight of the eyes than” → “Más vale vista de ojos que”
- quoted (Eccl 6:11, Eccl 6:12): “what is man the better” → “Qué más tiene el hombre”
- flag: 'fulness of joy and pleasures for evermore' echoes Sal 16:11; RV1909 words used: 'hartura de alegrías' … 'deleites … para siempre' (unlisted).

### 2286 Eccl 7:1-6 
- quoted (Eccl 7:2): “to go to the house of mourning” → “ir á la casa del luto”
- quoted (Eccl 7:5): “the rebuke of the wise, than” → “la reprensión del sabio, que … la canción de los necios”

### 2287 Eccl 7:7-10 
- quoted (Eccl 7:9): “in the bosom of fools” → “en el seno de los necios reposa”

### 2288 Eccl 7:11-22 
- quoted (Luke 18:13): “God be merciful to me a sinner” → “Dios, sé propicio á mí pecador”
- flag: 'If we say we have not sinned' is 1 Jn 1:10 (KJV); translated ('nos engañamos á nosotros mismos' is from 1 Jn 1:8). 'newness of life' in the RV1909's words, 'novedad de vida' (Ro 6:4, unlisted).

### 2289 Eccl 7:23-29 
- flag: Henry's 'I Kin. 11:1' written '1 R 11:1'.
- flag: 'many strange women' in the RV1909's words, 'muchas mujeres extranjeras' (1 R 11:1, unlisted).
- flag: sensitive: Henry on women (Ec 7:28: 'A woman thoroughly upright and godly, he had not found'); translated as written.

### 2291 Eccl 8:6-8 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Dt 32:29 ('Oh that men were wise …'); RV1909 words used: '¡Ojalá fueran sabios, que comprendieran esto, y entendieran su postrimería!'.
- flag: Henry's figures (ninety thousand a day) are his own estimate, kept.
