# To review: es commentary batch 016

### 512 Deut 3:12-20 The land of Gilead and Bashan.
- translated, not quoted (1Chr 5:18; not a quotation: Henry lists the tribes in his own words (the match is 1 Cr 5:18, about something else).): “Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh”
- flag: 'members one of another' (Ro 12:5): RV1909 'miembros los unos de los otros' used (unlisted).

### 513 Deut 3:21-29 Moses encourages Joshua.
- flag: unlisted Ro 8:31: RV1909 'Si Dios por nosotros, ¿quién contra nosotros' used, with Henry's addition 'so as to prevail'.
- flag: source typo: 'It God does not' for 'If'.
- flag: 'Let it suffice thee… speak no more' echoes Dt 3:26 (RV1909 'Bástate, no me hables más de este negocio'); adapted, not verbatim.
- flag: 'our Leader' rendered 'nuestro Capitán' for Henry's military image; 'Guía' would also do.

### 514 Deut 4:1-23 Earnest exhortations to obedience, and dissuasives from idolatry.
- quoted (Deut 4:23): “lest ye forget the covenant of the Lord your God” → “no os olvidéis del pacto de Jehová vuestro Dios”
- flag: source: a comma before 'Considering' where a sentence ends; full stop used.
- flag: 'keep our hearts with all diligence' echoes Pr 4:23 (RV1909 'Sobre toda cosa guardada guarda tu corazón'); translated.
- flag: 'which the Lord their God had imparted to all nations' echoes Dt 4:19 ('Jehová tu Dios los ha concedido'); 'Jehová su Dios' kept.

### 515 Deut 4:24-40 Warnings against disobedience, and promises of mercy.
- quoted (Job 28:28): “The fear of the Lord, that is wisdom” → “el temor del Señor es la sabiduría”
- quoted (Deut 4:1): “ye may live, and go in and possess the land” → “viváis, y entréis, y poseáis la tierra”
- quoted (Deut 4:40): “it may go well with thee, and with thy children after thee” → “te vaya bien á ti y á tus hijos después de ti”
- quoted (Deut 4:30): “these things are come upon thee” → “te alcanzaren todas estas cosas”
- translated, not quoted (Deut 4:30; Henry turns 'si… te volvieres á Jehová tu Dios' into a command ('turn to the Lord thy God'); 'vuélvete á Jehová tu Dios' keeps the RV1909's words but changes the verb.): “turn to the Lord thy God”
- flag: Sal 19:3: the KJV says the heavens' voice is heard without speech ('There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard'), Henry's point; the RV1909 reads 'No hay dicho, ni palabras, ni es oída su voz' (their voice is not heard). Its words 'dicho' / 'palabras' used for Henry's 'speech or language'.
- flag: unlisted 2 Ts 1:8: RV1909 'en llama de fuego' used.
- flag: 'the understanding of a man' kept literally ('el entendimiento de hombre').

### 516 Deut 4:41-49 Cities of refuge appointed.
- translated, not quoted (Deut 4:46, Deut 4:47; not a quotation: Henry's own sentence about the laws given after leaving Egypt; the match is Dt 4:46-47, about Sihon.): “out of Egypt; and they”
- flag: echoes Stg 1:23, 25 ('su rostro natural', 'la perfecta ley, que es la de la libertad', 'perseverado en ella') and 1 P 2:24 ('llevó nuestros pecados… sobre el madero'; Henry 'upon the cross'); adapted.
- flag: the title (cities of refuge, Dt 4:41-43) doesn't match the text, which is on Dt 4:44-49; kept as in the source.

### 518 Deut 5:6-22 The ten commandments repeated.

- flag: the RV1909 calls the sabbath 'el día del reposo' in Dt 5:12-15; 'sábado' used in Henry's sentences per the rules.

### 519 Deut 5:23-33 The request of the people that the law might be delivered through Moses.
- flag: unlisted quotations used from the RV1909: He 4:16 ('confiadamente al trono de la gracia', adapted), Is 3:10 ('Decid al justo que le irá bien'; KJV 'It shall be well with him', Henry 'them'); Dt 5:29 echoed ('tal corazón').

### 520 Deut 6:1-3 A persuasive to obedience.
- flag: source typo: 'statues' for 'statutes'.
- flag: Henry's three terms follow the KJV (commandments, statutes, judgments); the RV1909 of Dt 6:1 has 'mandamientos, estatutos, y derechos' ('derechos' for 'judgments'); 'juicios' kept for Henry's explanation.

### 521 Deut 6:4-5 An exhortation to obedience.
- quoted (Matt 22:38): “This is the first and great commandment” → “Este es el primero y el grande mandamiento”
- quoted (Mark 12:33): “To love him with all the heart, and with all the understanding” → “amarle de todo corazón, y de todo entendimiento”
- flag: unlisted Pr 23:26: RV1909 'Dame, hijo mío, tu corazón' used; 1 Jn 3:18 ('de palabra ni de lengua') and Ro 5:5 ('derramado en nuestros corazones') echoed.
- flag: 'the word translated God' refers to the Hebrew Elohim; Henry's Trinitarian reading kept as written.
- flag: 'with all our heart, and soul, and might' (Dt 6:5; RV1909 'con todo tu poder') rendered 'y poder'.

### 522 Deut 6:6-16 Obedience taught.
- quoted (Deut 6:12): “lest thou forget the Lord” → “guárdate que no te olvides de Jehová”
- quoted (Matt 4:7): “Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God” → “No tentarás al Señor tu Dios”
- flag: 'matters of doubtful disputation' (Ro 14:1): RV1909 'contiendas de disputas' used; 'the things that belong to our peace' (Lc 19:42, RV1909 'lo que toca á tu paz') adapted.
- flag: quote 1 listed as Mt 4:7 (Jesus quoting Dt 6:16); the RV1909's Matthew wording used ('al Señor'), as Dt 6:16 there reads 'No tentaréis á Jehová vuestro Dios'.
- flag: 'Thou shalt talk of these things' (Dt 6:7, RV1909 'hablarás de ellas'): Henry's words translated.

### 523 Deut 6:17-25 General precepts, Instructions to be given to their children.
- flag: echoes 1 Ti 4:8 (RV1909 'tiene promesa de esta vida presente') and Dt 6:25 ('tendremos justicia'), adapted.

### 524 Deut 7:1-11 Intercourse with the Canaanites forbidden.

### 525 Deut 7:12-26 Promises if they were obedient.

- flag: echoes Ef 1:11 ('el consejo de su voluntad') and Dt 7:22 ('poco á poco… las bestias del campo no se aumenten contra ti').

### 526 Deut 8:1-9 Exhortations and cautions, enforced by the Lord's former dealings with Israel, and his promises.
- flag: source garbled: a run of words repeated ('Look which way we will, both to look back and to look forward, to Canaan. Look which way we will…'); translated as it stands, the stray copy included.
- flag: 'verily they shall be fed' (Sal 37:3 KJV): RV1909 'en verdad serás alimentado', made plural ('en verdad serán alimentados').
- flag: 'Christ is the word of God': 'Verbo' (Jn 1:1) chosen to keep Henry's play on 'the word of God… food of the soul'.
- flag: echoes Is 61:3 ('árboles de justicia') and Sal 16:11 ('hartura de alegrías').

### 527 Deut 8:10-20 Exhortations and cautions further enforced.

- flag: "to do them good" (Dt 8:16, in Henry's quotation marks): RV1909 'para á la postre hacerte bien'; Henry changes the person, translated 'para hacerles bien'.
- flag: 'In everything we must give thanks' echoes 1 Ts 5:18 ('Dad gracias en todo'); 'anxious and troubled about many things' echoes Lc 10:41.

### 533 Deut 11:8-17 Promises and threatenings.
