# To review: es commentary batch 031

### 1192 Ezra 1:1-4 The proclamation of Cyrus for the rebuilding of the temple.
- quoted (2Chr 36:22): “The Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus” → “Jehová excitó el espíritu de Ciro”
- flag: 2 Cr 36:22 (= Esd 1:1): the RV1909's 'excitó' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'stirred up'; his following 'stirs up their spirits' rendered 'despierta su espíritu'.
- flag: 'The hearts of kings are in the hand of the Lord' echoes Pr 21:1 (RV1909 'así está el corazón del rey en la mano de Jehová'), translated.

### 1193 Ezra 1:5-11 The people provide for their return.
- quoted (Rom 8:21): “into the glorious liberty of the children of God” → “la libertad gloriosa de los hijos de Dios”

### 1194 Ezra 2:1-35 The numbers that returned.
- flag: Echoes Pr 14:34 (RV1909 'La justicia engrandece la nación'), translated.

### 1196 Ezra 2:64-70 The offerings for the temple.
- flag: 'Seek first the kingdom of God… added unto them' echoes Mt 6:33 (RV1909 'buscad primeramente el reino de Dios… os serán añadidas'), put in Henry's third person; 'mansions' rendered 'moradas', the RV1909's word in Jn 14:2.

### 1198 Ezra 3:8-13 The foundations of the temple laid.
- translated, not quoted (Ezra 3:6; Henry's own phrase ('upon laying the foundation of the temple'), not a quotation; the listed verse, Esd 3:6, reads 'el templo de Jehová no estaba aún fundado'): “the foundation of the temple”
- quoted (Ezra 3:12): “wept with a loud voice” → “lloraban en alta voz”
- flag: 'Menospreciaron el día de las pequeñeces' follows Zac 4:10 (RV1909 'los que menospreciaron el día de las pequeñeces'), unlisted.

### 1200 Ezra 4:6-24 The building of the temple is hindered.
- flag: Unlisted echoes in RV1909 words: 'obedecer a Dios antes que a los hombres' (Hch 5:29, RV1909 'obedecer á Dios antes que á los hombres') and 'apartarse de toda especie de mal' (1 Ts 5:22, RV1909 'Apartaos de toda especie de mal').

### 1201 Ezra 5:1-2 The leaders forward the building of the temple.
- flag: The English text opens with stray pieces of an outline ('letter against the Jews.' and '(3-17)', apparently the end of the previous heading, e.g. 'The adversaries' letter against the Jews', and the next section's verses); kept and translated as they stand, paragraph breaks included.
- flag: 'his word, which he magnifies above all his name' echoes Sal 138:2 (KJV; RV1909 'has hecho magnífico tu nombre, y tu dicho sobre todas las cosas'), translated.

### 1202 Ezra 5:3-17 
- quoted (Ezra 5:5): “The elders of the Jews” → “los ancianos de los Judíos”
- flag: Unlisted echoes: 'razón de la esperanza que hay en nosotros' follows 1 P 3:15 (RV1909 'razón de la esperanza que hay en vosotros'), 'para morada de Dios en Espíritu' Ef 2:22 (RV1909).
- flag: Henry says the elders answered 'the Samaritans'; in Esd 5 the letter is from Tatnai and his companions; translated as written.

### 1203 Ezra 6:1-12 The decree for completing the temple.
- quoted (Zech 4:7): “thereof with shoutings, crying, Grace, grace unto it” → “él sacará la primera piedra con aclamaciones de Gracia, gracia á ella”
- flag: Zac 4:7: the RV1909's 'la primera piedra' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'the head-stone' (the top stone that completes the building), so the image of finishing is weaker in Spanish.

### 1204 Ezra 6:13-22 The temple is finished.
- flag: Echoes, translated: Jud 20 ('santísima fe', RV1909), Fil 1:6, He 12:23 ('los espíritus de los justos hechos perfectos', RV1909), Sal 100:2 (RV1909 'Servid á Jehová con alegría').

### 1206 Ezra 7:11-26 The commission to Ezra.
- flag: 'the weapons… are not carnal' echoes 2 Co 10:4; 'bring men into obedience to Christ' 2 Co 10:5; translated.

### 1207 Ezra 7:27-28 Ezra blesses God for his favour.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Fil 2:13: 'en nosotros obra así el querer como el hacer' (RV1909 'el que en vosotros obra así el querer como el hacer'; person changed by Henry).
- flag: 'God has extended mercy to me' adapts Esd 7:28 (KJV 'hath extended mercy unto me'; RV1909 'inclinó hacia mí su misericordia'), translated; 'weak as water' echoes Ez 7:17.

### 1208 Ezra 8:1-20 The companions of Ezra.
- flag: 'los desterrados de Israel… los esparcidos de Judá' follows Is 11:12 (RV1909), unlisted.

### 1211 Ezra 8:31-36 Ezra arrives at Jerusalem.
- flag: 'what shall we render' echoes Sal 116:12; 'Then had the church rest' echoes Hch 9:31; translated.

### 1212 Ezra 9:1-4 Ezra mourns for the Jews' conduct.
- flag: The English has a stray semicolon ('excused, and even pleaded for; by many professors'); the sentence translated as one.

### 1213 Ezra 9:5-15 Ezra's confession of sins.
- quoted (Heb 9:26): “away sin by the sacrifice of himself” → “por el sacrificio de sí mismo”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Lc 18:13: 'Dios, sé propicio á mí pecador' (RV1909); the English 'to me sinner' lacks 'a', which the RV1909 also lacks.
- flag: The English has a typo, 'saddler presage', read as 'sadder'.
- flag: 'sins reach to the heavens' echoes Esd 9:6 (RV1909 'nuestros delitos han crecido hasta el cielo'), translated.

### 1214 Ezra 10:1-5 Ezra encourages to reformation.
- flag: 'Arise, be of good courage' follows Esd 10:4 (RV1909 'Levántate… esfuérzate'), unlisted, with the RV1909's words.
- flag: 'unequally yoked with unbelievers' echoes 2 Co 6:14 (RV1909 'No os juntéis en yugo con los infieles'), translated.

### 1215 Ezra 10:6-14 He assembles the people.
- flag: 'plenteous the redemption' echoes Sal 130:7 (RV1909 'abundante redención'), translated.

### 1216 Ezra 10:15-44 Reformation effected.
- flag: 'quitará… de Jacob la impiedad' follows Ro 11:26 (RV1909 'que quitará de Jacob la impiedad'), unlisted, Henry's 'effectually' inserted.

### 1217 Neh 1:1-11 Nehemiah's distress for the misery of Jerusalem, His prayer.
- quoted (James 5:16): “the effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man” → “la oración del justo, obrando eficazmente”
- flag: Stg 5:16: the RV1909's 'la oración del justo, obrando eficazmente, puede mucho' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'the effectual fervent prayer… availeth much'; his 'avails most' rendered 'es la que más puede'.
- flag: 'the word on which he has caused us to hope' follows Sal 119:49 (RV1909 'en la cual me has hecho esperar'), translated in Henry's person.

### 1218 Neh 2:1-8 Nehemiah's request to the king.
- quoted (Neh 2:4): “prayed to the God of heaven” → “al Dios de los cielos”
- flag: 'trono de la gracia' and 'llegarnos confiadamente' follow He 4:16 (RV1909 'Lleguémonos pues confiadamente al trono de la gracia'), unlisted.
- flag: 'the seed of Jacob never sought… in vain' echoes Is 45:19 (RV1909 'no dije á la generación de Jacob: En vano me buscáis'), translated.

### 1221 Neh 3:1-32 The rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem.
- flag: 'restauró enfrente de su cámara' follows Neh 3:30 (RV1909 'restauró Mesullam… enfrente de su cámara'), unlisted; 'if every one will mend one' (one person, oneself) rendered 'si cada uno se enmienda a sí mismo'.

### 1222 Neh 4:1-6 Opposition of Sanballat and others.
- flag: 'answer these fools according to their folly' echoes Pr 26:4 (RV1909 'Nunca respondas al necio en conformidad á su necedad'), translated; 'tiene ánimo para obrar' follows Neh 4:6 (RV1909 'el pueblo tuvo ánimo para obrar'; Henry's 'have a mind to it').
- flag: 'Sanballat' rendered 'Sanbalat', the usual Spanish form (not in the glossary).

### 1224 Neh 4:16-23 Nehemiah's precautions.
- flag: 'la espada del Espíritu' follows Ef 6:17 (RV1909), unlisted; 'working with one hand, and fighting with the other' echoes Neh 4:17, translated.

### 1225 Neh 5:1-5 The Jews complain of grievances.
- flag: 'by despising the poor they reproach their Maker' echoes Pr 14:31 / 17:5 (RV1909 'afrenta á su Hacedor'); 'judgment without mercy' Stg 2:13 (RV1909 'juicio sin misericordia'); 'putting our souls into their souls' stead' Job 16:4; translated.

### 1226 Neh 5:6-13 Nehemiah redresses the grievances.
- quoted (2Cor 8:9): “though he was rich, yet” → “siendo rico”
- quoted (2Cor 8:9): “through his poverty, might be rich” → “con su pobreza … enriquecidos”
- flag: 2 Co 8:9: the RV1909's words in Henry's order and person ('por amor de nosotros se hizo pobre', 'fuésemos'); the runs 'siendo rico' and 'con su pobreza… enriquecidos' are the RV1909's.

### 1227 Neh 5:14-19 Nehemiah's forbearance.
- flag: 'look upon… for good, according to all they have done for his people' echoes Neh 5:19 (RV1909 'Acuérdate de mí para bien, Dios mío, y de todo lo que hice á este pueblo'), translated; 'a cup of cold water' Mt 10:42.

### 1228 Neh 6:1-9 Sanballat's plot to hinder Nehemiah.
- quoted (Neh 6:9): “now, therefore, O God, strengthen my hands” → “esfuerza pues mis manos, oh Dios”
- flag: Neh 6:9: Henry's 'now, therefore, O God, strengthen my hands' given in the RV1909's order ('Esfuerza pues mis manos, oh Dios').

### 1229 Neh 6:10-14 False prophets try to frighten Nehemiah.
- flag: Henry's 'Should I…' questions echo Nehemiah's 'Should such a man as I flee?' (Neh 6:11, RV1909 '¿Un hombre como yo ha de huir?'); rendered '¿Habría yo…?'.

### 1230 Neh 6:15-19 The wall finished, Treachery of some among the Jews.
- flag: 'sworn to him' echoes Neh 6:18 (KJV 'many in Judah sworn unto him'), translated; 'laid down his life for the sheep' Jn 10:11.

### 1232 Neh 7:5-73 Register of those that first returned.
- flag: 'Every good gift… from above' echoes Stg 1:17 (RV1909 'Toda buena dádiva… es de lo alto'); 'loving this present world' 2 Ti 4:10; translated.

### 1233 Neh 8:1-8 The reading and expounding the law.
- flag: 'oscurecido el consejo con palabras sin sabiduría' follows Job 38:2 (RV1909 'oscurece el consejo con palabras sin sabiduría'; KJV 'without knowledge'), unlisted; 'let slip' echoes He 2:1.

### 1234 Neh 8:9-12 The people called upon to be joyful.
- quoted (Neh 8:9): “when they heard the words of the law” → “oyendo las palabras de la ley”
- flag: 'derramar el alma al hambriento' follows Is 58:10 (RV1909 'si derramares tu alma al hambriento'), unlisted; 'strength… joy in the Lord' echoes Neh 8:10 (RV1909 'el gozo de Jehová es vuestra fortaleza'), translated.

### 1235 Neh 8:13-18 The feast of tabernacles, The joy of the people.
- quoted (Neh 8:14): “They found written in the law” → “hallaron escrito en la ley”
- flag: 'the believer's tabernacle state' (living in tents, as a sojourner) rendered with a short paraphrase: 'el estado del creyente en este mundo, como quien vive en tiendas'.
- flag: The RV1909 calls the booths 'cabañas' (Neh 8:14); 'fiesta de los tabernáculos' is the usual name; 'strangers and pilgrims' echoes He 11:13 (RV1909 'peregrinos y advenedizos').

### 1236 Neh 9:1-3 A solemn fast.
- flag: 'el Espíritu ayuda nuestra flaqueza' follows Ro 8:26 (RV1909), unlisted; Henry's plural 'infirmities' is the KJV's.

### 1237 Neh 9:4-38 Prayer and confession of sin.
- quoted (2Pet 1:21): “spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost” → “hablaron siendo inspirados del Espíritu Santo”
- quoted (Neh 9:17): “A God ready to pardon” → “Dios de perdones”
- quoted (Heb 4:16): “the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need” → “confiadamente al trono de la gracia, para alcanzar misericordia, y hallar gracia para el oportuno socorro”
- quoted (Neh 9:17): “a God ready to pardon” → “Dios de perdones”
- flag: Neh 9:17: the RV1909's 'Dios de perdones' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'a God ready to pardon', so his 'sweet promise' reads as a title of God rather than a readiness.
- flag: 2 P 1:21: the RV1909's 'inspirados' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'moved'; He 4:16: 'el oportuno socorro' for 'help in time of need'; the run starts at 'confiadamente' since Henry's 'come boldly' is the verse's 'Lleguémonos… confiadamente'.

### 1239 Neh 10:32-39 Their engagement to sacred rites.
- flag: 'cease to do evil, but learn to do well' follows Is 1:16-17 (RV1909 'dejad de hacer lo malo: Aprended á hacer bien'), unlisted, in Henry's form.
