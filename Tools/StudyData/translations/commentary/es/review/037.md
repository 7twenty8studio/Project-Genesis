# To review: es commentary batch 037

### 1427 Ps 18:29-50 He gives to God the glory of all his mighty deeds.
- flag: The English is scrambled here (“Learn that we must seek release being made through Christ, shall be rejected. In David the type, we behold out of trouble through Christ. The prayer put up, without reconciliation Jesus our Redeemer, conflicting…”); the intended text is probably “Learn that we must seek release out of trouble through Christ. The prayer put up, without reconciliation being made through Christ, shall be rejected. In David the type, we behold Jesus our Redeemer, conflicting…”. Translated in the order given, nothing dropped or moved; a reviewer may prefer the restored order.

### 1428 Ps 19:1-6 The glory of God's works.
- quoted (Ps 19:1): “declare the glory of God” → “cuentan la gloria de Dios”
- flag: “They have no speech or language, so some read it”: Henry refers to a reading of Ps 19:3 (KJV “There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard”); the RV1909 reads “No hay dicho, ni palabras, ni es oída su voz”. Translated.
- flag: “the wonderful works of God” (Acts 2:11) rendered after the RV1909's “las maravillas de Dios”.

### 1429 Ps 19:7-10 His holiness and grace as shown in his word.
- quoted (Ps 19:8): “of the Lord are right” → “Los mandamientos de Jehová son rectos”
- quoted (Ps 19:9): “The judgments of the Lord” → “Los juicios de Jehová”
- flag: “The statues of the Lord” is a typo for “statutes”; Ps 19:8 is quoted in the RV1909's words, which call them “mandamientos”. The RV1909 then says “el precepto de Jehová, puro” where the KJV says “the commandment of the LORD is pure”, so Henry's “The commandments of the Lord are pure” is translated “Los preceptos del Señor son puros”.
- flag: “The word translated "law"” refers to Ps 19:7; the RV1909 also has “ley”.
- flag: Grammar slip in the English (“The pleasure of sense soon surfeit”); rendered as meant, in the plural.

### 1430 Ps 19:11-14 Prayer for the benefit of them.
- flag: “Surely in the Lord Jesus have I righteousness and strength” adapts Isa 45:24 (RV1909 “Ciertamente en Jehová está la justicia y la fuerza”); translated in Henry's form.
- flag: “Redeemer or Divine Kinsman” plays on the Hebrew go'el behind Ps 19:14 (RV1909 “redentor mío”); rendered “Redentor o Pariente divino”.

### 1431 Ps 20:1-9 This psalm is a prayer for the kings of Israel, but with relation to Christ.
- quoted (Jer 26:16): “in the name of the Lord our God” → “en nombre de Jehová nuestro Dios”
- quoted (Ps 20:6): “the saving strength of his right hand” → “la fuerza de la salvación de su diestra”
- flag: sensitive: “the pride and power of Jewish unbelief, and pagan idolatry” translated in full (“la incredulidad judía y de la idolatría pagana”).
- flag: Quote 0 is matched to Jer 26:16; Henry's phrase echoes Ps 20:5, 7 (RV1909 “alzaremos pendón en el nombre de nuestro Dios”; “del nombre de Jehová nuestro Dios”). The words used are in both.
- flag: “risen-from the dead” has a stray hyphen in the English; ignored.

### 1432 Ps 21:1-6 Thanksgiving for victory.
- quoted (Ps 21:6): “thou hast made him exceeding glad” → “llenástelo de alegría con tu rostro”
- flag: Henry explains the KJV word “prevented” (Ps 21:3 “thou preventest him with the blessings of goodness”, = went before); the RV1909 has “le has salido al encuentro con bendiciones de bien”. Rendered “se nos anticipó, o fue delante de nosotros”; “blessings of goodness” = RV1909 “bendiciones de bien”.
- flag: The English reads “God kingdom” (possessive missing); rendered “el reino de Dios”.
- flag: Ps 21:6: KJV “made him exceeding glad with thy countenance”, RV1909 “llenástelo de alegría con tu rostro”; Henry's following “countenance thou hast given to his undertaking” (= favour) rendered “con el favor que has mostrado a su empresa”.

### 1433 Ps 21:7-13 Confidence of further success.
- flag: The last sentence begins with a lower-case “yet” after an exclamation in the English; kept so (“y él se deleita”).

### 1434 Ps 22:1-10 Complaints of discouragement.
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow” → “las aflicciones que habían de venir á Cristo, y las glorias después de ellas”
- quoted (Ps 22:1): “Why hast thou forsaken me” → “por qué me has dejado”
- flag: “To cry our” is a typo for “To cry out”; rendered “Clamar”.
- flag: 1 Pet 1:11: KJV “the glory that should follow”, RV1909 “las glorias después de ellas”; quoted.
- flag: Ps 22:1: KJV “forsaken”, RV1909 “dejado”.

### 1435 Ps 22:11-21 With prayer for deliverance.
- quoted (Phil 3:10): “the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings” → “la virtud de su resurrección, y la participación de sus padecimientos”
- flag: Unlisted echoes in the RV1909's words: Ps 22:16 (“horadaron mis manos y mis pies”, here in Henry's third person “Horadaron sus manos y sus pies”), Luke 24:46 (“Así está escrito, y así fué necesario que el Cristo padeciese”, Henry's “therefore” kept) and 2 Cor 1:10 (“nos libró, y libra … nos librará”).
- flag: Phil 3:10: KJV “power … fellowship”, RV1909 “virtud … participación”; quoted.

### 1436 Ps 22:22-31 Praises for mercies and redemption.
- quoted (Ps 22:30): “A seed shall serve him” → “La posteridad le servirá”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”
- flag: Ps 22:30: KJV “A seed”, RV1909 “La posteridad”; quoted. Its “será ella contada por una generación de Jehová” differs from Henry's “accounted to him for a generation”, translated.
- flag: Unlisted echoes: Ps 22:29 (RV1909 “ninguno puede conservar la vida á su propia alma”, “conservar la vida a nuestra propia alma”) and Rom 10:3 (“procurando establecer la suya propia”).

### 1437 Ps 23:1-6 Confidence in God's grace and care.
- quoted (Ps 23:1): “The Lord is my shepherd” → “es mi pastor”
- quoted (Ps 23:3): “in the paths of righteousness” → “por sendas de justicia”
- quoted (Ps 23:4): “The valley of the shadow of death” → “valle de sombra de muerte”
- flag: Ps 23:1: the RV1909 prints the first word in capitals (“JEHOVÁ es mi pastor”), so the checked run starts at “es mi pastor”; the whole is quoted in Henry's quotation marks.
- flag: “still waters” follows the RV1909's “aguas de reposo” (Ps 23:2); “green pastures” is translated “verdes pastos” (RV1909 “delicados pastos”), keeping Henry's contrast with a “dry pasture”.
- flag: The English has two slips: a fragment “The work of righteousness in peace.” (probably after Isa 32:17 / Jas 3:18), translated as it stands; and a stray full stop in “unless. God lead us”, kept.
- flag: “a king of terrors” echoes Job 18:14 (RV1909 “rey de los espantos”); “the everlasting arms” Deut 33:27 (RV1909 “los brazos eternos”); “goodness and mercy … all the days of their lives” Ps 23:6 (RV1909 “el bien y la misericordia me seguirán todos los días de mi vida”).

### 1438 Ps 24:1-6 Concerning the kingdom of Christ, and the subjects of that kingdom.
- quoted (Mark 10:17): “What shall I do, that I may” → “qué haré para”
- quoted (Ps 24:5): “blessing from the Lord, and righteousness from the God” → “bendición de Jehová, y justicia del Dios de”
- flag: Ps 24:5: the RV1909 has “del Dios de salud”; Henry's “of our salvation” is kept after the quoted run (“del Dios de nuestra salvación”).
- flag: Quote 0 is matched to Mark 10:17; Henry's question rather echoes Ps 24:3 (RV1909 “¿Quién subirá al monte de Jehová? ¿y quién estará en el lugar de su santidad?”). Only the common words “qué haré para” are the verse's.
- flag: “the earth and the fulness thereof” rendered after the RV1909's Ps 24:1 (“la tierra y su plenitud”).

### 1439 Ps 24:7-10 Concerning the King of that kingdom.
- flag: Echoes of Ps 24:8 (RV1909 “Jehová el fuerte y valiente, Jehová el poderoso en batalla”), Ps 24:7 (“puertas eternas”) and Rev 3:20 (“yo estoy á la puerta y llamo”), translated in Henry's third person.

### 1440 Ps 25:1-7 Confidence in prayer.
- flag: “wait on God … wait for him” (two senses of the English “wait”) rendered “sirven a Dios … esperan en él”.

### 1441 Ps 25:8-14 Prayer for remission of sins.
- quoted (1Tim 1:15): “came into the world to save sinners” → “vino al mundo para salvar á los pecadores”
- quoted (Ps 25:10): “All the paths of the Lord” → “Todas las sendas de Jehová”
- quoted (Ps 25:10): “All the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth” → “Todas las sendas de Jehová son misericordia y verdad”
- flag: Ps 25:9 “guide in judgment” rendered after the RV1909's “Encaminará á los humildes por el juicio”; “find rest for their souls” after Matt 11:29 (“hallaréis descanso para vuestras almas”).

### 1442 Ps 25:15-22 For help in affliction.
- quoted (Lam 3:26): “wait for the salvation of the Lord” → “esperar callando en la salud de Jehová”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 32:8; Matched to Ps 32:8, but Henry echoes Ps 25:12 (“teach in the way that he shall choose”), turned into a prayer to God; the RV1909's Ps 32:8 (“el camino en que debes andar”) and Ps 25:12 (“el camino que ha de escoger”, the man choosing) don't fit his sense.): “thee in the way which thou shalt”
- flag: Lam 3:26: KJV “hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord”, RV1909 “Bueno es esperar callando en la salud de Jehová” (“salud” = salvation); Henry's “hope” kept before the quoted run.
- flag: Unlisted echoes: John 15:5 (“sin mí nada podéis hacer”, here “sin ti nada podemos hacer”) and Jer 23:6 in the RV1909's words (“Jehová, justicia nuestra”, printed in capitals there).

### 1443 Ps 26:1-12 David, in this psalm, appeals to God touching his integrity.
- flag: “We are complete in him” (Col 2:10) rendered after the RV1909's “en él estáis cumplidos”.

### 1444 Ps 27:1-6 The psalmist's faith.
- quoted (Ps 27:5): “the secret of his tabernacle” → “lo reservado de su pabellón”
- flag: Ps 27:5: KJV “the secret of his tabernacle”, RV1909 “lo reservado de su pabellón”; quoted.
- flag: Unlisted echoes in the RV1909's words: Ps 27:1 (“la fortaleza de mi vida”, here “de su vida”), John 8:35 (“el siervo no queda en casa para siempre”) and Luke 10:42 (“una cosa es necesaria”, here “la única cosa necesaria”).

### 1445 Ps 27:7-14 His desire toward God, and expectation from him.
- quoted (Ps 27:13): “see the goodness of the Lord” → “ver la bondad de Jehová”
- flag: Henry's quoted call and answer (Ps 27:8 “Seek ye my face … I will seek it”) are given in the RV1909's words (“Buscad mi rostro”, “Tu rostro buscaré”); Henry's “I will seek it” becomes “Tu rostro buscaré”.
- flag: Unlisted echoes in the RV1909's words: Jonah 2:8 (“vanidades ilusorias”) and Ps 110:3 (“de buena voluntad en el día de tu poder”, here “de su poder”).

### 1446 Ps 28:1-5 A prayer in distress.
- quoted (Ps 28:5): “the works of the Lord” → “las obras de Jehová”
- flag: “God is my rock” (Ps 28:1 KJV “O LORD my rock”) is translated; the RV1909 has “oh Jehová, fortaleza mía”.

### 1447 Ps 28:6-9 Thanksgiving for deliverance.
- quoted (Ps 28:7): “The Lord is my strength” → “Jehová es mi fortaleza”
- quoted (Ps 28:9): “lift them up for ever” → “ensálzalos para siempre”
- flag: The English reads “we have the less benefit from the light of the sun, nor from the light of God's countenance, for others' sharing therein”, apparently for “none the less benefit”; rendered as meant (“no tenemos menos beneficio … porque otros la compartan”).
- flag: Ps 28:9: Henry's “lift them up for ever” is given as the RV1909's prayer (“ensálzalos para siempre”), introduced by “También:”.

### 1448 Ps 29:1-11 Exhortation to give glory to God.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 29:2; Ps 29:2: the RV1909 has “humillaos á Jehová en el glorioso santuario” where the KJV has “worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness”; Henry's point about the beauty of holiness would be lost, so it is translated.): “in the beauty of holiness”
- quoted (Ps 29:2): “the Lord the glory due” → “á Jehová la gloria debida á su nombre”
- flag: “in repentance faith, and love” lacks a comma in the English; rendered as a list.
- flag: “the nature world” is a slip for “the natural world”; rendered “el mundo natural”.
- flag: “O Lord our God, thou art very great!” echoes Ps 104:1 (RV1909 “Jehová, Dios mío, mucho te has engrandecido”); translated in Henry's form.
- flag: “fled for refuge to the hope set before us” (Heb 6:18) rendered after the RV1909's “nos acogemos á trabarnos de la esperanza propuesta”.
- flag: Glossary gives Noah as “Noé (person); Noa (person)”; this is Noah of the ark, “Noé”.

### 1450 Ps 30:6-12 Others encouraged by his example.
- flag: “there is none of that device or working in the grave” echoes Eccl 9:10 (KJV “no work, nor device”), rendered after the RV1909's “en el sepulcro … no hay obra, ni industria”.

### 1451 Ps 31:1-8 Confidence in God.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 31:7; Henry turns Ps 31:7 into the third person (“he will be glad and rejoice in it”); the RV1909's first person “Me gozaré y alegraré” can't stand there.): “will be glad and rejoice”
- flag: “The redemption of the soul is so precious, that it must have ceased for ever” echoes Ps 49:8 (KJV “the redemption of their soul is precious, and it ceaseth for ever”; RV1909 “la redención de su vida es de gran precio, y no se hará jamás”); translated.
- flag: “though the outward man perish, the inward man” echoes 2 Cor 4:16; translated.

### 1452 Ps 31:9-18 Prayer in trouble.
- quoted (1Pet 2:15): “to silence the ignorance of foolish men” → “callar la ignorancia de los hombres vanos”
- flag: Unlisted echo of Isa 53:3 in the RV1909's words (“varón de dolores, experimentado en quebranto”).
- flag: “for his mercies's sake” has a slip in the English possessive; rendered “por amor de su misericordia”.
- flag: “The way of man is not in himself” (Jer 10:23 KJV) is translated; the RV1909 reads “el hombre no es señor de su camino”.
- flag: 1 Pet 2:15: KJV “foolish men”, RV1909 “hombres vanos”; quoted.

### 1453 Ps 31:19-24 Praise for God's goodness.

- flag: “cut off from before the eyes of the Lord” rendered after the RV1909's Ps 31:22 (“Cortado soy de delante de tus ojos”).
