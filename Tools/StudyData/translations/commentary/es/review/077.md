# To review: es commentary batch 077

### 3315 John 2:1-11 The miracle at Cana.
- quoted (Luke 21:34): “overcharged with surfeiting and drunkenness” → “cargados de glotonería y embriaguez”
- flag: "creature-comforts" (the good things of created life) rendered "los consuelos de las criaturas"; "make them comforts indeed" read as "makes".
- flag: "it is to profit withal" (an echo of 1 Cor 12:7, KJV) translated, not quoted.

### 3316 John 2:12-22 Christ casts the buyers and sellers out of the temple.
- quoted (John 2:19): “In three days I will raise it up” → “en tres días lo levantaré”
- quoted (John 2:22): “was risen from the dead, his disciples remembered” → “resucitó de los muertos, sus discípulos se acordaron que había dicho esto”
- flag: "Destroy ye this temple" (unlisted part of John 2:19) given in the RV1909's words, "Destruid este templo".
- flag: Source typo "he has said this" read as "he had said this" (John 2:22), so the RV1909's "que había dicho esto" is used.

### 3318 John 3:1-21 Christ's discourse with Nicodemus.
- quoted (John 3:8): “The wind bloweth where it listeth” → “El viento de donde quiere sopla”
- quoted (1Cor 2:14): “the things of the Spirit of God” → “las cosas que son del Espíritu de Dios”
- quoted (John 3:16): “God so loved the world” → “De tal manera amó Dios al mundo”
- quoted (2Cor 5:19): “God was in Christ reconciling the world” → “Dios estaba en Cristo reconciliando el mundo á sí”
- translated, not quoted (Rom 5:5; Henry changes the person ("in his heart" for Rom 5:5's "in our hearts"); the RV1909's "El amor de Dios está derramado" is kept within it.): “The love of God is shed abroad”
- flag: Source reads "Something is wrong, whereby such a life begins"; read as "Something is wrought" (a likely typo) and translated "Algo es obrado".
- flag: "shapen in sin" / "shapen in iniquity" echo Ps 51:5; translated ("formados en pecado", "formados en maldad", close to the RV1909's "en maldad he sido formado") rather than quoted, since Henry changes the person.
- flag: "foolishness to the natural man" (1 Cor 2:14): the RV1909 says "hombre animal"; Henry's "natural man" translated "hombre natural".
- flag: "there is no salvation in any other" (Acts 4:12): the RV1909 reads "en ningún otro hay salud"; translated with Henry's "salvación".
- flag: "he that believeth in Christ is not condemned", "condemned already", "lest their deeds should be reproved" (John 3:18, 20) adapted by Henry; translated, not quoted.
- flag: Closing quotation (in Henry's quotation marks) blends Matt 7:23 and Luke 13:27; translated close to the RV1909 of Matt 7:23 ("apartaos de mí… obradores de maldad") with Henry's "no os conozco".
- flag: "ver. (11-13)" kept with its parentheses as "v. (11-13)".
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 3319 John 3:22-36 The baptism of John of Christ John's testimony.

### 3321 John 4:4-26 His discourse with the Samaritan woman.
- quoted (John 4:6): “sat thus on the well” → “así se sentó á la fuente”
- quoted (John 4:42): “the Christ, the Saviour of the world” → “el Salvador del mundo”
- flag: John 4:42 in the RV1909 reads "el Salvador del mundo, el Cristo"; Henry's order "the Christ, the Saviour of the world" kept, quoting "el Salvador del mundo".
- flag: "Give it me… that I come not hither to draw" (John 4:15) and "I that speak to thee, am He" (John 4:26) translated: Henry shortens and adapts them (the RV1909's John 4:26 reads "Yo soy, que hablo contigo").
- flag: "Moderate men of all sides are men wondered at" echoes Zech 3:8 / Isa 8:18 ("men wondered at"); translated.
- flag: sensitive: 3321 — "the anger of his own nation against the Samaritans", "an alien and a hostile Samaritan".

### 3322 John 4:27-42 The effects of Christ's conversation with the woman of Samaria.
- quoted (John 4:42): “know that this is indeed the Christ” → “sabemos que verdaderamente éste es … el Cristo”
- quoted (John 4:42): “we have heard him ourselves” → “porque nosotros mismos hemos oído”
- flag: John 4:42 in the RV1909 reads "sabemos que verdaderamente éste es el Salvador del mundo, el Cristo"; Henry's shorter "this is indeed the Christ" quoted as two runs of it.
- flag: "where he records his name" echoes Ex 20:24 (KJV); translated "donde él hace memoria de su nombre".

### 3323 John 4:43-54 Christ heals the nobleman's son.
- flag: Title "nobleman's son": the RV1909 calls him "un señor del rey" (John 4:46); title rendered "el hijo del oficial del rey", and Henry's "nobleman" in the text "noble".

### 3324 John 5:1-9 The cure at the pool of Bethesda.

### 3329 John 5:39-44

- flag: "make a fair show in the flesh" (Gal 6:12 KJV) translated; the RV1909 reads "agradar en la carne".
- flag: sensitive: 3329 — the Jews' study of Scripture "with a view to their own glory".
