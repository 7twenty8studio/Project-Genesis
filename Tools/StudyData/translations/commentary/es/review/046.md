# To review: es commentary batch 046

### 1742 Prov 4:1-13 Exhortation to the study of wisdom.
- flag: 'Him who has the words of eternal life' echoes Jn 6:68; rendered with the RV1909's 'palabras de vida eterna'.

### 1743 Prov 4:14-27 Cautions against bad company, Exhortation to faith and holiness.
- quoted (Prov 4:23): “Keep thy heart with all diligence” → “Sobre toda cosa guardada guarda tu corazón”
- quoted (Prov 4:23): “out of it are the issues of life” → “porque de él mana la vida”
- quoted (Prov 4:24): “a froward mouth and perverse lips” → “la perversidad de la boca … la iniquidad de labios”
- flag: Pr 4:23: Henry's 'issues of life' becomes the RV1909's 'de él mana la vida'; his following 'issuing forth unto everlasting life' rendered 'que brota para vida eterna'.

### 1744 Prov 5:1-14 Exhortations to wisdom. The evils of licentiousness.
- flag: 'Lead us not into temptation' is an unlisted quotation of Mt 6:13; rendered with the RV1909's 'Y no nos metas en tentación'.

### 1745 Prov 5:15-23 Remedies against licentiousness, The miserable end of the wicked.
- quoted (Prov 5:21): “are before the eyes of the Lord” → “están ante los ojos de Jehová”
- flag: 'ponders all his goings' (Pr 5:21) rendered with the RV1909's 'considera todas sus veredas' (not listed as a quotation).

### 1750 Prov 7:1-5 Invitations to learn wisdom.
- flag: 'the apple of the eye' (Pr 7:2) is the RV1909's 'las niñas de tus ojos'; translated 'la niña de los ojos'.

### 1753 Prov 8:12-21 The nature and riches of Wisdom.
- quoted (Col 2:3): “all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge” → “todos los tesoros de sabiduría y conocimiento”
- flag: 'Man found out many inventions' echoes Ec 7:29 (RV1909 'ellos buscaron muchas cuentas'); translated 'halló muchas invenciones'.

### 1754 Prov 8:22-31 Christ one with the Father, in the creation of the world, and rejoicing in his work for the salvation of man.
- quoted (1John 4:14): “to be the Saviour of the world” → “para ser Salvador del mundo”

### 1756 Prov 9:1-12 The invitations of Wisdom.
- quoted (Mark 2:17): “came, not to call the righteous, but sinners” → “á llamar á los justos, sino á los pecadores”
- flag: Henry's 'mansions in heaven' (Jn 14:2, KJV) rendered with the RV1909's word there, 'moradas'.

### 1759 Prov 10:2-3 
- quoted (Prov 10:3): “the Lord will not suffer” → “Jehová no dejará”
- flag: Pr 10:3: the RV1909 continues 'no dejará hambrear el alma del justo'; only 'Jehová no dejará' is Henry's quotation, the rest is his own ('suffer him to want what is needful for spiritual life').

### 1760 Prov 10:4-4 
- flag: 'fervent in spirit, serving the Lord' echoes Ro 12:11 (RV1909 'ardientes en espíritu; sirviendo al Señor'); translated.

### 1770 Prov 10:14-14 
- flag: 'who is made of God unto us wisdom' echoes 1 Co 1:30; rendered with the RV1909's 'nos ha sido hecho por Dios sabiduría'.

### 1776 Prov 10:20-21 
- quoted (Prov 10:20): “The tongue of the just” → “La lengua del justo”
- flag: 'Fools die for want of a heart, so the word is' refers to the Hebrew (KJV 'for want of wisdom', margin 'heart'); the RV1909 has 'por falta de entendimiento'. Henry's 'heart' kept ('corazón').

### 1782 Prov 10:27-28 
- flag: 'What man is he that loves life?' echoes Sal 34:12 (RV1909 '¿Quién es el hombre que desea vida…?'); translated.
