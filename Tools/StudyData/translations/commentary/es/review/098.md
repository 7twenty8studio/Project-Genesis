# To review: es commentary batch 098

### 3938 1Pet 4:1-6 The consideration of Christ's sufferings is urged for purity and holiness.
- flag: "conversation" (old sense, conduct) rendered "conducta"
- flag: "Six sins are here mentioned": 1 P 4:3 in the RV1909 lists six as well ("lascivias, concupiscencias, embriagueces, glotonerías, banquetes, y abominables idolatrías")

### 3939 1Pet 4:7-11 And the approaching end of the Jewish state, as a reason for sobriety, watchfulness, and prayer.
- flag: "love covers" / "charity will cover" refer to 1 P 4:8 (RV1909 "la caridad cubrirá multitud de pecados"); Henry's "love" and "charity" kept as "amor" and "caridad"

### 3940 1Pet 4:12-19 Believers encouraged to rejoice and glory in reproaches and sufferings for Christ, and to commit their souls to the care of a faithful God.
- flag: "the righteous are scarcely saved" (1 P 4:18) given with the RV1909's "el justo con dificultad se salva" (singular)

### 3941 1Pet 5:1-4 Elders exhorted and encouraged.
- quoted (1Pet 5:1): “of the glory that shall be revealed” → “la gloria que ha de ser revelada”
- flag: sensitive: "He does not claim power to rule over all pastors and churches" is aimed at the papal claim for Peter (unnamed)
- flag: "God's heritage or clergy" turns on the Greek kleros behind KJV 1 P 5:3 "God's heritage" (RV1909 "las heredades del Señor"); translated "heredad o clero de Dios"
- flag: "chief Shepherd" (1 P 5:4) given with the RV1909's "Príncipe de los pastores"; "the flock of God" (1 P 5:2) with "la grey de Dios"

### 3942 1Pet 5:5-9 Younger Christians are to submit to their elders, and to yield with humility and patience to God, and to be sober, watchful, and stedfast in faith.
- flag: "Cast 'all you care'" (sic; KJV 1 P 5:7 "all your care"), in Henry's quotation marks, given with the RV1909's "toda vuestra solicitud"
- flag: "in due time" (1 P 5:6; RV1909 "cuando fuere tiempo") translated; "stedfast ... in faith" (1 P 5:9) RV1909 "firmes en la fe"

### 3943 1Pet 5:10-14 Prayers for their growth and establishment.
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's four verbs of 1 P 5:10 ("make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle"); given with the RV1909's "perfeccione, confirme, corrobore y establezca"

### 3944 2Pet 1:1-11 Exhortations to add the exercise of various other graces to faith.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ro 5:3 ("la tribulación produce paciencia")
- flag: the source passes from godliness straight to "with tender affection to all fellow Christians" (brotherly kindness, 2 P 1:7, unnamed); translated as written

### 3945 2Pet 1:12-15 The apostle looks forward to his approaching decease.
- flag: "every wind of doctrine" (Ef 4:14) given with the RV1909's "todo viento de doctrina"; "tabernacle" (2 P 1:13) RV1909 "tabernáculo"

### 3946 2Pet 1:16-21 And confirms the truth of the gospel, relating to Christ's appearing to judgment.
- flag: "worlding" read as "worldling"
- flag: "their hearts burn within them" (Lc 24:32) and "almost persuaded to be Christians" (Hch 26:28; RV1909 "Por poco me persuades á ser Cristiano") translated in Henry's wording

### 3947 2Pet 2:1-9 Believers are cautioned against false teachers, and the certainty of their punishment shown from examples.
- quoted (John 14:6): “the Way, the Truth, and the Life” → “el camino, y la verdad, y la vida”
- quoted (2Pet 2:9): “the day of judgment, to be punished” → “para ser atormentados en el día del juicio”
- flag: typo read as meant: full stop missing after "if we mark them"
- flag: "the wrath of God abides upon them" echoes Jn 3:36 (RV1909 "la ira de Dios está sobre él"); 2 P 2:9 "to be punished" is RV1909 "para ser atormentados" (kept in the quotation)

### 3948 2Pet 2:10-16 An account of these seducers, as exceedingly wicked.
- quoted (2Cor 10:5): “every thought to the obedience of Christ” → “todo intento á la obediencia de Cristo”
- flag: 2 Co 10:5 "bring into captivity every thought" is RV1909 "cautivando todo intento"; Henry's "bring" given as "llevar cautivo" before the quotation

### 3949 2Pet 2:17-22 But as making high pretences to liberty and purity.
- flag: "the mist of darkness" (KJV 2 P 2:17) given with the RV1909's "la oscuridad de las tinieblas"; "darken counsel by words" echoes Job 38:2 (RV1909 "oscurece el consejo con palabras")
- flag: "Believe in the Lord Jesus, and thou shalt be saved" (Hch 16:31; RV1909 "Cree en el Señor Jesucristo, y serás salvo tú, y tu casa") translated in Henry's shorter wording

### 3950 2Pet 3:1-4 The design here is to remind of Christ's final coming to judgement.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 55:19; Henry reads KJV "they have no changes" as "they see no changes"; RV1909 "no se mudan, ni temen á Dios" (they do not change) would lose his point): “no changes, therefore they fear not God”

### 3951 2Pet 3:5-10 He will appear unexpectedly, when the present frame of nature will be dissolved by fire.
- quoted (2Pet 3:7): “The heavens and the earth” → “los cielos que son ahora, y la tierra”
- flag: typo read as meant: "wordly-minded" (worldly-minded)
- flag: "slackness", "long-suffering ... to us-ward" (KJV 2 P 3:9; RV1909 "tardanza", "es paciente para con nosotros") translated "tardanza", "longanimidad ... para con nosotros"

### 3952 2Pet 3:11-18 From thence is inferred the need for holiness, and stedfastness in the faith.
- quoted (2Pet 3:13): “look for new heavens and a new earth” → “cielos nuevos y tierra nueva”
- quoted (2Pet 3:16): “things hard to be understood” → “difíciles de entender”
- quoted (2Pet 2:20): “the knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ” → “el conocimiento del Señor y Salvador Jesucristo”
- flag: "He is faithful, who has promised" (He 10:23) given with the RV1909's "fiel es el que prometió" word order ("Fiel es el que ha prometido")
- flag: source reasoning is odd ("before we can heartily receive all the truths of the gospel, therefore we are in great danger of rejecting the truth"; perhaps words are missing, e.g. "without these"); translated as written

### 3953 1John 1:1-4 The apostle prefaces his epistle to believers in general, with evident testimonies to Christ, for promoting their happiness and joy.
- quoted (1John 1:3): “fellowship is with the Father” → “comunión verdaderamente es con el Padre”
- flag: "the Word of life" (1 Jn 1:1; RV1909 "el Verbo de vida") and "the Word of God" as "el Verbo"

### 3954 1John 1:5-10 The necessity of a life of holiness, in order to communion with God, is shown.
- quoted (Rev 1:5, Rev 1:6): “us from our sins in his own blood” → “de nuestros pecados con su sangre”
- flag: Ap 1:5 "in his own blood" is RV1909 "con su sangre" (kept in the quotation)

### 3955 1John 2:1-2 The apostle directs to the atonement of Christ for help against sinful infirmities.
- quoted (1John 2:1): “have an Advocate with the Father” → “abogado tenemos para con el Padre”
- flag: typo read as meant: "When have an Advocate" (We have)
- flag: "the Righteous One", in Henry's quotation marks, given as "el Justo" (RV1909 1 Jn 2:1 "á Jesucristo el justo")

### 3956 1John 2:3-11 The effects of saving knowledge in producing obedience, and love to the brethren.
- flag: "The love of God is perfected in him that keeps his commandments" echoes 1 Jn 2:5 (RV1909 "la caridad de Dios está verdaderamente perfecta en él"); Henry's wording translated
- flag: "Where spiritual darkness dwells, in mind, the judgment, and the conscience": the source seems to lack "the" before "mind"; translated as one list

### 3957 1John 2:12-14 Christians addressed as little children, young men, and fathers.
- flag: "the wicked one" (1 Jn 2:13-14) given with the RV1909's "el maligno"; "strait gate" (Mt 7:13) with "la puerta estrecha"
- flag: "But especially young men in Christ Jesus, though ..." has no main verb in the source (to be written unto, from the sentence before); translated as it stands

### 3958 1John 2:15-17 All are cautioned against the love of this world, and against errors.
- quoted (1John 2:16): “The lust of the flesh” → “La concupiscencia de la carne”
- quoted (1John 2:16): “The lust of the eyes” → “La concupiscencia de los ojos”
- flag: "The pride of life" (same verse, 1 Jn 2:16, not listed) given with the RV1909's "La soberbia de la vida"

### 3959 1John 2:18-23 
- quoted (1John 2:20): “unction from the Holy One” → “la unción del Santo”
- flag: typo read as meant: "their names expresses this" (their name, Christians, from Christ, the Anointed)
- flag: "the father of lies" (Jn 8:44) given with the RV1909's "padre de mentira"

### 3960 1John 2:24-29 They are encouraged to stand fast in faith and holiness.
- flag: "little children", in Henry's quotation marks, given with the RV1909's "hijitos" (1 Jn 2:28)
- flag: "holding the truth in unrighteousness" (Ro 1:18) given with the RV1909's "detienen la verdad con injusticia" ("detener")
