# To review: es commentary batch 078

### 3346 John 8:1-11 The Pharisees and the adulteress.
- flag: "Go, and sin no more" (John 8:11, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "vete, y no peques más"; the second time with Henry's "then" ("Vete, pues, y no peques más").

### 3347 John 8:12-16 Christ's discourse with the Pharisees.
- quoted (John 8:12): “the Light of the world” → “la luz del mundo”
- quoted (Col 1:15): “is the image of the invisible God” → “es la imagen del Dios invisible”
- translated, not quoted (John 8:12; Henry puts it in the plural ("Those who follow Christ shall not walk"); "no andarán en tinieblas" follows the RV1909's "no andará en tinieblas".): “shall not walk in darkness”

### 3348 John 8:17-20 
- flag: "My times are in thy hand" (Ps 31:15, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "En tu mano están mis tiempos".

### 3349 John 8:21-29 
- flag: "over all, God blessed for evermore" (Rom 9:5, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "Dios sobre todas las cosas, bendito por los siglos".
- flag: "Will not this suffice?" is the objectors' question, as Henry gives it; translated as it stands.
- flag: sensitive: 3349 — "The Jews belonged to this present evil world".

### 3353 John 8:48-53 
- flag: "shall by no means see death for ever" (John 8:51, unlisted): "verá muerte para siempre" are the RV1909's words.
- flag: Henry's reference Ex 14:13 ("ye shall see them again no more for ever") kept as given.

### 3354 John 8:54-59 
- quoted (2Thess 1:8): “obey not the gospel” → “ni obedecen al evangelio”
- quoted (John 8:58): “Before Abraham was, I AM” → “Antes que Abraham fuese, yo soy”
- quoted (Rev 13:8): “the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world” → “Cordero, el cual fué muerto desde el principio del mundo”
- flag: Henry stresses "I AM" in capitals; the RV1909's John 8:58 has "yo soy" in lower case, kept verbatim; his "I AM, is the name of God" (Ex 3:14) rendered "YO SOY", as the RV1909 prints it there.
- flag: "Wisdom, Righteousness, Sanctification, and Redemption" (1 Cor 1:30): Henry's "Righteousness" translated "Justicia"; the RV1909 there reads "justificación".

### 3355 John 9:1-7 Christ give sight to one born blind.
- flag: Source title reads "Christ give sight"; read as "gives".

### 3356 John 9:8-12 The account given by the blind man.
- flag: Henry's echo of John 3:8 ("thou hearest the sound… whence it comes, nor whither it goes", unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "oyes su sonido; mas ni sabes de dónde viene, ni á dónde vaya".

### 3357 John 9:13-17 The Pharisees question the man that had been blind.
- quoted (1Pet 2:15): “to silence the ignorance of foolish men” → “callar la ignorancia de los hombres vanos”
- flag: 1 Pet 2:15: Henry's "foolish men" is "hombres vanos" in the RV1909.
- flag: sensitive: 3357 — Christ healing "in such a manner as would give offence to the Jews".

### 3358 John 9:18-23 They ask concerning him.
- quoted (2Tim 3:7): “come to the knowledge of the truth” → “llegar al conocimiento de la verdad”
- flag: "The fear of man brings a snare" (Prov 29:25, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "El temor del hombre pondrá lazo" (future tense in the RV1909).

### 3359 John 9:24-34 They cast him out.
- quoted (John 9:25): “Whereas I was blind, now I see” → “habiendo yo sido ciego, ahora veo”

### 3360 John 9:35-38 Christ's words to the man that had been blind.
- flag: Source reads "such a suffer"; read as "such as suffer".

### 3361 John 9:39-41 He reproves the Pharisees.
- quoted (John 9:39): “see might be made blind” → “los que ven, sean cegados”

### 3362 John 10:1-5 The parable of the good shepherd.
- flag: "the sheep of his pasture" (Ps 100:3) and "The great Shepherd of the sheep" (Heb 13:20), unlisted, given in the RV1909's words ("ovejas de su prado", "el gran pastor de las ovejas").

### 3364 John 10:10-18 Christ the good Shepherd.
- flag: "Lo, I come" (Heb 10:7 / Ps 40:7, unlisted): the RV1909's Heb 10:7 says "Heme aquí", used here.

### 3367 John 10:31-38 The Jews attempt to stone Jesus.
- flag: "over all, God blessed for evermore" (Rom 9:5, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words.
- flag: "by searching find out to perfection" echoes Job 11:7 (KJV); translated (the RV1909 reads differently).

### 3368 John 10:39-42 He departs from Jerusalem.
- flag: Source typo "our" for "out" twice ("deliver the godly our of their temptations", "drive… our of their own city"); read as "out".
- flag: "No weapon formed against… shall prosper" (Isa 54:17), "deliver the godly out of… temptations" (2 Pet 2:9) and "make a way… to escape" (1 Cor 10:13) translated; the RV1909 words them differently.

### 3369 John 11:1-6 The sickness of Lazarus.
- quoted (John 11:5): “Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus” → “Amaba Jesús á Marta, y á su hermana, y á Lázaro”

### 3370 John 11:7-10 Christ returns to Judea.
- quoted (Eph 2:2): “according to the course of this world” → “conforme á la condición de este mundo”
- quoted (John 11:10): “because there is no light in him” → “porque no hay luz en él”
- flag: Last sentence lacks a verb in the English ("that which light about us [is] to our natural actions"); translated with it supplied.
- flag: Eph 2:2: Henry's "the course of this world" is "la condición de este mundo" in the RV1909.

### 3371 John 11:11-16 The death of Lazarus.
- flag: "Let us go to him" (John 11:15) translated as Henry's own sentence.

### 3372 John 11:17-32 Christ arrives at Bethany.
- quoted (John 11:20): “Mary sat still in the house” → “María se estuvo en casa”
- quoted (John 11:25): “the Resurrection and the Life” → “la resurrección y la vida”
- flag: "let him do as seemeth him good" (1 Sam 3:18, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "haga lo que bien le pareciere".
- flag: John 11:20: Henry's "sat still" is "se estuvo" in the RV1909, which loses the picture of sitting.

### 3373 John 11:33-46 He raises Lazarus.
- quoted (Phil 2:8): “Being found in fashion as a man, he” → “hallado en la condición como hombre”
- quoted (Isa 53:3): “a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief” → “varón de dolores, experimentado en quebranto”
- flag: "we have not a High Priest who cannot be touched with a feeling of our infirmities" (Heb 4:15, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "no tenemos un Pontífice que no se pueda compadecer de nuestras flaquezas".
- flag: "In all the afflictions of believers he is afflicted" adapts Isa 63:9; translated.

### 3374 John 11:47-53 The Pharisees consult against Jesus.
- flag: "The fear of the wicked shall come upon them" adapts Prov 10:24 (KJV); translated (the RV1909 reads "Lo que el impío teme, eso le vendrá").

### 3375 John 11:54-57 The Jews seek for him.
- flag: "our gospel passover": Henry's figure for the Lord's Supper; translated literally ("nuestra pascua del evangelio").

### 3376 John 12:1-11 Christ anointed by Mary.
- flag: "the oil of gladness above his fellows" (Ps 45:7): "óleo de gozo" is the RV1909's phrase; the rest translated to fit Henry's sentence.
- flag: "The reigning love of money is heart-theft": rendered "robo del corazón" (theft committed in the heart).

### 3378 John 12:20-26 Greeks apply to see Jesus.
- flag: "the hope of glory" (Col 1:27): "la esperanza de gloria" matches the RV1909, though the run wasn't listed.

### 3379 John 12:27-33 A voice from heaven bears testimony to Christ.
- flag: Source typo "was the troubled of Christ's soul"; read as "the trouble".
- flag: "make his soul an offering for our sin" (Isa 53:10, KJV) translated; the RV1909 reads "hubiere puesto su vida en expiación por el pecado".

### 3382 John 12:44-50 Christ's address to them.
- translated, not quoted (John 12:46; The RV1909 has the first person ("Yo la luz he venido al mundo"), which can't stand in Henry's "Him, who came a Light into the world".): “a Light into the world”

### 3383 John 13:1-17 Christ washes the disciples' feet.
- flag: "a peculiar people" (Henry's KJV phrase) rendered "un pueblo propio".
- flag: "We see in the end what was the kindness from events which seemed most cross" is awkward in the English; translated as "the kindness of the events that seemed most adverse".

### 3384 John 13:18-30 The treachery of Judas foretold.
- flag: "lift up the heel against Him" (John 13:18 / Ps 41:9) and "love darkness rather than light" (John 3:19) translated to fit Henry's sentences, close to the RV1909 ("levantó contra mí su calcañar", "amaron más las tinieblas que la luz").
