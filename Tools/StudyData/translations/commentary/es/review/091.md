# To review: es commentary batch 091

### 3747 Eph 3:13-19 He prays for the Ephesians.
- flag: Unlisted echoes: "gracia por gracia" (John 1:16) and "llenos de toda la plenitud de Dios" (Eph 3:19, Henry "filled with the fulness of God") use the RV1909's words; "toda" comes from the RV1909, Henry has no "all".

### 3749 Eph 4:1-6 Exhortations to mutual forbearance and union.
- quoted (Matt 28:19): “in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost” → “en el nombre del Padre, y del Hijo, y del Espíritu Santo”
- flag: "lowliness" (Eph 4:2 KJV) is "humildad" in the RV1909, so Henry's gloss "By lowliness, understand humility" reads "Por humildad de ánimo entended la humildad".

### 3750 Eph 4:7-16 To a due use of spiritual gifts and graces.
- flag: The trailing "(Ep 4:17-24)" is in the source (a pointer to the next section) and is kept as "(Ef 4:17-24)".

### 3751 Eph 4:17-24 To purity and holiness.
- flag: "deceitful lusts" (Eph 4:22 KJV) translated "concupiscencias engañosas"; the RV1909 has "los deseos de error".

### 3752 Eph 4:25-28 And to take heed of the sins practised among the heathen.
- quoted (Eph 4:27): “give place to the devil” → “lugar al diablo”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry's "We give place" for the RV1909's "Ni deis lugar"; "Damos" is translated and "lugar al diablo" quoted.
- flag: "God hates robbery for burnt-offering" (Isa 61:8 KJV) translated in Henry's wording (RV1909: "aborrecedor del latrocinio para holocausto"), not quoted.

### 3753 Eph 4:29-32 
- quoted (Eph 4:32): “Be ye kind one to another” → “Sed los unos con los otros benignos”

### 3754 Eph 5:1-2 Exhortation to brotherly love.
- flag: "be ye followers of God" (Eph 5:1 KJV; RV1909 "Sed, pues, imitadores de Dios") translated, keeping Henry's two words "seguidores... imitadores".

### 3755 Eph 5:3-14 Cautions against several sins.
- quoted (Eph 5:8): “Walk as children of light” → “Andad como hijos de luz”
- flag: The English has a stray full stop ("so far from being profitable. that they pollute"); kept as in the source.
- flag: "Estas obras de las tinieblas son infructuosas" echoes the RV1909's "las obras infructuosas de las tinieblas" (Eph 5:11), not marked as a quotation.

### 3756 Eph 5:15-21 Directions to a contrary behaviour, and to relative duties.
- quoted (Eph 5:18): “be filled with the Spirit” → “llenos de Espíritu”
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909's imperative "sed" can't stand after Henry's "let us seek to be"; "ser" is translated and "llenos de Espíritu" quoted (the RV1909 has no article).
- flag: "Be not unwise" (Eph 5:17 KJV) translated "No seáis imprudentes" (RV1909: "no seáis imprudentes").

### 3758 Eph 6:1-4 The duties of children and parents.
- flag: The English is garbled: "though it may be awfully occasion it" (probably "though it may awfully occasion it"); translated as "aunque puede ser terriblemente la ocasión de ella".

### 3759 Eph 6:5-9 Of servants and masters.
- flag: Henry's "servants" (KJV "servants", RV1909 "siervos") is rendered "siervos"; his remark that slavery should be rooted out by Christianity is kept exactly.

### 3760 Eph 6:10-18 All Christians are to put on spiritual armour against the enemies of their souls.
- quoted (Eph 6:11): “put on the whole armour of God” → “de toda la armadura de Dios”
- quoted (Eph 6:15): “shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace” → “calzados los pies con el apresto del evangelio de paz”
- quoted (Eph 6:17): “the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God” → “la espada del Espíritu … que es la palabra de Dios”
- quoted (Eph 6:10): “in the power of his might” → “en la potencia de su fortaleza”
- flag: Source 'We must preserve in particular requests' read as 'persevere' ('Debemos perseverar').
- flag: Quote 2: the RV1909's semicolon after "Espíritu" is written as a comma.
- flag: "Resist him, and he will flee" (Jas 4:7) and "set on fire of hell" (Jas 3:6) translated; "girdle", "breastplate", "helmet" given in ordinary Spanish (the RV1909 of Eph 6:14-17 has "ceñidos vuestros lomos de verdad... la cota de justicia... el yelmo"); "coraza" used for Henry's "breastplate".
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 3761 Eph 6:19-24 The apostle desires their prayers, and ends with his apostolic blessing.
- quoted (Eph 6:23): “Peace be to the brethren, and love with faith” → “Paz sea á los hermanos y amor con fe”
- quoted (Eph 6:24): “love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity” → “aman á nuestro Señor Jesucristo en sinceridad”

### 3762 Phil 1:1-7 The apostle offers up thanksgivings and prayers, for the good work of grace in the Philippians.
- quoted (Phil 1:6): “the day of Jesus Christ” → “el día de Jesucristo”

### 3763 Phil 1:8-11 He expresses affection, and prays for them.
- flag: "Try things which differ; that we may approve the things which are excellent" follows the KJV margin of Phil 1:10; translated (RV1909: "Para que discernáis lo mejor"). Henry shifts from "Try" to "we"; kept.

### 3766 Phil 1:27-30 Exhortations to zeal, and constancy in professing the gospel.
- quoted (Phil 1:27): “the faith of the gospel” → “la fe del evangelio”
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's word "conversation" (Phil 1:27, Greek "live as citizens"); the RV1909 has the verb "converséis", so "conversación" is kept in his quotation marks.

### 3768 Phil 2:5-11 The example of Christ.
- quoted (Phil 2:6): “Who being in the form of God” → “El cual, siendo en forma de Dios”
- quoted (Phil 2:6): “robbery to be equal with God” → “no tuvo por usurpación ser igual á Dios”
- quoted (Phil 2:8): “the death of the cross” → “muerte de cruz”
- quoted (Phil 2:10): “At the name of Jesus” → “En el nombre de Jesús”
- quoted (Phil 2:11): “to the glory of God the Father” → “á la gloria de Dios Padre”
- quoted (Phil 2:11): “confess that Jesus Christ is Lord” → “que Jesucristo es el Señor”
- quoted (John 5:23): “all men should honour the Son” → “todos honren al Hijo”
- quoted (John 5:23): “as they honour the Father” → “como honran al Padre”
- flag: Quote 1: KJV "thought it not robbery" is "no tuvo por usurpación" in the RV1909; Henry's "had not thought it a robbery" is given with the RV1909's words.
- flag: "the glory he had with the Father before the world was" (John 17:5) translated.

### 3769 Phil 2:12-18 Diligence in the affairs of salvation, and to be examples to the world.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Phil 2:12-13: "Ocupaos en vuestra salvación" and "Dios es el que en vosotros obra" are the RV1909 verbatim (KJV "Work out your salvation... God which worketh in you").
- flag: "blameless and harmless" (Phil 2:15) given with the RV1909's "irreprensibles y sencillos"; the trailing "(Php 2:19-30)" is in the source and kept as "(Fil 2:19-30)".

### 3770 Phil 2:19-30 The apostle's purpose of visiting Philippi.
- flag: "Naturally, that is, sincerely" explains the KJV's "naturally care" (Phil 2:20); the RV1909 has "con sincera afición".

### 3771 Phil 3:1-11 The apostle cautions the Philippians against judaizing false teachers, and renounces his own former privileges.
- translated, not quoted (Phil 3:8; Henry turns the RV1909's first-person "lo he perdido todo" into his own past narrative ("He had suffered the loss of all"); translated "Lo había perdido todo".): “suffered the loss of all”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Isa 56:10 (cited by Henry): "perros mudos" is the RV1909 verbatim.
- flag: KJV "evil-workers" and "the concision" (Phil 3:2) given with the RV1909's "malos obreros" and "el cortamiento"; Henry's gloss on "concision" (they "cut" the church to pieces) still works with "cortamiento".
- flag: "the vilest refuse, offals thrown to dogs" explains the KJV's "dung" (Phil 3:8); the RV1909 has "estiércol".

### 3772 Phil 3:12-21 Expresses earnest desire to be found in Christ; also his pressing on toward perfection; and recommends his own example to other believers.
- quoted (Phil 3:18): “The enemies of the cross of Christ” → “enemigos de la cruz de Cristo”
- flag: "our vile bodies" (Phil 3:21 KJV) translated "nuestros cuerpos viles"; the RV1909 has "el cuerpo de nuestra bajeza".
- flag: "crucifying the flesh with its affections and lusts" (Gal 5:24) and "set his affections upon things above" (Col 3:2) translated.

### 3773 Phil 4:1-1 The apostle exhorts the Philippians to stand fast in the Lord.
- quoted (Phil 4:1): “stand fast in the Lord” → “firmes en el Señor”
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909's "estad así firmes en el Señor" is an imperative; Henry's infinitive "To stand fast" gives "Estar", and "firmes en el Señor" is quoted.
