# To review: es commentary batch 079

### 3386 John 13:36-38 
- quoted (Eph 4:3): “to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace” → “guardar la unidad del Espíritu en el vínculo de la paz”
- quoted (1Pet 1:22): “love one another with a pure heart fervently” → “unos á otros entrañablemente de corazón puro”
- flag: 1 Pet 1:22: the RV1909's imperative "amaos" can't stand in Henry's infinitive, so "amarnos" is translated and the rest of the run ("unos á otros entrañablemente de corazón puro") quoted.
- flag: "without Christ we can do nothing" (John 15:5), "hide pride from them" (Job 33:17) and "walk humbly with our God" (Mic 6:8) translated, not quoted.

### 3387 John 14:1-11 Christ comforts his disciples.
- flag: Henry weighs three English words of John 14:1 (KJV "Let not your heart be troubled"): the RV1909 reads "No se turbe vuestro corazón"; his "troubled", "heart", "your" rendered "turbado", "corazón", "vuestro".
- flag: "mansions" (John 14:2 KJV): the RV1909 has "moradas", used here; Henry's remark that mansions are lasting dwellings is translated as it stands.
- flag: "Believe" (in Henry's quotation marks) given as the RV1909's "Creed" (John 14:1, unlisted); "Father of lights" given as the RV1909's "Padre de las luces" (Jas 1:17).

### 3388 John 14:12-17 He further comforts his disciples.
- flag: "The word used here" is the Greek paraklētos (KJV "Comforter"); the RV1909 has "Consolador" (John 14:16).

### 3389 John 14:18-24 He still further comforts his disciples.
- quoted (John 14:18): “I will not leave you” → “No os dejaré huérfanos”
- quoted (John 14:18): “I will come to you” → “vendré á vosotros”
- flag: "orphans, or fatherless": Henry glosses the word; "huérfanos" is the RV1909's, "o sin padre" his gloss.
- flag: "rest in love to them" echoes Zeph 3:17 (KJV); "faith worketh by love" (Gal 5:6; the RV1909 "la fe que obra por la caridad") translated.

### 3390 John 14:25-27 
- flag: "a remembrancer" (one who reminds) rendered as a phrase, "para que les traiga las cosas a la memoria".

### 3391 John 14:28-31 
- quoted (John 14:30): “the prince of this world” → “el príncipe de este mundo”

### 3392 John 15:1-8 Christ the true Vine.
- flag: The source text opens with a stray fragment, "foretold." and "(18-25)" (left over from Henry's chapter outline, "…persecution foretold. (18-25)"); kept as separate paragraphs and translated ("anunciada.").
- flag: Garbled sentence in the source: "which Christ has promised to forward the sanctification of believers, they will be thankful, for them"; translated as it stands, without mending it.
- flag: "our life is hid with Christ" (Col 3:3) translated; "every good word and work" given as the RV1909's "toda buena palabra y obra" (2 Thess 2:17, unlisted).

### 3393 John 15:9-17 His love to his disciples.
- flag: Source slip "the love of Christ's in our hearts" read as "the love of Christ".

### 3397 John 16:7-15 The promise of the Holy Spirit, and his office.
- quoted (John 16:11): “Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged” → “Y de juicio, por cuanto el príncipe de este mundo es juzgado”
- flag: "all the world is guilty before God" (Rom 3:19 KJV; the RV1909 reads "que todo el mundo se sujete á Dios"), "the end thereof is death" (Rom 6:21) and "two or three are gathered together in his name" (Matt 18:20) translated.

### 3399 John 16:23-27 Encouragement to prayer.
- flag: "the Lord our Righteousness" (Jer 23:6; the RV1909 "JEHOVÁ, JUSTICIA NUESTRA") translated as Henry's title of Christ, "el Señor, justicia nuestra".

### 3400 John 16:28-33 Christ's discoveries of himself.
- flag: "Now are we sure" (John 16:30 KJV, in Henry's quotation marks): the RV1909 reads "Ahora entendemos"; Henry's wording translated.
- flag: "received up into glory" (1 Tim 3:16) and "take heed lest we fall" (1 Cor 10:12) translated.

### 3401 John 17:1-5 Christ's prayer for himself.
- flag: "endured the cross and despised the shame" (Heb 12:2, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "sufrió la cruz, menospreciando la vergüenza".
- flag: "this glory was the end of the sorrow of his soul, and in obtaining it he was fully satisfied" echoes Isa 53:11 (KJV); translated.

### 3402 John 17:6-10 His prayer for his disciples.
- quoted (2Thess 2:13): “salvation, through sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth” → “para salud, por la santificación del Espíritu y fe de la verdad”
- quoted (John 17:10): “All mine are thine, and thine are mine” → “Todas mis cosas son tus cosas, y tus cosas son mis cosas”
- flag: 2 Thess 2:13: Henry's "salvation" is "salud" in the RV1909 (old Spanish for salvation), kept inside the quotation.
- flag: "save to the uttermost, all that come unto God by him" (Heb 7:25, unlisted) given in the RV1909's words, "salvar eternamente á los que por él se allegan á Dios".

### 3403 John 17:11-16 His prayer.
- translated, not quoted (John 17:13; Not a quotation of John 17:13: "the world, that they might" is Henry's own phrase ("removed out of the world, that they might escape").): “the world, that they might”
- translated, not quoted (John 17:15; Henry turns John 17:15 into reported speech ("that the Father would keep them from the evil"); the RV1909's "los guardes del mal" can't stand, so "los guardara del mal" is written.): “keep them from the evil”

### 3416 John 20:11-18 Christ appears to Mary.
