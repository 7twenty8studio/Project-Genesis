# To review: es commentary batch 004

### 85 Gen 20:1-8 Abraham's sojourn at Gerar, Sarah is taken by Abimelech.
- flag: Abimelech written 'Abimelec' (modernized from the RV1909's 'Abimelech', as 'Lamec' in batch 001; not in the Spanish glossary).
- flag: 'shall certainly receive for the wrong which he has done' echoes Col 3:25; translated in Henry's syntax.

### 86 Gen 20:9-13 Abimelech's rebuke to Abraham.
- flag: 'hath not whereof to glory' (Ro 4:2) rendered with the RV1909's 'tiene de qué gloriarse'; 'upon all and unto all them that believe' (Ro 3:22 KJV) translated, since the RV1909 has only 'para todos los que creen en él'; 'he rose early' (Gn 20:8, 'se levantó de mañana').

### 87 Gen 20:14-18 Abimelech restores Sarah.
- flag: 'find the fear of God where we expected it not' answers Gn 20:11 (RV1909 'no hay temor de Dios en este lugar').

### 88 Gen 21:1-8 Birth of Isaac, Sarah's joy.
- quoted (Gen 21:2): “at the set time of which God had spoken” → “en el tiempo que Dios le había dicho”
- flag: 'Who would have said...' echoes Sara's words in Gn 21:7; translated in Henry's syntax.

### 89 Gen 21:9-13 Ishmael mocks Isaac.
- flag: 'Ishmael's conduct was persecution' rests on Gá 4:29 (RV1909 'perseguía'), not quoted by Henry.

### 90 Gen 21:14-21 Hagar and Ishmael are cast forth, They are relieved and comforted by an angel.
- flag: 'a well of water' and 'God was with the lad' echo Gn 21:19-20 (RV1909 'una fuente de agua', 'fué Dios con el muchacho'); in Henry's syntax.
- flag: 'a wild man' alludes to Gn 16:12 (KJV 'a wild man'; RV1909 'hombre fiero').

### 91 Gen 21:22-34 Abimelech's covenant with Abraham.
- quoted (Gen 13:4): “called on the name of the Lord” → “el nombre de Jehová”
- flag: quote 0 is listed at Gn 13:4 but Henry is at Gn 21:33 (RV1909 'é invocó allí el nombre de Jehová Dios eterno'); 'invocó... el nombre de Jehová' fits both, and 'el Dios eterno' follows 21:33.

### 92 Gen 22:1-2 God commands Abraham to offer up Isaac.
- flag: unlisted quotations of Gn 22:2 in the RV1909's words: 'Toma ahora tu hijo', 'tu único', 'á quien amas', 'ofrécelo allí en holocausto' (Henry has no 'there'; the RV1909's 'allí' kept to stay verbatim).
- flag: Henry's remark on the Hebrew (tempt / try / prove one word) holds in Spanish: the RV1909 of Gn 22:1 says 'tentó Dios á Abraham' (KJV 'God did tempt'); 'probó' used in Henry's own sentence.
- flag: source oddity: a full stop is missing after 'secure from trials'; added.

### 93 Gen 22:3-10 Abraham's faith and obedience to the Divine command.
- quoted (Gen 22:8): “My son, God will provide” → “Dios se proveerá… hijo mío”
- quoted (Gen 22:8): “a lamb for a burnt-offering” → “de cordero para el holocausto”
- quoted (John 1:29): “which taketh away the sin of the world” → “que quita el pecado del mundo”
- flag: quotes 0-1 (Gn 22:8): Henry's 'My son, God will provide a lamb...' follows the RV1909's order, with 'hijo mío' at the end.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the RV1909's words: Gn 22:5 'volveremos á vosotros' (KJV 'come again to you') and Gn 22:7 'Padre mío'.

### 94 Gen 22:11-14 Another sacrifice is provided instead of Isaac.
- quoted (John 3:18): “of the only begotten Son of God” → “del unigénito Hijo de Dios”
- flag: 'Jehovah-jireh' (KJV Gn 22:14): the RV1909 translates the name ('Jehová proveerá'); Henry's Hebrew form kept as 'Jehová-jireh' with the RV1909's words as its gloss.
- flag: source oddity: 'But in the mean while God would not...' read as 'since God would not'.
- flag: Moriah written as in the RV1909 ('Moriah'; no Spanish glossary entry).

### 95 Gen 22:15-19 The covenant with Abraham renewed.
- flag: echoes in Henry's syntax: Tit 3:4 ('la bondad de Dios nuestro Salvador'), Gn 22:12 ('no me rehusaste tu hijo, tu único'), Sal 116:12 ('por todos sus beneficios'), 2 Co 5:14-15 ('nos constriñe', 'para aquel que murió y resucitó').
- flag: source oddity: the outline reference '(Ge 22:20-24)' stands at the end of the text; kept there as '(Gn 22:20-24)'.

### 97 Gen 23:1-13 
- flag: no title in the source: the entry's text opens with Henry's chapter outline (two headings with verse numbers, as separate paragraphs); kept as given.
- flag: source oddity: 'loathsome lumps of clays' read as 'clay'; 'because he scorned' read as 'as if he scorned'.

### 98 Gen 23:14-20 
- quoted (Gen 23:16): “current money with the merchant” → “de buena ley entre mercaderes”
- flag: source oddities: 'was Abraham by promise' read as 'Abraham's'; 'in cave. or vault' read as 'in the cave, or vault'.
- flag: 'Dominion is not founded in grace' is a set theological maxim (against the claim that only the godly may rightly own or rule); rendered literally.

### 100 Gen 24:10-28 The journey of Abraham's servant to Mesopotamia, His meeting with Rebekah.
- quoted (Gen 24:16): “filled her pitcher, and came up” → “llenó su cántaro, y se volvía”
- quoted (Gen 24:26): “bowed down his head and worshipped” → “se inclinó, y adoró á Jehová”
- flag: quote 0 (Gn 24:16): the KJV's 'came up' is 'se volvía' in the RV1909.
- flag: quote 1 (Gn 24:26): the RV1909 has no 'his head' and adds 'á Jehová'; its words used.
- flag: 'Our times are in God's hand' echoes Sal 31:15 (RV1909 'En tu mano están mis tiempos'); source oddity: 'the strange man his camels' read as 'and his camels'.
- flag: Rebekah written 'Rebeca' (RV1909; not in the glossary).

### 101 Gen 24:29-53 Rebekah and her relatives consent to her marriage.
- flag: Henry's quoted 'appointed out' (Gn 24:44 KJV 'hath appointed out') rendered with the RV1909's verb 'destinados' (RV1909 'destinó Jehová'); 'proceeds from the Lord' echoes Gn 24:50 ('De Jehová ha salido esto').

### 102 Gen 24:54-67 The happy meeting and marriage of Isaac and rebekah.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 24:60: 'Y bendijeron á Rebeca' (RV1909); 'to forget her own people and her father's house' echoes Sal 45:10 ('olvida tu pueblo, y la casa de tu padre').
- flag: source oddity: 'rebekah' in lower case in the English title; written 'Rebeca'.
- flag: Henry's 'to meditate' (Gn 24:63 KJV) is 'á orar' in the RV1909; Henry's 'meditation and prayer' translated as written.

### 103 Gen 25:1-10 Abraham's family by Keturah, His death and burial.
- quoted (Gen 25:9): “his sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him” → “sepultáronlo Isaac é Ismael sus hijos”
- flag: source oddity: the text opens with an outline heading for Gn 25:29-34 ('Esau despises and sells his birth-right. (29-34)') that belongs to a later section; kept as given.
- flag: 'one hundred years' written in words, as in the English ('cien años'); the digits 175 kept.

### 105 Gen 25:19-26 The birth of Esau and Jacob.
- flag: Rebekah's words (Gn 25:22 KJV 'If it be so, why am I thus?') translated, since Henry's application needs that sense; the RV1909 has 'Si es así ¿para qué vivo yo?'.

### 106 Gen 25:27-28 The different characters of Esau and Jacob.
- quoted (Gen 25:27): “Jacob was a plain man” → “Jacob empero era varón quieto”
- flag: KJV-dependent: 'a plain man' (Gn 25:27) is 'varón quieto' in the RV1909; Henry's gloss (one who liked retirement) fits 'quieto'.

### 107 Gen 25:29-34 
- quoted (Gen 25:34): “rose up and went his way” → “levantóse, y fuése”
- flag: no title in the source; none added.
- flag: Henry's 'Give me some of that red' (Gn 25:30) rendered 'Dame de eso bermejo', close to the RV1909's 'Ruégote que me des á comer de eso bermejo' but not verbatim; please verify.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 25:34: 'Así menospreció Esaú la primogenitura' (RV1909); 'his character as a plain man' follows the RV1909's 'varón quieto' (Gn 25:27).
- flag: echoes in Henry's syntax: 1 Co 12:31 ('procurad los mejores dones'), Job 31:7 ('mi corazón se fué tras mis ojos'), Ro 16:18 ('á sus vientres'); 'I am going towards death' glosses Gn 25:32 (RV1909 'yo me voy á morir').

### 108 Gen 26:1-5 Isaac, because of famine, goes to Gerar.
- quoted (Gen 26:1): “a famine in the land” → “hambre en la tierra”

### 110 Gen 26:12-17 Isaac grows rich, The Philistines' envy.
- flag: 'God blessed Isaac' and 'The Philistines envied Isaac' paraphrase Gn 26:12, 14 (RV1909 'bendíjole Jehová', 'los Filisteos le tuvieron envidia'); 'the wisdom which is from above' echoes Stg 3:17; 'make room for us' echoes Gn 26:22 ('nos ha hecho ensanchar Jehová').

### 111 Gen 26:18-25 Isaac digs wells God blesses him.
- flag: KJV-dependent: Henry gives the wells' names in English ('Contention and Hatred', the KJV margin's sense of Esek and Sitnah); the RV1909 keeps the Hebrew names 'Esek' and 'Sitnah' (Gn 26:20-21).
- flag: source oddity: the title lacks a comma ('Isaac digs wells God blesses him'); written as two items.
- flag: 'study to be quiet' echoes 1 Ts 4:11 (RV1909 'procuréis tener quietud').

### 112 Gen 26:26-33 Abimelech makes a covenant with Isaac.
- quoted (Prov 16:7): “When a man's ways please the Lord, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him” → “Cuando los caminos del hombre son agradables á Jehová, aun á sus enemigos pacificará con él”
- flag: echoes in Henry's syntax: Pr 21:1 (the king's heart in the Lord's hand) and Ro 12:18 ('cuanto está en vosotros... paz con todos los hombres').

### 113 Gen 26:34-35 Esau's wives.
- flag: 'a grief of mind' (Gn 26:35 KJV) rendered with the RV1909's 'amargura de espíritu'.

### 114 Gen 27:1-5 Isaac sends Esau for venison.
- flag: 'the elder should serve the younger' (Gn 25:23) in Henry's indirect speech, close to the RV1909's 'el mayor servirá al menor'.

### 115 Gen 27:6-17 Rebekah teaches Jacob to obtain the blessing.
- quoted (Gen 17:1, Gen 17:2): “walk before me and be thou perfect” → “Yo soy el Dios Todopoderoso; anda delante de mí, y sé perfecto”
- quoted (Gen 27:13): “Upon me be thy curse, my son” → “Hijo mío, sobre mí tu maldición”
- quoted (Gen 27:13): “Upon me be thy curse” → “Sobre mí tu maldición”
- flag: quote 1 (Gn 27:13): the RV1909 puts 'Hijo mío' first; its order kept.

### 116 Gen 27:18-29 Jacob, pretending to be Esau, obtains the blessing.
- flag: 'leanness' (cf. Sal 106:15 KJV 'sent leanness into their soul') rendered 'flaqueza'.

### 117 Gen 27:30-40 Isaac's fear, Esau's importunity.
- quoted (Gen 27:34): “he cried with a great and exceeding bitter cry” → “clamó con una muy grande y muy amarga exclamación”
- quoted (Gen 27:28): “the fatness of the earth” → “las grosuras de la tierra”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 27:33: 'Yo le bendije, y será bendito' (RV1909); 'trembled exceedingly' echoes the same verse ('estremecióse Isaac con grande estremecimiento').
- flag: quote 1: the RV1909's 'las grosuras de la tierra' is at Gn 27:28 (Jacob's blessing); Esau's (27:39) is 'en grosuras de la tierra'. Henry's 'the plunder of the field' translated as written.

### 118 Gen 27:41-46 Esau threatens Jacob's life, Rebekah sends Jacob away.
- quoted (John 4:42): “The Christ, the Saviour of the world” → “el Salvador del mundo, el Cristo”
- flag: quote 0 (Jn 4:42): Henry's 'The Christ, the Saviour of the world' follows the RV1909's order, 'el Salvador del mundo, el Cristo'.
- flag: 'went in the way of Cain' echoes Jud 11 (RV1909 'el camino de Caín'); 'do evil that good may come' Ro 3:8 ('Hagamos males para que vengan bienes').
