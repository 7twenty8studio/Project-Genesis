# To review: es commentary batch 053

### 2422 Isa 40:1-11 
- quoted (Isa 40:2): “of the Lord's hand double for all” → “de la mano de Jehová por todos sus pecados”
- flag: 'the strong man armed' (Lc 11:21): unlisted; RV1909 'el fuerte armado' used verbatim.
- flag: 'as the grass and the flower thereof' echoes Is 40:6 (RV1909 'Toda carne es hierba, y toda su gloria como flor del campo'); Henry's wording translated.

### 2423 Isa 40:12-17 
- quoted (John 3:16): “he gave his only-begotten Son” → “ha dado á su Hijo unigénito”

### 2425 Isa 40:27-31 
- flag: 'As the day, so shall the strength be' echoes Dt 33:25; translated (RV1909 wording differs).

### 2426 Isa 41:1-9 
- flag: Outline heading 'they are encouraged not to fear' begins lowercase in the source; kept.
- flag: 'raise up one in righteousness' (Is 41:2; RV1909 'despertó del oriente al justo') and 'the seed of Abraham his friend' (Is 41:8; RV1909 'simiente de Abraham mi amigo', Henry in the third person) translated.

### 2427 Isa 41:10-20 
- quoted (Isa 41:10): “Fear thou not, for I am with thee” → “No temas, que yo soy contigo”
- quoted (John 7:38): “rivers of living water” → “ríos de agua viva”
- flag: 'the worm Jacob' (Is 41:14): RV1909 'gusano de Jacob' used; 'threshing instrument … new, and having sharp spikes' (Is 41:15): RV1909 has 'trillo, trillo nuevo, lleno de dientes'; 'trillo' and 'dientes' used in Henry's own wording.
- flag: 'I will strengthen thee … I will uphold thee with that right hand' paraphrases Is 41:10 (RV1909 'que te esfuerzo … te sustentaré con la diestra de mi justicia'); Henry's wording translated.

### 2429 Isa 42:1-4 
- flag: 'a bruised reed', 'smoking flax' (Is 42:3): unlisted; RV1909 'la caña cascada', 'el pábilo que humeare' used verbatim.

### 2432 Isa 42:18-25 
- quoted (Isa 42:21): “The Lord is well-pleased” → “Jehová se complació”
- translated, not quoted (Isa 42:21; Henry's own sentence in the present tense about saving sinners, not the verse's statement.): “the Lord is well-pleased”
- quoted (Heb 4:1): “fear, lest a promise being left us of entering into his rest, any” → “pues, que quedando aún la promesa de entrar en su reposo, parezca alguno”
- flag: Quote 0: RV1909's past 'se complació' used for Henry's present 'is well-pleased'.
- flag: Quote 2 (He 4:1): RV1909 'TEMAMOS' begins the verse in capitals, so 'temamos' stands just before the quoted run; Henry's 'any of us should be found to come short of it' (RV1909 'alguno de vosotros haberse apartado') ends in his own words, 'de nosotros haberse quedado atrás'.
- flag: sensitive: 'This fully came to pass in the destruction of the Jewish nation' (the Jews as a whole); also 'blind as the benighted heathen'.

### 2433 Isa 43:1-7 
- flag: 'they should be born up' read as 'borne up' (sostenidos).

### 2434 Isa 43:8-13 
- flag: The source text ends without a final full stop; kept as it stands.

### 2436 Isa 43:22-28 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 43:25: RV1909 'Yo, yo soy el que borro tus rebeliones' used verbatim; Henry's 'yet' added after it as 'todavía'.
- flag: 'The Master' rendered 'El Señor' (the master of the servants in Henry's image).
- flag: 'put him in remembrance … declare these things, that thou mayest be justified' echoes Is 43:26 (RV1909 'Hazme acordar … relata tú para abonarte'); Henry's wording translated.
- flag: Source 'his promise' sake' (apostrophe after promise) read as 'his promise's sake'.

### 2437 Isa 44:1-8 
- flag: 'Jeshurun' kept as the RV1909 spells it (Is 44:2); later Reina-Valera revisions write 'Jesurún'.

### 2438 Isa 44:9-20 
- quoted (Isa 44:20): “Is there not a lie in my right hand” → “¿No hay una mentira á mi mano derecha?”

### 2439 Isa 44:21-28 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 44:22: RV1909 'tórnate á mí' used verbatim for 'Return unto me'.

### 2441 Isa 45:5-10

- flag: 'together the Lord hath created them' echoes Is 45:8 (RV1909 'háganse brotar juntamente. Yo Jehová lo crié'); Henry's wording translated.

### 2442 Isa 45:11-19

### 2443 Isa 45:20-25 
- flag: 'the Lord our Righteousness' (Jer 23:6): unlisted; RV1909's name 'JEHOVÁ, JUSTICIA NUESTRA' used verbatim, its capitals kept.

### 2446 Isa 47:1-6

### 2448 Isa 48:1-8

### 2455 Isa 49:24-26

### 2456 Isa 50:1-3 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jn 1:11: RV1909 'y los suyos no le recibieron' used verbatim; 'vino a lo suyo' follows its wording ('A lo suyo vino').
- flag: sensitive: outline 'The rejection of the Jews' and 'they were at last rejected for crucifying the Lord of glory' (the Jews as a whole).

### 2457 Isa 50:4-9 
- flag: Source 'contend with those whom he is an Advocate' (a word missing: 'to whom' / 'for whom'); read as 'of whom he is Advocate'.
- flag: 'who will contend' echoes Is 50:8 (RV1909 '¿quién contenderá conmigo?'); Henry's wording translated.

### 2458 Isa 50:10-11 
- quoted (Isa 50:10): “Let him trust in the name of the Lord” → “confíe en el nombre de Jehová”
- flag: The source text begins with a stray ',11' (left from the verse range 10,11); kept as it stands.
- flag: 'The Lord our Righteousness' (Jer 23:6): unlisted; RV1909's name 'JEHOVÁ, JUSTICIA NUESTRA' used verbatim.
- flag: 'let him stay himself upon the promises' adapts Is 50:10 'stay upon his God' (RV1909 'apóyese en su Dios'); 'apóyese' kept in Henry's wording.

### 2459 Isa 51:1-3
