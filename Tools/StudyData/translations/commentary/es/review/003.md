### 57 Gen 12:6-9 He journeys through Canaan, and worships God in that land.
- quoted (Gen 12:8): “the name of the Lord” → “el nombre de Jehová”
- flag: quote 0: Henry reads Gn 12:8 'called upon the name of the LORD' as 'preached concerning the name of the Lord' (a marginal reading); the RV1909 has 'invocó el nombre de Jehová'. 'acerca de el' (not 'del') keeps the RV1909's words 'el nombre de Jehová' unbroken.
- flag: 'strangers and sojourners' (He 11:13) translated; the RV1909 has 'peregrinos y advenedizos'. 'a heavenly country' (He 11:16): RV1909 'la celestial'.

### 58 Gen 12:10-20 Abram is driven by a famine into Egypt, He feigns his wife to be his sister.

- flag: quote 0: Henry leaves out 'unto me' (RV1909 'conmigo'); left out here too.

### 60 Gen 13:5-9 Strife between the herdsmen of Abram and Lot. Abram gives Lot his choice of the country.
