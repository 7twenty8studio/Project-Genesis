# To review: es commentary batch 061

### 2788 Dan 5:1-9 
- quoted (Dan 5:5): “the part of the hand” → “la palma de la mano”
- flag: KJV 'the part of the hand' (Dn 5:5) is RV1909 'la palma de la mano'; Henry's 'that part which we do not see' keeps 'aquella parte'.
- flag: sensitive: 'the heathens' rendered 'los paganos'.

### 2790 Dan 5:18-31 
- flag: 'weighed in the balance, and found wanting' echoes Dn 5:27 (RV1909 'Pesado has sido en balanza, y fuiste hallado falto'); Henry's third person, so translated close to it. 'cut asunder' (Mt 24:51 KJV) rendered 'partido en dos'.

### 2791 Dan 6:1-5 
- flag: Source heading 'the lion's den' (singular) rendered 'el foso de los leones' as RV1909 has it.

### 2793 Dan 6:11-17 
- quoted (Dan 6:12): “into the den of lions” → “en el foso de los leones”
- quoted (Dan 6:17): “the king sealed it with his own signet” → “la cual selló el rey con su anillo”
- flag: Henry's 'with his own signet' given in RV1909's words 'con su anillo' (no 'own'). 'commit our lives and souls unto God, in well-doing' echoes 1 P 4:19; translated.

### 2794 Dan 6:18-24 
- quoted (Dan 6:23): “because he believed in his God” → “porque creyó en su Dios”
- quoted (Prov 11:8): “the righteous is delivered out of trouble, and the wicked cometh in his stead” → “el justo es librado de la tribulación: mas el impío viene en lugar suyo”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 1 P 5:8: RV1909 'buscando á quien devore' used ('león rugiente' too).

### 2795 Dan 6:25-28 
- quoted (Matt 6:13): “the power, and the glory, for ever” → “el reino, y el poder, y la gloria, por todos los siglos”
- flag: Source typo 'least' for 'lest' translated as 'no sea que'.

### 2796 Dan 7:1-8 
- flag: Source outline 'and of Christ's kingdom' starts in lower case after a full stop; kept as given. 'their wrath shall be made to praise the Lord, and the remainder of it he will restrain' echoes Sal 76:10 (RV1909 'tú reprimirás el resto de las iras'); translated.

### 2798 Dan 7:15-28 
- quoted (Dan 7:21): “made war with the saints, and prevailed against them” → “hacía guerra contra los santos, y los vencía”
- quoted (Dan 7:18): “The saints of the Most High shall” → “los santos del Altísimo”
- quoted (Dan 7:18): “possess the kingdom for ever” → “poseerán el reino”
- flag: sensitive: 'the rage of papal Rome against true Christians' and St John's prophecies pointing 'in the first place at Rome'; translated in full.
- flag: 'possess the kingdom for ever': RV1909 'poseerán el reino hasta el siglo'; 'poseerán el reino' quoted, 'para siempre' Henry's.

### 2799 Dan 8:1-14 
- quoted (Dan 8:8): “came up four notable ones” → “subieron otros cuatro maravillosos”
- quoted (Col 2:3): “in whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge” → “en el cual están escondidos todos los tesoros de sabiduría y conocimiento”
- flag: sensitive: 'the Mohammedan delusion' (Islam) rendered 'el engaño mahometano'.
- flag: KJV 'notable' (Dn 8:8) is RV1909 'maravillosos'.

### 2802 Dan 9:4-19 
- quoted (Prov 14:34): “Sin is a reproach to any people” → “El pecado es afrenta”
- quoted (Dan 9:19): “O Lord, hearken and do” → “presta oído, Señor, y haz”
- flag: 'not only forgiveness, but forgivenesses' plays on the KJV's plural 'forgivenesses' (Dn 9:9); RV1909 has 'el perdonar', so translated 'no solo perdón, sino perdones'. 'defer not' uses RV1909 Dn 9:19 'no pongas dilación'. 'make mention of his righteousness, even of his only' echoes Sal 71:16 (RV1909 'haré memoria de sola tu justicia'); translated.

### 2803 Dan 9:20-27 
- quoted (1Pet 3:18): “sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God” → “los pecados, el justo por los injustos, para llevarnos á Dios”
- flag: sensitive: the Jews' 'most obstinate unbelief' and the crucifixion filling up 'the measure of their guilt'; translated in full.
- flag: 'How can those escape who neglect so great salvation' echoes He 2:3 (RV1909 '¿Cómo escaparemos nosotros, si tuviéremos en poco una salud tan grande?'); Henry's third person, so translated, with Henry's own 'salvation' as 'salvación'.

### 2804 Dan 10:1-9

### 2805 Dan 10:10-21 
- flag: 'these Scriptures of truth' (Dn 10:21 KJV 'the scripture of truth'; RV1909 'la escritura de verdad'); translated 'estas Escrituras de verdad' as Henry has the plural.

### 2806 Dan 11:1-30 
- flag: Source 'From Dn 11:5-30, is generally considered …' lacks a subject and overlaps with 'from Dn 11:21'; translated as written. 'those who know God' echoes Dn 11:32.

### 2807 Dan 11:31-45 
- flag: sensitive: the Roman empire's 'papal' state read as part of antichrist's story; translated in full.

### 2808 Dan 12:1-4 
- quoted (Job 19:25, Job 19:26): “shall stand at the latter day upon the earth” → “al fin se levantará sobre el polvo”
- flag: Source paragraph begins with a stray '. ' (perhaps a lost heading); the stray full stop is left out, nothing else.
- flag: Quotation of Job 19:25: KJV 'stand at the latter day upon the earth' is RV1909 'al fin se levantará sobre el polvo' (dust, not earth); RV1909 used.
- flag: Source 'out of their heathenism of Judaism' read as a slip for 'heathenism or Judaism'; translated 'de su paganismo o judaísmo'.
- flag: Michael: glossary offers several; 'Miguel' (RV1909 Dn 12:1) used.
- flag: sensitive: 'heathenism' and 'Judaism' set side by side as states to be awakened out of.

### 2809 Dan 12:5-13 
- quoted (Dan 12:6): “be to the end of these wonders” → “el fin de estas maravillas”
- quoted (Dan 12:7): “be for a time, times” → “por tiempo, tiempos, y la mitad”
- quoted (Dan 12:7): “the power of the holy people” → “del pueblo santo”
- flag: sensitive: 'The imposture of Mohammed, and the papal usurpation … a twofold attack upon the church of God'; translated in full.
- flag: KJV 'the power of the holy people' (Dn 12:7) is RV1909 'el esparcimiento del escuadrón del pueblo santo'; only 'del pueblo santo' quoted, 'el poder' is Henry's. 'Go thou thy way' paraphrases Dn 12:13 (RV1909 'Y tú irás al fin'); translated. 'in the end of the days' = RV1909 'al fin de los días'.

### 2810 Hos 1:1-7

- flag: Henry's 'the second child' is a daughter (Os 1:6); rendered 'la segunda hija'.
