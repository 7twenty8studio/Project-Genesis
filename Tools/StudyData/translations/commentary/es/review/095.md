# To review: es commentary batch 095

### 3868 Heb 1:4-14 And in his superiority to all the holy angels.
- flag: "ministering spirits" (Heb 1:14) given with the RV1909's "espíritus administradores"; "conquering and to conquer" (Ap 6:2; RV1909 "salió victorioso, para que también venciese") translated in Henry's wording

### 3869 Heb 2:1-4 The duty of stedfastly adhering to Christ and his gospel.
- quoted (Heb 2:4): “gifts of the Holy Ghost” → “repartimientos del Espíritu Santo”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hch 1:1 ("comenzó á hacer y á enseñar")
- flag: "the good part that cannot be taken away" (Lc 10:42): RV1909 "la buena parte, la cual no le será quitada", with "le" changed to "nos" for Henry's "we"; "one thing needful" translated

### 3870 Heb 2:5-9 
- flag: "the prince of this world shall be cast out" (Jn 12:31): RV1909 "el príncipe de este mundo será echado fuera"; given in Henry's subjunctive ("sea echado fuera")

### 3871 Heb 2:10-13 The reason of his sufferings, and the fitness of them.
- quoted (Heb 2:10): “of their salvation perfect through sufferings” → “consumado por aflicciones al autor de la salud de ellos”
- quoted (Heb 2:11): “ashamed to call them brethren” → “no se avergüenza de llamarlos hermanos”
- flag: Heb 2:10 "salvation" is RV1909 "salud" (kept in the quotation)

### 3872 Heb 2:14-18 Christ's taking the nature of man, and not his taking the nature of angels, was necessary to his priestly office.
- flag: typo read as meant: "Not soul ever perished" (No soul)

### 3874 Heb 3:7-13 The Hebrews are warned of the sin and danger of unbelief.
- flag: "Take heed" (Heb 3:12) given with the RV1909's "Mirad"
- flag: "Let those that think they stand, take heed lest they fall" (1 Co 10:12) given with the RV1909's "El que piensa estar firme, mire no caiga" (singular)

### 3875 Heb 3:14-19 And of necessity of faith in Christ, and of stedfastly following him.
- quoted (Heb 3:14): “are made partakers of Christ” → “participantes de Cristo”

### 3876 Heb 4:1-10 Humble, cautious fear is urged, lest any should come short of the promised rest, through unbelief.
- flag: "remaineth a rest to the people of God" (Heb 4:9): RV1909 "queda un reposo para el pueblo de Dios"; Henry's wording translated around it
- flag: "sabbatism" (Henry's gloss of Greek sabbatismos) given as "sabatismo"; RV1909 Heb 4:9 has "reposo"

### 3877 Heb 4:11-16 Arguments and motives to faith and hope in our approaches to God.
- quoted (Rom 8:3): “his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh” → “á su Hijo en semejanza de carne de pecado”
- flag: source drops a word: "it was necessary he should actual experience" (read as "should have actual experience")
- flag: "throne of grace" (Heb 4:16) given with the RV1909's "trono de la gracia"

### 3878 Heb 5:1-10 The office and duty of a high priest abundantly answered in Christ.
- quoted (Heb 5:7): “In the days of his flesh” → “En los días de su carne”
- quoted (Heb 5:9): “the Author of eternal salvation” → “causa de eterna salud á todos los que le obedecen”
- flag: Heb 5:9 "Author of eternal salvation" is RV1909 "causa de eterna salud" (kept in the quotation)

### 3879 Heb 5:11-14 The Christian Hebrews reproved for their little progress in the knowledge of the gospel.
- flag: "unskilful" (KJV Heb 5:13): RV1909 "inhábil"; Henry's explanation translated with "inexperto"

### 3880 Heb 6:1-8 The Hebrews are urged to go forward in the doctrine of Christ, and the consequences of apostacy, or turning back, are described.
- quoted (Heb 10:26): “have received the knowledge of the truth” → “recibido el conocimiento de la verdad”
- quoted (Heb 6:6): “to renew them again unto repentance” → “sean otra vez renovados para arrepentimiento”

### 3881 Heb 6:9-10 The apostle expresses satisfaction, as to the most of them.
- flag: "things that accompany salvation" (KJV Heb 6:9): RV1909 "cosas ... más cercanas á salud"; Henry's wording translated

### 3882 Heb 6:11-20 And encourages them to persevere in faith and holiness.
- flag: "it is not possible for God to lie" (Heb 6:18) given with the RV1909's "es imposible que Dios mienta"; "sure and stedfast" (Heb 6:19) RV1909 "segura y firme"
- flag: "appear with him in glory" (Col 3:4): RV1909 "seréis manifestados con él en gloria", with "seremos" for Henry's "we"

### 3883 Heb 7:1-3 A comparison between the priesthood of Melchizedec and that of Christ.
- quoted (Rev 19:10): “the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy” → “el testimonio de Jesús es el espíritu de la profecía”

### 3884 Heb 7:4-10 The excellence of Christ's priesthood above the Levitical priesthood is shown.
- quoted (1Tim 4:8, 1Tim 4:9): “of the life that now is, and of that which is to come. This” → “de esta vida presente, y de la venidera”
- flag: the listed run includes Henry's next word "This", which belongs to his own sentence; only the quotation of 1 Ti 4:8 is given in the RV1909's words

### 3885 Heb 7:11-25 This is applied to Christ.
- flag: "Surety" (KJV Heb 7:22) as "Fiador" (RV1909 "fiador"); "save to the uttermost" (KJV Heb 7:25; RV1909 "salvar eternamente") and "power of endless life" (KJV Heb 7:16; RV1909 "virtud de vida indisoluble") translated in Henry's wording

### 3886 Heb 7:26-28 The faith and hope of the church encouraged from this.
- flag: "harmless ... undefiled" (Heb 7:26) given with the RV1909's "inocente, limpio"
- flag: "he did no violence, nor was there any deceit in his mouth" (Is 53:9): given with the RV1909's "nunca hizo él maldad, ni hubo engaño en su boca" ("maldad" for KJV "violence")

### 3887 Heb 8:1-6 The excellence of Christ's priesthood above that of Aaron is shown.
- flag: "accepted in the Beloved" (Ef 1:6) given with the RV1909's "aceptos en el Amado"

### 3889 Heb 9:1-5 The Jewish tabernacle and its utensils.
- flag: "the fulness of the Godhead dwelt bodily" (Col 2:9; RV1909 "habita toda la plenitud de la divinidad corporalmente") translated in Henry's past tense

### 3890 Heb 9:6-10 Their use and meaning.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sal 19:12 ("Los errores, ¿quién los entenderá?"); Henry's "all his errors" has no "all" in the RV1909

### 3891 Heb 9:11-14 These fulfilled in Christ.
- translated, not quoted (Heb 9:14; Henry's "dead, or deadly, works to serve the living God" glosses KJV "dead works"; RV1909 "obras de muerte para que sirváis al Dios vivo" would lose his "dead, or deadly" and its "sirváis" (you) does not fit his sentence): “works to serve the living God”

### 3893 Heb 9:23-28 The necessity, superior dignity, and power of his priesthood and sacrifice.
- flag: unlisted quotations: Job 19:25 ("Redentor vive", RV1909 "mi Redentor vive" with Henry's "his") and Ap 22:20 ("Amén, sea así. Ven, Señor Jesús" for KJV "Even so, come, Lord Jesus")
- flag: "the faith and patience of the church" echoes Ap 13:10 (RV1909 "la paciencia y la fe de los santos"); Henry's wording translated

### 3894 Heb 10:1-10 The insufficiency of sacrifices for taking away sin, The necessity and power of the sacrifice of Christ for that purpose.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Ti 3:16, in Henry's own quotation marks: "Dios ha sido manifestado en carne"
