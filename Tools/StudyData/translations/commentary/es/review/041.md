# To review: es commentary batch 041

### 1552 Ps 73:1-14 The psalmist's temptation.
- quoted (Ps 73:3): “the prosperity of the wicked” → “la prosperidad de los impíos”
- quoted (Ps 73:10): “therefore his people return hither” → “Por eso su pueblo vuelve aquí”

### 1553 Ps 73:15-20 How he gained a victory over it.
- flag: Henry's 'slippery places' (Sal 73:18) rendered with the RV1909's word 'deslizaderos'; not a listed quotation.

### 1554 Ps 73:21-28 How he profited by it.
- flag: Source garbled: 'a profession Christ' (missing 'of'); translated as 'una profesión de Cristo'.

### 1557 Ps 74:18-23 Petitions for deliverances.
- flag: unlisted quotation of He 13:8 (RV1909 'el mismo ayer, y hoy, y por los siglos'), addressed to Christ as Henry does.
- flag: sensitive: 'the dark nations of the earth' (non-Christian peoples), translated 'las naciones tenebrosas de la tierra'.

### 1558 Ps 75:1-5 The psalmist declares his resolution of executing judgment.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Co 1:30 (RV1909 'nos ha sido hecho por Dios sabiduría').

### 1559 Ps 75:6-10 He rebukes the wicked, and concludes with resolutions to praise God.
- quoted (Ps 75:6): “neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south” → “ni de oriente, ni de occidente, ni del desierto”
- flag: Sal 75:6: the RV1909 has 'ni del desierto' where the KJV has 'nor from the south'; Henry's point (the north not mentioned) still stands.
- flag: Henry's note that 'the same word' means north and secret place refers to the Hebrew (tsafon / tsafan), not the English; translated as written.

### 1561 Ps 76:7-12 All have to fear and to trust in him.
- quoted (Ps 76:9): “the meek of the earth” → “los mansos de la tierra”
- quoted (Ps 76:12): “He shall cut off the spirit of princes; he” → “Cortará él el espíritu de los príncipes”
- flag: 'hitherto it shall come, and no further' echoes Job 38:11 (RV1909 'Hasta aquí vendrás, y no pasarás adelante'); Henry changes the person, so translated.
- flag: Sal 76:12: Henry's 'so the word signifies' (to slip off) refers to the Hebrew; the RV1909 has 'Cortará'.

### 1562 Ps 77:1-10 The psalmist's troubles and temptation.
- flag: Source has a stray comma ('When, unbelief is working'); translated without it.

### 1563 Ps 77:11-20 He encourages himself by the remembrance of God's help of his people.
- quoted (Rom 8:32): “spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all” → “aun á su propio Hijo no perdonó, antes le entregó por todos nosotros”
- flag: Sal 77:13: Henry follows the KJV 'Thy way, O God, is in the sanctuary'; the RV1909 has 'en santidad es tu camino'. Translated as Henry wrote it.
- flag: Ro 8:32: Henry's closing clause ('that with him, he might freely give us all things') is his own wording of the verse; translated, not quoted.

### 1564 Ps 78:1-8 Attention called for.
- flag: 'dark sayings' is the KJV's word in Sal 78:2; the RV1909 has 'cosas reservadas de antiguo'. Translated 'dichos oscuros' as Henry explains the English.

### 1565 Ps 78:9-39 The history of Israel.
- flag: 'these doors of heaven' alludes to Sal 78:23 (RV1909 'las puertas de los cielos'); translated.
- flag: 'will never be estranged from it' echoes the KJV of Sal 78:30 ('not estranged from their lust'); the RV1909 has 'No habían quitado de sí su deseo'. Translated 'nunca se apartarán de ella'.
- flag: Source spelling 'imbolden' (embolden) translated normally.

### 1566 Ps 78:40-55 Their settlement in Canaan.
- quoted (Ps 78:41): “The Holy One of Israel” → “Santo de Israel”
- quoted (Ps 78:52): “made his own people to go forth like sheep; and guided them in the wilderness” → “hizo salir á su pueblo como ovejas, y llevólos por el desierto”
- flag: Sal 78:52: the RV1909 has no word for Henry's 'own' ('his own people'); its run used as it stands.
- flag: 'there remaineth a far more glorious rest for the people of God' adapts He 4:9 with Henry's added words; translated, not quoted (RV1909 'queda un reposo para el pueblo de Dios').

### 1567 Ps 78:56-72 The mercies of God to Israel contrasted with their ingratitude.
- quoted (Ps 78:72): “the skilfulness of his hands” → “la pericia de sus manos”
- quoted (Isa 9:7): “of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end” → “lo dilatado de su imperio y la paz no tendrán término”
- quoted (Jer 17:9): “the heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked” → “engañoso es el corazón más que todas las cosas, y perverso”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 11:2 (RV1909 'espíritu de sabiduría y de inteligencia', first letter capitalized as Henry's 'Spirit').
- flag: Jer 17:9: the RV1909 has only 'perverso' for the KJV's 'desperately wicked'.

### 1568 Ps 79:1-5 The deplorable condition of the people of God.
- flag: sensitive: 'the heathen' (KJV Sal 79:1; RV1909 'las gentes'), translated 'los paganos'.

### 1569 Ps 79:6-13 A petition for relief.
- quoted (Ps 79:9): “the glory of thy name” → “por la gloria de tu nombre”
- quoted (Ps 79:11): “prisoner come before thee, according to the greatness of thy power preserve thou those that are appointed to die” → “entre ante tu acatamiento el gemido de los presos: conforme á la grandeza de tu brazo preserva á los sentenciados á muerte”
- flag: Sal 79:11: Henry has 'a prisoner' (singular) and 'thy power'; the RV1909 has 'los presos' and 'tu brazo'. Its words used.
- flag: 'Shepherd and Bishop' alludes to 1 P 2:25 (RV1909 'Pastor y Obispo').

### 1572 Ps 80:17-19 A prayer for mercy.
- flag: 'the Man of God's right hand' alludes to Sal 80:17 (RV1909 'el varón de tu diestra'); translated in Henry's third person.

### 1574 Ps 81:8-16 Their obligations to him.
- flag: 'The Lord is unwilling that any should perish' echoes 2 P 3:9 (RV1909 'no queriendo que ninguno perezca'); Henry's own sentence, translated.

### 1577 Ps 83:1-8 The designs of the enemies of Israel.
- flag: Source has 'his hidden one' (singular; KJV Sal 83:3 'thy hidden ones'); rendered with the RV1909's plural 'sus escondidos'.

### 1578 Ps 83:9-18 Earnest prayer for their defeat.
- quoted (1Pet 2:11): “fleshly lusts, which war against the soul” → “deseos carnales que batallan contra el alma”
- flag: 'like a wheel' is the KJV of Sal 83:13; the RV1909 has 'como á torbellinos'. Translated 'como una rueda' as Henry explains it.
- flag: 'stubble' (KJV Sal 83:13) translated 'hojarasca', the RV1909's word there.

### 1579 Ps 84:1-7 The psalmist expresses his affection to the ordinances of God.
- flag: 'valley of weeping' is Henry's gloss on Baca (Sal 84:6; RV1909 'el valle de Baca'); translated.

### 1580 Ps 84:8-12 His desire towards the God of the ordinances.
- flag: Source typo 'Through he has not promised' read as 'Though'; translated 'Aunque'.
- flag: Allusions to Sal 84:9-12 (sun, shield, grace and glory, one day in thy courts) are Henry's paraphrase; translated, not quoted.

### 1582 Ps 85:8-13 Trust in God's goodness.
- quoted (Ps 85:9): “glory may dwell in our land” → “para que habite la gloria en nuestra tierra”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sal 85:8 (RV1909 'hablará paz á su pueblo').
- flag: 'sprang out of the earth' (Sal 85:11, RV1909 'brotará de la tierra') and 'the way of his steps' (Sal 85:13, RV1909 'sus pasos pondrá en camino') are Henry's adaptations; translated.
- flag: Source text ends without a final full stop; left so.

### 1583 Ps 86:1-7 The psalmist pleads his earnestness, and the mercy of God, as reasons why his prayer should be heard.
- flag: 'I am one whom thou favourest' is the KJV margin of Sal 86:2 ('I am holy'); the RV1909 has 'soy pío'. Translated as Henry wrote it.

### 1584 Ps 86:8-17 He renews his requests for help and comfort.
- flag: 2 Co 8:9 adapted by Henry to 'our sakes' / 'we'; translated in his person, close to the RV1909 ('por amor de vosotros se hizo pobre, siendo rico; para que vosotros con su pobreza fueseis enriquecidos').
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1585 Ps 87:1-3 The glory of the church.
- quoted (1Cor 3:11): “Other foundation can no man lay than that is laid” → “nadie puede poner otro fundamento que el que está puesto, el cual es Jesucristo”
- flag: 1 Co 3:11: Henry's 'even Jesus Christ' completes the verse; the RV1909's run 'el cual es Jesucristo' used.
- flag: 'glorious things are spoken of' alludes to Sal 87:3 (RV1909 'Cosas ilustres son dichas de ti'); translated.

### 1586 Ps 87:4-7 It is filled with the Divine blessing.
- quoted (Prov 24:12): “to every man according to his works” → “al hombre según sus obras”
- flag: Pr 24:12: the RV1909 has 'dará al hombre' (no word for Henry's 'every'); used with the verb as 'dé'. Henry's wording is closer to Ro 2:6 / Mt 16:27.
- flag: 'To them much was given, and of them much will be required' echoes Lc 12:48; translated.

### 1587 Ps 88:1-9 The psalmist pours out his soul to God in lamentation.
- flag: Allusions to He 12:2 and Is 53:5 ('wounded and bruised for our iniquities'); translated (RV1909 Is 53:5 'herido... molido por nuestros pecados').

### 1588 Ps 88:10-18 He wrestles by faith, in his prayer to God for comfort.
- flag: 'All forsook him, and fled' echoes Mr 14:50 (RV1909 'dejándole todos sus discípulos, huyeron'); translated.
- flag: 'take not thy Holy Spirit from us' adapts Sal 51:11 to 'us' (RV1909 'no quites de mí tu santo espíritu'); translated.

### 1591 Ps 89:15-18 The happiness of those in communion with him.
- quoted (Ps 89:18): “the Holy One of Israel” → “el Santo de Israel”
- flag: 'the joyful sound' is the KJV of Sal 89:15; the RV1909 has 'sabe aclamarte'. Translated.
- flag: 'The joy of the Lord is the strength' echoes Neh 8:10 (RV1909 'el gozo de Jehová es vuestra fortaleza'); Henry's own sentence ('of his people'), translated.
