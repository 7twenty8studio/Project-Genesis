# To review: es commentary batch 100

### 3986 Rev 1:1-3 The Divine origin, the design, and the importance of this book.
- quoted (Rev 1:1): “the Revelation of Jesus Christ” → “revelación de Jesucristo”
- quoted (Rev 1:1): “must shortly come to pass” → “las cosas que deben suceder presto”

### 3987 Rev 1:4-8 The apostle John salutes the seven churches of Asia.
- quoted (Rev 1:5): “the Prince of the kings of the earth” → “príncipe de los reyes de la tierra”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rev 1:4 ("who is, and who was, and who is to come"), given in the RV1909's words "que es y que era y que ha de venir"; Henry's own "Jehovah" kept as "Jehová".
- flag: "kings and priests to God and his Father" follows the RV1909's Rev 1:6 ("reyes y sacerdotes para Dios y su Padre").
- flag: source garbled: "we must now willing submit to him receive him" read as "willingly submit to him, receive him".

### 3988 Rev 1:9-11 Declares when, where, and how, the revelation was made to him.
- quoted (1Pet 4:14): “the Spirit of glory and of God” → “la gloria y el Espíritu de Dios”
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909's 1Pet 4:14 reads "la gloria y el Espíritu de Dios" (the glory and the Spirit of God) where the KJV has "the spirit of glory and of God"; the RV1909's wording is used.
- flag: "the Christian sabbath" rendered with the bracketed note "el sábado cristiano [es decir, el domingo, el día del Señor]" as the instructions require, though Henry names "the Lord's day" just before it.

### 3989 Rev 1:12-20 His vision, in which he saw Christ appear.
- quoted (Rev 1:14): “as a flame of fire” → “ojos como llama de fuego”
- quoted (Rev 1:15): “His voice as the sound of many waters” → “su voz como ruido de muchas aguas”
- quoted (Heb 4:12): “to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit” → “hasta partir el alma, y aun el espíritu”
- quoted (Rev 1:11): “the First and the Last” → “el primero y el último”
- flag: source garbled: "the apostle saw as though of the Lord Jesus Christ appeared" read as "saw as though [the likeness] of the Lord Jesus Christ appeared" ("como si la figura del Señor Jesucristo apareciera").
- flag: unlisted quotations of Rev 1:17 ("Fear not" -> "No temas") and Rev 1:18 ("I was dead" -> "he sido muerto"), given in the RV1909's words.

### 3990 Rev 2:1-7 
- flag: "first love" and "Tree of life" follow the RV1909's Rev 2:4, 7 ("primer amor", "árbol de la vida").
- flag: "their labour shall not be in vain in the Lord" echoes 1Cor 15:58 (RV1909 "vuestro trabajo en el Señor no es vano"); Henry changes the person and tense, so it is translated.

### 3991 Rev 2:8-11 
- quoted (Rev 2:8): “was dead and is alive” → “que fué muerto, y vivió”
- flag: unlisted quotations of Rev 2:10 ("Fear none of these things" -> "No tengas ningún temor de las cosas que has de padecer"; "I will give thee" -> "yo te daré"; "a crown of life" -> "la corona de la vida") and Rev 2:11 ("the second death" -> "la muerte segunda"), given in the RV1909's words; the RV1909's fuller "de las cosas que has de padecer" is the clause the KJV's "these things" stands for.
- flag: "First" / "Last" follow the RV1909's Rev 2:8 "El primero y postrero".

### 3993 Rev 2:18-29 
- flag: unlisted quotations of Rev 2:18 ("as a flame of fire" -> "como llama de fuego") and Rev 2:24 ("the depths of Satan" -> "las profundidades de Satanás"), given in the RV1909's words.

### 3994 Rev 3:1-6 
- quoted (Rev 3:5): “shall be clothed in white raiment” → “será vestido de vestiduras blancas”
- flag: "Thy works are hollow and empty ... not filled up" explains Rev 3:2, where the KJV has "not found thy works perfect" (Greek "filled up"); the RV1909 says "no he hallado tus obras perfectas". Henry's explanation is translated as he gives it.
- flag: "sabbaths" rendered "los sábados" per the term list; Henry means the Lord's days of public worship.
- flag: "a name to live" echoes Rev 3:1 (RV1909 "tienes nombre que vives"); translated as Henry's phrase.

### 3995 Rev 3:7-13 
- quoted (Rom 4:20): “strong in faith, giving glory to God” → “esforzado en fe, dando gloria á Dios”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 13:12 ("To him that hath shall be given"), given in the RV1909's words "á cualquiera que tiene, se le dará".
- flag: "a little strength" is the RV1909's "un poco de potencia" in Rev 3:8; Henry's phrase is translated ("un poco de fuerza").

### 3996 Rev 3:14-22 
- flag: unlisted quotations inside Henry's quotation marks: Rev 3:14 ("The Amen" -> "el Amén") and Rev 3:17 ("Because thou sayest" -> "Porque tú dices"), given in the RV1909's words; "white raiment" follows Rev 3:18 ("vestiduras blancas").
- flag: source typo: "our owns souls" read as "our own souls".
- flag: source garbled: "their rags that would defile them" (no verb) rendered "sus harapos eran tales que los contaminarían".

### 3997 Rev 4:1-8 
- quoted (Rev 4:4): “they had on their heads crowns of gold” → “tenían sobre sus cabezas coronas de oro”
- flag: "four living creatures" (Henry's term) is "cuatro animales" in the RV1909's Rev 4:6; translated "seres vivientes".
- flag: "the things that are" (in Henry's quotation marks) is given in the RV1909's Rev 1:19 words "las que son", as in the Revelation introduction.
- flag: "the laver" refers to the "sea of glass" (RV1909 "mar de vidrio") of Rev 4:6, which Henry doesn't name here.

### 3999 Rev 5:1-7 A book sealed with seven seals, which could be opened by none but Christ, who took the book to open it.
- quoted (Rev 5:7): “hand of Him that sat upon the throne” → “aquel que estaba sentado en el trono”
- quoted (Rev 5:6): “having seven horns and seven eyes” → “que tenía siete cuernos, y siete ojos”
- flag: source punctuation: "the gospel of Christ of that on which everlasting salvation depends" read with a comma ("del evangelio de Cristo, de aquello de lo cual depende...").
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909's Rev 5:7 has "de la mano derecha de aquel"; "en la mano de" stays Henry's own, outside the quoted run.

### 4000 Rev 5:8-14 Upon which all honour is ascribed to him, as worthy to open it.
- flag: "vials ... full of odours" are "copas de oro llenas de perfumes" in the RV1909's Rev 5:8; translated "copas" and "perfumes".

### 4001 Rev 6:1-8 The opening of the seals, The first, second, third, and fourth.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 28:20 ("Lo! I am with you always, even to the end of the world"), given in the RV1909's words "He aquí, yo estoy con vosotros todos los días, hasta el fin del mundo".
- flag: "a yoke in his hand": Henry reads the Greek word for the "pair of balances" (KJV) as "yoke"; the RV1909's Rev 6:5 has "un peso en su mano". Translated as Henry wrote it, since his point builds on the yoke.
- flag: "a pale colour": the RV1909's Rev 6:8 calls it "un caballo amarillo"; "the king of terrors" is the RV1909's Job 18:14 "el rey de los espantos", used here.

### 4003 Rev 6:12-17 The sixth.
- quoted (Rev 6:12): “there was a great earthquake” → “fué hecho un gran terremoto”
- quoted (Rev 6:16): “the wrath of the Lamb” → “la ira del Cordero”

### 4006 Rev 7:13-17 The blessedness and glory of those that suffered martyrdom for Christ.
- quoted (Rom 8:39): “separate us from the love of God” → “nos podrá apartar del amor de Dios”
- quoted (Rev 7:14): “the blood of the Lamb” → “la sangre del Cordero”

### 4007 Rev 8:1-6 The seventh seal is opened and seven angels appear with seven trumpets, ready to proclaim the purposes of God.
- flag: Henry's "silence in heaven for a space": the RV1909's Rev 8:1 has "casi por media hora"; translated as Henry's "por un espacio de tiempo".
