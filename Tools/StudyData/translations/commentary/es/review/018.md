# To review: es commentary batch 018

### 593 Deut 31:1-8 Moses encourages the people, and Joshua.
- quoted (Heb 4:2): “gospel preached, as well as unto them” → “también á nosotros se nos ha evangelizado como á ellos”
- quoted (Deut 31:6): “fail thee, nor forsake thee” → “te dejará ni te desamparará”
- flag: Q0: Henry's 'unto us is this gospel preached, as well as unto them' given wholly in the RV1909's words of He 4:2 ('también á nosotros se nos ha evangelizado como á ellos').
- flag: Q1: Henry cites He 13:5 but the KJV words listed are Dt 31:6; RV1909 Dt 31:6 used (He 13:5 RV1909 reads 'No te desampararé, ni te dejaré').

### 595 Deut 31:14-22 The Israelites' apostacy foretold, A song given to be witness against them.
- quoted (Deut 31:15): “the door of the tabernacle” → “la puerta del tabernáculo”
- quoted (Heb 4:12): “is a discerner of the thoughts and intents” → “discierne los pensamientos y las intenciones”

### 596 Deut 31:23-30 
- quoted (Deut 31:29): “I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt yourselves” → “yo sé que después de mi muerte, ciertamente os corromperéis”
- flag: source oddity: 'deposited in, or rather by the side, of the ark' (comma misplaced); translated for sense.

### 598 Deut 32:3-6 The character of God, The character of Israel.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Deut 32:4 ('He is a Rock', Henry's quotation marks): RV1909 'El es la Roca' used (verbatim, no accent on the capital).
- flag: 'His work is perfect' (Dt 32:4): RV1909 has 'cuya obra es perfecta', which can't stand alone in Henry's quotation marks; translated 'Su obra es perfecta'.
- flag: source oddity: 'the sin of Israel was not habitual, notorious, unrepented sin; which is a certain mark of the children of Satan' reads against Dt 32:5 (their spot is not that of his children); translated as it stands.

### 599 Deut 32:7-14 The great things God had done for Israel.
- quoted (1Pet 2:24): “bare our sins in his own body on the tree” → “llevó nuestros pecados en su cuerpo sobre el madero”

### 600 Deut 32:15-18 The judgments which would come upon them for their sins.
- translated, not quoted (Rom 13:14; RV1909 Ro 13:14 ('no hagáis caso de la carne en sus deseos') says it differently; Henry's 'make provision for the flesh' would be lost.): “provision for the flesh to fulfil the lusts”
- flag: 'Jeshurun' kept as the RV1909 spells it (Dt 32:15); Henry's glosses 'an upright people' / 'a seeing people' translated.
- flag: 'candid' (open-minded) rendered 'amplitud de criterio'.

### 602 Deut 32:26-38 Deserved vengeance withheld.
- flag: 'What will be in the end thereof?' (Jer 5:31, KJV 'what will ye do in the end thereof?'): RV1909 '¿Qué pues haréis á su fin?' differs from Henry's form; translated.

### 607 Deut 33:6-23 The blessings of the twelve tribes.
- quoted (Deut 33:16): “the good will of Him that dwelt in the bush” → “la gracia del que habitó en la zarza”
- flag: Q0: KJV 'the good will of Him that dwelt in the bush'; RV1909 has 'la gracia' for 'good will'.

### 608 Deut 33:24-25 Strength to believers.
- quoted (Eph 6:15): “shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace” → “con el apresto del evangelio de paz”
- quoted (Deut 33:25): “As thy days, so shall thy strength” → “como tus días tu fortaleza”
- flag: Q1: KJV 'As thy days, so shall thy strength be'; RV1909 'como tus días tu fortaleza' (plural 'días'), while Henry goes on to explain the singular 'day'.

### 609 Deut 33:26-29 The excellency of Israel.
- quoted (Deut 33:28): “shall dwell in safety alone” → “habitará confiado solo”

### 611 Deut 34:5-8 The death and burial of Moses, The mourning of the people.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Deut 34:5 ('at the mouth of the Lord', Henry's quotation marks): RV1909 'conforme al dicho de Jehová' used; the KJV's literal 'mouth' is lost, though Henry's own gloss 'according to the will of God' still fits.

### 612 Deut 34:9-12 Joshua succeeds Moses, The praise of Moses.
- quoted (Heb 7:19): “the law made nothing perfect” → “nada perfeccionó la ley”
- flag: source punctuation: no full stop after 'He 7:19' before 'It brings'; one added.
- flag: 'That honour was reserved for Joshua, our Lord Jesus, of whom Joshua was a type' reads oddly (Joshua and Jesus run together); translated 'para Josué, para nuestro Señor Jesús'.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 614 Josh 1:5-9 God promises to assist Joshua.

### 615 Josh 1:10-15 Preparation to pass over Jordan.
