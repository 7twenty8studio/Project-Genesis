# To review: es commentary batch 096

### 3896 Heb 10:19-25 An argument for holy boldness in the believer's access to God through Jesus Christ, And for steadfastness in faith, and mutual love and duty.
- quoted (Heb 10:19, Heb 10:20): “by the blood of Jesus” → “por la sangre de Jesucristo”
- flag: Heb 10:19: KJV "Jesus", RV1909 "Jesucristo" (kept in the quotation)
- flag: "bodies washed with pure water" (Heb 10:22) given with the RV1909's "agua limpia"; "adorn the doctrine of God their Saviour" (Tit 2:10; RV1909 "adornen en todo la doctrina de nuestro Salvador Dios") translated in Henry's wording

### 3897 Heb 10:26-31 The danger of apostacy.
- flag: "Him that cometh unto Christ, he will in no wise cast out" echoes Jn 6:37 in the third person (RV1909 "al que á mí viene, no le hecho fuera"); Henry's wording translated

### 3898 Heb 10:32-39 The sufferings of believers, and encouragement to maintain their holy profession.
- flag: typo read as meant: "great trails" (trials)

### 3900 Heb 11:4-7 It is set forth by instances from Abel to Noah.
- quoted (Heb 11:5): “he should not see death” → “para no ver muerte”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “huir de la ira que vendrá”
- flag: "By faith Abel, being dead, yet speaketh" (Heb 11:4) given with the RV1909's "difunto, aun habla"; "heirs of the righteousness of faith" (Heb 11:7) with "heredero de la justicia que es por la fe" in the plural
- flag: "Enoch" as "Henoch" (glossary); the RV1909 quotation itself has "Enoc"

### 3901 Heb 11:8-19 By Abraham and his descendants.
- quoted (Heb 11:16): “God is not ashamed to be called” → “Dios no se avergüenza de llamarse”

### 3902 Heb 11:20-31 By Jacob, Joseph, Moses, the Israelites, and Rahab.
- quoted (Heb 11:20): “Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau, concerning things to come” → “bendijo Isaac á Jacob y á Esaú respecto á cosas que habían de ser”
- translated, not quoted (Heb 11:26; Henry builds the words into his own comparison ("greater riches than the treasures of the richest empire"); the RV1909 run is broken by "el vituperio de Cristo"): “greater riches than the treasures”
- quoted (Heb 11:25): “with the people of God” → “con el pueblo de Dios”
- flag: "The Lord will make even Babylon fall before the faith of his people": so the source (Heb 11:30 speaks of Jericho); translated as written
- flag: "no man knoweth love or hatred" echoes Ec 9:1 (RV1909 "no sabe el hombre ni el amor ni el odio"); Henry's wording translated
- flag: "recompence of reward" (KJV Heb 11:26; RV1909 "remuneración") translated "la remuneración del galardón"

### 3903 Heb 11:32-38 By other Old Testament believers.
- flag: "the roaring lion that goeth about seeking whom he may devour" (1 P 5:8): RV1909 "león rugiente, anda alrededor buscando á quien devore"; given with "que anda" and "buscando á quien devore" from it
- flag: "a better resurrection" (Heb 11:35) given with the RV1909's "mejor resurrección"; "the world is not worthy" echoes Heb 11:38 (RV1909 "el mundo no era digno")

### 3905 Heb 12:1-11 An exhortation to be constant and persevere, The example of Christ is set forth, and the gracious design of God in all the sufferings believers endured.
- flag: "the sin that does so easily beset us" (KJV Heb 12:1): RV1909 "el peso del pecado que nos rodea"; Henry's explanation translated with "nos asedia"
- flag: title: the source joins two titles with a comma and a capital; given as two sentences

### 3906 Heb 12:12-17 Peace and holiness are recommended, with cautions against despising spiritual blessings.
- flag: typo read as meant: "there can be not right peace" (no right peace)
- flag: "follow peace ... and holiness" (Heb 12:14): RV1909 "Seguid la paz con todos, y la santidad"; Henry's wording translated with "seguir"

### 3907 Heb 12:18-29 The New Testament dispensation shown to be much more excellent than the Old.
- quoted (Heb 12:28): “with reverence and godly fear” → “con temor y reverencia”
- quoted (Heb 12:28): “with reverence and godly fear” → “con temor y reverencia”
- flag: Heb 12:28 "with reverence and godly fear" is RV1909 "con temor y reverencia" (no "godly"), used at both places
- flag: "See that you do not refuse Him who speaketh from heaven" (Heb 12:25) given with the RV1909's "Mirad que no desechéis al que habla"

### 3908 Heb 13:1-6 Exhortations to various duties, and to be content with what Providence allots.
- quoted (Titus 2:14): “himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works” → “para sí un pueblo propio, celoso de buenas obras”
- flag: Henry's own expanded rendering of Heb 13:5 ("I will never, no, never leave thee, no, never forsake thee", to show the Greek's five negatives) translated literally in his quotation marks; RV1909 "No te desampararé, ni te dejaré"

### 3909 Heb 13:7-15 To respect the instructions of faithful pastors, with cautions against being carried away by strange doctrines.
- quoted (Heb 13:13): “Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp” → “Salgamos pues á él fuera del real”
- quoted (Heb 13:15): “The sacrifice of praise to God” → “á Dios siempre sacrificio de alabanza”
- flag: typo read as meant: "in the Old Testament day. as in the gospel day" (stray full stop)
- flag: "the Bishop of their souls" (1 P 2:25) given with the RV1909's "Obispo" ("de sus almas" for Henry's "their")
- flag: "Sin, sinners, nor death, will not suffer us" (double negative) translated as meant

### 3910 Heb 13:16-21 Further exhortations to duties, that relate to God, to our neighbour, and to those set over us in the Lord.
- quoted (Heb 13:17): “with joy, and not with grief” → “con alegría, y no gimiendo”
- flag: Heb 13:17 "not with grief" is RV1909 "no gimiendo" (kept in the quotation)

### 3912 James 1:1-11 How to apply to God under troubles, and how to behave in prosperous and in adverse circumstances.
- quoted (James 2:5): “rich in faith and heirs of the kingdom” → “ricos en fe, y herederos del reino”
- flag: "To any that asketh, it shall be given" echoes Stg 1:5 (RV1909 "y le será dada"); Henry's wording translated

### 3913 James 1:12-18 To look upon all evil as proceeding from ourselves, and all good from God.
- flag: "crown of life" (Stg 1:12) and "first-fruits of his creatures" (Stg 1:18) given with the RV1909's "corona de vida" and "primicias de sus criaturas"

### 3915 James 1:22-25 And of living according thereto.
- quoted (Col 1:12): “the inheritance of the saints in light” → “la suerte de los santos en luz”
- flag: "not for his deeds ... but in his deed" turns on KJV Stg 1:25 "blessed in his deed"; RV1909 "bienaventurado en su hecho", so "en su hecho" keeps his point
- flag: Col 1:12 "inheritance" is RV1909 "suerte" (kept in the quotation)

### 3916 James 1:26-27 The difference between vain pretences and real religion.
- flag: "False religious may be known": perhaps a slip for "False religion(s)"; translated as "Los falsos religiosos" (false religious people)
- flag: "faith that worketh by love" (Gá 5:6): RV1909 "la fe que obra por la caridad"; Henry's "love" kept as "amor" in his own sentence
