# To review: es commentary batch 020

### 682 Josh 24:15-28 Joshua renews the covenant between the people and God.
- quoted (John 4:24, John 4:25): “worship him in spirit and in truth” → “los que le adoran, en espíritu y en verdad”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 8:7 ('The carnal mind ... is enmity against God'): RV1909 'la intención de la carne es enemistad contra Dios' used; Henry's 'of man' carried into the next clause ('la mente del hombre').
- flag: unlisted quotation of Josh 24:15 ('Choose you whom ye will serve'): RV1909 'escogeos … á quién sirváis' (its 'hoy' left out, as Henry leaves out 'this day').
- flag: unlisted quotation of Josh 24:18 ('We also will serve the Lord'): RV1909 'nosotros, pues, también serviremos á Jehová'.
- flag: 'he will put his fear into our hearts, that we shall not depart from him' echoes Jer 32:40; translated, not quoted.

### 683 Josh 24:29-33 Joshua's death, Joseph's bones buried, The state of Israel.
- quoted (Rev 1:5, Rev 1:6): “Unto him that loved us, and washed us from our sins in his own blood, and hath made us kings and priests unto God and his Father, to him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen” → “Al que nos amó, y nos ha lavado de nuestros pecados con su sangre, Y nos ha hecho reyes y sacerdotes para Dios y su Padre; á él sea gloria é imperio para siempre jamás. Amén.”
- flag: RV1909 Rev 1:5-6 has 'con su sangre' where the KJV has 'in his own blood', and 'imperio' for 'dominion'.

### 684 Judg 1:1-8 Proceedings of the tribes of Judah and Simeon.
- quoted (Judg 1:1): “after the death of Joshua” → “después de la muerte de Josué”

### 685 Judg 1:9-20 Hebron and other cities taken.
- quoted (Matt 5:5): “Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth” → “Bienaventurados los mansos: porque ellos recibirán la tierra por heredad.”

### 687 Judg 2:1-5 The angel of the Lord rebukes the people.
- quoted (Eph 5:11): “fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness” → “con las obras infructuosas de las tinieblas”
- quoted (Prov 22:5): “thorns and snares are in the way of the froward” → “espinas y lazos hay en el camino del perverso”
- flag: Eph 5:11: the RV1909's verb 'comuniquéis' (a command) doesn't fit Henry's sentence, so 'tienen comunión' is Henry's wording and only 'con las obras infructuosas de las tinieblas' is quoted.

### 688 Judg 2:6-23 The wickedness of the new generation after Joshua.
- flag: Unlisted echoes translated, not quoted: Jer 17:9 (RV1909 'Engañoso es el corazón ... y perverso'), 1 Thess 5:17 ('Orad sin cesar', here 'orar sin cesar'), Eph 3:17 ('arraigados y fundados en amor' matches the RV1909; 'Cristo habite por la fe en nuestros corazones' with Henry's 'our' for the RV1909's 'vuestros'), Heb 12:14 ('Seguid ... la santidad').

### 689 Judg 3:1-7 The nations left to prove Israel.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 690 Judg 3:8-11 Othniel delivers Israel.
- quoted (Judg 3:10): “The Spirit of the Lord came upon” → “El espíritu de Jehová fué sobre”
- flag: Judg 3:10: the RV1909 writes 'espíritu' in lower case; kept in the quotation, while Henry's next sentence has 'Espíritu'.

### 691 Judg 3:12-30 Ehud delivers Israel from Eglon.
- quoted (Judg 3:12): “Eglon, the king of Moab” → “Eglón rey de Moab”

### 692 Judg 3:31-31 Shamgar delivers and judges Israel.
- flag: unlisted echo of 2 Cor 4:7: RV1909 'la alteza del poder sea de Dios'; 'la alteza del poder' used, the rest Henry's wording ('may appear to be').

### 693 Judg 4:1-3 Israel again revolts, and is oppressed by Jabin.
- quoted (Judg 4:3): “Israel cried unto the Lord” → “los hijos de Israel clamaron á Jehová”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Prov 1:32: RV1909 'la prosperidad de los necios los echará á perder' (future, where Henry has 'destroys them').
- flag: Judg 4:3: the RV1909 has 'los hijos de Israel' where Henry has 'Israel'.

### 695 Judg 4:10-16 Sisera defeated.
- quoted (Judg 4:14): “is not the Lord gone” → “¿No ha salido Jehová delante de ti?”
- flag: source typo: 'Siser's' read as 'Sisera's'.
- flag: Judg 4:14: Henry's 'is not the Lord gone before thee?' is quoted whole from the RV1909 ('¿No ha salido Jehová delante de ti?'), as he quotes it.

### 696 Judg 4:17-24 Sisera put to death by Jael.
- flag: 'what we were sick for, God can make us sick of': Henry's wordplay on 'sick for / sick of' rendered by sense ('suspirábamos ... nos hastíe').
- flag: echo of 1 Cor 1:27: RV1909 'lo flaco del mundo ... para avergonzar lo fuerte'; 'lo flaco del mundo' kept, the verb fitted to Henry's sentence; not a listed quotation.

### 698 Judg 5:6-11 The distress and deliverance of Israel.
- flag: 'the walls of salvation, whence the waters of life are drawn': 'walls' read as 'wells' (a source slip), translated 'los pozos'; cf. Isa 12:3 and the 'places of drawing water' of Judg 5:11 (RV1909 'los abrevaderos').

### 699 Judg 5:12-23 Some commended, others censured.
- quoted (Phil 2:21): “All seek their own” → “Todos buscan lo suyo propio”

### 700 Judg 5:24-31 Sisera's mother disappointed.
- translated, not quoted (Judg 5:28; Henry's 'looked for his return' means she waited for him; the RV1909's 'se asoma á la ventana' (looked out) would change his sentence): “The mother of Sisera looked”
- flag: 'not in the least fearing his success' read as 'fearing for his success'.
- flag: 'shine for ever as the sun in the firmament' blends Judg 5:31, Dan 12:3 and Matt 13:43; translated, not quoted.

### 701 Judg 6:1-6 Israel oppressed by Midianites.
- flag: 'take it away in the season thereof' echoes Hos 2:9 (RV1909 'tomaré mi trigo á su tiempo'); translated.

### 703 Judg 6:11-24 Gideon set to deliver Israel.
- flag: source: a comma after 'there' ends a sentence ('...his Spirit there, The Angel'); read as a full stop.
- flag: 'valiant': the RV1909 has 'varón esforzado' at Judg 6:12; Bishop Hall's saying translated with 'valiente'.
- flag: echo of Judg 6:17 with the person changed ('si he hallado gracia delante de ti, me des señal'); translated.

### 706 Judg 7:1-8 Gideon's army reduced.
- flag: echo of Gal 4:18 (KJV 'zealously affected ... in a good thing'): RV1909 'celosos en bien' used; not a listed quotation.

### 708 Judg 7:16-22 The defeat of the Midianites.
- quoted (1Cor 1:27): “the foolish things of the world to confound the wise” → “Lo necio del mundo escogió Dios, para avergonzar á los sabios”
- quoted (2Cor 4:7): “the excellency of the power” → “la alteza del poder”
- translated, not quoted (Judg 7:18; listed verse Judg 7:18 has '¡Por Jehová y Gedeón!'; Henry's words are those of Judg 7:20, used verbatim from there ('la espada de Jehová y de Gedeón')): “the sword of the Lord and of Gideon”
- flag: quote 2 is Judg 7:20, not 7:18: RV1909 '¡La espada de Jehová y de Gedeón!' used verbatim.
- flag: 1 Cor 1:27: the RV1909 puts 'escogió Dios' after 'lo necio del mundo'; Henry's 'God chose' kept in that order.

### 711 Judg 8:4-12 Succoth and Penuel refuse to relieve Gideon.
- flag: Judg 8:4 'faint, yet pursuing' is the KJV's wording; the RV1909 has 'cansados del alcance' (tired from the chase), so Henry's point ('fainting, and yet pursuing') is translated, not quoted.

### 712 Judg 8:13-17 Succoth and Penuel punished.
- flag: 'taught with the briers and thorns' plays on the KJV of Judg 8:16 ('taught the men of Succoth'); the RV1909 has 'castigó ... con ellos' (punished).

### 714 Judg 8:22-28 Gideon declines the government, but given occasion for idolatry.
- flag: echo of 1 John 2:16: RV1909 'la concupiscencia de los ojos', 'la soberbia de la vida' used; not a listed quotation.
- flag: source title reads 'given occasion' (for 'gives occasion'); translated as 'da ocasión'.

### 715 Judg 8:29-35 Gideon's death, Israel's ingratitude.
- quoted (Judg 8:33): “As soon as Gideon was dead” → “como murió Gedeón”
- flag: echo of Rom 12:21: RV1909 'No seas vencido de lo malo; mas vence con el bien el mal', fitted to Henry's 'resolve ... not to be overcome' ('no ser vencidos de lo malo, sino vencer con el bien el mal'); not a listed quotation.
- flag: 'went after Baalim': RV1909 Judg 8:33 'fornicaron en pos de los Baales'; Henry's verb translated ('se fueron en pos de los Baales').

### 717 Judg 9:7-21 Jotham rebukes the Shechemites.
- quoted (Judg 9:9): “to be promoted over the trees” → “ir á ser grande sobre los árboles”
- flag: Judg 9:9: Henry's 'or, as the margin reads it, to go up and down for the trees' refers to the KJV margin; the RV1909 has 'por ir á ser grande sobre los árboles' and no such margin.
- flag: Judg 9:14-15: Henry's 'bramble or thistle'; the RV1909 has 'escaramujo'.

### 719 Judg 9:30-49 Abimelech destroys Shechem.
- flag: source typo: 'Schechemites' read as 'Shechemites'.
- flag: 'a refuge of lies' echoes Isa 28:15, 17 (RV1909 'la acogida de la mentira'); translated.

### 720 Judg 9:50-57 Abimelech slain.
- flag: source typo: 'thou has given' read as 'thou hast given'.
- flag: 'thy spirit' is lower case in the source; kept as 'tu espíritu'.

### 724 Judg 11:1-11 Jephtah and the Gileadites.
- flag: source typo in the title: 'Jephtah' read as 'Jephthah'.

### 725 Judg 11:12-28 He attempts to make peace.
- flag: 'the readiest to make advances to obtain' lacks an object in the English; read as 'to obtain it' (peace).
