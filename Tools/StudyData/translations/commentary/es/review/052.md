- quoted (1Cor 15:58): “your labour is not in vain in the Lord” → “vuestro trabajo en el Señor no es vano”
- flag: 'be ye stedfast, unmovable' (1 Co 15:58) rendered with RV1909's 'estad firmes y constantes'.

### 2390 Isa 26:1-4 
- flag: Henry's 'Thou wilt keep him in peace; in perfect peace' (Is 26:3) rendered with RV1909's 'Tú le guardarás en completa paz', split as Henry splits it.

### 2391 Isa 26:5-11 
- quoted (Heb 10:31): “it is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God” → “horrenda cosa es caer en las manos del Dios vivo”
- flag: 'The way of the just is evenness' (KJV Is 26:7 'uprightness', margin 'evenness'): rendered with RV1909's 'El camino del justo es rectitud' (unlisted).

### 2393 Isa 26:20-21 
- quoted (Isa 26:20): “as it were for a little moment” → “un poquito, por un momento”
- flag: The English text begins with a stray ',21' (from the range '20,21'); kept as it stands so the numbers match.
- flag: RV1909 Is 26:20 has 'un poquito, por un momento' where the KJV (and Henry) has 'as it were for a little moment'.

### 2394 Isa 27:1-5 
- quoted (Heb 2:14): “destroy him that had the power of death, that is, the devil” → “al que tenía el imperio de la muerte, es á saber, al diablo”
- flag: RV1909 He 2:14 has 'el imperio de la muerte' for KJV 'the power of death'. 'a strength to the needy' follows RV1909 Is 25:4 'fortaleza al menesteroso'.

### 2395 Isa 27:6-13 
- flag: sensitive: Henry on the Jews as a people kept separate, 'a continued warning against sin' (Is 27:6-13); translated in full.
- flag: Unlisted quotations: Mr 4:39 'Peace, be still' as RV1909 'Calla, enmudece'; Lc 4:19 'el año agradable del Señor' verbatim.

### 2397 Isa 28:5-15

- flag: Henry's 'the Lord of hosts' rendered 'Jehová de los ejércitos' (Is 28:5); 'the same precept and the same line' echoes Is 28:10 (RV1909 'mandamiento tras mandamiento … renglón tras renglón'), translated with its words.

### 2398 Isa 28:16-22

'the bed is too short, the covering too narrow' follows KJV Is 28:20 (RV1909: 'la cama es tan angosta que no basta, y la cubierta estrecha'); translated.
