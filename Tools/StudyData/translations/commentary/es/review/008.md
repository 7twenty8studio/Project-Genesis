# To review: es commentary batch 008

### 219 Ex 7:14-25 The river is turned into blood, The distress of the Egyptians.
- quoted (Ex 7:20): “in the sight of Pharaoh” → “en presencia de Faraón”
- quoted (Job 36:13): “the hypocrites in heart heap up wrath” → “los hipócritas de corazón”
- flag: quote 1 (Job 36:13): the RV1909 has 'los hipócritas de corazón lo irritarán más', not 'heap up wrath'; only 'los hipócritas de corazón' quoted, 'acumulan ira' translated from the KJV sense.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 9:12 ('no ha cesado su furor, antes todavía su mano extendida', RV1909) used for 'God's anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still'; 'God's' left implicit.

### 220 Ex 8:1-15 The plague of frogs.
- translated, not quoted (Ex 8:8; Henry reports Pharaoh's promise in the third person ('he will let'); the RV1909 has the first person 'dejaré ir al pueblo'.): “will let the people go”
- quoted (Ex 8:15): “there was respite, he hardened his heart” → “viendo Faraón que le habían dado reposo, agravó su corazón”

### 221 Ex 8:16-19 The plague of lice.
- quoted (Job 14:19): “out of the dust of the earth” → “el polvo de la tierra”
- quoted (Ex 8:17): “the dust of the earth” → “el polvo de la tierra”
- quoted (Ex 8:19): “This is the finger of God” → “Dedo de Dios es este”
- flag: quote 0 is listed with Job 14:19 but is Ex 8:16-17 (Aaron smiting 'el polvo de la tierra'); the same RV1909 words stand in both verses.

### 222 Ex 8:20-32 The plague of flies.
- quoted (Ex 8:29): “let not Pharaoh deal deceitfully any more” → “con tal que Faraón no falte más”
- quoted (Gal 6:7): “Be not deceived; God is not mocked” → “No os engañéis: Dios no puede ser burlado”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2 Ti 2:19 ('Conoce el Señor á los que son suyos', RV1909).
- flag: 'driven away in his wickedness' echoes Pr 14:32; rendered 'por su maldad será lanzado' after the RV1909 with 'el pobre pecador' in place of 'el impío' (not counted as a quotation).
- flag: quote 0: Henry's 'But let not Pharaoh deal deceitfully' rendered with the RV1909 'con tal que Faraón no falte más' (Ex 8:29), which reads 'fail no more' rather than 'deceive'.

### 223 Ex 9:1-7 The murrain of beasts.
- quoted (Ex 9:3): “The hand of the Lord” → “la mano de Jehová”
- flag: Mt 10:29 alluded to in Henry's own words ('a sparrow falls not to the ground without our Father'; RV1909 'ni uno de ellos cae á tierra sin vuestro Padre'): translated with Henry's 'our Father' (nuestro Padre), not quoted.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ex 9:4 ('Jehová hará separación', RV1909) for 'the Lord shall sever'.
- flag: 'stony heart', 'heart of flesh' (Ez 36:26) and 'broken and contrite heart' (Sal 51:17) translated as Henry's own phrases.

### 225 Ex 9:13-21 The plague of hail threatened.
- quoted (Acts 28:24): “Some believed the things which were spoken” → “algunos asentían á lo que se decía”
- quoted (Ex 9:20): “among the servants of Pharaoh” → “de los siervos de Faraón”
- flag: quote 0 (Hch 28:24): the RV1909 has 'asentían' where the KJV has 'believed'.

### 226 Ex 9:22-35 The plague of hail inflicted.
- translated, not quoted (Ex 9:33; the RV1909 reads 'salido Moisés de con Faraón de la ciudad', a participle clause with words Henry doesn't have; his plain statement translated.): “Moses went out of the city”
- flag: 'the Lord is righteous' (Ex 9:27) rendered 'Jehová es justo' as in the RV1909, in Henry's indirect speech; 'God must be justified when he speaks' echoes Sal 51:4, translated.

### 232 Ex 12:1-20 The beginning of the year changed, The passover instituted.

- flag: 'receive the atonement', Ro 5:11: Henry relies on the KJV's 'atonement'; the RV1909 reads 'hemos ahora recibido la reconciliación'. Translated 'expiación' as Henry's term.
