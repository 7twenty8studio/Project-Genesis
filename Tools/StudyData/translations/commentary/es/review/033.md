# To review: es commentary batch 033

### 1280 Job 4:7-11 And maintains that God's judgments are for the wicked.
- quoted (Eccl 9:2): “there is one event” → “un mismo suceso ocurre”
- quoted (Eccl 9:2): “to the righteous and to the wicked” → “al justo y al impío”

### 1281 Job 4:12-21 The vision of Eliphaz.
- quoted (Job 4:17): “be more just than God” → “más justo que Dios”
- quoted (Job 4:17): “more pure than his Maker” → “más limpio que el que lo hizo”
- flag: Henry's first 'more just, more pure, than God' (his own wording) is translated; the second, quoted form uses the RV1909 ('más limpio que el que lo hizo' for 'more pure than his Maker').

### 1282 Job 5:1-5 Eliphaz urges that the sin of sinners in their ruin.
- flag: Source title is garbled ('that the sin of sinners in their ruin', a verb missing); rendered 'termina en su ruina' from the sense of the section.
- flag: Henry glosses the word 'saints' ('holy, or more strictly, consecrated ones'); the RV1909 (Job 5:1) also has 'santos', so the gloss stands; Spanish 'santos' serves for both 'saints' and 'holy'.

### 1283 Job 5:6-16 God is to be regarded in affliction.
- quoted (Job 5:7): “as the sparks fly upward” → “como las centellas se levantan para volar por el aire”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Stg 5:13: '¿Está alguno entre vosotros afligido? haga oración.' (RV1909; Henry's KJV has no 'among you').

### 1284 Job 5:17-27 The happy end of God's correction.
- quoted (Job 5:17): “Despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty” → “no menosprecies la corrección del Todopoderoso”
- flag: Henry comments on the KJV word 'chastening' ('Call it a chastening'); the RV1909 has 'corrección', so his comment is given with 'corrección'.
- flag: unlisted quotation of He 12:2: 'autor y consumador de la fe' (RV1909).

### 1286 Job 6:8-13 He wishes for death.
- flag: Echo of Sal 39:13 (KJV 'O spare me'), translated; the RV1909 reads 'Déjame, y tomaré fuerzas'.

### 1287 Job 6:14-30 Job reproves his friends as unkind.
- translated, not quoted (Job 27:6; Henry turns Job's 'My righteousness I hold fast' (RV1909 'Mi justicia tengo asida, y no la cederé') into the third person; translated, keeping the RV1909's 'tiene asida'.): “fast, and will not let it go”
- flag: Henry quotes Job 6:21 in the KJV's words ('for now ye are nothing'); the RV1909 reads 'Ahora ciertamente como ellas sois vosotros', so his quotation is translated.
- flag: Echo of Is 2:22 ('cease ye from man'): RV1909 'Dejaos del hombre', given as 'dejarnos del hombre'.

### 1288 Job 7:1-6 Job's troubles.
- quoted (Rom 14:8): “we live, we live unto the Lord” → “vivimos, para el Señor vivimos”
- flag: 'man's life is a warfare' follows the KJV margin of Job 7:1; the RV1909 has 'tiempo limitado', so it is translated ('milicia').
- flag: Henry's sentence 'and as day-labourers, who have…' lacks a verb; rendered 'y es como la de los jornaleros'.

### 1289 Job 7:7-16 Job expostulates with God.
- flag: Echo of Sal 121:4 (RV1909 'no se adormecerá ni dormirá el que guarda á Israel') put in Henry's present tense.

### 1290 Job 7:17-21 He begs release.
- flag: Henry weighs the KJV's 'preserver of men' (Job 7:20) against 'Observer'; the RV1909 reads 'Guarda de los hombres', which leans to his 'Observer'.

### 1291 Job 8:1-7 Bildad reproves Job.
- quoted (Job 8:2): “How long wilt thou speak these things” → “¿Hasta cuándo hablarás tales cosas”
- flag: Source: 'or that they did for some great transgression' is read as 'died for' ('murieran').

### 1292 Job 8:8-19 Hypocrites will be destroyed.
- translated, not quoted (Job 8:10; The RV1909's 'de su corazón sacarán palabras' is a future question about the ancients; Henry makes it a general present statement about good teachers, so it is translated ('sacan palabras de su corazón').): “utter words out of their heart”

### 1293 Job 8:20-22 Bildad applies God's just dealing to Job.
- translated, not quoted (Job 8:20; The RV1909 says 'Dios no aborrece al perfecto'; Henry builds on the KJV's 'cast away' (cast down, not cast away), which 'aborrece' would lose.): “God will not cast away”

### 1294 Job 9:1-13 Job acknowledges God's justice.
- quoted (Job 9:2): “how should man be just with God” → “cómo se justificará el hombre con Dios”

### 1295 Job 9:14-21 He is not able to contend with God.
- quoted (Job 32:1): “righteous in his own eyes” → “justo en sus ojos”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Jn 1:8: 'Si dijéremos que no tenemos pecado' (RV1909); Henry's 'not only' kept before 'nos engañamos'.

### 1296 Job 9:22-24 Men not to be judged by outward condition.
- flag: Echoes of Lm 3:33 (KJV 'doth not afflict willingly'; RV1909 'no aflige ni congoja de su corazón') and Sal 141:3 (RV1909 'Pon… guarda á mi boca'), translated.

### 1297 Job 9:25-35 Job complains of troubles.
- quoted (Jude 1:24): “faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy” → “delante de su gloria irreprensibles, con grande alegría”
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's 'Daysman' (Job 9:33) as 'Umpire'; the RV1909 has 'árbitro', so 'Árbitro o Juez' is used.
- flag: Quote 0: the RV1909 order is 'delante de su gloria irreprensibles' (KJV 'faultless before the presence of his glory'); its words kept in its order.
- flag: unlisted echoes in Henry's words: Mt 11:28 'trabajados y cargados' and Sal 107:30 'al puerto que deseaban' (RV1909 wording kept); He 7:25 and Stg 4:7 translated.

### 1299 Job 10:8-13 He pleads with God as his Maker.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sal 100:3: 'nos hizo, y no nosotros á nosotros mismos' (RV1909, its accent kept).
- flag: 'instruments of unrighteousness' rendered with the RV1909's 'instrumentos de iniquidad' (Ro 6:13).

### 1300 Job 10:14-22 He complains of God's severity.
- flag: Echoes of Jud 13 ('blackness of darkness for ever'; RV1909 'la oscuridad de las tinieblas') and 1 Ts 1:10 (RV1909 'nos libró de la ira que ha de venir', here in Henry's present 'libra').

### 1302 Job 11:7-12 God's perfections and almighty power.
- quoted (Job 11:12): “born like a wild ass's colt” → “aunque nazca como el pollino del asno montés”
- flag: 'empty, so the word is' is Henry's note on the Hebrew behind 'vain'; the RV1909 also has 'El hombre vano'.

### 1303 Job 11:13-20 Zophar assures Job of blessings if he repented.
- quoted (Job 11:15): “Then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot” → “Entonces levantarás tu rostro limpio de mancha”
- quoted (Heb 10:22): “in full assurance of faith” → “en plena certidumbre de fe”
- translated, not quoted (Heb 10:22; The RV1909 has 'purificados los corazones de mala conciencia'; Henry's 'sprinkled' ties the verse to the blood of Christ just mentioned, so his word is kept and translated.): “sprinkled from an evil conscience”
- flag: Quote 2: 'rociados y limpios' adds 'y limpios' to make Spanish sense of 'sprinkled from' (the KJV's construction); a reviewer may prefer 'rociados para quedar libres de mala conciencia'.

### 1305 Job 12:6-11 The wicked often prosper.
- flag: The source ends with '(Job 12:12-25)', the next section's verses; kept as given.

### 1306 Job 12:12-25 Job speaks of the wisdom and power of God.
- flag: sensitive: 'the Lord Jesus was crucified by the malice of the Jews'.
- flag: Echo of Ro 11:33 (RV1909 '¡Cuán incomprensibles son sus juicios, é inescrutables sus caminos!') in Henry's order, translated.

### 1307 Job 13:1-12 Job reproves his friends.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Job 13:4: 'médicos nulos' (RV1909) for 'physicians of no value'.

### 1308 Job 13:13-22 He professes his confidence in God.
- flag: Echo of Job 13:15 (RV1909 'aunque me matare, en él esperaré') put in Henry's first person plural and translated.

### 1309 Job 13:23-28 Job entreats to know his sins.
- flag: 'possess the sins of their youth' follows the KJV of Job 13:26; the RV1909 reads 'me haces cargo de los pecados de mi mocedad' (its 'escribes contra mí amarguras' kept in 'escribe contra nosotros amarguras').

### 1310 Job 14:1-6 Job speaks of man's life.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hch 17:28: 'en él vivimos, y nos movemos' (RV1909).

### 1311 Job 14:7-15 Of man's death.
- flag: 'miserable comforters' given with the RV1909's 'consoladores molestos' (Job 16:2).

### 1312 Job 14:16-22 By sin man is subject to corruption.
- flag: Henry works Job 14:22 into his sentence ('flesh… shall have pain… soul… shall mourn'); 'se dolerá' and 'se entristecerá' follow the RV1909 ('su carne sobre él se dolerá, y entristecerse ha en él su alma').
- flag: Echo of Dt 32:29 (RV1909 '¡Ojalá fueran sabios… y entendieran su postrimería!') translated.

### 1313 Job 15:1-16 Eliphaz reproves Job.
- flag: 'restraining prayer' (KJV Job 15:4) given with the RV1909's 'menoscabas la oración' ('menoscabar la oración').

### 1314 Job 15:17-35 The unquietness of wicked men.
- quoted (Heb 12:11): “no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous, but grievous, nevertheless, afterward it yieldeth the peaceable” → “ningún castigo al presente parece ser causa de gozo, sino de tristeza; mas después da fruto apacible de justicia á los que en él son ejercitados”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry quotes the whole of He 12:11, so the whole RV1909 verse is used (its 'á' kept).
- flag: Echo of Ro 8:35 (RV1909 '¿Quién nos apartará del amor de Cristo?') put in Henry's third person.

### 1315 Job 16:1-5 Job reproves his friends.
- flag: Job 16:2 'miserable comforters are they all' given with the RV1909's words ('consoladores molestos sois todos vosotros') in Henry's third person.

### 1316 Job 16:6-16 He represents his case as deplorable.
- flag: unlisted quotations: Is 53:3 'varón de dolores' and Mt 5:4 'los que lloran: porque ellos recibirán consolación' (RV1909, its colon dropped to fit Henry's sentence).
