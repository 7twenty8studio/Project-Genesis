# To review: es commentary batch 026

### 969 1Kings 10:1-13 The queen of Sheba's visit to Solomon.
- flag: Sal 84:4 (RV1909 'Bienaventurados los que habitan en tu casa: perpetuamente te alabarán') is turned by Henry into the third person ('his house… praising him'); translated.
- flag: Echoes of 1 R 10:7 ('ni aun la mitad fué lo que se me dijo') and 2 Co 5:8 ('partir del cuerpo, y estar presentes al Señor') translated in Henry's syntax.

### 970 1Kings 10:14-29 Solomon's wealth.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 R 10:21 ('no era de estima', RV1909).
- flag: Source typo 'that all the wealth' read as 'than all the wealth' ('que toda la riqueza').
- flag: 'vexation of spirit' rendered with the RV1909's 'aflicción de espíritu' (Ec 1:14).

### 971 1Kings 11:1-8 Solomon's wives and concubines, His idolatry.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Co 10:12 ('el que piensa estar firme, mire no caiga', RV1909).

### 973 1Kings 11:14-25 Solomon's adversaries.
- flag: Source 'If against us' lacks a subject (presumably 'If God be against us'); rendered 'Si él está contra nosotros'.
- flag: 'the very grasshopper shall be a burden' echoes Ec 12:5 (RV1909 'se agravará la langosta'); translated with 'langosta'.

### 974 1Kings 11:26-40 Jeroboam's promotion.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Pr 19:21 ('el consejo de Jehová permanecerá', RV1909); the first half follows Henry's wording.
- flag: Ahijah rendered 'Ahías' (RV1909 in 1 R 11:29), one of the glossary's forms.

### 975 1Kings 11:41-43 The death of Solomon.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ec 1:14 ('vanidad y aflicción de espíritu', RV1909), in Henry's own quotation marks.
- flag: 'one greater than Solomon' (Mt 12:42; RV1909 'más que Salomón') translated.

### 976 1Kings 12:1-15 Rehoboam's accession, The people's petition, His rough answer.
- quoted (1Kings 12:14): “the counsel of the young men” → “conforme al consejo de los mancebos”

### 977 1Kings 12:16-24 Ten tribes revolt.
- quoted (1Kings 12:24): “to the word of the Lord” → “la palabra de Jehová”

### 979 1Kings 13:1-10 Jeroboam's sin reproved.
- translated, not quoted (1Kings 13:1; Henry's 'the word of the Lord will endure for ever' echoes 1 P 1:25, not the 'por palabra de Jehová' of 1 R 13:1; translated as his own sentence.): “the word of the Lord”

### 981 1Kings 13:23-34 The disobedient prophet is slain, Jeroboam's obstinacy.
- quoted (1Kings 13:33): “Jeroboam returned not from his evil way” → “no se tornó Jeroboam de su mal camino”

### 982 1Kings 14:1-6 Abijah being sick, his mother consults Ahijah.
- flag: Henry's 'At that time' (1 R 14:1) rendered with the RV1909's 'En aquel tiempo', his quotation marks kept.
- flag: unlisted quotations of Hch 20:27 ('todo el consejo de Dios') and Mt 24:51 ('su parte con los hipócritas'), RV1909; 1 R 22:8 ('nunca me profetiza bien, sino solamente mal') echoed and translated.
- flag: Abijah given as the glossary's 'Abías' (the RV1909 reads 'Abía' in 1 R 14:1).

### 983 1Kings 14:7-20 The destruction of Jeroboam's house.
- quoted (1Kings 14:13): “in the house of Jeroboam” → “en la casa de Jeroboam”
- quoted (1Kings 14:7): “the Lord God of Israel” → “Jehová Dios de Israel”

### 984 1Kings 14:21-31 Rehoboam's wicked reign.
- quoted (1Kings 14:25): “Shishak, king of Egypt, came” → “subió Sisac rey de Egipto”
- flag: 1 R 14:25: the RV1909's 'subió' (went up) for Henry's 'came'.
- flag: Lm 4:1 ('¡Cómo se ha oscurecido el oro! ¡Cómo el buen oro se ha demudado!', RV1909) echoed in Henry's syntax with its words 'oscurecido'/'demudado'.

### 985 1Kings 15:1-8 Wicked reign of Abijam, king of Judah.
- quoted (1Kings 15:3): “heart was not perfect with the Lord his God” → “no fué su corazón perfecto con Jehová su Dios”
- quoted (1Kings 15:3): “walked in all the sins of his father” → “anduvo en todos los pecados de su padre”
- flag: 1 R 15:3 quoted in the RV1909's word order ('no fué su corazón perfecto'), introduced by 'En cuanto a Abiam'.

### 986 1Kings 15:9-24 Good reign of Asa, king of Judah.
- quoted (1Kings 15:11): “was right in the eyes of the Lord” → “lo recto ante los ojos de Jehová”
- flag: Is 1:16-17 ('dejad de hacer lo malo: Aprended á hacer bien', RV1909) and Pr 3:5 ('Fíate de Jehová de todo tu corazón') echoed in Henry's syntax; translated.
- flag: Benhadad written 'Ben-adad' (RV1909 'Ben-adad'); not in the glossary.

### 989 1Kings 16:15-28 Reigns of Zimri and Omri in Israel.
- flag: Tibni written 'Tibni' (RV1909 'Thibni', modernized); not in the glossary.

### 990 1Kings 16:29-34 Ahab's wickedness, Hiel rebuilds Jericho.
- flag: Source repeats a garbled run ('devoted to God's honour: he began to build, in defiance of the curse well devoted to God's honour: he began to build, in defiance of the curse well known'); translated as it stands, repetition kept.

### 991 1Kings 17:1-7 Elijah fed by ravens.
- flag: Sal 46:4 ('alegrarán la ciudad de Dios') and Jn 4:14 ('una fuente de agua que salte para vida eterna'), RV1909, echoed in Henry's syntax; translated.

### 992 1Kings 17:8-16 Elijah sent to Zarephath.
- quoted (Luke 4:25): “Many widows were in Israel in the days of Elias” → “muchas viudas había en Israel en los días de Elías”
- quoted (Ps 37:19): “days of famine they shall be satisfied” → “no serán avergonzados en el mal tiempo… en los días de hambre serán hartos”
- flag: Lc 4:25: Henry's 'Elias' is the RV1909's 'Elías', the same name as Elijah in Spanish.
- flag: Mt 15:28 ('Oh mujer, grande es tu fe', RV1909) and Lc 7:9 / Mt 8:10 ('ni aun en Israel') echoed with Henry's change of tense ('great was thy faith'); translated. Ro 4:18 ('en esperanza contra esperanza') echoed and translated.

### 994 1Kings 18:1-16 Elijah sends Ahab notice of his coming.
- quoted (2Chr 11:13): “The priests and the Levites” → “sacerdotes y Levitas”
- quoted (2Chr 11:14): “to Judah and Jerusalem” → “á Judá y á Jerusalem”
- flag: Obadiah given as 'Abdías', the glossary form the RV1909 uses in 1 R 18:3.
- flag: Is 30:20: the RV1909's 'pan de congoja' (KJV 'bread of adversity'; Henry 'affliction') used; 'our eyes see our teachers' translated in Henry's first person.

### 996 1Kings 18:21-40 Elijah's trial of the false prophets.
- quoted (John 20:29): “Blessed are they that have not seen” → “bienaventurados los que no vieron”
- flag: Source typo 'the world of God' read as 'the word of God' ('la palabra de Dios').
- flag: unlisted quotations of 1 R 18:24 ('el Dios que respondiere por fuego, ése sea Dios'), 1 R 18:39 ('¡Jehová es el Dios!') and Is 1:12 ('¿Quién demandó esto de vuestras manos?', Henry 'these things'), RV1909.
- flag: 1 R 18:21 'halt' rendered with the RV1909's 'claudicar'; Stg 5:16 echoed with its 'puede mucho'.

### 997 1Kings 18:41-46 Elijah, by prayer, obtains rain.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Zac 4:10 ('el día de las pequeñeces', RV1909).

### 998 1Kings 19:1-8 Elijah flees to the wilderness.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ef 6:10 ('en el Señor, y en la potencia de su fortaleza', RV1909).
- flag: 1 R 19:4 ('no soy yo mejor que mis padres') put in the third person as Henry does; Fil 1:23 ('ser desatado, y estar con Cristo') echoed as 'partir y estar con Cristo'; translated.

### 999 1Kings 19:9-13 God manifests himself to Elijah.
- quoted (1Kings 19:9): “What doest thou here, Elijah” → “¿Qué haces aquí, Elías?”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 R 19:10 ('yo solo he quedado', RV1909).

### 1000 1Kings 19:14-18 God's answer to Elijah.
- flag: Sal 83:3: 'hidden ones' rendered 'escondidos', the RV1909's word ('tus escondidos'). 2 Ti 2:19 ('Conoce el Señor á los que son suyos') echoed and translated.

### 1001 1Kings 19:19-21 The call of Elisha.
- flag: Sal 110:3 ('en el día de tu poder', RV1909) and 2 P 1:10 ('hacer firme vuestra vocación y elección') echoed in Henry's syntax; translated.

### 1002 1Kings 20:1-11 Benhadad besieges Samaria.
- flag: 'Ahab became desperate' (1 R 20:9-11, where he answers boldly) rendered literally, 'Acab llegó a la desesperación'.
- flag: Ahab's saying (1 R 20:11, RV1909 'no se alabe el que se ciñe, como el que ya se desciñe') is only referred to, and Pr 27:1 echoed and translated.

### 1003 1Kings 20:12-21 Benhadad's defeat.
- quoted (1Kings 20:21): “slew the Syrians with a great slaughter” → “deshizo los Siros con grande estrago”
- flag: 1 R 20:21: the RV1909's 'deshizo' (routed) for 'slew' and 'Siros' for Syrians.

### 1005 1Kings 20:31-43 Ahab makes peace with Benhadad.
- quoted (Isa 26:10): “to the wicked, yet will he not learn righteousness” → “Alcanzará piedad el impío, y no aprenderá justicia”
- quoted (1Kings 20:43): “went to his house, heavy and displeased” → “se fué á su casa triste y enojado”
- flag: Is 26:10: the RV1909 says 'Alcanzará piedad el impío' for 'Let favour be showed to the wicked'; the whole verse half quoted, as Henry quotes it.
- flag: Source 'are busy and there' is garbled; read as 'busy here and there' (1 R 20:40, RV1909 'ocupado á una parte y á otra') and translated 'ocupados aquí y allá'.
- flag: 'the God of Israel is a merciful God' adapts 1 R 20:31 (RV1909 'reyes clementes'); Sal 130:4 ('hay perdón cerca de ti') echoed in the third person; translated.

### 1007 1Kings 21:5-16 Naboth murdered by Jezebel.
- flag: unlisted quotations of Gn 2:18 ('ayuda idónea') and Ef 2:2 ('los hijos de desobediencia'), RV1909.
