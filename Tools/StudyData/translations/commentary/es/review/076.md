### 3308 John 1:1-5 The Divinity of Christ.
- quoted (John 1:1): “The Word was with God” → “el Verbo era con Dios”
- quoted (John 1:3): “All things were made by him” → “Todas las cosas por él fueron hechas”
- quoted (John 1:3): “Without him was not any thing made that was made” → “sin él nada de lo que es hecho, fué hecho”
- flag: "the Word": the RV1909 says "el Verbo"; Henry's explanation compares it with "our words" ("nuestras palabras"), which in Spanish is a different word.
- flag: The source's "His coexistence with the Father. The Word was with God." is joined with a colon so the quotation follows as its proof; no words dropped.

- flag: Q0: the listed run spans two sentences ("...lower world. He was in the world"); only "En el mundo estaba" (Jn 1:10) is quoted.
