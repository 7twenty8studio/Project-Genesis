### 1490 Ps 47:1-4 
- flag: Sal 110:3 echoed ('de buena voluntad en el día de tu poder', RV1909) in Henry's syntax; Sal 47:4 ('El nos elegirá nuestras heredades', RV1909) given in Henry's first person singular, translated.

- flag: unlisted quotation of Hch 5:31 ('dar á Israel arrepentimiento y remisión de pecados', RV1909); 'a reasonable service' (Ro 12:1) rendered 'culto racional'.

### 1492 Ps 48:1-7

- flag: sensitive: 'the overthrow of heathenism'.

### 1493 Ps 48:8-14 
- flag: Sal 121:4 ('no se adormecerá ni dormirá', RV1909) echoed in Henry's present tense; 'loving-kindness' rendered 'misericordia' (Sal 48:9 RV1909).

### 1494 Ps 49:1-5 A call for attention.
- flag: 'the iniquity of our heels' follows the KJV of Sal 49:5 ('the iniquity of my heels'); the RV1909 has 'la iniquidad de mis insidiadores'. Henry's explanation translated.

### 1495 Ps 49:6-14 Folly of worldlings.
- quoted (Dan 12:2): “sleep in the dust” → “duermen en el polvo”
- flag: Dn 12:2: Henry's 'everlasting shame and contempt' given with the RV1909's 'vergüenza y confusión perpetua'.
- flag: Sal 49:8: Henry's 'shall cost very dear' follows the KJV ('the redemption of their soul is precious'); the RV1909 has 'es de gran precio'.

### 1496 Ps 49:15-20 Against fear of death.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Ti 6:7 ('nada hemos traído á este mundo, … sin duda nada podremos sacar', RV1909) in Henry's sentence. Mt 16:26 and Sal 49:18 echoed, translated.

### 1498 Ps 50:7-15 Sacrifices to be changed for prayers.
- flag: 1 S 15:22 ('el obedecer es mejor que los sacrificios', RV1909) and Pr 3:6 ('Reconócelo en todos tus caminos') echoed in Henry's syntax; translated.

### 1499 Ps 50:16-23 Sincere obedience required.
- flag: 'set their sins in order' follows the KJV of Sal 50:21; the RV1909 has 'pondrélas delante de tus ojos'. Translated. Ro 12:11 ('ardientes en espíritu', RV1909) echoed.

### 1500 Ps 51:1-6 The psalmist prays for mercy, humbly confessing and lamenting his sins.
- quoted (Prov 22:15): “is bound in the heart of a child” → “está ligada en el corazón del muchacho”
- quoted (Ps 51:6): “Thou desirest truth in the inward” → “He aquí, tú amas la verdad en lo íntimo”
- flag: Sal 51:6: the RV1909's 'amas' for the KJV's 'desirest'; its opening 'He aquí' included with the run. Pr 22:15: 'a child' given with the RV1909's 'muchacho'.

### 1501 Ps 51:7-15 He pleads for pardon, that he may promote the glory of God and the conversion of sinners.
- quoted (Heb 12:24): “the blood of sprinkling” → “la sangre del esparcimiento”
- quoted (Ps 51:9): “Hide thy face from my sins” → “Esconde tu rostro de mis pecados”
- quoted (Ps 51:9): “blot out all mine iniquities” → “borra todas mis maldades”
- quoted (Gal 5:1): “the liberty wherewith Christ” → “la libertad con que Cristo”
- quoted (Gal 5:1): “with the yoke of bondage” → “el yugo de servidumbre”
- quoted (Ps 25:5): “thou art the God of my salvation” → “tú eres el Dios de mi salud”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sal 51:7 ('Purifícame con hisopo', RV1909).
- flag: Sal 51:12: 'uphold me with thy Spirit. Thy Spirit is a free Spirit' follows the KJV ('thy free spirit'); the RV1909 reads 'el espíritu libre me sustente'. Translated.
- flag: Sal 25:5: the RV1909's 'el Dios de mi salud' (salud = salvation) used in the quotation, Henry's own 'Dios de salvación' kept around it. He 10:2 kept as Henry cites it, though the verse is only loosely related.

### 1502 Ps 51:16-19 God is pleased with a contrite heart, A prayer for the prosperity of Zion.
- quoted (Ps 51:17): “a broken spirit, a broken and a contrite heart” → “el espíritu quebrantado… corazón contrito y humillado”
- quoted (Eph 1:7): “the forgiveness of sins according to the riches of his grace” → “la remisión de pecados por las riquezas de su gracia”
- flag: Sal 51:17: Henry's 'a broken spirit, a broken and a contrite heart' given with the RV1909's two runs; the RV1909 has 'contrito y humillado' where the KJV has 'broken and contrite'.
- flag: Title: the source's comma between the two headings rendered as a full stop.

### 1503 Ps 52:1-5 The enemies of the truth and the church described, Their destruction.
- quoted (Ps 52:5): “the land of the living” → “la tierra de los vivientes”
- flag: Title: the source's comma between the two headings rendered as a full stop.

### 1504 Ps 52:6-9 The righteous rejoice.
- quoted (Ps 52:7): “trusted in the abundance of his riches” → “confió en la multitud de sus riquezas”
- quoted (Lam 3:26): “hope, and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord” → “esperar callando en la salud de Jehová”
- flag: Lm 3:26: the RV1909's 'esperar callando en la salud de Jehová' stands for Henry's 'to hope, and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord' (salud = salvation). Sal 52:8 has 'oliva verde' (singular); Henry's 'green olive-trees' translated 'olivos verdes'.

### 1505 Ps 53:1-6 The corruption of man by nature.
- quoted (Rom 3:18): “there is no fear of God before their eyes” → “no hay temor de Dios delante de sus ojos”
- quoted (Matt 1:21): “his name JESUS, for he shall save his people from their sins” → “su nombre JESUS, porque él salvará á su pueblo de sus pecados”
- flag: Mt 1:21: the RV1909's capitals 'JESUS' (without accent) kept as Henry has 'JESUS'.

### 1506 Ps 54:1-3 David complains of the malice of his enemies.
- quoted (Ps 54:3): “have not set God before them” → “no han puesto á Dios delante de sí”
- flag: 'the men of Judah': Henry probably means the Ziphites (Sal 54 title), who were of Judah; given as the glossary's 'Judá'. RV1909 writes '(Selah.)'; Henry's 'Selah' kept.

### 1507 Ps 54:4-7 Assurance of the Divine favour and protection.
- quoted (Ps 54:4): “Behold, God is mine Helper” → “He aquí, Dios es el que me ayuda”
- flag: Sal 54:4: the RV1909's 'Dios es el que me ayuda' for 'God is mine Helper'.

### 1508 Ps 55:1-8 Prayer to God to manifest his favour.
- flag: Henry numbers '1.' and '2.' but not the third point ('David in great alarm'); kept as written.
- flag: Is 53:10 echoed ('to put him to grief, and to make his soul an offering'); translated after the RV1909 ('quebrantarlo, sujetándole á padecimiento'), not as an exact quotation. Sal 55:2 'mourn in my complaint' (KJV; RV1909 'clamo en mi oración') translated 'clamar en nuestra queja'.

### 1510 Ps 55:16-23 He is sure that God would in due time appear for him.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 55:22; Not a quotation of Sal 55:22 here: Henry's 'call upon the Lord, and he will save us' echoes Sal 55:16; ordinary words in his own sentence.): “upon the Lord, and he”
- quoted (Ps 55:22): “never suffer the righteous to be moved” → “no dejará para siempre caído al justo”
- flag: Sal 55:22: the RV1909's 'no dejará para siempre caído al justo' for the KJV's 'never suffer the righteous to be moved'; Henry's explanation ('to be so shaken…') joined with 'es decir'. 1 S 3:18 ('haga lo que bien le pareciere', RV1909) echoed.

### 1511 Ps 56:1-7 David seeks mercy from God, amidst the malice of his enemies.
- quoted (Ps 56:1): “Be merciful unto me, O God” → “misericordia de mí, oh Dios”
- flag: Sal 56:1: the RV1909 prints the verse's first word in capitals ('TEN', after the title); written 'Ten' and the quotation matched from 'misericordia'.
- flag: Source 'come to throne of grace' read as 'come to the throne of grace'. Sal 90:11 echoed ('¿Quién conoce la fortaleza de tu ira?', RV1909), translated in Henry's third person.

### 1512 Ps 56:8-13 He rests his faith on God's promises, and declares his obligation to praise him for mercies.
- quoted (Heb 13:6): “may boldly say, The Lord is my helper” → “confiadamente: El Señor es mi ayudador”
- quoted (Heb 13:6): “I will not fear what man shall do unto me” → “no temeré lo que me hará el hombre”
- quoted (Ps 56:12): “Thy vows are upon me, O” → “Sobre mí, oh Dios, están tus votos”
- flag: Sal 56:12: Henry's 'Thy vows are upon me, O Lord' given with the RV1909's words, which read 'oh Dios' (KJV 'O God'), so 'Lord' becomes 'Dios'.
- flag: Sal 56:8: the KJV's 'bottle' given with the RV1909's 'redoma'.

### 1513 Ps 57:1-6 David begins with prayer and complaint.
- quoted (Luke 18:13): “God be merciful to me a sinner” → “Dios, sé propicio á mí pecador”
- flag: Mt 3:7 ('huir de la ira que vendrá', RV1909) echoed; Sal 57:1 'until these calamities be overpast' (KJV; RV1909 'hasta que pasen los quebrantos') translated.

### 1514 Ps 57:7-11 He concludes with joy and praise.
- flag: 'His heart is fixed' follows the KJV of Sal 57:7 ('My heart is fixed'); the RV1909 has 'mi corazón está dispuesto', used here as 'dispuesto'. 'Our tongue is our glory' explains the KJV's 'Awake up, my glory' (RV1909 'Despierta, oh gloria mía').

### 1516 Ps 58:6-11 A prayer that they may be disabled, and their ruin predicted.
- flag: Source typo 'orders all thing aright' read as 'all things'.
