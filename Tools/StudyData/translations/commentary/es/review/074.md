# To review: es commentary batch 074

### 3205 Luke 3:23-38 The genealogy of Christ.
- quoted (1Pet 1:25): “which by the gospel is preached unto” → “que por el evangelio”
- flag: Henry's quotation of Lc 3:38 ("Who was the son of Adam, the son of God") is given in the RV1909's words, "Que fué de Adam, que fué de Dios"; the RV1909 has no word "hijo" there, so Henry's "the offspring of God" follows it as "linaje de Dios". Unlisted quotation of Lc 3:38.
- flag: Heli (glossary: Elí) and Eli are the same name in Spanish; written "Elí, o Helí" to keep Henry's two spellings.
- flag: 1 P 1:25: Henry has "unto us" where the verse has "unto you"; only "que por el evangelio" is quoted and "nos es anunciada" translated.

### 3206 Luke 4:1-13 The temptation of Christ.
- quoted (Matt 4:8): “the kingdoms of the world and the glory of them” → “los reinos del mundo, y su gloria”
- quoted (Luke 4:5): “the kingdoms of the world” → “los reinos de la tierra”
- flag: Unlisted quotations used in the RV1909's words: He 2:17 ("semejante á los hermanos") and Gá 1:4 ("este presente siglo malo").
- flag: Q1 (Lc 4:5): the RV1909 says "los reinos de la tierra" where the KJV has "of the world"; those words are used at Henry's second mention.

### 3207 Luke 4:14-30 Christ in the synagogue of Nazareth.
- quoted (Luke 4:19): “the acceptable year of the Lord” → “el año agradable del Señor”
- flag: Is 9:6: the RV1909's "Admirable" renders Henry's "Wonderful"; his play on "wonder" ("We may well wonder") is kept as "admirarnos".
- flag: "such graceless wretches": rendered "criaturas tan desgraciadas y faltas de gracia" to keep the play on grace/graceless.

### 3208 Luke 4:31-44 He casts out an unclean spirit and heals the sick.
- quoted (Luke 4:42): “The people sought him, and came unto him” → “las gentes le buscaban, y vinieron hasta él”
- quoted (Col 1:14): “redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins” → “redención por su sangre, la remisión de pecados”

### 3209 Luke 5:1-11 The miraculous draught of fishes, Peter, James, and John called.
- flag: Lc 5:11: "forsook all, and followed Jesus" is given in the RV1909's words, "dejándolo todo, le siguieron" (unlisted quotation).
- flag: "thankworthy" (1 P 2:19, RV1909 "agradable") rendered "digno de alabanza".

### 3210 Luke 5:12-16 A leper cleansed.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 6:17; a common phrase in Henry's own sentence (bringing others to Christ), not a quotation of Lc 6:17, whose RV1909 wording ("á oirle, y para ser sanados de sus enfermedades") does not fit the sentence): “to hear him and to be healed”
- flag: Allusion to Is 1:6 in Henry's own order ("from the crown of the head to the sole of the foot"); translated, since the RV1909 puts it the other way round ("Desde la planta del pie hasta la cabeza").

### 3212 Luke 5:27-39 Levi called, Christ's answer to the Pharisees.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of He 12:3 in the RV1909's words: "tal contradicción de pecadores contra sí mismo".

### 3217 Luke 6:27-36 Christ exhorts to mercy.
- quoted (Acts 9:6): “Lord, what wilt thou have me to do” → “Señor, ¿qué quieres que haga?”

### 3218 Luke 6:37-49 And to justice and sincerity.
- flag: Unlisted quotations in the RV1909's words: 1 Co 3:11 ("nadie puede poner otro fundamento") and Jn 10:28 ("no perecerán para siempre"). 1 P 1:5 is adapted by Henry (Christ for God) and translated.

### 3219 Luke 7:1-10 The centurion's servant healed.
- flag: The last "sentence" in the English has no verb ("The cure soon wrought and perfect."); kept as a verbless phrase.

### 3220 Luke 7:11-18 The widow's son raised.
- quoted (Eph 5:14): “Arise from the dead, and Christ shall give” → “levántate de los muertos, y te alumbrará Cristo”
- flag: Ef 5:14: Henry has "shall give you light and life"; the RV1909's "te alumbrará Cristo" is quoted and "y te dará vida" added as his "and life".
- flag: Unlisted quotations of Jn 5:28-29 in the RV1909's words: "todos los que están en los sepulcros", "resurrección de vida".

### 3221 Luke 7:19-35 John the Baptist's inquiry concerning Jesus.
- quoted (Luke 7:22): “To the poor the gospel is preached” → “á los pobres es anunciado el evangelio”

### 3223 Luke 8:1-3 The ministry of Christ.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 8:1; Henry's "Tidings of the kingdom of God are glad tidings" plays on "tidings"; the RV1909 of Lc 8:1 says "el evangelio del reino de Dios", which would lose the play): “Tidings of the kingdom of God”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Lc 8:3 in the RV1909's words: "le servían de sus haciendas". 2 Co 8:9 is adapted by Henry ("our sakes") and translated.

### 3224 Luke 8:4-21 The parable of the sower.
- flag: Henry plays on "hold the truth in unrighteousness" (Ro 1:18, RV1909 "detienen la verdad con injusticia", used here) and "hold forth the word of life" (Fil 2:16, RV1909 "Reteniendo la palabra de vida", which means the opposite of "hold forth"); the second is translated "presentar en alto".

### 3225 Luke 8:22-40 Christ stilleth the tempest and casteth out devils.
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Os 11:4; the RV1909's "con cuerdas de amor" is used for "with the bands of love".

### 3226 Luke 8:41-56 The daughter of Jairus restored to life.
- quoted (Luke 8:55): “commanded to give her meat” → “mandó que le diesen de comer”
- flag: Lc 8:50: the RV1909's "No temas: cree solamente" is used; Henry's "and thy daughter shall be made whole" (the RV1909 has only "y será salva") is translated "y tu hija será sana".

### 3227 Luke 9:1-9 The apostles sent forth.
- quoted (Luke 9:7): “John was risen from the dead” → “Juan ha resucitado de los muertos”
- flag: The source's first two paragraphs ("Peter's testimony to Christ, Self-denial enjoined." and "(18-27)") are the heading of the next section (3229, Lc 9:18-27) misplaced into this one; translated as they stand, paragraphs kept.

### 3228 Luke 9:10-17 The multitude miraculously fed.
- quoted (Luke 9:11): “spake unto them of the kingdom of God” → “les hablaba del reino de Dios”
- flag: Lc 15:17 (unlisted): the RV1909's "abundancia de pan" is used for "bread enough"; "and to spare" translated.

### 3229 Luke 9:18-27 
- flag: This section has no title in the source; its heading appears misplaced at the start of 3227.

### 3230 Luke 9:28-36 The transfiguration.
- flag: He 13:14 (unlisted): the RV1909's "no tenemos aquí ciudad permanente" is close, but Henry says "in this world"; "no tenemos ciudad permanente" is kept as his wording.

### 3231 Luke 9:37-42 An evil spirit cast out.
- flag: The English has "Disease of that nature are" (singular noun, plural verb); translated as plural "Las enfermedades".

### 3232 Luke 9:43-50 Christ checks the ambition of his disciples.
- flag: "who do not follow with us" echoes Lc 9:49, RV1909 "no sigue con nosotros"; written "no siguen con nosotros" (plural), so not marked as a quotation.

### 3233 Luke 9:51-56 He reproves their mistaken zeal.
- flag: The English has "through they refused" for "though they refused"; translated as "aunque".
- flag: "Come, see our zeal for the Lord!" adapts 2 R 10:16 (RV1909 "Ven conmigo, y verás mi celo por Jehová"); Henry changes the person, so translated.

### 3235 Luke 10:1-16 Seventy disciples sent forth.
- flag: The English lacks a full stop after "very fearful"; one added.

### 3236 Luke 10:17-24 The blessedness of Christ's disciples.
- flag: Is 53:3 (unlisted): the RV1909's "varón de dolores" is used for "a man of sorrows".

### 3237 Luke 10:25-37 The good Samaritan.
- quoted (Titus 3:4): “the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward” → “la bondad de Dios nuestro Salvador, y su amor para con los hombres”

### 3238 Luke 10:38-42 Jesus at the house of Martha and Mary.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Lc 10:42 in the RV1909's words: "María escogió la buena parte", "una cosa es necesaria".

### 3239 Luke 11:1-4 The disciples taught to pray.
- quoted (Luke 11:1): “Lord, teach us to pray” → “Señor, enséñanos á orar”

### 3241 Luke 11:14-26 Christ casts out a devil, The blasphemy of the Pharisees.
- flag: The English has "endowments of mind of body", read as "of mind and body".
- flag: "the strong man armed" (Lc 11:21) is given in the RV1909's words, "el fuerte armado" (unlisted quotation).

### 3242 Luke 11:27-28 True happiness.
- quoted (Luke 11:28): “hear the word of God” → “la palabra de Dios”
