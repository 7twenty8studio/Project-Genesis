# To review: es commentary batch 038

### 1454 Ps 32:1-2 The happiness of a pardoned sinner.
- quoted (2Cor 5:21): “made the righteousness of God in him” → “hechos justicia de Dios en él”
- flag: Is 53:6 echoed ('laid upon him the iniquity of us all'); translated, since the RV1909 says 'cargó en él el pecado de todos nosotros'.

### 1455 Ps 32:3-7 The misery that went before, and the comfort that followed the confession of sins.
- flag: 'In a time of finding' echoes Sal 32:6 (KJV 'in a time when thou mayest be found'; RV1909 'en el tiempo de poder hallarte'); translated.

### 1458 Ps 33:12-22 His people encouraged by his power.
- quoted (Ps 33:22): “Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us” → “Sea tu misericordia, oh Jehová, sobre nosotros”

### 1459 Ps 34:1-10 David praises God, and encourages to trust him.
- quoted (Ps 34:6, Ps 34:7): “the Lord heard him, and saved him out of all his troubles” → “oyóle Jehová, y librólo de todas sus angustias”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 45:19 ('En vano me buscáis', RV1909) for Henry's 'Seek ye me in vain'.
- flag: Sal 34:8: the RV1909's 'Gustad, y ved' used, followed by Henry's own 'la bondad de Dios' (RV1909 'que es bueno Jehová'). 'shall want' / 'shall not want' rendered with the RV1909's 'tendrán falta' (Sal 34:10).

### 1460 Ps 34:11-22 He exhorts to fear.
- quoted (Ps 34:11): “the fear of the Lord” → “el temor de Jehová”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 34:14; Henry turns the verse's imperative into 'we must seek peace and pursue it'; the RV1909's 'busca la paz, y síguela' cannot stand in his first person plural.): “seek peace and pursue it”
- quoted (Ps 34:19): “the afflictions of the righteous” → “los males del justo”
- flag: Sal 34:19: Henry's 'afflictions' given with the RV1909's 'males'.

### 1461 Ps 35:1-10 David prays for safety.
- flag: Source 'in the hour temptation' read as 'in the hour of temptation' ('en la hora de la tentación').

### 1463 Ps 35:17-28 And calls upon God to support him.
- quoted (Ps 35:20): “devise deceitful matters against them” → “piensan palabras engañosas”
- quoted (Ps 35:22): “Lord, be not far from me” → “Señor, de mí no te alejes”
- flag: Sal 35:20: Henry's 'devise deceitful matters' given with the RV1909's 'piensan palabras engañosas'; 'it has been common for their enemies' recast as 'sus enemigos, como ha sido común' to keep the verb as the RV1909 has it.

### 1464 Ps 36:1-4 The bad state of the wicked.
- flag: 'loving-kindness' rendered 'misericordia', the RV1909's word in Sal 36:7.

### 1465 Ps 36:5-12 The goodness of God.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ap 22:17 ('el que quiere, tome del agua de la vida de balde', RV1909) used in Henry's syntax ('puede venir y tomar de él del agua de la vida de balde').
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ro 4:17 ('llama las cosas que no son, como las que son', RV1909). 'what he does we know not now' echoes Jn 13:7; translated.

### 1466 Ps 37:1-6 
- quoted (Ps 37:5): “Commit thy way unto the Lord” → “Encomienda á Jehová tu camino”
- flag: 'roll thy way upon the Lord, so the margin reads it' refers to the KJV's margin of Sal 37:5; translated ('haz rodar tu camino').
- flag: 'Cast thy burden upon the Lord' (Sal 55:22; RV1909 'Echa sobre Jehová tu carga') given with 'el Señor' in Henry's free wording; 'Thou shalt have food convenient for thee' (cf. Pr 30:8, RV1909 'manténme del pan que he menester') and 'A man's life consists not in abundance' (Lc 12:15) translated.
- flag: 'He shall bring that to pass' (Sal 37:5; RV1909 'él hará') given as 'Él lo hará'. Source typo 'thou has committed' read as 'thou hast committed'.

### 1467 Ps 37:7-20 
- flag: Sal 37:16 ('lo poco del justo', RV1909) echoed and kept; Jn 14:27 echoed, translated.

### 1468 Ps 37:21-33 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 1:17 ('aprender á hacer bien', RV1909 'Aprended á hacer bien'), given in Henry's infinitive; 2 Co 12:7 ('un mensajero de Satanás') echoed.

### 1469 Ps 37:34-40 
- quoted (Ps 37:39): “The salvation of the righteous” → “La salvación de los justos”
- flag: Mt 11:29 ('Llevad mi yugo sobre vosotros, y aprended de mí', RV1909) echoed in Henry's third person; translated.

### 1470 Ps 38:1-11 God's displeasure at sin.
- flag: Ro 8:26-27: the RV1909's 'gemidos indecibles' used for 'groanings which cannot be uttered'; the rest translated.

### 1471 Ps 38:12-22 The psalmist's sufferings and prayers.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sal 38:21 ('no te alejes de mí', RV1909), Henry's quotation marks kept.

### 1472 Ps 39:1-6 David meditates on man's frailty.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sal 39:5 ('es completa vanidad todo hombre', RV1909) given as 'completa vanidad es todo hombre'.
- flag: 'musing' and 'burning' follow the KJV of Sal 39:3 ('while I was musing the fire burned'); the RV1909 has 'en mi meditación se encendió fuego'. Rendered 'cavilar' and 'arder'.

### 1473 Ps 39:7-13 He applies for pardon and deliverance.
- quoted (Ps 39:11): “surely every man is vanity” → “ciertamente vanidad es todo hombre”
- flag: unlisted quotation of He 11:10 ('el artífice y hacedor de la cual es Dios', RV1909). 1 Co 7:31 ('use the world as not abusing it', KJV) translated: the RV1909 reads 'los que usan de este mundo, como los que no usan'.

### 1474 Ps 40:1-5 Confidence for deliverance.
- flag: Sal 40:2: Henry's 'horrible pit and miry clay' given with the RV1909's 'lago de miseria' and 'lodo cenagoso' (RV1909 'del lodo cenagoso'); 'Rock' as the glossary's 'Roca'.

### 1475 Ps 40:6-10 Christ's work of redemption.
- flag: 'the volume, or roll, of the book' follows the KJV of Sal 40:7 ('in the volume of the book'); the RV1909 has 'en el envoltorio del libro'. Translated 'volumen, o rollo'.

### 1476 Ps 40:11-17 Prayer for mercy and grace.
- flag: 'more than conquerors' (Ro 8:37; RV1909 'hacemos más que vencer') translated as 'más que vencedores'.

### 1478 Ps 41:5-13 The treachery of David's enemies.
- flag: Sal 41:9 ('el que de mi pan comía, alzó contra mí el calcañar', RV1909) echoed in Henry's first person plural ('alzamos contra él el calcañar').

### 1480 Ps 42:6-11 
- quoted (Isa 50:10): “trust in the name of the Lord” → “confíe en el nombre de Jehová”
- flag: Source typo 'is faith came off conqueror' read as 'his faith came off conqueror'.
- flag: Is 50:10: 'confíe en el nombre de Jehová' kept as the RV1909 has it ('animándolo a que confíe…'); 'and to stay himself upon his God' given as 'a apoyarse en su Dios' after the RV1909's 'apóyese en su Dios'. 'And my God' given with Sal 42:11's 'y el Dios mío'.

### 1481 Ps 43:1-5 David endeavours to still his spirit, with hope and confidence in God.
- flag: Sal 143:2 and Sal 43:1 ('Judge me, O God') adapted by Henry ('Enter not into judgment with me', 'Lord, judge me'); translated (RV1909 'no entres en juicio con tu siervo', 'Júzgame, oh Dios').
- flag: Source grammar 'the good that flow' read as 'the good that flows'; 'ordinance' (singular) kept as 'la ordenanza'.

### 1482 Ps 44:1-8 
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Co 1:31 ('El que se gloría, gloríese en el Señor', RV1909); Sal 29:2 ('la gloria debida á su nombre', RV1909) used for 'the glory due unto it'; Ap 6:2 ('conquering and to conquer') translated.

### 1484 Ps 44:17-26 
- quoted (Ps 44:26): “Redeem us for thy mercies' sake” → “Redímenos por tu misericordia”
- flag: Source typo 'Hearts sins' read as 'Heart sins' ('los pecados del corazón').
- flag: Sal 44:26: Henry's 'for thy mercies' sake' given with the RV1909's 'por tu misericordia'.

### 1485 Ps 45:1-5 
- flag: Sal 45:1: 'a ready writer' given with the RV1909's 'escribiente muy ligero'; 'this good matter' echoes the KJV's 'a good matter' (RV1909 'palabra buena'), translated.

### 1486 Ps 45:6-9 
- flag: Source 'the gladdening gifts influences' read as 'the gladdening gifts and influences'.
- flag: Sal 45:7 ('óleo de gozo', RV1909) and Ap 21:9 ('la esposa, mujer del Cordero', RV1909) used; 'oil of gladness' rendered 'óleo de gozo'.
