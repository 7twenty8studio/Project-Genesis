### 2305 Eccl 12:8-14 
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “what, or what manner of time, the Spirit of Christ” → “cuándo y en qué punto de tiempo significaba el Espíritu de Cristo”
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “in them did signify, when it testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow” → “que estaba en ellos, el cual prenunciaba las aflicciones que habían de venir á Cristo, y las glorias después de ellas”
- translated, not quoted (Eccl 12:13; Henry makes it a clause ('That to fear God, and keep his commandments, is the whole of man'); the RV1909's imperatives can't stand there, so translated, ending with its 'el todo del hombre'.): “fear God, and keep his commandments”

- flag: Henry's capitals 'VANITY OF VANITIES, ALL IS VANITY' (unlisted, Ec 12:8) set in the RV1909's words in capitals ('VANIDAD DE VANIDADES, TODO VANIDAD'); his later 'ALL IS VANITY' translated 'TODO ES VANIDAD'.
- flag: Henry's last words 'for this is all that concerns man' differ from the KJV/RV1909 'the whole of man' / 'el todo del hombre'; translated as he wrote it.
