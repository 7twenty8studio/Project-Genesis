# To review: es commentary batch 062

### 2821 Hos 5:8-15 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 3:7: 'flee from the wrath to come' given in the RV1909's words 'huir de la ira que vendrá'.
- flag: unlisted allusions: 'space to repent' (Ap 2:21) and 'plenteous redemption' (Sal 130:7, RV1909 'abundante redención'), 'a present help' (Sal 46:1, RV1909 'pronto auxilio'), worked into Henry's sentences.
- flag: 'Many a nation; as well as many a person' has a stray semicolon in the English; translated as one phrase.

### 2822 Hos 6:1-3 
- quoted (Hos 6:3): “as the latter and former rain unto the earth” → “como la lluvia tardía y temprana á la tierra”

### 2824 Hos 7:1-7 
- flag: Source has 'israel's' in lower case; translated as 'Israel'.

### 2825 Hos 7:8-16 
- quoted (Ps 51:10): “a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within” → “oh Dios, un corazón limpio; y renueva un espíritu recto dentro de”
- flag: Sal 51:10 kept in the RV1909's words with Henry's change from 'me' to 'us' ('en nosotros', 'de nosotros').

### 2827 Hos 8:5-10 
- translated, not quoted (Hos 8:7; Henry's 'the bud shall yield no fruit' adapts the KJV's 'the bud shall yield no meal'; the RV1909 reads 'ni el fruto hará harina', which does not fit his sentence.): “the bud shall yield no”
- quoted (Rom 6:21): “the end of those things is death” → “el fin de ellas es muerte”

### 2829 Hos 9:1-6 
- quoted (Hos 9:3): “dwell in the Lord's land” → “en la tierra de Jehová”

### 2831 Hos 9:11-17 
- flag: Source text begins with a stray '. '; kept as given.
- flag: sensitive: 2831 — 'the scattered Jews daily warn us to beware, lest we neglect or abuse the gospel' (the Jews as a whole).

### 2833 Hos 10:9-15 
- flag: 'be a broken and contrite spirit' is odd in the English (one would expect 'be of'); translated literally.
- flag: 'the Lord our righteousness' (Jer 23:6, RV1909 'JEHOVÁ, JUSTICIA NUESTRA') given as Henry's own words, 'el Señor justicia nuestra'.

### 2834 Hos 11:1-7 
- quoted (Eccl 8:11): “fully set in them to do evil” → “está en ellos lleno para hacer mal”
- flag: 'With bands of love; this word signifies stronger cords than the former' explains the KJV's 'cords of a man ... bands of love' (Os 11:4); the RV1909 has 'cuerdas humanas ... cuerdas de amor', so 'cuerdas de amor' is used and the contrast is kept as 'cuerdas más fuertes que las anteriores'.
- flag: 'Their hearts are fully set in them to do evil' quotes Ec 8:11 (listed); RV1909 words kept, 'Su corazón' singular as in the RV1909.

### 2835 Hos 11:8-12 
- quoted (1John 1:9): “He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins” → “él es fiel y justo para que nos perdone nuestros pecados”
- flag: Source typos: 'The religious service of Israel were' (service/were) and 'such us despise Him' (for 'such as'); translated as meant.
- flag: 'when men sin that away' rendered 'cuando los hombres lo malgastan pecando'.

### 2836 Hos 12:1-6 
- translated, not quoted (Hos 12:3; The RV1909 reads 'con su fortaleza venció al ángel' where the KJV has 'he had power with God'; Henry's point is power with God.): “by his strength he had power with God”

### 2837 Hos 12:7-14 
- flag: 'Ephraim became a merchant: the word also signifies a Canaanite' rests on the Hebrew of Os 12:7 (KJV 'He is a merchant'); the RV1909 has 'Es mercader' with the same double sense; Henry's remark translated.

### 2838 Hos 13:1-8 
- quoted (Hos 13:2): “Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves” → “hombres que sacrifican, que besen los becerros”
- flag: 'Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves' fitted to the RV1909's 'á los hombres que sacrifican, que besen los becerros' with 'Dígase a' ('Let it be said to') in front.

### 2840 Hos 14:1-3 
- flag: 'Take away iniquity' and 'Receive our prayer graciously' paraphrase Os 14:2 (KJV 'receive us graciously'; RV1909 'Quita toda iniquidad, y acepta el bien'); kept as Henry's own words. 'good of the world's showing' rendered 'un bien que el mundo muestra'.

### 2841 Hos 14:4-8 
- quoted (Hos 14:5): “shall grow as the lily” → “como lirio”
- quoted (Hos 14:8): “What have I to do any more with idols” → “¿Qué más tendré ya con los ídolos?”
- quoted (Hos 14:8): “tree; From me is thy fruit found” → “de mí será hallado tu fruto”
- flag: 'shall grow as the lily' given with the RV1909's 'florecerá como lirio' made plural ('florecerán') for Henry's 'They'.
- flag: 'even the taking away his sin, Is. 27:9' given with the RV1909's 'la remoción de su pecado' (unlisted quotation of Isa 27:9).
- flag: 'And as the root of a tree' — the RV1909 of Os 14:8 compares God to 'la haya verde' (KJV 'a green fir tree'); Henry's root image translated as written.

### 2842 Hos 14:9-9 
- quoted (1Pet 2:8): “a Stone of stumbling, and a Rock of offence” → “Piedra de tropiezo, y roca de escándalo”
- flag: 'Rock of Ages' (the hymn title) rendered 'la Roca de los siglos'.

### 2844 Joel 1:8-13 
- flag: unlisted quotation of John 6:27: 'the meat that perishes' given in the RV1909's words 'la comida que perece'.

### 2846 Joel 2:1-14 
- flag: 'rending the garments ... hearts were rent' alludes to Jl 2:13 (RV1909 'lacerad vuestro corazón, y no vuestros vestidos'); Henry's own words kept with 'rasgar'.

### 2847 Joel 2:15-27 
- flag: 'Spare us, good Lord' (from the Book of Common Prayer's Litany) rendered 'Perdónanos, buen Señor'.
- flag: 'he waits to be gracious' (Isa 30:18) given with the RV1909's 'esperará para tener piedad' adapted to the present ('espera para tener piedad').

### 2850 Joel 3:9-17 
- flag: 'The acceptable year of the Lord' (Is 61:2; RV1909 'año de la buena voluntad de Jehová') kept as Henry's own phrase, 'El año aceptable del Señor'.

### 2851 Joel 3:18-21 
- translated, not quoted (Joel 3:18; Henry's own phrase for the heavenly temple ('the house of the Lord above'), not a quotation of Jl 3:18.): “the house of the Lord”

### 2853 Amos 2:1-8 Judgments against Moab and Judah.
- quoted (Amos 2:4): “despised the law of the Lord” → “menospreciaron la ley de Jehová”
- flag: 'strong delusion' (2 Ts 2:11, KJV; RV1909 'operación de error') rendered as Henry's own phrase 'un poder engañoso'.

### 2855 Amos 3:1-8 Judgments against Israel.
- flag: 'God and man cannot walk together, except they are agreed' alludes to Am 3:3 (RV1909 '¿Andarán dos juntos, si no estuvieren de concierto?'); kept as Henry's sentence.

### 2856 Amos 3:9-15 The like to other nations.
- quoted (Rom 11:5): “a remnant, according to the election of grace” → “reliquias por la elección de gracia”

### 2858 Amos 4:6-13 Their impenitence shown.
- flag: Allusions to Is 55:2-3, 6 and Am 4:12 ('prepare to meet thy God'; RV1909 'aparéjate para venir al encuentro á tu Dios') translated as Henry's own sentences.
