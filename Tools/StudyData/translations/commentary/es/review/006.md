# To review: es commentary batch 006

### 154 Gen 39:7-12 Joseph resists temptation.
- flag: Source oddity: 'by avoiding the temper' is surely 'tempter'; rendered 'evitando a la tentadora'.
- flag: Echoes of Job 31:1, He 12:1, Gn 39:9 ('great wickedness', RV1909 'este grande mal') and Pr 6:5 are in Henry's own syntax; translated.

### 156 Gen 39:19-23 He is cast into prison, God is with him there.
- quoted (Gen 39:21): “the Lord was with Joseph” → “Jehová fué con José”
- quoted (Gen 39:21): “gave him favour in the sight of the keeper of the prison” → “dióle gracia en ojos del principal de la casa de la cárcel”

### 157 Gen 40:1-19 The chief butler and baker of Pharaoh in prison, Their dreams interpreted by Joseph.
- quoted (Ps 42:5): “Why art thou cast down, O my soul” → “Por qué te abates, oh alma mía”

### 158 Gen 40:20-23 The ingratitude of the chief butler.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Dt 32:6: 'pueblo loco é ignorante' (RV1909).
- flag: 'the day of our death, as better than the day of our birth' echoes Ec 7:1; translated.

### 159 Gen 41:1-8 Pharaoh's dreams.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 41:8: 'estaba agitado su espíritu' (RV1909); Henry's 'Pharaoh's spirit' rendered by the verse's 'su espíritu'.

### 160 Gen 41:9-32 Joseph interprets Pharaoh's dreams.
- quoted (Gen 41:17): “stood upon the bank of the river” → “estaba á la orilla del río”
- quoted (Isa 56:12): “to-morrow shall be as this day” → “será el día de mañana como este”
- translated, not quoted (Ex 16:18; Henry turns Ex 16:18 into a present-tense rule for every harvest; the RV1909's past 'no sobraba al que había recogido mucho, ni faltaba al que había recogido poco' would not fit his sentence.): “nothing over; and he”
- flag: Echoes of Fil 4:12, Jn 6:27 and Lc 16:25 in Henry's own syntax; translated.

### 161 Gen 41:33-45 Joseph's counsel, He is highly advanced.
- translated, not quoted (Gen 41:38, Gen 41:39; The RV1909 puts it as Pharaoh's question with a subjunctive ('¿Hemos de hallar otro hombre como éste, en quien haya espíritu de Dios?'); Henry makes it a statement.): “is a man in whom the spirit of God”
- flag: 'Zaphnath-paaneah' as in the RV1909 (Gn 41:45).

### 162 Gen 41:46-57 Joseph's children, The beginning of the famine.
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909: Ec 9:10 ('Todo lo que te viniere á la mano para hacer, hazlo'), Gn 42:36 ('José no parece', Henry's 'Joseph is not!') and Is 55:1 ('sin dinero y sin precio').
- flag: 'satisfy with goodness the hungry soul' echoes Sal 107:9 (RV1909 'llenó de bien al alma hambrienta'), rendered in the future to fit Henry's sentence; 'what he bids you, do' echoes Jn 2:5; translated.

### 164 Gen 42:7-20 Joseph's treatment of his brethren.
- flag: unlisted quotation in Henry's own quotation marks of Gn 42:18: 'Yo temo á Dios' (RV1909).

### 166 Gen 42:25-28 The rest return with corn.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 55:1: 'sin dinero y sin precio' (RV1909).

### 167 Gen 42:29-38 Jacob refuses to send Benjamin to Egypt.
- quoted (Gen 42:36): “All these things are against me” → “Contra mí son todas estas cosas”
- quoted (Gen 42:36): “all these things are against” → “todas estas cosas”
- flag: Echoes of Ro 8:28, 2 Co 4:17 ('peso de gloria'), 1 P 2:3 ('benigno') and 1 P 5:6 ('bajo su poderosa mano') in Henry's own syntax; translated.

### 168 Gen 43:1-14 Jacob is persuaded to send Benjamin into Egypt.
- quoted (Phil 3:8): “loss, for the excellency of the knowledge” → “pérdida por el eminente conocimiento”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mt 6:10: 'Sea hecha tu voluntad' (RV1909).
- flag: Quote 0 (Fil 3:8): the RV1909 continues 'de Cristo Jesús'; Henry has 'of Jesus Christ', kept as 'de Jesucristo' outside the quotation.

### 171 Gen 44:1-17 Joseph's policy to stay his brethren, and try their affection for Benjamin.
- quoted (Gen 44:5): “Is not this it in which my lord drinketh” → “No es ésta en la que bebe mi señor”
- flag: KJV-dependent: Henry's alternative readings play on the KJV's 'whereby indeed he divineth' (Gn 44:5); the RV1909 has 'por la que suele adivinar'. Translated as Henry gives them.

### 172 Gen 44:18-34 Judah's supplication to Joseph.
- quoted (Heb 7:14): “our Lord sprang out” → “el Señor nuestro nació”
- flag: Quote 0 (He 7:14): RV1909 'nació de la tribu de Judá'; Henry has 'sprang out of Judah', so only 'el Señor nuestro nació' is verbatim.

### 173 Gen 45:1-15 Joseph comforts his brethren, and sends for his father.
- quoted (Gen 45:4): “I am Joseph, your brother” → “Yo soy José vuestro hermano”
- quoted (Gen 45:15): “his brethren talked with him” → “sus hermanos hablaron con él”
- flag: unlisted quotations, RV1909: Hch 9:5 ('Yo soy Jesús') and Mt 14:27 ('yo soy; no tengáis miedo', Henry's 'It is I, be not afraid').
- flag: 'showing piety at home' echoes the KJV of 1 Ti 5:4; the RV1909 says 'gobernar su casa piadosamente'. Translated.

### 174 Gen 45:16-24 Pharaoh confirms Joseph's invitation, Joseph's gifts to his brethren.
- quoted (Gen 45:24): “See that ye fall not out by the way” → “No riñáis por el camino”

### 175 Gen 45:25-28 Jacob receives the news of Joseph's being alive.
- flag: 'It is enough' echoes Gn 45:28 (RV1909 'Basta'); Henry's own words around it translated.

### 176 Gen 46:1-4 God's promises to Jacob.
- quoted (Ps 23:4): “through the valley of the shadow of death” → “valle de sombra de muerte”
- flag: 'to fear no evil' echoes the same verse (Sal 23:4, RV1909 'no temeré mal alguno'); rendered 'no temer mal alguno'.

### 178 Gen 46:28-34 Joseph meets his father and his brethren.
- quoted (Gen 46:34): “in the land of Goshen” → “en la tierra de Gosén”
- flag: Echoes of Ro 12:16 ('mind not high things') and 1 Ti 6:8 (RV1909 'teniendo sustento y con qué cubrirnos') in Henry's own syntax.

### 179 Gen 47:1-6 Joseph presents his brethren to Pharaoh.
- quoted (Gen 47:4): “to sojourn in the land” → “por morar en esta tierra”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 47:3: '¿Cuál es vuestro oficio?' (RV1909, Henry's 'What is your calling?').

### 180 Gen 47:7-12 Jacob blesses Pharaoh.
- quoted (Job 14:1): “is of few days and full of trouble” → “corto de días, y harto de sinsabores”
- flag: 'The hoary head ... crown of glory' echoes Pr 16:31 (RV1909 'Corona de honra es la vejez'); translated from Henry's wording.

### 181 Gen 47:13-26 Joseph's dealings with the Egyptians during the famine.
- quoted (Job 2:4): “All that a man hath will he give for his life” → “todo lo que el hombre tiene dará por su vida”
- quoted (Gen 47:25): “Thou hast saved our lives” → “La vida nos has dado”
- flag: Quote 1 (Gn 47:25): the RV1909's 'La vida nos has dado' loses some of Henry's echo 'Thou hast saved our lives' / 'Thou hast saved our souls' in the next sentence.

### 182 Gen 47:27-31 Jacob's age. His desire to be buried in Canaan.

- quoted (Hos 12:4): “had power over the Angel, and prevailed” → “venció al ángel, y prevaleció”
