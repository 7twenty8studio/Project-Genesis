# To review: es commentary batch 015

### 468 Num 24:15-25 Balaam's prophecies.
- quoted (Num 24:16): “heard the words of God” → “oyó los dichos de Jehová”
- quoted (Num 24:16): “knew the knowledge of the Most High” → “la ciencia del Altísimo”
- flag: unlisted quotations used from the RV1909: Ap 1:7 ('todo ojo le verá'), Lc 16:23 ('de lejos'), Nm 24:21 ('en la peña tu nido'), Zac 3:2 ('tizón arrebatado del incendio', made plural), 2 P 2:15 ('el premio de la maldad'), Sal 2:9 ('vara de hierro', 'desmenuzarás' echoed).
- flag: 'shall not see him nigh' (Nm 24:17): RV1909 'mas no de cerca' echoed.
- flag: Henry's anti-papal application ('Rome papal also; antichrist') kept as written, per the rules.
- flag: 'the Most High and the Almighty': RV1909 'el Altísimo', 'el Omnipotente'.

### 471 Num 25:16-18 The Midianites to be punished.
- flag: 'a vexation to us' plays on the KJV of Nm 25:17-18 ('Vex the Midianites… they vex you'; RV1909 'Afligiréis… os afligieron'); rendered 'aflicción'.
- flag: 'thorn in the flesh' (2 Co 12:7): RV1909 'aguijón en mi carne' echoed as 'aguijón en la carne'.

### 476 Num 27:1-11 The daughters of Zelophehad apply for an inheritance, The law of inheritances.
- flag: '2. And earnest desire': 'And' (probably for 'An') translated as it stands ('Y un deseo ferviente').
- flag: 'other things shall be added to them' echoes Mt 6:33 (RV1909 'todas estas cosas os serán añadidas'); Henry changes the person, translated.

### 477 Num 27:12-14 Moses warned of his death.
- flag: echoes He 11:16 ('la mejor, es á saber, la celestial'), Sal 37:37 and Sal 23:4 ('no temeré mal alguno'), woven into Henry's sentences; translated with the RV1909's words where they fit.

### 478 Num 27:15-23 Joshua appointed to succeed Moses.
- quoted (Num 27:18): “a man in whom is the Spirit” → “varón en el cual hay espíritu”
- quoted (John 1:17): “grace and truth came by Jesus Christ” → “la gracia y la verdad por Jesucristo fué hecha”
- flag: 'who by reason of our transgression could not bring us to heaven': grammatically refers to Moses; rendered 'la cual' (the law) because the Spanish quotation now ends with 'fué dada'; reviewer may prefer 'el cual' (Moses).
- flag: 'the law was given by Moses' (Jn 1:17, in Henry's quotation marks, unlisted): RV1909 'la ley por Moisés fué dada' used.
- flag: 'fearing God and hating covetousness' echoes Éx 18:21 (RV1909 'temerosos de Dios… que aborrezcan la avaricia').
- flag: Josué's name: glossary also gives 'Jesuá' for another person; 'Josué' used.

### 479 Num 28:1-8 Offerings, The daily sacrifice.
- quoted (Phil 2:17): “the sacrifice and service” → “sobre el sacrificio y servicio de”
- flag: source layout kept: a second heading and '(16-31)' as separate paragraphs at the start of the text.
- flag: 'strong wine' (KJV Nm 28:7): RV1909 says 'superior vino'; Henry's 'vino fuerte' translated.

### 480 Num 28:9-15 The offering on the sabbath and new moons.
- flag: 'we must make the sacrifice of Christ… the fountain' reads as if a word is missing; rendered 'hacer del sacrificio de Cristo… la fuente'.
- flag: Henry applies the sabbath to Christian worship here but doesn't say 'Christian sabbath'; 'sábado' kept without the bracketed note.
- flag: 'Sun of righteousness' (Mal 4:2): RV1909 'el Sol de justicia'.

### 481 Num 28:16-31

### 482 Num 29:1-11 The offering at the feats of trumpets, and on the day of atonement.

- flag: unlisted 2 Ti 3:17: RV1909 'para que el hombre de Dios sea perfecto' used.

### 483 Num 29:12-40 Offerings at the feast of tabernacles.
- quoted (Rev 1:5): “loved us and washed us from our sins in his own blood” → “nos amó, y nos ha lavado de nuestros pecados con su sangre”
- flag: 'sin-offering' rendered 'expiación por el pecado' (RV1909 Nm 29:11 'macho cabrío por expiación').
- flag: 'The life that we live in the flesh must be by the faith of the Son of God' adapts Gá 2:20 (RV1909 'lo que ahora vivo en la carne, lo vivo en la fe del Hijo de Dios'); Henry changes the person, translated with its words.

### 484 Num 30:1-2 Vows to be kept.

- flag: 'he shall not break his words' (Nm 30:2): RV1909 'no violará su palabra' used (unlisted).

### 485 Num 30:3-16 The cases wherein vows might be released.

- flag: 'as unto the Lord' (Ef 5:22): RV1909 'como al Señor'.
- flag: Henry's view of household order kept as written, per the rules.

### 492 Num 32:1-5 The tribes of Reuben and Gad request an inheritance on the east of Jordan.

### 493 Num 32:6-15 Moses reproves the Reubenites and Gadites.

### 494 Num 32:16-27 They explain their views, Moses consents.
- translated, not quoted (Num 32:17; Henry reports their offer in the third person; the RV1909 has 'nos armaremos, é iremos con diligencia delante de los hijos de Israel' (first person, and 'con diligencia' for 'ready armed'), so translated.): “go ready armed before the children of Israel”

- flag: 'you sin against the Lord' (Nm 32:23, unlisted): RV1909 'habréis pecado á Jehová' used.
- flag: 'find you out… find our sins out': Henry plays on 'find out'; the RV1909 says 'os alcanzará', so the wordplay becomes 'alcanzar' / 'descubrir'.

### 495 Num 32:28-42 They take possession of the land to the east of Jordan.

### 496 Num 33:1-49 Encampments of the Israelites.
