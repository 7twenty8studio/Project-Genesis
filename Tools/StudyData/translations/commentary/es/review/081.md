# To review: es commentary batch 081

### 3451 Acts 7:17-29 
- flag: "fair toward God" is the KJV margin of Hch 7:20; the RV1909 has "agradable á Dios"; Henry's wording translated
- flag: "his holy child Jesus" (Hch 4:27, KJV): rendered "su santo Hijo Jesús" as the RV1909 does

### 3452 Acts 7:30-41 
- quoted (Acts 7:30): “in a flame of fire” → “en fuego de llama”
- flag: source has a full stop inside one sentence ("...ceremonial law. Stephen was so far..."); joined into one sentence as meant

### 3454 Acts 7:51-53 Stephen reproves the Jews for the death of Christ.
- quoted (John 4:23): “the Father in spirit and in truth” → “al Padre en espíritu y en verdad”
- flag: sensitive: Stephen's hearers, the Jews, called stubborn and wilful like their fathers

### 3455 Acts 7:54-60 The martyrdom of Stephen.
- quoted (Acts 7:55): “the right hand of God” → “á la diestra de Dios”
- flag: "he fell asleep" (Hch 7:60): the RV1909's single word "durmió" used
- flag: Henry plays on "lay it to their hearts" / "lay it to their charge" (KJV Hch 7:60; RV1909 "no les imputes este pecado"); rendered "tomarlo a pecho" / "tomárselo en cuenta"

### 3456 Acts 8:1-4 Saul persecutes the church.
- flag: "Saul" (the apostle) written "Saulo" as in the RV1909 of Hechos; the glossary's "Saúl" is the king

### 3457 Acts 8:5-13 Philip's success at Samaria. Simon the sorcerer baptized.
- quoted (Acts 8:10): “This man is the great power of God” → “Este es la gran virtud de Dios”
- flag: the RV1909 says "la gran virtud de Dios" where the KJV has "the great power of God"

### 3458 Acts 8:14-25 The hypocrisy of Simon detected.
- flag: source typo "coverts" for "converts"; translated as "convertidos"

### 3459 Acts 8:26-40 Philip and the Ethiopian.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hch 8:39 ("go on his way rejoicing"): RV1909 words "se fué por su camino gozoso" adapted to the future ("se irá por su camino gozoso"), so not verbatim; Henry's last sentence ("we") translated

### 3460 Acts 9:1-9 The conversion of Saul.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hch 22:14 ("that Just One"): RV1909 words "á aquel Justo" used
- flag: "sow in tears... reap in joy" alludes to Sal 126:5 (RV1909 "Los que sembraron con lágrimas, con regocijo segarán"); tense changed, translated

### 3461 Acts 9:10-22 Saul converted preaches Christ.
- quoted (Acts 9:6): “Lord, what wilt thou have me to do” → “Señor, ¿qué quieres que haga?”
- flag: "he said his prayers, now, he prayed them" is English wordplay; rendered "decía sus oraciones, ahora oraba"

### 3462 Acts 9:23-31 Saul is persecuted at Damascus, and goes to Jerusalem.
- quoted (1Cor 10:13): “will, with the temptation, also make a way to escape” → “dará también juntamente con la tentación la salida”
- quoted (Acts 9:31): “the comfort of the Holy Ghost” → “consuelo del Espíritu Santo”

### 3464 Acts 9:36-43 Dorcas raised to life.
- flag: sensitive: Henry's censure of "the numerous females" who seek only outward decoration and waste their lives on dress and vanity

### 3467 Acts 10:19-33 He goes to Cornelius.
- quoted (Acts 10:33): “to hear all things that are commanded” → “para oir todo lo que Dios”
- flag: quote 0: RV1909 "para oir todo lo que Dios te ha mandado"; "te" (Peter) left out because Henry generalises ("commanded of God")

### 3468 Acts 10:34-43 His discourse to Cornelius.
- quoted (Eph 2:20): “built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets” → “sobre el fundamento de los apóstoles y profetas”

### 3469 Acts 10:44-48 The gifts of the Holy Spirit poured out.
- quoted (John 6:63): “it is the Spirit that quickeneth, the flesh profiteth nothing” → “El espíritu es el que da vida; la carne nada aprovecha”
- quoted (Acts 10:45): “the gift of the Holy Ghost” → “el don del Espíritu Santo”
- quoted (Acts 10:47): “have received the Holy Ghost” → “han recibido el Espíritu Santo”
- flag: quote 0: the RV1909 writes "espíritu" in lower case; kept
- flag: source "we make difficult for ourselves" lacks a noun; translated as "nos creamos dificultades"

### 3470 Acts 11:1-18 Peter's defence.
- flag: unlisted quotations: Hch 11:18 ("arrepentimiento para vida") and Sal 51:17 ("Los sacrificios de Dios son el espíritu quebrantado"; KJV singular "sacrifice"), RV1909 words used

### 3471 Acts 11:19-24 The success of the gospel at Antioch.
- quoted (Acts 11:21): “The hand of the Lord was with them” → “La mano del Señor era con ellos”
- quoted (Acts 6:5): “full of faith and of the Holy Ghost” → “de fe y de Espíritu Santo”
- flag: "The wrath of man is made to praise God" alludes to Sal 76:10 (RV1909 "la ira del hombre te acarreará alabanza", second person); translated
- flag: source has a comma before "When the Lord Jesus..."; a full stop used

### 3472 Acts 11:25-30 The disciples named Christians, Relief sent to Judea.
- quoted (1Tim 4:8): “promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come” → “promesa de esta vida presente, y de la venidera”

### 3473 Acts 12:1-5 The martyrdom of James, and the imprisonment of Peter.
- translated, not quoted (Mark 10:38; Henry reports Christ's words in the third person ("that they should drink... that he was to drink"); the RV1909 of Mr 10:38 is a second-person question): “drink of, and be baptized with the baptism”

### 3475 Acts 12:12-19 Peter departs, Herod's rage.
- quoted (Luke 18:1): “men ought always to pray, and not to faint” → “es necesario orar siempre, y no desmayar”
- flag: source "the range of persecutors" read as "rage"; translated "la furia"

### 3476 Acts 12:20-25 The death of Herod.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hch 17:25 ("life, and breath, and all things"): RV1909 words "vida, y respiración, y todas las cosas" used

### 3477 Acts 13:1-3 The mission of Paul and Barnabas.
- flag: "to take pains, and not to take state" is English wordplay; rendered "tomarse trabajo, no para darse importancia"

### 3478 Acts 13:4-13 Elymas the sorcerer.
- flag: unlisted quotations: Hch 13:10 ("hijo del diablo", "enemigos de toda justicia" adapted) and Lc 9:62 ("poniendo su mano al arado", "para el reino de Dios"; verb made plural, so not wholly verbatim)

### 3479 Acts 13:14-31 Paul's discourse at Antioch.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hch 13:37 ("saw no corruption"): RV1909 words "no vió corrupción" used
