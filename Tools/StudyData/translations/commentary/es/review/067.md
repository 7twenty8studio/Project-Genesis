# To review: es commentary batch 067

### 2980 Matt 4:1-11 The temptation of Christ.
- quoted (James 1:14): “own lust, and enticed” → “de su propia concupiscencia es atraído, y cebado”
- quoted (Rom 6:1): “shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound” → “Perseveraremos en pecado para que la gracia crezca”
- quoted (Matt 4:8): “the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them” → “reinos del mundo, y su gloria”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “what is a man profited if he” → “de qué aprovecha al hombre, si”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “gain the whole world, and lose his own soul” → “granjeare todo el mundo, y perdiere su alma”
- flag: Source oddity: 'This passage, De 8:3, made against the tempter, therefore he left out part': the passage Satan cut short is Sal 91:11 ('to keep thee in all thy ways'); the reference is kept as 'Dt 8:3' as written.
- flag: Stg 1:14 is singular in the RV1909 ('cada uno … es atraído'); Henry's 'Others are tempted' recast as 'Los demás son tentados, cada uno cuando…' to keep the verse verbatim.
- flag: "It is written" and "Get thee hence, Satan!" given in the RV1909's words of Mt 4:4, 10: "Escrito está", "¡Vete, Satanás!" (unlisted).
- flag: 'Christ suffered, being tempted' (He 2:18) matches the RV1909's 'padeció siendo tentado'.

### 2983 Matt 4:23-25 Jesus teaches and works miracles.

### 2985 Matt 5:3-12 Who are blessed.
- quoted (Matt 5:6): “hunger and thirst after righteousness” → “tienen hambre y sed de justicia”
- quoted (Matt 5:10): “are persecuted for righteousness' sake” → “padecen persecución por causa de la justicia”
- flag: Henry's 'happy' (KJV 'blessed') translated 'dichosos'; 'blessed people' / 'blessed' kept as 'bienaventurados' (the RV1909's word).
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mt 5:8: 'ellos verán á Dios' in the RV1909's words.
- flag: 'Create in me such a clean heart, O God' (Sal 51:10) adapted by Henry ('such'); RV1909 'Crea en mí, oh Dios, un corazón limpio' followed with Henry's 'así de' added.

### 2995 Matt 6:9-15 How to pray.

### 2998 Matt 6:25-34 Trust in God commended.

- flag: 'Take no thought' (KJV): RV1909 'No os congojéis' (be not anxious); Henry's 'thought / thoughtfulness / care' rendered 'afán / cuidado' around it.
