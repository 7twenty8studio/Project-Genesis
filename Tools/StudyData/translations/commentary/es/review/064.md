# To review: es commentary batch 064

### 2900 Nah 1:1-8 The justice and power of the Lord.
- quoted (2Cor 11:31): “The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “El Dios y Padre del Señor nuestro Jesucristo”

### 2901 Nah 1:9-15 The overthrow of the Assyrians.
- quoted (Nah 1:8): “he will make an utter end” → “hará consumación”
- flag: 'make an utter end' given with the RV1909's 'hará consumación' (Nah 1:8-9).

### 2905 Nah 3:8-19 Its utter destruction.
- flag: 'the word rendered "merchants"' (Nah 3:16): the RV1909 also has 'mercaderes'; 'canker-worms' rendered 'pulgón', the RV1909's word there.

### 2907 Hab 1:12-17 These judgments to be inflicted by a nation more wicked than themselves.
- quoted (Hab 1:13): “of purer eyes than to behold” → “de ojos para ver”
- quoted (Eccl 8:11): “fully set in them to do evil” → “en ellos lleno para hacer mal”
- translated, not quoted (Hab 1:15; Henry turns Hab 1:15 into the present plural ('they catch ... and gather them'); the RV1909's singular futures ('cogerálos con su red, y juntarálos en su aljerife') cannot stand there; its word 'aljerife' is kept.): “in their net, and gather them in their drag”
- flag: 'of purer eyes than to behold it' given with the RV1909's 'limpio ... de ojos para ver' (Hab 1:13, 'Muy limpio eres de ojos para ver el mal'), 'demasiado limpio' for Henry's comparative.
- flag: Ec 8:11 (listed) fitted in the past: 'tanto más estaba su corazón en ellos lleno para hacer mal'; 'no se ejecutaba luego sentencia' follows the same verse.
- flag: 'drag' / 'drag-net' rendered with the RV1909's 'aljerife' and 'red barredera'.

### 2908 Hab 2:1-4 Habakkuk must wait in faith.
- translated, not quoted (Hab 2:1; The RV1909 of Hab 2:1 reads 'qué hablará en mí', which does not fit Henry's 'what he will say unto them'.): “what he will say unto”

### 2909 Hab 2:5-14 Judgments upon the Chaldeans.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1John 2:16 given in the RV1909's words 'La concupiscencia de la carne, y la concupiscencia de los ojos, y la soberbia de la vida'.
- flag: 'thick clay' (Hab 2:6, KJV) rendered with the RV1909's 'espeso lodo'.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Prov 15:27: 'he that is greedy of gain troubles his own house' given in the RV1909's words 'alborota su casa el codicioso'.
- flag: 'Thou hast sinned against thine own soul' (Hab 2:10): the RV1909 reads 'has pecado contra tu vida'; translated with 'alma', since Henry builds on 'soul'.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hab 2:14: 'as waters cover the sea' given in the RV1909's words 'como las aguas cubren la mar'.
- flag: Source typo 'wordly' (worldly); translated as 'mundanas'.

### 2910 Hab 2:15-20 Also upon drunkenness and idolatry.
- quoted (Hab 2:20): “The Lord is in his holy temple” → “Jehová está en su santo templo”

### 2911 Hab 3:1-2 The prophet beseeches God for his people.
- quoted (Hab 3:2): “in the midst of the years” → “en medio de los tiempos”
- flag: 'in the midst of the years' (Hab 3:2) given with the RV1909's 'en medio de los tiempos'; 'The word prayer' refers to the heading 'Oración de Habacuc' (Hab 3:1).

### 2912 Hab 3:3-15 He calls to mind former deliverances.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hab 3:13: 'It is for salvation with thine anointed' given with the RV1909's words 'para salvar con tu ungido'.
- flag: 'Joshua ... a figure of Him whose name he bare, even Jesus, our Joshua' rests on the two names being the same in Hebrew/Greek; translated as written.

### 2913 Hab 3:16-19 His firm trust in the Divine mercy.
- quoted (Heb 10:37): “Yet a little while, and He that shall come will come, and will not tarry” → “aun un poquito, y el que ha de venir vendrá, y no tardará”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Cant 1:3: 'as ointment poured forth' given with the RV1909's 'ungüento derramado'.
- flag: 'Yet a little while ...' (He 10:37) opens with the RV1909's 'Porque', kept from the verse.

### 2914 Zeph 1:1-6 Threatenings against sinners.
- quoted (Heb 10:39): “believe to the saving of the soul” → “fieles para ganancia del alma”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Isa 48:22: 'There is no peace for the wicked' given in the RV1909's words 'No hay paz para los malos'.
- flag: 'Malcham' (Sof 1:5, KJV): the RV1909 reads 'su rey'; the glossary's 'Malcam' is used.
- flag: He 10:39 (listed): 'draw back unto perdition' given with the RV1909's 'se retiran para perdición', and 'believe to the saving of the soul' with 'fieles para ganancia del alma'.

### 2915 Zeph 1:7-13 More threatenings.
- quoted (Zeph 1:9): “those that leap on the threshold” → “los que saltan la puerta”
- quoted (Zeph 1:12): “say in their heart, the Lord will not do good, neither will he do evil” → “dicen en su corazón: Jehová ni hará bien ni mal”
- flag: 'leap on the threshold' (KJV) given with the RV1909's 'saltan la puerta'.

### 2916 Zeph 1:14-18 Distress from the approaching judgments.
- quoted (Zeph 1:14): “the great day of the Lord” → “día grande de Jehová”
- quoted (Zeph 1:15): “a day of trouble and distress” → “día de angustia y de aprieto”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “What is a man profited if he” → “de qué aprovecha al hombre, si”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “gain the whole world, and lose his own soul” → “granjeare todo el mundo, y perdiere su alma”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “what shall a man give in exchange for his soul” → “qué recompensa dará el hombre por su alma”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 3:7: 'flee from the wrath to come' given as 'Huyamos de la ira que vendrá'.

### 2917 Zeph 2:1-3 An exhortation to repentance.
- flag: Henry's two readings ('not desiring' / 'not desirable') explain the KJV's 'O nation not desired' (Sof 2:1); the RV1909 has 'gente no amable'. Translated as written.

### 2918 Zeph 2:4-15 Judgments upon other nations.
- quoted (Zeph 2:5): “the word of the Lord” → “la palabra de Jehová”

### 2920 Zeph 3:8-13 Encouragement to look for mercy.
- flag: sensitive: 2920 — 'when vengeance would be executed on the Jewish nation' (the Jews as a whole).
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1Cor 1:30 given with the RV1909's words 'hecho por Dios sabiduría, y justificación, y santificación, y redención' (RV1909 'justificación' for the KJV's 'righteousness').
- flag: 'the pure language' alludes to Sof 3:9 (RV1909 'el labio limpio'); kept as Henry's phrase.

### 2921 Zeph 3:14-20 Promises of future favour and prosperity.
- quoted (Zeph 3:17): “He will save; he will” → “él salvará”
- quoted (Zeph 3:20): “a name and a praise” → “por renombre y por alabanza”
- flag: 'he will be Jesus' rests on the name meaning 'the Lord saves' (Mt 1:21); translated as written.

### intro Hag 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hag 2:7: 'the Desire of all nations' given in the RV1909's words 'el Deseado de todas las gentes'.

### 2922 Hag 1:1-11 Haggai reproves the Jews for neglecting the temple.
- flag: 'the meat that perishes ... the meat which lasts to eternal life' (Jn 6:27) given with the RV1909's 'la comida que perece' and 'la comida que ... permanece' adapted ('la comida que permanece para vida eterna').

### 2923 Hag 1:12-15 He promises God's assistance to them.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 8:31 given with the RV1909's words '¿quién contra nosotros?' after Henry's 'si él por nosotros'.

### 2924 Hag 2:1-9 Greater glory promised to the second temple than to the first.
- quoted (Hag 2:7): “the Desire of all nations” → “el Deseado de todas las gentes”
- quoted (Hag 2:9): “the glory of this latter house shall be greater than” → “la gloria de aquesta casa postrera será mayor que la de la primera”
- flag: sensitive: 2924 — 'This prophecy alone is enough to silence the Jews, and condemn their obstinate rejection of Him' (the Jews as a whole).
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 11:3: Henry's quoted "He that should come" given with the RV1909's 'Aquel que había de venir' (capitalised).
- flag: 'But the Jews under the latter temple had much trouble; but this promise...' has a doubled 'but' in the source; both kept ('Pero ... pero').

### 2926 Hag 2:20-23 The kingdom of Christ foretold.
- flag: 'the Signet' (Hag 2:23, KJV) rendered with the RV1909's 'anillo de sellar' ('el Anillo de sellar').
- flag: 'are yea and amen' (2 Co 1:20) given close to the RV1909's 'Sí, y ... Amén'.
