# To review: es commentary batch 001

### 1 Gen 1:1-2 God creates heaven and earth.
- quoted (Gen 1:2): “was without form, and void” → “estaba desordenada y vacía”
- flag: Henry says 'the world was without form, and void'; rendered 'la tierra estaba desordenada y vacía' to keep the RV1909 words of Gn 1:2 (its subject is la tierra).
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jn 1:10 ('el mundo fué hecho por él', RV1909); 'nothing was made without him' (Jn 1:3) translated, since the RV1909 wording differs.

### 2 Gen 1:3-5 The creation of light.
- quoted (Gen 1:3): “God said, Let there be light” → “dijo Dios: Sea la luz”
- quoted (Gen 1:4): “God divided the light from the darkness” → “apartó Dios la luz de las tinieblas”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2 Co 6:14 ('¿qué comunión la luz con las tinieblas?', RV1909, verbless as in that Bible); please verify.
- flag: Ef 5:8 ('darkness ... light in the Lord') and Sal 1:2 ('meditating in his law day and night') are echoed in Henry's own syntax and translated.

### 6 Gen 1:26-28 Man created in the image of God.
- quoted (Gen 1:26): “God said, "Let us make man” → “dijo Dios … Hagamos al hombre”
- flag: 'the beasts that perish' (Sal 49:12, 20) is the RV1909's own 'las bestias que perecen'.

### 8 Gen 1:31-31 The work of creation ended and approved.
- quoted (Ps 103:22): “All his works, in all places of his dominion, bless” → “Todas sus obras, en todos los lugares de su señorío”
- quoted (Ps 104:35): “bless thou the Lord, O my soul” → “Bendice, alma mía, á Jehová”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mt 3:7 ('la ira que vendrá', RV1909).
- flag: unlisted quotation in Henry's own quotation marks of 2 P 3:13: RV1909 'cielos nuevos y tierra nueva, según sus promesas, en los cuales mora la justicia'; Henry leaves out 'according to his promise', so 'según sus promesas' is left out here too.
- flag: source oddity: 'new--created' (double hyphen) in the English.

### 10 Gen 2:4-7 Particulars about the creation.
- flag: KJV-dependent: Henry explains that 'LORD' in capitals in English Bibles stands for Jehovah; translated as written ('nuestras Biblias inglesas', 'SEÑOR'), but the RV1909 itself prints 'Jehová' there. A reviewer may want a note or a different rendering.

### 11 Gen 2:8-14 The planting of the garden of Eden.
- translated, not quoted (Rev 2:7; matched to Ap 2:7, but here Henry quotes Gn 2:9 ('the tree of life in the midst of the garden'); rendered with the RV1909 of Gn 2:9, 'el árbol de vida en medio del huerto', given as quote 1): “the tree of life”
- quoted (Gen 2:9): “in the midst of the garden” → “el árbol de vida en medio del huerto”
- quoted (Rev 2:7): “the Tree of life” → “Árbol de la vida”
- quoted (Gen 2:17): “the tree of the knowledge of good and evil” → “el árbol de ciencia del bien y del mal”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 2:9: 'todo árbol delicioso á la vista, y bueno para comer' (RV1909) worked into Henry's sentence; please verify.
- flag: 'the tree of knowledge of good and evil': RV1909 'árbol de ciencia del bien y del mal'; Henry's 'so that by it man might know' rendered 'conocer'.

### 14 Gen 2:18-25 The animals named, The making of woman, The Divine institution of marriage.
- flag: KJV 'help meet' (Gn 2:18, 20) rendered with the RV1909's 'ayuda idónea'.
- flag: last sentence echoes Sal 49:20 (RV1909 'El hombre en honra que no entiende, Semejante es á las bestias que perecen'); Henry's own syntax translated, 'las bestias que perecen' as in the RV1909.

### 15 Gen 3:1-5 The serpent deceives Eve.
- translated, not quoted (Gen 3:1; not a quotation of Gn 3:1: Henry puts Satan's question to other commandments ('Has God said, Ye shall not lie...'), which the RV1909's 'No comáis' cannot carry): “God said, Ye shall not”
- translated, not quoted (James 4:7; Henry turns Stg 4:7 into the first person plural ('let us resist the devil, and he will flee from us'); the RV1909's 'resistid al diablo, y de vosotros huirá' would change his exhortation): “resist the devil, and he will flee from”

### 16 Gen 3:6-8 Adam and Eve transgress the Divine command, and fall into sin and misery.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Dn 12:2: 'vergüenza y confusión perpetua' (RV1909) for Henry's 'shame and everlasting contempt'; please verify.
- flag: 'miserable comforters' (Job 16:2) translated; the RV1909 has 'Consoladores molestos'.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 17 Gen 3:9-13 God calls upon Adam and Eve to answer.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 3:9: '¿Dónde estás tú?' (RV1909), with Henry's vocative 'Adán' kept.

### 18 Gen 3:14-15 The serpent cursed, The promised Seed.
- flag: source oddity: 'the drawn of the gospel day' is read as 'dawn' ('el amanecer').
- flag: 'Seed' rendered 'Simiente' (RV1909 Gn 3:15 'simiente'), capitalised where Henry means Christ; 'heel' 'calcañar' as in the RV1909.

### 19 Gen 3:16-19 The punishment of mankind.
- quoted (Gen 3:16): “He shall rule over thee” → “él se enseñoreará de ti”
- quoted (Gen 3:18): “eat the herb of the field” → “hierba del campo”
- quoted (Acts 2:24): “the pains of death” → “los dolores de la muerte”
- quoted (Gal 4:4): “made under the law” → “hecho súbdito á la ley”
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “made a curse for us” → “hecho por nosotros maldición”
- flag: unlisted quotation 'Wives, be subject to your own husbands' (Ef 5:22 / Col 3:18 / 1 P 3:1): used the RV1909 of Col 3:18 as I recall it, 'Casadas, estad sujetas á vuestros maridos'; please verify the verse and wording.
- flag: KJV-dependent: Henry links 'travailing pains' to 'the travail of his soul' (Is 53:11); the RV1909 says 'Del trabajo de su alma', so the link to 'dolores de parto' is weaker in Spanish; Hch 2:24 'dolores de la muerte' keeps it.
- flag: 'a man of sorrows' (Is 53:3, RV1909 'varón de dolores'), 'great drops of blood' (Lc 22:44) and 'obedient unto death' (Fil 2:8) are echoed in Henry's own syntax, in the RV1909's words where they fit.

### 20 Gen 3:20-21 The first clothing of mankind.
- quoted (Rom 13:14): “put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ” → “vestíos del Señor Jesucristo”
- flag: 'coats of skin' (Gn 3:21) rendered with the RV1909's 'túnicas de pieles'.

### 22 Gen 4:1-7 The birth, employment, and religion of Cain and Abel.
- quoted (Gen 4:1): “said, I have gotten a man from the Lord” → “dijo: Adquirido he varón por Jehová”
- quoted (Gen 4:7): “If thou doest not well, sin” → “si no hicieres bien, el pecado … está á la puerta”
- quoted (Rev 3:20): “stand at the door” → “á la puerta”
- flag: quote 2: Re 3:20 is 'yo estoy á la puerta' in the RV1909; Henry speaks of Christ in the third person, so only 'á la puerta' is the verse's words.
- flag: 'the good old way' (Jer 6:16) translated; the RV1909 has 'las sendas antiguas, cuál sea el buen camino'.
- flag: Mr. Dod (John Dod, Puritan) rendered 'el buen señor Dod'.

### 23 Gen 4:8-15 Cain murders Abel, The curse of Cain.
- quoted (1John 3:12): “because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous” → “porque sus obras eran malas, y las de su hermano justas”
- quoted (Matt 12:30): “He that is not with me is against me” → “El que no es conmigo, contra mí es”
- flag: 'Abel, being dead, yet speaketh' (He 11:4) translated; the RV1909 has 'y difunto, aun habla por ella'.

### 24 Gen 4:16-18 The conduct of Cain, His family.
- quoted (Gen 4:16): “Cain went out from the presence of the Lord” → “salió Caín de delante de Jehová”
- flag: source oddity: the English has unbalanced quotation marks (shaking,' or trembling,' ... a vagabond:'); written here as balanced single quotes.

### 25 Gen 4:19-24 Lamech and his wives, The skill of Cain's descendants.
- flag: 'Lamech' written 'Lamec' (modernized from the RV1909's 'Lamech'; not in the glossary).
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.
