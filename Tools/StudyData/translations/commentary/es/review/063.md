# To review: es commentary batch 063

### 2860 Amos 5:7-17 Earnest exhortations to repentance.
- flag: 'create in us a clean heart, and to renew a right spirit within us' (Sal 51:10) is worked into Henry's indirect sentence; translated, as the RV1909's imperative ('Crea en mí') cannot stand there.

### 2864 Amos 7:1-9 Visions of judgments to come upon Israel.
- flag: 'the produce of the earth of the former growth ... the latter growth' follows the KJV of Am 7:1 ('the latter growth'); the RV1909 has 'el heno tardío'. Henry's wording translated.

### 2865 Amos 7:10-17 Amaziah threatens Amos.
- quoted (1Cor 1:27): “foolish things of the world to confound the wise” → “para avergonzar á los sabios”

### 2866 Amos 8:1-3 The near approach of the ruin of Israel.
- quoted (Amos 8:1): “a basket of summer fruit” → “un canastillo de fruta de verano”

### 2867 Amos 8:4-10 Oppression reproved.
- flag: 'sabbath' here is the Jewish Sabbath of Am 8:5 applied to Christians; rendered 'sábado' without the Christian-sabbath note, since Henry does not call it the Christian sabbath. Reviewer may want to check.
- flag: 'in the midst of life we are in death' is from the Book of Common Prayer burial service; translated.
- flag: 'rich and poor meet together' alludes to Pr 22:2 (RV1909 'El rico y el pobre se encontraron'); kept as Henry's present tense.

### 2869 Amos 9:1-10 The ruin of Israel.
- quoted (Amos 9:1): “saw the Lord standing upon” → “al Señor que estaba sobre”
- quoted (Amos 9:4): “eyes upon them for evil” → “ojos para mal”
- quoted (Amos 9:4): “evil, and not for good” → “para mal, y no para bien”
- flag: Am 9:4 'pondré sobre ellos mis ojos para mal' turned to the third person ('pondrá sobre ellos sus ojos para mal'), as Henry does; the quoted run 'ojos para mal' is the RV1909's.

### 2870 Amos 9:11-15 The restoration of the Jews and the gospel blessing.
- quoted (John 11:52): “the children of God that were scattered abroad” → “los hijos de Dios que estaban derramados”

### 2873 Jonah 1:1-3 Jonah, sent to Nineveh, flees to Tarshish.
- translated, not quoted (Jonah 1:1; Henry's own general phrase ('when the word of the Lord comes to us'), not a quotation of Jon 1:1.): “the word of the Lord”

### 2874 Jonah 1:4-7 He is stayed by a tempest.
- quoted (Jonah 1:7): “The lot fell upon Jonah” → “la suerte cayó sobre Jonás”
- quoted (Job 28:21): “hid from the eyes of all living” → “á los ojos de todo viviente”
- flag: 'Lord, show me wherefore thou contendest with me' echoes Job 10:2; translated as Henry's prayer.
- flag: 'The lot fell upon Jonah' given as the RV1909's 'la suerte cayó sobre Jonás', with 'Y' in front from the verse.

### 2875 Jonah 1:8-12 His discourse with the mariners.
- quoted (Jonah 1:10): “him, Why hast thou done this” → “¿Por qué has hecho esto?”
- quoted (Jonah 1:9): “made the sea and the dry land” → “que hizo la mar y la tierra”
- flag: 'made the sea and the dry land' given with the RV1909's 'que hizo la mar y la tierra' (no 'dry').

### 2876 Jonah 1:13-17 He is cast into the sea, and miraculously preserved.
- flag: 'these heathen mariners' rendered 'estos marineros paganos' (Henry contrasts them favourably with Christians); not marked sensitive, reviewer may judge.
- flag: 'It was of the Lord's mercies that Jonah was not consumed' echoes Lm 3:22; translated.

### 2877 Jonah 2:1-9 The prayer of Jonah.
- quoted (Jonah 1:10): “fled from the presence of the Lord” → “de delante de Jehová”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jonah 2:8: 'those who observe lying vanities' given as the RV1909's 'los que guardan las vanidades ilusorias'; 'forsake their own mercy' kept as Henry's own words (RV1909 'su misericordia abandonan').

### 2879 Jonah 3:1-4 Jonah sent again to Nineveh, preaches there.
- translated, not quoted (Jonah 3:1; Henry's own general phrase ('whatever the word of the Lord commands'), not a quotation of Jon 3:1.): “the word of the Lord”
- flag: 'these words' refers to Jon 3:4, which the source does not quote; the RV1909 reads 'De aquí á cuarenta días Nínive será destruída'.

### 2880 Jonah 3:5-10 Nineveh is spared upon the repentance of the inhabitants.
- flag: 'a broken and a contrite heart ... he will not despise' alludes to Sal 51:17 (RV1909 'al corazón contrito y humillado no despreciarás tú'); kept as Henry's third-person sentence.

### 2881 Jonah 4:1-4 Jonah repines at God's mercy to Nineveh, and is reproved.
- flag: 'Jonah makes the subject of reflection upon God' uses 'reflection' in the old sense of reproach; rendered 'motivo de reproche contra Dios'.

### 2882 Jonah 4:5-11 He is taught by the withering of a gourd, that he did wrong.
- quoted (Jonah 4:5): “Jonah went out of the city” → “Salióse Jonás de la ciudad”
- flag: 'gourd' rendered 'calabacera', the RV1909's word in Jon 4:6-10.

### 2884 Micah 1:8-16 Also against Jerusalem and other cities, Their precautions vain.
- quoted (Micah 1:10): “Roll thyself in the dust” → “Revuélcate en el polvo”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Micah 1:10: 'declare it not in Gath' given in the RV1909's words 'no lo digáis en Gath' (the RV1909 spells Gath; the glossary's 'Gat').
- flag: 'a house of Aphrah, "a house of dust"' explains the Hebrew name; the RV1909 has 'Beth-le-aphrah'; rendered 'una casa de Afra'.
- flag: Source typo: 'their turn to be punished will some come' (for 'soon'); translated as 'pronto'.
- flag: Source title has a capital after a comma ('cities, Their precautions vain'); rendered with a semicolon.

### 2885 Micah 2:1-5 The sins and desolations of Israel.
- quoted (Micah 2:5): “the congregation of the Lord” → “la congregación de Jehová”

### 2886 Micah 2:6-11 Their evil practices.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Micah 2:6: Henry's quoted "Prophesy not" given in the RV1909's words "No profeticéis".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Heb 13:14: 'here we have no continuing city' given in the RV1909's words 'no tenemos aquí ciudad permanente'.

### 2888 Micah 3:1-8 The cruelty of the princes, and the falsehood of the prophets.
- quoted (Micah 3:8): “full of power by the Spirit of the Lord” → “lleno de fuerza del espíritu de Jehová”

### 2889 Micah 3:9-12 Their false security.
- quoted (Micah 3:12): “Therefore shall Zion for your sake” → “Por tanto, á causa de vosotros será Sión arada como campo”
- flag: Henry quotes Mi 3:12 through 'be ploughed as a field'; the full RV1909 run is used.

### 2890 Micah 4:1-8 The peace of the kingdom of Christ.
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's English word "halteth" (Mi 4:6-7); the RV1909 has 'la coja'. The English word is kept in quotation marks with a gloss '(que cojea)'; reviewer may prefer "la coja".
- flag: 'beat their swords into ploughshares' alludes to Mi 4:3 (RV1909 'martillarán sus espadas para azadones'); kept as Henry's phrase.
