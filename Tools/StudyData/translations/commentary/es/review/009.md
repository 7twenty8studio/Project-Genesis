# To review: es commentary batch 009

### 244 Ex 14:21-31 The Israelites pass through the Red sea, which drowns the Egyptians.
- quoted (Ex 14:29): “walked upon dry land in the midst of the sea” → “por medio de la mar en seco”
- quoted (Ex 14:25): “flee from the face of Israel” → “de delante de Israel”
- quoted (Ex 14:21): “out his hand over the sea” → “su mano sobre la mar”
- quoted (Ex 14:24): “the host of the Egyptians” → “el campo de los Egipcios”
- quoted (Ex 14:30): “saw the Egyptians dead upon” → “á los Egipcios muertos”
- flag: quote 3: KJV 'the host of the Egyptians', RV1909 'el campo de los Egipcios' (the camp, Ex 14:24).
- flag: the closing sentence echoes Ex 14:13 (RV1909 'nunca más para siempre los veréis'); translated in Henry's third person.

### 245 Ex 15:1-21 The song of Moses for the deliverance of Israel.
- flag: unlisted quotations: Sal 144:15 ('Bienaventurado el pueblo cuyo Dios es Jehová') and Ex 15:11 ('magnífico en santidad', 'terrible en loores', 'hacedor de maravillas'), RV1909; Henry's 'he is doing wonders' given as 'Es hacedor de maravillas'.
- flag: 'Holiness to the Lord' (Ex 28:36, RV1909 'SANTIDAD Á JEHOVÁ') translated 'Santidad al Señor' as Henry's own phrase.

### 246 Ex 15:22-27 The bitter waters at Marah, The Israelites come to Elim.
- quoted (Ex 15:25): “he cried unto the Lord” → “clamó á Jehová”
- flag: 'He is our life and the length of our days' adapts Dt 30:20 (RV1909 'él es tu vida, y la longitud de tus días') to the first person plural; translated.

### 247 Ex 16:1-12 The Israelites come to the wilderness of Sin. They murmur for food, God promises bread from heaven.
- quoted (Ex 16:3): “by the hand of the Lord” → “por mano de Jehová”

### 248 Ex 16:13-21 God sends quails and manna.
- quoted (Ex 16:14): “a small round thing, as small as the hoar frost” → “una cosa menuda, redonda, menuda como una helada”
- flag: 'Manna, Manhu' is the KJV margin's form of Ex 16:15 (RV1909 '¿Qué es esto?'); kept as Henry gives it.
- flag: 'like coriander seed, in colour like pearls' (Nm 11:7; RV1909 'su color como color de bdelio') translated from Henry's English.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Ti 6:17 ('todas las cosas en abundancia de que gocemos', RV1909) for 'all things richly to enjoy'.

### 249 Ex 16:22-31 Particulars respecting the manna.
- quoted (1Tim 4:5): “is sanctified by the word of God and prayer” → “por la palabra de Dios y por la oración es santificado”

### 250 Ex 16:32-36 An omer of manna to be preserved.
- quoted (1Cor 10:5): “with many of them God was not well pleased” → “de muchos de ellos no se agradó Dios”

### 251 Ex 17:1-7 The Israelites murmur for water at Rephidim, God sendeth it out of the rock.
- quoted (Ex 17:1): “The children of Israel journeyed” → “los hijos de Israel”
- quoted (Ex 17:1): “according to the commandment of the Lord” → “al mandamiento de Jehová”
- flag: quotes 0-1 (Ex 17:1): the RV1909 has 'toda la congregación de los hijos de Israel partió … por sus jornadas, al mandamiento de Jehová'; 'por sus jornadas' kept from that verse.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Co 10:4 ('la piedra era Cristo', RV1909), so Henry's 'Rock' is 'piedra' there and 'peña' elsewhere.
- flag: names: the RV1909 spells Ex 17:7 'Massah y Meribah'; the glossary's 'Masa' and 'Meriba' used.

### 252 Ex 17:8-16 Amalek overcome, The prayers of Moses.
- translated, not quoted (Dan 6:14; listed with Dn 6:14, but Henry quotes Ex 17:12; its RV1909 words 'hasta que se puso el sol' are used instead (flagged).): “till the going down of the sun”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ex 17:12 ('las manos de Moisés estaban pesadas', 'hasta que se puso el sol', RV1909); quote 0 is listed with the wrong verse (Dn 6:14).
- flag: unlisted echo of Is 9:12 ('todavía su mano extendida', RV1909) for 'whose hand is stretched out still'.

### 256 Ex 19:1-8 The people come to Sinai, God's message to them, and their answer.
- quoted (Heb 10:1): “a shadow of good things to come” → “la sombra de los bienes venideros”
- translated, not quoted (Jer 31:31; Henry reports the promise in indirect speech ('declared that he would make'); the RV1909 has the first person 'haré nuevo pacto'.): “make a new covenant”
- quoted (Ex 19:8): “All that the Lord hath spoken we will do” → “Todo lo que Jehová ha dicho haremos”
- quoted (Ex 19:8): “returned the words of the people” → “refirió las palabras del pueblo”
- flag: 'ready to vanish away' (He 8:13) rendered 'cerca de desvanecerse' as in the RV1909.
- flag: 'Oh that there had been such a heart in them!' echoes Dt 5:29 (RV1909 '¡Quién diera que tuviesen tal corazón'); translated in Henry's past form.

### 258 Ex 19:16-25 The presence of God on Sinai.
- quoted (Acts 16:30, Acts 16:31): “What must I do to be saved” → “qué es menester que yo haga para ser salvo”
- quoted (Acts 16:31): “the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved” → “Cree en el Señor Jesucristo, y serás salvo”
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us” → “Cristo nos redimió de la maldición de la ley, hecho por nosotros maldición”
- quoted (Col 1:14): “We have redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins” → “tenemos redención por su sangre, la remisión de pecados”
- translated, not quoted (Acts 13:39; Henry turns Hch 13:39 into the first person plural ('we could not be justified'); the RV1909 has 'no pudisteis ser justificados'.): “could not be justified by the law of Moses”

### 259 Ex 20:1-2 The preface to the ten commandments.
- quoted (James 2:10): “Whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all” → “cualquiera que hubiere guardado toda la ley, y ofendiere en un punto, es hecho culpado de todos”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ro 6:23 ('la paga del pecado es muerte', RV1909).

### 260 Ex 20:3-11 The commandments of the first table.
- quoted (Ex 20:7): “The Lord will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain” → “no dará por inocente Jehová al que tomare su nombre en vano”
- quoted (Mark 2:27): “the sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath” → “el sábado por causa del hombre es hecho; no el hombre por causa del sábado”
- translated, not quoted (Ex 20:10; 'the sabbath of the Lord' is Henry's own phrase here; the RV1909 verse has 'reposo para Jehová'.): “The sabbath of the Lord”
- flag: source oddity: 'Whatsoever ye do, do all the glory of God' lacks 'to'; given with the RV1909 of 1 Co 10:31 ('hacedlo todo á gloria de Dios'), unlisted quotation.
- flag: the fourth commandment's 'Remember' given with the RV1909 'Acordarte has' (Ex 20:8), keeping Henry's quotation marks.
- flag: source oddity: 'It matters not whether the word of God, or sacred things, all such-like things…' is incomplete (something like 'be abused' is missing); translated as it stands.

### 261 Ex 20:12-17 Of the second table.
- quoted (Lev 19:18): “Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself” → “amarás á tu prójimo como á ti mismo”
- quoted (Ex 20:12): “Honour thy father and thy mother” → “Honra á tu padre y á tu madre”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ex 20:17 ('No codiciarás', RV1909).
- flag: Henry's imperatives to children ('come when they call you…') rendered with vosotros plural, as in the RV1909's register.

### 263 Ex 20:22-26 Idolatry again forbidden.
- flag: 'there he will come unto them, and will bless them' adapts Ex 20:24 (RV1909 'vendré á ti, y te bendeciré') to the third person; translated.

### 264 Ex 21:1-11 Laws respecting servants.
- flag: 'without money and without price' given with the RV1909 of Is 55:1 ('sin dinero y sin precio'), unlisted quotation.

### 265 Ex 21:12-21 Judicial laws.
- quoted (Job 31:14): “shall I do, when God riseth up” → “Qué haría yo cuando Dios se levantase”

### 267 Ex 22:1-31 Judicial laws.
- quoted (Titus 2:12): “lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly in this present world” → “renunciando á la impiedad y á los deseos mundanos, vivamos en este siglo templada, y justa, y píamente”
- flag: source typo 'wordly lusts' read as 'worldly lusts'; the whole of Henry's 'denying ungodliness…' quoted from the RV1909, whose word order differs.

### 269 Ex 23:10-19 The year of rest, The sabbath, The three festivals.
- translated, not quoted (Josh 9:27; listed with Jos 9:27, but Henry echoes Deuteronomy's 'the place which he shall choose'; the RV1909 of Dt 16:16, 'en el lugar que él escogiere', is used (flagged).): “in the place which he should choose”
- quoted (Heb 13:16): “with such sacrifices God is well pleased” → “de tales sacrificios se agrada Dios”
- flag: quote 0: unlisted quotation of Dt 16:16 ('en el lugar que él escogiere', RV1909); the listed Jos 9:27 reads 'escogiese'.

### 270 Ex 23:20-33 God promises to conduct the Israelites to Canaan.
- quoted (Ex 23:20): “Behold, I send an angel before thee” → “He aquí yo envío el Angel delante de ti”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ex 23:23 ('mi Angel', RV1909, unaccented) for 'mine angel'; elsewhere Henry's 'Angel' is written 'Ángel'.
