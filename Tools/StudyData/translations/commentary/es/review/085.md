# To review: es commentary batch 085

### 3570 Rom 7:7-13 The use and excellence of the law.
- quoted (Rom 7:12): “the law is holy, and the commandment holy” → “la ley á la verdad es santa, y el mandamiento santo, y justo, y bueno”
- flag: quote 0 includes the RV1909's "á la verdad" inside the run, which Henry's English lacks

### 3571 Rom 7:14-17 The spiritual conflicts between corruption and grace in a believer.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Heb 12:23: RV1909 "los espíritus de los justos hechos perfectos" used verbatim (Henry: "the spirits of just made perfect")

### 3572 Rom 7:18-22 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Eph 4:24: RV1909 "criado conforme á Dios en justicia y en santidad de verdad" used verbatim (Henry: "after God is created in true holiness"; the RV1909 run adds "en justicia")

### 3573 Rom 7:23-25 
- quoted (Rom 7:25): “the law of God; but with the flesh” → “á la ley de Dios, mas con la carne”
- quoted (Rom 7:23): “against the law of my mind” → “contra la ley de mi espíritu”
- flag: quote 1: Henry's "the law of my mind" is the RV1909's "la ley de mi espíritu"; Henry's own "with my mind" kept as "con mi mente"

### 3574 Rom 8:1-9 The freedom of believers from condemnation.
- quoted (Rom 8:5): “the things of the Spirit” → “las cosas del espíritu”
- quoted (Rom 8:9): “Ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit” → “vosotros no estáis en la carne, sino en el espíritu”
- flag: quotes 0-1: the RV1909 writes "espíritu" in lower case in Rom 8:5, 9, where Henry means the Holy Spirit; kept as the RV1909 has it
- flag: "Those that live in pleasure are dead" (1 Tim 5:6) translated in Henry's plural, not quoted (RV1909 "la que vive en delicias, viviendo está muerta")

### 3575 Rom 8:10-17 Their privileges as being the children of God.
- quoted (Rom 8:1): “walk, not after the flesh, but after the Spirit” → “conforme á la carne, mas conforme al espíritu”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 8:15: RV1909 "el espíritu de servidumbre" and "el espíritu de adopción" used verbatim

### 3576 Rom 8:18-25 Their hopeful prospects under tribulations.
- quoted (Rom 8:18): “the sufferings of this present time” → “lo que en este tiempo se padece”
- flag: unlisted quotations: Rom 8:23 RV1909 "las primicias del Espíritu" and Heb 10:37 "el que ha de venir vendrá" used verbatim

### 3578 Rom 8:28-31 Their interest in the love of God.
- quoted (Rom 8:29): “Whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son” → “á los que antes conoció, también predestinó para que fuesen hechos conformes á la imagen de su Hijo”
- quoted (Rom 8:29): “be conformed to the image of his Son” → “fuesen hechos conformes á la imagen de su Hijo”
- quoted (Rom 8:30): “Whom he did predestinate, them he also called” → “á los que predestinó, á éstos también llamó”
- quoted (Rom 8:28): “called according to his purpose” → “conforme al propósito son llamados”
- quoted (Rom 8:30): “Whom he called, them he also justified” → “á los que llamó, á éstos también justificó”
- quoted (Rom 8:30): “Whom he justified, them he also glorified” → “á los que justificó, á éstos también glorificó”
- quoted (Eph 3:19): “the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge” → “el amor de Cristo, que excede á todo conocimiento”
- flag: "as for God, his way... is perfect" alludes to Ps 18:30 (RV1909 "Dios, perfecto su camino"); translated in Henry's wording

### 3579 Rom 8:32-39 Their final triumph, through Christ.
- quoted (Rom 8:34): “Yea, rather that is risen again” → “Más aún, el que también resucitó”
- quoted (Rom 8:34): “at the right hand of God” → “á la diestra de Dios”
- flag: unlisted quotations: Rom 4:5 RV1909 "cree en aquél que justifica al impío" and Rom 8:35 "¿Quién nos apartará" used verbatim
- flag: "You may even live to see and seek your parting" is obscure in the English; translated literally

### 3580 Rom 9:1-5 The apostle's concern that his countrymen were strangers to the gospel.
- flag: Henry quotes "accursed" (KJV Rom 9:3) in his own marks; the RV1909 says "apartado de Cristo"; rendered "anatema" (the Greek word) inside his marks
- flag: sensitive: the Jews' destruction "for their obstinate unbelief"

### 3581 Rom 9:6-13 The promises are made good to the spiritual seed of Abraham.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Eph 2:3: RV1909 "por naturaleza hijos de ira, también como los demás" used verbatim

### 3582 Rom 9:14-24 Answers to objections against God's sovereign conduct, in exercising mercy and justice.
- quoted (Rom 9:24): “not of the Jews only” → “no sólo de los Judíos”
- flag: "Why does he yet find fault?" (Rom 9:19) translated (RV1909: "¿Por qué, pues, se enoja?"); "vasos de misericordia" (Rom 9:23) matches the RV1909
- flag: "make our calling and election sure" (2 Pet 1:10) translated in the first person plural, after the RV1909's "hacer firme vuestra vocación y elección"
- flag: "preventing ... grace" in the old theological sense rendered "gracia preveniente"

### 3584 Rom 9:30-33 The falling short of the Jews is owing to their seeking justification, not by faith, but by the works of the law.
- flag: "refuges of lies" and "hiding-place" allude to Isa 28:17 (RV1909 "la acogida de la mentira", "el escondrijo"); translated in Henry's plural wording
- flag: "the Lord our Righteousness" (Jer 23:6) translated as in Henry's sentence, as in batch 083 section 3532

### 3586 Rom 10:5-11 The difference between the righteousness of the law, and the righteousness of faith.
- quoted (Rom 10:9): “raised him from the dead” → “Dios le levantó de los muertos”

### 3587 Rom 10:12-17 The Gentiles stand on a level with the Jews, in justification and salvation.
- translated, not quoted (1Kings 18:24; listed under 1 Kgs 18:24, but Henry is paraphrasing Rom 10:13 ("call on the name of the Lord Jesus"); Elijah's words don't belong here, so translated): “call on the name of the Lord”
- flag: "God manifest in the flesh" (1 Tim 3:16) translated (RV1909: "Dios ha sido manifestado en carne")
- flag: "by hearing" (Rom 10:17) rendered "por el oír", close to the RV1909's "por el oir"

### 3589 Rom 11:1-10 The rejection of the Jews is not universal.
- quoted (Rom 11:5): “according to the election of grace” → “por la elección de gracia”
- translated, not quoted (2Cor 8:1; a common phrase in Henry's own sentence, not a quotation of 2 Cor 8:1): “of the grace of God bestowed on”
- flag: sensitive: "The Jewish nation were as in a deep sleep"; the judgments of God on the Jews for the sufferings of Christ

### 3590 Rom 11:11-21 God overruled their unbelief for making the Gentiles partakers of gospel privileges.
- flag: "worketh by love" (Gal 5:6) rendered with the RV1909's "obra por la caridad"
- flag: sensitive: "the righteous rejection of the unbelieving Jews"; the Jews "cut off" as a nation

### 3591 Rom 11:22-32 The Gentiles cautioned against pride and unbelief, The Jews shall be called as a nation, and brought into God's visible covenant again.
- flag: source title has a comma before "The Jews shall be called"; supplied a full stop
- flag: "to believe in Christ, the true become one sheep-fold" is garbled (words probably lost, e.g. "the true Messiah, and become"); translated as it stands
- flag: sensitive: the Jews "become enemies to God" and "their hatred to the Gentiles"

### 3592 Rom 11:33-36 A solemn adoring of the wisdom, goodness, and justice of God.
- quoted (Luke 8:10): “the mysteries of the kingdom of God” → “los misterios del reino de Dios”
- flag: "to Him, and for Him" rendered "para él, y por causa de él" to keep the two phrases distinct

### 3593 Rom 12:1-2 Believers are to dedicate themselves to God.
- flag: Rom 12:1 phrases ("por las misericordias de Dios", "sacrificio vivo", "agradable á Dios") follow the RV1909; Henry's play on "a reasonable service, which we are able ... to give a reason for" kept with "culto racional ... dar razón" (RV1909 "vuestro racional culto")
