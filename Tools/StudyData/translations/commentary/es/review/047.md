# To review: es commentary batch 047

### 1938 Prov 16:7-7 
- translated, not quoted (Prov 16:7; Henry recasts the verse as 'can make … to be at peace'; the RV1909's future 'pacificará' doesn't fit his construction.): “enemies to be at peace with him”

### 1946 Prov 16:17-17 
- flag: 'every appearance of evil' echoes 1 Ts 5:22 (KJV); the RV1909 has 'toda especie de mal', so Henry's wording is translated.

### 1954 Prov 16:26-26 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jn 6:27 (KJV 'the meat which endureth to everlasting life'); RV1909 words used: 'la comida que á vida eterna permanece'.

### 1986 Prov 17:27-28 
- flag: The last sentence ('God knows his heart, and the folly that is bound there') seems to belong to a different verse (cf. Pr 22:15); translated as written.

### 2000 Prov 18:16-16 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 55:1 ('without money and without price'); RV1909 words used: 'sin dinero y sin precio'.

### 2008 Prov 18:24-24 
- quoted (John 13:1, John 13:2): “Having loved his own, which were in the world, he loved them unto the end” → “como había amado á los suyos que estaban en el mundo, amólos hasta el fin”

### 2019 Prov 19:11-11 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ro 12:21 ('overcome evil with good'); RV1909 words used: 'con el bien el mal' (its 'vence' made an infinitive).

### 2020 Prov 19:12-12 
- quoted (Prov 19:12): “as the roaring of a lion” → “como el bramido del cachorro de león”
- flag: The RV1909 has 'cachorro de león' (young lion) where the KJV has 'lion'; its words are kept.

### 2025 Prov 19:17-17 
- flag: Echoes Stg 2:5 in Henry's own syntax; translated, close to the RV1909 ('los pobres de este mundo, ricos en fe, y herederos').

### 2027 Prov 19:19-19 
- quoted (Prov 19:19): “a man of great wrath” → “de grande ira”

### 2047 Prov 20:10-10 
- quoted (1Tim 6:10): “the love of money is the root” → “el amor del dinero es la raíz”

### 2080 Prov 21:13-13 
- quoted (Prov 21:13): “ears at the cry of the poor” → “su oído al clamor del pobre”

### 2085 Prov 21:18-18 
- quoted (Prov 11:8): “delivered out of trouble, and the wicked” → “El justo es librado de la tribulación … mas el impío viene en lugar suyo”
- flag: Henry quotes Pr 11:8 here (listed), not Pr 21:18 which the section is on; RV1909 Pr 11:8 used in two runs around 'muchas veces'.

### 2096 Prov 21:30-31 
- quoted (Eph 6:10): “in the Lord, and in the power of his might” → “en el Señor, y en la potencia de su fortaleza”
- flag: unlisted echo of Ef 6:11 ('the whole armour of God'); RV1909 words used: 'toda la armadura de Dios'.

### 2107 Prov 22:11-11 
- quoted (Ps 32:2): “in whose spirit there is no guile” → “en cuyo espíritu no hay superchería”

### 2117 Prov 22:28-29 
- quoted (Prov 22:29): “Seest thou a man diligent” → “Has visto hombre solícito”

### 2118 Prov 23:1-3 
- flag: 'Do thyself no harm' is Hch 16:28 (KJV); RV1909 words used: 'No te hagas ningún mal' (unlisted quotation).

### 2125 Prov 23:19-28 
- quoted (Prov 23:23): “Buy the truth, and sell it not” → “Compra la verdad, y no la vendas”

### 2126 Prov 23:29-35 
- quoted (Prov 23:34): “upon the top of a mast” → “en la punta de un mastelero”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Pr 23:35 ('I will seek it again'); RV1909 words used: 'aun lo tornaré á buscar'.

### 2132 Prov 24:13-14 
- flag: 'wise unto salvation' echoes 2 Ti 3:15 (RV1909 'sabio para la salud'); translated in Henry's plural with 'salvación'.

### 2136 Prov 24:21-22 
- quoted (Prov 24:21): “with them that are given” → “no te entrometas con los veleidosos”
- flag: Source 'them that are given change' drops the KJV's 'to' ('given to change'); read as the KJV and rendered with the RV1909 'no te entrometas con los veleidosos', which also covers Henry's 'have nothing to do with'.

### 2152 Prov 25:19-19 
- quoted (Prov 25:19): “Confidence in an unfaithful man” → “la confianza en el prevaricador”

### 2157 Prov 25:25-25 
- flag: Echoes Ro 8:16 in Henry's own syntax; translated, close to the RV1909 ('da testimonio á nuestro espíritu que somos hijos de Dios'). Henry explains 'gospel' as 'glad tidings'; 'evangelio' = 'buenas nuevas' holds in Spanish too.

### 2160 Prov 25:28-28 
- flag: Echoes Ez 36:27 in the third person; translated (RV1909 has 'mis mandamientos' for 'my statutes').

### 2163 Prov 26:3-3 
- flag: 'born like the wild ass's colt' is Job 11:12; RV1909 words used: 'como el pollino del asno montés' (unlisted quotation).

### 2180 Prov 27:1-1 
- quoted (Prov 27:1): “not what a day may bring forth” → “qué dará de sí el día”
- flag: 'that one thing needful' echoes Lc 10:42 (RV1909 'una cosa es necesaria'); translated in Henry's phrasing.

### 2184 Prov 27:7-7 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mt 5:6; RV1909 words used: 'tienen hambre y sed de justicia'.

### 2186 Prov 27:9-10 
- quoted (Prov 18:24): “there is a Friend that sticketh closer than a brother” → “amigo hay más conjunto que el hermano”

### 2191 Prov 27:15-16 
- flag: sensitive: Henry contrasts a neighbour's quarrels with a wife's ('like constant rain'), following Pr 27:15; translated as written.

### 2194 Prov 27:19-19 
- flag: Echoes 1 Co 15:49; RV1909 words used: 'imagen del terreno', 'imagen del celestial' (unlisted).
