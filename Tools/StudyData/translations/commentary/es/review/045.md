# To review: es commentary batch 045

### 1703 Ps 136:23-26 For his blessings to all.

- quoted (Ps 136:20): “his mercy endureth for ever” → “porque para siempre es su misericordia”

### 1704 Ps 137:1-4 The Jews bewail their captivity.
- flag: The English reads 'Scoffers are not to be compiled with', a slip for 'complied with'; translated as 'complied' ('No se ha de complacer a los burladores').

### 1705 Ps 137:5-9 Their affection for Jerusalem.
- flag: 'Vengeance is mine' is an unlisted quotation of Ro 12:19 (He 10:30); rendered with the RV1909's 'Mía es la venganza'.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1706 Ps 138:1-5 The psalmist praises God for answering prayer.
- flag: 'If he spared not his own Son, how shall he not with him freely give us all things?' is an unlisted quotation of Ro 8:32; rendered with the RV1909's words ('Si aun á su propio Hijo no perdonó, ¿cómo no nos dará también con él todas las cosas?'), which has no word for 'freely'.

### 1707 Ps 138:6-8 The Lord's dealing with the humble and the proud.
- flag: 'it is God works in us both to will and to do' is an unlisted quotation of Fil 2:13; rendered with the RV1909's words, 'vosotros' changed to Henry's 'us' ('es Dios el que en nosotros obra así el querer como el hacer').
- flag: 'created anew in Christ Jesus unto good works' echoes Ef 2:10 (RV1909 'criados en Cristo Jesús para buenas obras'); translated 'creado de nuevo en Cristo Jesús para buenas obras'.

### 1710 Ps 139:17-24 The psalmist's hatred to sin, and desire to be led aright.
- translated, not quoted (Prov 23:17; A common phrase in Henry's own sentence ('keep us in the fear of the Lord all the day long'), not a quotation of Pr 23:17; translated.): “in the fear of the Lord all the day long”
- quoted (Ps 139:24): “if there be any wicked way in me” → “Si hay en mí camino de perversidad”
- flag: The English starts a sentence with a lower-case 'if there be any wicked way in me'; capitalised.

### 1711 Ps 140:1-7 David encourages himself in God.
- flag: 'If he be for us, who can be against us?' adapts Ro 8:31 (RV1909 'Si Dios por nosotros, ¿quién contra nosotros?'); rendered with the RV1909's words and Henry's 'he' ('Si él por nosotros').

### 1712 Ps 140:8-13 He prays for, and prophesies the destruction of, his persecutors.
- quoted (Ps 140:8): “the desires of the wicked” → “al impío sus deseos”
- flag: Henry's wordplay 'thanksgiving, even thanks-living' rendered 'acción de gracias, una vida de gracias'; the play on words is only partly kept.

### 1713 Ps 141:1-4 David prays for God's acceptance and assistance.
- flag: 'Make haste unto me' (Sal 141:1) rendered with the RV1909's 'apresúrate á mí', modern 'a' ('Apresúrate a mí'); not listed as a quotation.
- flag: 'dainties' (Sal 141:4, KJV) is 'deleites' in the RV1909; translated 'manjares'.

### 1714 Ps 141:5-10 That God would appear for his rescue.
- quoted (Ps 141:5): “shall not break my head” → “No me herirá la cabeza”
- flag: Sal 141:5: Henry plays on 'break my head … break my heart'; the RV1909 has 'no me herirá la cabeza', so the play on 'break' is lost ('herirá … quebrantar').

### 1715 Ps 142:1-7 David's comfort in prayer.
- flag: 'him who careth for us' echoes 1 P 5:7 (RV1909 'él tiene cuidado de vosotros'); translated 'aquel que tiene cuidado de nosotros'.
- flag: 'the Lord knoweth our path' echoes Sal 142:3 (RV1909 'tú conociste mi senda'); translated 'el Señor conoce nuestra senda'.

### 1718 Ps 144:1-8 David acknowledges the great goodness of God, and prays for help.
- flag: 'as a shadow that passes away' echoes Sal 144:4 (RV1909 'sus días son como la sombra que pasa'); rendered 'como la sombra que pasa'.

### 1720 Ps 145:1-9 David extols the power, goodness, and mercy of the Lord.

### 1721 Ps 145:10-21 The glory of God's kingdom, and his care of those that love him.

### 1722 Ps 146:1-4 Why we should not trust in men.

- quoted (Ps 146:4): “to his earth, in that very day” → “su tierra … en aquel día”
- flag: 'who, being in honour, did not abide' echoes Sal 49:12 (RV1909 'el hombre no permanecerá en honra'); translated.

### 1723 Ps 146:5-10 Why we should trust in God.
