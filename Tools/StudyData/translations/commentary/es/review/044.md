# To review: es commentary batch 044

### 1667 Ps 119:113-120 
- quoted (Heb 4:1): “fear, lest a promise being left us of entering into” → “quedando aún la promesa de entrar”
- quoted (Heb 4:1): “to come short of it” → “haberse apartado”
- flag: The English runs 'See what comes of sin Surely' with no stop between the sentences; a full stop is added in the translation ('Ved lo que resulta del pecado. Ciertamente').
- flag: He 4:1: Henry's 'come short of it' answered with the RV1909's 'haberse apartado' ('apartado de ella').

### 1668 Ps 119:121-128 
- flag: The English reads 'favour form God' (a typo for 'from'); translated as 'favor de Dios'.

### 1669 Ps 119:129-136 
- quoted (Ps 119:132): “Look thou upon me, and be merciful unto me as thou usest to do unto those that love thy name” → “Mírame, y ten misericordia de mí, como acostumbras con los que aman tu nombre”

### 1670 Ps 119:137-144 
- quoted (John 17:3): “This is life eternal” → “es la vida eterna”
- quoted (John 17:3): “God and Jesus Christ whom” → “Jesucristo, al cual”
- flag: 'they are in heaviness through manifold temptations' is an unlisted quotation of 1 P 1:6; rendered with the RV1909's words 'afligidos en diversas tentaciones'.
- flag: Jn 17:3: Henry says 'whom he has sent' (third person); the RV1909 has 'has enviado', so only 'Jesucristo, al cual' is quoted and 'él ha enviado' is translated.

### 1674 Ps 119:169-176 
- quoted (Ps 119:175): “Let thy judgments help me” → “tus juicios me ayuden”
- flag: 'Turn me, and I shall be turned' is an unlisted quotation of Jer 31:18 (cf. Lm 5:21); rendered with the RV1909's words 'conviérteme y seré convertido'.
- flag: 'Seek me, that is, Find me' explains the KJV's 'seek thy servant' (Sal 119:176); the RV1909 has 'busca á tu siervo'; translated 'Búscame, es decir, hállame'.

### 1678 Ps 122:1-5 Esteem for Jerusalem.

### 1680 Ps 123:1-4 Confidence in God under contempt.
- quoted (Luke 12:42): “their portion of meat in due season” → “á tiempo les dé su ración”

### 1682 Ps 124:6-8 Thankfulness for the deliverance.
- quoted (Ps 124:8): “who made heaven and earth” → “que hizo el cielo y la tierra”

### 1683 Ps 125:1-3 The security of the righteous.
- quoted (Ps 125:1): “shall be as Mount Zion” → “como el monte de Sión”
- flag: 'their families names' (sic, for 'families' names') read as 'los nombres de sus familias'.
- flag: 'shall not rest thereon' echoes Sal 125:3 (RV1909 'no reposará la vara de la impiedad sobre la suerte de los justos'), translated 'no reposará sobre ella'.

### 1684 Ps 125:4-5 Prayer for them, The ruin of the wicked.
- flag: The English title has a comma, 'Prayer for them, The ruin of the wicked.', which reads as two headings; given as two sentences.

### 1686 Ps 126:4-6 Those yet in captivity are encouraged.
- quoted (Gal 6:8, Gal 6:9): “to the Spirit, shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting” → “para el Espíritu, del Espíritu … vida eterna”
- quoted (Gal 6:7): “a man soweth that shall he” → “Dios no puede ser burlado … todo lo que el hombre sembrare, eso también segará”
- flag: Gá 6:8: Henry's 'they that sow … shall … reap' is plural; the RV1909's 'segará' is singular, so 'segarán' is Henry's and only 'para el Espíritu, del Espíritu' and 'vida eterna' are quoted.
- flag: 'Blessed are those that mourn, for they shall be for ever comforted' adapts Mt 5:4 (RV1909 'Bienaventurados los que lloran: porque ellos recibirán consolación'); Henry adds 'for ever', so the first half follows the RV1909 and the rest is translated.
- flag: 'sow in tears … reap in joy' echoes Sal 126:5 (RV1909 'Los que sembraron con lágrimas, con regocijo segarán'); translated 'siembran con lágrimas' / 'segar con gozo'.

### 1687 Ps 127:1-5 The value of the Divine blessing.
- quoted (Ps 127:1): “Except the Lord keep the city” → “si Jehová no guardare la ciudad”

### 1688 Ps 128:1-6 The blessings of those who fear God.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 128:2; Henry builds a sequence on it ('all shall be well with thee while thou livest, better when thou diest, best of all'); the RV1909's 'y tendrás bien' cannot carry it, so it is translated 'todo te irá bien'.): “shall be well with thee”

### 1689 Ps 129:1-4 Thankfulness for former deliverances.
- quoted (Dan 7:25): “wear out the saints of the Most High” → “los santos del Altísimo”
- flag: Dn 7:25: Henry's 'wear out' is the KJV's word; the RV1909 has 'quebrantará', so 'quebrantar' is used and only 'los santos del Altísimo' is quoted.

### 1691 Ps 130:1-4 The psalmist's hope in prayer.
- quoted (Ps 130:4): “There is forgiveness with thee” → “hay perdón cerca de ti … para que seas temido”
- flag: Henry's 'not that thou mayest be presumed upon' rendered 'no para que se abuse de él [del perdón] con presunción' to keep the sentence natural; a more literal option is 'no para que se presuma de ti'.

### 1692 Ps 130:5-8 His patience in hope.
- flag: 'plenteous redemption' (Sal 130:7) given as 'redención abundante', the RV1909's 'abundante redención'.

### 1693 Ps 131:1-3 The psalmist's humility. Believers encouraged to trust in God.
- quoted (Matt 18:3): “become as little children” → “como niños”
- quoted (Lam 3:26): “wait for the salvation of the Lord” → “esperar callando en la salud de Jehová”
- flag: Lm 3:26: the RV1909's 'callando' takes up Henry's 'quietly', so his 'quietly to wait for the salvation of the Lord' is the verse's 'esperar callando en la salud de Jehová' ('salud' = salvation in the RV1909).

### 1694 Ps 132:1-10 David's care for the ark.
- quoted (Ps 132:5): “a place for the Lord” → “lugar para Jehová”
- quoted (Prov 27:1): “not what a day may bring forth” → “qué dará de sí el día”

### 1696 Ps 133:1-3 The excellency of brotherly love.
- flag: Henry's 'the Lord commands the blessing; man can but beg a blessing' rests on the KJV of Sal 133:3 ('commanded the blessing'); the RV1909 has 'envía Jehová bendición'. Translated 'manda la bendición' to keep his contrast.

### 1700 Ps 135:15-21 The vanity of idols.
- flag: sensitive: 1700 speaks of 'the heathen', 'the Gentile nations that worship idols' and 'benighted heathens' ('paganos sumidos en tinieblas'); translated in full.

### 1702 Ps 136:10-22 As Israel's God and Saviour.
- quoted (Ps 136:7): “his mercy endureth for ever” → “para siempre es su misericordia”
- quoted (Jude 1:21): “the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “la misericordia de nuestro Señor Jesucristo”
