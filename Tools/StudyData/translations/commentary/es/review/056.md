# To review: es commentary batch 056

### 2543 Jer 12:7-13 
- flag: 'speckled bird' (KJV Jer 12:9): the RV1909 reads 'ave de muchos colores'; translated so.

### 2544 Jer 12:14-17 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ro 11:25: RV1909 'haya entrado la plenitud de los Gentiles' used verbatim (capital G as in the RV1909).

### 2545 Jer 13:1-11 
- flag: Outline: the source runs two headings together with a comma ('All ranks should suffer misery, An earnest exhortation'); rendered as two sentences.

### 2546 Jer 13:12-17 
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Ro 9:22: RV1909 'vasos de ira preparados' used verbatim.
- flag: 'bottle' (KJV Jer 13:12): the RV1909 says 'vaso'/'odre'; rendered 'odre'.

### 2547 Jer 13:18-27 
- translated, not quoted (Jer 13:22; Henry turns the RV1909's '¿Por qué me ha sobrevenido esto?' into the plural ('upon us').): “Wherefore come these things upon”
- flag: Unlisted quotations: Lc 18:27 ('lo que es imposible para con los hombres, posible es para Dios') and Is 63:1 ('grande para salvar'), RV1909 verbatim.
- flag: Source: 'whether she is determined not be made clean' lacks 'to'; translated as meant.
- flag: Henry calls sin 'the blackness of the soul' from Jer 13:23 (the Ethiopian's skin); a reviewer may wish to look at the skin-colour imagery.

### 2548 Jer 14:1-9 
- flag: 'answer against us' (KJV Jer 14:7 'testify against us'): rendered with the RV1909's verb 'testifican contra nosotros'.

### 2549 Jer 14:10-16 
- flag: Source typo: 'must not plead if for an excuse' read as 'plead it'.

### 2551 Jer 15:1-9 
- flag: sensitive: Henry draws from Jer 15:1 that 'saints in heaven do not pray for saints on earth' (implicitly against the intercession of saints); translated in full.

### 2554 Jer 16:1-9 
- flag: Source: 'that they believe it themselves' (it = the truths); rendered 'las creen'.

### 2556 Jer 16:14-21 
- flag: Henry's own 'Jehovah' rendered 'Jehová'.
- flag: sensitive: 'the future deliverance of the church from antichristian oppression' (Henry's era commonly meant Rome by 'antichristian'); translated as written.

### 2558 Jer 17:5-11 
- quoted (Jer 17:6): “shall be like the heath” → “será como la retama”
- quoted (Jer 17:9): “is deceitful above all things” → “Engañoso es el corazón más que todas las cosas”
- quoted (Jer 17:11): “riches, and not by right” → “allega riquezas, y no con justicia”
- flag: 'heath' (KJV Jer 17:6): the RV1909 has 'retama'; used so.
- flag: Unlisted quotations, RV1909 verbatim: Jer 17:5 'pone carne por su brazo' (as 'ponen carne por su brazo', person adapted) and Sal 19:12 'Los errores, ¿quién los entenderá?'.
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's sentence reordered so the RV1909 run stands whole before his gloss ('the heart, the conscience of man').

### 2560 Jer 17:19-27 
- flag: Henry applies Jer 17's sabbath to his readers' own day without saying 'Christian sabbath'; rendered 'sábado' throughout, no bracketed note.
- flag: Source punctuation slip 'Let all; by their own example' read as a comma.

### 2562 Jer 18:11-17 
- translated, not quoted (Jer 18:15; RV1909 Jer 18:15 'para que caminen' (purpose clause with the people as object of 'hácenles') can't stand in Henry's sentence ('wander from the ancient paths, to walk in ways of their own devising'); translated.): “from the ancient paths, to walk”

### 2565 Jer 19:10-15 
- flag: 'bottle' (KJV Jer 19:1, 10): the RV1909 has 'vasija'/'vaso'; rendered 'vasija'.

### 2566 Jer 20:1-6 
- flag: 'Fear on every side' (Jer 20:3, KJV margin's meaning of Magor-missabib): the RV1909 keeps the Hebrew name 'Magor-missabib'; Henry's English meaning translated.
- flag: Glossary has no Spanish for Pashur; RV1909 spells 'Pashur', rendered 'Pasur' (modernized) - reviewer may prefer 'Pashur'.

### 2567 Jer 20:7-13 
- flag: Jer 20:7: Henry offers his own alternative reading ('Thou hast persuaded me, and I was persuaded') against the KJV's 'deceived'; translated, since the RV1909 reads 'Alucinásteme... y hállome frustrado'. 'Más fuerte fuiste que yo' is the RV1909 verbatim.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ro 12:19: RV1909 'yo pagaré' used verbatim.

### 2568 Jer 20:14-18 
- flag: Unlisted quotations, RV1909 verbatim: Mt 6:13 'no nos metas en tentación'; He 12:3 'que sufrió tal contradicción de pecadores contra sí mismo' (RV1909 'tal' for Henry's 'the'). 1 Co 10:12 echoed with its 'piensa estar firme... no caiga' wording, adapted to the plural.

### 2569 Jer 21:1-10 
- flag: Glossary offers 'Sedequías' / 'Sedecías' for Zedekiah; 'Sedequías' used throughout.

### 2570 Jer 21:11-14 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ro 8:31: RV1909 'Si Dios por nosotros, ¿quién contra nosotros?' used verbatim.

### 2571 Jer 22:1-9 
- translated, not quoted (Jer 21:11; A common phrase in Henry's own sentence, not a quotation.): “The king of Judah”
- translated, not quoted (Jer 22:4; The RV1909 says it differently ('los reyes que en lugar de David se sientan sobre su trono'); Henry's phrase translated.): “sitting upon the throne of David”
- flag: Outline: Henry's 'captivity of Jehoiakim' over Jer 22:10-19 (a reviewer may note the section itself speaks of Shallum and Jehoiakim's death); translated as written.

### 2572 Jer 22:10-19 
- flag: Glossary offers 'Sallum' / 'Sellum' for Shallum; 'Salum' used (modernized). Reviewer may prefer the RV1909 'Sallum'.

### 2573 Jer 22:20-30 
- flag: Source typo 'thought it may be in a far country' read as 'though'.
- flag: 'signets' (KJV Jer 22:24): the RV1909 has 'anillo'; rendered 'anillos de sello'.

### 2574 Jer 23:1-8 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jer 23:6 in Henry's quotation marks (twice): RV1909 words used ('Jehová, justicia nuestra'; printed in capitals there).
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ro 8:34: RV1909 'Cristo es el que murió; más aún, el que también resucitó' used verbatim.
- flag: Source: 'the supply of this Spirit' (probably 'his Spirit'); translated literally ('este Espíritu').
- flag: Outline: the source runs two headings together with a comma ('of Judah, The people exhorted'); rendered as two sentences.

### 2575 Jer 23:9-22 
- flag: 'strong delusions' echoes 2 Ts 2:11 (RV1909 'operación de error'); Henry's wording translated.

### 2577 Jer 23:33-40 
- flag: Jer 23:33-40 plays on 'burden' (KJV); the RV1909 has 'carga'; Henry's 'the sinner's burden' rendered 'la carga del pecador' to keep the echo.

### 2578 Jer 24:1-10 
- flag: Source grammar 'The good figs represents' read as plural.
- flag: sensitive: 'the dispersion of the unbelieving Jews, in all the nations of the earth' (Jews); translated as written.

### 2579 Jer 25:1-7 
- flag: 'Rising early' (KJV Jer 25:3-4): the RV1909 has 'madrugando'; rendered 'El madrugar'.

### 2581 Jer 25:15-29 
- quoted (Jer 25:28): “refuse to take the cup at” → “no quieren tomar el vaso de tu mano”
- translated, not quoted (Isa 39:5; A common phrase in Henry's own sentence (and listed against Is 39:5); 'Lord' in his voice is 'el Señor'.): “the word of the Lord of hosts”
- flag: Quote 0: RV1909's present 'no quieren' kept for Henry's 'will refuse'; its 'vaso' for KJV 'cup' (the section otherwise says 'copa').
- flag: Source grammar 'who had just began to reign' read as 'begun'.
