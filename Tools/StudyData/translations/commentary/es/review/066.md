# To review: es commentary batch 066

### 2957 Zech 13:7-9 The death of Christ, and the saving of a remnant of the people.
- quoted (Zech 13:7): “Smite the Shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered” → “Hiere al pastor, y se derramarán las ovejas”
- quoted (1Pet 1:7): “praise, and honour, and glory, at the appearing” → “alabanza, gloria y honra”
- flag: 'my Fellow' (Zac 13:7): RV1909 'el hombre compañero mío'; Henry's quoted title given as "mi Compañero".
- flag: 1 P 1:7: the RV1909's order 'alabanza, gloria y honra' quoted (KJV 'praise and honour and glory'); 'at the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ' translated as Henry's own words.
- flag: sensitive: the Jews' sin in rejecting and crucifying Christ, and their destruction by the Romans.

### 2958 Zech 14:1-7 The sufferings of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Zech 14:4): “upon the Mount of Olives” → “sobre el monte de las Olivas”
- flag: Zac 14:4 quoted in the RV1909's words, 'el monte de las Olivas', although the glossary gives 'Monte de los Olivos'.

### intro Mal 
- flag: 'rememberance' is misspelt in the English; translated normally ('guarde en la memoria').
- flag: 'B.C. 420' given as 'hacia el 420 a.C.' ('is supposed to have prophesied').

### 2962 Mal 1:6-14 They are careless in God's institutions.
- quoted (Mal 1:7): “say, The table of the Lord is contemptible” → “La mesa de Jehová es despreciable”
- quoted (John 4:23): “the Father in Spirit and in truth” → “al Padre en espíritu y en verdad”
- flag: 'incense … a pure offering' (Mal 1:11): RV1909 'perfume, y presente limpio'; Henry's 'incense' and 'pure offering' translated ('incienso', 'ofrenda pura').
- flag: 'the torn' (Mal 1:13, KJV 'torn'): translated 'lo desgarrado'; RV1909 has 'hurtado'.

### 2963 Mal 2:1-9 The priests reproved for neglecting their covenant.
- flag: 'The priest's lips should … keep knowledge' (Mal 2:7): RV1909 'han de guardar la sabiduría'; Henry's 'knowledge' translated 'conocimiento' (his play on 'keep from' / 'keep for').

### 2964 Mal 2:10-17 The people reproved for their evil practices.
- quoted (Mal 2:16): “The God of Israel saith that he hateth putting away” → “Jehová Dios de Israel ha dicho que él aborrece que sea repudiada”
- quoted (2Pet 3:10): “the day of the Lord will come” → “el día del Señor vendrá”
- flag: Mal 2:16: the RV1909's 'que él aborrece que sea repudiada' (that she be put away) quoted for Henry's 'he hateth putting away'.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mal 2:17: '¿Dónde está el Dios de juicio?' in the RV1909's words.
- flag: 'Did not God make one' (Mal 2:15) translated from Henry's wording; RV1909 '¿no hizo él uno solo…?'.

### 2965 Mal 3:1-6 The coming of Christ.
- quoted (Mal 3:1): “He shall prepare the way before” → “preparará el camino delante de”
- quoted (Mal 3:1): “the Messenger of the covenant” → “el ángel del pacto”
- translated, not quoted (Mal 3:3; The RV1909 (Mal 3:3) says 'sentarse ha para afinar y limpiar la plata', with no noun 'Refiner'; Henry's title is translated.): “He shall sit as a Refiner”
- quoted (Lam 3:22): “we are not consumed; because his compassions fail not” → “no somos consumidos, porque nunca decayeron sus misericordias”
- flag: 'the Messenger of the covenant' (Mal 3:1): RV1909 'el ángel del pacto' quoted; Henry's next 'Messenger of peace' translated 'Mensajero de paz'.
- flag: Henry's quoted "He that should come" given in the RV1909's words of Mt 11:3, 'Aquel que había de venir' (unlisted).
- flag: 'to separate between the precious and the vile' (Jer 15:19) translated; RV1909 'sacares lo precioso de lo vil'.

### 2966 Mal 3:7-12 The Jews reproved for their corruptions.
- quoted (Mal 3:7): “said, Wherein shall we return” → “En qué hemos de tornar”

### 2967 Mal 3:13-18 God's care of his people; The distinction between the righteous and the wicked.
- quoted (Mal 3:16): “A book of remembrance was written before” → “fué escrito libro de memoria delante de”
- flag: 'God's jewels' (Mal 3:17, KJV 'my jewels'): RV1909 'especial tesoro'; Henry's 'jewels' translated 'joyas'.
- flag: Title joins two headings with a semicolon in the English; kept.

### 2968 Mal 4:1-3 The judgements on the wicked, and the happiness of the righteous.
- flag: 'the Sun of Righteousness' (Mal 4:2): matches the RV1909's 'el Sol de justicia'.
- flag: 'health' (Mal 4:2, RV1909 'salud') kept as 'salud' for a sick world, Henry's meaning.
- flag: 'calves of the stall' (Mal 4:2): RV1909 'becerros de la manada'; Henry's contrast with stall-fed calves kept ('del establo').

### 2969 Mal 4:4-6 Regard to be had to the law; John the Baptist promised as the forerunner of Messiah.
- quoted (Mal 4:5): “the great and dreadful day of the Lord” → “el día de Jehová grande y terrible”
- quoted (Rom 16:20): “The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “La gracia del Señor nuestro Jesucristo sea con”
- flag: 'call it enlightening': Henry's dig at the 'enlightenment' of proud reasoning given as 'ilustración'.
- flag: 'smitten … with a curse' (Mal 4:6): RV1909 'con destrucción hiera la tierra'; translated from Henry.

### 2971 Matt 1:18-25 An angel appears to Joseph.
- quoted (Matt 1:24): “did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him” → “hizo como el ángel del Señor le había mandado”

### 2972 Matt 2:1-8 The wise men's search after Christ.
- quoted (2Pet 1:19): “a light that shineth in a dark place” → “una antorcha que alumbra en lugar oscuro”
- flag: 2 P 1:19: RV1909 'antorcha' for KJV 'light'; 'the day-star' given in the RV1909's own words for it, 'el lucero de la mañana'.

### 2975 Matt 2:16-18 Herod causes the infants of Bethlehem to be massacred.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Pr 19:21: 'el consejo de Jehová permanecerá' in the RV1909's words.

### 2976 Matt 2:19-23 Death of Herod, Jesus brought to Nazareth.

### 2977 Matt 3:1-6 John the Baptist, His preaching, manner of life, and baptism.
- quoted (Matt 3:1): “in the wilderness of Judea” → “en el desierto de Judea”
- flag: Henry's quoted "Repent ye" given in the RV1909's word of Mt 3:2, "Arrepentíos" (unlisted).
- flag: 'The word here used implies a total alteration in the mind': Henry on the Greek (metanoia), not the English; holds for 'Arrepentíos' too.
- flag: 'fountain' (Zac 13:1) given as 'manantial', as in the RV1909 and batch 065.

### 2978 Matt 3:7-12 John reproves the Pharisees and Sadducees.
- quoted (Matt 3:10): “is hewn down and cast into the fire” → “es cortado y echado en el fuego”
- translated, not quoted (Acts 2:4; Not a quotation of Hch 2:4 (RV1909 'llenos del Espíritu Santo'); 'baptizes with the Holy Ghost' is Henry's own phrase, translated 'bautiza con el Espíritu Santo'.): “with the Holy Ghost”
- flag: 'the wrath to come' (Mt 3:7) given in the RV1909's words, 'la ira que vendrá' (unlisted).
- flag: 'the unquenchable fire' (Mt 3:12) given in the RV1909's words, 'el fuego que nunca se apagará' (unlisted).
- flag: 'the fruits meet for repentance' (Mt 3:8) translated 'los frutos dignos de arrepentimiento'.

### 2979 Matt 3:13-17 The baptism of Jesus.
- quoted (Gal 5:22): “The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace” → “El fruto del Espíritu es: caridad, gozo, paz, tolerancia, benignidad, bondad, fe”
- flag: Gá 5:22-23: Henry's list runs into v. 23; 'mansedumbre, templanza' continue in the RV1909's words (unlisted). RV1909 'caridad' for 'love'.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of He 12:29: 'fuego consumidor' in the RV1909's words.
- flag: 'to fulfil all righteousness' (Mt 3:15) matches the RV1909's 'cumplir toda justicia'.
