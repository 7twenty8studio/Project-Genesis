# To review: pt commentary batch 098

### 3938 1Pet 4:1-6 The consideration of Christ's sufferings is urged for purity and holiness.
- flag: "conversation" (KJV, conduct) rendered "conduta".

### 3939 1Pet 4:7-11 And the approaching end of the Jewish state, as a reason for sobriety, watchfulness, and prayer.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Pe 1:23 / 1 Jo 2:17: "que permanece para sempre" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3940 1Pet 4:12-19 Believers encouraged to rejoice and glory in reproaches and sufferings for Christ, and to commit their souls to the care of a faithful God.
- flag: KJV "on their part he is evil spoken of" (1 Pe 4:14) is "por eles é insultado" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's "evil spoken of" rendered "insultado". The echo of 1 Pe 4:18 ("se salva com dificuldade") is fitted to Henry's plural.

### 3941 1Pet 5:1-4 Elders exhorted and encouraged.
- quoted (1Pet 5:1): “of the glory that shall be revealed” → “da glória que se revelará”
- flag: sensitive: 3941: "He does not claim power to rule over all pastors and churches" reads as Henry's implicit answer to the papacy's claim from Peter; translated as written.
- flag: Henry's "God's heritage or clergy" plays on KJV "lords over God's heritage" (1 Pe 5:3, Greek kleros); the Bíblia Livre has "os que estão aos vossos cuidados". "chief Shepherd" is the Bíblia Livre's "Pastor Principal" (5:4); its "indestrutível coroa da glória" differs from Henry's "crown of unfading glory", translated.

### 3942 1Pet 5:5-9 Younger Christians are to submit to their elders, and to yield with humility and patience to God, and to be sober, watchful, and stedfast in faith.
- flag: Source typo: Henry's quoted "all you care" read as "all your care" (1 Pe 5:7); translated "todo o vosso cuidado" to keep his series of "cares"; the Bíblia Livre has "toda a vossa ansiedade".
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Pe 5:6: "no tempo adequado" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "in due time").

### 3943 1Pet 5:10-14 Prayers for their growth and establishment.
- flag: Henry explains the four KJV verbs of 1 Pe 5:10 ("make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle"); the Bíblia Livre's "aperfeiçoe, confirme, fortifique, e estabeleça fundamento" are used as the words he explains. "o Deus de toda graça" is the Bíblia Livre's phrase.

### 3944 2Pet 1:1-11 Exhortations to add the exercise of various other graces to faith.
- flag: KJV's list in 2 Pe 1:5-7 rendered with the Bíblia Livre's words: "temperance" "domínio próprio", "godliness" "devoção divina" (Henry's first "worketh godliness" kept as "piedade"); "brotherly kindness" appears in Henry only as "tender affection to all fellow Christians", translated.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Rm 5:3 ("A tribulação produz paciência"), 2 Pe 1:4 ("participantes da natureza divina") and Ef 4:23 ("no espírito da").

### 3945 2Pet 1:12-15 The apostle looks forward to his approaching decease.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ef 4:14: "todo vento de doutrina" in the Bíblia Livre's words; "tabernáculo" is its word in 2 Pe 1:13-14.

### 3946 2Pet 1:16-21 And confirms the truth of the gospel, relating to Christ's appearing to judgment.
- flag: Source typo: "the covetous worlding" read as "worldling" ("o mundano"). Henry's "ounce" kept as "onça" (the old weight).
- flag: Echoes of Lc 24:32 and At 26:28 ("Por pouco tu me convences a me tornar cristão" in the Bíblia Livre) are fitted to Henry's indirect speech, translated.

### 3947 2Pet 2:1-9 Believers are cautioned against false teachers, and the certainty of their punishment shown from examples.
- quoted (John 14:6): “the Way, the Truth, and the Life” → “o caminho, e a verdade, e a vida”
- quoted (2Pet 2:9): “the day of judgment, to be punished” → “para o dia do julgamento, para serem punidos”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry capitalizes "the Way, the Truth, and the Life"; the Bíblia Livre's words are kept in its own lower case.
- flag: Source slip: a missing full stop after "if we mark them"; read as two sentences.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: 2 Pe 2:3 ("palavras falsamente inventadas"; KJV "feigned words") and Jo 3:36 ("a ira de Deus continua sobre").

### 3948 2Pet 2:10-16 An account of these seducers, as exceedingly wicked.
- quoted (2Cor 10:5): “every thought to the obedience of Christ” → “todo pensamento para a obediência a Cristo”

### 3949 2Pet 2:17-22 But as making high pretences to liberty and purity.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Jó 38:2 ("o conselho com palavras"), 2 Pe 2:17 ("a escuridão das trevas"; KJV "the mist of darkness") and At 16:31 ("Crê no Senhor Jesus" ... "e serás salvo"; Henry leaves out "Christ" and "thy house").

### 3950 2Pet 3:1-4 The design here is to remind of Christ's final coming to judgement.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 55:19; The Bíblia Livre reads Sl 55:19 as "Porque eles não mudam de comportamento, nem temem a Deus" (they don't change their ways), which loses Henry's point that they see no changes in their circumstances.): “no changes, therefore they fear not God”

### 3951 2Pet 3:5-10 He will appear unexpectedly, when the present frame of nature will be dissolved by fire.
- quoted (2Pet 3:7): “The heavens and the earth” → “Os céus e a terra de agora”
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's run is taken to "de agora" (KJV "which are now"), which Henry also quotes.
- flag: Source typo: "wordly-minded" read as "worldly-minded". KJV "longsuffering to us-ward" (2 Pe 3:9) is "paciente para conosco" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's phrase translated "longanimidade, e isso para conosco".

### 3952 2Pet 3:11-18 From thence is inferred the need for holiness, and stedfastness in the faith.
- quoted (2Pet 3:13): “look for new heavens and a new earth” → “novos céus e nova terra”
- quoted (2Pet 3:16): “things hard to be understood” → “coisas difíceis de entender”
- quoted (2Pet 2:20): “the knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ” → “conhecimento do Senhor e Salvador Jesus Cristo”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 10:23: "aquele que prometeu é fiel" in the Bíblia Livre's words. Source oddity: "therefore we are in great danger of rejecting the truth" reads like "otherwise"; translated as written.

### 3953 1John 1:1-4 The apostle prefaces his epistle to believers in general, with evident testimonies to Christ, for promoting their happiness and joy.
- quoted (1John 1:3): “fellowship is with the Father” → “comunhão é com o Pai”
- flag: "the Word of life" (1 Jo 1:1) is the Bíblia Livre's "Palavra da vida", used here.

### 3954 1John 1:5-10 The necessity of a life of holiness, in order to communion with God, is shown.
- translated, not quoted (Rev 1:5, Rev 1:6; Henry turns Ap 1:5 into "died to wash us from our sins in his own blood"; the Bíblia Livre's "em seu sangue nos lavou de nossos pecados" is past tense in another order, so it can't stand in his sentence.): “us from our sins in his own blood”

### 3955 1John 2:1-2 The apostle directs to the atonement of Christ for help against sinful infirmities.
- quoted (1John 2:1): “have an Advocate with the Father” → “temos um advogado com o Pai”
- flag: Source typo: "When have an Advocate" read as "We have".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 10:20: "caminho novo e vivo" in the Bíblia Livre's words; Henry's quoted "the Righteous One" given as "o Justo" (1 Jo 2:1, "o justo" there).

### 3956 1John 2:3-11 The effects of saving knowledge in producing obedience, and love to the brethren.
- flag: Source punctuation: "Where spiritual darkness dwells, in mind, the judgment, and the conscience will be darkened" read as "dwells in the mind, the judgment and the conscience will be darkened"; translated with the comma as given.

### 3957 1John 2:12-14 Christians addressed as little children, young men, and fathers.
- flag: Source oddity: "But especially young men in Christ Jesus, though they are arrived at strength ..." has no main verb; translated as the same unfinished sentence.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Mt 7:13 ("porta estreita") and 1 Jo 2:13 ("aquele que é desde o princípio"; KJV "the wicked one" is "o maligno" there).

### 3958 1John 2:15-17 All are cautioned against the love of this world, and against errors.
- quoted (1John 2:16): “The lust of the flesh” → “A cobiça da carne”
- quoted (1John 2:16): “The lust of the eyes” → “A cobiça dos olhos”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Jo 2:16: "A ostentação da vida" (KJV "the pride of life") in the Bíblia Livre's words. KJV "lust" there is "cobiça", used throughout this section.

### 3959 1John 2:18-23 
- quoted (1John 2:20): “unction from the Holy One” → “unção do Santo”
- flag: Source typo: "their names expresses this" read as "their name expresses this".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jo 8:44: "pai da mentira" in the Bíblia Livre's words (Henry's "father of lies").

### 3960 1John 2:24-29 They are encouraged to stand fast in faith and holiness.
- flag: Henry's quoted "little children" (1 Jo 2:28) given as the Bíblia Livre's "filhinhos". "holding the truth in unrighteousness" echoes KJV Rm 1:18; the Bíblia Livre has "bloqueiam a verdade pela injustiça", so it is translated.
- flag: Source oddity: "their glory in the gospel" (whose glory is unclear) translated literally as "da glória deles".
