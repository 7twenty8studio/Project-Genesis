# To review: pt commentary batch 079

### 3386 John 13:36-38 
- quoted (Eph 4:3): “to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace” → “guardar a unidade do Espírito pelo vínculo da paz”
- quoted (1Pet 1:22): “love one another with a pure heart fervently” → “intensamente uns aos outros com um coração puro”
- flag: Henry's "to love one another" takes the first person ("amar-nos"); the rest of 1 Pe 1:22's words are quoted verbatim.

### 3387 John 14:1-11 Christ comforts his disciples.
- flag: Henry stresses three words of the KJV's "Let not your heart be troubled" (Jo 14:1); the Bíblia Livre has "Não se perturbe vosso coração", so the words are given as "perturbe", "coração", "vosso".
- flag: "the word *mansions*": the Bíblia Livre's Jo 14:2 has "moradas", used here ("As moradas são habitações duradouras").

### 3388 John 14:12-17 He further comforts his disciples.
- flag: "The word used here" is the Greek parakletos; the Bíblia Livre's Jo 14:16 has "Consolador".

### 3389 John 14:18-24 He still further comforts his disciples.
- quoted (John 14:18): “I will not leave you” → “Não vos deixarei”
- quoted (John 14:18): “I will come to you” → “eu virei a vós”

### 3391 John 14:28-31 
- quoted (John 14:30): “the prince of this world” → “o príncipe deste mundo”

### 3392 John 15:1-8 Christ the true Vine.
- flag: The source text opens with stray outline fragments, "foretold." and "(18-25)" (from Henry's chapter outline: "Christ's discourse... persecution foretold, ver. 18-25"), as separate paragraphs; kept and translated ("predita.") to keep the paragraphs.
- flag: Garbled source: "...that require to be taken away, which Christ has promised to forward the sanctification of believers, they will be thankful, for them" (probably "the pruning which Christ has promised... for which they will be thankful"); translated word for word.

### 3393 John 15:9-17 His love to his disciples.
- flag: Source has "the love of Christ's in our hearts" (stray "'s"); translated as "o amor de Cristo".

### 3397 John 16:7-15 The promise of the Holy Spirit, and his office.
- quoted (John 16:11): “Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged” → “Do juízo, porque o Príncipe deste mundo já está julgado”

### 3400 John 16:28-33 Christ's discoveries of himself.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jo 16:30: "Agora sabemos" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "Now are we sure").

### 3402 John 17:6-10 His prayer for his disciples.
- quoted (2Thess 2:13): “salvation, through sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth” → “para a salvação, em santificação do Espírito, e fé na verdade”
- quoted (John 17:10): “All mine are thine, and thine are mine” → “todas as minhas coisas são tuas; e as tuas coisas são minhas”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jo 17:6: "tu os deste a mim" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "thou gavest them me").

### 3403 John 17:11-16 His prayer.
- translated, not quoted (John 17:13; Not a quotation: a chance match of common words ("the world, that they might") with Jo 17:13.): “the world, that they might”
- translated, not quoted (John 17:15; Henry reports the prayer in the third person ("that the Father would keep them from the evil"); the Bíblia Livre's "que os guardes do maligno" is second person, and "the evil" here is a list of evils, not "o maligno" alone.): “keep them from the evil”

### 3404 John 17:17-19

- flag: KJV "through thy truth" (Jo 17:17); the Bíblia Livre has "em tua verdade", used.

### 3407 John 18:1-12 Christ taken in the garden.
- quoted (John 18:4): “knowing all things that should come upon him, went forth” → “todas as coisas que viriam sobre ele, adiantou-se”

- flag: "Whom seek ye?" (Jo 18:4) given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "A quem buscais?" (same verse as the listed quotation).

### 3408 John 18:13-27 Christ before Annas and Caiaphas.
- flag: "The beginning of sin is as the letting forth of water" adapts Pv 17:14 (where the KJV has "strife"); translated.

### 3410 John 18:33-40 
- quoted (John 18:33): “Art thou the King of the Jews” → “És tu o Rei dos Judeus”
- flag: Source has "it glory" (for "its glory") and "patience to preserve in their search" (for "persevere"); translated as "a sua glória" and "perseverar".
- flag: unlisted quotations of Jo 18:38 ("O que é a verdade?") and Sl 25:5 ("Guia-me em tua verdade") in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3411 John 19:1-18 Christ condemned and crucified.
- flag: sensitive: "the Jews should first purpose his death, and the Gentiles carry that purpose into effect" assigns the purpose of Christ's death to the Jews as a people (alongside the Gentiles); translated in full.

### 3412 John 19:19-30 Christ on the cross.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jo 19:30: "Está consumado" (five times) in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3413 John 19:31-37 His side pierced.
- flag: Source attributes the legs not being broken to "Pilate's not allowing" it (Jo 19:31-33 has the soldiers sparing them); translated as written.

### 3414 John 19:38-42 The burial of Jesus.
- quoted (Isa 53:9): “with the rich in his death” → “com um rico em sua morte”
- flag: Is 53:9: the Bíblia Livre has the singular "com um rico" where the KJV has "with the rich"; quoted as the Bíblia Livre has it.

### 3415 John 20:1-10 The sepulchre found to be empty.
- quoted (John 20:9): “must rise again from the dead” → “ressuscitasse dos mortos”
- flag: Garbled source: "they Christ must rise again from the dead" (probably "that Christ must").

### 3416 John 20:11-18 Christ appears to Mary.
- flag: "It might be read, Is it my Master?" refers to the word Rabboni (Jo 20:16, Bíblia Livre "Rabôni! (que quer dizer Mestre)").
- flag: Garbled source: "He forbids her to expect that his bodily presence look further, than the present state of things" (probably "forbids her to look for his bodily presence further than..."); translated word for word.
- flag: "I ascend" (Jo 20:17) given as the Bíblia Livre's "Subo"; "the things which are above" (Cl 3:1) as "as coisas de cima".
