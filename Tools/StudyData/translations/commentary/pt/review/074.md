# To review: pt commentary batch 074

### 3205 Luke 3:23-38 The genealogy of Christ.
- translated, not quoted (1Pet 1:25; Henry's 'preached unto us' is applied to eternal happiness; the Bíblia Livre's 'foi evangelizada entre vós' (the word, second person) doesn't fit his sentence): “which by the gospel is preached unto”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Luke 3:38 in Henry's quotation marks: the Bíblia Livre reads 'filho de Adão, e Adão de Deus' (KJV 'which was the son of Adam, which was the son of God'); used verbatim, so the words 'son of God' are implied rather than written
- flag: 'Eli, or Heli': Henry gives both spellings; kept as 'Eli, ou Heli'

### 3206 Luke 4:1-13 The temptation of Christ.
- quoted (Matt 4:8): “the kingdoms of the world and the glory of them” → “os reinos do mundo, e a glória deles”
- quoted (Luke 4:5): “the kingdoms of the world” → “os reinos do mundo”
- flag: 'if we resist the devil, he will flee from us' (Jas 4:7) and 'this present evil world' (Gal 1:4) translated in Henry's person; the Bíblia Livre has 'resisti ao diabo, e ele fugirá de vós' and 'da presente era maligna'

### 3207 Luke 4:14-30 Christ in the synagogue of Nazareth.
- quoted (Luke 4:19): “the acceptable year of the Lord” → “o ano agradável do Senhor”
- flag: 'words of grace to such graceless wretches': Henry's wordplay on grace/graceless rendered 'palavras de graça a criaturas tão desgraçadas e sem graça'

### 3208 Luke 4:31-44 He casts out an unclean spirit and heals the sick.
- quoted (Luke 4:42): “The people sought him, and came unto him” → “As multidões o buscavam; então vieram a ele”
- quoted (Col 1:14): “redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins” → “a libertação pelo seu sangue: o perdão dos pecados”
- flag: Q1: the Bíblia Livre's 'libertação' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'redemption' in this quotation

### 3210 Luke 5:12-16 A leper cleansed.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 6:17; the listed verse (Luke 6:17) doesn't hold these words; Henry echoes Luke 5:15, whose Bíblia Livre wording ('para serem por ele curadas', of the multitudes) doesn't fit his sentence): “to hear him and to be healed”
- flag: 'from the crown of the head to the sole of the foot there is no soundness' (Isa 1:6, unlisted): the Bíblia Livre's wording followed (its order, foot to head, where Henry has head to foot) with 'em nós' for 'nele'; not an exact run, so treated as translation

### 3211 Luke 5:17-26 A paralytic cured.
- flag: 'who do not sit under the word, but sit by': Henry's contrast of 'under' and 'by' rendered 'sob a palavra, mas ao lado dela'; 'as a tale that is told' echoes Ps 90:9 in the KJV, which the Bíblia Livre renders differently ('como um suspiro')

### 3213 Luke 6:1-5 The disciples pluck corn on the sabbath.
- flag: 'ears of corn': 'corn' in Henry's English is grain; rendered 'espigas de trigo' (the Bíblia Livre at Luke 6:1 has 'espigas')

### 3217 Luke 6:27-36 Christ exhorts to mercy.
- quoted (Acts 9:6): “Lord, what wilt thou have me to do” → “Senhor, que queres que eu faça?”

### 3218 Luke 6:37-49 And to justice and sincerity.
- flag: Henry joins several unlisted echoes (Ex 23:2, Matt 7:13, 1 Cor 3:11, 1 Pet 1:5, John 10:28) into his own sentences, with Christ for God in 1 Pet 1:5; translated, since the Bíblia Livre's wording differs and Henry adapts them

### 3219 Luke 7:1-10 The centurion's servant healed.
- flag: last sentence has no verb in the English ('The cure soon wrought and perfect.'); translated as 'A cura foi logo operada, e perfeita'

### 3220 Luke 7:11-18 The widow's son raised.
- translated, not quoted (Eph 5:14; Henry turns Eph 5:14 to the plural ('you') and adds 'light and life'; the Bíblia Livre's 'levanta-te dos mortos, e Cristo te iluminará' can't carry his change): “Arise from the dead, and Christ shall give”

### 3221 Luke 7:19-35 John the Baptist's inquiry concerning Jesus.
- quoted (Luke 7:22): “To the poor the gospel is preached” → “aos pobres é anunciado o Evangelho”

### 3223 Luke 8:1-3 The ministry of Christ.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 8:1; the Bíblia Livre's 'anunciando o evangelho do reino de Deus' would lose Henry's point that these tidings are glad tidings): “Tidings of the kingdom of God”
- flag: 'Though rich, yet for our sakes he became poor' echoes 2 Cor 8:9 (unlisted), translated in Henry's words

### 3224 Luke 8:4-21 The parable of the sower.
- flag: 'hold the truth in unrighteousness' (Rom 1:18) and 'hold forth the word of life' (Phil 2:16): Henry's play on 'hold'/'hold forth' rendered 'deter a verdade em injustiça' / 'manter a palavra da vida' (the Bíblia Livre has 'bloqueiam a verdade pela injustiça' and 'mantende a palavra da vida')
- flag: 'doers of the work': so the English (perhaps for 'doers of the word'); translated as written, 'praticantes da obra'

### 3226 Luke 8:41-56 The daughter of Jairus restored to life.
- quoted (Luke 8:55): “commanded to give her meat” → “mandou que dessem de comer a ela”
- flag: 'Fear not, believe only, and thy daughter shall be made whole' (Luke 8:50, unlisted, with Henry's 'thy daughter' for the KJV's 'she'): the Bíblia Livre's 'Não temas; crê somente' used, the rest translated in Henry's words
- flag: 'in perfect faith there is no fear' adapts 1 John 4:18 ('perfect love'); translated as Henry wrote it

### 3227 Luke 9:1-9 The apostles sent forth.
- quoted (Luke 9:7): “John was risen from the dead” → “João tinha ressuscitado dos mortos”
- flag: the source text opens with the heading of the next section ('Peter's testimony to Christ, Self-denial enjoined.' and '(18-27)') as separate paragraphs; kept as given

### 3228 Luke 9:10-17 The multitude miraculously fed.
- quoted (Luke 9:11): “spake unto them of the kingdom of God” → “lhes falava do Reino de Deus”

### 3229 Luke 9:18-27 
- flag: this section has no title in the source; its heading appears at the start of section 3227's text

### 3230 Luke 9:28-36 The transfiguration.
- flag: 'no continuing city' echoes Heb 13:14 (unlisted); the Bíblia Livre's 'não temos aqui cidade permanente' fits, but Henry's 'in this world' replaces 'here', so translated: 'neste mundo não temos cidade permanente'

### 3231 Luke 9:37-42 An evil spirit cast out.
- flag: 'Disease of that nature are': singular noun with plural verb in the English; translated as plural 'Doenças'

### 3233 Luke 9:51-56 He reproves their mistaken zeal.
- flag: 'and through they refused': typo in the English for 'though'; translated as 'embora'
- flag: 'Come, see our zeal for the Lord' echoes 2 Kings 10:16 (Jehu); translated in Henry's words

### 3234 Luke 9:57-62 Every thing to be given up for Christ.
- flag: 'He only that endures to the end shall be saved' adapts Matt 10:22 (unlisted, Henry adds 'only'); translated, close to the Bíblia Livre's 'aquele que perseverar até o fim, esse será salvo'
- flag: 'drawing back is to perdition' echoes Heb 10:39 (the Bíblia Livre: 'dos que retrocedem para a perdição'); 'retroceder' chosen to keep the echo

### 3235 Luke 10:1-16 Seventy disciples sent forth.
- flag: missing full stop after 'very fearful' in the English; supplied

### 3236 Luke 10:17-24 The blessedness of Christ's disciples.
- flag: 'a man of sorrows' (Isa 53:3, the Bíblia Livre 'homem de dores') and 'resisted the proud, and given grace to the humble' (Jas 4:6 / 1 Pet 5:5, in Henry's tense) are unlisted echoes; 'homem de dores' matches the Bíblia Livre, the other translated in Henry's tense

### 3237 Luke 10:25-37 The good Samaritan.
- quoted (Titus 3:4): “the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward” → “a bondade e amor de Deus nosso Salvador”
- flag: Q0: the Bíblia Livre goes on 'para a humanidade'; Henry's 'toward sinful, miserable men' translated after the quoted words

### 3238 Luke 10:38-42 Jesus at the house of Martha and Mary.
- flag: 'Mary hath chosen the good part' (Luke 10:42, unlisted): the Bíblia Livre's 'Maria escolheu a parte boa' used verbatim
- flag: 'professors of the gospel': those who profess the gospel; rendered 'professores do evangelho' in the old sense (professing believers), which a reviewer may prefer as 'os que professam o evangelho'

### 3239 Luke 11:1-4 The disciples taught to pray.
- quoted (Luke 11:1): “Lord, teach us to pray” → “Senhor, ensina-nos a orar”

### 3241 Luke 11:14-26 Christ casts out a devil, The blasphemy of the Pharisees.
- flag: 'endowments of mind of body': so the English (for 'mind or body' / 'mind and body'); translated 'da mente e do corpo'
- flag: 'the sin that besets the sinner' echoes Heb 12:1 (the Bíblia Livre: 'o pecado que facilmente nos envolve'); 'envolve' chosen to keep the echo
- flag: 'garnished' / 'furnished': Henry's contrast of the KJV's 'garnished' (the Bíblia Livre at Luke 11:25 has 'adornada') rendered 'adornada' / 'mobiliada'

### 3242 Luke 11:27-28 True happiness.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 11:28; Henry's 'to hear the word of God' is an infinitive in his own sentence ('a great privilege'); the Bíblia Livre's finite 'ouvem a palavra de Deus' doesn't fit): “hear the word of God”
