### 4041 Rev 20:11-15 The last and general resurrection.
- quoted (Rev 20:12): “the dead, small and great” → “os mortos, grandes e pequenos”
- quoted (Rev 20:14): “This is the second death” → “esta é a segunda morte”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's order "grandes e pequenos" for Henry's "small and great"; his following pairs (young and old, low and high, poor and rich) kept in his order
- flag: unlisted quotation of Isa 33:14 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("Quem dentre nós pode conviver com o fogo consumidor?"; KJV "Who among us shall dwell with the devouring fire?", which Henry gives without "among us" and as "devouring flames")
- flag: Henry's "hell" destroyed together with death (Rev 20:14, KJV) translated "inferno"; the Bíblia Livre has "o Xeol"

### 4042 Rev 21:1-8 A new heaven, and new earth: the new Jerusalem where God dwells, and banishes all sorrow from his people.
- quoted (Rev 21:3): “he will dwell with them” → “com eles habitará”
- quoted (Rev 21:6): “Alpha and Omega, the Beginning and the End” → “o Alfa e o Ômega, o princípio e o fim”
- flag: source slips: "their glorified, bodies" (stray comma), "will not be interrupt" (for "interrupted") and "in order hearts" (for "in our hearts"); translated as meant
- flag: Henry's "The fearful" (Rev 21:8, KJV) given as "Os covardes", the Bíblia Livre's word

### 4043 Rev 21:9-21 Its heavenly origin, glory, and secure defence.
- quoted (Rev 21:21): “The street of the city was pure gold” → “a praça da cidade era de ouro puro, como vidro transparente”
- flag: source typo "things of a heavenly nature of the heirs of salvation" read as "to the heirs"
- flag: quote 0: Henry's "street" (KJV) is "praça" in the Bíblia Livre; the verse's following words ("como vidro transparente", Henry's "like transparent glass") are kept with it

### 4044 Rev 21:22-27 Its perfect happiness, as enlightened with the presence of God and the Lamb, and in the free access of multitudes, made holy.
- quoted (Rev 21:23): “the Lamb is the Light thereof” → “sua lâmpada é o Cordeiro”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's "sua lâmpada" (lamp) for Henry's "the Light thereof" (KJV)
- flag: source "can more full express" read as "more fully"

### 4045 Rev 22:1-5 A description of the heavenly state, under the figures of the water and the tree of life, and of the throne of God and the Lamb.

### 4046 Rev 22:6-19 The truth and certain fulfilling of all the prophetic visions, The Holy Spirit, and the bride, the church, invite, and say, Come.
