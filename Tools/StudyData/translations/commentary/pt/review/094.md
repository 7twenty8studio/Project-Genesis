# To review: pt commentary batch 094

### 3839 2Tim 1:1-5 Paul expresses great affection for Timothy.
- quoted (2Tim 1:2): “God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord” → “Deus Pai, e de Cristo Jesus, nosso Senhor”
- flag: "unfeigned" faith (2 Tm 1:5) given as "não fingida", the Bíblia Livre's word.

### 3840 2Tim 1:6-14 Exhorts him to improve his spiritual gifts.
- quoted (2Tim 1:7): “not given us the spirit of fear” → “não nos deu espírito de medo”
- flag: Henry expounds the KJV's "power, and of love, and of a sound mind" (2 Tm 1:7); the Bíblia Livre has "de força, de amor, e de moderação"; his "spirit of power" is "força", his "sound mind" translated "mente sã".
- flag: The source ends with "(2Ti 1:15-18)", apparently the next section's heading reference; kept as "(2 Tm 1:15-18)".

### 3841 2Tim 1:15-18 Tells of many who basely deserted him; but speaks with affection of Onesiphorus.
- quoted (2Tim 1:18): “may find mercy of the Lord” → “misericórdia diante do Senhor”
- quoted (2Cor 5:10): “appear before the judgment seat of Christ” → “comparecer diante do Tribunal de Cristo”

### 3842 2Tim 2:1-7 The apostle exhorts Timothy to persevere with diligence, like a soldier, a combatant, and a husbandman.
- flag: "strive lawfully" (2 Tm 2:5, KJV) rendered "lutam segundo as regras", after the Bíblia Livre's "se não seguir as regras"; "understanding in all things" (2 Tm 2:7) as the Bíblia Livre's "entendimento em tudo".

### 3843 2Tim 2:8-13 Encouraging him by assurances of a happy end of his faithfulness.
- quoted (Heb 12:2): “faith, who for the joy that was set before him, endured the cross” → “Autor e aperfeiçoador da fé … pela alegria que lhe estava proposta, suportou a cruz”
- quoted (Heb 12:2): “set down at the right hand of the throne of God” → “assentou-se à direita do trono de Deus”
- quoted (2Tim 2:9): “the word of God is not bound” → “a palavra de Deus não está presa”
- flag: Hb 12:2: Henry's "their faith" is "da fé" as in the Bíblia Livre; his "who" joins what the Bíblia Livre begins with "Ele", so quote 0 is two runs; "despised the shame" also uses its "desprezando a humilhação".

### 3844 2Tim 2:14-21 Warnings to shun vain babblings and dangerous errors.
- quoted (Titus 2:14): “gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity” → “deu a si mesmo por nós, para nos libertar de toda injustiça”
- quoted (Eph 3:19): “be filled with all the fulness of God” → “cheios de toda a plenitude de Deus”
- flag: The source runs two sentences together ("must make conscience of the duty Christ gave himself for us"; a full stop seems lost after "duty"); translated as it stands ("do dever Cristo deu a si mesmo").
- flag: "two writings" on the foundation are the two inscriptions of the seal of 2 Tm 2:19 (Bíblia Livre: "tem este selo").

### 3845 2Tim 2:22-26 Charges to flee youthful lusts, and to minister with zeal against error, but with meekness of spirit.
- flag: Henry's quoted "peradventure" is the KJV's word in 2 Tm 2:25; the Bíblia Livre has "talvez", used in his quotation marks.

### 3846 2Tim 3:1-9 The apostle foretells the rise of dangerous enemies to the gospel.
- flag: "form of godliness" (2 Tm 3:5) rendered "aparência de piedade"; the Bíblia Livre has "aparência de devoção divina".

### 3847 2Tim 3:10-13 Proposes his own example to Timothy.
- quoted (2Tim 3:13): “worse, deceiving and being deceived” → “de mal a pior, enganando e sendo enganados”

### 3849 2Tim 4:1-5 The apostle solemnly charges Timothy to be diligent, though many will not bear sound doctrine.
- translated, not quoted (2Tim 4:3; Henry's present "when they will not endure" (they refuse) can't take the Bíblia Livre's future "não suportarão"): “when they will not endure”
- flag: "fables" (2 Tm 4:4, KJV) kept as "fábulas"; the Bíblia Livre has "mitos".

### 3850 2Tim 4:6-8 Enforces the charge from his own martyrdom, then at hand.
- flag: "kept the faith" and "laid up" (2 Tm 4:7-8) follow the Bíblia Livre's "guardei a fé" and "me está reservada"; "a crown of righteousness" as "coroa de justiça" (Bíblia Livre: "a coroa da justiça").

### 3851 2Tim 4:9-13 Desires him to come speedily.
- flag: "our profiting may appear to all" echoes 1 Tm 4:15; rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "progresso seja visível por todos" (unlisted quotation, person changed).

### 3852 2Tim 4:14-18 He cautions, and complains of such as had deserted him; and expresses his faith as to his own preservation to the heavenly kingdom.
- quoted (2Tim 4:17): “was delivered out of the mouth of the lion” → “livrado da boca do leão”

### 3854 Titus 1:1-4 The apostle salutes Titus.
- quoted (Rom 10:17): “by hearing, and hearing by the word of God” → “pelo ouvir, e o ouvir pela palavra de Deus”

### 3856 Titus 1:10-16 The evil temper and practices of false teachers.
- quoted (1Tim 6:10): “the love of money is the root of all evil” → “o amor ao dinheiro é raiz de todos os tipos de males”
- flag: The source lacks a full stop between "they may go no further" and "They had"; kept as it stands.
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has "raiz de todos os tipos de males" (all kinds of evils) for the KJV's "root of all evil".
- flag: sensitive: Henry's "Shameful actions, the reproach of heathens" and "national differences of character" comment on Tt 1:12 ("Os cretenses sempre são mentirosos"); translated in full.
- flag: "To those who are defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure" follows Tt 1:15; rendered close to, not exactly, the Bíblia Livre ("para os contaminados e infiéis, nada é puro").

### 3857 Titus 2:1-8 The duties which become sound doctrine.
- quoted (Titus 2:1, Titus 2:2): “doctrine. That the aged men be sober” → “doutrina … Aos velhos, que sejam sóbrios”
- flag: Quote 0 spans Tt 2:1-2; the Bíblia Livre's "Aos velhos, que sejam sóbrios" stands as its own sentence, as Henry's "That the aged men be sober" does.

### 3858 Titus 2:9-10 Believing servants must be obedient.
- quoted (Luke 16:12): “If ye have not been faithful in that which is another man's, who shall give you that which is your own” → “E se nas coisas dos outros não fostes fiéis, quem vos dará o que é vosso”
- flag: sensitive: the duties of slaves ("servants") to their masters (Tt 2:9-10), translated in full.
- flag: "all good fidelity" (Tt 2:10) given as the Bíblia Livre's "toda a boa lealdade".

### 3859 Titus 2:11-15 All is enforced from the holy design of the gospel, which concerns all believers.
- quoted (Titus 2:12): “denying ungodliness and worldly lusts” → “renunciarmos à irreverência e aos maus desejos mundanos … de maneira sóbria, justa e devota”
- quoted (Titus 2:13): “great God and our Saviour” → “grande Deus e nosso Salvador”
- flag: "ungodliness" (Tt 2:12) appears in the Bíblia Livre as "irreverência", used in the quotation.
- flag: "All Scripture is profitable" (2 Tm 3:16) rendered "Toda a Escritura é proveitosa", the Bíblia Livre's words without "divinamente inspirada e".

### 3860 Titus 3:1-7 Obedience to magistrates, and becoming behaviour towards all, are enforced from what believers were before conversion, and what they are made, through Christ.
- quoted (Titus 3:4): “the kindness and love of God” → “a bondade e amor de Deus”
- quoted (Titus 3:5): “renewing of the Holy Ghost” → “renovação do Espírito Santo”
- flag: "washing of regeneration" (Tt 3:5, KJV) given as the Bíblia Livre's "banho do novo nascimento"; Henry's following "outward washing" is "lavagem exterior".
- flag: "hateful" (Tt 3:3) rendered "detestáveis", the Bíblia Livre's word.
- flag: "the answer of a good conscience" alludes to 1 Pe 3:21 (KJV); translated.

### 3862 Titus 3:12-15 Directions and exhortations.
- quoted (Phil 1:11): “filled with the fruits of righteousness, which are by Jesus Christ” → “cheios dos frutos da justiça, que, por meio de Jesus Cristo, são para glória e louvor a Deus”
- quoted (Titus 3:15): “Grace be with you all” → “A graça seja com todos vós”
- flag: Henry's quoted "ours" is the KJV's word in Tt 3:14; the Bíblia Livre also has "os nossos", used in his quotation marks.
- flag: Quote 0 is listed under Fp 1:11, whose Bíblia Livre words were used; it runs on to "para glória e louvor a Deus", which Henry also writes.

### 3863 Philemon 1:1-7 The apostle's joy and praise for Philemon's steady faith in the Lord Jesus, and love to all the saints.
- flag: "must not make difference of affection, as to the truth": the closing phrase is unclear in the English (perhaps "as to the truth of affection"); translated literally.

### 3864 Philemon 1:8-14 He recommends Onesimus as one who would make rich amends for the misconduct of which he had been guilty; and on behalf of whom the apostle promises to make up any loss Philemon had sustained.
- flag: Henry plays on the name Onesimus ("profitable") and the KJV's "unprofitable ... profitable" (Fm 1:11); the Bíblia Livre has "inútil ... útil", used for the rest of the wordplay.
- flag: sensitive: Onesimus is a runaway slave; Henry approves his return to his master and calls "religious servants" treasures in a family; translated in full.

### 3865 Philemon 1:15-22 
- flag: "it is shamefully prevented" read as "perverted" ("pervertido"), the evident sense; "prevented" may be a misprint.
- flag: "The communion of saints does not destroy distinction of property": Henry's point on Fm 1:18 concerning a slave owner's loss; see the sensitive flag on 3864.

### 3866 Philemon 1:23-25 Salutations and a blessing.
- quoted (Philemon 1:25): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “a graça de nosso Senhor Jesus”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre text of Fm 1:25 has the misprint "Cirsto"; the quotation stops at "Jesus" and "Cristo" is written correctly after it.

### 3867 Heb 1:1-3 The surpassing dignity of the Son of God in his Divine person, and in his creating and mediatorial work.
- flag: "at sundry times ... in divers manners" (Hb 1:1, KJV) rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "muitas vezes ... de muitas maneiras".
