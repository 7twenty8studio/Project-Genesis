# To review: pt commentary batch 008

### 219 Ex 7:14-25 The river is turned into blood, The distress of the Egyptians.
- quoted (Ex 7:20): “in the sight of Pharaoh” → “em presença de Faraó”
- quoted (Job 36:13): “the hypocrites in heart heap up wrath” → “os hipócritas de coração acumulam a ira”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Isa 5:25 ("his hand is stretched out still"): "sua mão ainda está estendida" from the Bíblia Livre.

### 220 Ex 8:1-15 The plague of frogs.
- translated, not quoted (Ex 8:8; Henry turns Pharaoh's first person (deixarei) into the third person): “will let the people go”
- quoted (Ex 8:15): “there was respite, he hardened his heart” → “vendo Faraó que lhe haviam dado repouso, agravou seu coração”

### 221 Ex 8:16-19 The plague of lice.
- quoted (Job 14:19): “out of the dust of the earth” → “o pó da terra”
- quoted (Ex 8:17): “the dust of the earth” → “o pó da terra”
- quoted (Ex 8:19): “This is the finger of God” → “Dedo de Deus é este”
- flag: Quote 0 is listed as Job 14:19, but Henry is on Ex 8:16-17; "o pó da terra" is in both verses.

### 222 Ex 8:20-32 The plague of flies.
- translated, not quoted (Ex 8:29; the Bíblia Livre's "contanto que Faraó não falte mais" loses "deal deceitfully", on which Henry's next sentences (deceived, cheat) build): “let not Pharaoh deal deceitfully any more”
- quoted (Gal 6:7): “Be not deceived; God is not mocked” → “Não vos enganeis: Deus não se deixa escarnecer”

### 223 Ex 9:1-7 The murrain of beasts.
- quoted (Ex 9:3): “The hand of the Lord” → “A mão do SENHOR”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ex 9:4 ("the Lord shall sever"): "o SENHOR fará separação" from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "A sparrow falls not to the ground without our Father" (Matt 10:29) is Henry's paraphrase; translated, not quoted.

### 225 Ex 9:13-21 The plague of hail threatened.
- translated, not quoted (Acts 28:24; Henry borrows Acts 28:24's words for the Egyptians; the Bíblia Livre's "que ele dizia" (Paul speaking) doesn't fit his sentence): “Some believed the things which were spoken”
- quoted (Ex 9:20): “among the servants of Pharaoh” → “servos de Faraó”

### 226 Ex 9:22-35 The plague of hail inflicted.
- translated, not quoted (Ex 9:33; the Bíblia Livre has "saído Moisés da presença de Faraó da cidade", which can't stand in Henry's short sentence): “Moses went out of the city”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ex 9:27 ("the Lord is righteous"): "o SENHOR é justo" from the Bíblia Livre.

### 227 Ex 10:1-11 The plague of locusts threatened, Pharaoh, moved by his servants, inclines to let the Israelites go.
- quoted (Eccl 12:1): “thy Creator in the days of thy youth” → “Lembra-te de teu Criador nos dias de tua juventude”

### 228 Ex 10:12-20 The plague of locusts.
- translated, not quoted (Job 41:10; the Bíblia Livre's Job 41:10 has "quem pois, ousa se opor a mim?", not "stand before"): “Who then is able to stand before”
- quoted (Ex 10:13): “east wind brought the locusts” → “vento oriental trouxe os gafanhotos”
- flag: unlisted quotation of John 3:8 ("The wind bloweth where it listeth"): "O vento sopra onde quer" from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "this goodness of God might lead us to repentance" echoes Rom 2:4 (Bíblia Livre: "a bondade de Deus que te conduz ao arrependimento"); translated in Henry's wish.

### 229 Ex 10:21-29 The plague of thick darkness.
- quoted (Ex 10:23): “had light in their dwellings” → “tinham luz em suas habitações”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Prov 3:33 ("the house of the wicked ... the habitation of the just"): "a casa do perverso" and "a morada dos justos" from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "darkness which might be felt" (Ex 10:21): the Bíblia Livre says "tão intensas que qualquer um as apalpe"; Henry's phrase translated ("trevas que se podiam apalpar").

### 230 Ex 11:1-3 God's last instructions to Moses respecting Pharaoh and the Egyptians.
- quoted (Ex 11:3): “favour in the sight of the Egyptians” → “fez o povo ser favorecido aos olhos dos egípcios”
- flag: "The Lord gave them favour" opens a quotation, so the Bíblia Livre's "O SENHOR fez o povo ser favorecido" is used, "SENHOR" included.
- flag: The second paragraph "(4-10)" is Henry's outline heading for the next verses, kept as given.

### 231 Ex 11:4-10 
- quoted (Ex 11:7): “any of the children of Israel” → “filhos de Israel”
- quoted (Ex 11:8): “he went out from Pharaoh” → “saiu-se muito irado da presença de Faraó”
- quoted (Rev 12:11): “him by the blood of the Lamb” → “pelo sangue do Cordeiro”
- flag: "not so much as a dog should bark": the Bíblia Livre's Ex 11:7 says "nem um cão moverá sua língua"; Henry's wording translated.

### 232 Ex 12:1-20 The beginning of the year changed, The passover instituted.
- quoted (Ex 12:17): “The feast of unleavened bread” → “A festa dos pães ázimos”
- quoted (Ex 12:14): “by an ordinance for ever” → “por estatuto perpétuo”
- flag: Rm 5:11: Henry's "receive the atonement" rests on the KJV's word; the Bíblia Livre has "recebemos a reconciliação". Translated as "expiação".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Col 2:6 ("Having received Christ Jesus the Lord"): "recebestes Cristo Jesus, o Senhor" adapted to "Tendo recebido Cristo Jesus, o Senhor" (participle; not verbatim).
- flag: "the leaven of malice ... sincerity" (1 Cor 5:8): the Bíblia Livre has "fermento do mal e da perversão" and "sinceridade"; Henry's words translated.
- flag: The paragraph "(29-36)" is Henry's outline heading for later verses, kept as given.

### 233 Ex 12:21-28 The people instructed how to observe the passover.

- flag: Quote 0: Henry's "it is good" (Lam 3:26) left in his own words after the quoted run ("é bom fazer assim").

### 239 Ex 13:17-20 Joseph's bones carried with the Israelites, They come to Etham.

### 240 Ex 13:21-22 God guideth the Israelites by a pillar of cloud fire.

- flag: "a light to our feet, a lantern to our paths" echoes Ps 119:105 with the words swapped; translated in Henry's order, not quoted.

### 241 Ex 14:1-9 God directs the Israelites to Pihahiroth, Pharaoh pursues after them.

- flag: Quote 0: the KJV's "honoured upon" is "glorificado em" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's play on "honoured by / honoured upon" is rendered "honrado ... glorificado".
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2Tim 3:12 ("live godly in Christ Jesus"): "viver devotamente em Cristo Jesus" from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: Title: Pihahiroth written "Pi-Hairote" (not in the glossary; Almeida's spelling).
