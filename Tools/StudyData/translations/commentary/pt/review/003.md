# To review: pt commentary batch 003

### 52 Gen 11:5-9 The confusion of tongues, The builders of Babel dispersed.
- quoted (Gen 11:5): “The Lord came down to see the city” → “E desceu o SENHOR para ver a cidade”
- quoted (Gen 11:8): “They left off to build the city” → “deixaram de edificar a cidade”
- quoted (Matt 19:28): “when the Son of man shall sit” → “quando o Filho do homem se sentar no trono de sua glória”
- flag: quote 2: the source lists Mt 19:28; Henry's "and all nations shall be gathered before him" is Mt 25:31-32 (the Bíblia Livre's "serão ajuntadas diante dele todas as nações" is echoed)
- flag: unlisted quotation of Sl 103:10 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("não nos trata conforme nossos pecados")
- flag: allusions translated in Henry's words: Jó 9:4 ("hardened ... and prospered"; the Bíblia Livre: "e teve paz?") and Pv 21:30 ("no wisdom nor counsel against the Lord"; the Bíblia Livre adds "nem entendimento")

### 54 Gen 11:27-32 Terah, father of Abram, grandfather of Lot, they remove to Haran.
- quoted (Mark 12:34): “not far from the kingdom of God” → “longe do Reino de Deus”
- flag: "Charran" (the KJV's spelling in At 7:4) rendered "Harã", as in the title and the Bíblia Livre's Gn 11:31
- flag: quote 0: Henry turns "thou art not far" into the third person; only "longe do Reino de Deus" is the Bíblia Livre's wording

### 55 Gen 12:1-3 God calls Abram, and blesses him with a promise of Christ.
- quoted (Gen 12:2): “I will make of thee a great nation” → “farei de ti uma grande nação”
- quoted (Gen 12:2): “Thou shalt be a blessing” → “tu serás uma bênção”
- quoted (Gen 12:3): “I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee” → “abençoarei os que te abençoarem, e amaldiçoarei os que te amaldiçoarem”
- quoted (Gen 28:14): “shall all the families of the earth be blessed” → “todas as famílias da terra serão abençoadas em ti”
- flag: quote 3: the source lists Gn 28:14, whose Bíblia Livre words are used; the passage commented on is Gn 12:3 (the Bíblia Livre: "e em ti todas as famílias da terra serão benditas"), which a reviewer may prefer
- flag: promises 2 and 3 given with the Bíblia Livre's Gn 12:2 ("te abençoarei", "engrandecerei o teu nome")

### 57 Gen 12:6-9 He journeys through Canaan, and worships God in that land.
- quoted (Gen 12:8): “the name of the Lord” → “o nome do SENHOR”
- flag: "He preached concerning the name of the Lord" follows the KJV margin of Gn 12:8 ("called upon" / "preached"); the Bíblia Livre has "invocou o nome do SENHOR"; Henry's "preached" is kept
- flag: "strangers and sojourners" given as "estrangeiros e peregrinos" (the Bíblia Livre's Hb 11:13)

### 58 Gen 12:10-20 Abram is driven by a famine into Egypt, He feigns his wife to be his sister.
- quoted (Gen 12:18): “What is this that thou hast done” → “Que é isto que fizeste”

### 59 Gen 13:1-4 Abram returns out of Egypt with great riches.
- quoted (Gen 13:4): “called on the name of the Lord” → “invocou ali Abrão o nome do SENHOR”
- flag: "very heavy, so the Hebrew word is": Henry explains the Hebrew of Gn 13:2; the Bíblia Livre has "riquíssimo"
- flag: "load themselves with thick clay" is the KJV's Hc 2:6; the Bíblia Livre has "se enriquece por meio de extorsão", so Henry's words are translated
- flag: "rich without sorrow" alludes to Pv 10:22 (the Bíblia Livre: "ele não lhe acrescenta dores")

### 60 Gen 13:5-9 Strife between the herdsmen of Abram and Lot. Abram gives Lot his choice of the country.
- quoted (Gen 13:7): “the Canaanite and the Perizzite” → “os cananeus e os ferezeus habitavam então na terra”
- quoted (2Sam 12:14): “occasion to the enemies of the Lord to blaspheme” → “blasfemar aos inimigos do SENHOR”
- flag: quote 1: Henry turns 2 Sm 12:14 into a general statement; only "blasfemar aos inimigos do SENHOR" is the Bíblia Livre's wording
- flag: allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: Pv 15:1 ("resposta suave desvia o furor") and 1 Pe 3:9 ("insulto com insulto" for KJV "railing for railing")
- flag: "make-bates" (old word for those who stir up strife) rendered "semeadores de discórdia"

### 61 Gen 13:10-13 Lot chooses to dwell at Sodom.

- flag: allusion to 1 Jo 2:16 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("a cobiça da carne, a cobiça dos olhos, e a ostentação da vida")
