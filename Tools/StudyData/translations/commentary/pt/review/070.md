# To review: pt commentary batch 070

### 3069 Matt 20:29-34 Jesus gives sight to two blind men near Jericho.
- quoted (2Pet 3:18): “grow in grace and in the knowledge” → “em graça e conhecimento de nosso Senhor e Salvador Jesus Cristo”

### 3070 Matt 21:1-11 Christ enters Jerusalem.
- quoted (Matt 21:9): “Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord” → “Bendito o que vem no nome do Senhor”
- quoted (Matt 21:10): “was come into Jerusalem all the city was moved” → “entrava em Jerusalém, toda a cidade se alvoroçou”
- flag: Henry plays on "moved" (KJV "all the city was moved … moved with joy … moved with envy"); the Bíblia Livre's "se alvoroçou" is carried into his two applications ("se alvoroçaram de alegria / de inveja").
- flag: "They brought the ass": "jumenta" (Mt 21:2, 7 have a donkey and her colt); Henry doesn't say which.

### 3071 Matt 21:12-17 He drives out those who profaned the temple.
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Mt 21:13: "covil de ladrões" and "casa de oração" are the Bíblia Livre's words; the promise about "the latter house" (Ag 2:9) is translated, not quoted.

### 3072 Matt 21:18-22 
- flag: sensitive: the barren fig tree as "the state of the nation and people of the Jews": "blindness and hardness grew upon them, till they were undone … The Lord was righteous in it"; translated in full.

### 3075 Matt 21:33-46 The parable of the wicked husbandmen.
- flag: sensitive: "This parable plainly sets forth the sin and ruin of the Jewish nation"; translated in full.
- flag: "the remainders of wrath" echoes Sl 76:10 (KJV "the remainder of wrath shalt thou restrain"); not listed, translated.

### 3076 Matt 22:1-14 The parable of the marriage feast.
- quoted (Matt 22:11): “had not on a wedding-garment” → “não estava vestido com roupa adequada para a festa de casamento”
- flag: Henry's "wedding-garment" (KJV) is in the Bíblia Livre "roupa adequada para a festa de casamento" (v. 11) and "roupa para a festa" (v. 12); quoted at its first use, then rendered "veste nupcial" in his own sentences.
- flag: sensitive: "The utter ruin coming upon the Jewish church and nation" as the king's burning of the city; translated in full.
- flag: "Take him away" (Mt 22:13) not listed; translated ("Levai-o daqui").

### 3077 Matt 22:15-22 The Pharisees question Jesus as to the tribute.
- quoted (Matt 22:16): “their disciples with the Herodians” → “seus discípulos, juntamente com os apoiadores de Herodes”
- flag: "the Herodians" is "os apoiadores de Herodes" in the Bíblia Livre (Mt 22:16), used as quoted; Henry's gloss "a party among the Jews" follows it.

### 3078 Matt 22:23-33 The question of the Sadducees as to the resurrection.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 3079 Matt 22:34-40 The substance of the commandments.
- quoted (Matt 22:38, Matt 22:39): “is the first and great commandment” → “o primeiro e grande mandamento”

### 3080 Matt 22:41-46 Jesus questions the Pharisees.
- flag: "What think we of Christ?" adapts Mt 22:42 (KJV "What think ye of Christ?") to the first person, inside Henry's quotation marks; translated.

### 3081 Matt 23:1-12 Jesus reproves the scribes and Pharisees.
- quoted (Gal 6:6): “him that is taught in the word” → “aquele que é instruído na palavra”

### 3082 Matt 23:13-33 Crimes of the Pharisees.
- translated, not quoted (Matt 5:20; Henry's words are his own phrase, not a quotation of Mt 5:20; the Bíblia Livre there has "a vossa justiça … a dos escribas e dos fariseus", with no unbroken run naming their righteousness.): “The righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees”

### 3083 Matt 23:34-39 The guilt of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Matt 23:37): “her chickens under her wings” → “ajunta os seus pintos debaixo das asas”
- flag: sensitive: "The present dispersion and unbelief of the Jews" and "their punishment has been signal"; translated in full.

### 3084 Matt 24:1-3 Christ foretells the destruction of the temple.
- quoted (Matt 24:2): “See ye not all these things” → “Não vedes tudo isto”

### 3085 Matt 24:4-28 The troubles before the destruction of Jerusalem.
- quoted (2Thess 2:1): “the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “vinda de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- flag: sensitive: "From the time that the Jews rejected Christ … the sword never departed from them", "the ruin coming upon the people of the Jews" and "loathsome carcasses"; translated in full.
- flag: Source: "When we looked forward" (past tense) read as "When we look forward"; translated in the present.
- flag: "sabbath day" here is the Jewish sabbath of Mt 24:20 applied to a good man generally; kept as "dia de sábado" with no note, as Henry doesn't say "Christian sabbath".

### 3086 Matt 24:29-41 Christ foretells other signs and miseries, to the end of the world.
- quoted (Matt 24:30): “they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds” → “verão ao Filho do homem, que vem sobre as nuvens”
- quoted (Matt 24:39): “knew not, until the flood came” → “não sabiam, até que veio o dilúvio”
- flag: Henry reads v. 34's "this generation" as the Jewish people ("Judah shall never cease to exist as a distinct people"); kept as he wrote it. The Bíblia Livre's v. 34 also says "esta geração".
- flag: "Him whom they have pierced" (Zc 12:10; Jo 19:37) and "sow in tears … reap in joy" (Sl 126:5) are unlisted allusions; translated.

### 3087 Matt 24:42-51 Exhortations to watchfulness.
- quoted (Col 1:12): “to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light” → “participar da herança dos santos na luz”

### 3088 Matt 25:1-13 The parable of the ten virgins.
- quoted (Matt 25:5): “They all slumbered and slept” → “todas cochilaram e adormeceram”
- quoted (Ps 23:4): “the valley of the shadow of death” → “vale da sombra da morte”
- quoted (Prov 23:17): “in the fear of the Lord all the day long” → “no temor ao SENHOR o dia todo”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mt 25:6: "Go ye forth to meet Him" rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "Ide ao seu encontro".
- flag: "would die the death of the righteous" alludes to Nm 23:10; not listed, translated.
- flag: Quote 2 (Pv 23:17) keeps the Bíblia Livre's "SENHOR" inside Henry's own closing exhortation.

### 3089 Matt 25:14-30 The parable of the talents.
- quoted (1Cor 12:7): “The manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal” → “a cada um é dada a manifestação do Espírito, para o que for conveniente”
- flag: Henry has "to every man"; the Bíblia Livre's "a cada um" is used.
- flag: "the things that belong to their peace hid from their eyes" (Lc 19:42) and "the love of Christ constrains" (2 Co 5:14-15) are unlisted allusions; translated.

### 3090 Matt 25:31-46 The judgment.
- quoted (Matt 25:34): “from the foundation of the world” → “desde a fundação do mundo”
- flag: "saints and sinners, holy and unholy": "santos e pecadores, santos e profanos" repeats "santos" (Portuguese has one word for "saints" and "holy").

### 3092 Matt 26:6-13 Christ anointed at Bethany.
- flag: Matthew doesn't name the woman; Henry calls her Mary (from Jo 12:3); kept.

### 3093 Matt 26:14-16 Judas bargains to betray Christ.
- flag: "the love of money … the root of all evil" (1 Tm 6:10) is an unlisted allusion; translated.

### 3094 Matt 26:17-25 The Passover.
- quoted (Matt 26:19): “The disciples did as Jesus had appointed” → “Os discípulos fizeram como Jesus havia lhes mandado”
- flag: "Christ our Passover is sacrificed for us" (1 Co 5:7) and "be not high-minded, but fear" (Rm 11:20) are unlisted allusions; translated.
