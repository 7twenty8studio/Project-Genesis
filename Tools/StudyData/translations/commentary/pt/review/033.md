# To review: pt commentary batch 033

### 1279 Job 4:1-6 Eliphaz reproves Job.
- flag: Henry's "only a touch" plays on Job 4:5 KJV ("it toucheth thee"); the Bíblia Livre also has "isso te tocou".

### 1280 Job 4:7-11 And maintains that God's judgments are for the wicked.
- quoted (Eccl 9:2): “there is one event” → “o mesmo acontece”
- quoted (Eccl 9:2): “to the righteous and to the wicked” → “ao justo e ao perverso”

### 1281 Job 4:12-21 The vision of Eliphaz.
- quoted (Job 4:17): “be more just than God” → “mais justo que Deus”
- quoted (Job 4:17): “more pure than his Maker” → “mais puro que seu Criador”
- flag: Ps 4:4 KJV ("commune with your own heart … and be still") translated; the Bíblia Livre has "meditai em vosso coração … e fazei silêncio".
- flag: "Can a man be cleansed without his Maker?" is Henry's own question, translated.

### 1282 Job 5:1-5 Eliphaz urges that the sin of sinners in their ruin.
- flag: source title garbled ("urges that the sin of sinners in their ruin", a verb missing): translated as "is their ruin".
- flag: Henry's "holy, or more strictly, consecrated ones" explains the word "saints" (Job 5:1 KJV); the Bíblia Livre's wording in that verse may differ.

### 1283 Job 5:6-16 God is to be regarded in affliction.
- quoted (Job 5:7): “as the sparks fly upward” → “assim como as faíscas das brasas se levantam a voar”
- flag: "born to trouble" (Job 5:7 KJV) translated "nasce para a aflição"; the Bíblia Livre has "nasce para a opressão".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jas 5:13 ("Alguém entre vós está aflito? Ore.", Bíblia Livre).

### 1284 Job 5:17-27 The happy end of God's correction.
- quoted (Job 5:17): “Despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty” → “não rejeites o castigo do Todo-Poderoso”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Heb 12:2 ("Autor e aperfeiçoador da fé", Bíblia Livre); Henry's "chastening" follows the Bíblia Livre's "castigo" in Job 5:17.
- flag: Henry's "housed" corn alludes to Job 5:26 (the Bíblia Livre: "como o amontoado de trigo que se recolhe a seu tempo").

### 1286 Job 6:8-13 He wishes for death.
- flag: "O spare me a little" (Ps 39:13 KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre's verse reads differently ("Não prestes atenção em mim em tua ira").

### 1287 Job 6:14-30 Job reproves his friends as unkind.
- translated, not quoted (Job 27:6; Job 27:6 is first person in the Bíblia Livre ("não a deixarei ir"); Henry turns it into the third person about Job.): “fast, and will not let it go”
- flag: Henry's own quotation "for now ye are nothing" (Job 6:21 KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre reads differently ("Agora, vós vos tornastes semelhantes a elas").
- flag: "cease from man" (Isa 2:22) translated ("deixar de confiar no homem", near the Bíblia Livre's "cessai de confiar no homem").

### 1288 Job 7:1-6 Job's troubles.
- quoted (Rom 14:8): “we live, we live unto the Lord” → “vivemos, para o Senhor vivemos”
- flag: "a time appointed" and "warfare" follow Job 7:1 KJV and its margin; the Bíblia Livre has "um trabalho duro sobre a terra".

### 1289 Job 7:7-16 Job expostulates with God.
- flag: Ps 121:4 alluded to; adapted from the Bíblia Livre ("não cochilará nem dormirá o Guardião de Israel").
- flag: "I will complain" is Job 7:11 KJV, translated.

### 1290 Job 7:17-21 He begs release.
- flag: "the Preserver … the Observer of men" turns on Job 7:20 KJV ("O thou preserver of men", margin "observer"); the Bíblia Livre's wording there may differ.

### 1291 Job 8:1-7 Bildad reproves Job.
- quoted (Job 8:2): “How long wilt thou speak these things” → “Até quando falarás tais coisas”
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1292 Job 8:8-19 Hypocrites will be destroyed.
- quoted (Job 8:10): “utter words out of their heart” → “palavras de seu coração”

### 1293 Job 8:20-22 Bildad applies God's just dealing to Job.
- quoted (Job 8:20): “God will not cast away” → “Deus não rejeita ao íntegro”
- flag: "die the death of the righteous" (Num 23:10 KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "Morra minha pessoa da morte dos corretos".

### 1294 Job 9:1-13 Job acknowledges God's justice.
- quoted (Job 9:2): “how should man be just with God” → “como pode o ser humano ser justo diante de Deus”

### 1295 Job 9:14-21 He is not able to contend with God.
- quoted (Job 32:1): “righteous in his own eyes” → “justo em seus olhos”
- flag: 1John 1:8 alluded to in Henry's form ("If we say, We have no sin"); "enganamos a nós mesmos" is the Bíblia Livre's.

### 1296 Job 9:22-24 Men not to be judged by outward condition.
- flag: Lam 3:33 and Ps 141:3 alluded to and translated; the Bíblia Livre: "não é sua vontade afligir", "Põe, SENHOR, uma guarda em minha boca".

### 1297 Job 9:25-35 Job complains of troubles.
- quoted (Jude 1:24): “faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy” → “irrepreensíveis com alegria diante de sua glória”
- flag: Henry's "Daysman, or Umpire" is the KJV's word in Job 9:33; the Bíblia Livre has "árbitro", so "um Árbitro, ou Juiz entre as partes".
- flag: unlisted quotations adapted from the Bíblia Livre: Heb 7:25 ("pode salvar de maneira completa … os que se aproximam de Deus por meio dele"), Ps 51:7 ("mais branco que a neve"), Matt 11:28 ("cansados e sobrecarregados"), John 6:37 ("em maneira nenhuma … lançarei fora").
- flag: Jude 24's "exceeding" isn't in the Bíblia Livre ("com alegria").

### 1300 Job 10:14-22 He complains of God's severity.
- flag: Jude 13 and 1Thess 1:10 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's words ("escuridão das trevas", "livra da ira futura"), adapted.

### 1302 Job 11:7-12 God's perfections and almighty power.
- translated, not quoted (Job 11:12; the Bíblia Livre's Job 11:12 says something else ("quando do asno selvagem nascer um humano"); Henry's point, man born like a wild ass's colt, would be lost.): “born like a wild ass's colt”
- flag: Henry's "empty, so the word is" explains the Hebrew behind the KJV's "vain man" (Job 11:12); the Bíblia Livre has "O homem tolo".
- flag: "wise above what is written" adapted from 1Cor 4:6 (the Bíblia Livre: "além do que está escrito").

### 1303 Job 11:13-20 Zophar assures Job of blessings if he repented.
- quoted (Job 11:15): “Then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot” → “Então levantarás teu rosto sem mácula”
- quoted (Heb 10:22): “in full assurance of faith” → “em plena certeza de fé”
- quoted (Heb 10:22): “sprinkled from an evil conscience” → “aspergidos … da má consciência”
- flag: quote 2: the Bíblia Livre has "aspergidos e purificados da má consciência"; "e purificados" left out to keep Henry's length.

### 1306 Job 12:12-25 Job speaks of the wisdom and power of God.
- flag: sensitive: "the Lord Jesus was crucified by the malice of the Jews".
- flag: Eph 1:11 and Rom 11:33 alluded to and translated.

### 1307 Job 13:1-12 Job reproves his friends.
- flag: Job 13:4 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's "médicos inúteis" (KJV "physicians of no value").

### 1308 Job 13:13-22 He professes his confidence in God.
- flag: Job 13:15-16 ("though he slay me", "he also shall be my salvation") alluded to; the Bíblia Livre: "ainda que ele me mate", "Ele mesmo será minha salvação".

### 1309 Job 13:23-28 Job entreats to know his sins.
- flag: Job 13:26 (KJV "writest bitter things … possess the iniquities of my youth") and Eccl 12:1 alluded to, adapted from the Bíblia Livre ("escreves contra mim amarguras", "herdar as transgressões", "lembra-te de teu Criador nos dias de tua juventude").

### 1310 Job 14:1-6 Job speaks of man's life.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Acts 17:28 ("nele vivemos, e nos movemos", Bíblia Livre).

### 1312 Job 14:16-22 By sin man is subject to corruption.
- flag: Henry follows Job 14:22 KJV ("his flesh upon him shall have pain, and his soul within him shall mourn"); the Bíblia Livre reads differently ("Ele apenas sente as dores em sua própria carne, e lamenta por sua própria alma").
- flag: Deut 32:29 and Luke 10:42 alluded to and translated.

### 1313 Job 15:1-16 Eliphaz reproves Job.
- flag: "restraining prayer" follows Job 15:4 KJV ("restrainest prayer"); the Bíblia Livre has "menosprezas a oração".

### 1314 Job 15:17-35 The unquietness of wicked men.
- quoted (Heb 12:11): “no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous, but grievous, nevertheless, afterward it yieldeth the peaceable” → “no presente, nenhuma disciplina parece ser motivo de alegria, mas sim, de sofrimento; mas depois produz um fruto pacífico”
- flag: Rom 8:35 alluded to ("Quem nos separará do amor de Cristo?" in the Bíblia Livre), Henry's "him" kept.

### 1316 Job 16:6-16 He represents his case as deplorable.
- flag: unlisted quotations of Isa 53:3 ("homem de dores") and Matt 5:4 ("os que choram, porque eles serão consolados"), Bíblia Livre.
