### 1048 2Kings 10:29-36 Jehu follows Jeroboam's sins.
- quoted (2Kings 10:32): “in those days the Lord began to cut Israel short” → “em aqueles dias começou o SENHOR a diminuir o território de Israel”
- flag: Henry plays on the KJV's "cut Israel short" ("They were short in their duty ... God cut them short"); the Bíblia Livre has "diminuir o território", so the play is rendered "ficaram aquém ... os diminuiu".
- flag: "o que se lê" was added so the Bíblia Livre's words can stand verbatim after "não é estranho".

### 1049 2Kings 11:1-12 Athaliah usurps the government of Judah, Jehoash made king.

- flag: "no pavilhão de Deus" follows the KJV of Sl 27:5 ("in his pavilion"; Bíblia Livre "em seu abrigo"); translated.
- flag: Athaliah: the glossary gives "Atalias; Atalia"; "Atalia" used, as in the Bíblia Livre of 2 Rs 11.

### 1050 2Kings 11:13-16 Athaliah put to death.
- flag: "Traição! Traição!" is 2 Rs 11:14 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 1051 2Kings 11:17-21 The worship of the Lord restored.

### 1052 2Kings 12:1-16 Jehoash orders the repair of the temple.

### 1053 2Kings 12:17-21 He is slain by his servants.

- flag: Quote 0: Hb 9:14 in the Bíblia Livre is "das obras mortas, para servirdes ao Deus vivo" (second person); only "das obras mortas" kept verbatim, the rest (Henry's "to serve the living and true God", joined with 1 Ts 1:9) translated.
- flag: "não saber coisa alguma senão Cristo" echoes 1 Co 2:2 (Bíblia Livre: "não quis saber coisa alguma entre vós, a não ser Jesus Cristo").

### 1054 2Kings 13:1-9 Reign of Jehoahaz.

### 1055 2Kings 13:10-19 Jehoash, king of Israel, Elisha dying.
- flag: "abandonar a sua própria misericórdia" echoes Jn 2:8 (Bíblia Livre: "abandonam sua própria misericórdia"; KJV "forsake their own mercy", Henry "mercies").

### 1058 2Kings 14:8-14 Amaziah provokes Jehoash king of Israel, and is overcome.
- flag: "teu coração te envaideceu" is 2 Rs 14:10 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted; KJV "thine heart hath lifted thee up").

### 1060 2Kings 14:23-29 Wicked reign of Jeroboam II.
- flag: Micah the prophet is "Miqueias" (glossary also has "Mica" for the Micah of Judges).

### 1062 2Kings 15:8-31 The latter kings of Israel.
- quoted (Jer 17:9): “deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked” → “mais enganoso que todas as coisas, e perverso”
- flag: KJV "desperately wicked" is just "perverso" in the Bíblia Livre of Jr 17:9; its words kept. Henry's "our hearts" rendered "o nosso coração" to fit the singular.
- flag: "renove dentro de nós um espírito reto" follows the KJV of Sl 51:10 ("a right spirit"); the Bíblia Livre has "um espírito firme em meu interior"; translated.

### 1066 2Kings 16:17-20 Ahaz spoils the temple.

- flag: "desprezou o sábado" refers to 2 Rs 16:18 (KJV "the covert for the sabbath", Bíblia Livre "a tenda do sábado").

### 1068 2Kings 17:7-23 Captivity of the Israelites.

### 1069 2Kings 17:24-41 The nations placed in the land of Israel.

### 1070 2Kings 18:1-8 Good reign of Hezekiah in Judah, Idolatry.

### 1072 2Kings 18:17-37 Rabshakeh's blasphemies.

- flag: KJV "wherein thou trustest" is "em que tu estás" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's echo "which those trust in" follows it ("em que estão").

### 1076 2Kings 19:35-37 The Assyrian army destroyed, Sennacherib slain.
- flag: The English ends a statement with "?" ("ought to consider whether ... in heaven?"); rendered as a statement.
- flag: "Senhor, ajuda-nos na nossa incredulidade" echoes Mc 9:24 (Bíblia Livre: "Ajuda-me na minha incredulidade"), in Henry's plural; "Quem jamais se endureceu contra ele e prosperou?" echoes Jó 9:4; both translated.

### 1077 2Kings 20:1-11 Hezekiah's sickness, His recovery in answer to prayer.
- flag: "enfermo de morte" is 2 Rs 20:1 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "Ó Senhor, lembra-te agora" (Henry's quotation marks, KJV 2 Rs 20:3 "remember now") translated; the Bíblia Livre has "Rogo-te, ó SENHOR, rogo-te faças memória".
- flag: "see Is 38." kept as "veja Is 38" (whole chapter).

### 1078 2Kings 20:12-21 Hezekiah shows his treasures to the ambassadors from Babylon, His death.

- flag: Quote 0: Henry quotes Ap 14:13 from "blessed are the dead"; the Bíblia Livre's own "para que" stands where the KJV has "for".
