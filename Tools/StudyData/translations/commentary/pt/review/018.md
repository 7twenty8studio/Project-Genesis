# To review: pt commentary batch 018

### 593 Deut 31:1-8 Moses encourages the people, and Joshua.
- quoted (Heb 4:2): “gospel preached, as well as unto them” → “foi pregado o evangelho, assim como a eles”
- quoted (Deut 31:6): “fail thee, nor forsake thee” → “te deixará nem te desamparará”
- flag: Quote 1 uses the Bíblia Livre's Dt 31:6 as listed; its Hb 13:5, which Henry cites, has "Não te deixarei, nem te desampararei".
- flag: "resistirmos ao diabo, ele fugirá de nós" follows the Bíblia Livre's Tg 4:7 (unlisted).

### 594 Deut 31:9-13 The law to be read every seventh year.
- flag: "o ano agradável do Senhor" follows the Bíblia Livre's Lc 4:19 (unlisted).

### 595 Deut 31:14-22 The Israelites' apostacy foretold, A song given to be witness against them.
- quoted (Deut 31:15): “the door of the tabernacle” → “porta do tabernáculo”
- quoted (Heb 4:12): “is a discerner of the thoughts and intents” → “é apta para julgar os pensamentos e intenções”
- flag: Henry's "is a discerner of" (KJV Hb 4:12) is "é apta para julgar" in the Bíblia Livre.

### 596 Deut 31:23-30 
- quoted (Deut 31:29): “I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt yourselves” → “eu sei que depois de minha morte, certamente vos corrompereis”
- flag: "o fundamento de Deus continua firme" follows the Bíblia Livre's 2 Tm 2:19 (unlisted).

### 598 Deut 32:3-6 The character of God, The character of Israel.
- flag: Henry's quotations "He is a Rock" and "His work is perfect" (Dt 32:4, unlisted) are given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "Ele é a Rocha", "sua obra é perfeita"; "justo e correto" is also its wording.
- flag: "se dissermos que não temos pecado … enganamos a nós mesmos" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Jo 1:8 (unlisted), with Henry's "no spot" inserted.
- flag: Henry's point on "spots" rests on the KJV's Dt 32:5 ("their spot is not the spot of his children"); the Bíblia Livre has "a falha é deles".
- flag: "vaidades enganosas" translates the KJV's "lying vanities" (Jn 2:8); the Bíblia Livre has "coisas inúteis e ilusórias".

### 599 Deut 32:7-14 The great things God had done for Israel.
- quoted (1Pet 2:24): “bare our sins in his own body on the tree” → “levou nossos pecados em seu próprio corpo sobre o madeiro”
- flag: Henry's eagle (KJV Dt 32:11): the Bíblia Livre's text reads "Como a água desperta sua ninhada", apparently a slip for "águia"; "águia" used.

### 600 Deut 32:15-18 The judgments which would come upon them for their sins.
- translated, not quoted (Rom 13:14; The Bíblia Livre's Rm 13:14 ("não fiqueis pensando em realizar os desejos da carne") loses Henry's "make provision for the flesh", on which his sentence rests.): “provision for the flesh to fulfil the lusts”
- flag: "Jesurum" as in the Bíblia Livre's Dt 32:15; Henry's glosses of the name ("upright", "seeing") translated.
- flag: "do seu ventre um deus" echoes Fp 3:19; "candid" (old sense: impartial, open-minded) given as "imparcialidade".

### 602 Deut 32:26-38 Deserved vengeance withheld.
- flag: "Que haverá no fim disso?" translates Henry's KJV echo of Jr 5:31; the Bíblia Livre has "o que fareis quando isto chegar ao fim?".

### 604 Deut 32:44-47 The exhortation with which the song was delivered.
- flag: "a religião é a sua vida" echoes Dt 32:47 (Bíblia Livre: "é vossa vida").

### 606 Deut 33:1-5 The glorious majesty of God.
- flag: "línguas repartidas como que de fogo" and "amor de Deus derramado" follow the Bíblia Livre's At 2:3 and Rm 5:5 (unlisted).
- flag: "uma lei de fogo": Henry's "fiery law" is the KJV's Dt 33:2; the Bíblia Livre's wording there differs.

### 607 Deut 33:6-23 The blessings of the twelve tribes.
- quoted (Deut 33:16): “the good will of Him that dwelt in the bush” → “a benevolência daquele que habitou na sarça”
- flag: "Pai das luzes" and "Sol da justiça" follow the Bíblia Livre's Tg 1:17 and Ml 4:2.

### 608 Deut 33:24-25 Strength to believers.
- quoted (Eph 6:15): “shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace” → “calçados os pés com a prontidão do evangelho da paz”
- quoted (Deut 33:25): “As thy days, so shall thy strength” → “tua força será como os teus dias”
- flag: Quote 1: the Bíblia Livre puts "strength" first ("tua força será como os teus dias"), reversing the KJV's order; Henry's sense kept.
- flag: Henry's note on the word "day" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "dias".

### 609 Deut 33:26-29 The excellency of Israel.
- quoted (Deut 33:28): “shall dwell in safety alone” → “habitará confiante sozinho”
- flag: "os braços eternos", "mais que vencedores … por meio de Cristo, que os amou", "toda a armadura de Deus" and "o Deus de paz esmagará Satanás" follow the Bíblia Livre's Dt 33:27, Rm 8:37, Ef 6:11 and Rm 16:20 (unlisted).

### 611 Deut 34:5-8 The death and burial of Moses, The mourning of the people.
- flag: Henry's quotation "at the mouth of the Lord" (the KJV margin of Dt 34:5) given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "conforme o dito do SENHOR" (unlisted).

### 612 Deut 34:9-12 Joshua succeeds Moses, The praise of Moses.
- quoted (Heb 7:19): “the law made nothing perfect” → “a Lei não tornou perfeito nada”
- flag: "não em tábuas de pedra, mas em tábuas de carne do coração" follows the Bíblia Livre's 2 Co 3:3 (unlisted).
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 613 Josh 1:1-4 The Lord appoints Joshua to succeed Moses.
- quoted (Phil 2:7): “took upon him the form of a servant” → “a forma de servo”
- flag: "Levanta-te, passa o Jordão" translates Henry's "Arise, go over Jordan" (Js 1:2); the Bíblia Livre has "levanta-te, pois, agora, e passa este Jordão".
- flag: Quote 0: "tomou sobre si" for Henry's "took upon him"; only "a forma de servo" is the Bíblia Livre's ("tomando a forma de servo").

### 614 Josh 1:5-9 God promises to assist Joshua.
- translated, not quoted (Josh 1:8; Henry's "he is charged to meditate therein" refers to the law; the Bíblia Livre's "meditarás nele" (the book) does not fit the sentence, so it is translated.): “meditate therein day and night”
- flag: Js 1:8: the Bíblia Livre has "de dia e de noite meditarás nele" (the book of the law); Henry's indirect wording is translated with "nela" (the law).

### 615 Josh 1:10-15 Preparation to pass over Jordan.
- flag: "Passareis o Jordão e possuireis a terra" translates Henry's paraphrase of Js 1:11; the Bíblia Livre has "passareis o Jordão, para que entreis a possuir a terra".

### 616 Josh 1:16-18 The people promise to obey Joshua.
- quoted (Josh 1:16): “All that thou commandest us” → “todas as coisas que nos mandaste”
- quoted (Josh 1:16): “whithersoever thou sendest us we will go” → “iremos aonde quer que nos mandares”
- flag: "Capitão da nossa salvação" translates the KJV's Hb 2:10; the Bíblia Livre has "o Autor da salvação deles".

### 617 Josh 2:1-7 Rahab receives and hides two Israelites.

### 618 Josh 2:8-21 Rahab and the spies.
