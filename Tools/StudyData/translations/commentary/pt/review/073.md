# To review: pt commentary batch 073

### 3173 Mark 13:28-37 Watchfulness urged.
- quoted (Mark 13:32): “of that day and that hour knoweth no man” → “daquele dia e hora ninguém sabe”

### 3174 Mark 14:1-11 Christ anointed at Bethany.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Isa 53:12: 'derramou sua alma na morte' (Bíblia Livre); Henry's question 'Did Christ pour out his soul…?' recast as a statement followed by his question, to keep the Bible's words.

### 3175 Mark 14:12-21 The passover, Jesus declares that Judas would betray him.
- quoted (Mark 14:21): “woe to that man by whom” → “ai daquele homem por quem”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mark 14:21: 'o Filho do homem vai, como está escrito sobre ele' (Bíblia Livre).

### 3176 Mark 14:22-31 The Lord's supper instituted.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mark 14:24: 'derramado por muitos' (Bíblia Livre).

### 3177 Mark 14:32-42 Christ's agony in the garden.
- quoted (Mark 14:36): “Nevertheless, not what I will, but what thou wilt” → “porém não se faça o que eu quero, mas sim o que tu queres”
- flag: Henry comments on the KJV's words 'sore amazed' (Mark 14:33) as 'not used in St. Matthew'; the Bíblia Livre's 'começou a ficar muito apavorado' (unlisted quotation, used verbatim) carries the point.
- flag: 'the terrors of God … and he allowed him to contemplate them': English 'he allowed him' is ambiguous (God allowed Christ); translated literally ('ele lhe permitiu').

### 3179 Mark 14:53-65 Christ before the high priest.
- flag: 'Art thou the Son of the Blessed?' (Mark 14:61, without 'the Christ') translated: the Bíblia Livre's 'És tu o Cristo, o Filho daquele que é Bendito?' doesn't match Henry's shortened question.

### 3180 Mark 14:66-72 Peter denies Christ.
- quoted (1Cor 10:12): “Let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall” → “aquele que pensa estar de pé, olhe para que não caia”

### 3181 Mark 15:1-14 Christ before Pilate.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mark 15:13-14: 'Crucifica-o!' (Bíblia Livre), doubled as in Henry.
- flag: sensitive: Henry says that by delivering up the King they delivered up the kingdom of God, which was 'taken from them, and given to another nation'; translated in full.

### 3183 Mark 15:22-32 The crucifixion.
- translated, not quoted (Mark 15:30; Henry reports the taunt indirectly ('They told him to come down from the cross'); the Bíblia Livre's imperative 'desce da cruz!' cannot stand in indirect speech.): “come down from the cross”
- flag: unlisted quotations (Bíblia Livre): Mark 15:22 'o lugar da caveira' (KJV 'place of a scull'); Mark 15:28 'contado com os malfeitores' (KJV 'transgressors'), used both times Henry says 'numbered with the transgressors'.

### 3184 Mark 15:33-41 The death of Christ.
- quoted (Mark 15:38): “the veil of the temple was rent” → “o véu do Templo se rasgou”
- quoted (Mark 15:38): “from the top to the bottom” → “do alto abaixo”
- quoted (Heb 10:19): “into the holiest by the blood of Jesus” → “Santuário pelo sangue de Jesus”
- flag: English 'for the service saints' lacks a word ('of'); translated as 'para o serviço dos santos'.
- flag: unlisted quotations (Bíblia Livre): Mal 4:2 'o Sol da justiça'; Heb 10:20 'caminho novo e vivo'. Quote 2: the Bíblia Livre has 'Santuário' for the KJV's 'holiest'.
- flag: sensitive: 'The Jews were doing their utmost to extinguish the Sun of Righteousness'; the rent veil 'spake terror to the unbelieving Jews, and was a sign of the destruction of their church and nation'; translated in full.

### 3185 Mark 15:42-47 His body buried.
- quoted (Mark 15:43): “waited for the kingdom of God” → “esperava o Reino de Deus”
- flag: 'planted in the likeness of it' follows the KJV's Rom 6:5 ('planted together in the likeness of his death'); the Bíblia Livre has 'unidos a ele na semelhança'. Henry's 'planted' kept ('plantados').

### 3186 Mark 16:1-8 Christ's resurrection made known the women.
- quoted (Mark 16:6): “He is risen, he is not here” → “já ressuscitou, não está aqui”
- flag: English title 'made known the women' lacks 'to'; translated as 'to the women'.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mark 16:6: 'o lugar onde o puseram' (Bíblia Livre). 'The men ran' follows the English as written (Mark 16:8 speaks of the women; possibly a slip for 'they' or 'the women'); not changed.

### 3188 Mark 16:14-18 His commission to the apostles.
- flag: 'in the bonds of iniquity' follows the KJV's Acts 8:23; the Bíblia Livre has 'atado em injustiça'. Translated as Henry wrote it.

### 3189 Mark 16:19-20 Christ's ascension.
- quoted (Mark 16:19): “After the Lord had spoken” → “o Senhor, depois de haver lhes falado”
- quoted (Mark 16:19): “the right hand of God” → “à direita de Deus”
- quoted (Mark 16:20): “went forth, and preached every where” → “saíram e pregaram por todas as partes”
- quoted (Rom 1:16): “is the power of God unto salvation” → “é o poder de Deus para a salvação”
- flag: 'the sound went forth unto the ends of the earth' (Rom 10:18, Ps 19:4) translated: the Bíblia Livre's 'Sua voz saiu por toda a terra' doesn't fit Henry's sentence.

### intro Luke 
- flag: Henry says Luke 'was a Jew' while his name speaks 'his Gentile origin'; translated as written.

### 3191 Luke 1:5-25 Zacharias and Elisabeth.
- quoted (Luke 1:10): “the whole multitude of the people were praying without” → “toda a multidão do povo estava fora orando”
- quoted (Luke 1:17): “He shall go before Him” → “irá adiante dele”
- flag: 'the wisdom of their righteous forefathers' echoes Luke 1:17 ('the wisdom of the just'), where the Bíblia Livre has 'prudência dos justos'; translated as Henry wrote it.

### 3192 Luke 1:26-38 Christ's birth announced.
- quoted (1Tim 3:16): “the mystery of godliness, God” → “mistério da devoção divina: Deus”
- quoted (1Tim 3:16): “manifest in the flesh” → “revelado em carne”
- quoted (Luke 1:38): “unto me according to thy word” → “cumpra-se em mim conforme a tua palavra”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has 'devoção divina' for the KJV's 'godliness' and 'revelado' for 'manifest'.
- flag: unlisted quotations (Bíblia Livre): Luke 1:30 'encontrado graça diante de Deus' (adapted: 'encontraste'); Luke 1:32 'Filho do Altíssimo'; Luke 1:37 'para Deus nada será impossível'. Henry's paraphrase of the greeting, 'Hail, thou that art…', translated; the Bíblia Livre's Luke 1:28 has 'Alegra-te, agraciada'.
- flag: sensitive: 'though we are not to pray to her' (Mary), an implicit reference to Roman Catholic practice; translated in full.

### 3193 Luke 1:39-56 Interview of Mary and Elisabeth.
- quoted (Luke 1:41, Luke 1:42): “was filled with the Holy Ghost” → “foi cheia do Espírito Santo”

### 3194 Luke 1:57-66 The birth of John the Baptist.
- quoted (Luke 1:66): “The hand of the Lord was” → “A mão do Senhor estava com”
- flag: English 'he believers, therefore he speaks' is a typo for 'believes' (echoing 2Cor 4:13); translated as 'ele crê, por isso fala'.
- flag: quote 0: Henry's 'was working with John' (KJV 'was with him'); the Bíblia Livre's 'a mão do Senhor estava com' kept, with 'João, operando' for Henry's wording.

### 3195 Luke 1:67-80 The song of Zacharias.
- quoted (2Cor 4:6): “the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ” → “a iluminação do conhecimento da glória de Deus no rosto de Jesus Cristo”
- quoted (Luke 1:79): “in the shadow of death” → “sombra de morte”
- flag: unlisted quotations of Luke 1:79: 'iluminar os que estão sentados nas trevas' and 'guiar os nossos pés pelo caminho da paz' (Bíblia Livre).

### 3196 Luke 2:1-7 The birth of Christ.
- quoted (Gal 4:4): “forth his Son, made of a woman” → “o seu Filho, nascido de mulher, nascido sob a Lei”

### 3197 Luke 2:8-20 It is made known to the shepherds.
- quoted (Luke 2:8): “keeping watch over their flock” → “vigiavam o seu rebanho”
- quoted (Luke 2:14): “Glory to God in the highest” → “Glória nas alturas a Deus”
- quoted (Luke 2:14): “glory to God in the highest” → “glória nas alturas a Deus, paz na terra”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1Tim 1:15: 'uma palavra fiel' … 'digna de toda aceitação' (Bíblia Livre). 'to us is born a Saviour, Christ the Lord' (Luke 2:11, Henry's 'to us' for 'unto you') translated.

### 3200 Luke 2:36-40 Anna prophesies concerning him.
- quoted (Heb 2:17): “to be made like unto his brethren” → “se tornasse semelhante aos irmãos”
- translated, not quoted (Luke 2:40; Henry reshapes the verse into 'nothing but the grace of God was upon him'; the Bíblia Livre's 'a graça de Deus estava sobre ele' cannot keep that word order and stress in Portuguese.): “the grace of God was upon him”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has 'aos irmãos' for the KJV's 'unto his brethren'.
- flag: 'hoary heads … a crown of glory, being found in the way of righteousness' (Prov 16:31) translated: the Bíblia Livre has 'coroa de honra'. 'foolishness bound in their hearts' adapted from Prov 22:15 with the Bíblia Livre's 'tolice amarrada ao coração'.

### 3201 Luke 2:41-52 Christ with the learned men in the temple.
- quoted (Luke 2:49): “I must be about my Father's business” → “devo estar nos negócios de meu Pai”
- flag: Henry gives three renderings of Luke 2:49 ('in my Father's house; at my Father's work; … about my Father's business'); the Bíblia Livre's 'Não sabeis que devo estar nos negócios de meu Pai' supplies the opening and the last, the other two translated.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 17:4: 'Bom é para nós estarmos aqui' (Bíblia Livre).

### 3202 Luke 3:1-14 John the Baptist's ministry.
- quoted (Luke 3:7): “from the wrath to come” → “da ira que está para chegar”
- flag: unlisted quotations (Bíblia Livre): Luke 3:7 'raça de víboras' (KJV 'generation of vipers'); Luke 3:6 'a salvação de Deus'; Luke 3:8 'frutos condizentes com o arrependimento' (KJV 'meet for repentance').

### 3203 Luke 3:15-20 John the Baptist testifies concerning Christ.
- quoted (Luke 3:20): “shut up John in prison” → “encarcerou João na prisão”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Acts 20:27: 'não deixei de vos anunciar todo o conselho de Deus' adapted to the third person ('não deixou de anunciar todo o conselho de Deus'); translated with the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3204 Luke 3:21-22 The baptism of Christ.
- quoted (Luke 3:22): “The Holy Ghost descended in a bodily shape like a dove upon him” → “o Espírito Santo desceu sobre ele em forma corpórea, como pomba”
- translated, not quoted (Luke 9:35; Listed with Luke 9:35, but Henry here quotes Luke 3:22 ('there came a voice from heaven'); its Bíblia Livre words 'veio do céu uma voz' are used, which are not in Luke 9:35.): “there came a voice”
- flag: quote 1 uses Luke 3:22 (Bíblia Livre: 'veio do céu uma voz'). Unlisted quotation of 2Pet 1:17: 'magnífica glória' (KJV 'excellent glory').
