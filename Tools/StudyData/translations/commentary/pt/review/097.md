# To review: pt commentary batch 097

### 3917 James 2:1-13 All professions of faith are vain, if not producing love and justice to others.
- flag: Source typo: "more attention that usually is the case" read as "than".
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Tg 2:1 ("acepção de pessoas"), Tg 2:5 ("para serem ricos na fé") and Tg 2:8 ("lei real"). KJV "the Lord of glory" (Tg 2:1) is "nosso glorioso Senhor" there; Henry's title translated "o Senhor da glória".

### 3918 James 2:14-26 The necessity of good works to prove the sincerity of faith, which otherwise will be of no more advantage than the faith of devils.
- quoted (James 2:23): “Abraham believed God, and it was” → “Abraão creu em Deus, e isso lhe foi considerado como justiça”
- quoted (James 2:24): “how that by works a man is justified” → “que o ser humano é justificado pelas obras”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry quotes the whole clause ("...it was reckoned unto him for righteousness"); the Bíblia Livre's clause is used to its end.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Mt 16:26 ("ganhar o mundo todo" ... "perder a sua alma") and Tg 2:14 ("a fé pode salvá-lo"); the echo of 1 Pe 5:12 ("na qual estais") is fitted to Henry's first person.

### 3919 James 3:1-12 Cautions against proud behaviour, and the mischief of an unruly tongue.

### 3927 1Pet 1:1-9 The apostle blesses God for his special benefits through Christ.
