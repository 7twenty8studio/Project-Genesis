# To review: pt commentary batch 011

### 316 Ex 36:1-38 The making of the tabernacle, The liberality of the people restrained.
- quoted (2Cor 5:18): “the ministry of reconciliation” → “o ministério da reconciliação”
- flag: "a Palavra que se fez carne" follows the Bíblia Livre's wording of Jo 1:14 ("aquela Palavra se fez carne"); Henry's point that the Greek says "tabernacled" is translated (the Bíblia Livre has "habitou entre nós").

### 317 Ex 37:1-29 The making of the ark, and the furniture of the tabernacle.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jo 1:29 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("o Cordeiro de Deus, que tira o pecado do mundo"); Henry writes "sins" (plural).
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mt 5:6 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("têm fome e sede de justiça").

### 321 Ex 39:1-31 The priests' garments.
- quoted (Rev 19:8): “is the righteousness of saints” → “são as justiças dos santos”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre has the plural "as justiças dos santos" (KJV "the righteousness of saints"); kept verbatim.

### 322 Ex 39:32-43 The tabernacle completed.
- quoted (Col 2:9): “all the fulness of the Godhead bodily” → “habita corporalmente toda a plenitude da divindade”
- flag: Henry's "dwelt" (past) becomes the Bíblia Livre's present "habita" to keep the quotation verbatim.

### 325 Ex 40:34-38 The glory of the Lord fills the tabernacle.
- quoted (Ex 40:34): “The glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle” → “a glória do SENHOR encheu o tabernáculo”
- quoted (Ex 40:35): “Moses was not able to enter into the tent of the congregation” → “não podia Moisés entrar no tabernáculo do testemunho”
- quoted (Prov 8:20): “in the midst of the paths of judgment” → “no meio das veredas do juízo”
- flag: Unlisted quotations of 1 Jo 1:5 and Hb 12:29 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("Deus é luz", "o nosso Deus é um fogo consumidor").
- flag: The Bíblia Livre says "tabernáculo do testemunho" where the KJV has "tent of the congregation".

### 327 Lev 1:3-9 From the herds.
- quoted (Lev 1:3): “at the door of the tabernacle” → “à porta do tabernáculo”
- quoted (Lev 1:4): “put his hand upon the head” → “sua mão sobre a cabeça”
- translated, not quoted (Lev 1:4; Henry recasts Lv 1:4 as the offerer's hope ("that it might be accepted from him, to make atonement for him"); only "para expiar-lhe" of the Bíblia Livre fits, too short to count as the quotation.): “him, to make atonement for him”
- quoted (1Pet 1:2): “sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ” → “aspersão do sangue de Jesus Cristo”

### 329 Lev 2:1-11 
- flag: Henry explains the English word "meat" (any provision); "oferta de manjares" is used, and his remark is applied to "manjar".

### 331 Lev 3:1-5 
- quoted (Ps 69:31): “shall please the Lord better than an ox or bullock” → “isto agradará ao SENHOR mais do que o sacrifício de um boi ou de um bezerro”

### 332 Lev 3:6-17 
- quoted (Lev 3:17): “eat neither fat nor blood” → “nenhuma gordura nem nenhum sangue comereis”
- flag: Henry's indirect "that they should eat neither fat nor blood" is given in the Bíblia Livre's direct words of Lv 3:17.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Cl 1:20 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("feito a paz pelo sangue da sua cruz").

### 333 Lev 4:1-12 
- quoted (Heb 10:1): “a shadow of good things to come” → “uma sombra dos bens futuros”

### 335 Lev 4:22-26 For a ruler.
- quoted (Job 34:32): “which I see not, teach thou me” → “Ensina-me o que não vejo”
- flag: "Show me wherein I have erred" echoes Jó 6:24; given as "Faze-me entender em que errei", adapted from the Bíblia Livre's plural "fazei-me entender em que errei".

### 336 Lev 4:27-35 For any of the people.
- quoted (Heb 10:4): “not possible that the blood of bulls and of goats should take away” → “é impossível que o sangue de touros e de bodes tire”
- flag: Henry's past "it was not possible" follows the Bíblia Livre's present "é impossível" (Hb 10:4).
- flag: Unlisted echoes of Pv 22:2 ("o rico e o pobre se encontram") and 1 Jo 1:7 ("purifica de todo pecado") in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 337 Lev 5:1-13 Concerning various trespasses.
- quoted (Lev 5:1): “he shall bear his iniquity” → “ele levará seu pecado”
- quoted (Lev 5:5): “he hath sinned in that thing” → “confessará aquilo em que pecou”
- quoted (Lev 5:7): “a sin-offering, and the other for a burnt-offering” → “para expiação, e o outro para holocausto”
- flag: Lv 5:1 in the Bíblia Livre says "levará seu pecado" (KJV "bear his iniquity").
- flag: "o outro" (masculine) is kept from the Bíblia Livre's verse ("um para expiação, e o outro para holocausto") although "pombas" is feminine; the verse speaks of "duas rolinhas ou dois pombinhos".

### 338 Lev 5:14-19 Concerning trespasses against the Lord.
- flag: The English repeats a phrase by mistake ("…guilty of. The law of God is so very broad, the occasions of sin in this guilty of. The law of God is so very broad, the occasions of sin in this world…"); translated once. The English also says "against a neighbour" under a heading about trespasses "against the Lord"; kept as written.

### 351 Lev 10:1-2 The sin and death of Nadab and Abihu.

### 352 Lev 10:3-7 Aaron and his sons forbidden to mourn for Nadab and Abihu.
- quoted (1Sam 3:18): “It is the Lord, let him do what seemeth him good” → “o SENHOR é; faça o que bem lhe parecer”

### 353 Lev 10:8-11 Wine forbidden to the priests when in the service of the tabernacle.
- quoted (1Tim 3:3): “not given to wine” → “não dado ao vinho”
- flag: Lv 10:9 given in the Bíblia Livre's words ("não bebereis vinho nem bebida forte", "para que não morrais"); Henry's "Do not drink" is an imperative.
- flag: Henry says "gospel ministers" (plural); singular "o ministro" so the Bíblia Livre's "não dado ao vinho" fits.

### 354 Lev 10:12-20 Of eating the holy things.
- flag: "misericórdia, e não sacrifício" as in the Bíblia Livre's Mt 9:13 (unlisted).

### 356 Lev 12:1-8 Ceremonial purification.
- flag: Henry's quotation "Increase and multiply" given in the Bíblia Livre's words of Gn 1:28 ("Frutificai e multiplicai").
