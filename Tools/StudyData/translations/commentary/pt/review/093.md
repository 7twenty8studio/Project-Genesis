# To review: pt commentary batch 093

### 3806 1Thess 5:12-15 He directs to several particular duties.
- flag: Source "Whatever man do to us" read as "Whatever men do to us" ("os homens").

### 3807 1Thess 5:16-22 
- quoted (Eccl 11:8): “live many years, and rejoice in them all” → “viver muitos anos, e em todos eles se alegrar”
- quoted (Matt 3:11): “with the Holy Ghost and with fire” → “com o Espírito Santo e com fogo”
- translated, not quoted (1Thess 5:21; the Bíblia Livre's imperative "mantende o que é bom" can't stand in Henry's sentence ("must be to hold fast that which is good")): “hold fast that which is good”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Ts 5:19: the Bíblia Livre's "Não apagueis o Espírito." used.
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's "prophesyings" (1 Ts 5:20); the Bíblia Livre has "profecias", used in his explanation.
- flag: "whatever looks like sin" reflects the KJV's "appearance of evil" (1 Ts 5:22); the Bíblia Livre has "qualquer forma de mal".

### 3808 1Thess 5:23-28 And concludes with prayer, greetings, and a blessing.
- quoted (2Cor 8:9): “know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “a graça de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- flag: "transplanted" (of the word of God out of an unknown tongue) rendered "traduzida" (translated), its evident sense here.
- flag: sensitive: "The word of God should not be kept in an unknown tongue" is Henry's implicit polemic against the Latin Scriptures of the Roman Church; translated in full.

### intro 2Thess 
- quoted (2Thess 3:18): “The second epistle to the Thessalonians was written” → “A segunda carta aos tessalonicenses foi escrita”
- flag: Quote 0 is Henry's own opening sentence, matched in the Bíblia Livre's subscription to 2 Ts 3:18; its words were used ("carta" for Henry's "epistle").

### 3809 2Thess 1:1-4 The apostle blesses God for the growing state of the love and patience of the Thessalonians.
- quoted (Prov 4:18): “The path of the just is as the shining light” → “O caminho dos justos é como a luz brilhante”
- quoted (Prov 4:18): “more and more unto the perfect day” → “ilumina até o dia ficar claro por completo”
- flag: Quote 1: the KJV's "shineth more and more unto the perfect day" is in the Bíblia Livre "que vai, e ilumina até o dia ficar claro por completo"; its words used.

### 3810 2Thess 1:5-10 And encourages them to persevere under all their sufferings for Christ, considering his coming at the great day of account.
- quoted (2Thess 1:8): “obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “não obedecem ao Evangelho do nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”

### 3811 2Thess 1:11-12 
- translated, not quoted (2Thess 1:11; the KJV's "the good pleasure of his goodness" is in the Bíblia Livre "todo bom desejo"; Henry's point (it is called grace) rests on the KJV's words): “the good pleasure of his goodness”
- quoted (2Thess 1:11): “the work of faith with power” → “a obra da fé com poder”
- flag: Henry's "the good pleasure of his goodness" follows the KJV of 2 Ts 1:11; the Bíblia Livre's "cumpra todo bom desejo" reads differently, so the phrase was translated.
- flag: "This is to their doing every other good work" is elliptical; read as "this leads to their doing every other good work".

### 3812 2Thess 2:1-4 Cautions against the error that the time of Christ's coming was just at hand. There would first be a general apostasy from the faith, and a revealing of the antichristian man of sin.
- flag: "man of sin" and "son of perdition" (2 Ts 2:3) given as in the Bíblia Livre: "homem do pecado", "filho da perdição"; "falling away" as "apostasia", the Bíblia Livre's word.

### 3813 2Thess 2:5-12 His destruction, and that of those who obey him.
- quoted (2Thess 2:4): “himself above all that is called God, or that is worshipped” → “sobre tudo o que se chama Deus, ou sobre tudo que é adorado”
- quoted (2Thess 2:8): “with the brightness of his coming” → “pelo aparecimento de sua vinda”
- flag: sensitive: Henry identifies the man of sin with "the system of popery, as it prevails in the Romish church, and under the Romish popes", with "lying miracles and all kinds of frauds"; translated in full ("papismo", "igreja romana", "papas romanos").
- flag: The KJV's "brightness of his coming" (2 Ts 2:8) is "aparecimento de sua vinda" in the Bíblia Livre; used both in the quotation and in Henry's earlier "brightness of Christ's coming".
- flag: "mystery of iniquity" given as the Bíblia Livre's "mistério da injustiça" (2 Ts 2:7); "lying wonders" (KJV 2 Ts 2:9) translated "prodígios de mentira" (Bíblia Livre: "milagres falsos").

### 3814 2Thess 2:13-15 The security of the Thessalonians from apostacy; an exhortation to stedfastness, and prayer for them.
- quoted (Rom 11:5): “there is a remnant according to the election of grace” → “um remanescente, escolhido pela graça”
- flag: sensitive: the closing rejection of "all additions, and vain traditions" beyond Scripture is Henry's implicit Protestant polemic (against Roman tradition) on 2 Ts 2:15; translated in full.

### 3815 2Thess 2:16-17 
- quoted (2Thess 2:16): “our Lord Jesus Christ himself” → “ele mesmo, o nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”

### 3816 2Thess 3:1-5 The apostle expresses confidence in the Thessalonians, and prays for them.
- flag: The source repeats "that we may be enabled, without reserve" and then joins it awkwardly to "to the love of God, and the patience of Christ" (2 Ts 3:5, KJV "direct your hearts into the love of God"); translated as it stands, repetition kept.
- flag: "for there is otherwise no confidence in man" alludes to 2 Ts 3:2 ("a fé não é de todos" in the Bíblia Livre); Henry's own sentence.
- flag: "o amor de Deus e a paciência de Cristo" are the Bíblia Livre's words of 2 Ts 3:5 (unlisted).

### 3817 2Thess 3:6-15 He charges them to withdraw from disorderly walkers, particularly from the lazy and busybodies.
- flag: Source typo "The Lords is with you" read as "The Lord is with you".

### 3818 2Thess 3:16-18 And concludes with a prayer for them, and a greeting.
- quoted (2Cor 8:9): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “graça de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”

### 3820 1Tim 1:5-11 The design of the law as given by Moses.
- quoted (1Tim 1:11): “gospel of the blessed God” → “evangelho … do Deus bendito”
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre reads "o evangelho da glória do Deus bendito"; Henry's shorter run (without "glorious") is given as two pieces.

### 3821 1Tim 1:12-17 Of his own conversion and call to the apostleship.
- quoted (1Tim 1:15): “This is a faithful saying” → “Esta é uma palavra fiel”
- flag: "if the Lord had been extreme to mark what was amiss" alludes to Sl 130:3 (KJV); the Bíblia Livre's wording differs, so Henry's phrase was translated.

### 3823 1Tim 2:1-7 Prayer to be made for all persons, since the grace of the gospel makes no difference of ranks or stations.
- quoted (1Tim 2:3): “acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour” → “agradável diante de Deus nosso Salvador”
- quoted (1Tim 2:6): “gave himself a ransom for all” → “se deu como resgate por todos”
- quoted (2Tim 3:7): “come to the knowledge of the truth” → “chegar ao conhecimento da verdade”
- flag: Quote 2 is listed under 2 Tm 3:7, whose Bíblia Livre words ("chegar ao conhecimento da verdade") were used; Henry is commenting on 1 Tm 2:4 ("venham ao conhecimento da verdade").

### 3824 1Tim 2:8-15 How men and women ought to behave, both in their religious and common life.
- translated, not quoted (1Pet 3:4; the Bíblia Livre's "precioso diante de Deus" (1 Pe 3:4, of a meek spirit) doesn't agree with Henry's subject (good works, plural) or his order): “in the sight of God, of great price”
- flag: sensitive: women not allowed to be public teachers, woman's subjection (last in creation, first in the transgression), the authority of men (1 Tm 2:9-15); translated in full.
- flag: "men must pray every where" (1 Tm 2:8): rendered "os homens devem orar em todo lugar", close to the Bíblia Livre's "que os homens orem em todo lugar".
- flag: "saved in child-bearing, or with child-bearing": Henry weighs the KJV's preposition (1 Tm 2:15); the Bíblia Livre has "se salvará tendo parto"; rendered "no parto, ou com o parto".

### 3825 1Tim 3:1-7 The qualifications and behaviour of gospel bishops.
- quoted (Matt 28:20): “Lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world” → “eis que eu estou convosco todos os dias, até o fim dos tempos”

### 3827 1Tim 3:14-16 The reason of writing about these, and other church affairs.
- quoted (1Tim 3:16): “was manifest in the flesh” → “foi revelado em carne”
- quoted (1Tim 3:16): “God was manifest in the flesh” → “Deus foi revelado em carne”
- quoted (Titus 2:14, Titus 2:15): “purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works. These” → “para nos libertar de toda injustiça, e para purificar para si mesmo um povo particular, zeloso de boas obras”
- flag: "the pillar and ground of truth" (1 Tm 3:15): the Bíblia Livre's "a coluna e o alicerce da verdade" used (unlisted quotation).
- flag: "mystery of godliness" rendered "mistério da piedade" in Henry's sentence; the Bíblia Livre has "mistério da devoção divina".
- flag: Quote 2's listed run ends with "These", which is Henry's own "These doctrines", not Tt 2:15; the run used includes Henry's preceding "to redeem us from all iniquity", also Tt 2:14.

### 3829 1Tim 4:6-10 Several directions, with motives for due discharge of duties.
- flag: Henry says "Christ" is "the Saviour of all men" (1 Tm 4:10, where the text says "the living God"); kept as he wrote it.

### 3830 1Tim 4:11-16 
- flag: Source typo "wordly" read as "worldly" ("mundanos").
- flag: "profiting" (1 Tm 4:15, KJV) rendered "progresso", the Bíblia Livre's word; "o seu progresso seja visível por todos" follows its wording (unlisted quotation, person changed from "teu").

### 3832 1Tim 5:3-8 And as to poor widows.
- quoted (1Tim 5:3): “Honour widows that are widows indeed” → “Honra as viúvas que são verdadeiramente viúvas”
- flag: "dead while they live" and "worse than infidels" (1 Tm 5:6, 8) translated in Henry's plural; the Bíblia Livre has "enquanto vive, está morta" and "negou a fé, e é pior que um incrédulo".

### 3834 1Tim 5:17-25 The respect to be paid to elders. Timothy is to take care in rebuking offenders, in ordaining ministers, and as to his own health.
- quoted (1Tim 5:22): “of other men's sins. Keep thyself pure” → “dos pecados alheios … conserva-te puro”
- quoted (1Tim 5:24): “some men's sins are open” → “os pecados de algumas pessoas são evidentes”
- quoted (1Cor 4:5): “will bring to light the hidden things of darkness” → “trará à luz as coisas ocultas nas trevas, e manifestará as intenções dos corações”
- flag: Quote 2: Henry's "the counsels of all hearts" is matched by the Bíblia Livre's "as intenções dos corações" (no "all").

### 3835 1Tim 6:1-5 The duty of Christians towards believing, as well as other masters.
- quoted (1Tim 6:3): “the words of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “palavras do nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- quoted (1Tim 6:4): “envy, strife, railings, evil-surmisings” → “invejas, brigas, blasfêmias, suspeitas maldosas”
- flag: sensitive: the duty of slaves ("servants") to their heathen and believing masters (1 Tm 6:1-2), translated in full.
- flag: The source ends with "(1Ti 6:6-10)", apparently the next section's heading reference; kept as "(1 Tm 6:6-10)".

### 3836 1Tim 6:6-10 The advantage of godliness with contentment.
- flag: Henry contrasts "they that are rich" with the KJV's "they that will be rich" (1 Tm 6:9); the Bíblia Livre's "os que querem ser ricos" carries the same point and was used (unlisted quotation).
- flag: "the love of money" given as "o amor ao dinheiro", the Bíblia Livre's words in 1 Tm 6:10.

### 3838 1Tim 6:17-21 The apostle repeats his warning to the rich, and closes with a blessing.
- translated, not quoted (1Tim 6:19; Henry's "lay hold on" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre's 1 Tm 6:19 has "obtenham a vida eterna" (third person), which doesn't fit "let us"): “lay hold on eternal life”
- flag: "not true science" plays on the KJV's "science falsely so called" (1 Tm 6:20); the Bíblia Livre has "conhecimento"; rendered "ciência" to keep Henry's word, with "conhecimento" for his "real knowledge".
