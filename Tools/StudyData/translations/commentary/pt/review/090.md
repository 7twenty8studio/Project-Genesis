# To review: pt commentary batch 090

### 3718 Gal 3:6-14 This doctrine established from the example of Abraham.
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “redeemed us from the curse of the law; being made” → “nos resgatou da maldição da Lei”
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “made a curse for us” → “se fazer maldição para o nosso benefício”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “a fugir da ira futura”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rom 8:32: "nem mesmo ao seu próprio Filho poupou" (Bíblia Livre).
- flag: Henry's "being made sin, or a sin-offering, for us" (from 2 Co 5:21) is translated within his sentence; the Bíblia Livre's "se fazer maldição para o nosso benefício" carries Gl 3:13.

### 3719 Gal 3:15-18 From the covenant of promises, which the law could not disannul.
- flag: Henry's "his Seed" (Gl 3:16, Christ) is given as "Descendência", the Bíblia Livre's word there; "spiritual seed" as "descendência espiritual".
- flag: Henry's "Rightly to divide the word of truth" (2 Tm 2:15) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "usar bem a palavra da verdade".

### 3720 Gal 3:19-22 The law was a school master to lead them to Christ.
- quoted (Gal 3:19): “wherefore then serveth the law” → “para que, pois, é a Lei”
- quoted (Gal 3:19): “the hand of a mediator” → “mão de um mediador”
- quoted (Gal 3:22): “the promise by faith of Jesus Christ, might be given” → “que a promessa fosse dada aos crentes por meio da fé em Jesus Cristo”
- flag: Title: the KJV's "schoolmaster" (Gl 3:24) is given as "tutor", the Bíblia Livre's word.
- flag: Henry's "by … the hand of a mediator" reads "pela mão de um mediador"; the quoted words "mão de um mediador" are the Bíblia Livre's.

### 3721 Gal 3:23-25 
- translated, not quoted (Gal 3:24; Henry puts Gl 3:24's "we might be justified by faith" into the third person ("they"); the Bíblia Livre's first-person "pela fé fossemos justificados" cannot stand in his sentence.): “might be justified by faith”
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's "schoolmaster" (Gl 3:24) as "properly … a servant, to lead to Christ"; the Bíblia Livre has "tutor em condução a Cristo". His explanation is translated as written.
- flag: The English has the typo "wordly" for "worldly"; translated "mundanos".

### 3722 Gal 3:26-29 Under the gospel state true believers are all one in Christ.
- quoted (Rom 8:9): “if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none” → “se alguém não tem o Espírito de Cristo, esse não lhe pertence”

### 3723 Gal 4:1-7 The folly of returning to legal observances for justification.
- flag: Henry's "tutors and governors" (Gl 4:2) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "tutores e administradores".

### 3725 Gal 4:12-18 The apostle reasons against following false teachers.
- quoted (Gal 4:18): “always in a good thing” → “para o bem sempre”
- flag: Henry's "to be zealous always in a good thing" (Gl 4:18) follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "mostrar interesse para o bem sempre". Its words are used, with "ser zeloso" kept for Henry's "zealous", which his next sentence ("this zeal") takes up.

### 3727 Gal 4:21-27 And then explains the difference between what is to be expected from the law, and from the gospel.
- flag: Henry's "allegory" follows the KJV of Gl 4:24; the Bíblia Livre has "Isto serve de ilustração". Translated "alegoria" to keep his explanation.

### 3728 Gal 4:28-31 
- quoted (Gal 4:31): “then, brethren, we are not children” → “Portanto, irmãos, não somos filhos da escrava, mas sim da livre”

### 3729 Gal 5:1-6 An earnest exhortation to stand fast in the liberty of the gospel.
- quoted (Gal 5:5): “through the Spirit, wait for the hope of righteousness by faith” → “por meio do Espírito, pela fé… a esperança da justiça”
- flag: The source text opens with what is the next section's heading ("And to walk in the Spirit… (16-26)"), set off by blank lines; kept and translated in place.
- flag: Henry's "wait" takes the third person ("aguardam") in his sentence; the rest is the Bíblia Livre's words of Gl 5:5, in two runs.
- flag: "Faith working by love" (Gl 5:6) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "opera por meio do amor".

### 3731 Gal 5:13-15 To take heed of indulging a sinful temper.
- quoted (Gal 5:14): “all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself” → “toda a Lei se cumpre em uma só regra, que é: Amarás ao teu próximo como a ti mesmo”
- flag: Henry's "a doctrine according to godliness, 1Ti 6:3" is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "doutrina que é conforme a devoção divina".
- flag: The KJV's "in one word" (Gl 5:14) is "em uma só regra" in the Bíblia Livre.

### 3732 Gal 5:16-26 
- quoted (Gal 5:19): “The works of the flesh are” → “As obras da carne são”
- quoted (Gal 5:19): “the works of the flesh” → “as obras da carne”
- flag: "Mortify the deeds of the body" (Rm 8:13) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "fazer morrer as ações do corpo".
- flag: Henry's "conversation" in the old sense of manner of life is translated "proceder".

### 3733 Gal 6:1-5 Exhortations to meekness, gentleness, and humility.
- translated, not quoted (Gal 6:2; Henry turns Gl 6:2's "ye shall fulfil" into "we shall fulfil"; the Bíblia Livre's "cumprireis a lei de Cristo" cannot stand in his first-person sentence.): “fulfil the law of Christ”
- flag: "No man can pay a ransom for his brother" alludes to Sl 49:7; the Bíblia Livre's wording ("ninguém pode livrar o seu irmão, nem pagar a Deus o seu resgate") differs, so Henry's phrase is translated.

### 3734 Gal 6:6-11 To kindness towards all men, especially believers.
- quoted (Gal 6:8): “shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting” → “do Espírito colherá a vida eterna”
- flag: Henry's plural "those who … shall of the Spirit reap" is rendered with the singular "quem … colherá" so the Bíblia Livre's words of Gl 6:8 stand unchanged.

### 3735 Gal 6:12-15 The Galatians guarded against the judaizing teachers.
- quoted (Eph 2:10): “created in Christ Jesus unto good works” → “criados em Cristo Jesus para as boas obras”
- flag: "Old things are passed away" (2 Co 5:17) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "as coisas velhas já passaram".

### 3736 Gal 6:16-18 A solemn blessing.
- quoted (Gal 6:16): “walk according to this rule” → “andarem conforme esta regra”
- quoted (Gal 6:17): “body the marks of the Lord Jesus” → “corpo as marcas do Senhor Jesus”
- quoted (Gal 6:18): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “graça do nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”

### 3737 Eph 1:1-2 A salutation, and an account of saving blessings, as prepared in God's eternal election, as purchased by Christ's blood.
- quoted (2John 1:3): “from God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ” → “de Deus Pai, e do Senhor Jesus Cristo”

### 3738 Eph 1:3-8 
- quoted (Eph 1:4): “before the foundation of the world” → “antes da fundação do mundo”
- quoted (Gal 3:26): “children of God by faith in Christ Jesus” → “filhos de Deus por meio da fé em Cristo Jesus”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rom 8:32: "nem mesmo ao seu próprio Filho poupou" (Bíblia Livre).

### 3739 Eph 1:9-14 And as conveyed in effectual calling: this is applied to the believing Jews, and to the believing Gentiles.
- quoted (Eph 1:9): “according to his good pleasure” → “conforme o seu bom prazer”
- quoted (Eph 1:14): “unto the praise of his glory” → “para louvor da sua glória”

### 3740 Eph 1:15-23 The apostle thanks God for their faith and love, and prays for the continuance of their knowledge and hope, with respect to the heavenly inheritance, and to God's powerful working in them.
- flag: The English mixes tenses ("if we dispute less, and prayed more"); translated as one conditional ("se disputássemos menos e orássemos mais").

### 3741 Eph 2:1-10 The riches of God's grace towards men, shown from their deplorable state by nature, and the happy change Divine grace makes in them.
- quoted (Eph 2:1): “dead in trespasses and sins” → “em ofensas e pecados”
- quoted (Eph 2:9): “not of works, lest any man should boast” → “não por obras, para que ninguém tenha orgulho de si mesmo”
- flag: Henry's singular "A man dead in trespasses and sins" keeps "morto"; the quoted run is the Bíblia Livre's "em ofensas e pecados" (Ef 2:1).
- flag: Children of disobedience" and "children of wrath" (Ef 2:2-3) are given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "filhos da desobediência", "filhos da ira".

### 3742 Eph 2:11-13 The Ephesians called to reflect on their state of heathenism.
- quoted (Eph 2:12): “of promise, having no hope” → “da promessa, não tendo esperança”
- flag: "Covenant of promise" is given as "pacto da promessa", after the Bíblia Livre's "pactos da promessa" in Ef 2:12, since the quotation runs on from it.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ps 119:155: "A salvação está longe dos perversos" (Bíblia Livre).

### 3744 Eph 2:19-22 
- flag: Henry's "habitations of God by the Spirit" (Ef 2:22) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "morada de Deus em Espírito".

### 3746 Eph 3:8-12 Also the noble purposes answered by it.
- quoted (Eph 3:8): “the unsearchable riches of Christ” → “as inimagináveis riquezas de Cristo”
- flag: The KJV's "unsearchable" (Ef 3:8) is "inimagináveis" in the Bíblia Livre; used in the quotation and in Henry's echo ("tão inimagináveis").
