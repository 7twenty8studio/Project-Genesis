# To review: pt commentary batch 020

### 682 Josh 24:15-28 Joshua renews the covenant between the people and God.
- quoted (John 4:24, John 4:25): “worship him in spirit and in truth” → “os que o adoram … em espírito e em verdade”
- flag: "Escolhei hoje a quem sirvais" follows the Bíblia Livre's Js 24:15 (unlisted; it adds "hoje", which the KJV also has but Henry leaves out).
- flag: "nós, pois, também serviremos ao SENHOR" follows the Bíblia Livre's Js 24:18 (unlisted).
- flag: "A mente carnal do homem é inimizade contra Deus" echoes Rm 8:7, translated from Henry's KJV wording (the Bíblia Livre has "a mentalidade da carne").

### 683 Josh 24:29-33 Joshua's death, Joseph's bones buried, The state of Israel.
- quoted (Rev 1:5, Rev 1:6): “Unto him that loved us, and washed us from our sins in his own blood, and hath made us kings and priests unto God and his Father, to him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen” → “àquele que nos amou, e em seu sangue nos lavou de nossos pecados; E nos fez reis e sacerdotes a Deus e seu Pai; a ele seja a glória e o poder para todo o sempre, Amém”

- flag: Echoes of At 13:36-37 ("served their generation… saw no corruption") translated from Henry's KJV wording; the Bíblia Livre has "degradação".

### 687 Judg 2:1-5 The angel of the Lord rebukes the people.

### 696 Judg 4:17-24 Sisera put to death by Jael.

### 698 Judg 5:6-11 The distress and deliverance of Israel.

### 700 Judg 5:24-31 Sisera's mother disappointed.

### 703 Judg 6:11-24 Gideon set to deliver Israel.
- flag: The English has a comma before "The Angel turned…" ("his Spirit there, The Angel"); read as the end of a sentence and given a full stop.

### 708 Judg 7:16-22 The defeat of the Midianites.

### 711 Judg 8:4-12 Succoth and Penuel refuse to relieve Gideon.

### 717 Judg 9:7-21 Jotham rebukes the Shechemites.

### 719 Judg 9:30-49 Abimelech destroys Shechem.

- flag: The section ends with a bare "(Jdg 9:50-57)", kept as in the source; it may be an outline heading left in the text.

### 720 Judg 9:50-57 Abimelech slain.
