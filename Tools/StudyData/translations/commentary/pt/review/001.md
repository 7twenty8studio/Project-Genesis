# To review: pt commentary batch 001

### 1 Gen 1:1-2 God creates heaven and earth.
- quoted (Gen 1:2): “was without form, and void” → “estava desordenada e vazia”
- flag: Henry has "the world was without form, and void"; the Bíblia Livre's subject (a terra) is kept so the quotation stays verbatim.

### 2 Gen 1:3-5 The creation of light.
- quoted (Gen 1:3): “God said, Let there be light” → “disse Deus: Haja luz”
- quoted (Gen 1:4): “God divided the light from the darkness” → “separou Deus a luz das trevas”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2 Co 6:14, in the Bíblia Livre's words ("que parceria tem a luz com as trevas?")
- flag: unlisted allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: Ef 5:8 ("luz no Senhor") and Sl 1:2 ("em sua Lei de dia e de noite")

### 6 Gen 1:26-28 Man created in the image of God.
- quoted (Gen 1:26): “God said, "Let us make man” → “disse Deus … Façamos ao ser humano”
- flag: the Bíblia Livre's "ser humano" stands for Henry's "man" inside the quotation; elsewhere "o homem" as Henry writes

### 8 Gen 1:31-31 The work of creation ended and approved.
- quoted (Ps 103:22): “All his works, in all places of his dominion, bless” → “todas as suas obras, em todos os lugares de seu domínio”
- quoted (Ps 104:35): “bless thou the Lord, O my soul” → “Bendizei, ó minha alma, ao SENHOR”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2 Pe 3:13 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("novos céus e nova terra, em que a justiça habita"); also Mt 3:7 "da ira futura"
- flag: quote 1: Henry's words are Sl 103:22 / 104:35; the Bíblia Livre's 104:35 wording ("Bendizei, ó minha alma") was used

### 10 Gen 2:4-7 Particulars about the creation.
- flag: Henry explains the English Bibles' "LORD" in capitals; the Bíblia Livre also prints SENHOR for the divine name, so "SENHOR" is used for his "LORD" while "nas nossas Bíblias em inglês" is kept as he wrote it

### 11 Gen 2:8-14 The planting of the garden of Eden.
- quoted (Rev 2:7): “the tree of life” → “árvore da vida”
- quoted (Gen 2:9): “in the midst of the garden” → “em meio do jardim”
- quoted (Rev 2:7): “the Tree of life” → “árvore da vida”
- quoted (Gen 2:17): “the tree of the knowledge of good and evil” → “árvore do conhecimento do bem e do mal”

### 14 Gen 2:18-25 The animals named, The making of woman, The Divine institution of marriage.
- flag: "a help meet" (KJV Gn 2:18, 20) rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "ajuda idônea"
- flag: allusion to Sl 49:12, 20 ("man being in honour... like the beasts that perish"), translated, not listed

### 15 Gen 3:1-5 The serpent deceives Eve.
- quoted (Gen 3:1): “God said, Ye shall not” → “Deus vos disse”
- translated, not quoted (James 4:7; Henry turns the verse into the first person plural ("let us resist the devil, and he will flee from us"); the Bíblia Livre's "resisti ao diabo, e ele fugirá de vós" is second person): “resist the devil, and he will flee from”
- flag: unlisted allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: Jo 8:44 ("homicida desde o princípio") and Is 14:14 ("semelhante ao Altíssimo")

### 16 Gen 3:6-8 Adam and Eve transgress the Divine command, and fall into sin and misery.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 3:6 in the steps ("Ela viu", "Ela tomou", "E comeu", "E deu também ao seu marido com ela", "E ele comeu"), following the Bíblia Livre's wording
- flag: unlisted allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: Dn 12:2 ("vergonha e o desprezo eterno", here "vergonha e desprezo eterno") and Jó 16:2 ("consoladores miseráveis")
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 17 Gen 3:9-13 God calls upon Adam and Eve to answer.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 3:9: the Bíblia Livre has only "Onde estás?"; Henry's "Adam, where art thou?" (KJV) is kept as "Adão, onde estás?"
- flag: Hb 3:13 "engano do pecado" follows the Bíblia Livre

### 18 Gen 3:14-15 The serpent cursed, The promised Seed.
- flag: "the Seed of the woman" (KJV Gn 3:15) kept as "a Semente da mulher", the usual theological term; the Bíblia Livre's Gn 3:15 has "descendência"
- flag: source oddity: "the drawn of the gospel day" read as "dawn" (alvorecer)
- flag: "breaking of his head" / "bruising his heel": the Bíblia Livre's Gn 3:15 has "ferirá a cabeça" / "ferirás o calcanhar"; "ferir" is used for the heel, "esmagamento" for Henry's breaking of the head

### 19 Gen 3:16-19 The punishment of mankind.
- quoted (Gen 3:16): “He shall rule over thee” → “ele te dominará”
- quoted (Gen 3:18): “eat the herb of the field” → “erva do campo”
- quoted (Acts 2:24): “the pains of death” → “as dores da morte”
- quoted (Gal 4:4): “made under the law” → “nascido sob a Lei”
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “made a curse for us” → “ao se fazer maldição para o nosso benefício”
- flag: Is 53:11: Henry links "travailing pains" with "the travail of Christ's soul" (KJV); the Bíblia Livre has "o trabalho de sua alma", so the link is weaker; rendered "a aflição da alma de Cristo"
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Ef 5:22 ("Mulheres, sujeitai-vos aos vossos próprios maridos"), Is 53:3 ("homem de dores"), Mt 26:38 ("completamente triste"), Fp 2:8 ("obediente até a morte"); Lc 22:44 translated (Bíblia Livre lacks "great")
- flag: quote 4 (Gl 3:13): Henry's "Christ was made a curse for us, he died a cursed death" is recast around the Bíblia Livre's "ao se fazer maldição para o nosso benefício"
- flag: source oddity: "Did subjection came in" read as "come in"

### 20 Gen 3:20-21 The first clothing of mankind.
- quoted (Rom 13:14): “put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ” → “revesti-vos do Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- flag: unlisted allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: Gn 3:21 ("túnicas de peles"), Gn 3:7 ("aventais" of "folhas de figueira"), Gn 12:3 ("todas as famílias da terra serão benditas", here "seriam"), 1 Tm 6:8 ("alimento e algo com que" ... "cobrir")

### 21 Gen 3:22-24 Adam and Eve are driven out from paradise.
- flag: unlisted allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: Gn 3:23 ("lavrasse a terra de que foi tomado", here "lavrar a terra de que fora tomado"), Gn 3:24 ("o caminho da árvore da vida") and Hb 10:20 ("caminho novo e vivo"); "the holiest" rendered "o Santo dos Santos" (the Bíblia Livre's Hb 10:19 has "o Santuário")

### 22 Gen 4:1-7 The birth, employment, and religion of Cain and Abel.
- quoted (Gen 4:1): “said, I have gotten a man from the Lord” → “disse: Obtive um homem pelo SENHOR”
- quoted (Gen 4:7): “If thou doest not well, sin” → “se não fizeres bem, o pecado … está à porta”
- translated, not quoted (Rev 3:20; Henry recasts Ap 3:20 in the third person ("Christ is said to stand at the door"); the Bíblia Livre's words are "eu estou à porta"): “stand at the door”
- flag: "Mr. Dod" is the Puritan John Dod; rendered "o bom Sr. Dod"
- flag: Henry's "the same word signifies sin, and a sacrifice for sin" is about the Hebrew; the Bíblia Livre's Gn 4:7 has "o pecado está à porta", so the explanation still reads naturally

### 23 Gen 4:8-15 Cain murders Abel, The curse of Cain.
- quoted (1John 3:12): “because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous” → “porque as suas obras eram más, e as do seu irmão justas”
- quoted (Matt 12:30): “He that is not with me is against me” → “Quem não é comigo é contra mim”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 11:4 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("mesmo estando morto, ainda fala")
- flag: "Murder is a crying sin" rendered "um pecado que clama aos céus" (the traditional "sins crying to heaven"; cf. Gn 4:10 "clama a mim desde a terra")

### 24 Gen 4:16-18 The conduct of Cain, His family.
- quoted (Gen 4:16): “Cain went out from the presence of the Lord” → “saiu Caim de diante do SENHOR”
- flag: source oddity: Henry's quotation marks are broken ("shaking,' or trembling,'", "vagabond:'"); rendered as 'abalo', 'tremor', 'a terra de um errante'
- flag: "vagabond" echoes KJV Gn 4:12 "a fugitive and a vagabond"; the Bíblia Livre has "errante e fugitivo", so "errante" is used

### 25 Gen 4:19-24 Lamech and his wives, The skill of Cain's descendants.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.
