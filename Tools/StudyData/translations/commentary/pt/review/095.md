# To review: pt commentary batch 095

### 3868 Heb 1:4-14 And in his superiority to all the holy angels.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 1:14: "espíritos servidores" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: Allusions to Hb 1:13 ("estrado de teus pés", Henry changes the person) and Ap 6:2 ("conquering and to conquer"; the Bíblia Livre's "como conquistador, e para que conquistasse" doesn't fit his future tense) translated, not quoted.

### 3869 Heb 2:1-4 The duty of stedfastly adhering to Christ and his gospel.
- quoted (Heb 2:4): “gifts of the Holy Ghost” → “distribuições do Espírito Santo”
- flag: Source oddity: the text opens with what looks like the next section's heading ("His sufferings are no objection against his pre-eminence.") and "(5-9)"; translated and kept as separate paragraphs, as given.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 2:3: "tão grande salvação" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: KJV "gifts of the Holy Ghost" (Hb 2:4) is "distribuições do Espírito Santo" in the Bíblia Livre; used here. Henry's clause "qualified for the work" rendered "sendo eles habilitados".

### 3871 Heb 2:10-13 The reason of his sufferings, and the fitness of them.
- quoted (Heb 2:10): “of their salvation perfect through sufferings” → “quando trouxesse muitos filhos à glória, aperfeiçoasse por meio de aflições o Autor da salvação deles”
- quoted (Heb 2:11): “ashamed to call them brethren” → “não se envergonha de lhes chamar de irmãos”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry quotes Hb 2:10 from "in bringing many sons to glory"; the Bíblia Livre's run is used from "quando trouxesse" (KJV "sufferings" is "aflições" there).

### 3872 Heb 2:14-18 Christ's taking the nature of man, and not his taking the nature of angels, was necessary to his priestly office.
- flag: Source typo: "Not soul ever perished" read as "No soul ever perished" ("Nenhuma alma jamais pereceu").

### 3873 Heb 3:1-6 The superior worth and dignity of Christ above Moses is shown.
- flag: KJV "Apostle ... of our profession" (Hb 3:1) is "Apóstolo ... da nossa declaração de fé" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's phrase rendered to match.

### 3874 Heb 3:7-13 The Hebrews are warned of the sin and danger of unbelief.
- flag: Henry's quoted "Take heed" (Hb 3:12) given as the Bíblia Livre's "Cuidado".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 3:13: "engano do pecado" in the Bíblia Livre's words; the allusion to 1 Co 10:12 is translated (Henry makes it plural).
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 3875 Heb 3:14-19 And of necessity of faith in Christ, and of stedfastly following him.
- quoted (Heb 3:14): “are made partakers of Christ” → “participantes de Cristo”

### 3876 Heb 4:1-10 Humble, cautious fear is urged, lest any should come short of the promised rest, through unbelief.
- flag: "rest" rendered "repouso", the Bíblia Livre's word in Hb 3-4. Henry's "sabbatism" (Greek sabbatismos, Hb 4:9) kept as "sabatismo"; the Bíblia Livre has "um repouso como o do sábado".

### 3877 Heb 4:11-16 Arguments and motives to faith and hope in our approaches to God.
- quoted (Rom 8:3): “his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh” → “o seu próprio Filho em semelhança da carne pecadora”
- flag: Source slip: "it was necessary he should actual experience" lacks "have"; read as "should have actual experience".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 4:16: "com confiança ao trono da graça" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3878 Heb 5:1-10 The office and duty of a high priest abundantly answered in Christ.
- quoted (Heb 5:7): “In the days of his flesh” → “Nos dias de seu corpo carnal”
- quoted (Heb 5:9): “the Author of eternal salvation” → “autor da eterna salvação a todos os que lhe obedecem”
- flag: Quote 0: KJV "in the days of his flesh" (Hb 5:7) is "nos dias de seu corpo carnal" in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's run goes on with "to all that obey him"; the Bíblia Livre's words are used to the end of the clause.

### 3879 Heb 5:11-14 The Christian Hebrews reproved for their little progress in the knowledge of the gospel.
- flag: Henry explains KJV "unskilful" (Hb 5:13): the Bíblia Livre's word is "inexperiente", used here, so his gloss ("want of experience") reads as a near-tautology. KJV "dull of hearing" (Hb 5:11) is "negligentes para ouvir" there; Henry's "dull hearers" rendered "ouvintes tardios".

### 3880 Heb 6:1-8 The Hebrews are urged to go forward in the doctrine of Christ, and the consequences of apostacy, or turning back, are described.
- quoted (Heb 10:26): “have received the knowledge of the truth” → “recebido o conhecimento da verdade”
- quoted (Heb 6:6): “to renew them again unto repentance” → “outra vez renová-los para o arrependimento”

### 3881 Heb 6:9-10 The apostle expresses satisfaction, as to the most of them.
- flag: KJV "things that accompany salvation" (Hb 6:9) is "melhores coisas, relacionadas à salvação" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's phrase translated as "as coisas que acompanham a salvação".

### 3883 Heb 7:1-3 A comparison between the priesthood of Melchizedec and that of Christ.

### 3884 Heb 7:4-10 The excellence of Christ's priesthood above the Levitical priesthood is shown.

### 3885 Heb 7:11-25 This is applied to Christ.
- flag: unlisted quotations of Hb 7:16 ("poder de uma vida indestrutível"; KJV "endless life") and Hb 7:25 ("pode salvar de maneira completa"; KJV "to the uttermost") in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3886 Heb 7:26-28 The faith and hope of the church encouraged from this.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Is 53:9: "nunca fez injustiça, nem houve engano em sua boca" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "violence" is "injustiça" there). Hb 7:26's "harmless" and "undefiled" are the Bíblia Livre's "inocente" and "incontaminado".

### 3889 Heb 9:1-5 The Jewish tabernacle and its utensils.

### 3890 Heb 9:6-10 Their use and meaning.
- flag: "Sins are errors" plays on KJV "errors of the people" (Hb 9:7); the Bíblia Livre has "pecados de ignorância do povo". "who can understand all his errors?" echoes Sl 19:12; translated, not quoted (Henry adds "all").

### 3891 Heb 9:11-14 These fulfilled in Christ.
- quoted (Heb 9:14): “works to serve the living God” → “das obras mortas … ao Deus vivo”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry inserts "or deadly" and the Bíblia Livre has "para servirdes" (second person); its words are quoted on either side of Henry's insertion.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 9:12: "obteve uma redenção eterna" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3893 Heb 9:23-28 The necessity, superior dignity, and power of his priesthood and sacrifice.
