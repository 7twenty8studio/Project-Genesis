# To review: pt commentary batch 054

### 2462 Isa 51:17-23 
- flag: "possesion" is misspelt in the source; translated "posse".

### 2463 Isa 52:1-12 
- flag: Is 52:2, 7: "shake themselves from the dust", "loose themselves from those bands" and "beautiful" follow the Bíblia Livre's words ("Sacode-te do pó", "Solta-te das ataduras", "Como são agradáveis ... os pés"); the ministers are "agradáveis" (KJV "beautiful").
- flag: Is 52:12: Henry's "distrustful haste" rests on the KJV's "ye shall not go out with haste"; the Bíblia Livre has "não saireis apressadamente", which still fits.

### 2464 Isa 52:13-15

- flag: 1 Co 2:7: the Bíblia Livre's "oculta em mistério" stands for the KJV's "in a mystery".
- flag: "never was sorrow like unto his sorrow" echoes Lm 1:12 KJV; translated.

### 2465 Isa 53:1-3

### 2469 Isa 54:6-10 
- flag: "with great mercies, with everlasting kindness" and "mountains and hills" (Is 54:7-10) follow the Bíblia Livre's words ("com grandes misericórdias", "com bondade eterna", "montes ... morros").

### 2470 Isa 54:11-17 
- flag: "Wasters made to destroy" (Henry's quotation marks) is his paraphrase of Is 54:16 KJV "the waster to destroy"; the Bíblia Livre has "o destruidor, para causar ruína"; translated.

### 2471 Isa 55:1-5 
- quoted (Isa 55:3): “Hear, and your soul shall live” → “ouvi, e vossa alma viverá”
- quoted (Isa 55:3): “the sure mercies of David” → “as firmes bondades prometidas a Davi”
- quoted (Isa 55:5): “the Holy One of Israel” → “o Santo de Israel”
- flag: Is 55:3: the Bíblia Livre's "as firmes bondades prometidas a Davi" (KJV "the sure mercies of David") is quoted; Henry's following "mercies" are "misericórdias".
- flag: "the streams that make glad the city of our God" echoes Sl 46:4 (Bíblia Livre "ribeiros alegram a cidade de Deus"); "ribeiros que alegram" follows it, "do nosso Deus" is Henry's.
- flag: "a Leader, a Commander" follows Is 55:4 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "príncipe e governante"; translated.
- flag: "Come, and buy ... Come, and eat" follow Is 55:1 (Bíblia Livre "vinde, comprai, e comei").

### 2472 Isa 55:6-13 
- flag: "God will multiply to pardon" rests on Is 55:7 KJV ("he will abundantly pardon", margin "multiply to pardon"); the Bíblia Livre has "grandemente perdoa"; Henry's wordplay is translated.

### 2473 Isa 56:1-2 
- translated, not quoted (Gal 5:5; Henry turns Gl 5:5's "aguardamos" into the third person ("Those who ... wait"); the Bíblia Livre's words are kept around the changed verb: "por meio do Espírito, aguardam pela fé a esperança da justiça".): “through the Spirit, wait for the hope of righteousness by faith”
- flag: The paragraph opens with ",2", a fragment of the verse range (Is 56:1,2) left over in the source; kept as given.
- flag: Henry applies "the sabbath day" to his readers' own week (the Christian sabbath) without naming it; kept as "o sábado" with no bracketed note, since he doesn't say "Christian sabbath" here.
- flag: "Blessed is the man that keeps his hand" follows Is 56:2 (Bíblia Livre "Bem-aventurado o homem ... guarda sua mão").

### 2476 Isa 57:1-2

### 2477 Isa 57:3-12

### 2478 Isa 57:13-21

- flag: "like the troubled sea" follows Is 57:20 (Bíblia Livre "como o mar bravo"); "the fruit of our lips" Is 57:19 ("os frutos dos lábios").

### 2479 Isa 58:1-2

- flag: Henry's play on "Spare not" (Is 58:1 KJV) rests on the English; the Bíblia Livre has "não te retenhas"; "Não poupes" is translated so the play with "Poupa-te" holds.
- flag: The outline's "with promises to real godliness, and, (Is. 58:3-12) to the keeping the sabbath" is broken around the reference in the source; kept as given.

### 2481 Isa 58:13-14 
- quoted (Ex 20:8): “remember the sabbath day to keep it holy” → “do dia do repouso, para santificá-lo”
- flag: The text opens with ",14", a fragment of the verse range (Is 58:13,14) left over in the source; kept as given.
- flag: Henry moves from the Old Testament sabbath to the Lord's day (Ap 1:10) without saying "Christian sabbath"; kept as "o sábado" / "o dia do Senhor" with no bracketed note. Ex 20:8 is quoted from the Bíblia Livre, which says "dia do repouso" there.
- flag: "It is good to draw near to God" echoes Sl 73:28 (Bíblia Livre "bom me é me aproximar de Deus"); translated.

### 2484 Isa 59:16-21
