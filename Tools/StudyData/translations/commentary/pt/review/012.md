# To review: pt commentary batch 012

### 357 Lev 13:1-17 Directions to the priest to judge concerning leprosy.
- flag: Echoes rendered close to the Bíblia Livre: Rm 3:20 ("o conhecimento do pecado"), At 8:23 ("fel amargo" for KJV "gall of bitterness"), 1 Co 4:5 ("antes do tempo"), Pv 20:9 ("estou limpo de meu pecado"); 1 Tm 5:24 translated from Henry's KJV wording.

### 358 Lev 13:18-44 Further directions.
- flag: The English has "thy all entered by sin", read as a slip for "they all entered".
- flag: "escapado das imundícies do mundo, novamente se envolvem nelas" follows the Bíblia Livre's 2 Pe 2:20 (unlisted).

### 359 Lev 13:45-46 How the leper must be disposed of.
- flag: Henry's "Unclean, unclean" (Lv 13:45) given as "Imundo, imundo"; the Bíblia Livre has "-Imundo! -Imundo!".
- flag: "humilhar-se debaixo da poderosa mão de Deus" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Pe 5:6 (unlisted).

### 361 Lev 14:1-9 Of declaring the leper to be clean.
- quoted (Lev 14:6): “in the blood of the bird that was killed” → “no sangue da ave morta”

### 364 Lev 14:54-57 Summary of the law concerning leprosy.
- quoted (Eph 2:4, Eph 2:5): “God who is rich in mercy, for his great love wherewith he loved us, even when we were dead in sins, hath quickened us” → “Deus, que é rico em misericórdia, pelo seu muito amor com que nos amou, estando nós ainda mortos em nossas ofensas, nos deu vida”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre has "mortos em nossas ofensas" where Henry/KJV has "dead in sins".

### 366 Lev 16:1-14 The great day of atonement.
- flag: The English has "is a statue", read as a slip for "statute" (estatuto).
- flag: The outline line names only (15-34); the source has no (1-14) line here; kept as given.

### 367 Lev 16:15-34 
- quoted (Heb 2:17): “reconciliation for the sins of the people” → “fazer sacrifício de perdão dos pecados do povo”
- quoted (Lev 16:21): “upon the head of the goat” → “sobre a cabeça do bode”
- quoted (Lev 16:22): “shall bear upon him all their iniquities” → “levará sobre si todas as iniquidades deles”
- quoted (John 1:29): “away the sin of the world” → “tira o pecado do mundo”
- quoted (1John 1:9): “is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness” → “é fiel e justo para nos perdoar os pecados e nos purificar de toda injustiça”
- flag: Hb 2:17 in the Bíblia Livre says "fazer sacrifício de perdão dos pecados do povo" where the KJV has "make reconciliation for the sins of the people".

### 371 Lev 20:1-9 Law against sacrificing children to Moloch, Of children that curse their parents.
- flag: Unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Lv 20:7 ("Santificai-vos, pois, e sede santos"), Fp 2:12-13 ("exercei a vossa salvação", "é Deus quem opera em vós"); Lv 20:9 adapted to Henry's past tense.

### 372 Lev 20:10-27 Laws repeated, Holiness enjoined.
- quoted (Titus 2:10): “adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour in all things” → “em tudo … a doutrina de Deus nosso Salvador”
- flag: "regra sobre regra" is the Bíblia Livre's Is 28:10 for KJV "line upon line"; Ef 5:11 follows the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).

### 375 Lev 23:1-3 The feasts of the Lord, The Sabbath.
- translated, not quoted (Lev 23:3; Henry uses "the sabbath of the Lord" as a noun phrase in his own sentence; the Bíblia Livre's "sábado é do SENHOR" is a clause and doesn't fit.): “The sabbath of the Lord”

### 378 Lev 23:23-32 The feast of Trumpets, The day of atonement.
- flag: The English starts with a lower-case "the"; kept lower-case ("o toque").

### 379 Lev 23:33-44 The feast of Tabernacles.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 24:1 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("Ao SENHOR pertence a terra, e sua plenitude").
- flag: Henry's quotation of Lv 23:38 abridges the verse; translated, as the Bíblia Livre's fuller wording ("Além disso dos sábados do SENHOR … e além de todas as vossas ofertas voluntárias") doesn't match his short form.

### 381 Lev 24:10-23 The law of blasphemy, blasphemer is stoned.
- quoted (Heb 10:28): “he that despised Moses' law, died without mercy” → “alguém que rejeita a Lei de Moisés morre sem misericórdia”
- flag: Henry's past tense ("despised… died") follows the Bíblia Livre's present ("rejeita… morre") in Hb 10:28.

### 382 Lev 25:1-7 The sabbath of rest for the land in the seventh year.
- flag: The English has "These statues", read as "statutes"; Lc 12:15 follows the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).

### 383 Lev 25:8-22 The jubilee of the fiftieth year, Oppression forbidden.
- quoted (Lev 25:14): “Ye shall not oppress one another” → “não engane ninguém a seu irmão”
- quoted (Lev 25:17): “thou shalt fear thy God” → “mas terás temor de teu Deus”
- quoted (Lev 25:20): “What shall we eat the seventh year” → “Que comeremos no sétimo ano”
- flag: The English is garbled here: "This was a miracle for an encouragement to all neither sowed or reaped. This was a miracle for an encouragement to all God's people…" (text lost, apparently about the sixth year's crop while they "neither sowed or reaped"); translated once, as the complete sentence.
- flag: Lv 25:14 in the Bíblia Livre says "não engane ninguém a seu irmão" for KJV "Ye shall not oppress one another".

### 385 Lev 25:35-38 Compassion towards the poor.
- flag: "Tu o ampararás" and "Terás temor de teu Deus" are the Bíblia Livre's words of Lv 25:35-36 (unlisted); "para que viva contigo" translates Henry's "that they may live with thee" (the Bíblia Livre: "teu irmão viverá contigo").

### 386 Lev 25:39-55 Laws respecting bondmen, Oppression forbidden.
- quoted (Col 4:1): “servants that which is just and equal” → “de maneira justa e imparcial com os”
- flag: Cl 4:1 in the Bíblia Livre: "procedei de maneira justa e imparcial com os vossos servos" (KJV "give unto your servants that which is just and equal").

### 387 Lev 26:1-13 Promises upon keeping the precepts.
- quoted (1Sam 14:6): “the Lord to save by many or by few” → “ao SENHOR salvar com multidão ou com pouco número”
- quoted (Jer 7:23): “I will be your God, and ye shall be my people” → “eu serei vosso Deus, e vós sereis meu povo”

### 390 Lev 27:1-13 The law concerning vows, Of persons and animals.
- translated, not quoted (Gal 4:18; Henry uses the KJV phrase of Gl 4:18 in his own sentence; the Bíblia Livre ("É bom, porém, mostrar interesse para o bem") says it so differently that it wouldn't fit his point about zeal for the Lord's service.): “It is good to be zealously affected”

### 392 Lev 27:26-33 Devoted things not to be redeemed.
- flag: "devoted" (Hebrew herem) rendered "consagradas por anátema" to keep Henry's distinction from things merely "santificadas".

### 393 Lev 27:34-34 Conclusion.
- quoted (Heb 4:2): “the gospel preached, as well as unto them” → “foi pregado o evangelho, assim como a eles”
- quoted (Heb 10:19): “boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus” → “temos a confiança de entrar no Santuário pelo sangue de Jesus”
- quoted (Heb 10:22): “let us draw near with a true heart” → “aproximemo-nos com um coração sincero em plena certeza de fé”
- flag: Hb 10:19 in the Bíblia Livre says "no Santuário" where the KJV has "into the holiest".

### 394 Num 1:1-43 The numbering of the Israelites.
- quoted (Num 1:20): “were able to go forth to war” → “os que podiam sair à guerra”

### 396 Num 1:47-54 The Levites not numbered with the rest.
- quoted (1Cor 1:9): “the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord” → “a comunhão do seu Filho Jesus Cristo, nosso Senhor”

### 399 Num 3:14-39 The Levites numbered by their families, Their duties.
- quoted (Gen 46:11, Gen 46:12): “the sons of Levi; Gershon, Kohath, and Merari” → “os filhos de Levi: Gérson, Coate, e Merari”
