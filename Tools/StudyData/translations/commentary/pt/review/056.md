# To review: pt commentary batch 056

### 2543 Jer 12:7-13 
- flag: The KJV's "speckled bird" (Jer 12:9) is "ave de rapina de muitas cores" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's image is rendered "ave de muitas cores".

### 2545 Jer 13:1-11 
- flag: The outline has "All ranks should suffer misery, An earnest exhortation" with a comma and capital in the English; kept as is ("…miséria, Uma exortação…").

### 2546 Jer 13:12-17 
- flag: Henry's "bottle" (KJV Jer 13:12) is rendered "odre", as in the Bíblia Livre.

### 2547 Jer 13:18-27 
- translated, not quoted (Jer 13:22; Henry turns the question to the plural ("upon us"); the Bíblia Livre has "Por que estas coisas me aconteceram?" in the singular.): “Wherefore come these things upon”
- flag: sensitive: Henry uses skin colour as the image of sin ("Sin is the blackness of the soul") in commenting on Jer 13:23, "the Ethiopian's skin"; translated in full.
- flag: Jer 13:23: the KJV's "Ethiopian" is "o negro" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's phrase is translated "a pele do etíope".
- flag: Henry's "whether she is determined not be made clean" (missing "to") translated as meant.
- flag: "mighty to save" (Is 63:1) is "poderoso para salvar" in the Bíblia Livre; unlisted quotation of Is 63:1.

### 2549 Jer 14:10-16 
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 2551 Jer 15:1-9 
- flag: sensitive: "saints in heaven do not pray for saints on earth" is aimed at the Roman Catholic teaching on the intercession of saints; translated in full.

### 2556 Jer 16:14-21 
- flag: sensitive: "antichristian oppression" in Henry's day commonly meant the papacy; translated literally ("opressão anticristã").

### 2558 Jer 17:5-11 
- quoted (Jer 17:6): “shall be like the heath” → “será como um arbusto no deserto”
- quoted (Jer 17:9): “is deceitful above all things” → “é mais enganoso que todas as coisas”
- quoted (Jer 17:11): “riches, and not by right” → “o que junta riquezas, mas não com justiça”
- flag: Jer 17:6: the KJV's "heath" is "arbusto" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's following "a sorry shrub" is also "um mísero arbusto", so the word repeats.
- flag: Jer 17:9: Henry's "desperately wicked … it is desperate" plays on the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has only "perverso". Translated as "desesperadamente perverso … é desesperado".
- flag: "Who can understand his errors?" (Ps 19:12) translated in Henry's sentence; the Bíblia Livre has "Quem pode entender seus próprios erros?" (close but not used verbatim).

### 2560 Jer 17:19-27 
- flag: Henry applies the Jewish sabbath of Jer 17 to the duty of his own day without saying "Christian sabbath"; kept as "sábado" with no bracketed note.
- flag: Source punctuation "Let all; by their own example" (stray semicolon) translated as meant.

### 2562 Jer 18:11-17 
- translated, not quoted (Jer 18:15; Henry recasts the verse in his own sentence ("wander from the ancient paths, to walk in ways of their own devising"); the Bíblia Livre's "nas veredas antigas, para que andassem por veredas" doesn't fit; its "veredas antigas" is kept.): “from the ancient paths, to walk”

### 2566 Jer 20:1-6 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jer 20:3: the KJV's "Fear on every side" (Magor-missabib) is "Terror por todos os lados" in the Bíblia Livre, used verbatim; Henry's echo "in fear on every side" follows it ("em terror por todos os lados").

### 2567 Jer 20:7-13 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jer 20:7: Henry offers "persuaded" against the KJV's "deceived"; the Bíblia Livre itself reads "Persuadiste-me … e eu fiquei persuadido; mais forte foste que eu", used verbatim.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rom 12:19: "I will repay" is "eu retribuirei" in the Bíblia Livre, used verbatim.

### 2568 Jer 20:14-18 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Matt 6:13: "Lead us not into temptation" is "E não nos conduzas à tentação" in the Bíblia Livre, used verbatim.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Heb 12:3: "aquele que suportou tal hostilidade dos pecadores contra si mesmo" from the Bíblia Livre (KJV "contradiction of sinners"); the rest of the verse, adapted by Henry, is translated.
- flag: 1Co 10:12 ("think they stand … lest they fall") echoed in Henry's sentence, translated close to the Bíblia Livre ("pensam estar de pé … para que não caiam").

### 2570 Jer 21:11-14 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rom 8:31: "Se Deus é por nós, quem será contra nós?" from the Bíblia Livre, used verbatim.

### 2571 Jer 22:1-9 
- translated, not quoted (Jer 21:11; a common phrase in Henry's own sentence, not a quotation of Jer 21:11.): “The king of Judah”
- translated, not quoted (Jer 22:4; Henry's words are from Jer 22:2, not the listed 22:4; the Bíblia Livre's 22:2 words "sobre o trono de Davi" are used (flagged).): “sitting upon the throne of David”
- flag: Quote 1 is really Jer 22:2 ("o rei de Judá, que te sentas sobre o trono de Davi" in the Bíblia Livre); its words "sobre o trono de Davi" are used verbatim.

### 2572 Jer 22:10-19 
- flag: Henry's "Covetousness, which is the root of all evil" echoes 1Ti 6:10 (KJV "the love of money"); translated in his words, not the Bíblia Livre's.

### 2573 Jer 22:20-30 
- flag: Jer 22:24: the KJV's "signet" is "anel" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's "signets" rendered "anéis de selar".
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 2574 Jer 23:1-8 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jer 23:6: Henry's name "the Lord our Righteousness" is "O SENHOR é nossa justiça" in the Bíblia Livre, used verbatim both times; the following "our Righteousness" is "a nossa Justiça".
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rom 8:34: "Christ has died, yea, rather is risen again" given in the Bíblia Livre's words "Cristo é o que morreu; além disto é o que também ressuscitou".
- flag: Outline has "Judah, The people exhorted" (comma and capital) in the English; kept. "the supply of this Spirit" (probably "his Spirit") translated literally as "deste Espírito".

### 2577 Jer 23:33-40 
- flag: Henry plays on "burden" (KJV "the burden of the LORD", Jer 23:33-40); the Bíblia Livre has "carga" there, so "carga do pecador" keeps the link.

### 2578 Jer 24:1-10 
- flag: sensitive: "the dispersion of the unbelieving Jews, in all the nations of the earth" read as foretold judgment; translated in full.
- flag: Source grammar "The good figs represents" translated as plural.

### 2579 Jer 25:1-7 
- flag: "Rising early" refers to the KJV's "rising early and sending" (Jer 25:3-4); the Bíblia Livre has "insistentemente", so "O madrugar" has no match in its wording.

### 2581 Jer 25:15-29 
- quoted (Jer 25:28): “refuse to take the cup at” → “tomar o copo de tua mão”
- quoted (Isa 39:5): “the word of the Lord of hosts” → “a palavra do SENHOR dos exércitos”
- flag: Jer 25:28: the Bíblia Livre's "copo" is kept in the quotation, while Henry's "cups" elsewhere are "cálices". Source grammar "who had just began" translated as meant.
