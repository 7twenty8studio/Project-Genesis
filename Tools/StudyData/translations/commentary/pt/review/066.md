# To review: pt commentary batch 066

### 2957 Zech 13:7-9 The death of Christ, and the saving of a remnant of the people.
- quoted (Zech 13:7): “Smite the Shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered” → “Fere ao pastor, e as ovelhas se dispersarão”
- quoted (1Pet 1:7): “praise, and honour, and glory, at the appearing” → “louvor, honra, e glória, na revelação”
- flag: sensitive: 'Because of the sin of the Jews in rejecting and crucifying Christ … the Romans would destroy the greater part' (section 2957).
- flag: Henry's "my Fellow" (KJV Zc 13:7) translated "meu Companheiro"; the Bíblia Livre reads 'o homem que é companheiro'.
- flag: Henry's 'Smite the Shepherd' capitalises Shepherd; the Bíblia Livre's 'pastor' is lowercase inside the quotation.
- flag: '1 Pe 1:7' quoted as 'at the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ'; the Bíblia Livre's run ends 'na revelação de Jesus Cristo', Henry's 'nosso Senhor' kept after it.

### 2958 Zech 14:1-7 The sufferings of Jerusalem.

### intro Mal

### 2962 Mal 1:6-14 They are careless in God's institutions.
- quoted (Mal 1:7): “say, The table of the Lord is contemptible” → “A mesa do SENHOR é desprezível”
- quoted (John 4:23): “the Father in Spirit and in truth” → “ao Pai em espírito e em verdade”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mal 1:8 ('And is not this evil?'): Bíblia Livre 'isso não é mal?' used verbatim.
- flag: Henry writes 'in Spirit and in truth' with a capital S; the Bíblia Livre's 'espírito' is lowercase inside the quotation.
- flag: 'the torn' (KJV Ml 1:13) translated 'o dilacerado' for Henry's image; the Bíblia Livre reads 'o roubado' there.

### 2963 Mal 2:1-9 The priests reproved for neglecting their covenant.
- flag: 'The priest's lips should not keep knowledge from his people, but keep it for them' plays on KJV Ml 2:7 'keep knowledge'; the Bíblia Livre also has 'guardar o conhecimento', so 'reter … longe do seu povo, mas guardá-lo para ele' keeps the play.
- flag: 'lightly esteemed' (KJV 1 Sm 2:30) translated 'tidos em pouca conta'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'serão rejeitados'.

### 2964 Mal 2:10-17 The people reproved for their evil practices.
- quoted (Mal 2:16): “The God of Israel saith that he hateth putting away” → “Deus de Israel diz que ele odeia o divórcio”
- quoted (2Pet 3:10): “the day of the Lord will come” → “o dia do Senhor virá”
- flag: Henry's 'putting away' (KJV Ml 2:16) is 'o divórcio' in the Bíblia Livre, used in the quotation; his 'put away the wives' translated 'repudiavam'.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mal 2:17 ('Where is the God of judgement?'): Bíblia Livre 'Onde está o Deus do juízo?' used verbatim.
- flag: 'a godly seed' (KJV Ml 2:15) translated 'uma descendência piedosa'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'uma descendência de Deus'. 'Did not God make one' (KJV 'did not he make one?') translated; the Bíblia Livre reads 'não fez ele somente um'.
- flag: Henry's words 'Consider she is thy wife; thy own' addressed to the husband in the singular (tu).

### 2965 Mal 3:1-6 The coming of Christ.
- quoted (Mal 3:1): “He shall prepare the way before” → “preparará o caminho adiante de”
- quoted (Mal 3:1): “the Messenger of the covenant” → “o mensageiro do pacto”
- quoted (Mal 3:3): “He shall sit as a Refiner” → “se sentará como refinador”
- quoted (Lam 3:22): “we are not consumed; because his compassions fail not” → “não somos consumidos, porque suas misericórdias não têm fim”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Matt 11:3 (Henry's "He that should come"): Bíblia Livre 'aquele que havia de vir' used verbatim, inside Henry's own quotation marks.
- flag: Henry capitalises 'the Messenger of the covenant' and 'a Refiner'; the Bíblia Livre's lowercase kept inside the quotations, his capital kept in his own 'Mensageiro de paz e consolação'.
- flag: 'because his compassions fail not' (KJV Lm 3:22) is 'porque suas misericórdias não têm fim' in the Bíblia Livre.

### 2966 Mal 3:7-12 The Jews reproved for their corruptions.
- quoted (Mal 3:7): “said, Wherein shall we return” → “Em que temos de voltar”
- flag: 'honouring the Lord with his substance' (Pv 3:9) translated 'honrando ao Senhor com os seus bens', close to the Bíblia Livre ('Honra ao SENHOR com teus bens').

### 2967 Mal 3:13-18 God's care of his people; The distinction between the righteous and the wicked.
- quoted (Mal 3:16): “A book of remembrance was written before” → “foi escrito um livro de memória diante”
- flag: The title joins two outline headings with a semicolon; rendered as two sentences.
- flag: 'The saints are God's jewels' (KJV Ml 3:17 'jewels'): the Bíblia Livre reads 'minha propriedade especial'; Henry's 'joias' kept for his image.
- flag: 'As evil communications corrupt good minds and manners' (1 Co 15:33, Henry adds 'minds') translated 'as más conversações corrompem os bons pensamentos e costumes'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'As más companhias corrompem os bons costumes'.

### 2968 Mal 4:1-3 The judgements on the wicked, and the happiness of the righteous.
- flag: 'the Sun of Righteousness' given the Bíblia Livre's 'Sol da justiça' (Ml 4:2); 'calves of the stall' is 'bezerros do curral' there.
- flag: 'in flaming fire' (2 Ts 1:8) translated with the Bíblia Livre's 'labareda de fogo'; 'burn as an oven' (Ml 4:1; Bíblia Livre 'ardendo como forno') translated 'arderá como um forno'.
- flag: 'a Sun and a Shield' (Sl 84:11; Bíblia Livre 'sol e escudo') translated 'um Sol e um Escudo'.

### 2969 Mal 4:4-6 Regard to be had to the law; John the Baptist promised as the forerunner of Messiah.

### 2975 Matt 2:16-18 Herod causes the infants of Bethlehem to be massacred.

### 2976 Matt 2:19-23 Death of Herod, Jesus brought to Nazareth.

- flag: The title joins two headings with a comma ('Death of Herod, Jesus brought to Nazareth'); rendered as two sentences.
- flag: 'if we suffer with him, we shall also be glorified with him' (Rm 8:17) translated in Henry's wording, close to the Bíblia Livre.

### 2977 Matt 3:1-6 John the Baptist, His preaching, manner of life, and baptism.
- quoted (Matt 3:1): “in the wilderness of Judea” → “no deserto da Judeia”
- flag: Henry's "Repent ye." given in the Bíblia Livre's 'Arrependei-vos' (Mt 3:2), inside his own quotation marks; 'Consider your ways' is the Bíblia Livre's 'Considerai vossos caminhos' (Ag 1:5); 'prepare the way of the Lord' is its 'preparar o caminho do Senhor' (Mt 3:3).
- flag: Henry's 'the paths must be made straight, He 12:13' translated; the Bíblia Livre's Hb 12:13 reads 'fazei caminhos retos para os vossos pés'.

### 2978 Matt 3:7-12 John reproves the Pharisees and Sadducees.
- quoted (Matt 3:10): “is hewn down and cast into the fire” → “é cortada e lançada ao fogo”
- translated, not quoted (Acts 2:4; 'the Holy Ghost' is a common name here, not a quotation of Acts 2:4 ('cheios do Espírito Santo'); translated 'o Espírito Santo'.): “with the Holy Ghost”
- flag: sensitive: Henry calls some of the Pharisees 'detestable hypocrites' and the Sadducees 'the scornful infidels of that time and country' (section 2978).
- flag: 'a wrath to come' and 'fruits meet for repentance' given the Bíblia Livre's 'ira futura' (Mt 3:7) and 'frutos condizentes com o arrependimento' (Mt 3:8); 'the unquenchable fire' its 'fogo que nunca se apaga' (Mt 3:12).
- flag: 'carried about with every wind' (Ef 4:14) translated 'levados por todo vento'.

### 2979 Matt 3:13-17 The baptism of Jesus.
- quoted (Gal 5:22): “The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace” → “o fruto do Espírito é: amor, alegria, paz, paciência, benignidade, bondade, fidelidade”
- flag: The fruit of the Spirit given in the Bíblia Livre's words through Gl 5:23 ('mansidão, domínio próprio'); Henry's KJV list ('long-suffering, gentleness … faith, meekness, temperance') differs in wording.
- flag: 'fulfil all righteousness' (Mt 3:15) translated with the Bíblia Livre's 'cumprir toda a justiça'; 'a consuming fire' (Hb 12:29) with its 'um fogo consumidor'.
