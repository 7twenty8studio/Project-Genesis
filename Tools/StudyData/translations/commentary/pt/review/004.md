# To review: pt commentary batch 004

### 86 Gen 20:9-13 Abimelech's rebuke to Abraham.
- flag: allusion to Rm 3:22 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("para todos e sobre todos os que creem"); "hath not whereof to glory" (Rm 4:2) and "that grace may abound" (Rm 6:1) translated in Henry's words

### 88 Gen 21:1-8 Birth of Isaac, Sarah's joy.
- quoted (Gen 21:2): “at the set time of which God had spoken” → “no tempo que Deus lhe havia dito”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's "lhe" (to Abraham) is kept inside the quotation; the verse has no word for KJV "set"

### 90 Gen 21:14-21 Hagar and Ishmael are cast forth, They are relieved and comforted by an angel.
- flag: "a well of water" and "God was with the lad" (Gn 21:19-20) given with the Bíblia Livre's "uma fonte de água" and "foi Deus com o jovem"

### 91 Gen 21:22-34 Abimelech's covenant with Abraham.
- quoted (Gen 13:4): “called on the name of the Lord” → “o nome do SENHOR”
- flag: quote 0: the source lists Gn 13:4, but Henry's "called on the name of the Lord, as the everlasting God" is Gn 21:33; the Bíblia Livre's words of 21:33 are used ("invocou ali o nome do SENHOR Deus eterno"), and the answer gives the words shared with 13:4
- flag: Henry's "Jehovah" in his own sentence kept as "Jeová"

### 92 Gen 22:1-2 God commands Abraham to offer up Isaac.
- flag: source oddity: missing full stop in "secure from trials In Hebrew"
- flag: Henry notes that Hebrew uses one word for "tempt" and "try" (KJV Gn 22:1 "God did tempt Abraham"); the Bíblia Livre has "Deus provou Abraão", which is used
- flag: the words of the command follow the Bíblia Livre's Gn 22:2 ("Toma ... teu filho, teu único, Isaque, a quem amas ... oferece-o ali em holocausto"), broken up as Henry breaks them

### 93 Gen 22:3-10 Abraham's faith and obedience to the Divine command.
- quoted (Gen 22:8): “My son, God will provide” → “Deus se proverá … filho meu”
- quoted (Gen 22:8): “a lamb for a burnt-offering” → “de cordeiro para o holocausto”
- quoted (John 1:29): “which taketh away the sin of the world” → “que tira o pecado do mundo”
- flag: quotes 0-1: the Bíblia Livre puts "filho meu" at the end of Gn 22:8; its order is kept
- flag: "We will come again to you" (Gn 22:5) and "My father" (Gn 22:7) given with the Bíblia Livre's "Voltaremos a vós" and "Meu pai"

### 94 Gen 22:11-14 Another sacrifice is provided instead of Isaac.
- quoted (John 3:18): “of the only begotten Son of God” → “unigênito Filho de Deus”
- flag: "Jehovah-jireh" is the KJV's transliteration in Gn 22:14; the Bíblia Livre translates it ("O SENHOR proverá"), so the Hebrew name is given as "Jeová-Jiré" followed by the Bíblia Livre's words
- flag: "God will provide himself a lamb" given with the Bíblia Livre's Gn 22:8 ("Deus se proverá de cordeiro")

### 95 Gen 22:15-19 The covenant with Abraham renewed.
- flag: allusions translated in Henry's words: Sl 116:12 ("What then shall we render...") and 2 Co 5:14-15 ("live not to ourselves, but to Him who died for us, and rose again")
- flag: the trailing "(Ge 22:20-24)" is kept as in the source

### 97 Gen 23:1-13 
- flag: source oddity: "loathsome lumps of clays" read as "clay"

### 98 Gen 23:14-20 
- quoted (Gen 23:16): “current money with the merchant” → “de acordo com o padrão dos mercadores”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's "de acordo com o padrão dos mercadores" for KJV "current money with the merchant"
- flag: source oddities: "was Abraham by promise" read as "Abraham's"; "in cave. or vault" read as "in the cave, or vault"

### 100 Gen 24:10-28 The journey of Abraham's servant to Mesopotamia, His meeting with Rebekah.
- quoted (Gen 24:16): “filled her pitcher, and came up” → “encheu seu cântaro, e se voltava”
- quoted (Gen 24:26): “bowed down his head and worshipped” → “se inclinou, e adorou”
- flag: quote 1: the Bíblia Livre's Gn 24:26 has no word for KJV "his head"
- flag: source oddity: "the strange man his camels" read as "and his camels"

### 101 Gen 24:29-53 Rebekah and her relatives consent to her marriage.
- flag: Henry's quoted "appointed out" is the KJV's Gn 24:14, 44; translated ("designados")
- flag: "when it proceeds from the Lord" echoes Gn 24:50 (the Bíblia Livre: "Do SENHOR saiu isto"); translated

### 102 Gen 24:54-67 The happy meeting and marriage of Isaac and rebekah.
- flag: source oddity: title has "rebekah" in lower case
- flag: "They blessed Rebekah" given with the Bíblia Livre's Gn 24:60 ("abençoaram a Rebeca"); "forget her own people and her father's house" alludes to Sl 45:10, translated

### 103 Gen 25:1-10 Abraham's family by Keturah, His death and burial.
- quoted (Gen 25:9): “his sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him” → “sepultaram-no Isaque e Ismael seus filhos”
- flag: source oddity: the text opens with the outline line "Esau despises and sells his birth-right. (29-34)", which belongs to section 107; kept as in the source

### 105 Gen 25:19-26 The birth of Esau and Jacob.
- flag: Rebekah's words (KJV Gn 25:22 "If it be so, why am I thus?") are translated: the Bíblia Livre's "Se é assim para que vivo eu?" would not carry Henry's application ("why so careless...?")

### 106 Gen 25:27-28 The different characters of Esau and Jacob.
- quoted (Gen 25:27): “Jacob was a plain man” → “Jacó porém era homem quieto”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has "homem quieto" for KJV "a plain man"; used again in section 107 ("his character as a plain man")

### 107 Gen 25:29-34 
- quoted (Gen 25:34): “rose up and went his way” → “levantou-se, e foi-se”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 25:34 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("Assim menosprezou Esaú a primogenitura")
- flag: "Give me some of that red" (Gn 25:30) translated close to the Bíblia Livre's "disso vermelho"; "I am going towards death" is Henry's gloss on Gn 25:32 (the Bíblia Livre: "Eis que vou morrer")
- flag: allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: 1 Co 12:31 ("desejai com zelo ... os melhores dons") and Rm 16:18 ("ao próprio ventre")

### 108 Gen 26:1-5 Isaac, because of famine, goes to Gerar.
- quoted (Gen 26:1): “a famine in the land” → “houve fome na terra”

### 110 Gen 26:12-17 Isaac grows rich, The Philistines' envy.
- flag: "The Philistines envied Isaac" follows the Bíblia Livre's Gn 26:14 ("os filisteus tiveram inveja dele")

### 111 Gen 26:18-25 Isaac digs wells God blesses him.
- flag: "Contention and Hatred" are Henry's translations of the wells' names; the Bíblia Livre keeps the names (Eseque, Sitna, Gn 26:20-21)
- flag: source oddity: title lacks a comma ("Isaac digs wells God blesses him")
- flag: "study to be quiet" alludes to 1 Ts 4:11 (the Bíblia Livre: "procurai viver quietos")

### 112 Gen 26:26-33 Abimelech makes a covenant with Isaac.
- quoted (Prov 16:7): “When a man's ways please the Lord, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him” → “Quando os caminhos do homem são agradáveis ao SENHOR, ele faz até seus inimigos terem paz com ele”
- flag: "as much as in us lies, to live peaceable with all men" alludes to Rm 12:18 (the Bíblia Livre: "No que for possível de vossa parte, tende paz com todos"); translated

### 113 Gen 26:34-35 Esau's wives.
- flag: "a grief of mind" (KJV Gn 26:35) given with the Bíblia Livre's "amargura de espírito"

### 114 Gen 27:1-5 Isaac sends Esau for venison.
- flag: "the elder should serve the younger" (Gn 25:23) given close to the Bíblia Livre's "o maior servirá ao menor"

### 115 Gen 27:6-17 Rebekah teaches Jacob to obtain the blessing.
- quoted (Gen 17:1, Gen 17:2): “walk before me and be thou perfect” → “Eu sou o Deus Todo-Poderoso; anda diante de mim, e sê íntegro”
- quoted (Gen 27:13): “Upon me be thy curse, my son” → “Filho meu, sobre mim tua maldição”
- quoted (Gen 27:13): “Upon me be thy curse” → “sobre mim tua maldição”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has "sê íntegro" for KJV "be thou perfect"
- flag: quote 1: the Bíblia Livre puts "Filho meu" first; its order is kept

### 116 Gen 27:18-29 Jacob, pretending to be Esau, obtains the blessing.
- flag: "leanness" echoes Sl 106:15 (the Bíblia Livre: "magreza")

### 117 Gen 27:30-40 Isaac's fear, Esau's importunity.
- quoted (Gen 27:34): “he cried with a great and exceeding bitter cry” → “clamou com uma muito grande e muito amarga exclamação”
- quoted (Gen 27:28): “the fatness of the earth” → “gorduras da terra”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gn 27:33 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("Eu o abençoei, e será bendito")
- flag: quote 1: the source lists Gn 27:28 (Jacob's blessing); Esau's blessing (Gn 27:39) has the same words in the Bíblia Livre ("sem gorduras da terra"), so either fits

### 118 Gen 27:41-46 Esau threatens Jacob's life, Rebekah sends Jacob away.
- quoted (John 4:42): “The Christ, the Saviour of the world” → “o Cristo, o Salvador do mundo”
- flag: allusions in the Bíblia Livre's words: Jd 11 ("entraram pelo caminho de Caim") and Rm 3:8 ("o mal, para que venha o bem")
