# To review: pt commentary batch 031

### 1192 Ezra 1:1-4 The proclamation of Cyrus for the rebuilding of the temple.
- quoted (2Chr 36:22): “The Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus” → “o SENHOR induziu o espírito de Ciro”
- flag: "stirred up" (2 Cr 36:22): the Bíblia Livre says "induziu"; Henry's following "stirs up their spirits" translated "desperta".

### 1193 Ezra 1:5-11 The people provide for their return.
- quoted (Rom 8:21): “into the glorious liberty of the children of God” → “para a liberdade da glória dos filhos de Deus”
- flag: Rm 8:21: the Bíblia Livre's "a liberdade da glória dos filhos de Deus" for the KJV's "glorious liberty".

### 1196 Ezra 2:64-70 The offerings for the temple.
- flag: Source mixes persons ("Seek first … then will all other things be added unto them"); kept so ("Buscai… lhes serão acrescentadas").

### 1198 Ezra 3:8-13 The foundations of the temple laid.
- quoted (Ezra 3:6): “the foundation of the temple” → “postos os fundamentos do templo”
- quoted (Ezra 3:12): “wept with a loud voice” → “choravam em alta voz”
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Zc 4:10: the Bíblia Livre's "o dia das pequenas coisas" used.

### 1201 Ezra 5:1-2 The leaders forward the building of the temple.
- flag: Source opens with a stray fragment, "letter against the Jews." and "(3-17)", each its own paragraph (apparently left over from an outline heading, Ed 5:3-17); translated and kept as they stand.
- flag: "his word, which he magnifies above all his name" (Sl 138:2): the Bíblia Livre reads "engrandeceste tua palavra e teu nome acima de tudo", so Henry's wording is translated.

### 1202 Ezra 5:3-17 
- quoted (Ezra 5:5): “The elders of the Jews” → “Os anciãos dos judeus”
- flag: Unlisted allusions: 1 Pe 3:15 ("a razão da esperança que há em", Bíblia Livre, with Henry's "us": "em nós") and Ef 2:21-22 ("cresce para ser um templo santo no Senhor", "morada de Deus em Espírito", Bíblia Livre).

### 1203 Ezra 6:1-12 The decree for completing the temple.
- quoted (Zech 4:7): “thereof with shoutings, crying, Grace, grace unto it” → “trará a pedra angular com gritos de: Graça! graça seja”
- flag: "head-stone" (Zc 4:7): the Bíblia Livre has "pedra angular", used.

### 1204 Ezra 6:13-22 The temple is finished.
- flag: "serve the Lord with gladness" (Sl 100:2): the Bíblia Livre's "ao SENHOR com alegria" kept, with Henry's "let us serve".

### 1207 Ezra 7:27-28 Ezra blesses God for his favour.
- flag: "God has extended mercy to me" (Ed 7:28): the Bíblia Livre says "sobre mim inclinou bondade"; Henry's wording translated.

### 1208 Ezra 8:1-20 The companions of Ezra.
- flag: "the outcasts of Israel, and the dispersed of Judah" (Is 11:12, unlisted): the Bíblia Livre's "desterrados de Israel" and "dispersos de Judá" used.

### 1212 Ezra 9:1-4 Ezra mourns for the Jews' conduct.
- flag: Source punctuation "excused, and even pleaded for; by many professors" (semicolon for a comma) read as one clause.

### 1213 Ezra 9:5-15 Ezra's confession of sins.
- quoted (Heb 9:26): “away sin by the sacrifice of himself” → “aniquilar o pecado pelo sacrifício de si mesmo”
- flag: Source "God be merciful to me sinner" lacks "a" (Lc 18:13, unlisted): the Bíblia Livre's "Ó Deus, tem misericórdia de mim, que sou pecador" used.
- flag: Source "saddler presage" read as "sadder"; translated "mais triste".
- flag: Hb 9:26: the Bíblia Livre's "aniquilar" stands for the KJV's "put away", which Henry gives as "take away".

### 1214 Ezra 10:1-5 Ezra encourages to reformation.
- flag: "Arise, be of good courage" (Ed 10:4, unlisted): the Bíblia Livre's "Levanta-te" and "esforça-te" used.
- flag: "unequally yoked with unbelievers" (2 Co 6:14): the Bíblia Livre says "outro jugo com os descrentes"; Henry's phrase translated "jugo desigual".

### 1217 Neh 1:1-11 Nehemiah's distress for the misery of Jerusalem, His prayer.
- quoted (James 5:16): “the effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man” → “oração de um justo”
- flag: Tg 5:16: the Bíblia Livre's "A oração de um justo muito pode efetuar" has no "effectual fervent"; the quoted run is "oração de um justo", and Henry's "effectual fervent" is translated "eficaz e fervorosa".

### 1218 Neh 2:1-8 Nehemiah's request to the king.
- quoted (Neh 2:4): “prayed to the God of heaven” → “ao Deus dos céus”
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Hb 4:16: the Bíblia Livre's "com confiança ao trono da graça" used.

### 1221 Neh 3:1-32 The rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem.
- flag: "if every one will mend one" read as "mend one [person, himself]": "se cada um emendar um".

### 1226 Neh 5:6-13 Nehemiah redresses the grievances.
- quoted (2Cor 8:9): “though he was rich, yet” → “que, sendo rico”
- quoted (2Cor 8:9): “through his poverty, might be rich” → “com a pobreza dele”
- flag: 2 Co 8:9 put in the first person plural as Henry has it ("for our sakes", "we"); only the unchanged runs are quoted.

### 1228 Neh 6:1-9 Sanballat's plot to hinder Nehemiah.
- quoted (Neh 6:9): “now, therefore, O God, strengthen my hands” → “esforça pois minhas mãos, ó Deus”
- flag: "strengthen my hands" (Ne 6:9): the Bíblia Livre has "Esforça pois minhas mãos, ó Deus"; Henry's "now" kept as "agora" before it.

### 1233 Neh 8:1-8 The reading and expounding the law.
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Jó 38:2: the Bíblia Livre's "o conselho com palavras sem conhecimento" used.

### 1234 Neh 8:9-12 The people called upon to be joyful.
- quoted (Neh 8:9): “when they heard the words of the law” → “ouvia as palavras da lei”
- flag: Ne 8:9: the Bíblia Livre's singular "o povo… ouvia"; Henry's "they heard" given as "o povo ouvia".

### 1235 Neh 8:13-18 The feast of tabernacles, The joy of the people.
- quoted (Neh 8:14): “They found written in the law” → “acharam escrito na lei”
- flag: "the believer's tabernacle state" (living in tents, as a pilgrim) translated "estado de peregrino do crente" (the Bíblia Livre's Ne 8:14 has "tendas").

### 1237 Neh 9:4-38 Prayer and confession of sin.
- quoted (2Pet 1:21): “spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost” → “falaram conduzidos pelo Espírito Santo”
- translated, not quoted (Neh 9:17; The Bíblia Livre's Ne 9:17 addresses God in the second person ("tu, que és Deus que perdoas"); Henry's exclamation is in the third, so translated.): “A God ready to pardon”
- quoted (Heb 4:16): “the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need” → “trono da graça, para que possamos receber misericórdia e encontrar graça para sermos socorridos no tempo adequado”
- translated, not quoted (Neh 9:17; Same as quote 1: Henry's "He is a God ready to pardon" is third person; the Bíblia Livre's "Deus que perdoas" second person.): “a God ready to pardon”

### 1239 Neh 10:32-39 Their engagement to sacred rites.
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Is 1:16-17: the Bíblia Livre's "parai de fazer maldades" / "Aprendei a fazer o bem" used, in Henry's first person plural.
