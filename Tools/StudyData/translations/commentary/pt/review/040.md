# To review: pt commentary batch 040

### 1518 Ps 59:8-17 He foresees their destruction.
- flag: "The God of our mercy" follows Ps 59:10, 17 KJV ("The God of my mercy"); the Bíblia Livre has "O Deus que tem bondade para comigo"; translated.

### 1519 Ps 60:1-5 David prays for the deliverance of Israel from their enemies.
- flag: "A banner to those that fear God" echoes Ps 60:4 (Bíblia Livre: "Deste uma bandeira aos que te temem"); translated in Henry's third person.

### 1520 Ps 60:6-12 He entreats God to carry on and complete their victories.
- flag: Unlisted quotations: Rom 8:37 "mais que vencedor" (singular for "the Israel of God") and Ps 60:9 "cidade fortificada" (the KJV's "strong city") from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "It is our Father's good pleasure to give us the kingdom" echoes Luke 12:32; translated (Bíblia Livre: "vosso Pai se agradou de dar a vós o Reino").

### 1521 Ps 61:1-4 David seeks God upon former experience.
- quoted (Ps 61:2): “rock that is higher than” → “uma rocha alta”
- flag: Quote 0: the KJV's "the rock that is higher than I" (Ps 61:2) is "uma rocha alta para mim" in the Bíblia Livre; "uma rocha alta" quoted and Henry's "higher than we are" added ("mais alta do que nós").
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ps 61:3 ("shelter and strong tower"): the Bíblia Livre's "refúgio e torre forte" used.

### 1522 Ps 61:5-8 He vows to serve God.
- quoted (Luke 1:32, Luke 1:33): “shall give unto him the throne of his father David” → “lhe dará o trono de Davi, seu ancestral”
- quoted (Luke 1:33): “of his kingdom there shall be no end” → “o seu reino não terá fim”
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's Luke 1:32 has "o Senhor Deus" and "seu ancestral" for the KJV's "his father"; Henry's "the Lord" kept before the quoted run.

### 1523 Ps 62:1-7 David's confidence in God.
- quoted (Ps 62:2): “my Rock and my salvation” → “minha rocha, minha salvação”

### 1524 Ps 62:8-12 No trust to be put in worldly things.
- quoted (Ps 62:8): “trust in him at all times” → “nele em todo o tempo”
- flag: Source slips: "the good counsel" begins a sentence in lower case (capitalized), and "lay out wants and our wishes" read as "our wants".
- flag: "Lighter than vanity itself" follows Ps 62:9 KJV ("lighter than vanity"); the Bíblia Livre has "mais leves que o vazio"; translated ("vaidade").
- flag: "Pouring out our hearts" echoes Ps 62:8 (Bíblia Livre: "derramai vosso coração").

### 1525 Ps 63:1-2 David's desire toward God.
- quoted (Ps 63:1): “Early will I seek thee” → “Eu te busco ao amanhecer”
- quoted (Rev 21:6): “the Fountain of the water of life” → “Fonte da água da vida”
- flag: Quote 0: the KJV's "Early will I seek thee" is in the present in the Bíblia Livre's Ps 63:1 ("Eu te busco ao amanhecer").

### 1526 Ps 63:3-6 His satisfaction in God.
- flag: Unlisted quotations of Ps 63:5-6: the Bíblia Livre's "com lábios alegres" and "nas vigílias da noite" used.
- flag: "Sorrow and sighing flee away" echoes Isa 35:10 (Bíblia Livre: "a tristeza e o gemido deles fugirão"); translated close to it.

### 1527 Ps 63:7-11 His dependence upon God, and assurance of safety.
- flag: "Follow hard after God" follows Ps 63:8 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "Minha alma está apegada a ti"; translated ("seguem de perto").
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Heb 12:2 ("the joy set before him"): the Bíblia Livre's "a alegria que lhe estava proposta" used.

### 1529 Ps 64:7-10 The destruction of the wicked, encouragement to the righteous.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 64:10; The Bíblia Livre's Ps 64:10 has the misprint "se alegará" (for "se alegrará"); translated with its other words ("O justo se alegrará no SENHOR") rather than repeat the slip.): “The righteous shall be glad in the Lord”
- flag: "Those who love cursing, it shall come upon them" echoes Ps 109:17; translated close to the Bíblia Livre ("que ela lhe sobrevenha").

### 1530 Ps 65:1-5 God is to be praised in the kingdom of grace.
- flag: "Praise is silent unto thee" follows the KJV margin of Ps 65:1 ("Praise waiteth for thee"); the Bíblia Livre has "A ti, Deus, pertence a tranquilidade e o louvor"; translated.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Isa 55:1 ("without money and without price"): the Bíblia Livre's "sem dinheiro e sem preço" used.

### 1531 Ps 65:6-13 In the kingdom of providence.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 65:8; Henry's own phrase ("some understand it of the morning and evening sacrifices"); the Bíblia Livre's Ps 65:8 has "o nascer e o pôr do sol", which would lose his point about the sacrifices.): “of the morning and evening”
- quoted (Ps 65:12): “upon the pastures of the wilderness” → “sobre os pastos do deserto”
- flag: Unlisted quotations: James 1:17 ("toda boa dádiva e todo dom perfeito vem do alto"), Mal 4:2 ("Sol da justiça") and Ps 65:9 ("rio de Deus") from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "His paths drop fatness" follows Ps 65:11 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "teus caminhos transbordam fartura"; translated.
- flag: The closing line adapts Ps 104:24 ("in wisdom"), Henry writing "in loving-kindness"; translated as he has it.

### 1532 Ps 66:1-7 Praise for God's sovereign power in the creation.

### 1533 Ps 66:8-12 For his favour to his church.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Acts 14:22: the Bíblia Livre's "por meio de muitas aflições" and "entrar no Reino de Deus" used.

### 1534 Ps 66:13-20 And the psalmist's praise for his experience of God's goodness.
- quoted (Prov 15:8): “the sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination” → “o sacrifício dos perversos é abominável ao SENHOR”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 66:20; Henry changes Ps 66:20 to the first person plural ("hath not turned away our prayer nor his mercy from us"); the Bíblia Livre's "minha oração ... de mim" can't stand.): “prayer nor his mercy from”
- flag: Source slip: "from the gross practice, Sin, regarded": the comma read as a full stop.
- flag: "Sin, regarded in the heart" follows Ps 66:18 KJV ("If I regard iniquity in my heart"); the Bíblia Livre has "Se eu tivesse dado valor para a maldade"; translated ("acalentado").

### 1535 Ps 67:1-7 A prayer for the enlargement of Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 67:7): “the ends of the earth” → “os limites da terra”
- flag: Unlisted quotations: Ps 67:1 ("Que Deus tenha misericórdia de nós") and Prov 14:34 ("a justiça exalta a nação") from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: Source slips: "shall became" read as "shall become"; "accompany the word the Holy Ghost" read as "by the Holy Ghost". The kingdoms line echoes Rev 11:15, translated in Henry's future.
- flag: sensitive: 1535 "the conversion of the heathen", "make known to the heathen" ("os pagãos"), translated as written.

### 1538 Ps 68:15-21 The presence of God in his church.

### 1541 Ps 68:32-35 The glory and grace of God.

### 1545 Ps 69:30-36 He concludes with joy and praise.
- quoted (Ps 69:36): “love his name shall dwell” → “amam o nome dele habitarão”
- quoted (Rom 8:32): “He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things” → “Aquele que nem mesmo ao seu próprio Filho poupou … como não nos dará também com ele todas as coisas”
- flag: Quote 1: the Bíblia Livre's Rom 8:32 lacks "but delivered him up for us all" and "freely"; the two runs it has are quoted and Henry's middle clause is translated between them ("mas o entregou por todos nós"); "freely" is not rendered, as the run can't be broken. A reviewer may prefer mode translated here.
- flag: "Restorer of the ancient places to dwell in" echoes Isa 58:12 (KJV "paths to dwell in"); translated.

### 1550 Ps 72:2-17 He passes into a prophecy of the glories of his reign, and of Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 72:6): “come down like rain upon the mown grass” → “descerá como chuva sobre a erva cortada”

- flag: Quote 0: Henry explains the KJV's "mown grass" ("not on that cut down, but that which is left growing"); the Bíblia Livre's "erva cortada" is used and his contrast rendered "não sobre a que foi ceifada".
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's capitals in "call HIM blessed" can't be kept inside the Bíblia Livre's words ("o chamarão").
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ps 72:7 ("abundance of peace"): the Bíblia Livre's "abundância de paz" used.
