# To review: pt commentary batch 091

### 3747 Eph 3:13-19 He prays for the Ephesians.
- flag: "Breadth … length … depth … height" (Ef 3:18) follow the Bíblia Livre's words ("largura, comprimento, profundidade, e altura"); "grace for grace" (Jo 1:16) is its "graça por graça".

### 3749 Eph 4:1-6 Exhortations to mutual forbearance and union.
- quoted (Matt 28:19): “in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost” → “em nome do Pai, do Filho, e do Espírito Santo”
- flag: Henry distinguishes "lowliness" (the KJV's word in Ef 4:2) from humility; the Bíblia Livre has "humildade" there, so "lowliness" is given as "humildade de espírito".

### 3750 Eph 4:7-16 To a due use of spiritual gifts and graces.
- flag: The source text ends with the next section's reference, "(Ep 4:17-24)"; kept as "(Ef 4:17-24)".

### 3751 Eph 4:17-24 To purity and holiness.
- flag: Henry's "The corrupt nature is called a man" rests on the KJV's "old man" / "new man" (Ef 4:22-24); the Bíblia Livre has "velho ser humano" / "novo ser humano". Translated "homem" to keep his point.
- flag: "The vanity of their minds" (Ef 4:17) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "na futilidade das suas mentes".

### 3752 Eph 4:25-28 And to take heed of the sins practised among the heathen.
- translated, not quoted (Eph 4:27; Henry turns Ef 4:27's command ("nem deis lugar ao diabo") into a statement in the first person plural ("We give place"), so the verse's form cannot stand.): “give place to the devil”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Isa 61:8: "o roubo com sacrifício de queima" (Bíblia Livre).
- flag: "All appearance of evil" (1 Ts 5:22) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "qualquer forma de mal".

### 3753 Eph 4:29-32 
- quoted (Eph 4:32): “Be ye kind one to another” → “sede benignos uns com os outros”
- flag: "Bitterness, wrath, anger, clamour, evil-speaking, and malice" (Ef 4:31) follows the Bíblia Livre's words ("amargura, raiva, ira, gritaria, e maledicência … malícia").

### 3754 Eph 5:1-2 Exhortation to brotherly love.
- flag: Henry's "followers of God, imitators of God" glosses the KJV's "followers" (Ef 5:1); the Bíblia Livre already reads "imitadores de Deus".

### 3755 Eph 5:3-14 Cautions against several sins.
- quoted (Eph 5:8): “Walk as children of light” → “andai como filhos da luz”
- flag: The English has a stray full stop mid-sentence ("so far from being profitable. that they pollute"); translated as one sentence with a comma.

### 3756 Eph 5:15-21 Directions to a contrary behaviour, and to relative duties.
- translated, not quoted (Eph 5:18; Henry turns Ef 5:18's command into "let us seek to be filled with the Spirit"; the Bíblia Livre's imperative "enchei-vos do Espírito" cannot stand in his sentence.): “be filled with the Spirit”
- flag: Henry's "redeem time" (and "gladly redeem at the price of the whole world") plays on the KJV's "redeeming the time" (Ef 5:16); the Bíblia Livre has "aproveitai o tempo". Translated "resgatar" to keep his wordplay.
- flag: "Be not unwise" (Ef 5:17) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "Não sejais imprudentes".

### 3757 Eph 5:22-33 The duties of wives and husbands are enforced by the spiritual relation between Christ and the church.
- flag: "Without spot or wrinkle" (Ef 5:27) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "sem mancha, nem ruga".

### 3758 Eph 6:1-4 The duties of children and parents.
- flag: The English "though it may be awfully occasion it" is garbled (probably "may awfully occasion it"); translated as "embora possa ser terrivelmente ocasião dela", nothing dropped.

### 3759 Eph 6:5-9 Of servants and masters.
- flag: Henry speaks of ancient slavery and expects Christianity to root it out; translated as written. A reviewer may wish to note it, though it is not harsh toward any people.

### 3760 Eph 6:10-18 All Christians are to put on spiritual armour against the enemies of their souls.
- quoted (Eph 6:11): “put on the whole armour of God” → “de toda a armadura de Deus”
- quoted (Eph 6:15): “shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace” → “com a prontidão do evangelho da paz”
- quoted (Eph 6:17): “the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God” → “a espada do Espírito, que é a palavra de Deus”
- quoted (Eph 6:10): “in the power of his might” → “na força do seu poder”
- flag: "We must preserve in particular requests" is evidently a slip for "persevere"; translated as meant, "Devemos perseverar".
- flag: "In the power of his mighty" lacks a word (probably "might"); translated "no poder do seu poderio".
- flag: "Resist him, and he will flee" echoes Tg 4:7; translated in Henry's words. The KJV's "preparation" (Ef 6:15) is "prontidão" in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: The source text ends with the next section's reference, "(Ep 6:19-24)"; kept as "(Ef 6:19-24)".
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 3761 Eph 6:19-24 The apostle desires their prayers, and ends with his apostolic blessing.
- quoted (Eph 6:23): “Peace be to the brethren, and love with faith” → “Paz seja com os irmãos, e amor com fé”
- quoted (Eph 6:24): “love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity” → “amam ao nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo com amor que não falha”
- flag: The KJV's "in sincerity" (Ef 6:24) is "com amor que não falha" in the Bíblia Livre; its words are used.

### 3762 Phil 1:1-7 The apostle offers up thanksgivings and prayers, for the good work of grace in the Philippians.
- quoted (Phil 1:6): “the day of Jesus Christ” → “o dia de Jesus Cristo”

### 3763 Phil 1:8-11 He expresses affection, and prays for them.
- flag: "Approve the things which are excellent" (Fp 1:10) follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "aproveis as melhores coisas". Translated "excelentes" because Henry's next sentence ("are excellent") takes up the word.

### 3766 Phil 1:27-30 Exhortations to zeal, and constancy in professing the gospel.
- quoted (Phil 1:27): “the faith of the gospel” → “a fé do Evangelho”
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's word "conversation" (Fp 1:27, Greek politeuesthe, to live as citizens); the Bíblia Livre has "procedei de maneira digna". His quoted word is given as "procedimento" so the explanation reads naturally.

### 3767 Phil 2:1-4 Exhortations to a kind, humble spirit and behaviour.
- flag: "Busy-bodies in other men's matters" (1 Pe 4:15) is given close to the Bíblia Livre's words, "se intromete em assuntos alheios", as "nos intrometer em assuntos alheios".

### 3768 Phil 2:5-11 The example of Christ.
- quoted (Phil 2:6): “Who being in the form of God” → “sendo em forma de Deus”
- translated, not quoted (Phil 2:6; Henry's point rests on the KJV's "thought it not robbery to be equal with God" (Christ's rightful equality); the Bíblia Livre's "não considerou a igualdade a Deus como algo para se apegar" gives a different sense, so his words are translated ("não teve por usurpação ser igual a Deus").): “robbery to be equal with God”
- quoted (Phil 2:8): “the death of the cross” → “morte de cruz”
- quoted (Phil 2:10): “At the name of Jesus” → “no nome de Jesus”
- quoted (Phil 2:11): “to the glory of God the Father” → “para a glória de Deus Pai”
- quoted (Phil 2:11): “confess that Jesus Christ is Lord” → “que Jesus Cristo é o Senhor”
- quoted (John 5:23): “all men should honour the Son” → “todos honrem ao Filho”
- quoted (John 5:23): “as they honour the Father” → “como honram ao Pai”
- flag: Fp 2:6: Henry's "robbery to be equal with God" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre reads "não considerou a igualdade a Deus como algo para se apegar". Translated from Henry, as "não teve por usurpação ser igual a Deus".
- flag: "The glory he had with the Father before the world was" echoes Jo 17:5; "antes que o mundo existisse" follows the Bíblia Livre.

### 3769 Phil 2:12-18 Diligence in the affairs of salvation, and to be examples to the world.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Fp 2:12-13: "Exercei a vossa salvação" and "pois é Deus quem opera em vós" (Bíblia Livre), joined as Henry joins them.
- flag: "Without murmurings", "blameless and harmless" and "the word of life" (Fp 2:14-16) follow the Bíblia Livre ("sem murmurações", "irrepreensíveis e puros", "a palavra da vida").
- flag: The source text ends with the next section's reference, "(Php 2:19-30)"; kept as "(Fp 2:19-30)".

### 3770 Phil 2:19-30 The apostle's purpose of visiting Philippi.
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's "naturally care" (Fp 2:20) as "sincerely"; the Bíblia Livre already reads "se importe sinceramente". His gloss is translated as written.
- flag: Henry's "the work of God" here follows the KJV's "the work of Christ" (Fp 2:30) loosely; translated as he wrote it.

### 3771 Phil 3:1-11 The apostle cautions the Philippians against judaizing false teachers, and renounces his own former privileges.
- translated, not quoted (Phil 3:8; The Bíblia Livre's "aceitei perder todas essas coisas" is first person and differs from the KJV's "suffered the loss of all", which Henry turns into a third-person statement; translated ("sofrera a perda de tudo").): “suffered the loss of all”
- flag: "Dumb dogs" (Is 56:10), "evil-workers" and "the concision" (Fp 3:2) are given in the Bíblia Livre's words: "cães mudos", "os que operam o mal", "a mutilação". Henry's "rent the church … cut it to pieces" explains the KJV's "concision"; it still fits "mutilação".
- flag: Henry's "the vilest refuse, offals thrown to dogs" explains the KJV's "dung" (Fp 3:8); the Bíblia Livre has "dejetos". Translated as he wrote it.
- flag: The source text ends with the next section's reference, "(Php 3:12-21)"; kept as "(Fp 3:12-21)".

### 3772 Phil 3:12-21 Expresses earnest desire to be found in Christ; also his pressing on toward perfection; and recommends his own example to other believers.
- quoted (Phil 3:18): “The enemies of the cross of Christ” → “inimigos da cruz de Cristo”
- flag: "Crucifying the flesh with its affections and lusts" (Gl 5:24) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "a carne com as paixões e os maus desejos".
- flag: Henry's "our vile bodies" (Fp 3:21) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "o nosso degradante corpo" (singular, as there).

### 3773 Phil 4:1-1 The apostle exhorts the Philippians to stand fast in the Lord.
- quoted (Phil 4:1): “stand fast in the Lord” → “firmes no Senhor”
