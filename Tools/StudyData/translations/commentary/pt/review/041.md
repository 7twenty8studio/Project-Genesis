# To review: pt commentary batch 041

### 1552 Ps 73:1-14 The psalmist's temptation.
- quoted (Ps 73:3): “the prosperity of the wicked” → “a prosperidade dos perversos”
- quoted (Ps 73:10): “therefore his people return hither” → “por isso seu povo volta para cá”

### 1554 Ps 73:21-28 How he profited by it.
- flag: The English reads "a profession Christ" (a missing "of"); translated as "uma profissão de Cristo".

### 1559 Ps 75:6-10 He rebukes the wicked, and concludes with resolutions to praise God.
- quoted (Ps 75:6): “neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south” → “não vem do oriente, nem do ocidente, nem do deserto”
- flag: Henry's point rests on the KJV's "nor from the south" (Ps 75:6) and the Hebrew word for north (tsaphon) meaning "hidden place"; the Bíblia Livre has "nem do deserto" for the third direction, so Henry's "he mentions not the north" reads a little less smoothly.

### 1561 Ps 76:7-12 All have to fear and to trust in him.
- quoted (Ps 76:9): “the meek of the earth” → “os mansos da terra”
- quoted (Ps 76:12): “He shall cut off the spirit of princes; he” → “Ele cortará o espírito dos governantes”
- flag: "so the word signifies": Henry explains the Hebrew verb behind "cut off" (Ps 76:12) as slipping off a flower or grape; the Bíblia Livre has "cortará".

### 1563 Ps 77:11-20 He encourages himself by the remembrance of God's help of his people.
- quoted (Rom 8:32): “spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all” → “nem mesmo ao seu próprio Filho poupou”
- flag: Rom 8:32: the Bíblia Livre's verse has no "but delivered him up for us all"; that clause ("antes o entregou por todos nós") is translated from Henry's KJV wording.

### 1564 Ps 78:1-8 Attention called for.
- flag: "dark and deep sayings" follows the KJV's "dark sayings" (Ps 78:2); the Bíblia Livre has "mistérios".

### 1565 Ps 78:9-39 The history of Israel.
- flag: "imbolden them": the English shifts from "our hearts" to "them"; kept as written ("os encorajam").

### 1566 Ps 78:40-55 Their settlement in Canaan.
- quoted (Ps 78:41): “The Holy One of Israel” → “O Santo de Israel”
- quoted (Ps 78:52): “made his own people to go forth like sheep; and guided them in the wilderness” → “levou a seu povo como a ovelhas; e os guiou pelo deserto”

### 1567 Ps 78:56-72 The mercies of God to Israel contrasted with their ingratitude.
- quoted (Ps 78:72): “the skilfulness of his hands” → “as habilidades de suas mãos”
- quoted (Isa 9:7): “of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end” → “à grandeza de seu governo e à paz não haverá fim”
- quoted (Jer 17:9): “the heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked” → “o coração é mais enganoso que todas as coisas, e perverso”
- flag: Jer 17:9: the Bíblia Livre has only "e perverso" where the KJV has "and desperately wicked"; the Bíblia Livre's words are used.

### 1569 Ps 79:6-13 A petition for relief.
- quoted (Ps 79:9): “the glory of thy name” → “para glorificar o teu nome”
- quoted (Ps 79:11): “prisoner come before thee, according to the greatness of thy power preserve thou those that are appointed to die” → “chegue diante de ti o gemido dos prisioneiros; conforme o teu grande poder, salva a vida dos sentenciados à morte”
- flag: Ps 79:11: Henry has "a prisoner" (singular); the Bíblia Livre's "dos prisioneiros" is used.

### 1577 Ps 83:1-8 The designs of the enemies of Israel.
- flag: "his hidden one" follows the KJV's "thy hidden ones" (Ps 83:3); the Bíblia Livre has "teus preciosos".

### 1578 Ps 83:9-18 Earnest prayer for their defeat.
- quoted (1Pet 2:11): “fleshly lusts, which war against the soul” → “desejos carnais, que batalham contra a alma”
- flag: "his name JEHOVAH" (Ps 83:18) is Henry's own word, rendered "JEOVÁ" (the Bíblia Livre has SENHOR there).

### 1579 Ps 84:1-7 The psalmist expresses his affection to the ordinances of God.
- flag: "valley of weeping" glosses the KJV's "valley of Baca" (Ps 84:6); the Bíblia Livre keeps "Vale de Baca".

### 1580 Ps 84:8-12 His desire towards the God of the ordinances.
- flag: "Through he has not promised" is a slip for "Though"; translated as "Embora".

### 1582 Ps 85:8-13 Trust in God's goodness.
- quoted (Ps 85:9): “glory may dwell in our land” → “para que a glória habite em nossa terra”
- flag: The source's last sentence has no closing full stop; kept so.

### 1583 Ps 86:1-7 The psalmist pleads his earnestness, and the mercy of God, as reasons why his prayer should be heard.
- flag: "one whom thou favourest" glosses the KJV's "I am holy" (Ps 86:2, margin "one whom thou favourest"); the Bíblia Livre has "dedicado a ti".

### 1584 Ps 86:8-17 He renews his requests for help and comfort.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1585 Ps 87:1-3 The glory of the church.
- quoted (1Cor 3:11): “Other foundation can no man lay than that is laid” → “Ninguém pode pôr fundamento diferente do que já está posto”

### 1586 Ps 87:4-7 It is filled with the Divine blessing.
- quoted (Prov 24:12): “to every man according to his works” → “ao homem conforme sua obra”
- flag: Henry's "to every man according to his works" is the general phrase (Rom 2:6 and elsewhere); the listed Prov 24:12 wording is used.

### 1591 Ps 89:15-18 The happiness of those in communion with him.
- quoted (Ps 89:18): “the Holy One of Israel” → “o Santo de Israel”
- flag: "the joyful sound" follows the KJV of Ps 89:15; the Bíblia Livre has "o grito de alegria"; translated "o som alegre".
