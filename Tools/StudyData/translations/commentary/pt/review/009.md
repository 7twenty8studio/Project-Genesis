### 244 Ex 14:21-31 The Israelites pass through the Red sea, which drowns the Egyptians.
- quoted (Ex 14:29): “walked upon dry land in the midst of the sea” → “foram por meio do mar em seco”
- quoted (Ex 14:25): “flee from the face of Israel” → “de diante de Israel”
- quoted (Ex 14:21): “out his hand over the sea” → “sua mão sobre o mar”
- translated, not quoted (Ex 14:24; the listed verse (Ex 14:24) has "o campo / o acampamento dos egípcios", which can't be what the waters overwhelmed; Henry's phrase translated): “the host of the Egyptians”
- quoted (Ex 14:30): “saw the Egyptians dead upon” → “Israel viu aos egípcios mortos”
- flag: The last sentence adapts Ex 14:13 (Bíblia Livre: "os egípcios que hoje vistes, nunca mais para sempre os vereis"); translated in Henry's third person.

### 260 Ex 20:3-11 The commandments of the first table.
- quoted (Ex 20:7): “The Lord will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain” → “Não dará por inocente o SENHOR ao que tomar seu nome em vão”
- quoted (Mark 2:27): “the sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath” → “o sábado foi feito por causa do ser humano, não o ser humano por causa do sábado”
- translated, not quoted (Ex 20:10; the listed verse (Ex 20:10) has "repouso para o SENHOR teu Deus", which can't be the subject of Henry's sentence): “The sabbath of the Lord”
- flag: Henry's point rests on the KJV's "Remember" (Ex 20:8); the Bíblia Livre has "Tu te lembrarás do dia do repouso", so "Tu te lembrarás" is used in his quotation marks (the future form still shows a known duty).
- flag: "sabbath" is "sábado" throughout (as in Ex 16:29 and Mk 2:27); the Bíblia Livre's Ex 20:8-10 says "dia do repouso".
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1Cor 10:31 ("do all to the glory of God"; the source drops "to"): "fazei tudo para a glória de Deus" from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "It matters not whether the word of God, or sacred things, all such-like things": the English sentence is elliptical; translated as it stands.
