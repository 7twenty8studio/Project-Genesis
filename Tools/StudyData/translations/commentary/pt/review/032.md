# To review: pt commentary batch 032

### 1240 Neh 11:1-36 The distribution of the people.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Phil 2:21 ("buscam as suas próprias coisas, e não as de Cristo") and 2Cor 5:8 ("deixar o corpo, e habitar com o Senhor"), Bíblia Livre words used in Henry's sentence.

### 1241 Neh 12:1-26 The priests and Levites that returned.
- flag: allusion to Heb 13:7 ("que vos falaram a palavra de Deus"); Henry's "guides" kept as "guias" (the Bíblia Livre has "líderes").

### 1242 Neh 12:27-43 The dedication of the wall.
- flag: "holiness to the Lord" (Zech 14:20 KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "CONSAGRADO AO SENHOR".

### 1244 Neh 13:1-9 Nehemiah turns out the mixed multitude.
- flag: "household stuff" (Neh 13:8 KJV) rendered "utensílios"; the Bíblia Livre's wording in that verse may differ.

### 1245 Neh 13:10-14 Nehemiah's reform in the house of God.
- quoted (Neh 13:10): “had not been given them” → “não estavam sendo dadas a eles”
- quoted (Neh 13:14): “Wipe not out my good deeds” → “Não risques minhas bondades”

### 1246 Neh 13:15-22 
- flag: Henry links "the Lord's day" with the sabbath here; rendered "o dia do Senhor" as the instructions say, no bracketed note since he doesn't say "Christian sabbath".

### 1247 Neh 13:23-31 The dismissal of strange wives.
- quoted (Neh 13:29): “Remember them, O my God” → “Lembra-te deles, meu Deus”
- quoted (Neh 13:31): “Remember me, O my God, for good” → “Lembra-te de mim, meu Deus, para o bem”
- flag: source typo "they mercy" (= thy mercy) read as "thy"; unlisted quotation of Neh 13:22 ("perdoa-me segundo a grandeza de tua bondade", Bíblia Livre, with Henry's "meu Deus" inserted as in the KJV).
- flag: Job 42:6 alluded to with the person changed ("nos abominarmos e nos arrependermos no pó e na cinza"); the end is the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: source: "on this occasion He prays" lacks a full stop; a full stop added.

### intro Esther 
- flag: sensitive: "the heathen" ("os pagãos").

### 1248 Esther 1:1-9 The royal feast of Ahasuerus.
- flag: allusion to Prov 15:17 translated (Henry adapts it: "with quietness"); the Bíblia Livre has "Melhor é a comida de hortaliças tendo amor".
- flag: sensitive: "a heathen prince" contrasted with "many called Christians".

### 1251 Esther 2:21-23 Mordecai discovers a plot against the king.
- translated, not quoted (1Thess 1:3; the Bíblia Livre's "obra da vossa fé" (second person) can't stand in Henry's third-person sentence about "their work of faith".): “work of faith and labour of love”
- flag: allusion to Heb 6:10 ("Deus não é injusto para se esquecer") worked in.

### 1252 Esther 3:1-6 Haman seeks to destroy the Jews.
- flag: unlisted quotation of John 8:44 ("foi homicida desde o princípio", Bíblia Livre); Rom 13:7 and Acts 5:29 alluded to and translated.

### 1255 Esther 4:5-17 Esther undertakes to plead for the Jews.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 4:20 ("não duvidou da promessa", Bíblia Livre); Heb 4:16 and Rom 4:18 ("against hope believed in hope") translated, the Bíblia Livre's Rom 4:18 reads differently ("Com esperança, Abraão creu, contra as expectativas").
- flag: Henry addresses Esther ("thou art bound … thy being raised up"), kept as second person singular feminine.

### 1256 Esther 5:1-8 Esther's application received.
- quoted (Matt 7:7): “Ask, and it shall be given you” → “Pedi, e vos será dado”
- quoted (1John 2:1): “we have an Advocate with the Father” → “temos um advogado com o Pai”
- flag: Rev 22:17 translated as Henry splits it ("the Spirit says, Come, and the Bride says, Come"); the Bíblia Livre: "o Espírito e a noiva dizem: “Vem!”".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Heb 4:16 ("acheguemo-nos com confiança ao trono da graça", Bíblia Livre).

### 1257 Esther 5:9-14 Haman prepares to hang Mordecai.
- flag: Henry comments on Prov 21:24 in the KJV ("Proud and haughty scorner is his name"); the Bíblia Livre's verse reads differently ("“Zombador” é o nome do arrogante e orgulhoso") but still fits.
- flag: "Many call the proud happy" (Mal 3:15 KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "Chamemos de sortudos os soberbos".

### 1260 Esther 6:12-14 Haman's friends tell him of his danger.
- flag: allusion to Job 16:2 ("consoladores miseráveis", the Bíblia Livre's words) in Henry's own sentence.

### 1261 Esther 7:1-6 Esther accuses Haman.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 10:28 ("pode destruir tanto a alma como o corpo no inferno", Bíblia Livre order: soul before body, Henry has "both body and soul").
- flag: Eph 3:20 alluded to ("muito mais abundantemente … do que pedimos ou pensamos"), adapted.

### 1262 Esther 7:7-10 Haman hanged on his own gallows.
- quoted (Esther 7:10): “Then was the king's wrath pacified” → “então o furor do rei se apaziguou”

### 1263 Esther 8:1-2 Mordecai is advanced.
- flag: Ps 39:6 KJV ("he heapeth up riches, and knoweth not who shall gather them") translated; the Bíblia Livre reads "ajuntam bens , e não sabem que os levará".

### 1264 Esther 8:3-14 Esther makes suit for the Jews.
- flag: sensitive: the Medes and Persians' "pride and folly"; the closing question is Henry's implied criticism of Scripture kept in Latin (the Roman Church).
- flag: "We will be as gods" echoes Gen 3:5 in the first person; translated (the Bíblia Livre: "sereis como deuses").

### 1265 Esther 8:15-17 Mordecai honoured, The joy of the Jews.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Acts 9:31 ("andando no temor do Senhor, e na consolação do Espírito Santo"); Henry's form kept ("andam no temor do Senhor e na consolação do Espírito Santo"), close to the Bíblia Livre.

### 1266 Esther 9:1-19 The success of the Jews.
- quoted (Esther 9:1): “The enemies of the Jews hoped to have power over them” → “os inimigos dos judeus esperavam vencê-los”
- flag: Phil 1:27 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's "num mesmo espírito, com um mesmo ânimo, combatendo juntos" (unlisted, adapted).
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1267 Esther 9:20-32 The feast of Purim in remembrance of this.
- flag: sensitive: Henry's argument to the Jews that their Messiah has long since come; "the superstitions of the heathen" ("os pagãos").

### 1268 Esther 10:1-3 Greatness of Ahasuerus--Mordecai's advancement.
- flag: Gal 5:6 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's "opera por meio do amor" (unlisted).

### 1269 Job 1:1-5 The piety and prosperity of Job.
- quoted (Matt 19:24): “a rich man to enter into the kingdom” → “o rico entrar no reino”
- flag: Henry says "the kingdom of heaven" (Matt 19:23-24); the Bíblia Livre's Matt 19:24 has "reino de Deus": its words "o rico entrar no reino" used, then Henry's "dos céus".

### 1270 Job 1:6-12 Satan obtains leave to try Job.
- flag: Prov 10:22 alluded to ("A bênção do Senhor enriquece"), Henry's own sentence; Bíblia Livre: "É a bênção do SENHOR que enriquece".

### 1272 Job 1:20-22 Job's patience and piety.
- quoted (1Tim 6:7): “it is certain we can carry nothing out” → “é evidente que nada podemos levar dele”
- translated, not quoted (Neh 9:33; Henry turns Neh 9:33 into the third person ("God has done right") and parallels it with "done wisely"; the Bíblia Livre's "agiste fielmente" is second person and wouldn't fit.): “done right, but we have done wickedly”
- flag: "charge God with folly" (Job 1:22 KJV) translated as "acusam Deus de loucura"; the Bíblia Livre's wording there may differ.
- flag: 1John 3:8 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's "desfazer as obras do diabo" (unlisted).

### 1274 Job 2:7-10 Job's sufferings.
- flag: sensitive: Henry on Job's wife, "spared to him, to be a troubler and tempter to him".
- flag: 1Pet 5:6 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's "poderosa mão de Deus" (unlisted).

### 1275 Job 2:11-13 His friends come to comfort him.
- quoted (Job 2:13): “they sat down with him” → “se sentaram com ele”
- flag: "vailed" read as "veiled" ("encoberto").
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1276 Job 3:1-10 Job complains that he was born.
- flag: source typo "consolidation" read as "consolation" ("consolação").
- flag: "a man of like passions" (Jas 5:17 KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "tão humano quanto nós".

### 1277 Job 3:11-19 Job complaining.
- quoted (Job 3:17): “There the wicked cease from troubling” → “Ali os maus deixam de perturbar”
- flag: unlisted second half of Job 3:17 ("There the weary are at rest"): the Bíblia Livre's "ali repousam os cansados de forças" used.
- flag: Matt 11:29 and John 16:33 alluded to ("descanso para a nossa alma", "no mundo tenhamos aflição"), adapted from the Bíblia Livre.
