# To review: pt commentary batch 025

### 917 2Sam 18:19-33 
- flag: The English ends its last sentence, a question ("may we not … perceive…"), with a full stop; kept as in the source.
- flag: Henry's wordplay "over-grieve for what we over-loved" rendered "afligir-nos em excesso pelo que amamos em excesso".

### 919 2Sam 19:9-15 David returns to Jordan.
- flag: Echoes of Sl 110:2-3 ("Domina tu no meio de teus inimigos. Teu povo será voluntário no dia do teu poder" in the Bíblia Livre) given close to its words, in Henry's third person.

### 923 2Sam 19:40-43 Israel quarrels with Judah.
- translated, not quoted (2Sam 19:43; The Bíblia Livre's 2 Sm 19:43 has "o argumento dos homens de Judá prevaleceu", not "words"; Henry's point is the fierceness of their words, which "argumento" would lose.): “words of the men of Judah”
- flag: The English has "though themselves despised", read as "thought themselves despised".
- flag: "a resposta suave desvia o furor" follows the Bíblia Livre's Pv 15:1 (unlisted).

### 924 2Sam 20:1-3 Sheba's rebellion.
- flag: "Hosana ao Filho de Davi" follows the Bíblia Livre's Mt 21:9 (unlisted); "Crucify him, crucify him" (Lc 23:21) translated.
- flag: "Sheba" (the son of Bichri) given as "Seba", one of the glossary's spellings.

### 926 2Sam 20:14-22 Sheba takes refuge in Abel.
- flag: "will not have Christ to reign over them" echoes Lc 19:14, translated.

### 929 2Sam 21:10-14 Rizpah's care for the bodies of Saul's descendants.
- flag: "God was entreated for the land" (2 Sm 21:14 KJV) rendered "Deus se aplacara para com a terra"; "made a curse for us" (Gl 3:13) translated.

### 930 2Sam 21:15-22 Battles with the Philistines.
- flag: "mais que vencedores" follows the Bíblia Livre's Rm 8:37 (unlisted); "last enemy" echoes 1 Co 15:26.

### 931 2Sam 22:1-51 David's psalm of thanksgiving.
- flag: "socorro oportuno" follows the Bíblia Livre's Sl 46:1 (unlisted; Henry's "a present help").

### 932 2Sam 23:1-7 David's last words.
- quoted (2Pet 1:21): “as they were moved by the Holy Ghost” → “conduzidos pelo Espírito Santo”
- flag: "um pacto perpétuo" follows the Bíblia Livre's 2 Sm 23:5 (unlisted), which Henry is quoting; elsewhere in the section "covenant" is "aliança" as usual.
- flag: "all David's salvation … all his desire" (2 Sm 23:5) and "the Spirit of God spake by him" (2 Sm 23:2, the Bíblia Livre: "O espírito do SENHOR falou por mim") translated in Henry's third person.

### 933 2Sam 23:8-39 David's mighty men.
- flag: "tão grande salvação" follows the Bíblia Livre's Hb 2:3 (unlisted).

### 934 2Sam 24:1-9 David numbers the people.
- quoted (Heb 4:12): “the thoughts and intents of the heart” → “os pensamentos e intenções do coração”
- flag: "an arm of flesh" (Jr 17:5; 2 Cr 32:8) translated.

### 935 2Sam 24:10-15 He chooses the pestilence.
- flag: Henry's "a heart within to smite him" alludes to 2 Sm 24:10 ("David's heart smote him"), translated.

### 936 2Sam 24:16-17 The staying the pestilence.
- quoted (2Sam 24:16): “the Lord repented him of the evil” → “o SENHOR se arrependeu daquele mal”

### 937 2Sam 24:18-25 David's sacrifice, The plague removed.
- flag: "God hates robbery for burnt-offering" (Is 61:8) translated from Henry's KJV wording; the Bíblia Livre has "odeio o roubo com sacrifício de queima".
- flag: "peace-offerings" rendered "ofertas pacíficas".

### 940 1Kings 1:11-31 David makes Solomon king.
- quoted (1Kings 1:12): “own life, and the life of thy son” → “guardes tua vida, e a vida de teu filho”
- flag: Henry's "how to save thy own life" given with the Bíblia Livre's "para que guardes tua vida" (1 Rs 1:12; "own" not rendered separately).
- flag: "ninguém tome a nossa coroa" adapts the Bíblia Livre's Ap 3:11 ("para que ninguém tome tua coroa") to Henry's "our crown".

### 941 1Kings 1:32-53 Solomon is anointed king, and Adonijah's usurpation stopped.
- flag: "Combinations" (Adonijah's party) rendered "as alianças" in the ordinary sense of a league, not the covenant term.

### 942 1Kings 2:1-4 David's dying charge to Solomon.
- quoted (1Kings 2:3): “keep the charge of the Lord” → “Guarda a ordenança do SENHOR”
- quoted (1Kings 2:4): “a man on the throne of Israel” → “homem do trono de Israel”
- flag: The section ends with a bare "(1Ki 2:5-11)", kept as in the source; it may be an outline heading left in the text.
- flag: Henry's "to keep the charge of the Lord" given as the Bíblia Livre's imperative (1 Rs 2:3) after a colon.

### 943 1Kings 2:5-11 David's charge as to Joab and others.
- flag: The English is garbled here ("for the security of Solomon's throne, which was the murders he had committed, but would readily repeat them"); words seem lost (probably about Joab, who did not repent of the murders he had committed). Translated as it stands, the clause kept as given.
- flag: "Não o absolverás" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Rs 2:9 (unlisted; Henry's "Hold him not guiltless").

### 946 1Kings 2:35-46 Shimei is put to death.
- flag: "it is a vain thing they imagine" (Sl 2:1) translated from Henry's KJV wording; the Bíblia Livre has "planejam em vão".

### 947 1Kings 3:1-4 Solomon's marriage.
- flag: Henry's quotation "He loved the Lord" (1 Rs 3:3, unlisted) given with the Bíblia Livre's "amava ao SENHOR".

### 948 1Kings 3:5-15 His vision, His prayer for wisdom.
- quoted (1Kings 3:7): “I am but a little child” → “sou um tenro jovem”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's 1 Rs 3:7 has "um tenro jovem" for the KJV's "a little child"; quoted verbatim.

### 949 1Kings 3:16-28 The judgment of Solomon.
- flag: "brands out of the burning" (Am 4:11; Zc 3:2) rendered "tições do fogo", close to the Bíblia Livre's Zc 3:2 ("um tição tirado do fogo").

### 951 1Kings 4:20-28 Solomon's dominions, His daily provision.
- quoted (1Kings 4:24): “He had peace on all sides” → “teve paz por todos os lados”
- quoted (Rom 14:17): “righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost” → “justiça, paz, e alegria no Espírito Santo”
- flag: "as nações por herança" follows the Bíblia Livre's Sl 2:8 (unlisted); "the bread that perishes … that which endures" (Jo 6:27) translated.

### 952 1Kings 4:29-34 The wisdom of Solomon.
- quoted (1Kings 4:34): “to hear the wisdom of Solomon” → “ouvir a sabedoria de Salomão”
- flag: "largura de coração" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Rs 4:29 (unlisted; KJV "largeness of heart").
- flag: "ocultos todos os tesouros da sabedoria e do conhecimento" follows the Bíblia Livre's Cl 2:3 (unlisted); 1 Co 1:30 and Mt 11:29 echoes given close to its wording.

### 953 1Kings 5:1-9 Solomon's agreement with Hiram.
- flag: Henry notes that the Hebrew word for "adversary" in 1 Rs 5:4 is "satan"; the Bíblia Livre has "nem há adversários".
- flag: The English has "benefitted" (a spelling variant), translated normally; the source's "--" before "If Tyre" is kept.

### 954 1Kings 5:10-18 Solomon's workmen for the temple.
- quoted (1Pet 2:5): “as lively stones, are built up a spiritual house” → “como pedras vivas … edificados como casa espiritual”
- flag: "morada de Deus em Espírito" follows the Bíblia Livre's Ef 2:22 (unlisted); "a chosen and precious Stone" echoes 1 Pe 2:4-6 ("escolhida e preciosa").

### 955 1Kings 6:1-10 The building of Solomon's temple.
- quoted (1Kings 6:1): “the house of the Lord” → “a casa do SENHOR”
- quoted (1Kings 6:1): “the house of the Lord” → “a casa do SENHOR”
- flag: Henry's reference "Mr 5:27" seems to be a slip for Mc 4:27 (the seed that grows "he knoweth not how"); kept as in the source.
- flag: "no iron tool must be heard" after 1 Rs 6:7, translated.

### 957 1Kings 6:15-38 Particulars respecting the temple.
- flag: "toda a plenitude da divindade" (Cl 2:9) and "cresce para ser um templo santo no Senhor" (Ef 2:21) follow the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).

### 959 1Kings 7:13-47 Furniture of the temple.
- quoted (1Kings 7:21): “pillars in the porch of the temple” → “no pórtico do templo”
- flag: "Bom ao coração é ser fortificado pela graça" (Hb 13:9), "tanto o querer como o agir" (Fp 2:13) and "fonte aberta … contra o pecado e a impureza" (Zc 13:1) follow the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).
- flag: Henry's glosses on the names ("Jachin", he will establish; "Boaz", in him is strength) translated; the names given as the Bíblia Livre spells them ("Jaquim", "Boaz").

### 961 1Kings 8:1-11 The dedication of the temple.
- quoted (2Cor 3:18): “as in a glass, the glory of the Lord” → “com o rosto descoberto … a glória do Senhor”
- flag: The text opens with a second heading ("Solomon's peace-offerings.") and a bare "(62-66)", kept as separate paragraphs as in the source.
- flag: Quotation 0: the Bíblia Livre's 2 Co 3:18 has "refletindo como que um espelho" where Henry (KJV) has "we behold, as in a glass"; only "com o rosto descoberto" and "a glória do Senhor" are quoted verbatim, the verb translated from Henry, since "refletindo" would change his point (seeing).

### 963 1Kings 8:22-53 Solomon's prayer.
- quoted (1Kings 8:38): “shall know every man the plague of his own heart” → “reconhecer a ferida de seu coração”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's 1 Rs 8:38 has "ferida" for the KJV's "plague"; Henry's later "Sin is the plague of our own hearts" uses "ferida" too, to keep his link.
- flag: Quotation 0 is quoted verbatim with the Bíblia Livre's infinitive ("reconhecer"), the sentence opening "Cada um há de" being Henry's "every man".

### 965 1Kings 8:62-66 
- flag: The section ends without a full stop and with "Goodness" capitalized ("God's Goodness"); both kept as in the source.

### 966 1Kings 9:1-9 God's answer to Solomon.

### 967 1Kings 9:10-14 The presents of Solomon and Hiram.
