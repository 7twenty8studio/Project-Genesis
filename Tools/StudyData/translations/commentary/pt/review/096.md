# To review: pt commentary batch 096

### 3896 Heb 10:19-25 An argument for holy boldness in the believer's access to God through Jesus Christ, And for steadfastness in faith, and mutual love and duty.
- quoted (Heb 10:19, Heb 10:20): “by the blood of Jesus” → “pelo sangue de Jesus”
- flag: Title: the source joins two headings with a comma ("..., And for steadfastness ..."); kept as given.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Tt 2:10: "a doutrina de Deus" in the Bíblia Livre's words (it has "Deus nosso Salvador"; Henry's "their Saviour" translated).

### 3897 Heb 10:26-31 The danger of apostacy.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Jo 6:68 ("palavras da vida eterna") and Jo 6:37 ("Ao que vem a" ... "em maneira nenhuma o"; Henry's third person "lançará" for its "lançarei").

### 3898 Heb 10:32-39 The sufferings of believers, and encouragement to maintain their holy profession.
- flag: Source typo: "great trails" read as "great trials" ("grandes provações").
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ap 2:10: "a coroa da vida" in the Bíblia Livre's words; the echo of 1 Pe 1:9 ("o resultado da vossa fé") is fitted to Henry's third person.

### 3899 Heb 11:1-3 The nature and power of faith described.
- flag: Henry's "subsistence or reality" plays on KJV "substance" and "evidence" (Hb 11:1); the Bíblia Livre has "certeza" and "prova". KJV "obtained a good report" (11:2) is "obtiveram testemunho" there.

### 3900 Heb 11:4-7 It is set forth by instances from Abel to Noah.
- quoted (Heb 11:5): “he should not see death” → “para não experimentar a morte”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “fugir da ira futura”
- flag: unlisted quotations of Hb 11:4 ("mesmo estando morto, ainda fala") and Hb 11:7 ("da justiça segundo a fé"; Henry's plural "heirs") in the Bíblia Livre's words; "translated" (KJV) is the Bíblia Livre's "transportado".

### 3901 Heb 11:8-19 By Abraham and his descendants.
- quoted (Heb 11:16): “God is not ashamed to be called” → “Deus não se envergonha … de ser chamado o Deus”
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has "Deus não se envergonha deles, de ser chamado o Deus deles"; its words are quoted on either side of "deles".

### 3902 Heb 11:20-31 By Jacob, Joseph, Moses, the Israelites, and Rahab.
- quoted (Heb 11:20): “Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau, concerning things to come” → “Isaque abençoou Jacó e Esaú quanto às coisas futuras”
- quoted (Heb 11:26): “greater riches than the treasures” → “riquezas maiores que os tesouros”
- quoted (Heb 11:25): “with the people of God” → “com o povo de Deus”
- flag: Henry's "no man knoweth love or hatred" echoes Ec 9:1; translated, not quoted (the Bíblia Livre words it differently).
- flag: KJV "the reproach of Christ" (Hb 11:26) is "as humilhações por causa de Cristo" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's "reproached people" rendered "povo vituperado". KJV "recompence of the reward" is just "recompensa" there; Henry's phrase translated "a recompensa do galardão".
- flag: Source oddity: Henry writes "even Babylon fall" in comments on Hb 11:30 (the walls of Jericho); kept as "Babilônia".

### 3903 Heb 11:32-38 By other Old Testament believers.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: 1 Pe 5:8 ("anda ao redor" ... "buscando a quem possa devorar"; it has "rugindo como um leão", so Henry's "roaring lion" is translated) and Hb 11:35 ("uma melhor ressurreição").
- flag: Source oddity: the section ends with a bare reference "(He 11:39)"; kept. Henry's "They are not worthy" means the world, rendered in the singular.

### 3904 Heb 11:39-40 The better state of believers under the gospel.
- flag: unlisted quotation of 1 Ts 1:3: "trabalho do amor" in the Bíblia Livre's words (its "firmeza da esperança" differs from Henry's "patience of hope", translated). The echo of Mt 13:43 is fitted to Henry's first person.

### 3905 Heb 12:1-11 An exhortation to be constant and persevere, The example of Christ is set forth, and the gracious design of God in all the sufferings believers endured.
- flag: Title: the source joins two headings with a comma ("..., The example of Christ ..."); kept as given.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 12:1: "o pecado que facilmente nos envolve" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "so easily"; it has no "so").

### 3906 Heb 12:12-17 Peace and holiness are recommended, with cautions against despising spiritual blessings.
- flag: Source typo: "there can be not right peace" read as "no right peace".
- flag: KJV "holiness" (Hb 12:14) is "santificação" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's word kept as "santidade".

### 3907 Heb 12:18-29 The New Testament dispensation shown to be much more excellent than the Old.
- quoted (Heb 12:28): “with reverence and godly fear” → “com devoção e temor”
- quoted (Heb 12:28): “with reverence and godly fear” → “com devoção e temor”
- flag: Quotes 0-1: KJV "reverence and godly fear" (Hb 12:28) is "devoção e temor" in the Bíblia Livre; used both times.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 12:25: "Tende o cuidado de não rejeitardes" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3908 Heb 13:1-6 Exhortations to various duties, and to be content with what Providence allots.
- quoted (Titus 2:14): “himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works” → “um povo particular, zeloso de boas obras”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry has "purchase to himself" where Tt 2:14 (KJV "purify", Bíblia Livre "purificar para si mesmo") differs; his verb is translated and the Bíblia Livre's words used from "um povo particular".
- flag: Henry's own emphatic rendering of Hb 13:5 in quotation marks ("I will never, no, never leave thee...") is translated to keep his point about the five negatives; the Bíblia Livre has "Não te deixarei, nem te desampararei".

### 3909 Heb 13:7-15 To respect the instructions of faithful pastors, with cautions against being carried away by strange doctrines.
- quoted (Heb 13:13): “Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp” → “Assim, saiamos até ele, fora do acampamento”
- quoted (Heb 13:15): “The sacrifice of praise to God” → “sacrifício de louvor a Deus”
- flag: Source slip: a stray full stop in "in the Old Testament day. as in the gospel day"; read as one sentence.
- flag: "the Bishop of their souls" (1 Pe 2:25 KJV) kept as "Bispo"; the Bíblia Livre has "Supervisor de vossas almas". Henry's double negative "Sin, sinners, nor death, will not suffer us" rendered as a plain negative.

### 3910 Heb 13:16-21 Further exhortations to duties, that relate to God, to our neighbour, and to those set over us in the Lord.
- quoted (Heb 13:17): “with joy, and not with grief” → “com alegria, e não gemendo”
- flag: Quote 0: KJV "with grief" (Hb 13:17) is "gemendo" in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: 1 Ts 2:13 ("a palavra de Deus, a qual também opera"), Hb 13:20 ("O Deus da paz") and Hb 13:21 ("em toda boa obra").

### 3912 James 1:1-11 How to apply to God under troubles, and how to behave in prosperous and in adverse circumstances.
- quoted (James 2:5): “rich in faith and heirs of the kingdom” → “ricos na fé, e herdeiros do reino”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Tg 1:5: "falta de sabedoria" and "lhe será dada" in the Bíblia Livre's words; Henry's "single" mind contrasts KJV "double minded" (Tg 1:8), "de dupla mentalidade" there.

### 3913 James 1:12-18 To look upon all evil as proceeding from ourselves, and all good from God.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: Tg 1:12 ("A coroa da vida"), Tg 1:17 ("toda boa dádiva") and Tg 1:18 ("os primeiros frutos dentre as suas criaturas"; KJV "a kind of firstfruits", Henry's "a kind of" as "como que").

### 3915 James 1:22-25 And of living according thereto.
- quoted (Col 1:12): “the inheritance of the saints in light” → “herança dos santos na luz”
- flag: Henry's point "not for his deeds ... but in his deed" rests on KJV Tg 1:25 "blessed in his deed"; the Bíblia Livre has "será bendito no que fizer", whose "no que fizer" is used here.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rm 8:15: "Espírito de adoção" in the Bíblia Livre's words. Henry's switch to "His gracious recompence" (the doer of Tg 1:25) kept in the third person.

### 3916 James 1:26-27 The difference between vain pretences and real religion.
- flag: Source oddity: "False religious may be known" (probably "false religionists" or "false religion"); rendered "Os falsos religiosos".
- flag: unlisted quotation of Gl 5:6: "que opera por meio do amor" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
