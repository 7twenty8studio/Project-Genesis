# To review: pt commentary batch 081

### 3450 Acts 7:1-16 Stephen's defence.
- flag: sensitive: "Stephen reminds the Jews of their mean beginning … the same spirit was still working in them toward Christ and his ministers"; translated in full.

### 3451 Acts 7:17-29 
- flag: Henry's "fair toward God" is the KJV margin of At 7:20 ("exceeding fair"); not listed, translated ("formoso diante de Deus"), not the Bíblia Livre's wording of v. 20.
- flag: "his holy child Jesus" echoes At 4:27 (KJV); translated ("seu santo filho Jesus").

### 3452 Acts 7:30-41 
- quoted (Acts 7:30): “in a flame of fire” → “em uma sarça inflamada”
- flag: Henry's "in a flame of fire" (KJV) is "em uma sarça inflamada" in the Bíblia Livre ("in a burning bush"), used as quoted; his next words "yet the bush was not consumed" follow it.
- flag: Source: "In asserting that Jesus should change the customs of the ceremonial law." is a fragment ended by a full stop (it belongs with the next sentence); kept as a fragment.
- flag: "for us men, and for our salvation, he came down from heaven" is the Nicene Creed's wording; translated.

### 3454 Acts 7:51-53 Stephen reproves the Jews for the death of Christ.
- quoted (John 4:23): “the Father in spirit and in truth” → “ao Pai em espírito e em verdade”

### 3455 Acts 7:54-60 The martyrdom of Stephen.
- quoted (Acts 7:55): “the right hand of God” → “à direita de Deus”
- flag: Henry's play "lay it to their hearts … not lay it to their charge" (KJV At 7:60) is rendered "tomassem a peito … não lho imputaria"; the play on "lay" is lost.
- flag: "fulness of joy … pleasures … at his right hand, for evermore" alludes to Sl 16:11; not listed, translated.

### 3457 Acts 8:5-13 Philip's success at Samaria. Simon the sorcerer baptized.
- quoted (Acts 8:10): “This man is the great power of God” → “Este é o grande poder de Deus”
- flag: Henry's "This man is the great power of God": the Bíblia Livre has "Este é", without "man".

### 3458 Acts 8:14-25 The hypocrisy of Simon detected.
- flag: Source typo: "none of these coverts" read as "converts" ("convertidos").
- flag: "the honour which cometh from God" echoes Jo 5:44; not listed, translated.

### 3459 Acts 8:26-40 Philip and the Ethiopian.
- flag: "go on his way rejoicing" echoes At 8:39 (KJV); not listed, translated ("seguirá o seu caminho alegre").

### 3460 Acts 9:1-9 The conversion of Saul.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 126:5 ("sow in tears … reap in joy"): the Bíblia Livre's "semeiam em lágrimas" and "colherão com alegria" used.
- flag: "he thought he … did God service" echoes Jo 16:2; translated.

### 3461 Acts 9:10-22 Saul converted preaches Christ.
- quoted (Acts 9:6): “Lord, what wilt thou have me to do” → “Senhor, que queres que eu faça”
- flag: "before, he said his prayers, now, he prayed them": rendered "recitava as suas orações … orava-as" to keep Henry's contrast.

### 3462 Acts 9:23-31 Saul is persecuted at Damascus, and goes to Jerusalem.
- quoted (1Cor 10:13): “will, with the temptation, also make a way to escape” → “com a tentação também dará a saída”
- quoted (Acts 9:31): “the comfort of the Holy Ghost” → “consolação do Espírito Santo”
- flag: "the Lord knows how to deliver the godly" (2 Pe 2:9) is an unlisted allusion; translated.
- flag: "Christ's witnesses cannot be slain till they have finished their testimony" alludes to Ap 11:7; translated.

### 3464 Acts 9:36-43 Dorcas raised to life.
- flag: sensitive: "How mean then the cares of the numerous females who seek no distinction but outward decoration, and who waste their lives in the trifling pursuits of dress and vanity!"; translated in full.
- flag: "whose own works praise them" echoes Pv 31:31; not listed, translated.

### 3465 Acts 10:1-8 Cornelius directed to send for Peter.
- flag: "those converts who had been circumcised": "converts" here means proselytes to Judaism, rendered "prosélitos".

### 3467 Acts 10:19-33 He goes to Cornelius.
- translated, not quoted (Acts 10:33; Henry turns Cornelius's words into the third person ("they are all present … to hear"); the Bíblia Livre's run "tudo quanto Deus tem te ordenado" is addressed to Peter and can't stand in his sentence.): “to hear all things that are commanded”

### 3468 Acts 10:34-43 His discourse to Cornelius.
- quoted (Eph 2:20): “built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets” → “sobre o fundamento dos apóstolos e dos profetas”
- flag: Source: "so every one must seek his favour, and to have him as our Friend" mixes persons; translated as it stands.

### 3469 Acts 10:44-48 The gifts of the Holy Spirit poured out.
- quoted (John 6:63): “it is the Spirit that quickeneth, the flesh profiteth nothing” → “O Espírito é o que vivifica, a carne para nada aproveita”
- quoted (Acts 10:45): “the gift of the Holy Ghost” → “o dom do Espírito Santo”
- quoted (Acts 10:47): “have received the Holy Ghost” → “receberam o Espírito Santo”

### 3470 Acts 11:1-18 Peter's defence.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 51:17 ("The sacrifice of God is a broken spirit"): the Bíblia Livre's "Os sacrifícios a Deus são um espírito quebrado" used (its verse goes on "em arrependimento").
- flag: "takes away the heart of stone, and gives us a heart of flesh" (Ez 36:26) is an unlisted allusion; translated.

### 3471 Acts 11:19-24 The success of the gospel at Antioch.
- quoted (Acts 11:21): “The hand of the Lord was with them” → “A mão do Senhor era com eles”
- quoted (Acts 6:5): “full of faith and of the Holy Ghost” → “cheio de fé e do Espírito Santo”
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's plural "really good men, who are full of faith and of the Holy Ghost" is put in the singular ("todo homem realmente bom, cheio de fé e do Espírito Santo") so At 6:5's words stand unchanged.
- flag: Source: "will turn to the Lord, When the Lord Jesus" has a comma where a full stop belongs; a full stop used.
- flag: "The wrath of man is made to praise God" alludes to Sl 76:10; not listed, translated.

### 3472 Acts 11:25-30 The disciples named Christians, Relief sent to Judea.
- quoted (1Tim 4:8): “promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come” → “as promessas da vida atual e da futura”
- flag: Source: "the possession of it will give both the promise of the life that now is…": "it" read as the reality of the name ("a posse da realidade"); Henry's singular "promise" follows the Bíblia Livre's plural "promessas" in the quotation.

### 3473 Acts 12:1-5 The martyrdom of James, and the imprisonment of Peter.
- translated, not quoted (Mark 10:38; Henry reports Christ's words in the third person and past tense ("that they should drink … that he was to drink of"); the Bíblia Livre's question in Mc 10:38 ("podeis vós beber o copo que eu bebo") can't stand in his sentence.): “drink of, and be baptized with the baptism”
- flag: Henry cites Mt 20:23; the listed words are from Mc 10:38. Translated with "cálice" (the Bíblia Livre's Mc 10:38 has "copo").

### 3474 Acts 12:6-11 He is delivered from prison by an angel.
- flag: "proclaims liberty to the captives" (Is 61:1): the Bíblia Livre's "anunciar liberdade aos cativos" used; unlisted.

### 3475 Acts 12:12-19 Peter departs, Herod's rage.
- quoted (Luke 18:1): “men ought always to pray, and not to faint” → “sempre orar, e nunca se cansar”
- flag: Source typo: "the range of persecutors" read as "rage" ("fúria").

### 3476 Acts 12:20-25 The death of Herod.
- flag: sensitive: "Many heathen princes claimed and received Divine honours" ("príncipes pagãos"); translated in full.

### 3477 Acts 13:1-3 The mission of Paul and Barnabas.
- flag: "separated to take pains, and not to take state": Henry's wordplay on "take" is lost ("para se esforçarem no trabalho, e não para ostentarem grandeza").

### 3478 Acts 13:4-13 Elymas the sorcerer.
- flag: "put their hand to the plough and look back, are not fit for the kingdom of God" (Lc 9:62), unlisted: put in the plural with the Bíblia Livre's words "colocar a sua mão no arado … olhar para trás … apto para o reino de Deus" as nearly as the plural allows.
- flag: "he gave Elymas his true character": i.e. described him truly ("deu a Elimas o seu verdadeiro caráter"), kept literally.

### 3479 Acts 13:14-31 Paul's discourse at Antioch.
- flag: "saw no corruption" (At 13:37, KJV) is translated ("não viu corrupção"); not listed.

### 3480 Acts 13:32-37 
- quoted (Acts 13:34): “The sure mercies of David” → “As fiéis beneficências de Davi”
- flag: Henry's "sure mercies of David" (KJV) is "as fiéis beneficências de Davi" in the Bíblia Livre, used as quoted.
