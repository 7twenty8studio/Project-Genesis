# To review: pt commentary batch 089

### 3686 2Cor 6:11-18 By affection for them, And by earnest concern, that they might have no fellowship with unbelievers and idolaters.
- flag: The English reads "they must expect must distress", evidently a slip for "much distress"; translated as "muita aflição".
- flag: "Unequally yoked": the Bíblia Livre's 2 Co 6:14 has "não vos ajunteis em outro jugo"; translated with the usual "jugo desigual" since Henry's phrase is his own sentence.
- flag: "Help meet" (Gn 2:18) given as "ajuda idônea", the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3687 2Cor 7:1-4 An exhortation to holiness, and the whole church entreated to bear affection to the apostle.
- quoted (2Cor 7:1): “cleanse ourselves from all filthiness” → “de toda impureza da carne e do espírito”
- flag: Henry's "we must cleanse ourselves" changes the verse's exhortation into "devemos limpar-nos"; the rest ("de toda impureza da carne e do espírito") is the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3688 2Cor 7:5-11 He rejoiced in their sorrowing to repentance.
- quoted (2Cor 7:10): “the sorrow of the world” → “a tristeza do mundo”
- flag: Henry's "revenge" follows the KJV of 2 Co 7:11; the Bíblia Livre has "desejo de justiça" there. Translated as "vingança" to keep his explanation.

### 3691 2Cor 8:7-9 Enforces this by their gifts, and by the love and grace of Christ.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Heb 11:6: "sem fé é impossível agradar a Deus" (Bíblia Livre).

### 3694 2Cor 9:1-5 The reason for sending Titus to collect their alms.
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Titus 2:10: "a doutrina de Deus" follows the Bíblia Livre's words ("adornem a doutrina de Deus nosso Salvador"), with Henry's "their Saviour" as "seu Salvador".

### 3695 2Cor 9:6-15 The Corinthians to be liberal and cheerful, The apostle thanks God for his unspeakable gift.
- quoted (2Cor 9:8): “is able to make all grace abound” → “é poderoso para fazer abundar”
- quoted (Acts 20:35): “more blessed to give than to receive” → “mais bem-aventurado é dar do que receber”
- flag: Henry's "unspeakable gift" (2 Co 9:15) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "dom indescritível".

### 3696 2Cor 10:1-6 The apostle states his authority with meekness and humility.
- quoted (2Cor 10:4): “the weapons of our warfare are” → “as armas de nossa batalha”

### 3697 2Cor 10:7-11 Reasons with the Corinthians.
- flag: The English is garbled: "We must not think that none outward appearance, as if…" has words missing (perhaps "think the worse of any for their mean outward appearance"); translated word for word as "Não devemos pensar que nenhuma aparência exterior, como se…", nothing supplied.

### 3698 2Cor 10:12-18 Seeks the glory of God, and to be approved of him.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of John 5:44: "a honra que vem somente de Deus" (Bíblia Livre).

### 3700 2Cor 11:5-15 Shows that he had freely preached the gospel.
- flag: "Children of disobedience" (Ef 2:2) and "deceitful workers" (2 Co 11:13) are given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "filhos da desobediência" and "trabalhadores fraudulentos".

### 3702 2Cor 11:22-33 He gives an account of his labours, cares, sufferings, dangers, and deliverances.
- quoted (Rom 15:6): “the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “Pai de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”

### 3703 2Cor 12:1-6 The apostle's revelations.
- quoted (1Pet 5:1): “of the glory that shall be revealed” → “da glória que se revelará”

### 3704 2Cor 12:7-10 Which were improved to his spiritual advantage.
- quoted (Rom 16:20): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “graça de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- flag: Henry's "His strength is made perfect in our weakness" (2 Co 12:9) is given close to the Bíblia Livre ("meu poder se aperfeiçoa na fraqueza"), with his change of person: "O seu poder se aperfeiçoa na nossa fraqueza".

### 3705 2Cor 12:11-21 The signs of an apostle were in him, His purpose of making them a visit; but he expresses his fear lest he should have to be severe with some.
- flag: "These vast verses" is odd in the English (perhaps a slip for "these last verses"); translated literally as "Estes vastos versículos".

### 3707 2Cor 13:7-10 He prays for their reformation.
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Isa 1:16-17: "parem de fazer maldades" and "aprendam a fazer o bem" follow the Bíblia Livre's words ("parai de fazer maldades", "Aprendei a fazer o bem") in Henry's third person.

### 3709 Gal 1:1-5 The apostle Paul asserts his apostolic character against such as lessened it.
- quoted (Gal 1:4): “gave himself for our sins” → “deu a si mesmo pelos nossos pecados”
- quoted (Gal 1:4): “from this present evil world” → “da presente era maligna”
- flag: "This present evil world" is given in the Bíblia Livre's words of Gl 1:4, "da presente era maligna" ("age" rather than "world").
- flag: The English places the full stop inside the quotation ("for our sins."); the Portuguese puts it after, as Portuguese punctuation does.

### 3712 Gal 1:15-24 And how he proceeded after it.
- quoted (Eph 1:6): “to the praise of the glory of his grace” → “para louvor da glória de sua graça”

### 3713 Gal 2:1-10 The apostle declares his being owned as an apostle of the Gentiles.
- quoted (Gal 2:5): “the truth of the gospel” → “a verdade do Evangelho”
- flag: Henry's "the right hand of fellowship" (Gl 2:9) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "as mãos direitas de comunhão".
- flag: Henry's "heathen" here means the Gentiles as a mission field; translated "gentios".

### 3714 Gal 2:11-14 He had publicly opposed Peter for judaizing.
- quoted (Gal 2:14): “the truth of the gospel” → “a verdade do Evangelho”

### 3715 Gal 2:15-19 And from thence he enters upon the doctrine of justification by faith in Christ, without the works of the law.
- quoted (Gal 2:16): “we might be justified by the faith of Christ” → “para que fôssemos justificados pela fé em Cristo”
- quoted (Gal 2:17): “if, while we seek to be justified by Christ, we ourselves also are found sinners” → “se nós, que buscamos ser justificados em Cristo, também nós mesmos somos achados pecadores”
- quoted (Gal 2:17): “Christ the minister of sin” → “por isso Cristo contribui com o pecado”
- translated, not quoted (Gal 2:17; Henry's own sentence turns the verse's question into "to make Christ the minister of sin"; the Bíblia Livre's "Cristo contribui com o pecado" cannot carry his phrase, so it is translated ("fazer de Cristo ministro do pecado").): “Christ the minister of sin”
- flag: Henry's point rests on the KJV's "minister of sin" (Gl 2:17); the Bíblia Livre has "Cristo contribui com o pecado". The quotation keeps the Bíblia Livre's words; his later phrase is translated "ministro do pecado".

### 3716 Gal 2:20-21 
- quoted (Gal 2:20): “loved me, and gave himself for me” → “me amou, e entregou a si mesmo por mim”
- flag: Henry's "The old man is crucified, Ro 6:6" is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "O nosso velho ser foi crucificado"; his following "the new man" is translated "o novo homem".
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 2 Tim 3:5: "têm uma aparência de devoção divina" (Bíblia Livre); Henry's "without the power of it" translated "sem o poder dela".

### 3717 Gal 3:1-5 The Galatians reproved for departing from the great doctrine of justification alone, through faith in Christ.
- flag: "The god of this world" (2 Co 4:4) is given in the Bíblia Livre's words, "O deus destes tempos".
- flag: The English title reads "justification alone, through faith in Christ"; translated as "justificação somente pela fé em Cristo".
