# To review: pt commentary batch 063

### 2860 Amos 5:7-17 Earnest exhortations to repentance.
- flag: "create in us a clean heart, and to renew a right spirit within us" alludes to Ps 51:10; rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "coração puro" and "espírito firme" in Henry's construction.
- flag: "pass through them" echoes Am 5:17 (Bíblia Livre "passarei por meio de ti").

### 2861 Amos 5:18-27 Threatenings respecting idolatries.
- flag: the English has a lower-case "but" after the exclamation mark; a new sentence begins in the translation.

### 2862 Amos 6:1-7 The danger of luxury and false security.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Am 6:1: "os que estão tranquilos em Sião" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 2864 Amos 7:1-9 Visions of judgments to come upon Israel.
- flag: "He measures it" refers to the plumb line of Am 7:7-8 (Bíblia Livre "prumo").

### 2865 Amos 7:10-17 Amaziah threatens Amos.
- quoted (1Cor 1:27): “foolish things of the world to confound the wise” → “as coisas loucas deste mundo para envergonhar as sábias”
- flag: "set his face as a flint" alludes to Is 50:7 (Bíblia Livre "pus meu rosto como pedra muito dura"); translated.

### 2866 Amos 8:1-3 The near approach of the ruin of Israel.
- quoted (Amos 8:1): “a basket of summer fruit” → “um cesto com frutos de verão”

### 2867 Amos 8:4-10 Oppression reproved.
- flag: Henry applies the sabbath to Christians here ("many who are called Christians"), meaning the Lord's day; "sábado" kept as he wrote it, without the bracketed note, since he doesn't say "the Christian sabbath".
- flag: "reproaches his Maker" (Pv 17:5) and "rich and poor meet together" (Pv 22:2) use the Bíblia Livre's "insulta o seu Criador" and "o rico e o pobre se encontram" (unlisted quotations).
- flag: "in the midst of life we are in death" is from the Book of Common Prayer's burial service, not Scripture; translated.

### 2869 Amos 9:1-10 The ruin of Israel.
- quoted (Amos 9:1): “saw the Lord standing upon” → “o Senhor que estava sobre”
- quoted (Amos 9:4): “eyes upon them for evil” → “olhos sobre eles para o mal”
- quoted (Amos 9:4): “evil, and not for good” → “para o mal, e não para o bem”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Am 9:9: "assim como o grão é cirandado na peneira" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 2870 Amos 9:11-15 The restoration of the Jews and the gospel blessing.
- quoted (John 11:52): “the children of God that were scattered abroad” → “filhos de Deus, que estavam dispersos”

### 2872 Obad 1:17-21 The restoration of the Jews, and their flourishing state in the latter times.
- flag: unlisted quotations: "o reino pertencerá ao SENHOR" (Ob 21) and "cheiro de vida, para a vida" (2 Co 2:16) in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 2873 Jonah 1:1-3 Jonah, sent to Nineveh, flees to Tarshish.
- translated, not quoted (Jonah 1:1; a common phrase in Henry's own sentence about us ("when the word of the Lord comes to us"), not a quotation of Jonah 1:1.): “the word of the Lord”

### 2874 Jonah 1:4-7 He is stayed by a tempest.
- quoted (Jonah 1:7): “The lot fell upon Jonah” → “A sorte caiu sobre Jonas”
- quoted (Job 28:21): “hid from the eyes of all living” → “aos olhos de todo vivente”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jó 10:2: "faz-me saber por que brigas comigo" in the Bíblia Livre's words, after Henry's "Lord, show me wherefore thou contendest with me".

### 2875 Jonah 1:8-12 His discourse with the mariners.
- quoted (Jonah 1:10): “him, Why hast thou done this” → “Por que fizeste isto”
- quoted (Jonah 1:9): “made the sea and the dry land” → “que fez o mar e a terra”
- flag: the Bíblia Livre's Jonah 1:9 has "a terra" for the KJV's "the dry land".

### 2876 Jonah 1:13-17 He is cast into the sea, and miraculously preserved.
- flag: "It was of the Lord's mercies that Jonah was not consumed" adapts Lm 3:22 (Bíblia Livre "É pelas bondades do SENHOR que não somos consumidos"); translated in Henry's form.

### 2877 Jonah 2:1-9 The prayer of Jonah.
- translated, not quoted (Jonah 1:10; the listed verse (Jonah 1:10, "estava fugindo do SENHOR") is not Henry's wording, which is Jonah 1:3's "from the presence of the Lord"; his past-perfect sentence about Jonah is translated.): “fled from the presence of the Lord”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jn 2:8: "dão atenção a coisas inúteis e ilusórias" and "abandonam sua própria misericórdia" follow the Bíblia Livre (KJV "observe lying vanities forsake their own mercy").

### 2879 Jonah 3:1-4 Jonah sent again to Nineveh, preaches there.
- translated, not quoted (Jonah 3:1; a common phrase in Henry's own sentence ("we must do whatever the word of the Lord commands"), not a quotation of Jonah 3:1.): “the word of the Lord”

### 2880 Jonah 3:5-10 Nineveh is spared upon the repentance of the inhabitants.
- flag: "a broken and a contrite heart ... he will not despise" alludes to Ps 51:17 (Bíblia Livre "tu não desprezarás um coração quebrado e triste"); translated in Henry's form.

### 2881 Jonah 4:1-4 Jonah repines at God's mercy to Nineveh, and is reproved.
- flag: "Do I well to be angry" follows the KJV of Jonah 4:4; the Bíblia Livre has "É correta essa tua ira?"; Henry's questions are translated as "Faço bem em irar-me".

### 2882 Jonah 4:5-11 He is taught by the withering of a gourd, that he did wrong.
- quoted (Jonah 4:5): “Jonah went out of the city” → “Jonas saiu da cidade”
- flag: "gourd" (KJV) rendered "aboboreira" throughout; the Bíblia Livre's Jonah 4:6-10 has "planta".

### 2884 Micah 1:8-16 Also against Jerusalem and other cities, Their precautions vain.
- quoted (Micah 1:10): “Roll thyself in the dust” → “Revolve-te no pó”
- flag: the title has a capital after a comma in the English ("cities, Their precautions"); made two sentences.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mq 1:10: "Não anunciai isto em Gate" in the Bíblia Livre's words; the Bíblia Livre's place name is "Bete-Le-Afra", Henry's "Aphrah" given as "Afra".
- flag: the English ends "will some come", a typo for "soon"; translated "logo virá".

### 2885 Micah 2:1-5 The sins and desolations of Israel.
- quoted (Micah 2:5): “the congregation of the Lord” → “congregação do SENHOR”

### 2886 Micah 2:6-11 Their evil practices.
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: "Não profetizeis" (Mq 2:6, inside Henry's own quotation marks) and "não temos aqui cidade permanente" (Hb 13:14).

### 2888 Micah 3:1-8 The cruelty of the princes, and the falsehood of the prophets.
- quoted (Micah 3:8): “full of power by the Spirit of the Lord” → “cheio do poder do Espírito do SENHOR”

### 2889 Micah 3:9-12 Their false security.
- quoted (Micah 3:12): “Therefore shall Zion for your sake” → “Portanto por causa de vós Sião será arada como campo”
- flag: the listed run ("Therefore shall Zion for your sake") stops before Henry's "be ploughed as a field"; the Bíblia Livre's words are used for the whole of his quotation.

### 2890 Micah 4:1-8 The peace of the kingdom of Christ.
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's word "halteth" (Mq 4:6); the Bíblia Livre has "a que mancava", which is what the translation names.
- flag: "beat their swords into ploughshares" (Mq 4:3, KJV); the Bíblia Livre has "adaptarão suas espadas para enxadas"; Henry's phrase translated.
