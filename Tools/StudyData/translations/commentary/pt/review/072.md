### 3130 Mark 4:21-34 Other parables.

- flag: unlisted quotation of Zech 4:10: 'o dia das pequenas coisas' (Bíblia Livre). Henry's 'conversation' (conduct) rendered 'conduta'.

### 3131 Mark 4:35-41 Christ stills the tempest.
- flag: unlisted quotations (Bíblia Livre): Isa 57:20 'o mar bravo, que não pode se aquietar'; 2Cor 7:5 'lutas por fora, temores por dentro'; Mark 4:39 'Cala-te, aquieta-te!' (in Henry's quotation marks) and 'grande bonança'; Mark 4:40 'Por que sois tão covardes?' (KJV 'so fearful'; Henry's next sentence speaks of 'fear').

### 3132 Mark 5:1-20 The demoniac healed.
- quoted (Eph 6:10): “in the Lord, and in the power of his might” → “no Senhor e na força do seu poder”
- quoted (Mark 5:20): “great things Jesus had done for him” → “quão grandes coisas Jesus havia feito com ele”
- flag: 'Many there are that rise up against us' echoes Ps 3:1 in the first person plural; translated. 'wonder after him' (Rev 13:3 idiom) rendered 'vão … admirados após ele'.

### 3133 Mark 5:21-34 A woman healed.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Job 13:4: 'médicos inúteis' (Bíblia Livre; KJV 'physicians of no value').

### 3134 Mark 5:35-43 The daughter of Jairus raised.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Eph 2:1: 'mortos em ofensas e pecados' (Bíblia Livre; KJV 'trespasses and sins').

### 3135 Mark 6:1-6 Christ despised in his own country.
- quoted (Mark 6:3): “Is not this the carpenter” → “Não é este o carpinteiro”
- flag: unlisted quotation of 2Cor 2:16: 'cheiro de morte' (Bíblia Livre; KJV 'savour of death').

### 3136 Mark 6:7-13 The apostles sent forth.
- flag: English 'wordly' is a typo for 'worldly'; translated as 'mundana'.

### 3142 Mark 7:24-30 The woman of Canaan's daughter cured.

### 3146 Mark 8:22-26 A blind man healed.
- flag: 'shines more and more to the perfect day' (Prov 4:18) translated: the Bíblia Livre's wording ('ilumina até o dia ficar claro por completo') doesn't fit Henry's sentence.

### 3147 Mark 8:27-33 Peter's testimony to Christ.

### 3149 Mark 9:1-13 The transfiguration.
- flag: English 'the near approach Christ's kingdom' lacks 'of'; translated as 'of'.

### 3150 Mark 9:14-29 An evil spirit cast out.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mark 9:23: 'Se podes crer' (Bíblia Livre).

### 3151 Mark 9:30-40 The apostles reproved.
- flag: 'whoever shall receive one like this child, receives me' (Mark 9:37) is Henry's paraphrase; translated, ending with the Bíblia Livre's 'recebe a mim'.

### 3152 Mark 9:41-50 Pain to be preferred to sin.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mark 9:44, 48: 'verme não morre' and 'o fogo nunca se apaga' (Bíblia Livre). 'salted with salt' (Mark 9:49) put in the plural ('salgados com sal').

### 3154 Mark 10:13-16 Christ's love to little children.
- quoted (Mark 10:15): “receive the kingdom of God” → “receber o Reino de Deus”

### 3155 Mark 10:17-22 Christ's discourse with the rich young man.
- quoted (Matt 6:24): “Ye cannot serve God and mammon” → “Não podeis servir a Deus e às riquezas”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has 'riquezas' for the KJV's 'mammon'. 'He went away grieved' (Mark 10:22) given with the Bíblia Livre's 'foi-se triste'.

### 3158 Mark 10:46-52 Bartimeus healed.
- quoted (Heb 12:1): “every weight, and the sin” → “toda sobrecarga, e o pecado”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mark 10:52: 'a tua fé te salvou' (Bíblia Livre; KJV 'hath made thee whole'). Henry's 'free themselves from' rendered 'deixar' to fit the Bíblia Livre's 'deixemos toda sobrecarga'.

### 3159 Mark 11:1-11 Christ's triumphant entry into Jerusalem.
- quoted (Mark 11:9): “Blessed is he that cometh” → “Bendito o que vem”
- quoted (Mark 11:9): “in the name of the Lord” → “no Nome do Senhor”
- flag: source oddity: this entry's text opens with the heading of the next section ('The barren fig-tree cursed, The temple cleansed.') and its verse range '(12-18)', which seem to belong to section 3160 (which has no title); translated where they stand.
- flag: unlisted quotations (Bíblia Livre): Matt 11:3 'aquele que havia de vir' (in Henry's quotation marks); Rom 9:5 'Deus bendito eternamente'. 'mind not high things, condescend to … low estate' (Rom 12:16) translated (the Bíblia Livre words it differently).

### 3160 Mark 11:12-18 
- quoted (Mark 11:18): “how they might destroy him” → “uma maneira de o matar”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre has 'matar' for the KJV's 'destroy'. Unlisted quotation of Rom 11:26: 'as irreverências de Jacó' (Bíblia Livre; KJV 'ungodliness').
- flag: sensitive: Henry calls the cursed fig-tree 'a figure of the doom upon the Jewish church'; translated in full.

### 3161 Mark 11:19-26 Prayer in faith.
