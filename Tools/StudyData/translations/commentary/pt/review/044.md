# To review: pt commentary batch 044

### 1667 Ps 119:113-120 
- quoted (Heb 4:1): “fear, lest a promise being left us of entering into” → “havendo sido deixada a promessa de entrar”
- quoted (Heb 4:1): “to come short of it” → “tenha ficado para trás”
- flag: The English runs "See what comes of sin Surely" without a stop; translated as two sentences ("Vede no que dá o pecado. Certamente…").

### 1668 Ps 119:121-128 
- flag: The English has the typo "favour form God"; translated as "favor de Deus".

### 1669 Ps 119:129-136 
- quoted (Ps 119:132): “Look thou upon me, and be merciful unto me as thou usest to do unto those that love thy name” → “Olha-me, e tem piedade de mim; conforme teu costume para com os que amam o teu nome”

### 1670 Ps 119:137-144 
- quoted (John 17:3): “This is life eternal” → “esta é a vida eterna”
- quoted (John 17:3): “God and Jesus Christ whom” → “e a Jesus Cristo, a quem”

### 1674 Ps 119:169-176 
- quoted (Ps 119:175): “Let thy judgments help me” → “que teus juízos me socorram”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jer 31:18 ("Turn me, and I shall be turned"): the Bíblia Livre's "converte-me e serei convertido" used.
- flag: "Seek me" paraphrases Ps 119:176 KJV "seek thy servant"; the Bíblia Livre has "busca a teu servo"; Henry's "Busca-me" kept.

### 1675 Ps 120:1-4 The psalmist prays to God to deliver him from false and malicious tongues.
- flag: "coals of juniper" (Ps 120:4): the Bíblia Livre also has "brasas de zimbro".
- flag: "all that love and make a lie" echoes Rev 22:15 KJV; translated (close to the Bíblia Livre's "todo aquele que ama e pratica a mentira").

### 1678 Ps 122:1-5 Esteem for Jerusalem.
- quoted (Eph 4:3): “the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace” → “a unidade do Espírito pelo vínculo da paz”

### 1680 Ps 123:1-4 Confidence in God under contempt.
- quoted (Luke 12:42): “their portion of meat in due season” → “alimento no tempo certo”
- flag: Henry's "Master" (of the servant, and of God) is rendered "senhor"/"Senhor", as Portuguese has no separate word; the Bíblia Livre's Ps 123:2 also has "senhores".

### 1682 Ps 124:6-8 Thankfulness for the deliverance.
- quoted (Ps 124:8): “who made heaven and earth” → “que fez o céu e a terra”

### 1683 Ps 125:1-3 The security of the righteous.
- quoted (Ps 125:1): “shall be as Mount Zion” → “como o monte de Sião”
- flag: The English "their families names" lacks a comma or apostrophe; read as "their families, names" ("as suas famílias, os seus nomes").
- flag: "The rod of the wicked … shall not rest" follows Ps 125:3 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "o cetro da maldade não repousará sobre a sorte dos justos": Henry's "vara" (rod) kept, "sorte" for "lot" as in the Bíblia Livre.

### 1684 Ps 125:4-5 Prayer for them, The ruin of the wicked.
- flag: The English "befal" is an old spelling of "befall"; translated normally.

### 1686 Ps 126:4-6 Those yet in captivity are encouraged.
- quoted (Gal 6:8, Gal 6:9): “to the Spirit, shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting” → “para o Espírito, do Espírito colherá a vida eterna”
- quoted (Gal 6:7): “a man soweth that shall he” → “Deus não se deixa escarnecer; pois, o que o ser humano semear, isso também colherá”
- flag: Henry's "they that sow … shall … reap" (plural) is put in the singular ("quem semear … colherá") so the Bíblia Livre's words of Gal 6:8 stand unchanged.
- flag: "Blessed are those that mourn, for they shall be for ever comforted" is Henry's expansion of Mt 5:4 ("for ever" added); translated (the Bíblia Livre has "Benditos são os que choram, porque eles serão consolados").

### 1687 Ps 127:1-5 The value of the Divine blessing.
- quoted (Ps 127:1): “Except the Lord keep the city” → “se o SENHOR não estiver guardando a cidade”
- flag: "a heritage, and a reward" follows Ps 127:3 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "um presente do SENHOR" and "uma recompensa"; Henry's "herança" kept.

### 1688 Ps 128:1-6 The blessings of those who fear God.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 128:2; Henry speaks to the reader ("all shall be well with thee"); the Bíblia Livre's Ps 128:2 "bem lhe sucederá" is in the third person and can't stand in his sentence.): “shall be well with thee”

### 1689 Ps 129:1-4 Thankfulness for former deliverances.
- quoted (Dan 7:25): “wear out the saints of the Most High” → “os santos do Altíssimo”
- flag: Henry's "wear out" (Dan 7:25 KJV) is "destruirá" in the Bíblia Livre; rendered "destruir", the quotation keeping "os santos do Altíssimo".

### 1691 Ps 130:1-4 The psalmist's hope in prayer.
- quoted (Ps 130:4): “There is forgiveness with thee” → “contigo está o perdão”

### 1692 Ps 130:5-8 His patience in hope.
- flag: "plenteous redemption" follows Ps 130:7 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "com ele muito resgate"; rendered "redenção copiosa".

### 1693 Ps 131:1-3 The psalmist's humility. Believers encouraged to trust in God.
- quoted (Matt 18:3): “become as little children” → “como crianças”
- quoted (Lam 3:26): “wait for the salvation of the Lord” → “É bom esperar e tranquilo aguardar a salvação do SENHOR”
- flag: Henry's "weaned child" (Ps 131:2 KJV) is rendered "criança desmamada"; the Bíblia Livre has "tal como uma criança com sua mãe; como um bebê", without the weaning.
- flag: "When our condition is not to our mind, we must bring our mind to our condition": the play on "mind" rendered with "gosto" ("do nosso gosto … o nosso gosto").

### 1694 Ps 132:1-10 David's care for the ark.
- quoted (Ps 132:5): “a place for the Lord” → “um lugar para o SENHOR”
- quoted (Prov 27:1): “not what a day may bring forth” → “o que o dia trará”

### 1700 Ps 135:15-21 The vanity of idols.
- flag: sensitive: Henry speaks of "the heathen" and "benighted heathens" ("os pagãos em trevas"), section 1700.

### 1701 Ps 136:1-9 God to be praised as the Creator of the world.
- flag: Henry explains the word "mercy" of Ps 136 KJV ("his mercy endureth for ever"); the Bíblia Livre has "bondade" ("sua bondade dura para sempre"); his "misericórdia" kept.

### 1702 Ps 136:10-22 As Israel's God and Saviour.
- quoted (Ps 136:7): “his mercy endureth for ever” → “sua bondade dura para sempre”
- quoted (Jude 1:21): “the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “a misericórdia de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- flag: "his mercy endureth for ever" is "sua bondade dura para sempre" in the Bíblia Livre (Ps 136), so "mercy" here is "bondade".
