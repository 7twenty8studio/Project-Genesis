# To review: pt commentary batch 014

### 436 Num 15:1-21 The law of the meat-offering and the drink-offering, The stranger under the same law.
- quoted (Num 15:2): “which I give unto you” → “Quando houverdes entrado na terra de vossas habitações, que eu vos dou”
- flag: Henry quotes only "which I give unto you"; the Portuguese quotes the whole clause of Nm 15:2 he paraphrases ("When ye come into the land of your habitation"), since that clause is his own wording of the verse.

### 437 Num 15:22-29 The sacrifice for the sin of ignorance.
- quoted (Luke 23:34): “Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do” → “Pai, perdoa-lhes, porque não sabem o que fazem”

### 438 Num 15:30-36 The punishment of presumption, The sabbath-breaker stoned.
- quoted (Num 15:31): “the word of the Lord” → “a palavra do SENHOR”

### 439 Num 15:37-41 The law for fringes on garment.
- quoted (Num 15:38): “the borders of their garments” → “nos arremates de suas roupas”
- quoted (2Pet 3:1): “minds by way of remembrance” → “por meio da relembrança”
- quoted (Num 15:40): “Be holy unto your God” → “santos a vosso Deus”
- quoted (Num 15:41): “I am the Lord your God” → “Eu sou o SENHOR vosso Deus”
- flag: 2 Pe 3:1: Henry's "stir up their minds"; the Bíblia Livre says "desperto vosso sincero entendimento", so "despertar o seu entendimento" is used before the quoted words.

### 440 Num 16:1-11 The rebellion of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram Korah contends for the priesthood.
- flag: Point 2 is garbled in the English ("What usage they have been serviceable"); rendered as "what treatment those who served them receive"; check against a full edition.
- flag: "The heart of the wise studies to answer" echoes Pv 15:28 (KJV "righteous"); not listed, translated as Henry's own sentence.

### 442 Num 16:16-22 The glory of the Lord appears, The intercession of Moses and Aaron.
- quoted (Lev 9:23): “glory of the Lord” → “glória do SENHOR”

### 444 Num 16:35-40 The company of Korah consumed.
- quoted (Num 16:35): “from the Lord, and consumed the two hundred and fifty men that offered incense” → “fogo do SENHOR, e consumiu os duzentos e cinquenta homens que ofereciam o incenso”
- quoted (Prov 15:8): “The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord” → “O sacrifício dos perversos é abominável ao SENHOR”
- translated, not quoted (Deut 17:13; Henry turns Dt 17:13's future ("all the people shall hear") into a purpose clause about others; the Bíblia Livre's "não se ensoberbecerão mais" also differs from "do no more presumptuously".): “hear and fear, and do no more presumptuously”

### 445 Num 16:41-50 The people murmur A plague sent.
- quoted (Num 16:41, Num 16:42): “the people of the Lord” → “o povo do SENHOR”
- quoted (Num 16:48): “between the dead and the living” → “entre os mortos e os vivos”
- quoted (Rom 5:8): “us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us” → “o seu amor por nós através de Cristo haver morrido por nós, quando ainda éramos pecadores”
- flag: "redemption through His blood, even the remission of sins" (Ef 1:7 / Cl 1:14) is unlisted and changed by Henry ("We must have"); translated, not quoted (the Bíblia Livre has "libertação pelo seu sangue").

### 447 Num 17:8-13 Aaron's rod buds, and is kept for a memorial.
- quoted (Isa 11:1): “a rod out of the stem of Jesse” → “brotará do tronco cortado de Jessé”
- quoted (Num 17:12): “Behold, we die, we perish, we all perish” → “Eis que nós somos mortos, perdidos somos, todos nós somos perdidos”
- flag: Is 11:1: the Bíblia Livre says "vara brotará do tronco cortado de Jessé" (KJV "stem"); Henry's "little prospect... that he should ever flourish" fits the cut stump.

### 448 Num 18:1-7 The charge of the priests and Levites.
- flag: "Be not high-minded, but fear" (Rm 11:20) is unlisted; translated, as the Bíblia Livre's "Não tenhas orgulho, mas sim temor" reads awkwardly as a standalone maxim.

### 449 Num 18:8-19 The priests' portion.
- flag: Unlisted: 1 Tm 4:8 paraphrased (translated); "Deus não se deixa escarnecer" is Gl 6:7 in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: "Scandalous maintenance makes scandalous ministers": "scandalous" in the old sense of shamefully meagre; rendered "vergonhoso".

### 450 Num 18:20-32 The Levites' portion.
- translated, not quoted (Num 18:32; Henry changes the person ("so as to bear no sin by reason of them", our possessions); not the verse's own words there.): “bear no sin by reason”
- quoted (Num 18:32): “Ye shall bear no sin by reason of it, when ye have heaved” → “não levareis por ele pecado … quando vós houverdes oferecido disso o melhor seu”
- flag: Unlisted: "não será contado entre as nações" (Nm 23:9) and "se alguém não quiser trabalhar, também não coma" (2 Ts 3:10) in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: Nm 18:32: the Bíblia Livre's clause order is reversed ("quando ... o melhor seu, não levareis..."); the two runs are given in Henry's order.

### 452 Num 19:11-22 Used to purify the unclean.
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "trouxe para a luz a vida e a imortalidade" (2 Tm 1:10) and "rios de água viva" (Jo 7:38).

### 453 Num 20:1-13 The people come to Zin, They murmur for water, Moses directed to smite the rock, The infirmity of Moses and Aaron.
- quoted (Num 20:2): “There was no water for the congregation” → “não havia água para a congregação”
- flag: "Must we fetch water?" (Nm 20:10) is Henry's shortening of the KJV; translated, since the Bíblia Livre has "Faremos para vós sair águas desta rocha?".
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "falou imprudentemente com seus lábios" (Sl 106:33) and "sabedoria, justiça, santificação, e resgate" (1 Co 1:30; "resgate" where the KJV has redemption).

### 454 Num 20:14-21 The Israelites are refused a passage through Edom.
- quoted (Gen 27:41): “Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing” → “odiou Esaú a Jacó pela bênção”

### 455 Num 20:22-29 Aaron reigns the priest's office to Eleazar, and dies in mount Hor.
- flag: The English title has "reigns", a slip for "resigns"; translated as "entrega".
- flag: "the waters of strife" (Nm 20:24): the Bíblia Livre's "águas da briga" is used.

### 457 Num 21:4-9 The people murmuring, are plagued with fiery serpents, They repenting, are healed through the brazen serpent.
- quoted (John 3:14): “as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness” → “como Moisés levantou a serpente no deserto, assim deve o Filho do homem ser levantado”
- translated, not quoted (John 12:34; Henry is quoting Jo 3:14 (listed under 12:34); the Bíblia Livre's words of Jo 3:14 for this clause are given within quote 0.): “the Son of man must be lifted up”
- quoted (John 3:15): “believeth in him, should not perish” → “Para que todo aquele que nele crer não pereça”
- flag: "light bread" (Nm 21:5, KJV): the Bíblia Livre has "pão tão miserável"; rendered "pão miserável".
- flag: Unlisted: "Senhor, salva-nos! Estamos sendo destruídos!" is Mt 8:25 in the Bíblia Livre (KJV "we perish"); "bites like a serpent, and stings like an adder" (Pv 23:32) translated, Henry applies it to sin.

### 458 Num 21:10-20 Further journeys of the Israelites.
- quoted (Isa 12:3): “draw water out of the wells of salvation” → “águas das fontes da salvação”
- flag: "They set forward" (Nm 21:10) in Henry's quotation marks: the Bíblia Livre has "E partiram os filhos de Israel"; "E partiram" is used.
- flag: Unlisted: "rios de água viva" (Jo 7:38) in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 459 Num 21:21-35 Sihon and Og overcome, Their land possessed.
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "o dia das pequenas coisas" (Zc 4:10) and "mais que vencedores" (Rm 8:37).

### 460 Num 22:1-14 Balak's fear of Israel, He sends for Balaam.
- flag: Unlisted: "o pagamento da injustiça" (2 Pe 2:15) in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "the wages of unrighteousness").

### 462 Num 22:22-35 The opposition to Balaam by the way.
- quoted (Eccl 8:11): “fully set in them to do evil” → “cheio de vontade … para fazer o mal”
- quoted (Num 22:28): “The Lord opened the mouth of the ass” → “o SENHOR abriu a boca da jumenta”
- flag: Ec 8:11: the Bíblia Livre's "cheio de vontade neles, para fazer o mal" is used without "neles" (two runs).
- flag: "I have sinned" (Nm 22:34) is the Bíblia Livre's "Pequei".

### 463 Num 22:36-41 Balaam and Balak meet.
- flag: "Our Father which art in heaven, lead us not into temptation" (Mt 6:9, 13) is unlisted and joined by Henry from two verses; given in the Bíblia Livre's words of the two verses.

### 464 Num 23:1-10 Balak's sacrifice, Balaam pronounces a blessing instead of a curse.
- quoted (Num 23:10): “die the death of the righteous” → “da morte dos corretos”
- flag: Nm 23:10: the Bíblia Livre says "corretos" where Henry (KJV) has "the righteous"; elsewhere in the section "os justos" is used.
- flag: "it shall be given what they should speak" (Mt 10:19) is unlisted and changed in person; translated.

### 465 Num 23:11-30 Balak's disappointment, and second sacrifice, Balaam again blesses Israel.
- flag: Unlisted: "muitos pensamentos no coração do homem" (Pv 19:21) and "dará testemunho e não mentirá" (Hc 2:3) follow the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "devices", "speak").

### 466 Num 24:1-9 Balaam, leaving divinations, prophesies the happiness of Israel.
- quoted (Prov 28:1): “The righteous are bold as a lion” → “os justos são confiantes como um leão”
- flag: Two sentences ("Their power and victory. He looks back upon what had been done for them.") are repeated in the English; kept as in the source.
- flag: "The righteous... more excellent than his neighbour" (Pv 12:26, KJV) is unlisted; translated, as the Bíblia Livre reads very differently.
