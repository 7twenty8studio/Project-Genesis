# To review: pt commentary batch 047

### 1938 Prov 16:7-7 
- quoted (Prov 16:7): “enemies to be at peace with him” → “seus inimigos terem paz com ele”

### 1946 Prov 16:17-17 
- flag: "every appearance of evil" alludes to 1 Thess 5:22 KJV; the Bíblia Livre reads "qualquer forma de mal", so Henry's phrase is translated.

### 1954 Prov 16:26-26 
- flag: unlisted quotation of John 6:27 ("labour for the meat which endureth to everlasting life"); the Bíblia Livre's words "pela comida que permanece para vida eterna" are used.

### 2000 Prov 18:16-16 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Isa 55:1 ("without money and without price"); the Bíblia Livre's words "sem dinheiro e sem preço" are used.

### 2008 Prov 18:24-24 
- quoted (John 13:1, John 13:2): “Having loved his own, which were in the world, he loved them unto the end” → “havendo amado aos seus, que estavam no mundo, até o fim os amou”
- flag: "we are his friends if we do whatever he commands us" is Henry's third-person adaptation of John 15:14 ("Vós sois meus amigos, se fizerdes o que eu vos mando" in the Bíblia Livre); translated.

### 2019 Prov 19:11-11 
- flag: alludes to Rom 12:21 ("vence o mal com o bem" in the Bíblia Livre); the verb is adapted to Henry's sentence, so translated.

### 2020 Prov 19:12-12 
- quoted (Prov 19:12): “as the roaring of a lion” → “como o rugido de um leão”

### 2025 Prov 19:17-17 
- flag: unlisted quotation of James 2:5; the Bíblia Livre's words "escolheu os pobres deste mundo para serem ricos na fé, e herdeiros do" are used, then Henry's own "his kingdom" (the Bíblia Livre: "do reino que ele prometeu").

### 2027 Prov 19:19-19 
- translated, not quoted (Prov 19:19; The Bíblia Livre has "Aquele que tem grande irá" (with the slip "irá" for "ira"), a different construction that can't stand in Henry's "become a man of great wrath".): “a man of great wrath”

### 2047 Prov 20:10-10 
- quoted (1Tim 6:10): “the love of money is the root” → “o amor ao dinheiro é raiz”

### 2080 Prov 21:13-13 
- quoted (Prov 21:13): “ears at the cry of the poor” → “ouvido ao clamor do pobre”

### 2085 Prov 21:18-18 
- quoted (Prov 11:8): “delivered out of trouble, and the wicked” → “livrado da angústia; e o perverso vem em seu lugar”

### 2096 Prov 21:30-31 
- quoted (Eph 6:10): “in the Lord, and in the power of his might” → “no Senhor e na força do seu poder”
- flag: "the whole armour of God" is Eph 6:11; the Bíblia Livre's "toda a armadura de Deus" is used.

### 2107 Prov 22:11-11 
- quoted (Ps 32:2): “in whose spirit there is no guile” → “em cujo espírito não há engano”

### 2117 Prov 22:28-29 
- translated, not quoted (Prov 22:29; The Bíblia Livre reads "Viste um homem habilidoso em sua obra?", where Henry's point (and the KJV) is diligence, so his words are translated.): “Seest thou a man diligent”

### 2118 Prov 23:1-3 
- flag: "Do thyself no harm" is the KJV of Acts 16:28; the Bíblia Livre's words there, "Não te faças nenhum mal", are used.

### 2125 Prov 23:19-28 
- quoted (Prov 23:23): “Buy the truth, and sell it not” → “Compra a verdade, e não a vendas”

### 2126 Prov 23:29-35 
- quoted (Prov 23:34): “upon the top of a mast” → “no topo do mastro”
- flag: "I will seek it again" follows Prov 23:35 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "Vou buscar mais uma bebida" there, so Henry's words are translated.

### 2130 Prov 24:10-10 
- flag: alludes to Ps 27:14 ("esforça-te, e ele fortalecerá o teu coração" in the Bíblia Livre); Henry puts "God" for "he", so the words are fitted, not quoted exactly.

### 2132 Prov 24:13-14 
- flag: "wise unto salvation" is 2 Tim 3:15; the Bíblia Livre has "sábio para a salvação" (singular), here "sábios para a salvação".

### 2136 Prov 24:21-22 
- translated, not quoted (Prov 24:21; The Bíblia Livre has "não te envolvas com os rebeldes", while Henry's point rests on the KJV's "given to change"; his words are translated.): “with them that are given”
- flag: the English reads "them that are given change" (the KJV's "to" is missing); translated as "given to change".

### 2152 Prov 25:19-19 
- quoted (Prov 25:19): “Confidence in an unfaithful man” → “Confiar num infiel”

### 2157 Prov 25:25-25 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 8:16; the Bíblia Livre's words "com o nosso espírito de que somos filhos de Deus" are used.

### 2160 Prov 25:28-28 
- flag: alludes to Ezek 36:27 (the Bíblia Livre: "porei meu Espírito dentro de vós, e farei com que andeis em meus estatutos"); Henry turns it into a prayer in the third person, so it is translated.

### 2163 Prov 26:3-3 
- flag: "born like the wild ass's colt" follows Job 11:12 KJV; the Bíblia Livre reads that verse quite differently ("quando do asno selvagem nascer um humano"), so Henry's words are translated.

### 2172 Prov 26:16-16 
- flag: "his labour shall not be in vain" alludes to 1 Cor 15:58 ("o vosso trabalho não é vão" in the Bíblia Livre); translated to fit Henry's sentence.

### 2180 Prov 27:1-1 
- quoted (Prov 27:1): “not what a day may bring forth” → “o que o dia trará”
- flag: "that one thing needful" alludes to Luke 10:42 ("somente uma coisa é necessária" in the Bíblia Livre); translated.

### 2184 Prov 27:7-7 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Matt 5:6; the Bíblia Livre's words "têm fome e sede de justiça" are used.

### 2186 Prov 27:9-10 
- quoted (Prov 18:24): “there is a Friend that sticketh closer than a brother” → “há um amigo mais chegado que um irmão”

### 2194 Prov 27:19-19 
- flag: "the image of the earthly … of the heavenly" is 1 Cor 15:49; the Bíblia Livre's "a imagem do terrestre … a imagem do celestial" is used.
