# To review: pt commentary batch 023

### 814 1Sam 17:1-11 Goliath's challenge.
- flag: The section opens with a fragment ("and goes to meet him.") and a bare "(40-47)", apparently the tail of an outline left in the text; kept as separate paragraphs as in the source.
- flag: "cannot find their hearts or hands" rendered "não encontram nem coragem nem forças".

### 815 1Sam 17:12-30 David comes to the camp.
- flag: Echoes of 1 Pe 3:9 ("railing for railing") and Pv 15:1 ("a soft answer turneth away wrath") translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre's Pv 15:1 has "A resposta suave desvia o furor".

### 816 1Sam 17:31-39 David undertakes to fight Goliath.
- flag: "the weak and foolish things of the world" (1 Co 1:27) and "laid down his life for the sheep" (Jo 10:11) translated from Henry's wording; "toda a armadura de Deus" (Ef 6:11) and "o modo de pensar que … esteve em Cristo" (Fp 2:5) follow the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).
- flag: "He that has delivered, does and will continue to do so" (an echo of 2 Co 1:10) rendered "Aquele que livrou, livra e continuará a livrar".

### 817 1Sam 17:40-47 
- flag: "The security and presumption of fools destroy them" echoes Pv 1:32, translated from Henry's wording.

### 818 1Sam 17:48-58 He kills Goliath.
- quoted (James 4:7): “resist the devil, and he will flee from you” → “resisti ao diabo, e ele fugirá de vós.”
- flag: "Deus resiste aos soberbos" (Tg 4:6) and "coração mau e infiel" (Hb 3:12, Henry's "an evil heart of unbelief") follow the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).
- flag: "Let not the strong man glory in his strength" (after Jr 9:23) and "hardened his heart against God and prospered" (after Jó 9:4) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre's Jr 9:23 has "nem o valente se orgulhe em sua valentia".

### 821 1Sam 18:12-30 Saul's fear of David.
- quoted (Heb 12:3): “Consider Him that endured” → “Considerai, pois, aquele que suportou”
- quoted (Heb 12:3): “contradiction of sinners against himself, lest ye” → “hostilidade dos pecadores contra si mesmo, para que não”
- quoted (Heb 12:3): “faint in your minds” → “nem vossas almas se debilitem”
- flag: Henry's "magnified the honour" rendered "tinha em tão alta conta a honra"; the Bíblia Livre's Hb 12:3 has "hostilidade" where the KJV has "contradiction".

### 822 1Sam 19:1-10 Jonathan reconciles his father to David, Saul again tries to slay him.
- flag: "so deceitful and desperately wicked" (Jr 17:9) translated from Henry's KJV wording; the Bíblia Livre has "mais enganoso que todas as coisas, e perverso".

### 823 1Sam 19:11-24 David flees to Samuel.
- quoted (John 4:14): “a well of water springing up into everlasting life” → “fonte de água, que salte para vida eterna”

### 824 1Sam 20:1-10 David consults Jonathan.
- quoted (1Sam 20:3): “there is but a step between” → “apenas há um passo entre”
- flag: "e será feito para vós" follows the Bíblia Livre's Jo 15:7 (unlisted); Henry's "Ask" given as "Pedi".
- flag: "through much tribulation" (At 14:22) and "served our generation according to his will" (At 13:36) translated from Henry's wording.

### 826 1Sam 20:24-34 Saul, missing David, seeks to kill Jonathan.
- flag: "may be waved" read as "may be waived" (dispensadas).

### 827 1Sam 20:35-42 Jonathan takes leave of David.
- flag: Echoes of 1 Ts 4:13 and Ap 7:17 translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "como os outros que não têm esperança" and "Deus limpará toda lágrima dos seus olhos".

### 828 1Sam 21:1-9 David with Ahimelech.
- flag: The section ends with a bare "(1Sa 21:10-15)", kept as in the source; it may be an outline heading left in the text.
- flag: "mercy is to be preferred to sacrifice" (Mt 12:7) translated from Henry's wording.

### 830 1Sam 22:1-5 David at Adullam, Many resort to him.
- quoted (Ps 37:23): “The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord” → “Os passos do homem justo são preparados pelo SENHOR”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's Sl 37:23 has "homem justo" where Henry (KJV) has "a good man"; quoted verbatim.

### 834 1Sam 23:7-13 God warns him to escape from Keilah.
- translated, not quoted (Prov 24:12; Henry uses an infinitive ("how to render to every man") and the plural "works"; the Bíblia Livre's Pv 24:12 has the future "Ele retribuirá ao homem conforme sua obra", which can't stand in his sentence.): “render to every man according to his works”
- flag: "sabe livrar os devotos das tentações" follows the Bíblia Livre's 2 Pe 2:9 (unlisted; Henry has the singular "temptation").
- flag: "rewarded him evil for good, and that for his love they were his adversaries" (after Sl 109:4-5) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "Retribuíram o bem com o mal".

### 836 1Sam 23:19-29 He is rescued from Saul by an invasion of the Philistines.
- quoted (Isa 28:29): “is wonderful in counsel and excellent in working” → “maravilhoso em conselhos, e grande em sabedoria”
- quoted (Isa 7:9): “If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established” → “Se não crerdes, não ficareis firmes”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's Is 28:29 has "grande em sabedoria" where Henry (KJV) has "excellent in working"; quoted verbatim.

### 837 1Sam 24:1-7 David spares Saul's life.
- flag: "not to be overcome of evil, but to overcome evil with good" (Rm 12:21) translated in Henry's infinitive form; the Bíblia Livre has "Não sejas vencido pelo mal, mas vence o mal com o bem".

### 839 1Sam 24:16-22 Saul acknowledges his fault.
- quoted (Ps 37:6): “righteousness as the light” → “justiça como a luz”
- flag: "sinagoga de Satanás" (Ap 3:9) and "raiz de amargura" (Hb 12:15) follow the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).
- flag: Henry plays on "found David … could not find in his heart"; rendered "encontrara Davi … não pôde encontrar no seu coração ânimo".

### 841 1Sam 25:2-11 David's request; Nabal's churlish refusal.
- flag: The English has a stray comma after "suffers want!" ("want!, David pleaded"); kept as in the source.
- flag: Henry's gloss on the name, "A fool", rendered "Um louco".

### 842 1Sam 25:12-17 David's intention to destroy Nabal.
- flag: "não nos conduzas à tentação" follows the Bíblia Livre's Mt 6:13 (unlisted).
- flag: "kind to the evil and unthankful" (Lc 6:35) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "benigno até para com os ingratos e maus".
- flag: "Is this thy voice, O David?" echoes Saul's "Is this thy voice, my son David?" (1 Sm 24:16), translated.

### 843 1Sam 25:18-31 Abigail takes a present to David.
- flag: Henry's "atoned for" (made amends) rendered "reparou", not "expiou".

### 844 1Sam 25:32-44 He is pacified, Nabal dies.
- quoted (Prov 14:13): “the end of that mirth is heaviness” → “o fim da alegria é a tristeza”
- quoted (1Sam 25:38): “the Lord smote Nabal, that he died” → “o SENHOR feriu a Nabal, e morreu”
- flag: "estava muito embriagado" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Sm 25:36 (unlisted); "the laughter of the fool" (Ec 7:6) translated.

### 846 1Sam 26:13-20 David exhorts Saul.
- quoted (1Sam 26:19): “stirred thee up against me” → “te incita contra mim”
- quoted (1Sam 26:19): “let Him accept an offering” → “aceite um sacrifício”
- flag: Henry's "let Him accept an offering" given with the Bíblia Livre's "aceite um sacrifício" (1 Sm 26:19); his "the Lord" in the quotation given as "o SENHOR".

### 847 1Sam 26:21-25 Saul acknowledges his sin.
- flag: The English has "founded in an cemented by", read as "founded in and cemented by".
- flag: "play the fool, and err exceedingly" (1 Sm 26:21) given close to the Bíblia Livre ("agi loucamente, e errei em grande maneira"), in Henry's third person.

### 848 1Sam 27:1-7 David retires to Gath.
- flag: "lutas por fora, temores por dentro" (2 Co 7:5) and "acrescenta-nos fé" (Lc 17:5) follow the Bíblia Livre (unlisted); "easily besets" echoes Hb 12:1 ("facilmente nos envolve").

### 849 1Sam 27:8-12 David deceives Achish.
- flag: "devoted nations" (peoples under the ban) rendered "nações condenadas"; "equivocation" rendered "equívoco" (an ambiguous answer).

### 850 1Sam 28:1-6 Achish puts confidence in David, Saul's fear.
- quoted (1Sam 28:3): “those that had familiar spirits” → “os encantadores e adivinhos”
- flag: The English has a stray comma in "zealous against, sin"; dropped.
- flag: "filhos da desobediência" follows the Bíblia Livre's Ef 2:2 (unlisted); "an Israelite indeed" (Jo 1:47) rendered "um verdadeiro israelita"; "double, unstable mind" echoes Tg 1:8, translated.
- flag: Quotation 0: the Bíblia Livre's 1 Sm 28:3 has "os encantadores e adivinhos" for the KJV's "those that had familiar spirits".

### 851 1Sam 28:7-19 Saul consults a witch at Endor.
- flag: "Faze-me vir a Samuel" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Sm 28:11 (unlisted).
- flag: "Thou and thy sons shall be with me" (1 Sm 28:19) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "amanhã estareis comigo, tu e teus filhos".

### 853 1Sam 29:1-5 David objected to by the Philistines.
- quoted (1Sam 29:3): “the princes of the Philistines” → “os príncipes dos filisteus”

### 855 1Sam 30:1-6 Ziklag spoiled by the Amalekites.
- quoted (1Sam 30:6): “David encouraged himself in the Lord his God” → “Davi se esforçou no SENHOR seu Deus”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's 1 Sm 30:6 has "se esforçou" for the KJV's "encouraged himself"; quoted verbatim, and Henry's later "by encouraging himself in God" rendered "animando-se em Deus".
- flag: "the soul of the people was bitter" (1 Sm 30:6) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "estava com ânimo amargo".

### 856 1Sam 30:7-15 David overtakes the Amalekites.
- flag: Henry's "frames" (state, condition) of his followers rendered "a condição".

### 857 1Sam 30:16-20 He recovers what had been lost.
- quoted (1Sam 30:16): “Eating and drinking, and dancing” → “Comendo e bebendo e fazendo festa”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's 1 Sm 30:16 has "fazendo festa" for the KJV's "dancing"; quoted verbatim.
- flag: "Paz e segurança" (1 Ts 5:3) and "ajuntamento dos mortos" (Pv 21:16) follow the Bíblia Livre (unlisted); "put the evil day far" (Am 6:3) translated.
- flag: Henry's "Eating and drinking, and dancing, have been the … way" restructured with a colon so the verse's participles stand as given.

### 858 1Sam 30:21-31 David's distribution of the spoil.
- flag: "men of Belial" kept as "homens de Belial" (Henry's KJV phrase); the Bíblia Livre's 1 Sm 30:22 has "os maus e perversos".

### 859 1Sam 31:1-7 Saul's defeat and death.
- quoted (Eccl 9:2): “there is one event to the righteous and to the wicked” → “o mesmo acontece ao justo e ao perverso”
- quoted (Eph 6:13): “may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand” → “resistir no dia mal … continuar firmes”
- flag: The text opens with a second heading ("Saul's body rescued by the men of Jabesh-gilead.") and a bare "(8-13)", kept as separate paragraphs as in the source.
- flag: Quotation 1: Henry turns Ef 6:13 into the first person plural; the Bíblia Livre's runs "resistir no dia mal" and "continuar firmes" are kept verbatim, and "tendo feito tudo" (its "depois que fizerdes tudo") is adapted.
- flag: "o Guardião de Israel" follows the Bíblia Livre's Sl 121:4 (unlisted); "toda a armadura de Deus" its Ef 6:13.

### 860 1Sam 31:8-13 
- flag: The English has "he praises of men", read as "the praises of men".
- flag: "a honra que vem somente de Deus" follows the Bíblia Livre's Jo 5:44 (unlisted); "destroy both body and soul in hell" (Mt 10:28) translated in Henry's order (the Bíblia Livre has "tanto a alma como o corpo"); "secret things belong not to us" echoes Dt 29:29.
