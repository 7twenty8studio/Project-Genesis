# To review: pt commentary batch 067

### 2980 Matt 4:1-11 The temptation of Christ.
- translated, not quoted (James 1:14; Henry makes the verse plural ('Others are tempted, when drawn aside of their own lust'), so the Bíblia Livre's singular 'atraído e seduzido pelo seu próprio mau desejo' (Tg 1:14) is given in the plural.): “own lust, and enticed”
- quoted (Rom 6:1): “shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound” → “continuaremos no pecado, para que a graça aumente”
- quoted (Matt 4:8): “the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them” → “reinos do mundo, e a glória deles”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “what is a man profited if he” → “que proveito há para alguém, se”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “gain the whole world, and lose his own soul” → “ganhar o mundo todo, mas perder a sua alma”
- flag: Henry's "It is written" and "Get thee hence, Satan!" given in the Bíblia Livre's words 'Está escrito' (Mt 4:4) and 'Vai embora, Satanás!' (Mt 4:10), inside his own quotation marks.
- flag: 'This passage, De 8:3, made against the tempter, therefore he left out part': the passage Satan cut short is Ps 91:11-12 ('to keep thee in all thy ways'), quoted in Mt 4:6; Dt 8:3 is Christ's first answer. Reference kept as in the source (Dt 8:3); a reviewer may want to check it.
- flag: 'advancements in the world makes' (sic, plural subject) translated normally; 'fiery darts' given the Bíblia Livre's 'flechas inflamadas' (Ef 6:16).

### 2981 Matt 4:12-17 The opening of Christ's ministry in Galilee.
- flag: 'They were sitting in this condition' refers to Mt 4:16 ('The people which sat in darkness'; Bíblia Livre 'O povo sentado em trevas'); translated.

### 2982 Matt 4:18-22 Call of Simon and others.
- quoted (1Cor 1:27): “the foolish things of the world to confound the wise” → “as coisas loucas deste mundo para envergonhar as sábias”
- flag: 'with God nothing is impossible' (Lc 1:37; Bíblia Livre 'para Deus nada será impossível') translated in Henry's present tense.

### 2983 Matt 4:23-25 Jesus teaches and works miracles.
- flag: 'lunacy' (KJV Mt 4:24 'lunatick') translated 'a loucura' for Henry's 'malady of the mind'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'epiléticos' there.

### 2985 Matt 5:3-12 Who are blessed.
- quoted (Matt 5:6): “hunger and thirst after righteousness” → “têm fome e sede de justiça”
- quoted (Matt 5:10): “are persecuted for righteousness' sake” → “sofrem perseguição por causa da justiça”
- flag: Henry writes 'happy' for the KJV's 'Blessed' throughout; translated 'felizes', with the Bíblia Livre's names for each group (humildes de espírito, os que choram, mansos, misericordiosos, limpos de coração, pacíficos; Mt 5:3-9); 'for they shall see God' is its 'porque eles verão a Deus'.
- flag: 'Create in me such a clean heart, O God' adapts Ps 51:10 (Bíblia Livre 'Cria em mim um coração puro, ó Deus'), with Henry's 'such' kept: 'um tal coração puro'.
- flag: 'study to be quiet' (1 Ts 4:11) given the Bíblia Livre's 'procurai viver quietos' ('procuram viver quietos'); 'brands out of the burning' translated 'tições tirados do fogo'.

### 2986 Matt 5:13-16 Exhortations and warnings.
- quoted (Matt 5:13): “Ye are the salt of the earth” → “Vós sois o sal da terra”
- flag: 'graceless' (without grace) translated 'sem graça' in the sense of lacking saving grace; a reviewer may prefer 'destituído da graça', since 'sem graça' can also mean 'dull' in Portuguese.

### 2988 Matt 5:21-26 The sixth commandment.
- flag: Henry's "Raca" kept as he writes it; the Bíblia Livre renders it 'Idiota!' in Mt 5:22. His "Thou fool" given its 'Louco!' (without the exclamation, inside his own quotation marks).
- flag: 'we are all made of one blood' (At 17:26; Bíblia Livre 'de um sangue ele fez toda nação humana') translated 'feitos de um só sangue'.

### 2991 Matt 5:38-42 The law of retaliation.
- translated, not quoted (1Cor 6:9; Listed against 1 Co 6:9, but Henry quotes 1 Co 15:50, 'flesh and blood shall not inherit'; its Bíblia Livre words ('a carne e o sangue não podem herdar o Reino de Deus') are used instead, as in the Spanish.): “shall not inherit the kingdom of God”
- flag: Quotation listed with 1 Co 6:9, whose Bíblia Livre words 'não herdarão o Reino de Deus' match Henry's 'shall not inherit the kingdom of God' and are used; his sentence ('flesh and blood shall not inherit…') echoes 1 Co 15:50, which the Bíblia Livre reads 'a carne e o sangue não podem herdar o Reino de Deus'.

### 2992 Matt 5:43-48 The law of love explained.
- flag: sensitive: the Jewish teachers said to have restricted 'neighbour' to their own country, nation and religion (section 2992; also 2988 on their teaching).

### 2993 Matt 6:1-4 Against hypocrisy in almsgiving.
- flag: The section opens with 'Evil of being worldly-minded.' and '(19-24)', an outline heading and verse range that seem to belong to the section on Mt 6:19-24 (2997); kept as separate paragraphs, as in the source.
- flag: 'to be seen of men' translated 'para serem vistos pelos homens'; the Bíblia Livre's Mt 6:2 reads 'para serem honrados pelas pessoas'.

### 2994 Matt 6:5-8 Against hypocrisy in prayer.
- flag: Henry's "Verily they have their reward" (Mt 6:5) translated inside his own quotation marks; the Bíblia Livre's run ('em verdade vos digo que já receberam sua recompensa') has words between.
- flag: 'groanings that cannot be uttered' (Rm 8:26) given the Bíblia Livre's 'gemidos inexprimíveis'.
- flag: 'If prayerless, then graceless' rendered 'Se sem oração, então sem graça' (without saving grace).

### 2995 Matt 6:9-15 How to pray.
- quoted (Matt 6:33): “first the kingdom of God and his righteousness” → “primeiramente o Reino de Deus e a sua justiça”
- quoted (Prov 31:27): “the bread of idleness” → “pão da preguiça”
- quoted (Matt 6:14): “your heavenly Father will also forgive” → “vosso Pai celestial também vos perdoará”
- flag: 'the bread of deceit, Pr 20:17' given the Bíblia Livre's 'o pão da mentira' there.
- flag: Henry's 'Give it to us' (the Lord's Prayer) translated 'Dá-nos'; the Lord's Prayer's own Bíblia Livre wording was not used, as Henry quotes no full petition.

### 2996 Matt 6:16-18 Respecting fasting.
- flag: 'Fasting is the humbling of the soul, Psa 35:13' (KJV 'I humbled my soul with fasting'): translated 'a humilhação da alma'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'eu afligia a minha alma com jejuns'.
- flag: 'God sees in secret, and will reward openly' (Mt 6:18) translated, close to the Bíblia Livre ('vê em segredo … recompensará em público').

### 2997 Matt 6:19-24 
- flag: 'an inheritance incorruptible' (1 Pe 1:4) given the Bíblia Livre's 'uma herança incorruptível'; 'those things not seen which are eternal' (2 Co 4:18) translated.

### 2998 Matt 6:25-34 Trust in God commended.
- quoted (Matt 6:25): “Take no thought for your life” → “Não andeis ansiosos por vossa vida”
- quoted (Matt 6:34): “no thought for the morrow” → “ansiosos pelo dia de amanhã”
- quoted (Matt 6:33): “first the kingdom of God” → “primeiramente o Reino de Deus”
- flag: Henry's 'Take no thought' is the KJV's old sense of anxious care; the Bíblia Livre's 'não andeis ansiosos' (Mt 6:25, 34) used, and his 'thoughtfulness for our souls … for the world' translated 'preocupação'.
- flag: 'our times are in his hand' (Sl 31:15), 'not boast of tomorrow' (Pv 27:1) and 'let none of these things move us' (At 20:24) translated as allusions.

### 2999 Matt 7:1-6 Christ reproves rash judgment.
- flag: 'motes' and 'beams' given the Bíblia Livre's 'ciscos' and 'traves' (Mt 7:3-5); 'gnat' and 'camel' its 'mosquito' and 'camelo' (Mt 23:24).
- flag: 'the god of this world blinds their minds' (2 Co 4:4) translated; the Bíblia Livre reads 'o deus destes tempos cegou os entendimentos'.

### 3000 Matt 7:7-11 Encouragements to prayer.
- quoted (Matt 7:8): “every one that asketh receiveth” → “qualquer um que pede recebe”
- flag: 'the merchantman that seeks goodly pearls' (Mt 13:45) given the Bíblia Livre's 'boas pérolas'.

### 3001 Matt 7:12-14 The broad and narrow way.
- flag: 'the strait gate' and 'the narrow way' given the Bíblia Livre's 'porta estreita' and 'caminho … apertado' (Mt 7:13-14); 'the gate is wide' its 'larga é a porta', 'a broad way' its 'espaçoso'.
- flag: 'we must go through much tribulation' (At 14:22) given the Bíblia Livre's 'muitas aflições'.

### 3003 Matt 7:21-29 To be doers of the word, not hearers only.
- quoted (Rom 8:35): “shall separate us from the love of Christ” → “nos separará do amor de Cristo”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Job 27:8 ('where is the hope of the hypocrite … when God taketh away his soul'): Bíblia Livre 'qual é a esperança do hipócrita' used verbatim, Henry's order kept.
- flag: 'This is his will, even our sanctification' (1 Ts 4:3), 'a lie in our right hand' (Is 44:20) and 'every one that names the name of Christ, depart' (2 Tm 2:19) translated as allusions.

### 3006 Matt 8:5-13 A centurion's servant healed.
- flag: 'children of the kingdom' given the Bíblia Livre's 'filhos do reino' (Mt 8:12).
- flag: 'This centurion was a heathen' translated 'um pagão' (descriptive, not harsh).

### 3007 Matt 8:14-17 Cure of Peter's wife's mother.
- quoted (Matt 8:16): “healed all that were sick” → “curou todos os que estavam doentes”
- flag: sensitive: 'The church of Rome, which forbids ministers to marry, goes contrary to that apostle upon whom they rest so much' (section 3007).
- flag: 'Jesus Christ bore our sicknesses and carried our sorrows' blends Mt 8:17 and Is 53:4; translated (Bíblia Livre Mt 8:17 'tomou sobre si as nossas enfermidades, e levou as nossas doenças').

### 3008 Matt 8:18-22 The scribe's zealous proposal.
- flag: Unlisted quotations of Matt 8:22 ('Follow me') and Matt 8:19 ('I will follow thee'): Bíblia Livre 'Segue-me' and 'eu te seguirei' used verbatim; 'not a place to lay his head on' (Mt 8:20) given its 'onde recostar a cabeça'.
- flag: 'but it is not thy work at this time' keeps Henry's sudden address in the singular (tu).
