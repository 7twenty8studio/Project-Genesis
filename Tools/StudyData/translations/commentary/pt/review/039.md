# To review: pt commentary batch 039

### 1487 Ps 45:10-17 
- flag: "Companions of the bride" follows Ps 45:14 (the virgins, "suas companheiras" in the Bíblia Livre); feminine kept for the companions ("companheiras", "Estas, cada uma delas").

### 1488 Ps 46:1-5 Confidence in God.
- flag: "A present Help, a Help found" plays on Ps 46:1 KJV ("a very present help", margin "found"); the Bíblia Livre has "socorro oportuno"; Henry's words translated.

### 1489 Ps 46:6-11 An exhortation to behold it.
- flag: "Come and see" opens with Ps 46:8: the Bíblia Livre's "Vinde, observai" used.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rom 8:31: the Bíblia Livre's "Se Deus é por nós, quem será contra nós?" used.

### 1490 Ps 47:1-4 
- flag: "A willing people in the day of his power" echoes Ps 110:3; translated close to the Bíblia Livre ("Teu povo será voluntário no dia do teu poder").
- flag: "The Lord shall choose my inheritance for me" adapts Ps 47:4 KJV; translated (Bíblia Livre: "Ele escolhe para nós nossa herança").

### 1491 Ps 47:5-9 
- quoted (Ps 47:7): “Sing ye praises with understanding” → “Cantai louvores com entendimento”
- quoted (2Cor 10:5, 2Cor 10:6): “into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ” → “como prisioneiro todo pensamento para a obediência a Cristo”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Acts 5:31: the Bíblia Livre's "dar a Israel arrependimento e perdão de pecados" used; "Príncipe e Salvador" as there.

### 1492 Ps 48:1-7 
- quoted (Ps 48:2): “the joy of the whole earth” → “alegria de toda a terra”
- flag: sensitive: 1492 "the overthrow of heathenism" ("a derrubada do paganismo"), translated as written.

### 1493 Ps 48:8-14 
- flag: "Neither slumbers nor sleeps" echoes Ps 121:4; translated close to the Bíblia Livre ("não cochilará nem dormirá").

### 1494 Ps 49:1-5 A call for attention.
- flag: "The iniquity of our heels" explains Ps 49:5 KJV ("the iniquity of my heels"); the Bíblia Livre reads "a maldade dos meus adversários", so Henry's "heels, or steps" won't be found there; translated.
- flag: "Let all hear this with application to ourselves" mixes persons in the English; kept ("aplicando-o a nós mesmos").

### 1495 Ps 49:6-14 Folly of worldlings.
- quoted (Dan 12:2): “sleep in the dust” → “dormem no pó”

### 1496 Ps 49:15-20 Against fear of death.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 1 Tim 6:7: the Bíblia Livre's "nada trouxemos ao mundo" and "é evidente que nada podemos levar dele" used.
- flag: "Men will praise thee ... done well for thyself" follows Ps 49:18 KJV; translated (the Bíblia Livre words the verse differently).
- flag: "The soul is often put for the life": Henry's remark on the Hebrew/KJV "soul" (Ps 49:15); translated.

### 1498 Ps 50:7-15 Sacrifices to be changed for prayers.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 1 Sam 15:22 ("To obey is better than sacrifice"): the Bíblia Livre's "o obedecer é melhor que os sacrifícios" used.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ps 50:15 ("the day of trouble"; Henry "distress"): the Bíblia Livre's "no dia da angústia" used.

### 1499 Ps 50:16-23 Sincere obedience required.
- flag: "Set their sins in order" follows Ps 50:21 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "mostrarei teus males diante de teus olhos"; translated.
- flag: "Whoso offers praise, glorifies him" follows Ps 50:23 KJV; translated (Bíblia Livre: "Quem oferece sacrifício de louvor me glorificará").
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rom 12:11 ("fervent in spirit"): the Bíblia Livre's "fervorosos de espírito" used.

### 1500 Ps 51:1-6 The psalmist prays for mercy, humbly confessing and lamenting his sins.
- quoted (Prov 22:15): “is bound in the heart of a child” → “está amarrada ao coração do menino”
- quoted (Ps 51:6): “Thou desirest truth in the inward” → “Tu te agradas da verdade interior”
- flag: Source: "he drew up" begins a sentence in lower case; capitalized.
- flag: Quote 1: the KJV's "desirest" is "te agradas" in the Bíblia Livre's Ps 51:6.

### 1501 Ps 51:7-15 He pleads for pardon, that he may promote the glory of God and the conversion of sinners.
- quoted (Heb 12:24): “the blood of sprinkling” → “o sangue da aspersão”
- quoted (Ps 51:9): “Hide thy face from my sins” → “Esconde tua face de meus pecados”
- quoted (Ps 51:9): “blot out all mine iniquities” → “desfaz todas as minhas perversidades”
- quoted (Gal 5:1): “the liberty wherewith Christ” → “a liberdade com que Cristo”
- quoted (Gal 5:1): “with the yoke of bondage” → “com o jugo da escravidão”
- quoted (Ps 25:5): “thou art the God of my salvation” → “tu és o Deus de minha salvação”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Ps 51:7 ("Purge me with hyssop"): the Bíblia Livre's "Limpa-me do pecado com hissopo" used.
- flag: Quote 2: the KJV's "blot out" is "desfaz" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's following "blot them out, as a cloud is blotted out" is rendered with the same verb ("desfaze-as como uma nuvem é desfeita").
- flag: "Thy Spirit is a free Spirit" explains Ps 51:12 KJV ("uphold me with thy free spirit"); the Bíblia Livre has "com um espírito de boa vontade"; translated.
- flag: "Create in him a clean heart" (Ps 51:10) translated with the Bíblia Livre's "coração puro".

### 1502 Ps 51:16-19 God is pleased with a contrite heart, A prayer for the prosperity of Zion.
- quoted (Ps 51:17): “a broken spirit, a broken and a contrite heart” → “um espírito quebrado em arrependimento … um coração quebrado e triste”
- quoted (Eph 1:7): “the forgiveness of sins according to the riches of his grace” → “o perdão dos pecados, conforme as riquezas da sua graça”
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's Ps 51:17 has "um espírito quebrado em arrependimento" and "um coração quebrado e triste" for the KJV's "a broken spirit" and "a broken and a contrite heart"; both runs used.
- flag: Title: the English joins two headings with a comma ("... contrite heart, A prayer ..."); rendered as two sentences.

### 1503 Ps 52:1-5 The enemies of the truth and the church described, Their destruction.
- quoted (Ps 52:5): “the land of the living” → “terra dos viventes”
- flag: "The land of the living on earth": "on earth" rendered "aqui embaixo" to avoid "terra" twice.

### 1504 Ps 52:6-9 The righteous rejoice.
- quoted (Ps 52:7): “trusted in the abundance of his riches” → “abundância de suas riquezas”
- quoted (Lam 3:26): “hope, and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord” → “esperar e tranquilo aguardar a salvação do SENHOR”
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's Ps 52:7 reads "preferiu confiar a abundância de suas riquezas" (no preposition); only the run "abundância de suas riquezas" is quoted, after Henry's "trusted in" ("confiava na").

### 1505 Ps 53:1-6 The corruption of man by nature.
- quoted (Rom 3:18): “there is no fear of God before their eyes” → “Diante dos seus olhos não há temor a Deus”
- quoted (Matt 1:21): “his name JESUS, for he shall save his people from their sins” → “porque ele salvará seu povo de seus pecados”
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's capitals "JESUS" kept in his own words; the quoted run starts after the name (the Bíblia Livre has "Jesus").
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Heb 3:13 ("hardened through the deceitfulness of sin"): the Bíblia Livre's "pelo engano do pecado" used.

### 1507 Ps 54:4-7 Assurance of the Divine favour and protection.

### 1508 Ps 55:1-8 Prayer to God to manifest his favour.
- flag: "It pleased the Lord to put him to grief, and to make his soul an offering" follows Isa 53:10 KJV; translated (the Bíblia Livre: "agradou ao SENHOR esmagá-lo" ... "posta como expiação do pecado").
- flag: Henry numbers only two points ("1. ... 2. ..."), then "David in great alarm" without a number; kept.

### 1510 Ps 55:16-23 He is sure that God would in due time appear for him.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 55:22; Not a quotation of Ps 55:22: Henry's own "call upon the Lord, and he will save us" (closer to Ps 55:16); the four words matched by chance.): “upon the Lord, and he”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 55:22; Henry explains "moved" ("so shaken ... as to quit their duty"); the Bíblia Livre's Ps 55:22 has "não permitirá que o justo fique caído para sempre", which would lose his point.): “never suffer the righteous to be moved”
- flag: "Cast our burden upon God" and "Care is a burden" follow Ps 55:22 KJV ("Cast thy burden", margin "gift"); the Bíblia Livre has "Entrega tuas preocupações ao SENHOR"; translated with "fardo".
