# To review: pt commentary batch 065

### 2927 Zech 1:1-6 An exhortation to repentance.
- quoted (Zech 1:4): “Turn ye now from your evil ways, and from your evil doings” → “Convertei-vos de vossos maus caminhos e de vossos maus atos”
- flag: Henry shifts between 'Review…/Be persuaded' (imperative to the reader) and 'your fathers'; kept the second person plural (vós) throughout as in the quotation.
- flag: 'not one jot or title' (sic for 'tittle', Mt 5:18) translated 'nem um jota ou til'.

### 2928 Zech 1:7-17 A vision of the ministry of angels.
- quoted (Matt 13:11): “the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven” → “mistérios do Reino dos céus”
- quoted (Zech 1:13): “The Lord answered the Angel” → “O SENHOR respondeu ao anjo”
- quoted (Zech 1:13): “good words and comfortable words” → “palavras boas, palavras de consolo”
- flag: 'The earth sat still, and was at rest' (Zc 1:11) translated in Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre reads 'toda a terra está tranquila e quieta'.
- flag: Henry capitalises 'the Angel' (Christ, the Angel of the covenant); the Bíblia Livre's 'anjo' is lowercase inside the quotation, Henry's 'Anjo da aliança' kept capitalised.

### 2929 Zech 1:18-21 The security of the Jews and the destruction of their enemies.
- flag: 'four workmen' (KJV 'four carpenters', Zc 1:20) translated 'quatro artífices'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'quatro ferreiros'.

### 2930 Zech 2:1-5 The prosperity of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Zech 2:1): “with a measuring line in his hand” → “em sua mão uma corda de medir”
- quoted (Zech 2:5): “a Wall of fire round” → “muro de fogo ao redor”

### 2931 Zech 2:6-9 The Jews called to return to their own land.
- quoted (Gen 19:17): “Escape for thy life, look not behind thee” → “Escapa por tua vida; não olhes atrás de ti”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Zech 2:8 ('He that touches you, touches the apple of his eye'): Bíblia Livre 'quem vos toca, toca a menina de seu olho' used verbatim.
- flag: Henry's 'He that touches you' keeps 'you' while 'his eye' refers to God; kept as in the verse.

### 2932 Zech 2:10-13 A promise of God's presence.
- translated, not quoted (Isa 26:21; The Bíblia Livre (Is 26:21) reads 'punir a maldade dos moradores da terra': its words around Henry's run are not one unbroken phrase matching 'punish the inhabitants of the earth', and Henry adds 'for their sins'.): “to punish the inhabitants of the earth”

### 2933 Zech 3:1-5 The restoration of the church.
- quoted (Zech 3:2): “a brand plucked out of the fire” → “um tição tirado do fogo”
- quoted (1Cor 6:11): “in the name of the Lord Jesus, and by the Spirit of our God” → “no nome do Senhor Jesus, e pelo Espírito de nosso Deus”
- quoted (Rev 19:8): “The righteousness of saints” → “justiças dos santos”
- quoted (Rev 19:8): “fine linen, clean and white” → “linho fino puro e brilhante”
- flag: Henry's 'The righteousness of saints' rendered with the Bíblia Livre's plural 'As justiças dos santos' (Ap 19:8); 'fine linen, clean and white' is 'linho fino puro e brilhante' there.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Rev 21:9 ('the bride, the Lamb's wife'): Bíblia Livre 'a noiva, a mulher do Cordeiro' used verbatim.
- flag: 'made of God to us both righteousness and sanctification' (1 Co 1:30) and 'kings and priests' (Ap 1:5-6) translated as allusions, close to the Bíblia Livre.

### 2934 Zech 3:6-10 A promise concerning the Messiah.
- flag: 'the Branch' rendered 'o Renovo', the Bíblia Livre's word in Zc 3:8.
- flag: 'buried in the grave' translated 'sepultado na cova' (the image of foundation stones under ground).

### 2935 Zech 4:1-7 A vision of a candlestick, with two olive trees.
- flag: Allusion to Mt 26:41 ('the spirit … willing, but the flesh … weak') translated in Henry's adapted form, not quoted (Bíblia Livre: 'o espírito está pronto, mas a carne é fraca').

### 2936 Zech 4:8-10 Further encouragement.
- quoted (Zech 4:10): “the eyes of the Lord” → “olhos do SENHOR”
- flag: 'shine more and more unto the perfect day' (Pv 4:18) translated in Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre reads differently ('ilumina até o dia ficar claro por completo').
- flag: The trailing '(Zec 4:11-14)' looks like an outline reference that belongs to the next section; kept as in the source.

### 2937 Zech 4:11-14 An explanation respecting the olive trees.
- flag: 'the unction or anointing' translated 'a unção' (Portuguese has one word for both English terms).

### 2938 Zech 5:1-4 The vision of a flying roll.
- translated, not quoted (Gal 3:13; Henry changes the verb ('who came to redeem us … being himself made a curse for us'); the Bíblia Livre's 'nos resgatou … ao se fazer maldição para o nosso benefício' can't stand in his sentence. The words 'da maldição da Lei' are the Bíblia Livre's.): “us from the curse of the law, being”
- flag: Ps 147:15 cited in Henry's paraphrase ('runs very swiftly'); the Bíblia Livre reads 'sua palavra corre velozmente'.
- flag: 'flee for refuge to lay hold on the hope set before us' (Hb 6:18) translated; 'agarrar a esperança posta diante de nós' matches the Bíblia Livre.

### 2939 Zech 5:5-11 The vision of a woman and an ephah.
- translated, not quoted (Zech 5:7; The Bíblia Livre calls the ephah 'cesto' ('no meio do cesto', Zc 5:7); Henry's whole section explains it as an ephah, a measure, so 'efa' is kept for his point.): “in the midst of the ephah”
- flag: sensitive: Henry speaks of the Jewish nation as a whole filling the measure of its iniquity, condemned for crucifying Christ, scattered and 'settled upon their own unbelief' (section 2939).
- flag: The Bíblia Livre renders the ephah 'cesto de medir' / 'cesto' (Zc 5:6-7); 'efa' kept in Henry's explanation.
- flag: 'Blindness is happened unto Israel' (Rm 11:25) translated with the Bíblia Livre's word 'endurecimento' ('o endurecimento veio em parte sobre Israel'); 'treasure up wrath against the day of wrath' (Rm 2:5) translated.

### 2940 Zech 6:1-8 The vision of the chariots.
- quoted (Zech 6:5): “four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from” → “quatro espíritos dos céus, que saem de”
- quoted (Num 16:22): “the God of the spirits of all flesh” → “Deus dos espíritos de toda carne”
- flag: 'The mixed colour, signify' (sic, plural verb with a singular subject) translated as singular.

### 2941 Zech 6:9-15 Joshua, the high priest, crowned as a type of Christ.
- translated, not quoted (Zech 6:11; The Bíblia Livre's run is 'sobre a cabeça do sumo sacerdote Josué'; Henry quotes only 'upon the head of Joshua', so using it would lengthen his quotation.): “upon the head of Joshua”
- flag: 'an exceeding weight of glory' (2 Co 4:17) translated 'um peso de excelentíssima glória', close to the Bíblia Livre ('um peso eterno de excelentíssima glória'), without its 'eterno', which Henry doesn't have.
- flag: 'The counsel of peace should be between' (KJV Zc 6:13 'shall be') translated with the future 'estará'.

### 2943 Zech 7:8-14 Sin the cause of their captivity.
- flag: 'Sin, if regarded in the heart' alludes to Ps 66:18 (KJV 'If I regard iniquity in my heart'); translated, the Bíblia Livre reads differently.

### 2944 Zech 8:1-8 The restoration of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Matt 19:26): “With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible” → “Para os seres humanos, isto é impossível; mas para Deus tudo é possível”
- flag: 'With men this is impossible' given in the Bíblia Livre's words ('Para os seres humanos'), which are less literal than Henry's KJV 'With men'.

### 2945 Zech 8:9-17 The people encouraged by promises of God's favour, and exhorted to holiness.
- flag: 'to speak every man peace with his neighbour' (sic; Zc 8:16 and Ef 4:25 say 'truth'): translated as written, 'falar cada um paz com o seu próximo'.

### 2946 Zech 8:18-23 The Jews in the latter days.
- translated, not quoted (Col 3:15; In the Bíblia Livre (Cl 3:15) 'a paz de Deus' and 'governe em vossos corações' are split by a clause; Henry's parallel sentence ('truths of God rule in your heads') needs them together.): “let the peace of God rule in your hearts”
- flag: 'as much as in you lies, live peaceably with all men' (Rm 12:18) and 'living epistles … known and read of all men' (2 Co 3:2) translated as allusions; 'conhecidas e lidas por todos' is close to the Bíblia Livre.

### 2947 Zech 9:1-8 God's defence of his church.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Song 2:2 ('a lily among thorns'): Bíblia Livre 'como um lírio entre os espinhos' used verbatim.
- flag: 'no exactor shall pass by her' (Henry's word; KJV Zc 9:8 'oppressor') translated 'nenhum exator'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'o opressor não passe mais por eles'.

### 2948 Zech 9:9-17 Christ's coming and his kingdom.
- translated, not quoted (Zech 9:17; The Bíblia Livre (Zc 9:17) reads 'como será grande sua prosperidade', which would lose Henry's point about God's goodness.): “How great is his goodness”
- flag: 'prisoners of hope' (Zc 9:12) translated 'prisioneiros de esperança'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'prisioneiros que tendes esperança'. 'a pit … in which there is no water' (Zc 9:11; Bíblia Livre 'cova em que não havia água') translated in Henry's present tense.
- flag: 'there is hope in Israel concerning them' echoes Ed 10:2 (KJV 'yet now there is hope in Israel concerning this thing'; Bíblia Livre 'ainda há esperança para Israel'); translated.

### 2949 Zech 10:1-5 Blessings to be sought from the Lord.
- translated, not quoted (Zech 10:1; The KJV's 'bright clouds' is 'relâmpagos' (lightnings) in the Bíblia Livre (Zc 10:1), and Henry's 'would make … and give showers of rain' changes the tense; translated in his wording.): “make bright clouds, and give”
- flag: Henry's 'bright clouds' (KJV Zc 10:1) is 'relâmpagos' in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Isa 45:19 ('Seek ye me in vain'): Bíblia Livre 'Buscai-me, em vão' used verbatim.

### 2950 Zech 10:6-12 God will restore his people.
- quoted (Col 3:17): “do all in the name of the Lord Jesus” → “tudo no nome do Senhor Jesus”

### 2951 Zech 11:1-3 Destruction to come upon the Jews.
- flag: 'fir trees' (KJV Zc 11:2) translated 'ciprestes', the Bíblia Livre's word there ('Lamenta, ó cipreste').
- flag: 'young lions' translated 'leõezinhos' (KJV Zc 11:3).

### 2952 Zech 11:4-14 The Lord's dealing with the Jews.
- flag: sensitive: Henry calls the Jewish church and nation 'wretchedly corrupt and degenerate' (section 2952).
- flag: The staves 'Beauty' and 'Bands' (KJV Zc 11:7) given the Bíblia Livre's names 'Agrado' and 'União'; Henry's explanations (privileges; the harmony uniting them) still fit.
- flag: 'the final method of applying it' (the use made of the thirty pieces) translated 'o destino final dado ao dinheiro'.
- flag: Henry's 'professors' (those who profess religion) translated 'os que professam a religião'.

### 2953 Zech 11:15-17 The emblem and curse of a foolish shepherd.
- flag: 'The idol shepherd' (KJV Zc 11:17) translated 'o pastor inútil', the Bíblia Livre's phrase.

### 2954 Zech 12:1-8 Punishment of the enemies of Judah.
- quoted (Zech 12:6): “on the right hand and on the left” → “à direita e à esquerda”
- flag: 'as fire among wood, as a torch in a sheaf' (KJV Zc 12:6) translated in Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre reads 'braseiro de fogo debaixo da lenha … tocha de fogo debaixo de gravetos'.

### 2955 Zech 12:9-14 Repentance and sorrow of the Jews.
- flag: 'the Spirit of supplications' (KJV Zc 12:10 'the spirit of grace and of supplications') translated 'Espírito de súplicas'; the Bíblia Livre reads 'o Espírito de graça e de orações'.
- flag: 'after a godly sort' (2 Co 7:11) translated 'segundo Deus', the Bíblia Livre's phrase ('tristeza segundo Deus').

### 2956 Zech 13:1-6 The Fountain for the remission of sins, The conviction of the false prophets.
- quoted (Isa 64:6): “We are all as an unclean thing” → “todos nós somos como um imundo”
- quoted (Prov 27:6): “faithful are the wounds of a friend” → “fiéis são as feridas feitas por um amigo”
- flag: The title joins two outline headings with a comma ('…of sins, The conviction…'); rendered as two sentences.
- flag: Henry's 'We are all as an unclean thing' given in the Bíblia Livre's words 'todos nós somos como um imundo' (Is 64:6); his own earlier echo (section 2933) was translated 'como coisa imunda'.
