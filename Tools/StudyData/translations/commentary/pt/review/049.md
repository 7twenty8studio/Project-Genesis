# To review: pt commentary batch 049

### 2293 Eccl 8:14-17 
- quoted (Eccl 8:15): “no better thing under the sun” → “o homem não tem nada melhor abaixo do sol”
- flag: "when flesh and heart shall fail" alludes to Ps 73:26 ("Minha carne e meu coração desfalecem" in the Bíblia Livre); fitted to Henry's sentence.

### 2294 Eccl 9:1-3 
- flag: the outline's "All men must die, Their portion" has a capital after the comma in the English; rendered as two clauses.

### 2296 Eccl 9:11-12 
- flag: the English begins ",12", left over from the outline reference "(Eccl. 9:11,12)"; kept as it stands.

### 2297 Eccl 9:13-18 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rom 8:31; the Bíblia Livre's words "Se Deus é por nós, quem será contra nós" are used, then Henry's own "or stand before us".

### 2299 Eccl 10:4-10 
- quoted (1Cor 12:25): “the members should have the same care one for another” → “os membros tenham igual cuidado uns com os outros”

### 2300 Eccl 10:11-15 
- quoted (James 3:8): “is an unruly evil, full of deadly poison” → “um mal difícil de se controlar, cheia de veneno mortal”
- flag: Henry's "Is. 25:8" is kept as given, but the words about wayfaring men who "shall not err" are from Isa 35:8 (very likely a slip for 35:8).

### 2301 Eccl 10:16-20 
- quoted (1Pet 1:18): “with corruptible things, as silver and gold” → “por coisas destrutíveis, como prata ou ouro”
- quoted (1Tim 6:15): “the King of kings, and Lord of lords” → “o Rei dos reis, e Senhor dos senhores”
- flag: Henry switches from "thy" to "your" in the last sentences; both rendered with "tu".

### 2302 Eccl 11:1-6

### 2303 Eccl 11:7-10 
- flag: "an effecting address" in the English is read as "an affecting address" (a slip); rendered "um apelo comovente".
- flag: "remember their Creator in the days of their youth" adapts Eccl 12:1 ("lembra-te de teu Criador nos dias de tua juventude" in the Bíblia Livre) to the third person; translated.

### 2304 Eccl 12:1-7

### 2305 Eccl 12:8-14 
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “what, or what manner of time, the Spirit of Christ” → “qual era o tempo ou ocasião que o Espírito de Cristo”
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “in them did signify, when it testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow” → “dentro deles indicava, quando dava prévio testemunho dos sofrimentos de Cristo e das glórias seguintes”
- translated, not quoted (Eccl 12:13; Henry turns the command into a statement ("That to fear God, and keep his commandments, is the whole of man"); the Bíblia Livre's imperative "teme a Deus, e guarda os mandamentos dele" can't stand in it, so it is translated.): “fear God, and keep his commandments”
- quoted (Eccl 12:13): “Fear God, and keep his commandments, for this” → “Teme a Deus, e guarda os mandamentos dele; porque isto é”
- flag: Henry's capitals "VANITY OF VANITIES, ALL IS VANITY" (Eccl 12:8, unlisted) are given in the Bíblia Livre's words for that verse, "Futilidade das futilidades… Tudo é futilidade", kept in capitals; the later "ALL IS VANITY", in Henry's own sentence, is "TUDO É VAIDADE".

### 2307 Cant 1:2-6 
- quoted (Cant 1:2): “The kisses of his mouth” → “Os beijos de sua boca”
- flag: Song 1:5's "black" (KJV) is "morena" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's "black … blackness" is rendered "negra … negrura" to keep his figure.
- flag: sensitive: Henry reads the bride's blackness as sin and defilement by nature ("The believer is black, as being defiled and sinful by nature").

### 2308 Cant 1:7-8

- quoted (Cant 1:8): “the footsteps of the flock” → “os rastros das ovelhas”
- flag: the English begins ",8", left over from the outline reference "(Song 1:7,8)"; kept as it stands.
- flag: the KJV's "the flock" is "as ovelhas" in the Bíblia Livre ("os rastros das ovelhas").

### 2309 Cant 1:9-17

### 2319 Cant 5:2-8 
- quoted (Cant 5:5): “sweet-smelling myrrh upon the handles” → “mirra sobre os puxadores da fechadura”
- flag: the KJV's "sweet smelling myrrh" (Song 5:5) is plain "mirra" in the Bíblia Livre, so "sweet-smelling" is not in the quoted words.
- flag: "Lord, help my unbelief" alludes to Mark 9:24 ("Creio, Senhor! Ajuda-me na minha incredulidade" in the Bíblia Livre); "ajuda-me na minha incredulidade" is used. "The watchmen wounded me" follows Song 5:7 KJV ("Os guardas … me feriram" in the Bíblia Livre); fitted.

### 2323 Cant 6:4-10

### 2325 Cant 7:1-9 
- translated, not quoted (Cant 7:5; The Bíblia Livre reads "o rei está como que atado em tuas tranças" (tresses), where the KJV has "held in the galleries"; Henry's explanation rests on "galleries" (the assemblies), so his words are translated.): “The King is held in the galleries”
- flag: "adorn the doctrine of God their Saviour in all things" is Titus 2:10; the Bíblia Livre's "em tudo adornem a doutrina de Deus nosso Salvador" is fitted ("adornam … seu Salvador").
