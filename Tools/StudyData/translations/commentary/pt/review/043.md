# To review: pt commentary batch 043

### 1640 Ps 109:21-31 Prayers and praises.
- quoted (1Pet 4:19): “suffer according to the will of God, commit the keeping of their souls to him” → “os que sofrem segundo a vontade de Deus, confiem suas almas”
- flag: "thy-mercy" has a stray hyphen in the source; translated "tua misericórdia".
- flag: 1 Pet 4:19: the Bíblia Livre has "confiem suas almas ao fiel Criador"; Henry's "to him" ("a ele, para que as guarde" for "the keeping of their souls") is translated.

### 1641 Ps 110:1-7 Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 110:7): “he shall drink of the brook in the way” → “Ele beberá do ribeiro no caminho”
- flag: "the power of the world" is probably a slip for "the power of the word"; translated as written ("o poder do mundo").
- flag: "the people of Christs" has a stray "s"; translated "o povo de Cristo".

### 1642 Ps 111:1-10 The Lord is to be praised for his works.
- quoted (Ps 111:2): “the works of the Lord” → “as obras do SENHOR”
- quoted (Ps 111:2): “the works of the Lord are” → “obras do SENHOR”
- quoted (Ps 111:5, Ps 111:6): “He will ever be mindful of his covenant; he” → “ele se lembrará para sempre de seu pacto”
- quoted (Ps 111:9): “He sent redemption unto his people” → “Ele enviou resgate a seu povo”
- flag: Henry's "verity, or truth" and "judgment" follow the KJV of Ps 111:7 ("verity and judgment"); the Bíblia Livre has "verdade e juízo" too.

### 1643 Ps 112:1-10 The blessedness of the righteous.

### 1644 Ps 113:1-9 An exhortation to praise God.
- quoted (Ps 113:3): “the Lord's name is to be praised” → “seja louvado o nome do SENHOR”
- quoted (Rev 15:4): “all nations shall come and worship before” → “todas as nações virão, e adorarão diante de”
- quoted (Neh 9:5): “is exalted above all blessing and praise” → “está exaltado sobre toda bênção e louvor”

- flag: Unlisted quotation of Luke 19:10: "buscar e salvar o que tinha se perdido" uses the Bíblia Livre's words (its "para buscar, e para salvar" shortened to Henry's "seek and save").

### 1645 Ps 114:1-8 An exhortation to fear God.

- flag: Unlisted quotation of 1 Cor 10:4: "a pedra era Cristo" from the Bíblia Livre.

### 1646 Ps 115:1-8 Glory to be ascribed to God.
- flag: The text opens with a fragment ("by trusting in him and praising him.") and "(9-18)" as separate paragraphs, left over from the full commentary's outline; translated as given.
- flag: "that they mercy and truth" is a slip for "thy"; translated "a tua misericórdia e a tua verdade".

### 1648 Ps 116:1-9 The psalmist declares his love to the Lord.
- flag: Unlisted echoes: "It is of his mercies that we are not consumed" (Lam 3:22, Bíblia Livre "É pelas bondades do SENHOR que não somos consumidos", adapted to Henry's "his") and Matt 11:28-29 ("cansados e sobrecarregados", "encontrareis descanso para as vossas almas", adapted to the third person).

### 1649 Ps 116:10-19 His desire to be thankful.
- quoted (Ps 116:13): “I will take the cup of salvation” → “Tomarei o copo da salvação”
- quoted (Ps 116:16): “truly I am thy servant” → “verdadeiramente eu sou teu servo”
- quoted (Ps 116:16): “the son of thine handmaid” → “filho de tua serva”
- quoted (Ps 116:16): “thou hast loosed my bonds” → “tu me soltaste das correntes que me prendiam”
- flag: Ps 116:13: the Bíblia Livre has "copo" (cup); "copo" is used throughout the section (cup of affliction, of consolation) so Henry's play on "cup" holds; "cálice" would read more dignified.
- flag: "the portion ... of my cup" echoes Ps 16:5 KJV; translated.

### 1651 Ps 118:1-18 It is good to trust in the Lord.
- flag: "God is ... the strength of his people, when he is not their song" plays on Ps 118:14 KJV ("my strength and song"); the Bíblia Livre has "minha força e minha canção"; Henry's "song" is rendered "cântico".

### 1652 Ps 118:19-29 The coming of Christ in his kingdom.
- quoted (Ps 118:22): “the Stone which the builders refused” → “a pedra que os construtores rejeitaram”
- quoted (Ps 118:24): “We will rejoice and be glad” → “alegremos e enchamos de alegria”
- flag: "The duty which the Lord has made, brings light with it" is evidently a slip for "The day which the Lord has made" (Ps 118:24, 27); translated "O dia que o Senhor fez".
- flag: "the Lord's day" here is Ps 118:24's "the day which the LORD hath made", which Henry also reads of the Christian Sunday ("Sabbath days ought to be rejoicing days"); kept as "o dia do Senhor" and "dias de sábado" without the bracketed note, since he doesn't say "Christian sabbath".
- flag: Ps 118:24: the Bíblia Livre's "alegremos e enchamos de alegria" is used as written.
