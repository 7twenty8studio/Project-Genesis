# To review: pt commentary batch 061

### 2788 Dan 5:1-9 
- quoted (Dan 5:5): “the part of the hand” → “parte da mão”

### 2790 Dan 5:18-31 
- flag: 'weighed in the balance, and found wanting' echoes Dn 5:27 (not listed); the Bíblia Livre's 'pesado… na balança, e… achado em falta' fitted in Henry's future tense.

### 2793 Dan 6:11-17 
- quoted (Dan 6:12): “into the den of lions” → “lançado na cova dos leões”
- quoted (Dan 6:17): “the king sealed it with his own signet” → “o rei … selou com seu anel”

### 2794 Dan 6:18-24 
- quoted (Dan 6:23): “because he believed in his God” → “havia confiado em seu Deus”
- quoted (Prov 11:8): “the righteous is delivered out of trouble, and the wicked cometh in his stead” → “o justo é livrado da angústia; e o perverso vem em seu lugar”
- flag: 'because he believed in his God' (KJV): the Bíblia Livre has 'havia confiado em seu Deus' (had trusted).

### 2795 Dan 6:25-28 
- quoted (Matt 6:13): “the power, and the glory, for ever” → “o Reino, o poder, e a glória, para sempre”
- flag: English has 'least at the last' (a slip for 'lest'); translated as 'lest' ('para que… não').

### 2798 Dan 7:15-28 
- quoted (Dan 7:21): “made war with the saints, and prevailed against them” → “fazia guerra contra os santos, e os vencia”
- quoted (Dan 7:18): “The saints of the Most High shall” → “Os santos do Altíssimo”
- quoted (Dan 7:18): “possess the kingdom for ever” → “possuirão o reino para todo o sempre”
- flag: sensitive: 2798 the little horn read as 'the rage of papal Rome against true Christians'; translated in full.

### 2799 Dan 8:1-14 
- quoted (Dan 8:8): “came up four notable ones” → “subiram outros quatro muito visíveis”
- quoted (Col 2:3): “in whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge” → “nele estão ocultos todos os tesouros da sabedoria e do conhecimento”
- flag: sensitive: 2799 the little horn read as 'the Mohammedan delusion' (Islam), which 'nearly destroyed the holy religion'; translated in full ('a ilusão maometana').

### 2802 Dan 9:4-19 
- quoted (Prov 14:34): “Sin is a reproach to any people” → “o pecado é a desgraça dos povos”
- quoted (Dan 9:19): “O Lord, hearken and do” → “presta atenção, Senhor, e faze”
- flag: 'Sin is a reproach to any people' (Pv 14:34): the Bíblia Livre's 'o pecado é a desgraça dos povos' used.
- flag: 'O Lord, hearken and do': Henry's play on 'hearken and do' rests on the KJV of Dn 9:19; the Bíblia Livre has 'presta atenção, Senhor, e faze', and his explanation is built on those words.

### 2803 Dan 9:20-27 
- quoted (1Pet 3:18): “sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God” → “sofreu uma vez pelos pecados, o justo pelos injustos; para que nos levasse a Deus”
- flag: sensitive: 2803 Henry charges 'the Jews' with 'most obstinate unbelief' in expecting another Messiah, and with the crime of the crucifixion that 'filled up the measure of their guilt'; translated in full.
- flag: unlisted echo of Hb 2:3 ('neglect so great salvation'): the Bíblia Livre's 'descuidarmos de tão grande salvação' used in Henry's third person ('descuidarem de tão grande salvação').

### 2804 Dan 10:1-9 
- flag: The second paragraph begins with a stray '. ' in the English; kept as given.
- flag: 'Hiddekel' has no Portuguese form in the glossary; written 'Hidequel' as in Almeida (the Bíblia Livre at Dn 10:4 reads 'rio Tigre').

### 2806 Dan 11:1-30 
- flag: 'From Dn 11:5-30, is generally considered to relate…': the English lacks a subject; rendered 'Dn 11:5-30 é geralmente considerado relativo…'. The overlap with 'from Dn 11:21' is Henry's own.

### 2807 Dan 11:31-45 
- flag: sensitive: 2807 the Roman empire's 'papal' state named as part of the antichristian power; translated in full.

### 2808 Dan 12:1-4 
- quoted (Job 19:25, Job 19:26): “shall stand at the latter day upon the earth” → “ao fim se levantará sobre a terra”
- flag: The second paragraph begins with a stray '. ' in the English; kept as given.
- flag: 'the great Prince' (Dn 12:1, not listed): the Bíblia Livre's 'o grande príncipe' used inside Henry's quotation marks; Michael is 'Miguel' as in Dn 12:1.
- flag: English has 'out of their heathenism of Judaism', apparently a slip for 'heathenism or Judaism'; rendered 'do seu paganismo ou judaísmo'.
- flag: sensitive: 2808 Judaism set beside heathenism as a state Jews must be 'awakened' out of; translated in full.

### 2809 Dan 12:5-13 
- quoted (Dan 12:6): “be to the end of these wonders” → “o fim destas maravilhas”
- quoted (Dan 12:7): “be for a time, times” → “um tempo, tempos, e a metade de um tempo”
- quoted (Dan 12:7): “the power of the holy people” → “o poder do povo santo”
- flag: sensitive: 2809 'the imposture of Mohammed, and the papal usurpation' as a twofold attack on the church; translated in full.
- flag: 'Go thou thy way' (Dn 12:13, KJV): the Bíblia Livre has 'caminha até o fim'; Henry's paraphrase translated ('Vai pelo teu caminho').

### 2810 Hos 1:1-7 
- flag: 'Lo-ruhamah' written 'Lo-Ruama' (Almeida's form); the Bíblia Livre translates the name, 'Não-Amada' (Os 1:6). Henry's 'second child' is Hosea's daughter: 'segunda filha'.
- flag: Henry's gloss of Jezreel as 'dispersion' kept as written.

### 2811 Hos 1:8-11 
- quoted (1John 4:19): “We love him, because he first loved us” → “Nós o amamos, porque ele nos amou primeiro”
- flag: 'Lo-ammi' written 'Lo-Ami' (Almeida's form); the Bíblia Livre translates the name, 'Não-Meu-Povo' (Os 1:9).

### 2813 Hos 2:6-13 
- flag: 'The way of God and duty is often hedged about with thorns, but… it is a sinful way that is hedged up with thorns': Henry contrasts 'hedged about' with 'hedged up' (blocked); rendered 'cercado' / 'fechado'.

### 2814 Hos 2:14-23 
- flag: 'Baali' and 'Ishi' (KJV, Os 2:16) kept as names with Henry's glosses ('Isi' as in the glossary); the Bíblia Livre translates them, 'meu Baal' and 'meu marido'.
- flag: 'Thou art my people' is Os 2:23 (not listed): the Bíblia Livre's 'Tu és meu povo' used; Henry's 'Thou art our God' (BL 'Tu és meu Deus') translated in his plural.

### 2816 Hos 3:4-5 
- quoted (Hos 3:5): “the Lord and his goodness” → “ao SENHOR e à sua bondade”
- flag: sensitive: 2816 the Jews said to be kept 'a separate people' for their future conversion to Christ; translated in full.

### 2817 Hos 4:1-5 
- quoted (Hos 4:1): “knowledge of God in the land” → “nem misericórdia, nem conhecimento de Deus na terra”
- flag: 'There was no truth…' (KJV): the Bíblia Livre has 'fidelidade' for 'truth'; Henry's whole line follows its wording.

### 2819 Hos 4:12-19 
- flag: 'Let them alone' (Os 4:17, KJV 'let him alone'): the Bíblia Livre has 'deixa-o'; Henry's plural translated ('Deixai-os').

### 2820 Hos 5:1-7 
- quoted (Isa 55:6): “the Lord while he may be found” → “ao SENHOR enquanto se pode achar”
