# To review: pt commentary batch 053

### 2422 Isa 40:1-11 
- quoted (Isa 40:2): “of the Lord's hand double for all” → “em dobro da mão do SENHOR por”
- flag: Is 40:2: the Bíblia Livre has "recebeu punição em dobro da mão do SENHOR por todo os seus pecados"; Henry reads "double" as the full satisfaction received in Christ, so only "em dobro da mão do SENHOR por" is quoted and "todos os seus pecados" is written in normal grammar (the Bíblia Livre's "todo os" is a slip).

### 2423 Isa 40:12-17 
- quoted (John 3:16): “he gave his only-begotten Son” → “deu o seu Filho unigênito”

### 2425 Isa 40:27-31 
- flag: "As the day, so shall the strength be" echoes Dt 33:25 (KJV "as thy days, so shall thy strength be"); the Bíblia Livre has "tua força será como os teus dias"; Henry's proverb is translated.

### 2426 Isa 41:1-9 
- flag: "the seed of Abraham his friend" echoes Is 41:8 (Bíblia Livre "semente de Abraão, meu amigo"); "a semente de Abraão, seu amigo" follows its wording in Henry's third person.

### 2427 Isa 41:10-20 
- quoted (Isa 41:10): “Fear thou not, for I am with thee” → “Não temas, porque eu estou contigo”
- quoted (John 7:38): “rivers of living water” → “rios de água viva”
- flag: "the worm Jacob" (Is 41:14): the Bíblia Livre has "verme de Jacó", used here; the threshing instrument "having sharp spikes" (Is 41:15, KJV "having teeth") is translated; the Bíblia Livre has "debulhador afiado, novo, que tem dentes pontiagudos".

### 2429 Isa 42:1-4 
- flag: Is 42:3: "bruised reed" and "smoking flax" follow the Bíblia Livre ("cana rachada", "pavio que fumega"); Henry's gloss of flax as a lamp's wick is translated ("mecha").

### 2432 Isa 42:18-25 
- quoted (Isa 42:21): “The Lord is well-pleased” → “O SENHOR se agradou”
- translated, not quoted (Isa 42:21; Henry's own sentence in the present tense ("the Lord is well-pleased in saving sinners"), not a quotation of Is 42:21; translated "o Senhor se agrada".): “the Lord is well-pleased”
- quoted (Heb 4:1): “fear, lest a promise being left us of entering into his rest, any” → “temamos, pois, para que, havendo sido deixada a promessa de entrar no seu repouso, não pareça que algum”
- flag: Is 42:21: the Bíblia Livre's past "O SENHOR se agradou" is used for Henry's present "The Lord is well-pleased".
- flag: Heb 4:1: Henry's "any of us" (the verse has "any of you") is written "algum de nós tenha ficado para trás" after the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: sensitive: "the destruction of the Jewish nation" as punishment for their sins, and "blind as the benighted heathen" (section 2432).

### 2433 Isa 43:1-7 
- flag: "they should be born up" is a slip for "borne up"; translated "seriam sustentados".

### 2434 Isa 43:8-13 
- flag: The source text ends without a full stop; left as given.

### 2436 Isa 43:22-28 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 43:25: "Eu, eu sou o que anulo tuas transgressões" from the Bíblia Livre; Henry's "yet" is kept outside it ("E ainda assim:"); the Bíblia Livre's "anulo" stands for the KJV's "blotteth out".
- flag: "his promise' sake" has a stray apostrophe in the source; translated "por amor da sua promessa".
- flag: "The Master" (God as master of the servants) is translated "O Senhor".
- flag: "declare these things, that thou mayest be justified" echoes Is 43:26 (Bíblia Livre "mostra teus argumentos, para que possas te justificar"); translated.

### 2437 Isa 44:1-8 
- flag: "Jeshurun" is written "Jesurum", the Bíblia Livre's spelling at Is 44:2.
- flag: "Israelites indeed, in whom is no guile" echoes Jo 1:47 (Bíblia Livre "verdadeiramente um israelita, em quem não há engano"); "em quem não há engano" follows it.

### 2438 Isa 44:9-20 
- quoted (Isa 44:20): “Is there not a lie in my right hand” → “Por acaso não é uma mentira o que há na minha mão direita?”

### 2439 Isa 44:21-28 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 44:22: "Volta a mim" from the Bíblia Livre. Henry's "thick cloud" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "como a névoa ... como a nuvem".

### 2441 Isa 45:5-10 
- translated, not quoted (Isa 45:5; Henry turns Is 45:5's first person ("fora de mim não há Deus") into a statement about Jehovah; translated.): “There is no God beside”
- flag: Is 45:7: Henry explains the KJV's "create evil" (not the evil of sin but of punishment); the Bíblia Livre has "crio a adversidade"; his "cria o mal" is translated.
- flag: "together the Lord hath created them" echoes Is 45:8 (Bíblia Livre "eu, o SENHOR, as criei"); translated.
- flag: "the Judge of all the earth will do right" echoes Gn 18:25 (Bíblia Livre "O juiz de toda a terra, não há de fazer o que é justo?"); "o Juiz de toda a terra fará o que é justo" follows its wording.

### 2443 Isa 45:20-25 
- flag: "the Lord our Righteousness" is Jr 23:6 used as a title; the Bíblia Livre's sentence ("O SENHOR é nossa justiça") doesn't read as a name, so it is translated "o Senhor, Justiça Nossa" (also in section 2458).

### 2445 Isa 46:5-13 
- flag: "show themselves men" follows the KJV of Is 46:8; the Bíblia Livre has "sede corajosos"; translated.

### 2448 Isa 48:1-8 
- translated, not quoted (Isa 48:1; "take the name of the Lord in vain" is Henry's own phrase (the commandment), not a quotation of Is 48:1's "jurais pelo nome do SENHOR"; translated.): “the name of the Lord”
- quoted (Isa 48:5): “before it came to pass” → “antes que acontecesse”

### 2449 Isa 48:9-15 
- quoted (Isa 48:10): “in the furnace of affliction” → “na fornalha da aflição”
- flag: "but not as silver" (Is 48:10): "porém não como a prata" follows the Bíblia Livre, whose verb is "purifiquei" (KJV "refined"); Henry's "refine" is written "purificá-los" there and "refinam" afterwards.

### 2450 Isa 48:16-22 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of 1 Co 10:4: "a pedra era Cristo" from the Bíblia Livre (Henry's "that Rock").
- flag: sensitive: "the rescue of the church from antichristian tyranny" (section 2450), in Henry's usage commonly aimed at the papacy.

### 2451 Isa 49:1-6 
- flag: "spake as never man spake" echoes Jo 7:46 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "Ninguém jamais falou assim como este homem"; translated (also in section 2457).
- flag: sensitive: the outline's "The unbelief and rejection of the Jews" (section 2451).

### 2452 Isa 49:7-12 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 49:9: "aos que estão em trevas: Aparecei" from the Bíblia Livre (KJV "Shew yourselves"); Henry's "Not only see, but be seen" still fits "Aparecei".
- flag: "in him God was reconciling the world to himself" echoes 2 Co 5:19; translated in Henry's word order.

### 2455 Isa 49:24-26 
- quoted (Isa 49:25): “the prey of the terrible shall be delivered” → “a presa do tirano escapará”
- quoted (Isa 49:26): “the mighty One of Jacob” → “o Poderoso de Jacó”
- flag: Is 49:25: the Bíblia Livre's "a presa do tirano escapará" stands for the KJV's "the prey of the terrible shall be delivered".

### 2456 Isa 50:1-3 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jo 1:11: "veio ao seu próprio, e os seus não o receberam" from the Bíblia Livre ("Ao seu próprio veio", in Henry's word order).
- flag: sensitive: the Jews "at last rejected for crucifying the Lord of glory" and the outline's "The rejection of the Jews" (section 2456).

### 2457 Isa 50:4-9 
- flag: "those whom he is an Advocate" lacks a "for"; translated "aqueles de quem ele é Advogado".

### 2458 Isa 50:10-11 
- quoted (Isa 50:10): “Let him trust in the name of the Lord” → “confie no nome do SENHOR”
- flag: The text opens with ",11", a fragment of the verse range (Is 50:10,11) left over in the source; kept as given.
- flag: "stay himself upon" (Is 50:10 KJV) is translated "se apoie"; the Bíblia Livre has "dependa de seu Deus".

### 2459 Isa 51:1-3 
- flag: "They make Christ more precious" has no clear antecedent in the source (perhaps such reflections); translated literally "Elas".
- flag: "shapen in sin" echoes Sl 51:5 (Bíblia Livre "em perversidade fui formado"); "formados em pecado" is translated.

### 2460 Isa 51:4-8 
- quoted (Heb 2:3): “How shall we escape if we neglect” → “Como nós escaparemos, se descuidarmos”
- flag: The source text ends without a full stop; left as given.
- flag: "The soul shall ... vanish like smoke" applies Is 51:6 (the heavens vanish like smoke, the earth waxes old like a garment) to soul and body; translated as written.
