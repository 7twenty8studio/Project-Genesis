# To review: pt commentary batch 017

### 546 Deut 16:1-17 The yearly feasts.
- flag: "aquele que prometeu é fiel" follows the Bíblia Livre's Hb 10:23 (unlisted); "alegrar-se na esperança" echoes Rm 12:12.

### 547 Deut 16:18-22 Of judges, Groves and images forbidden.
- flag: The title's "Groves" (KJV "grove", Dt 16:21) given as "bosques"; the Bíblia Livre has "bosque" there.

### 550 Deut 17:14-20 The choice of a king, His duties.
- quoted (Deut 17:19): “read therein all the days of his life” → “lerá nele todos os dias de sua vida”
- flag: "a cobiça da carne, a cobiça dos olhos e a ostentação da vida" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Jo 2:16 (unlisted).

### 552 Deut 18:9-14 The abominations of the Canaanites to be avoided.
- quoted (Deut 18:9): “to do after the abominations” → “a fazer segundo as abominações”
- flag: "não aprender a fazer" follows the Bíblia Livre's Dt 18:9 ("não aprenderás a fazer"); Henry has only "not to do after".

### 553 Deut 18:15-22 Christ the great Prophet.
- quoted (John 8:12): “the Light of the world” → “a luz do mundo”
- flag: The English has "the World by whom God speaks", read as a slip for "the Word" (as Jo 1:1 shows); translated "a Palavra".
- flag: "aquele que havia de vir" follows the Bíblia Livre's Mt 11:3 (unlisted; Henry's own quotation marks kept).

### 554 Deut 19:1-13 
- flag: "o Senhor, nossa Justiça" is Henry's title from Jr 23:6 (KJV "THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS"); the Bíblia Livre has "O SENHOR é nossa justiça".

### 556 Deut 19:15-21 The punishment of false witnesses.
- flag: "a esperança posta diante deles" follows the Bíblia Livre's Hb 6:18 ("a esperança posta diante de nós", unlisted).

### 557 Deut 20:1-9 Exhortation and proclamation respecting those who went to war.
- quoted (Ps 110:3): “Thy people shall be willing” → “Teu povo será voluntário”
- quoted (Deut 20:8): “as well as his heart” → “como seu coração”
- flag: "lutar a boa luta da fé" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Tm 6:12 (unlisted).
- flag: Henry's "fear not with the fear of them that are afraid" (Is 8:12) is his paraphrase, translated; the Bíblia Livre has "não temais o que eles temem".

### 558 Deut 20:10-20 Peace to be offered, What cities were to be devoted.
- flag: "Toda criatura de Deus é boa" and "nada deve ser rejeitado" follow the Bíblia Livre's 1 Tm 4:4 (unlisted).
- flag: "God is a better friend to man than he is to himself": read "he" as man (man is a worse friend to himself than God is); translated accordingly.

### 559 Deut 21:1-9 The expiation of uncertain murder.
- quoted (Eph 5:11): “fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness” → “das obras infrutíferas das trevas”
- flag: Source oddity: the second heading ("The first-born not to be disinherited…", with "(15-17)") sits at the start of the text, though the comment is on the murder law; kept as given.
- flag: Henry's "have fellowship with" given as "participamos das", as in the Bíblia Livre's Ef 5:11 ("não participeis das").

### 562 Deut 21:18-21 A stubborn son to be stoned.
- quoted (Deut 21:18): “a stubborn and rebellious son” → “filho obstinado e rebelde”

### 563 Deut 21:22-23 Malefactors not to be left hanging all night.
- quoted (Deut 21:23): “He that is hanged is accursed of God” → “maldição de Deus é o pendurado”
- flag: Henry's "accursed of God" (KJV) is "maldição de Deus" in the Bíblia Livre's Dt 21:23; the following "ser maldito de Deus" translates his phrase.

### 564 Deut 22:1-4 Of humanity towards brethren.
- flag: The golden rule in Henry's quotation marks is his own wording (cf. Mt 7:12), translated.

### 566 Deut 22:13-30 Against impurity.
- quoted (1Pet 2:11): “fleshly lusts which war against the soul” → “desejos carnais, que batalham contra a alma”

### 568 Deut 23:9-14 Of fugitive servants, Usury, and other precepts.
- flag: Source oddity: this title and the next one ("Cleanliness enjoined") look swapped; this text is on the camp's cleanness (Dt 23:9-14). Titles translated as given.

### 569 Deut 23:15-25 Cleanliness enjoined.
- quoted (Deut 23:23): “which is gone out of thy lips” → “o que teus lábios pronunciarem”
- translated, not quoted (Deut 23:23; In the Bíblia Livre's Dt 23:23 "Guardarás" and "e farás" stand apart, around the clause already quoted; Henry's "thou shalt keep and perform it" is translated.): “thou shalt keep and perform”
- flag: Source oddity: this title and the previous one look swapped; this text is on fugitive servants, usury and vows (Dt 23:15-25). Translated as given.

### 570 Deut 24:1-4 Of divorce.
- flag: "ajuda idônea" for Henry's "help meet" follows the Bíblia Livre's Gn 2:18 (unlisted).
- flag: Source oddity: the next section's heading ("Of new-married persons…", with "(5-13)") sits at the start of this text; kept as given.

### 571 Deut 24:5-13 
- quoted (Deut 24:13): “sleep in his own raiment” → “durma em sua roupa”

### 573 Deut 25:1-3 Extent of punishment.
- quoted (1Cor 11:32): “should not be condemned with the world” → “condenados com o mundo”
- flag: Henry's "they should not be" is "não sejamos" in the Bíblia Livre's 1 Co 11:32; only "condenados com o mundo" is quoted, with "não sejam" around it.

### 578 Deut 26:1-11 
- quoted (Deut 26:5): “A Syrian ready to perish was my father” → “Um arameu a ponto de perecer foi meu pai”
- flag: Henry's point "Jacob is here called a Syrian" rests on the KJV's "Syrian"; the Bíblia Livre's Dt 26:5 has "arameu" (Aram = Syria).

### 580 Deut 26:16-19 The covenant between God and the people.
- quoted (Eph 1:4): “we should be holy” → “para que fôssemos santos”
- flag: "escolhidos … para a obediência" and "povo particular, zeloso de boas obras" echo the Bíblia Livre's 1 Pe 1:2 and Tt 2:14 (unlisted).

### 581 Deut 27:1-10 The law to be written on stones in the promised land.
- quoted (Deut 27:3): “the words of this law” → “as palavras desta lei”
- translated, not quoted (Dan 2:45; The Bíblia Livre's Dn 2:45 puts it in another order ("do monte foi cortada uma pedra sem auxílio de mãos"), which can't follow "Cristo, nosso Altar, é"; translated, keeping its "sem auxílio de mãos".): “cut out of the mountain without hands”
- flag: "não tendo boa aparência nem formosura", "cabeça de esquina" and "sábios para a salvação" follow the Bíblia Livre's Is 53:2, Sl 118:22 and 2 Tm 3:15 (unlisted).

### 582 Deut 27:11-26 The curses to be pronounced on mount Ebal.
- quoted (Titus 2:12): “live soberly, righteously, and godly in this present world” → “neste tempo presente de maneira sóbria, justa e devota”
- flag: "renunciar à irreverência e aos maus desejos mundanos" and "quanto ao ser interior" follow the Bíblia Livre's Tt 2:12 and Rm 7:22 (unlisted); "irreverência e a injustiça" echoes its Rm 1:18.
- flag: "Ebal" kept as in the glossary.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 584 Deut 28:15-44 The curses for disobedience.
- flag: "andam pela vista, e não pela fé" echoes the Bíblia Livre's 2 Co 5:7 ("andamos pela fé, e não pela vista").

### 585 Deut 28:45-68 Their ruin, if disobedient.
- quoted (Deut 28:63): “plucked from off the land” → “tirados de sobre a terra”
- quoted (Jer 26:6): “to all the nations of the earth” → “a todas as nações da terra”
- quoted (Gal 3:13): “Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law” → “Cristo nos resgatou da maldição da Lei ao se fazer maldição para o nosso benefício”
- quoted (Deut 28:47): “with gladness of heart, for the abundance” → “com alegria de coração, pela abundância”
- flag: "o começo das dores" and "seu verme não morre, e o fogo nunca se apaga" follow the Bíblia Livre's Mt 24:8 and Mc 9:44 (unlisted).
- flag: Quote 2 runs on through Henry's "by being made a curse for us" (KJV Gl 3:13), in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: "redundar-nos em testemunho" translates Henry's "turn unto us for a testimony" (KJV Lc 21:13); the Bíblia Livre's wording differs.

### 586 Deut 29:1-9 Moses calls Israel's mercies to remembrance.
- quoted (Deut 29:1): “the words of the covenant” → “as palavras do pacto”

### 587 Deut 29:10-21 The Divine wrath on those who flatter themselves in their wickedness.
- flag: "coração mau e infiel, que … se afastarem do Deus vivo" follows the Bíblia Livre's Hb 3:12 (unlisted).
- flag: "raízes que produzem fel e absinto" translates the KJV's "gall and wormwood" (Dt 29:18); the Bíblia Livre has "veneno e amargura", but Henry's "bitter morsel" and "taste" build on the KJV image.

### 588 Deut 29:22-28 The ruin of the Jewish nation.
- quoted (Amos 9:15): “their land; and they shall no more be pulled up” → “sua terra, e nunca mais serão arrancados”
- flag: "guardados pelo poder de Deus" (KJV 1 Pe 1:5) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "guardados no poder de Deus".

### 589 Deut 29:29-29 Secret things belong unto God.
- quoted (Deut 29:29): “we may do all the words of this law” → “cumpramos todas as palavras desta lei”

### 590 Deut 30:1-10 Mercies promised to the repentant.
- translated, not quoted (Isa 60:22; The Bíblia Livre's Is 60:22 is in the first person ("eu, o SENHOR, o farei depressa em seu devido tempo"); Henry puts it in the third, so it is translated close to those words.): “the Lord will hasten it in his time”
- flag: Henry's quotation "circumcise their hearts" (Dt 30:6) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "circuncidará o SENHOR teu Deus teu coração".
- flag: "uma nação nascerá num dia" translates Henry's echo of Is 66:8; the Bíblia Livre has it as a question ("Nasceria uma nação de uma só vez?").
- flag: The English has "the prophetic promise … yet remain"; translated in the singular.

### 591 Deut 30:11-14 The commandment manifest.
- flag: "quem corre possa lê-la" echoes Hc 2:2; "a palavra está perto de nós" echoes the Bíblia Livre's Rm 10:8 ("A palavra está perto de ti").
