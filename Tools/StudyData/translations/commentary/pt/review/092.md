# To review: pt commentary batch 092

### 3774 Phil 4:2-9 Gives directions to some, and to all in general.
- quoted (Phil 4:3): “in the book of life” → “no livro da vida”
- quoted (Phil 4:6): “in every thing by prayer” → “em tudo, por meio de orações”
- translated, not quoted (Phil 4:7; Henry changes the person ("our hearts and minds"); the Bíblia Livre has "os vossos corações e as vossas mentes"): “hearts and minds through Christ Jesus”

### 3776 Phil 4:20-23 He concludes with prayer to God the Father, and his usual blessing.
- quoted (Phil 4:23): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “a graça do nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”

### intro Col 
- flag: "they sent to the apostle": rendered "enviaram mensageiros ao apóstolo" (sent word/messengers), the plain sense of Henry's elliptical phrase.

### 3777 Col 1:1-8 The apostle Paul salutes the Colossians, and blesses God for their faith, love, and hope.
- flag: "the word of truth" (Col 1:5) is Henry's own sentence here; the Bíblia Livre has "pala [sic] palavra da verdade do evangelho"; rendered "a palavra da verdade" without quoting.
- flag: The source ends with "(Col 1:9-14)", apparently the heading reference of the next section, kept as "(Cl 1:9-14)".

### 3778 Col 1:9-14 Prays for their fruitfulness in spiritual knowledge.
- quoted (Col 1:9): “might be filled with the knowledge” → “cheios do conhecimento”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Ap 7:14 ("come out of great tribulation"): Bíblia Livre "vieram da grande tribulação"; Henry's present tense kept ("vêm da grande tribulação"), so translated, not quoted.

### 3779 Col 1:15-23 Gives a glorious view of Christ.
- quoted (Col 1:18): “is the Head of the body, the church” → “é a cabeça do corpo, da Igreja”
- flag: unlisted allusion to Jo 14:9 ("he that hath seen Him hath seen the Father"): Henry turns Christ's "me" into "Him", so translated ("quem o viu, viu o Pai"), not quoted; Bíblia Livre: "Quem a mim tem visto, já tem visto ao Pai".

### 3780 Col 1:24-29 And sets out his own character, as the apostle of the Gentiles.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Cl 1:24 ("fill up that which remains of the sufferings of Christ"): Bíblia Livre's words used, "cumpre o restante das aflições de Cristo" (it has "aflições" where the KJV has "afflictions" and Henry "sufferings"); not in the quotes list, so not checked.

### 3782 Col 2:8-17 He cautions against the errors of heathen philosophy; also against Jewish traditions, and rites which had been fulfilled in Christ.
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's word "complete" (Col 2:10, "ye are complete in him"); the Bíblia Livre says "vos tornais plenos nele". Rendered "completos" in his quotation marks to keep his point; a reviewer may prefer "plenos".
- flag: "hand-writing" (Col 2:14, KJV "handwriting of ordinances") rendered "escrito de dívida"; the Bíblia Livre has "certidão de nossa dívida".
- flag: "we, who were dead in sins, are quickened" alludes to Cl 2:13 (Bíblia Livre: "mortos em pecados"); kept as Henry's own sentence.

### 3783 Col 2:18-23 Against worshipping angels; and against legal ordinances.
- quoted (Col 2:23): “the satisfying of the flesh” → “a satisfação da carne”
- flag: Henry's quoted "in any honour" is the KJV of Col 2:23 ("not in any honour to the satisfying of the flesh"); the Bíblia Livre reads "não têm valor algum contra a satisfação da carne", so his words were translated ("em honra alguma"), keeping his quotation marks.
- flag: Henry's last sentences follow the KJV's sense of Col 2:23; the Bíblia Livre's "contra a satisfação da carne" gives a different sense, though the words "a satisfação da carne" are quoted.

### 3784 Col 3:1-4 
- flag: "whose life is now hid with Christ" matches Cl 3:3 in the Bíblia Livre ("está escondida com Cristo"), used as Henry's own sentence; not listed as a quotation.

### 3786 Col 3:12-17 
- quoted (Col 3:12): “the elect of God, holy and beloved” → “escolhidos de Deus, santos e amados”
- quoted (Col 3:15): “Let the peace of God rule in your hearts” → “a paz de Deus … governe em vossos corações”
- quoted (Acts 9:29): “in the name of the Lord Jesus” → “no nome do Senhor Jesus”
- flag: Quote 2 is listed under At 9:29, but Henry is paraphrasing Cl 3:17; both verses read "no nome do Senhor Jesus" in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: Quote 1: the Bíblia Livre puts a clause between "a paz de Deus" and "governe em vossos corações"; Henry's short form is given as two runs.

### 3787 Col 3:18-25 
- quoted (Col 3:25): “doeth wrong, shall receive for the wrong which he hath done” → “aquele que fizer injustiça receberá conforme a injustiça que cometer”
- flag: sensitive: Henry's teaching on wives' submission and on servants' (slaves') obedience and contentment "at the providence of God which put them in that relation" (Col 3:18-25), translated in full.

### 3789 Col 4:2-6 Persons of all ranks to persevere in prayer, and Christian prudence.
- flag: "Though it be not always of grace, it must always be with grace" plays on Col 4:6 (Bíblia Livre: "seja sempre com graça"); kept as Henry's own words.

### 3791 Col 4:10-18 Sends greetings; and concludes with a blessing.
- flag: "The effectual, fervent prayer ... availeth much" echoes Tg 5:16 (KJV); the verse lookup failed for Jas 5:16, so it was translated, not quoted.

### 3792 1Thess 1:1-5 The faith, love, and patience of the Thessalonians, are evident tokens of their election which was manifested in the power with which the gospel came to them.
- flag: Source typo "with out lips" read as "with our lips" ("com os nossos lábios").

### 3793 1Thess 1:6-10 Its powerful and exemplary effects upon their hearts and lives.
- quoted (1Thess 1:10): “from the wrath to come” → “da ira futura”

### 3794 1Thess 2:1-6 The apostle reminds the Thessalonians of his preaching and behaviour.
- flag: Source typo "wordly" read as "worldly" ("mundano").
- flag: "this God who trieth our hearts" (1 Ts 2:4): the Bíblia Livre's words "que prova os nossos corações" were used; unlisted quotation of 1 Ts 2:4.

### 3796 1Thess 2:13-16 And of their receiving the gospel as the word of God.
- flag: "filling up the measure of their sins" (1 Ts 2:16, KJV "to fill up their sins alway"); the Bíblia Livre says "estão se enchendo constantemente de pecados"; Henry's phrase translated.
- flag: sensitive: 1 Ts 2:14-16 speaks of the Jews who killed the Lord and forbade preaching to the Gentiles; Henry's condemnation of murder and persecution and of those "who deny them the Bible" (probably aimed at the Roman Church) is translated in full.
- flag: "no zeal ... can excuse it": the singular "it" (for murder and persecution) is rendered plural ("desculpá-los").

### 3797 1Thess 2:17-20 His joy on their account.
- quoted (Rom 1:9): “spirit in the gospel of his Son” → “espírito no evangelho do seu Filho”
- flag: The source's last sentence ends without a full stop ("…who are in darkness"); kept as it stands.

### 3798 1Thess 3:1-5 The apostle sent Timothy to establish and comfort the Thessalonians.
- flag: "kept from being improved by any devices of the tempter": "improved" read in its old sense "made use of, taken advantage of" ("explorados"); it may be a slip for "imposed upon".

### 3799 1Thess 3:6-10 He rejoiced at the good tidings of their faith and love.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rm 10:17 ("faith cometh by hearing"): the Bíblia Livre's words "a fé vem pelo ouvir" used.

### 3800 1Thess 3:11-13 And for their increase in grace.
- flag: "acknowledge God in all our ways, and he will direct our paths" alludes to Pv 3:6 with the person changed; translated (Bíblia Livre: "Dá reconhecimento a ele em todas os teus caminhos; e ele endireitará tuas veredas").

### 3802 1Thess 4:9-12 To brotherly love, peaceable behaviour, and diligence.
- flag: "let us study to be quiet" (1 Ts 4:11): rendered "procuremos viver quietos", close to the Bíblia Livre's "procurai viver quietos" (person changed, so not quoted); "to work with their own hands" likewise echoes "trabalhando com as vossas próprias mãos".

### 3803 1Thess 4:13-18 Not to sorrow unduly for the death of godly relations and friends, considering the glorious resurrection of their bodies at Christ's second coming.
- flag: Source slip "in times sorrow" read as "in times of sorrow" ("nos tempos de tristeza").

### 3804 1Thess 5:1-5 The apostle exhorts to be always ready for the coming of Christ to judgment, which will be with suddenness and surprise.
- flag: unlisted quotations of 1 Ts 5:5 ("filhos da luz") and 1 Ts 5:3 ("Paz e segurança", "repentina destruição"; KJV "sudden destruction", Henry "utter destruction") use the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3805 1Thess 5:6-11 
- quoted (1Thess 5:9, 1Thess 5:10): “by our Lord Jesus Christ, who died for us” → “por meio do nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo, que morreu por nós”
- flag: "Our moderation ... should be known to all men" echoes the KJV of Fp 4:5 ("Let your moderation be known unto all men"); the Bíblia Livre has "A vossa bondade seja conhecida"; translated as Henry's words.
