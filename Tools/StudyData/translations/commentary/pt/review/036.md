# To review: pt commentary batch 036

### 1402 Ps 3:4-8 He triumphs over his fears, and gives God the glory, and takes to himself the comfort.
- quoted (Ps 3:8): “Salvation belongeth unto the Lord” → “A salvação vem do SENHOR”
- flag: "a joyful resurrection" rendered "uma ressurreição gloriosa"; consider "alegre" if a closer word is wanted.

### 1403 Ps 4:1-5 The children of men proved, and the happiness of godly people.
- quoted (Ps 4:5): “Put your trust in the Lord” → “Confiai no SENHOR”
- flag: The English is garbled near the end: "All confidence must be pan answer. Open not the mouth to excuse sin. All confidence only:" repeats a sentence and loses words (probably "All confidence must be placed in God only"); translated as it stands ("pma resposta" for "pan answer").
- flag: Two sentences begin with a lower-case "when" in the English; kept lower case.
- flag: "they may be spoken with" (your hearts) rendered "ele pode ser falado" (the heart); the sense is "you may speak with them".
- flag: "Be still" (Sl 4:4) given as "Fazei silêncio", the Bíblia Livre's words there.

### 1404 Ps 4:6-8 God's favour is happiness.
- flag: "Wordly" in the English, a slip for "Worldly"; translated "mundanas".

### 1405 Ps 5:1-6 God will certainly hear prayer: David gives to God the glory, and takes to himself the comfort.
- flag: A sentence begins with a lower-case "sin" in the English; kept lower case.

### 1406 Ps 5:7-12 He prayed for himself, that God would guide him, and for all the Lord's people, that God would give them joy, and keep them safe.
- flag: "the righteous or just One": the two English synonyms become one Portuguese word, "o Justo".

### 1407 Ps 6:1-7 The psalmist deprecates God's wrath, and begs for the return of his favour.
- quoted (Phil 1:24): “to abide in the flesh is more” → “ficar na carne é mais”
- flag: Fp 1:24 in the Bíblia Livre: "ficar na carne é mais necessário por causa de vós"; Henry's "profitable for the church" translated after the quoted words.

### 1408 Ps 6:8-10 He assures himself of an answer of peace.
- flag: "The word signifies prayer made to God, the righteous Judge" seems to belong to a fuller comment on another verse (Sl 7) or to have lost its context; translated as it stands.

### 1409 Ps 7:1-9 The psalmist prays to God to plead his cause, and judge for him.
- quoted (Ps 7:9): “the righteous God trieth the hearts” → “tu, ó justo Deus, que provas os corações e os sentimentos”
- flag: Sl 7:9: Henry's "For the righteous God trieth the hearts and the reins" is a statement; the Bíblia Livre's words are an address ("tu, ó justo Deus, que provas…"), and "sentimentos" stands for the KJV's "reins".

### 1410 Ps 7:10-17 He expresses confidence in God, and will give him the glory of his deliverance.
- quoted (2Pet 3:9): “to us-ward, not willing that any should perish” → “paciente para conosco, não querendo que alguns pereçam”
- flag: 2 Pe 3:9 in the Bíblia Livre has "alguns" where the KJV has "any".
- flag: "If he turn not" (Sl 7:12) translated: the Bíblia Livre's verse reads differently ("para aquele que não se arrepende").

### 1411 Ps 8:1-2 God is to be glorified, for making known himself to us.
- flag: Echo of 2 Co 4:7 ("a excelência do poder seja de Deus, e não de nós" in the Bíblia Livre) in Henry's sentence; translated, since he changes "us" to "man".

### 1412 Ps 8:3-9 And for making even the heavenly bodies useful to man, thereby placing him but little lower than the angels.
- quoted (Ps 8:6): “to have dominion over the works” → “ter controle sobre as obras”
- quoted (Ps 8:1): “Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth” → “nosso Senhor, quão glorioso é o teu nome sobre toda a terra”
- flag: Sl 8:9 (the psalm's close, which Henry has in mind) repeats Sl 8:1; the quotation is matched to v. 1 and given from the second half of the Bíblia Livre's address ("Ah DEUS, nosso Senhor, …"), lower case after the colon.
- flag: "glorioso" in the Bíblia Livre for the KJV's "excellent".

### 1413 Ps 9:1-10 David praises God for protecting his people.
- quoted (Rom 2:2): “We are sure that the judgment of God is according to truth” → “Sabemos que o julgamento de Deus é segundo a verdade”
- flag: Echo of Sl 9:10 ("nunca desamparaste aos que te buscam" in the Bíblia Livre) in Henry's third person; translated.

### 1414 Ps 9:11-20 And for cause to praise him.
- flag: "dust in his and about him" in the English, apparently a slip for "dust in him and about him"; rendered "pó em si e ao redor de si".
- flag: "the cry of the humble" (Sl 9:12) and "a very present help" (Sl 46:1) translated in Henry's own words; the Bíblia Livre has "o clamor dos que sofrem" and "socorro oportuno".

### 1415 Ps 10:1-11 The psalmist complains of the wickedness of the wicked.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 10:4; The Bíblia Livre's Sl 10:4 has no word for 'seek after God' ("o perverso não se importa; Deus não existe em todos os seus pensamentos"), so Henry's point would be lost.): “will not seek after God”

### 1416 Ps 10:12-18 He prays to God to appear for the relief of his people.
- flag: Echo of Rm 16:20 ("esmagará Satanás em pedaços debaixo dos vossos pés" in the Bíblia Livre) in Henry's first person plural; translated.

### 1417 Ps 11:1-7 David's struggle with, and triumph over a strong temptation to distrust God, and betake himself to indirect means for his own safety, in a time of danger.
- flag: "the Lord is that Spirit" (2 Co 3:17) given as "o Senhor é o Espírito", the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted quotation).
- flag: Echo of Rm 10:6-8 ("A palavra está perto de ti" in the Bíblia Livre) turned into Henry's "us"; translated.

### 1418 Ps 12:1-8 The psalmist begs help of God, because there were none among men whom he durst trust.
- quoted (2Tim 3:1, 2Tim 3:2): “perilous times shall come” → “virão tempos difíceis”
- quoted (Ps 12:5): “the oppression of the poor” → “opressão aos humildes”
- quoted (Ps 12:5): “the sighing of the needy” → “gemido dos necessitados”
- flag: Sl 12:5 in the Bíblia Livre has "humildes" where the KJV Henry quotes has "poor".

### 1419 Ps 13:1-6 The psalmist complains that God had long withdrawn. He earnestly prays for comfort. He assures himself of an answer of peace.
- flag: "our countenances will be no more said" in the English, a slip for "sad" (1 Sm 1:18); rendered "o nosso semblante já não estará triste".
- flag: Henry's verse numbers (v. 5 joy, v. 4 dejection) follow the KJV's Sl 13; check them against the Bíblia Livre's numbering.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 13:6 ("He has dealt bountifully with me"): "ele tem me feito muito bem" in the Bíblia Livre's words; the closing "the Lord hath dealt bountifully with them" given the same way in the third person.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 53:3: "homem de dores" as in the Bíblia Livre.

### 1420 Ps 14:1-7 A description of the depravity of human nature, and the deplorable corruption of a great part of mankind.
- quoted (Ps 14:1): “The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God” → “O tolo diz em seu coração: Não há Deus”
- quoted (Ps 14:3): “They are all gone aside” → “Todos se desviaram”
- quoted (Ps 14:3): “there is none that doeth good, no, not one” → “não há quem faça o bem, nem um sequer”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's "Não há Deus" is also used for Henry's later "There is no God" in his own sentence.

### 1421 Ps 15:1-5 The way to heaven, if we would be happy, we must be holy. We are encouraged to walk in that way.
- quoted (Col 4:12): “complete in all the will of God” → “em toda a vontade de Deus”
- quoted (Ps 15:2, Ps 15:3): “the truth in his heart. He” → “com seu coração fala a verdade”
- quoted (Ps 15:5): “He that doeth these things shall” → “quem faz isto, nunca se abalará”
- flag: Cl 4:12: the Bíblia Livre's "íntegros e plenos em toda a vontade de Deus" is plural; only "em toda a vontade de Deus" is quoted, "pleno" in the singular for Henry's "complete".
- flag: "Wicked people are vile people … so the word signifies": Henry explains the KJV's "vile person" (Sl 15:4); the Bíblia Livre has "pessoa que é digna de reprovação".
- flag: Henry's "He that doeth these things shall not be moved for ever" given in the Bíblia Livre's words, which lack "for ever" but say "nunca".

### 1422 Ps 16:1-11 This psalm begins with expressions of devotion, which may be applied to Christ; but ends with such confidence of a resurrection, as must be applied to Christ, and to him only.
- quoted (Ps 116:7): “Return unto thy rest, O my soul” → “Minha alma, volta ao teu descanso”
- quoted (Col 1:18): “the Head of the body, the church” → “a cabeça do corpo, da Igreja”
- quoted (Ps 16:8): “set the Lord always before” → “ao SENHOR continuamente diante de”
- flag: "Those that have God for their portion, have a goodly heritage" (Sl 16:6), "fulness of joy … pleasures for evermore" (Sl 16:11) and "forsake our own mercies for lying vanities" (Jn 2:8) translated in Henry's words; the Bíblia Livre words these differently ("bela propriedade", "fartura de alegrias", "coisas inúteis e ilusórias").
- flag: "The saints in the earth are excellent ones" (Sl 16:3, KJV "the excellent") rendered "os excelentes".

### 1423 Ps 17:1-7 David's integrity.
- flag: The English repeats a phrase ("it will be very lead to destruction. If we carefully avoid the paths of sin, it will be very comfortable"); the repetition is translated as it stands.
- flag: "by faith prayer" in the English, read as "by faith and prayer"; rendered "pela fé e oração".
- flag: Echoes of Sl 17:5, 17:7 and 119:132 translated in Henry's words; the Bíblia Livre words them differently.

### 1424 Ps 17:8-15 The character of his enemies. His hope of happiness.
- quoted (Ps 17:14): “have their portion in this life” → “cuja parte está … esta vida”
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's Sl 17:14 has a split word ("cuja parte está n esta vida"); the quotation is written "cuja parte está nesta vida" and answered in two runs.
- flag: Echo of Sl 17:15 ("serei satisfeito de tua semelhança, quando eu despertar" in the Bíblia Livre) in Henry's prayer; translated.

### 1425 Ps 18:1-19 David rejoices in the deliverances God wrought for him.
- quoted (Ps 18:1): “I will love thee, O Lord, my strength” → “Eu te amarei, SENHOR, tu és minha força”
- flag: "the powers of darkness prevented him": "prevented" in the old sense of Sl 18:18 KJV ("came against"); rendered "o confrontaram" after the Bíblia Livre's "Eles me confrontaram".
- flag: "a greater than David is here" echoes Mt 12:42 ("quem é maior que Salomão" in the Bíblia Livre); rendered "aqui está quem é maior que Davi".
- flag: Henry's verse numbers (v. 7-15, v. 18) follow the KJV's Sl 18; check them against the Bíblia Livre's numbering.

### 1426 Ps 18:20-28 He takes the comfort of his integrity, which God had cleared up.
- quoted (Ps 18:21): “the ways of the Lord” → “os caminhos do SENHOR”
- quoted (Ps 12:6): “The words of the Lord are pure words” → “As palavras do SENHOR são palavras puras”
- quoted (Ps 18:27): “wilt bring down high looks” → “humilhas aos olhos que se exaltam”
- quoted (Ps 18:28): “Thou wilt light my candle” → “Tu acendes minha lâmpada”
- quoted (Ps 18:28): “Thou wilt light my candle” → “Tu acendes minha lâmpada”
- flag: Henry's "Thou wilt light my candle" is future; the Bíblia Livre's Sl 18:28 is present ("tu acendes minha lâmpada"), kept in all three places; "lâmpada" for the KJV's "candle".
- flag: Sl 12:6 ("As palavras do SENHOR são palavras puras") is quoted while commenting on Sl 18:30, whose Bíblia Livre wording may differ.
