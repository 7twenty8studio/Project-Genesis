# To review: pt commentary batch 027

### 1008 1Kings 21:17-29 Elijah denounces judgments against Ahab.
- flag: "vendido como servo do pecado" is Rm 7:14 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted; KJV "sold under sin").
- flag: "vendeu-se ao pecado": KJV 1 Rs 21:25 "sold himself to work wickedness", Bíblia Livre "se vendeu a fazer o mal"; Henry's own phrase, translated.
- flag: "irá para sua casa justificado" alludes to Lc 18:14; translated.

### 1009 1Kings 22:1-14 Jehoshaphat makes a league with Ahab.
- quoted (1Kings 21:28): “the word of the Lord” → “palavra do SENHOR”
- flag: "Sobe" is 1 Rs 22:6 and "Assim disse o SENHOR" the Bíblia Livre's usual form of "Thus saith the Lord" (unlisted).
- flag: The English "to add ... to words that men would put into their mouths" mixes singular and plural (the preacher / their mouths); rendered "lhe poriam na boca".
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1011 1Kings 22:29-40 Death of Ahab.
- flag: The English spells "Johoshaphat", a slip for Jehoshaphat; translated "Josafá".
- flag: "Como eu, assim tu" is 1 Rs 22:4 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted; KJV "I am as thou art").

### 1015 2Kings 1:9-18 
- quoted (Isa 55:6): “the Lord while he may be found” → “ao SENHOR enquanto se pode achar”
- flag: "revelar do céu a ira de Deus contra a impiedade e a injustiça dos homens" echoes Rm 1:18 (KJV wording); translated.

### 1016 2Kings 2:1-8 Elijah divides Jordan.
- flag: "os resgatados do SENHOR" is Is 35:10 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "Ó morte, onde está o teu aguilhão" echoes 1 Co 15:55 (Bíblia Livre: "Onde está, ó morte, o teu aguilhão?"), extended by Henry; translated.

### 1018 2Kings 2:13-18 Elisha is manifested to be Elijah's successor.
- quoted (Rev 22:6): “The Lord God of the holy prophets” → “o Senhor, o Deus dos santos profetas”
- quoted (2Kings 2:15): “The sons of the prophets” → “Os filhos dos profetas”
- flag: "o mesmo ontem, hoje, e eternamente" is Hb 13:8 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 1019 2Kings 2:19-25 Elisha heals the waters of Jericho, Those that mocked Elisha destroyed.
- flag: "sobe" (Henry's quotation marks, KJV "go up") is the Bíblia Livre's word in 2 Rs 2:23 ("Sobe, calvo!").
- flag: "a tolice que está amarrada ao seu coração" echoes Pv 22:15 (Bíblia Livre: "A tolice está amarrada ao coração do menino").
- flag: "uma maldição sem causa" alludes to Pv 26:2 (KJV "the curse causeless"); translated.

### 1020 2Kings 3:1-5 Jehoram, king of Israel.
- quoted (2Kings 3:2): “put away the image of Baal” → “tirou as estátuas de Baal”
- flag: KJV "the image of Baal" (singular) is "as estátuas de Baal" (plural) in the Bíblia Livre; its words kept.

### 1021 2Kings 3:6-19 War with Moab, The intercession of Elisha.
- quoted (Prov 19:3): “the foolishness of man perverteth his way” → “a loucura do homem perverte seu caminho”
- quoted (Prov 19:3): “his heart fretteth against the Lord” → “seu coração se ira contra o SENHOR”
- quoted (Ps 84:6): “the valley of Baca” → “Vale de Baca”
- flag: The English "He called these kings together" is rendered "Ele mesmo reunira esses reis" to keep Henry's point that he blames Providence for his own act.
- flag: "fazer até dele uma fonte" follows the Bíblia Livre of Sl 84:6 ("fazem dele uma fonte"; KJV "make it a well").
- flag: "mais que vencedores" is Rm 8:37 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 1022 2Kings 3:20-27 Water supplied, Moab overcome.
- flag: "Paz, paz" is Jr 6:14 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 1023 2Kings 4:1-7 Elisha multiplies the widow's oil.
- flag: "viver do que restasse" echoes 2 Rs 4:7 (Bíblia Livre: "vivei do que restar"; KJV "live thou and thy children of the rest").

### 1024 2Kings 4:8-17 The Shunammite obtains a son.
- flag: "habitar em meio do nosso povo" echoes 2 Rs 4:13 (Bíblia Livre: "Eu habito em meio de meu povo").

### 1025 2Kings 4:18-37 The Shunammite's son restored to life.
- flag: "Ó mulher, grande é a tua fé" is Mt 15:28 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "Está tudo bem": Henry builds on the KJV's "It is well" (2 Rs 4:26); the Bíblia Livre has "Paz" there, so his point rests on the KJV wording; translated.

### 1026 2Kings 4:38-44 The miracle of healing the pottage, and of feeding the sons of the prophets.
- quoted (2Kings 4:38): “the sons of the prophets” → “os filhos dos profetas”
- quoted (Ex 15:26): “I am the Lord that healeth thee” → “eu sou o SENHOR que te sara”
- quoted (Ps 132:15): “will abundantly bless her provision” → “Abençoarei seu sustento abundantemente”
- quoted (Ps 132:15): “satisfy her poor with bread” → “fartarei seus necessitados de pão”
- flag: Quotes 2-3: Henry reports Sl 132:15 indirectly ("that he will abundantly bless her provision"); given as the Bíblia Livre's direct words after a colon so they stay verbatim.
- flag: "fome de pão, mas não de ouvir a palavra" alludes to Am 8:11; "Tendo recebido de graça, de graça deu" to Mt 10:8; both translated.

### 1027 2Kings 5:1-8 Naaman's leprosy.
- flag: The English has "our of the reach", a slip for "out of"; translated accordingly.
- flag: "algum 'mas'" renders Henry's "some but or other", playing on KJV 2 Rs 5:1 "but he was a leper" (Bíblia Livre: "mas leproso"); "allay" read as "alloy" (liga).
- flag: "Deus não está em todos os seus pensamentos" echoes Sl 10:4 (KJV wording; Bíblia Livre: "Deus não existe em todos as seus pensamentos"); translated.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1028 2Kings 5:9-14 The cure of it.
- flag: "Não farias tu qualquer coisa?" paraphrases 2 Rs 5:13 as Henry does (Bíblia Livre: "se o profeta te mandasse alguma grande coisa, não a farias?"); translated.
- flag: "lava-te, e serás limpo" is close to 2 Rs 5:13 in the Bíblia Livre ("Lava-te, e serás limpo"); the series is Henry's own, translated.

### 1029 2Kings 5:15-19 Elisha refuses Naaman's gifts.
- flag: The English has "Elijah refused any recompence", a slip for Elisha (2 Rs 5:16); translated "Eliseu".
- flag: "toda aparência do mal" follows the KJV of 1 Ts 5:22; the Bíblia Livre has "qualquer forma de mal"; translated.

### 1030 2Kings 5:20-27 Gehazi's covetousness and falsehood.
- quoted (Prov 12:19): “a lying tongue is but for a moment” → “a língua da falsidade dura por apenas um momento”
- flag: "raiz de todos os tipos de males" is 1 Tm 6:10 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted; KJV "the root of all evil").
- flag: "Quem encobre o seu pecado não prosperará" echoes Pv 28:13 (Bíblia Livre: "Quem encobre suas transgressões nunca prosperará"); Henry says "his sin", translated.
- flag: "mentir ao Espírito Santo" echoes At 5:3 (Bíblia Livre: "mentisses ao Espírito Santo").

### 1031 2Kings 6:1-7 The sons of the prophets enlarge their habitations, Iron made to swim.
- quoted (2Kings 6:1): “the sons of the prophets” → “Os filhos dos profetas”
- flag: "Assim era este homem quanto ao ferro do machado" renders the elliptical "This man was so respecting the axe-head" (careful of the borrowed axe, 2 Rs 6:5).

### 1033 2Kings 6:13-23 Syrians sent to seize Elisha.
- quoted (2Kings 6:16): “us, are more than they” → “mais são os que estão conosco”
- translated, not quoted (Rom 12:21; Henry turns Rm 12:21 into the first person plural ("Let us not be overcome of evil, but overcome evil"); the Bíblia Livre's "Não sejas vencido pelo mal, mas vence o mal com o bem" is second person singular.): “overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good”
- flag: "lutas por fora, temores por dentro" is 2 Co 7:5 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "o deus destes tempos" is the Bíblia Livre's wording of 2 Co 4:4 (KJV "the god of this world").
- flag: "aquele temor que traz tormento" alludes to 1 Jo 4:18 (KJV "fear hath torment"); translated.

### 1034 2Kings 6:24-33 Samaria besieged, A famine, The king sends to slay Elisha.
- quoted (1Cor 15:58): “always abounding in the work of the Lord” → “firmes, imóveis e sempre abundantes na obra do Senhor”
- quoted (1Cor 15:58): “labour is not in vain in the Lord” → “trabalho não é vão no Senhor”
- flag: "a loucura do homem perverte seu caminho, e então seu coração se ira contra o SENHOR" is Pv 19:3 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted here; Henry repeats it from 2 Rs 3).
- flag: Quote 1: Henry has "our labour" for 1 Co 15:58's "your labour"; "o nosso" before the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 1036 2Kings 7:3-11 The flight of the Syrian army.

- flag: "as inimagináveis riquezas de Cristo" is Ef 3:8 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted; KJV "unsearchable riches").

### 1038 2Kings 8:1-6 A famine in Israel, The Shunammite obtains her land.

### 1039 2Kings 8:7-15 Elisha consulted by Hazael, Death of Benhadad.

### 1040 2Kings 8:16-24 Jehoram's wicked reign in Judah.

### 1044 2Kings 9:16-29 Joram and Ahaziah slain by Jehu.
- quoted (2Kings 9:21): “in the portion of Naboth” → “na propriedade de Nabote”
- flag: "Melhor é aquele que domina seu espírito do que o valente" joins the Bíblia Livre's two clauses of Pv 16:32 ("melhor é aquele que domina seu espírito" and "do que o valente") as Henry joins the KJV's (unlisted).
- flag: "como o rosto corresponde ao rosto num espelho" follows the KJV of Pv 27:19 ("as in water face answereth to face"); the Bíblia Livre words it differently, so translated.
