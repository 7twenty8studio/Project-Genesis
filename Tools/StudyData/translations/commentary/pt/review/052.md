# To review: pt commentary batch 052

### 2388 Isa 25:6-8 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Eph 2:1 ('dead in trespasses and sins'): Bíblia Livre 'mortos em ofensas e pecados' used verbatim. 'God shall wipe away all tears' (Is 25:8; Rev 7:17) translated in Henry's wording (Bíblia Livre Is 25:8: 'enxugará as lágrimas de todos os rostos').

### 2389 Isa 25:9-12 
- quoted (1Cor 15:58): “always abounding in the work of the Lord” → “sempre abundantes na obra do Senhor”
- quoted (1Cor 15:58): “your labour is not in vain in the Lord” → “o vosso trabalho não é vão no Senhor”
- flag: Henry quotes all of 1 Cor 15:58; the Bíblia Livre's 'sede firmes, imóveis' is used for 'be ye stedfast, unmovable' too, and his 'for' rendered 'pois' (Bíblia Livre 'sabendo que').

### 2390 Isa 26:1-4 
- flag: Henry's 'Thou wilt keep him in peace; in perfect peace' follows the KJV of Is 26:3 (Hebrew 'peace, peace'); the Bíblia Livre says 'Tu guardarás em completa paz'; translated so that his 'in peace; in perfect peace' stays.

### 2391 Isa 26:5-11 
- quoted (Heb 10:31): “it is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God” → “cair nas mãos do Deus vivo é algo terrível”
- flag: Henry's 'The way of the just is evenness' paraphrases the KJV of Is 26:7 ('uprightness'); the Bíblia Livre's 'O caminho do justo é todo plano', which matches his 'evenness', used verbatim (unlisted).

### 2393 Isa 26:20-21 
- quoted (Isa 26:20): “as it were for a little moment” → “por um momento”
- flag: The English begins with a stray ',21' (left over from the verse range '20,21'); kept as it stands so the numbers match.
- flag: KJV 'as it were for a little moment' (Is 26:20) is 'por um momento' in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's 'as it were' rendered 'como que'.

### 2394 Isa 27:1-5

### 2395 Isa 27:6-13

- flag: Unlisted quotations: Mark 4:39 ('Peace, be still') Bíblia Livre 'Cala-te, aquieta-te!' and Luke 4:19 'o ano agradável do Senhor' used verbatim. 'wild grapes' rendered 'uvas bravas' as in batch 050 (Bíblia Livre Is 5:2 'uvas imprestáveis').

### 2398 Isa 28:16-22
