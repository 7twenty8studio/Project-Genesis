# To review: pt commentary batch 010

### 271 Ex 24:1-8 Moses is called up into the mountain, The people promise obedience.
- flag: The source has a stray apostrophe ("sprinkling' of blood"); ignored.
- flag: The closing "(Ex 24:9-11)" is Henry's outline reference, kept as given.

### 272 Ex 24:9-11 The glory of the Lord appears.
- flag: "saw the God of Israel" (Ex 24:10): the Bíblia Livre's "viram ao Deus de Israel" is close; Henry's sentence translated plainly ("viram o Deus de Israel"). The Bíblia Livre has "como um pavimento de safira" (singular); Henry's "sapphires" kept plural.

### 273 Ex 24:12-18 Moses goes up into the mountain.
- quoted (Ex 24:15): “A cloud covered the mount” → “uma nuvem cobriu o monte”
- quoted (1Cor 13:12): “Now we see through a glass darkly” → “agora vemos por espelho em enigma”
- flag: Quote 1: the KJV's "through a glass darkly" is "por espelho em enigma" in the Bíblia Livre.

### 274 Ex 25:1-9 What the Israelites were to offer for making the tabernacle.

### 277 Ex 25:31-40 The candlestick.

- flag: Quote 2 is listed as Matt 28:19; "temples of the Holy Ghost" is 1 Cor 6:19, but only "do Espírito Santo" is quoted, which both share.
- flag: Quote 1: Mt 28:20's Bíblia Livre has "ensinando-lhes a guardar", so Henry's imperative "Observe" becomes the infinitive "guardar".

### 278 Ex 26:1-6 The curtains of the tabernacle.
- flag: The closing "(Ex 26:7-14)" is Henry's outline reference, kept as given.

### 280 Ex 26:15-30 The boards, sockets, and bars.

### 281 Ex 26:31-37 The vail of the holy of holies, and for the entrance.
- quoted (Ex 26:33): “between the holy place and the most holy” → “entre o lugar santo e o santíssimo”
- quoted (Heb 9:8): “into the holiest of all” → “para o Santuário”
- quoted (Heb 9:8): “the way into the holiest of all was not” → “o caminho para o Santuário não havia sido revelado”
- quoted (Heb 9:8): “the first tabernacle was” → “o primeiro Tabernáculo ainda estava de pé”

- flag: unlisted quotation of Heb 10:1 ("could not make the comers thereunto perfect"): "tornar perfeitos os que se aproximam" from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "boldness to enter into the holiest ... by the blood of Jesus" (Heb 10:19) follows the Bíblia Livre's words ("entrar no Santuário pelo sangue de Jesus") with Henry's insertion between them.

### 284 Ex 27:20-21 The oil for the lamps.

### 287 Ex 28:15-30 The breastplate, The Urim and Thummim.

### 288 Ex 28:31-39 The robe of the ephod, The plate of the mitre.

- flag: "Holiness to the Lord", in Henry's quotation marks, is the Bíblia Livre's "SANTIDADE AO SENHOR" (Ex 28:36), capitals kept.

### 289 Ex 28:40-43 The garments for Aaron's sons.

- quoted (Heb 4:16): “the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need” → “ao trono da graça, para que possamos receber misericórdia e encontrar graça para sermos socorridos no tempo adequado”

### 290 Ex 29:1-37 The sacrifice and ceremony for the consecration of the priests.

### 293 Ex 30:11-16 The ransom of souls.

### 297 Ex 31:12-17 The observance of the sabbath.

### 299 Ex 32:1-6 The people cause Aaron to make a golden calf.

- flag: "Thou shalt not make to thyself any graven image" rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "Não te farás imagem" (Ex 20:4).
- flag: "by it is the knowledge of sin" follows Rom 3:20 (Bíblia Livre: "o que vem pela Lei é o conhecimento do pecado"); translated in Henry's sentence.

### 300 Ex 32:7-14 God's displeasure, The intercession of Moses.

- flag: Quote 0: Henry's "of his own lust" (James 1:14) is joined from the same verse ("pelo seu próprio mau desejo"), leaving out its "e seduzido", which he doesn't quote.
