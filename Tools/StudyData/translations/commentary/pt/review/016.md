# To review: pt commentary batch 016

### 512 Deut 3:12-20 The land of Gilead and Bashan.
- translated, not quoted (1Chr 5:18; Not a quotation of 1 Cr 5:18: Henry simply names the tribes settled east of the Jordan (Dt 3:12-13).): “Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh”
- flag: "membros uns dos outros" is Rm 12:5 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 513 Deut 3:21-29 Moses encourages Joshua.
- flag: "Se Deus é por nós, quem será contra nós" is Rm 8:31 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: The English has "It God does not", a slip for "If"; translated accordingly.

### 514 Deut 4:1-23 Earnest exhortations to obedience, and dissuasives from idolatry.
- quoted (Deut 4:23): “lest ye forget the covenant of the Lord your God” → “Guardai-vos que não vos esqueçais do pacto do SENHOR vosso Deus”
- flag: Rm 6:23 ("the gift of God, which is eternal life") and Pv 4:23 ("keep our hearts with all diligence") are unlisted and adapted by Henry; translated, the Bíblia Livre's wording differs.

### 515 Deut 4:24-40 Warnings against disobedience, and promises of mercy.
- quoted (Job 28:28): “The fear of the Lord, that is wisdom” → “o temor ao Senhor é a sabedoria”
- quoted (Deut 4:1): “ye may live, and go in and possess the land” → “vivais, e entreis, e possuais a terra”
- quoted (Deut 4:40): “it may go well with thee, and with thy children after thee” → “para que te vá bem a ti e a teus filhos depois de ti”
- quoted (Deut 4:30): “these things are come upon thee” → “te alcançarem todas estas coisas”
- quoted (Deut 4:30): “turn to the Lord thy God” → “ao SENHOR teu Deus”

### 516 Deut 4:41-49 Cities of refuge appointed.
- translated, not quoted (Deut 4:46, Deut 4:47; Not a quotation: Henry's own sentence about the laws given after the exodus; the match with Dt 4:46-47 is accidental.): “out of Egypt; and they”
- flag: The title ("Cities of refuge appointed") covers Dt 4:41-43, which the text does not discuss; kept as in the source.

### 518 Deut 5:6-22 The ten commandments repeated.
- quoted (Rom 8:21): “into the glorious liberty of the children of God” → “liberdade da glória dos filhos de Deus”
- flag: Rm 8:21: the Bíblia Livre says "liberdade da glória" (KJV "glorious liberty").
- flag: "com mão forte e braço estendido" is Dt 5:15 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 519 Deut 5:23-33 The request of the people that the law might be delivered through Moses.
- flag: "Say to the righteous, It shall be well with them" (Is 3:10, unlisted) translated: the Bíblia Livre reads "Dizei a justo, que o bem lhe sucederá".

### 520 Deut 6:1-3 A persuasive to obedience.
- flag: Henry explains the English words of Dt 6:1 ("commandments, statutes, judgments"; the source misprints "statues"); the Bíblia Livre has "mandamentos, estatutos, e regulamentos", so "regulamentos" is used and his link between "judgments" and the judges is less visible.

### 521 Deut 6:4-5 An exhortation to obedience.
- quoted (Matt 22:38): “This is the first and great commandment” → “este é o primeiro e grande mandamento”
- quoted (Mark 12:33): “To love him with all the heart, and with all the understanding” → “amá-lo com todo o coração, com todo o entendimento”
- flag: Henry's "Jehovah" in his own sentence rendered "Jeová".
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "Meu filho, dá para mim teu coração" (Pv 23:26) and "derramado em nossos corações" (Rm 5:5); Henry's "the plural number of the word translated God" refers to the Hebrew Elohim.

### 522 Deut 6:6-16 Obedience taught.
- quoted (Deut 6:12): “lest thou forget the Lord” → “que não te esqueças do SENHOR”
- quoted (Matt 4:7): “Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God” → “Não tentarás o Senhor teu Deus”

### 524 Deut 7:1-11 Intercourse with the Canaanites forbidden.
- flag: Unlisted, following the Bíblia Livre's words: "obras infrutíferas das trevas" (Ef 5:11) and "que batalham contra a alma" (1 Pe 2:11).

### 525 Deut 7:12-26 Promises if they were obedient.
- flag: "which is our rule of duty" follows the English, whose antecedent is unclear; kept literally.
- flag: "pouco a pouco" and "os animais do campo" echo Dt 7:22 in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 526 Deut 8:1-9 Exhortations and cautions, enforced by the Lord's former dealings with Israel, and his promises.
- flag: The English repeats a garbled fragment ("to Canaan. Look which way we will, both to look back and to look forward"); kept as in the source.
- flag: "observe to do" is Dt 8:1 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("cuidareis de pôr por obra"); "fartura de alegrias" follows Sl 16:11 (unlisted); "verily they shall be fed" (Sl 37:3, KJV) translated.

### 527 Deut 8:10-20 Exhortations and cautions further enforced.
- translated, not quoted (Rom 15:19; Not a quotation of Rm 15:19: "the power of the Spirit of God" is Henry's own phrase (and the Bíblia Livre's verse has a typo, "Espíritode").): “the power of the Spirit of God”
- flag: "to do them good" in Henry's quotation marks is Dt 8:16 with the person changed (Bíblia Livre "para ao fim fazer-te bem"); translated.

### 528 Deut 9:1-6 The Israelites not to think their success came by their own worthiness.
- quoted (Deut 9:4): “the wickedness of these nations” → “pela impiedade destas nações”

### 530 Deut 10:1-11 God's mercies to Israel after their rebellion.
- flag: "sabbaths" in a Christian list (Bibles, sabbaths, sacraments) means Lord's days; kept as "sábados" without the bracket note, since Henry doesn't say "the Christian sabbath" here; a reviewer may want the note.

### 531 Deut 10:12-22 An exhortation to obedience.
- quoted (Rom 8:7, Rom 8:8): “the carnal mind is enmity against God, for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be; so then they that are in the flesh cannot please God” → “a mentalidade da carne é inimizade contra Deus, pois não se sujeita à Lei de Deus, porque nem sequer pode. Portanto, os que permanecem na carne não podem agradar a Deus”
- flag: "Circumcise your hearts" (Dt 10:16, unlisted) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "Circuncidai pois o prepúcio de vosso coração".
- flag: "de glória em glória, segundo a mesma imagem" follows 2 Co 3:18 in the Bíblia Livre (unlisted).

### 532 Deut 11:1-7 The great work God wrought for Israel.
- quoted (Deut 11:1): “Thou shalt love the Lord” → “Amarás … ao SENHOR”
- flag: Dt 11:1: Henry shortens the verse; the Bíblia Livre's "Amarás, pois, ao SENHOR teu Deus, e guardarás sua ordenança" is given without "pois" and "teu Deus".

### 533 Deut 11:8-17 Promises and threatenings.
- translated, not quoted (2Chr 32:28; Not a quotation of 2 Cr 32:28: Henry echoes Sl 4:7 in his own words ("more than the increase of corn, and wine, and oil").): “the increase of corn, and wine, and oil”
- flag: "Guardai-vos, pois, que vosso coração não se engane" is Dt 11:16 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 535 Deut 11:26-32 The blessings and the curse set forth.
- quoted (Deut 11:26, Deut 11:27): “a blessing and a curse. A blessing, if” → “a bênção e a maldição: A bênção, se”
- flag: "se descuidarmos de tão grande salvação" follows Hb 2:3 in the Bíblia Livre (unlisted); "Gerizim" spelled as usual in Portuguese Bibles.

### 536 Deut 12:1-4 Monuments of idolatry to be destroyed.
- flag: "serve God and mammon" (Mt 6:24): the Bíblia Livre has "riquezas" for mammon.

### 537 Deut 12:5-32 The place of God's service to be kept.
- quoted (Col 3:17): “do all in the name of the Lord Jesus” → “tudo no nome do Senhor Jesus”
- flag: "profundezas de Satanás" follows Ap 2:24 in the Bíblia Livre (unlisted); 1 Co 10:31 changed in person by Henry, translated.

### 538 Deut 13:1-5 Enticers to idolatry to be put to death.
- flag: The English has "wrought upon the forsake", a slip for "to forsake"; translated accordingly.

### 539 Deut 13:6-11 Relations who entice to idolatry not to be spared.
- quoted (Prov 1:10): “If sinners entice thee, consent thou not” → “se os pecadores tentarem te convencer, não te deixes influenciar”
- flag: "Não consentirás com ele" is Dt 13:8 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted); the rest of Henry's sentence is his own.

### 541 Deut 14:1-21 The Israelites to distinguish themselves from other nations.
- quoted (Deut 14:2): “The Lord hath chosen thee” → “o SENHOR te escolheu”
- quoted (Deut 14:1): “Ye are the children of the Lord your God” → “Filhos sois do SENHOR vosso Deus”
- translated, not quoted (Deut 14:1; Henry turns Dt 14:1 into a question about ourselves ("Are we of the children of the Lord our God?"); the verse's words can't stand there.): “the children of the Lord”
- flag: "És povo santo" (Dt 14:2), in Henry's quotation marks, is unlisted; given in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "zeloso de boas obras" (Tt 2:14) and "Não te faças nenhum mal" (At 16:28, which Henry applies to the reader).
- flag: The English has "the grace God", a slip for "the grace of God".

### 543 Deut 15:1-11 The year of release.
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "o ano agradável do Senhor" (Lc 4:19) and "Deus ama ao que dá com alegria" (2 Co 9:7).
