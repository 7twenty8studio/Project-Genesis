# To review: pt commentary batch 080

### 3417 John 20:19-25 He appears to the disciples.
- quoted (John 20:19): “the first day of the week” → “o primeiro dia da semana”
- quoted (John 20:22): “Receive ye the Holy Ghost” → “Recebei o Espírito Santo”
- flag: "a false professor": "um falso professo" (one who professes religion falsely), as Henry's term for a hypocrite.

### 3418 John 20:26-29 The unbelief of Thomas.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jo 20:28 ("My Lord and my God", twice): the Bíblia Livre's words "Senhor meu, e Deus meu" used.
- flag: "Christless and graceless": "sem Cristo e sem graça" (as "destituído da graça" elsewhere; here kept in Henry's paired rhythm).

### 3419 John 20:30-31 Conclusion.
- quoted (John 20:31): “believe that Jesus is the Christ” → “Jesus é o Cristo”
- quoted (John 20:31): “have life through his name” → “vida em seu nome”

### 3420 John 21:1-14 Christ appears to his disciples.
- quoted (John 21:6): “the net on the right side of the ship” → “a rede do lado direito do barco”
- flag: "It is the Lord" (Jo 21:7) is not listed; translated ("É o Senhor").
- flag: "food convenient" echoes Pv 30:8 (KJV "food convenient for me"); not listed, translated ("o alimento conveniente").

### 3421 John 21:15-19 His discourse with Peter.
- quoted (John 21:15): “Thou knowest that I love thee” → “tu sabes que te amo”
- flag: Source: "earnest, preserving prayer" read as "persevering" ("perseverante").

### 3422 John 21:20-24 Christ's declaration concerning John.
- quoted (1Cor 2:13): “which the Holy Ghost” → “que o Espírito Santo ensina”
- flag: "What is this to us?" adapts Jo 21:22 (KJV "what is that to thee?"); translated.

### 3423 John 21:25-25 The conclusion.
- quoted (John 20:31): “These are written that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name” → “estes estão escritos, para que creiais que Jesus é o Cristo, o Filho de Deus; e para que crendo, tenhais vida em seu nome”
- flag: Henry's "These are written that ye might believe…" quotes v. 31 of the previous chapter; the Bíblia Livre's verse begins "Porém", kept so the run is whole.

### 3424 Acts 1:1-5 Proofs of Christ's resurrection.
- flag: "in Him all the promises of God are yea and amen" (2 Co 1:20) is an unlisted allusion; translated.

### 3426 Acts 1:12-14 The apostles unite in prayer.
- flag: "if any is afflicted, let him pray" (Tg 5:13) is an unlisted allusion; translated.

### 3427 Acts 1:15-26 Matthias chosen in the place of Judas.
- quoted (Acts 1:24): “knowest the hearts of all men” → “Tu, Senhor, conhecedor dos corações de todos”
- flag: Source typos: "wordly" read as "worldly"; "shows whom he was chosen" read as "whom he has chosen".
- flag: Henry's quotation is "who knowest the hearts"; the Bíblia Livre's "conhecedor dos corações" is a noun, so his "which we do not" becomes "o que nós não somos".

### 3428 Acts 2:1-4 The descent of the Holy Spirit at the day of Pentecost.
- quoted (Matt 3:11): “He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire” → “Ele vos batizará com o Espírito Santo e com fogo”
- quoted (Acts 2:4): “They were all filled with the Holy Ghost” → “Eles foram todos cheios do Espírito Santo”
- quoted (Acts 2:4): “as the Spirit gave them utterance” → “conforme o Espírito lhes dava a discursarem”
- flag: Source typo: "from previous though" read as "thought".
- flag: "where brethren dwell together in unity, there the Lord commands his blessing" (Sl 133:1, 3) is an unlisted allusion; translated.
- flag: Quote 2 keeps the Bíblia Livre's own wording "lhes dava a discursarem" (Henry's KJV "gave them utterance").

### 3430 Acts 2:14-21 Peter's address to the Jews.
- translated, not quoted (Gen 4:26; Listed as Gn 4:26, but Henry follows Peter's quotation of Joel (At 2:21, Jl 2:32); the Bíblia Livre's At 2:21 has "chamar ao nome do Senhor", so Henry's own sentence is translated.): “call upon the name of the Lord”
- flag: "spake with tongues as the Spirit gave him utterance" adapts At 2:4 to Peter alone; translated, not quoted.

### 3432 Acts 2:37-41 Three thousand souls converted.
- quoted (Acts 2:38): “the gift of the Holy Ghost” → “o dom do Espírito Santo”
- quoted (Acts 2:39): “to all that are afar off” → “todos que ainda estão longe”
- flag: Henry's closing "still … to all that are afar off" sits beside the Bíblia Livre's own "ainda" ("todos que ainda estão longe"), so "ainda" appears twice in the last clause.

### 3433 Acts 2:42-47 The piety and affection of the disciples.
- flag: "till the earth is filled with the knowledge of his glory" alludes to Hc 2:14; not listed, translated.

### 3434 Acts 3:1-11 A lame man healed by Peter and John.
- quoted (Acts 3:6): “in the name of Jesus” → “no nome de Jesus”
- quoted (Acts 3:6): “the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth” → “o nome de Jesus Cristo, o nazareno”
- flag: Quote 0: Henry's "in the name of Jesus of Nazareth"; the Bíblia Livre's run is "no nome de Jesus", followed by Henry's own "de Nazaré".

### 3435 Acts 3:12-18 Peter's address to the Jews.
- quoted (Ps 115:1): “Not unto us, O Lord, not unto us” → “Não a nós, SENHOR; não a nós, mas a teu nome dá glória”

### 3436 Acts 3:19-21 
- quoted (Acts 3:19): “sins may be blotted out” → “pecados sejam apagados”
- quoted (Acts 3:19): “come from the presence of the Lord” → “os tempos do refrigério da presença do Senhor”
- flag: Quote 1: Henry's "times of refreshment will come from the presence of the Lord" is rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "os tempos do refrigério da presença do Senhor" after "virão".

### 3437 Acts 3:22-26 
- flag: "wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, and redemption" (1 Co 1:30) is an unlisted allusion; translated.

### 3438 Acts 4:1-4 Peter and John imprisoned.
- quoted (Acts 4:2): “preached through Jesus the resurrection from the dead” → “no nome de Jesus a ressurreição dos mortos”
- translated, not quoted (1Thess 1:3; Henry applies 1 Ts 1:3 to the apostles in the third person; the Bíblia Livre's run is "da vossa fé", which can't stand in his sentence.): “work of faith and labour of love”
- flag: Henry's "preached through Jesus" is the KJV's; the Bíblia Livre has "anunciarem no nome de Jesus", used in the quotation; his next clause "preached through Jesus Christ, to be had through him only" keeps "por meio de".

### 3439 Acts 4:5-14 The apostles boldly testify to Christ.
- quoted (Acts 4:8): “filled with the Holy Ghost” → “cheio do Espírito Santo”

### 3441 Acts 4:23-31 The believers unite in prayer and praise.
- quoted (Ps 71:16): “in the strength of the Lord God” → “pelos poderes do Senhor DEUS”
- quoted (Acts 4:31): “they were all filled with the Holy Ghost” → “foram todos cheios do Espírito Santo”
- quoted (Acts 4:31): “the word of God with boldness” → “a palavra de Deus com ousadia”
- flag: Source reference "Is 17" (no verse) kept as written; Henry's words "the Lord God will help me … I shall not be confounded" are Is 50:7, so "17" looks like a slip.
- flag: Quote 0 (Sl 71:16): the Bíblia Livre's "pelos poderes do Senhor DEUS" for Henry's "in the strength of the Lord God".

### 3442 Acts 4:32-37 The holy charity of the Christians.
- quoted (Isa 32:15): “be poured upon us from on high” → “derramado sobre nós o Espírito do alto”
- quoted (Acts 4:32): “they had all things common” → “todas as coisas lhes eram comuns”
- flag: "They did not take away others' property, but they were indifferent to it": "it" read as their own property ("à sua"), as the next sentence ("They did not call it their own") shows.

### 3443 Acts 5:1-11 The death of Ananias and Sapphira.
- quoted (Acts 5:3): “part of the price of the land” → “parte do valor da propriedade”
- flag: Henry's "the price of the land" (KJV) is "o valor da propriedade" in the Bíblia Livre, used as quoted.

### 3445 Acts 5:17-25 The apostles imprisoned, but set free by an angel.
- quoted (Acts 5:20): “all the words of this” → “todas as palavras desta vida”

### 3447 Acts 5:34-42 The advice of Gamaliel, The council let the apostles go.
- flag: Title: Henry's two titles joined with a comma in the source ("…of Gamaliel, The council…"); rendered as two sentences.

### 3448 Acts 6:1-7 The appointment of deacons.
- translated, not quoted (2Kings 2:24; Listed as 2 Rs 2:24, where Elisha curses the boys "no nome do SENHOR"; Henry's blessing is his own phrase, not a quotation of that verse.): “them in the name of the Lord”
- flag: "men of truth, hating covetousness" echoes Êx 18:21; not listed, translated.

### 3449 Acts 6:8-15 Stephen falsely accused of blasphemy.
- quoted (Jer 17:9): “is deceitful above all things and desperately wicked” → “mais enganoso que todas as coisas, e perverso”
- flag: Quote 0 (Jr 17:9): the Bíblia Livre has "perverso" where the KJV has "desperately wicked"; its words are used as they stand, so Henry's "desperately" has no separate word.
