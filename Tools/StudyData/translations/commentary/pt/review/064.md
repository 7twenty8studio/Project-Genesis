# To review: pt commentary batch 064

### 2896 Micah 6:9-16 The wickedness of Israel.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mq 6:9: "Ouvi a vara" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 2900 Nah 1:1-8 The justice and power of the Lord.
- quoted (2Cor 11:31): “The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “O Deus e Pai de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- flag: "will by no means acquit the wicked" (Na 1:3) and "tribulation and anguish for every soul that doeth evil" (Rm 2:9) follow the Bíblia Livre's "não terá ao culpado por inocente" and "aflição e angústia a toda pessoa que pratica o mal", fitted to Henry's sentence.

### 2901 Nah 1:9-15 The overthrow of the Assyrians.
- translated, not quoted (Nah 1:8; Henry applies the KJV's "he will make an utter end" to "all his enemies"; the Bíblia Livre ("ele acabará com Nínive") names Nineveh and cannot stand in his sentence.): “he will make an utter end”

### 2905 Nah 3:8-19 Its utter destruction.
- flag: "canker-worms" (KJV, Na 3:15-16) is "o inseto devorador" in the Bíblia Livre, used here; "the word rendered 'merchants'" is Na 3:16, where the Bíblia Livre also has "mercadores".
- flag: "he knoweth those that trust in Him" uses the Bíblia Livre's Na 1:7 "ele conhece os que nele confiam" (unlisted quotation).

### 2906 Hab 1:1-11 The wickedness of the land. The fearful vengeance to be executed.
- flag: "a bitter and hasty nation" uses the Bíblia Livre's Hc 1:6 "uma nação amarga e veloz" (unlisted quotation).

### 2907 Hab 1:12-17 These judgments to be inflicted by a nation more wicked than themselves.
- translated, not quoted (Hab 1:13; Henry turns the verse into the third person ("he is of purer eyes than to behold it"); the Bíblia Livre's "Teus olhos são puros demais para veres o mal" is addressed to God.): “of purer eyes than to behold”
- quoted (Eccl 8:11): “fully set in them to do evil” → “cheio de vontade neles, para fazer o mal”
- translated, not quoted (Hab 1:15; Henry makes the verse plural ("they ... their net ... their drag"); the Bíblia Livre's singular "em sua malha os ajunta, e os colhe em seu rede" (with its slip "seu rede") doesn't fit his sentence.): “in their net, and gather them in their drag”

### 2908 Hab 2:1-4 Habakkuk must wait in faith.
- translated, not quoted (Hab 2:1; Henry turns the prophet's "what he will say unto me" into "unto them"; the Bíblia Livre's "o que ele me falará" cannot stand in his sentence.): “what he will say unto”
- flag: "The just shall live by faith" (Hc 2:4): the Bíblia Livre has "o justo viverá por sua fé"; Henry's phrase translated "pela fé" to carry "in these precious promises".

### 2909 Hab 2:5-14 Judgments upon the Chaldeans.
- flag: Henry's "thick clay" follows the KJV of Hc 2:6 ("ladeth himself with thick clay"); the Bíblia Livre reads "daquele de se enriquece por meio de extorsão", so his image is translated as he wrote it.
- flag: "he that is greedy of gain troubles his own house" uses the Bíblia Livre's Pv 15:27 ("quem pratica a ganância perturba sua própria casa"); "Thou hast sinned against thine own soul" (Hc 2:10) is "pecaste contra tua vida" in the Bíblia Livre, kept as "alma" for Henry's point; "como as águas cobrem o mar" is Hc 2:14 in its words.
- flag: the English has "wordly", a typo for "worldly"; translated "mundanas".

### 2910 Hab 2:15-20 Also upon drunkenness and idolatry.
- quoted (Hab 2:20): “The Lord is in his holy temple” → “o SENHOR está em seu santo templo”

### 2911 Hab 3:1-2 The prophet beseeches God for his people.
- quoted (Hab 3:2): “in the midst of the years” → “no meio dos anos”
- flag: "The word prayer" refers to the heading of Hc 3:1, which is "Oração" in the Bíblia Livre too.

### 2912 Hab 3:3-15 He calls to mind former deliverances.
- flag: "for salvation with thine anointed" follows the KJV of Hc 3:13; the Bíblia Livre has "para salvar o teu ungido", which would change Henry's point, so his words are translated.
- flag: "Him whose name he bare, even Jesus, our Joshua": Henry relies on Jesus being the Greek form of Joshua (Josué).

### 2913 Hab 3:16-19 His firm trust in the Divine mercy.
- quoted (Heb 10:37): “Yet a little while, and He that shall come will come, and will not tarry” → “Ainda um pouquinho de tempo, e aquele que vem virá, e não tardará”

### 2914 Zeph 1:1-6 Threatenings against sinners.
- quoted (Heb 10:39): “believe to the saving of the soul” → “creem para a conservação da alma”
- flag: "Malcham" is "Malcã" as the glossary gives it; the Bíblia Livre's Sf 1:5 has "Milcom".
- flag: "There is no peace for the wicked" alludes to Is 57:21 (Bíblia Livre "Os perversos ... não tem paz"); translated.

### 2915 Zeph 1:7-13 More threatenings.
- quoted (Zeph 1:9): “those that leap on the threshold” → “os que saltam sobre o umbral”
- quoted (Zeph 1:12): “say in their heart, the Lord will not do good, neither will he do evil” → “dizem em seu coração: O SENHOR nem fará bem nem mal”

### 2916 Zeph 1:14-18 Distress from the approaching judgments.
- quoted (Zeph 1:14): “the great day of the Lord” → “grande dia do SENHOR”
- quoted (Zeph 1:15): “a day of trouble and distress” → “dia de angústia e de aflição”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “What is a man profited if he” → “que proveito há para alguém, se”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “gain the whole world, and lose his own soul” → “ganhar o mundo todo, mas perder a sua alma”
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “what shall a man give in exchange for his soul” → “que dará alguém em resgate da sua alma”
- flag: unlisted quotations in the Bíblia Livre's words: "ira futura" (Mt 3:7) and "nos separar do amor de Deus, que está em Cristo Jesus, nosso Senhor" (Rm 8:39).

### 2917 Zeph 2:1-3 An exhortation to repentance.
- flag: Henry's two readings ("not desiring" / "not desirable") explain the KJV of Sf 2:1 ("O nation not desired"); the Bíblia Livre has "ó nação desagradável".

### 2918 Zeph 2:4-15 Judgments upon other nations.
- quoted (Zeph 2:5): “the word of the Lord” → “palavra do SENHOR”

### 2920 Zeph 3:8-13 Encouragement to look for mercy.
- flag: sensitive: Henry links the preaching of the gospel to the time "when vengeance would be executed on the Jewish nation".
- flag: "the pure language" follows the KJV of Sf 3:9; the Bíblia Livre has "lábio puro".

### 2921 Zeph 3:14-20 Promises of future favour and prosperity.
- translated, not quoted (Zeph 3:17; Henry's "He will save" (KJV Sf 3:17) leads into his play on the name Jesus; the Bíblia Livre's "guerreiro que salva" cannot carry it.): “He will save; he will”
- quoted (Zeph 3:20): “a name and a praise” → “renome e louvor”

### intro Hag 
- flag: "the Desire of all nations" is the KJV's messianic reading of Ag 2:7; the Bíblia Livre has "as coisas preciosas de todas as nações".

### 2922 Hag 1:1-11 Haggai reproves the Jews for neglecting the temple.
- flag: "Not yet" reflects the KJV of Ag 1:2 ("The time is not come"); the Bíblia Livre has "O tempo não chegou"; Henry's "Not yet" translated "Ainda não".

### 2924 Hag 2:1-9 Greater glory promised to the second temple than to the first.
- translated, not quoted (Hag 2:7; Henry's messianic title follows the KJV of Ag 2:7; the Bíblia Livre's "as coisas preciosas de todas as nações" cannot carry it.): “the Desire of all nations”
- quoted (Hag 2:9): “the glory of this latter house shall be greater than” → “glória desta última casa será maior que”
- flag: sensitive: "This prophecy alone is enough to silence the Jews, and condemn their obstinate rejection of Him".
- flag: the English repeats "But ... but" in "But the Jews under the latter temple had much trouble; but this promise"; both kept ("Mas ... mas").

### 2926 Hag 2:20-23 The kingdom of Christ foretold.
- flag: "the Signet" (Ag 2:23, KJV) is "anel de selar" in the Bíblia Livre, used here as Henry's title.
