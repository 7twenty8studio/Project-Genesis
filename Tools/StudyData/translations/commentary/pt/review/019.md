# To review: pt commentary batch 019

### 636 Josh 8:1-2 God encourages Joshua.
- flag: "accursed thing" given as "anátema", the Bíblia Livre's word in Js 7 (as in batch 018).

### 639 Josh 8:30-35 The law read on Ebal and Gerizim.
- translated, not quoted (Josh 8:33; Henry's own phrase for the covenant, not a quotation of Js 8:33 (the ark of the covenant); translated "a aliança do Senhor".): “the covenant of the Lord”

### 640 Josh 9:1-2 The kings combine against Israel.
- flag: Source oddity: the reference "(Jos 9:3-13)" stands at the end of the text, apparently the next section's outline heading; kept as given.

### 641 Josh 9:3-13 The Gibeonites apply for peace.
- quoted (2Cor 2:16): “savour of life unto life” → “cheiro de vida, para a vida”
- quoted (2Cor 2:16): “savour of death unto death” → “cheiro de morte, para a morte”
- quoted (Josh 9:6): “come from a far country” → “viemos da terra muito distante”
- flag: The English is garbled twice: "We must not do evil that good may themselves to the God of Israel" (words lost after "good may [come]…", probably something like "…had they submitted themselves to the God of Israel") and "they were led to say it made of skins, and their clothes" (words lost, probably about their bread and wine bottles); translated as it stands, with "[…]" marking each gap. Nothing supplied.
- flag: "para que venha o bem" follows the Bíblia Livre's Rm 3:8 (unlisted).

### 642 Josh 9:14-21 They obtain peace, but are soon detected.
- quoted (Prov 12:19): “A lying tongue is but for a moment” → “a língua da falsidade dura por apenas um momento”
- flag: "mantém o seu juramento até sob o seu próprio prejuízo, e não muda" follows the Bíblia Livre's Sl 15:4 (unlisted).
- flag: "We make more haste than good speed" (an English proverb) given as "Apressamo-nos mais do que convém".

### 643 Josh 9:22-27 The Gibeonites are to be bondmen.
- quoted (Josh 9:25): “seemeth good and right unto thee” → “o que te parecer bom e correto fazer de nós, faze-o”
- quoted (Ps 27:4): “in the house of the Lord all the days” → “na casa do SENHOR todos os dias”
- flag: "ao que vem a ele, em maneira nenhuma o lançará fora" follows the Bíblia Livre's Jo 6:37 (unlisted), put in the third person as Henry has it.

### 644 Josh 10:1-6 Five kings war against Gibeon.
- flag: Source: "that they make peace with God" read as "to make peace with God".

### 645 Josh 10:7-14 Joshua succours Gibeon, The sun and moon stand still.
- quoted (Gen 18:14): “Is any thing too hard for the Lord” → “Há para Deus alguma coisa difícil?”
- flag: Quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's Gn 18:14 has "Deus" where the KJV has "the LORD".
- flag: "Observai os feitos do SENHOR" follows the Bíblia Livre's Sl 46:8 (unlisted); the rest of the proclamation echoes Dt 4:7 and is translated.
- flag: "Ajalon" given as "Aijalom", as in the Bíblia Livre's Js 10:12.

### 646 Josh 10:15-27 The kings are taken, their armies defeated, and they are put to death.
- quoted (Josh 10:21): “None moved his tongue against any of the children of Israel” → “não houve quem movesse sua língua contra os filhos de Israel”
- flag: "Refuges of lies will but secure for God's judgment" is elliptical in the English (the kings' cave kept them for judgment); translated with "guardá-los".

### 647 Josh 10:28-43 Seven other kings defeated and slain.
- flag: "Se Deus é por nós, quem será contra nós?" follows the Bíblia Livre's Rm 8:31 (unlisted); "o Senhor lutava por Israel" echoes its Js 10:14.

### 648 Josh 11:1-9 Divers kings overcome at the waters of Merom.
- flag: Source oddity: "(Jos 11:10-14)" stands at the end of the text, apparently the next section's outline heading; kept as given.

### 650 Josh 11:15-23 All that country subdued, The Anakims cut off.
- quoted (Josh 11:23): “The land rested from war” → “a terra repousou de guerra”
- flag: "Anakims" given as "anaquins", as in the Bíblia Livre's Js 11:21; "Anak" as "Anaque" (glossary).
- flag: "vive sempre para interceder" and "a fé deles não se acabará" echo the Bíblia Livre's Hb 7:25 and Lc 22:32.

### 652 Josh 12:7-24 The kings whom Joshua smote.
- flag: Henry's description of the land "in our day" (early 1700s) translated as given.

### 653 Josh 13:1-6 Bounds of the land not yet conquered.
- quoted (Josh 13:1): “old and stricken in years” → “velho, de idade avançada”
- flag: The English has "God promise" and "through Joshua was old", read as "God promises" and "though Joshua was old".
- flag: Quote 0: Henry's plural "those who are old" put in the singular ("quem já é velho") to keep the Bíblia Livre's Js 13:1 words.

### 654 Josh 13:7-33 Inheritance of Reuben.
- quoted (Josh 13:33): “the tribe of Levi Moses gave” → “à tribo de Levi não deu Moisés herança”
- quoted (Josh 13:14): “the Lord God of Israel” → “SENHOR Deus de Israel”

### 655 Josh 14:1-5 The nine tribes and a half to have their inheritance.
- flag: "eterna, nos céus" and "acepção de pessoas" follow the Bíblia Livre's 2 Co 5:1 and At 10:34 (unlisted); "o grande mistério da piedade" translates the KJV's 1 Tm 3:16 (Bíblia Livre: "o mistério da devoção divina").

### 656 Josh 14:6-15 Caleb obtains Hebron.
- quoted (Josh 14:14): “he wholly followed the Lord God of Israel” → “porque cumpriu seguindo ao SENHOR Deus de Israel”
- flag: Henry's quotation "Give me this mountain" (Js 14:12) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "Dá-me, pois, agora este monte".
- flag: The English has "let Israel knows", read as "know".
- flag: Henry's meaning of "Caleb" ("all heart") translated as given.

### 658 Josh 15:13-19 Caleb's portion, His daughter's blessing.
- flag: "a south land" (KJV Js 15:19) is "terra de secura" in the Bíblia Livre; "the upper and the nether springs" is "as fontes de acima, e as de abaixo" there; Henry's terms translated.

### 659 Josh 15:20-63 The cities of Judah.
- quoted (Micah 5:2): “little among the thousands of Judah” → “pequena entre as famílias de Judá”
- flag: The KJV's "thousands of Judah" (Mq 5:2) is "famílias de Judá" in the Bíblia Livre.

### 661 Josh 17:1-6 The lot of Manasseh.
- quoted (Col 1:12): “the inheritance of the saints in light” → “herança dos santos na luz”
- translated, not quoted (Josh 17:4; Henry turns the daughters' words in Js 17:4 into a prayer ("give us"); the Bíblia Livre's "nos desse herança entre" can't stand in the imperative.): “give us an inheritance among”

### 663 Josh 17:14-18 Joseph desires a larger portion.
- flag: "não comerão os que não quiserem trabalhar" translates Henry's restatement of 2 Ts 3:10; "os nossos limites serão ampliados" echoes the Bíblia Livre's 1 Cr 4:10 ("ampliares os meus limites").
- flag: The English has "if we belong" with a lower-case "if" after a full stop; capitalised.

### 664 Josh 18:1-1 The tabernacle set up at Shiloh.
- flag: The Bíblia Livre's Gn 49:10 has "Até que venha Siló", so Henry's point holds.

### 665 Josh 18:2-10 The remainder of the land described and divided.
- flag: "stand in our own light" (English idiom: to hinder ourselves) given by its sense, "seremos um estorvo para nós mesmos" (reviewer); "vaidades enganosas" as in batch 018 (KJV Jn 2:8).

### 666 Josh 18:11-28 The boundaries of Benjamin.
- quoted (1Sam 3:18): “It is the Lord; let him do what seemeth him good” → “o SENHOR é; faça o que bem lhe parecer”

### 667 Josh 19:1-9 The lot of Simeon.
- flag: "O amor não busca os próprios interesses" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Co 13:5 (unlisted); "não se porta de modo inconveniente" translates the KJV's "doth not behave itself unseemly" (1 Co 13:5 in the KJV's verse; the Bíblia Livre's wording differs).

### 669 Josh 19:17-51 The lot of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Dn
- flag: The English title ends "and Dn", read as "and Dan".
- flag: "não tendo onde recostar a cabeça" and "Cristo não agradou a si mesmo" follow the Bíblia Livre's Mt 8:20 and Rm 15:3 (unlisted).

### 671 Josh 20:7-9 The cities appointed as refuges.
- quoted (Josh 20:8): “on the other side Jordan” → “da outra parte do Jordão”
- translated, not quoted (Isa 38:22; A common phrase in Henry's own sentence; the listed Is 38:22 is Hezekiah's first person ("subirei à casa do SENHOR").): “go up to the house of the Lord”
- quoted (Ps 18:30): “to all those that trust in him” → “para todos os que nele confiam”
- flag: "o governo está sobre seus ombros", "a este Deus exaltou com sua mão direita" and "serão justificados e se gloriarão" follow the Bíblia Livre's Is 9:6, At 5:31 and Is 45:25 (unlisted); "comunhão" echoes its 1 Co 1:9.
- flag: The name meanings are Henry's ("some observe"), translated as given; names as the app writes them (Quedes, Siquém, Hebrom, Bezer, Ramote, Golã).
- flag: Henry's "shoulder" (singular, KJV Is 9:6) is "ombros" in the Bíblia Livre.

### 672 Josh 21:1-8 Cities for the Levites.
- quoted (1Cor 9:14): “they which preach the gospel should live” → “aos que anunciam o Evangelho, que vivam do Evangelho”
- flag: "foi ordenado" carries the Bíblia Livre's 1 Co 9:14 construction ("ordenou o Senhor, aos que anunciam…") for Henry's "should live".
- flag: Source oddity: "(Jos 21:9-42)" at the end, apparently the next section's outline heading; kept.

### 673 Josh 21:9-42 The cities allotted to the Levites.
- flag: Source oddity: "(Jos 21:43-45)" at the end, apparently the next section's outline heading; kept.

### 674 Josh 21:43-45 God gave the land and rest to the Israelites, according to his promise.
- quoted (Josh 21:43, Josh 21:44): “they possessed it, and dwelt therein” → “possuíram-na, e habitaram nela”
- quoted (Josh 21:44): “There stood not a man” → “nenhum de todos os inimigos lhes parou diante”
- quoted (Josh 21:45): “of any good thing, which the Lord had spoken” → “de todas as boas coisas que o SENHOR falou”
- flag: Quote 1: the KJV's "There stood not a man" is "nenhum de todos os inimigos lhes parou diante" in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "aquele que não pode mentir" echoes Tt 1:2 (Bíblia Livre: "Deus, que não pode mentir").

### 675 Josh 22:1-9 Reuben and Gad, with the half tribe of Manasseh, dismissed to their homes.
- flag: Henry follows Js 22:5 closely; "de todo o nosso coração e de toda a nossa alma" adapts the Bíblia Livre's "de todo o vosso coração e de toda a vossa alma" to his "our".

### 676 Josh 22:10-20 They build an altar of testimony, The congregation offended thereat.
- quoted (Lev 19:17): “suffer sin upon him” → “sobre ele pecado”
- flag: Quote 0: "não consintamos sobre ele pecado" adapts the Bíblia Livre's "não consentirás sobre ele pecado" (Lv 19:17) to Henry's "lest we"; only "sobre ele pecado" is verbatim.

### 677 Josh 22:21-29 The answer of the Reubenites.
- quoted (Heb 4:12): “the thoughts and intents of the heart” → “os pensamentos e intenções do coração”
- flag: Henry's "God knows" (Js 22:22) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "ele sabe".

### 678 Josh 22:30-34 The children of Israel satisfied.
- quoted (Eph 4:3): “to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace” → “guardar a unidade do Espírito pelo vínculo da paz”
- flag: "ED" kept as Henry writes it (KJV margin, Js 22:34); the Bíblia Livre names the altar "Ede".
- flag: "candour" given as "franqueza".

### 680 Josh 23:11-16 Joshua warns the people of idolatry.
- quoted (Josh 23:11): “Love the Lord your God” → “ameis ao SENHOR vosso Deus”
- translated, not quoted (1Kings 15:5; The listed verse (1 Rs 15:5) is wrong: Henry echoes Sl 23:6 ("goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life"), which the Bíblia Livre has in the first person; translated close to its words.): “him all the days of his life”
- flag: Quote 1 is an unlisted echo of Sl 23:6; "o bem e a bondade o seguiram todos os dias de sua vida" puts the Bíblia Livre's "o bem e a bondade me seguirão todos os dias de minha vida" in Henry's third person.
- flag: "Aquele que prometeu é fiel" follows the Bíblia Livre's Hb 10:23 (unlisted).

### 681 Josh 24:1-14 God's benefits to their fathers.
- flag: "o mesmo modo de pensar que esteve em Cristo Jesus" echoes the Bíblia Livre's Fp 2:5 (unlisted).
