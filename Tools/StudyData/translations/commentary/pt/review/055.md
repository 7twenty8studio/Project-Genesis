# To review: pt commentary batch 055

### 2497 Isa 64:1-5 
- quoted (1Thess 4:16): “the Lord himself shall descend from heaven” → “o mesmo Senhor descerá do céu”
- flag: "Thou meetest him" follows Is 64:5 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "Tu foste ao encontro do alegre"; translated in Henry's present tense.
- flag: "in his favour is life" echoes Sl 30:5 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "seu favor dura por toda a vida"; translated.

### 2498 Isa 64:6-12 
- flag: Is 64:6: Henry's "filthy rags" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "roupas contaminadas"; his "trapos imundos" is translated.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Is 64:8: "tu és nosso Pai" from the Bíblia Livre; "the fountain open for sin and uncleanness" echoes Zc 13:1 ("uma fonte aberta ... contra o pecado e a impureza"), whose words are used; "stir themselves to lay hold upon him" Is 64:7 ("se desperte para se apegar a ti").

### 2499 Isa 65:1-7 
- flag: "Behold me" (Is 65:1 KJV) is written "Eis-me aqui", the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: sensitive: "the rejection of the Jews", "It is not without cause they are rejected of God" and Christ's woes against "the pride and hypocrisy of the Jews" (section 2499).

### 2500 Isa 65:8-10 
- flag: Is 65:8: Henry's "new wine ... in the cluster" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "suco num cacho de uvas"; translated.

### 2501 Isa 65:11-16 
- translated, not quoted (Isa 65:11; Is 65:11: Henry explains the KJV's "a table for that troop ... drink offering unto that number"; the Bíblia Livre has "mesa para o ídolo da sorte ... para o ídolo do destino", so his "troop of deities" and "countless number" would be lost; translated.): “a table for that troop”
- quoted (Gen 28:14): “all the families of the earth be blessed” → “todas as famílias da terra serão abençoadas”
- flag: "Wordly people" is a slip for "Worldly"; translated "As pessoas mundanas".
- flag: "bless themselves in him" and "the God of truth" follow Is 65:16 (Bíblia Livre "se bendirá no Deus da verdade").

### 2502 Isa 65:17-25 
- flag: "moritified" is misspelt in the source; translated "mortificadas".

### 2503 Isa 66:1-4 
- quoted (Isa 66:3): “as if he blessed an idol” → “adora a um ídolo”
- flag: Is 66:3: the Bíblia Livre's "adora a um ídolo" stands for the KJV's "blessed an idol".

### 2505 Isa 66:15-24 
- quoted (Matt 25:34): “Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world” → “Vinde, benditos de meu Pai! Herdai o Reino que está preparado para vós desde a fundação do mundo”
- flag: sensitive: "all antichristian opposers of the gospel in the latter days" (section 2505), in Henry's usage commonly aimed at the papacy.

### 2506 Jer 1:1-10 
- quoted (Jer 1:11): “of an almond-tree” → “uma vara de amendoeira”
- flag: Jr 1:11, 13: the outline's "almond-tree" is "vara de amendoeira" (the Bíblia Livre's words, KJV "a rod of an almond tree") and "seething-pot" is "panela fervente".
- flag: "wordly wise men" is a slip for "worldly"; translated "os sábios mundanos".

### 2509 Jer 2:9-13 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jr 2:13: "cisternas rachadas, que não retêm águas" from the Bíblia Livre (KJV "broken cisterns, that can hold no water").
- flag: "the enthusiast" is the 18th-century term for a religious fanatic; translated "o entusiasta"; a reviewer may prefer "o fanático".

### 2510 Jer 2:14-19 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jr 2:14: "Por acaso Israel é um servo?" from the Bíblia Livre.

### 2512 Jer 2:29-37 
- flag: "a wilderness and a land of darkness" follows Jr 2:31 (Bíblia Livre "um deserto ou uma terra de escuridão"); "the lines fallen to them in pleasant places" echoes Sl 16:6 ("Em lugares agradáveis foram postos os limites do meu terreno"), whose words are adapted.

### 2513 Jer 3:1-5 
- quoted (Jer 3:21): “The children of Israel” → “filhos de Israel”
- translated, not quoted (Jer 3:4; "wilt thou not from this time" is Henry's paraphrase of Jr 3:4 KJV ("Wilt thou not from this time cry unto me"); the Bíblia Livre reads it as a statement ("Não foi agora que clamaste a mim"), so his appeal would be lost; translated.): “wilt thou not from this time”
- flag: "the Guide of their youth" follows Jr 3:4 (Bíblia Livre "o guia de minha juventude").

### 2515 Jer 3:12-20 
- flag: "If we confess our sins, the Lord is faithful and just to forgive them" echoes 1 Jo 1:9 (Bíblia Livre "Se confessarmos nossos pecados, ele é fiel e justo para nos perdoar"); its words are followed, with Henry's "the Lord" and "them".

### 2516 Jer 3:21-25 
- quoted (Prov 28:13): “he that covereth his sins shall not prosper” → “quem encobre suas transgressões nunca prosperará”
- flag: Pv 28:13: the Bíblia Livre's "transgressões" stands for the KJV's "sins"; its words run on to "alcançará misericórdia" for Henry's "shall find mercy".
- flag: "heal their backslidings" follows Jr 3:22 KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "eu sararei vossas rebeliões", whose "sarará as suas rebeliões" is used.

### 2517 Jer 4:1-2 
- flag: The paragraph opens with ",2", a fragment of the verse range (Jr 4:1,2) left over in the source; kept as given.

### 2518 Jer 4:3-4 
- quoted (John 3:3): “except a man be born again, he cannot” → “aquele que não voltar a nascer, não pode”
- flag: The text opens with ",4", a fragment of the verse range (Jr 4:3,4) left over in the source; kept as given.
- flag: Henry's "fallow" ground rests on Jr 4:3 KJV ("Break up your fallow ground"); the Bíblia Livre has "Fazei lavoura para vós"; translated.
- flag: Henry joins Jo 3:3 ("born again") with "enter into the kingdom of heaven"; the Bíblia Livre's words are used up to "não pode", the rest is his.
- flag: "create in us a clean heart ... a right spirit" echoes Sl 51:10 (Bíblia Livre "coração puro ... espírito firme"); "espírito reto" is translated.

### 2520 Jer 4:19-31 
- flag: "may the Lord make of good understanding" lacks an object in the source (probably "make us"); translated "que o Senhor nos dê bom entendimento".
- flag: "a full end" follows Jr 4:27 KJV ("yet will I not make a full end"); the Bíblia Livre has "não a destruirei por completo"; translated.

### 2522 Jer 5:10-18 
- flag: Henry points to the KJV's word "nevertheless" in Jr 5:18; the Bíblia Livre's verse begins "Porém naqueles dias", so his quoted word is written "porém".

### 2523 Jer 5:19-31 
- quoted (Jer 5:29): “Shall I not visit for these things” → “Por acaso eu não os puniria por causa disto?”
- quoted (2Sam 2:26): “will be bitterness in the latter end” → “ao fim se segue amargura”
- flag: Jr 5:24: Henry's "appointed weeks of harvest" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre has "os tempos estabelecidos da ceifa"; translated "as semanas determinadas da ceifa".
- flag: Jr 5:29: the Bíblia Livre's "puniria" stands for the KJV's "visit".

### 2524 Jer 6:1-8 
- flag: "as a fountain casts out her waters" follows Jr 6:7 (Bíblia Livre "a fonte jorra suas águas").

### 2525 Jer 6:9-17 
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jr 6:16: "perguntai pelas veredas antigas" from the Bíblia Livre (twice).

### 2526 Jer 6:18-30

### 2529 Jer 7:21-28

### 2532 Jer 8:4-13 
- quoted (Jer 8:11): “peace when there is no peace” → “sem que haja paz”
- flag: Jr 8:11: "peace when there is no peace" is "paz sem que haja paz", the Bíblia Livre's "sem que haja paz" after Henry's single "peace" (the verse has "Paz, paz").

### 2533 Jer 8:14-22 
- flag: "balm in Gilead" and "the Physician" follow Jr 8:22 (Bíblia Livre "bálsamo em Gileade ... médico").

### 2534 Jer 9:1-11 
- quoted (Ps 107:34): “a fruitful land into barrenness for the wickedness” → “terra frutífera em salgada, pela maldade dos que nela habitam”
- flag: Sl 107:34: the Bíblia Livre's "em salgada" stands for the KJV's "into barrenness".
- flag: The outline's "God's loving-kindness, He threatens ..." is run together in the source; kept as given.

### 2535 Jer 9:12-22 
- quoted (Jer 9:21): “the children from without, and the young men from the streets” → “os meninos das ruas, os rapazes das praças”
- flag: Jr 9:21: the Bíblia Livre has "os meninos das ruas, os rapazes das praças" for the KJV's "the children from without, and the young men from the streets".

### 2536 Jer 9:23-26 
- translated, not quoted (Phil 3:3; Henry turns Fp 3:3's first person ("orgulhamo-nos em Cristo Jesus, e não confiamos na carne") into the third ("Those ... who rejoice ... and have no confidence"); translated close to its words.): “rejoice in Christ Jesus, and have no confidence in the flesh”

### 2537 Jer 10:1-16

- flag: sensitive: "the wicked customs of the heathen" (section 2537).
