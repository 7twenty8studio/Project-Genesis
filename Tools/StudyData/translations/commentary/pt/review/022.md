# To review: pt commentary batch 022

### 766 1Sam 2:1-10 Hannah's song of thanksgiving.
- quoted (James 4:12): “is able to save, and to destroy” → “que pode salvar e destruir”
- flag: "his Anointed" (1 Sm 2:10) is "seu ungido" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's point that this is the name Messiah holds in Portuguese too.

### 768 1Sam 2:27-36 The prophecy against Eli's family.
- quoted (Eph 6:4): “in the nurture and admonition of the Lord” → “na disciplina e correção do Senhor”

### 769 1Sam 3:1-10 The word of the Lord first revealed to Samuel.
- flag: "till we come to the call" read as "till we answer the call" ("até que atendamos ao chamado").

### 770 1Sam 3:11-18 God tells Samuel the destruction of Eli's house.
- flag: "It is the iniquity which our own heart knoweth" adapts 1 Sm 3:13 ("for the iniquity which he knoweth"; Bíblia Livre "pela iniquidade que ele sabe"); translated, since Henry changes the person.

### 771 1Sam 3:19-21 Samuel established to be a prophet.
- flag: Echoes of 1 Sm 2:30 and Ec 12:1 ("lembra-te de teu Criador nos dias de tua juventude") adapted to Henry's third person.

### 772 1Sam 4:1-9 The Israelites overcome by the Philistines.
- flag: Pv 19:3 rendered close to the Bíblia Livre ("A loucura do homem perverte seu caminho; e seu coração se ira contra o SENHOR", unlisted), with "o Senhor" as in Henry's own sentence.

### 774 1Sam 4:12-18 The death of Eli.
- flag: Henry's quotation "The ark of God is taken" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Sm 4:17 ("a arca de Deus foi tomada", unlisted).

### 777 1Sam 5:6-12 The Philistine smitten.
- quoted (1Sam 5:6): “The hand of the Lord was heavy upon” → “Agravou-se a mão do SENHOR sobre”
- flag: The source's title has "The Philistine smitten" (singular); translated as plural, "Os filisteus feridos".

### 778 1Sam 6:1-9 The Philistines consult how to send back the ark.
- flag: The English has "the heavies of calamities", read as "the heaviest of calamities".

### 779 1Sam 6:10-18 They bring it to Bethshemesh.
- flag: The English has "Hophin", read as "Hophni" (Hofni).
- flag: "Bethshemesh" given as "Bete-Semes", the Bíblia Livre's spelling (1 Sm 6:12).

### 780 1Sam 6:19-21 The people smitten for looking into the ark.
- quoted (Gal 6:7): “Be not deceived, God is not mocked” → “Não vos enganeis: Deus não se deixa escarnecer”

### 781 1Sam 7:1-4 
- flag: The text opens with a heading ("The ark removed to Kirjath-jearim.") and a bare "(1-4)", kept as separate paragraphs as in the source.
- flag: "Kirjath-jearim" given as "Quiriate-Jearim" (the Bíblia Livre prints "Quriate-Jearim" in 1 Sm 7:2).

### 782 1Sam 7:5-6 The Israelites solemnly repent.
- quoted (1Sam 7:6): “drew water and poured it out before the Lord” → “tiraram água, e derramaram-na diante do SENHOR”
- quoted (1Sam 7:6): “We have sinned against the Lord” → “Contra o SENHOR temos pecado”
- quoted (1John 1:9): “faithful and just to forgive us our sins” → “fiel e justo para nos perdoar os pecados”

### 783 1Sam 7:7-12 The Lord discomfits the Philistines.
- flag: Henry's quotation "hitherto the Lord hath helped her" adapts 1 Sm 7:12 (unlisted; Bíblia Livre "Até aqui nos ajudou o SENHOR"), given with "a" for his "her".
- flag: "Eben-ezers" given as "Ebenézeres", after the Bíblia Livre's "Ebenézer".

### 785 1Sam 8:1-3 The evil government of Samuel's sons.
- flag: "they turned aside after lucre" (1 Sm 8:3) rendered close to the Bíblia Livre ("se inclinaram atrás a ganância"), unlisted.

### 786 1Sam 8:4-9 The Israelites ask for a king.
- quoted (1Sam 8:6): “when they said, Give us a king to judge us” → “Dá-nos rei que nos julgue”

### 787 1Sam 8:10-22 The manner of a king.
- flag: "The manner of a king" (title; 1 Sm 8:9, 11) given with the Bíblia Livre's "o direito do rei"; Henry's contrast with "the manner of men" is kept as "a maneira dos homens", so the echo of the title is lost.

### 790 1Sam 9:18-27 Samuel's treatment of Saul.
- quoted (1Sam 9:25): “Samuel communed with Saul upon” → “falou com Saul no terraço”
- flag: Henry's "communed" and "the flat roof of the house": the Bíblia Livre has "falou com Saul no terraço"; "da casa" is Henry's own addition, kept.

### 791 1Sam 10:1-8 Samuel anoints Saul.
- quoted (1Sam 10:6): “be turned into another man” → “transformado em outro homem”
- flag: "High Priest of our profession" (Hb 3:1) rendered "Sumo Sacerdote da nossa confissão".

### 792 1Sam 10:9-16 Saul prophesies.
- flag: "God had given him another heart" (1 Sm 10:9, KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "mudou-lhe Deus seu coração".

### 793 1Sam 10:17-27 Saul chosen king.
- quoted (1Sam 10:19): “Ye have this day rejected your God” → “Vós rejeitastes hoje a vosso Deus”
- translated, not quoted (1Sam 10:26; Henry says God "disposed" their hearts to support Saul, where 1 Sm 10:26 says "touched" (Bíblia Livre: "o coração dos quais Deus havia tocado"); the verse's wording would change his clause. His next sentences take up "touched".): “a band of men whose hearts God”
- quoted (1Sam 10:27): “How shall this man save us” → “Como nos há de salvar este”

### 794 1Sam 11:1-11 
- flag: The text opens with a heading ("Jabesh-gilead delivered.") and a bare "(1-11)", kept as separate paragraphs as in the source.

### 796 1Sam 12:1-5 Samuel testifies his integrity.
- flag: The English lacks a verb ("a just debt which all men to their own good name"); read as "which all men owe to".

### 797 1Sam 12:6-15 Samuel reproves the people.
- quoted (1Sam 12:7): “the righteous acts of the Lord” → “justiças do SENHOR”
- flag: "the righteous acts of the Lord" is the Bíblia Livre's "justiças do SENHOR" (1 Sm 12:7).

### 799 1Sam 13:1-7 The invasion of the Philistines.
- flag: The English has "gave the Philistine time" (singular); translated as plural.

### 802 1Sam 14:1-15 Jonathan smites the Philistines.
- quoted (1Sam 14:15): “There was trembling in the host” → “houve tremor no acampamento”
- flag: "a trembling of God" is the KJV margin's literal reading of 1 Sm 14:15 ("a very great trembling"); the Bíblia Livre has "grande pânico", so Henry's explanation is translated as he gives it.

### 805 1Sam 14:36-46 Jonathan pointed out by lot.
- flag: The source's last sentence is a question ending in a full stop; given a question mark.

### 808 1Sam 15:10-23 Saul excuses and commends himself.
- quoted (1Sam 15:11): “He is turned back from following me” → “se desviou de me seguir”
- quoted (1Sam 15:19): “evil in the sight of the Lord” → “mau aos olhos do SENHOR”
- quoted (1Sam 15:19): “not obey the voice of the Lord” → “não ouviste a voz do SENHOR”

### 809 1Sam 15:24-31 Saul's imperfect humiliation.
- quoted (1Sam 15:29): “The Strength of Israel will not lie” → “o Poderoso de Israel não mentirá”
- flag: "The Strength of Israel" is the Bíblia Livre's "o Poderoso de Israel" (1 Sm 15:29).

### 810 1Sam 15:32-35 Agag put to death, Samuel and Saul part.
- quoted (1Sam 15:32): “the bitterness of death is past” → “se passou a amargura da morte”

### 811 1Sam 16:1-5 Samuel sent to Bethlehem to Jesse.
- flag: "I come peaceably, for I come to sacrifice" (1 Sm 16:5) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "Sim, venho a sacrificar ao SENHOR" without "peaceably".
- flag: "A body hast thou prepared me" follows the Bíblia Livre's Hb 10:5 ("me preparaste um corpo", unlisted).

### 812 1Sam 16:6-13 David is anointed.
- translated, not quoted (Judg 6:34; Listed against Jz 6:34 (Gideon); Henry is speaking of David, 1 Sm 16:13, where the Bíblia Livre has "o espírito do SENHOR tomou a Davi", used verbatim within quotation 1.): “the Spirit of the Lord came upon”
- quoted (1Sam 16:13): “David from that day forward” → “desde aquele dia em diante o espírito do SENHOR tomou a Davi”
- flag: Quotations 0 and 1 together are 1 Sm 16:13, given as one verbatim run of the Bíblia Livre ("desde aquele dia em diante o espírito do SENHOR tomou a Davi"), in its word order.

### 813 1Sam 16:14-23 Saul troubled with an evil spirit, is quieted by David.
- quoted (1Sam 16:14): “The Spirit of the Lord departed from” → “O espírito do SENHOR se afastou”
