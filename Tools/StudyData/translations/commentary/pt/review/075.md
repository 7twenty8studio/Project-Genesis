# To review: pt commentary batch 075

### 3243 Luke 11:29-36 Christ reproves the Jews.
- flag: sensitive: title 'Christ reproves the Jews' (the section reproves 'this generation'); translated as written

### 3245 Luke 12:1-12 Christ reproves the interpreters of the law.
- quoted (Luke 12:8): “before the angels of God” → “diante dos anjos de Deus”

### 3246 Luke 12:13-21 A caution against covetousness, The parable of the rich man.
- quoted (Luke 12:20): “Thy soul shall be required” → “te pedirão tua alma”
- flag: 'folly of carnal worldling while they live': singular noun with plural verb in the English; translated as plural 'dos mundanos carnais'
- flag: 'wordly dominion': typo in the English for 'worldly'; translated 'o domínio mundano'
- flag: 'God will call them by their own name': the parable's 'Thou fool' (the Bíblia Livre: 'Louco'); rendered with 'loucos' to keep the link

### 3247 Luke 12:22-40 Worldly care reproved.
- quoted (Luke 12:36): “men that wait for their lord” → “pessoas que esperam a seu senhor”
- quoted (Luke 12:40): “Be ye therefore ready also” → “Vós, pois, também estejais prontos”
- quoted (Luke 12:39): “what hour the thief would come” → “à que hora o ladrão viria”
- flag: Q2: the Bíblia Livre's 'à que hora' (with a grave accent, for 'a que hora') kept verbatim as the quotation; a reviewer may prefer to treat this as translated

### 3248 Luke 12:41-53 Watchfulness enforced.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 12:50; the Bíblia Livre's 'um batismo que tenho que ser batizado' is in Christ's first person; Henry's third-person sentence needs its own wording): “a baptism to be baptized”

### 3249 Luke 12:54-59 A warning to be reconciled to God.
- flag: 'God in Christ reconciling the world to himself' adapts 2 Cor 5:19 (unlisted; the Bíblia Livre: 'Deus estava em Cristo reconciliando consigo ao mundo'); translated close to it

### 3250 Luke 13:1-5 Christ exhorts to repentance from the case of the Galileans and others.
- quoted (Matt 4:17): “repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand” → “Arrependei-vos, porque perto está o Reino dos céus”

### 3251 Luke 13:6-9 Parable of the barren fig-tree.
- flag: sensitive: the parable applied 'in the first place' to the nation and people of the Jews (3251)

### 3252 Luke 13:10-17 The infirm woman strengthened.
- flag: Henry moves from the sabbath of the passage to 'the Lord's day' (o dia do Senhor) in the last sentence, as written

### 3253 Luke 13:18-22 The parables of the mustard seed, and leaven.
- flag: 'until the little one becomes a thousand' echoes Isa 60:22; translated in Henry's words

### 3254 Luke 13:23-30 Exhortation to enter at the strait gate.
- quoted (Luke 13:24): “Strive to enter in at the strait gate” → “Trabalhai para entrar pela porta estreita”
- quoted (Luke 13:24): “enter in at the strait gate” → “entrar pela porta estreita”
- flag: the Bíblia Livre's 'Trabalhai' (Luke 13:24) stands for the KJV's 'Strive'; Henry's 'it is, Strive ye' and 'must strive to enter' rendered with the same verb ('Trabalhai vós', 'trabalhar para entrar') to keep his emphasis on the word; a reviewer may prefer 'esforçar-se'

### 3255 Luke 13:31-35 Christ's reproof to Herod, and to the people of Jerusalem.
- quoted (Luke 13:35): “in the name of the Lord” → “no nome do Senhor”

### 3256 Luke 14:1-6 Christ heals a man on the sabbath.

### 3257 Luke 14:7-14 He teaches humility.

### 3258 Luke 14:15-24 Parable of the great supper.

### 3259 Luke 14:25-35

### 3261 Luke 15:11-16 The prodigal son, his wickedness and distress.

- flag: 'a state constant discontent': a missing 'of' in the English; translated 'um estado de descontentamento constante'
- flag: 'dead in trespasses and sins' (Eph 2:1, unlisted) translated in Henry's words

### 3262 Luke 15:17-24 His repentance and pardon.

### 3263 Luke 15:25-32 The elder brother offended.

### 3265 Luke 16:13-18 Christ reproves the hypocrisy of the covetous Pharisees.
- flag: 'Ye cannot serve God and the world': Henry's 'the world' for the KJV's 'mammon' (the Bíblia Livre: 'às riquezas'); translated as Henry wrote it

### 3266 Luke 16:19-31 The rich man and Lazarus.
- quoted (2Pet 1:19): “sure word of prophecy” → “muito confiável palavra dos profetas”
- flag: 'The rich man in hell': Henry follows the KJV's 'hell' at Luke 16:23; the Bíblia Livre has 'Xeol'; translated 'no inferno' as Henry wrote it
- flag: sensitive: Henry argues against praying to departed saints (a Roman Catholic practice), calling the rich man's prayer to Abraham 'the mistake of a damned sinner' (3266)
- flag: 'the sure word of prophecy': the Bíblia Livre's 'muito confiável palavra dos profetas' (2 Pet 1:19) used
- flag: 'seek to the law and to the testimony' (Isa 8:19-20, KJV): the Bíblia Livre has 'À Lei e ao testemunho!'; translated in Henry's words

### 3267 Luke 17:1-10 Ten lepers cleansed.
- flag: the titles of 3267 and 3268 appear swapped in the source (3267's text is on offences, faith and humility, Luke 17:1-10; 3268's on the ten lepers); each title translated where the source has it

### 3269 Luke 17:20-37 Christ's kingdom.

### 3272 Luke 18:15-17 Children brought to Christ.

### 3273 Luke 18:18-30 The ruler hindered by his riches.
