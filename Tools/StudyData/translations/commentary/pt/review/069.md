# To review: pt commentary batch 069

### 3037 Matt 13:31-43 
- flag: "the parable of the seed sown" here is the mustard seed (Mt 13:31-32), as the section's verses show; translated literally.

### 3038 Matt 13:44-52 The parables of the hidden treasure, the pearl of great price, the net cast into the sea, and the householder.
- flag: "pearl of great price" and "treasure hid in the field" (Mt 13:44, 46) are not listed quotations; rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "pérola de grande valor" and "tesouro escondido" (its v. 44 has "num campo").

### 3039 Matt 13:53-58 Jesus is again rejected at Nazareth.
- quoted (Matt 13:55): “Is not this the carpenter's son” → “Não é este o filho do carpinteiro”
- quoted (Matt 13:58): “He did not many mighty works there, because of their unbelief” → “não fez ali muitos milagres por causa da incredulidade deles”
- flag: Source has "those who have repulsed them" (the plural apparently meaning his offers); translated as "que a tinham repelido" (the offer).

### 3042 Matt 14:22-33 Jesus walks upon the sea.
- quoted (Heb 11:6): “to God, must believe” → “necessário que aquele que se aproxima de Deus creia”

### 3043 Matt 14:34-36 Jesus healing the sick.
- quoted (Mark 1:32): “They brought unto him all that were diseased” → “trouxeram-lhe todos os doentes”
- quoted (Matt 14:36): “As many as touched, were made perfectly whole” → “todos os que tocavam ficaram curados”
- flag: Mt 14:36: the KJV's "made perfectly whole" is "ficaram curados" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's next sentence ("he heals perfectly") builds on the KJV's "perfectly".
- flag: "The healing virtue was not in the finger": Henry probably means the hem ("fringe") of the garment; translated literally ("dedo").

### 3044 Matt 15:1-9 Jesus discourses about human traditions.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mt 15:9 inside Henry's quotation marks: "Em vão, porém, me veneram" from the Bíblia Livre, its "porém" kept.

### 3046 Matt 15:21-28 He heals the daughter of a Syrophenician woman.
- quoted (Isa 52:10): “the ends of the earth shall see” → “os confins da terra verão”

### 3050 Matt 16:13-20 Peter's testimony that Jesus was the Christ.
- flag: Henry's "The word translated 'rock'" (Gk. petra vs. Petros): rendered "pedra", the Bíblia Livre's word at Mt 16:18 ("tu és Pedro, e sobre esta pedra"), which keeps his point that the words are similar but not the same.
- flag: sensitive: "Nothing can be more wrong than to suppose that … Peter was the rock" and "all pretensions of any man … to absolve or retain men's sins, are blasphemous and absurd" are Protestant polemic against Roman Catholic teaching (papal primacy, priestly absolution); translated in full.

### 3052 Matt 16:24-28 
- flag: Unlisted quotations of Mt 16:24 inside Henry's quotation marks: "Negue-se a si mesmo" and "Tome sobre si a sua cruz" from the Bíblia Livre.

### 3053 Matt 17:1-13 The transfiguration of Christ.
- quoted (John 1:14): “glory, as of the only begotten of the Father” → “como glória do unigênito do Pai”
- quoted (Matt 17:5): “a bright cloud overshadowed them” → “uma nuvem brilhante os cobriu”

### 3054 Matt 17:14-21 Jesus casts out a dumb and deaf spirit.
- flag: The title says "a dumb and deaf spirit" (from Mc 9:25); Matthew's account doesn't use those words; translated as given.

### 3056 Matt 17:24-27 He works a miracle to pay the tribute money.
- quoted (Phil 4:19): “need, according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus” → “segundo as suas riquezas em glória em Cristo Jesus”

### 3058 Matt 18:7-14 Caution against offences.
- flag: "If we live after the flesh … we shall live" is Rm 8:13 (not listed, and Henry changes the person to "we"); rendered after the Bíblia Livre's wording in the first person plural, not as a quotation.

### 3061 Matt 19:1-2 Jesus enters Judea.
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Ml 4:2: "o Sol da justiça" and "trazendo cura em suas asas" are the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 3065 Matt 19:23-30 The recompence of Christ's followers.
- quoted (Matt 19:25): “Who then can be saved” → “Quem, pois, pode se salvar”
- quoted (Matt 19:28): “when the Son of man shall sit” → “quando o Filho do homem se sentar”
- quoted (Matt 19:28): “the throne of his glory” → “trono de sua glória”
- flag: Henry asks "Who then can be saved?" twice; the first is the Bíblia Livre's words ("Quem, pois, pode se salvar?"), the second ("Who can be saved? say the disciples") translated plainly as "Quem pode ser salvo?".

### 3066 Matt 20:1-16 The parable of the labourers in the vineyard.
- quoted (Matt 20:16): “the last shall be first, and the first last” → “os últimos serão primeiros; e os primeiros, últimos”
- flag: "The Roman penny was sevenpence halfpenny in our money": Henry's English money of his day; translated "sete pence e meio na nossa moeda" ("our" = England's); "penny" rendered "denário", the coin of Mt 20:2.
- flag: Unlisted allusions: "unprofitable servants" (Lc 17:10, Bíblia Livre "servos inúteis") and "now is the accepted time" (2 Co 6:2, "eis agora o tempo agradável") use the Bíblia Livre's words.
