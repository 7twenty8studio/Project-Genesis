# To review: pt commentary batch 029

### 1098 1Chr 4:1-43 Genealogies.
- quoted (1Chr 4:9): “Jabez was more honourable than his brethren” → “Jabez foi mais honrado que seus irmãos”
- quoted (1Chr 4:10): “Oh that thou wouldest bless me” → “Ah, se tu me abençoares”
- flag: "bless me indeed" (1 Cr 4:10): the Bíblia Livre has "me abençoares muito"; Henry's point 1 ("bless him indeed") translated as "de fato"; the quoted run stops before "muito" as Henry's stops before "indeed".
- flag: "enlarge his coast" translated "ampliasse os seus limites", the Bíblia Livre's word in 1 Cr 4:10.
- flag: "a Jabez indeed, a man of sorrow": Henry plays on the name (sorrow); the Bíblia Livre's v. 9 says "com dores", so "homem de dor" keeps the link.

### 1101 1Chr 7:1-40 Genealogies.
- flag: Source reads "Zebulun or Dn We": "Dan." has been turned into the abbreviation "Dn" and the full stop lost; translated as "Dã." (the tribe), a new sentence after it.

### 1103 1Chr 9:1-44 Genealogies.
- quoted (Rev 4:8): “they rest not day” → “tem repouso de dia nem de noite”
- quoted (Col 1:12): “the inheritance of the saints in light” → “a herança dos santos na luz”
- flag: Ap 4:8: the Bíblia Livre's "não tem repouso" is singular (each creature); worked in impersonally as "onde não se tem repouso de dia nem de noite" for Henry's "they rest not day nor night".

### 1104 1Chr 10:1-14 The death of Saul.

- quoted (1Chr 10:13): “the word of the Lord” → “palavra do SENHOR”
- flag: Source "May be delivered from unbelief…" lacks its subject (read as "May we be delivered"); translated "Que sejamos livrados".
- flag: Unlisted allusion to Hb 12:28 ("a kingdom that cannot be moved"): the Bíblia Livre's "um Reino inabalável" used.

### 1108 1Chr 12:23-40 Those who came to Hebron.

### 1110 1Chr 13:6-14 The removal of the ark.

- flag: "receive it in the love of it" (an allusion to 2 Ts 2:10) translated "recebamo-lo… com amor".

### 1112 1Chr 15:1-24 Preparations for the removal of the ark.
