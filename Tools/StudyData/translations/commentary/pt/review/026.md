# To review: pt commentary batch 026

### 969 1Kings 10:1-13 The queen of Sheba's visit to Solomon.
- flag: "não lhes contaram nem a metade" alludes to 1 Rs 10:7 (Bíblia Livre: "nem ainda a metade foi o que se me disse"); Henry changes the person, so translated.
- flag: "Bem-aventurados os que habitam em sua casa; eles o louvarão continuamente" is Sl 84:4 with Henry's change to the third person (Bíblia Livre: "em tua casa; eles louvam a ti continuamente"), so translated.
- flag: "deixar o corpo, e habitar com o Senhor" is 2 Co 5:8 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 970 1Kings 10:14-29 Solomon's wealth.
- flag: The English reads "that all the wealth", a slip for "than"; translated as "do que".

### 971 1Kings 11:1-8 Solomon's wives and concubines, His idolatry.
- flag: "Aquele que pensa estar de pé, olhe para que não caia" is 1 Co 10:12 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 973 1Kings 11:14-25 Solomon's adversaries.
- flag: The English "If against us" leaves the subject implied (God); translated "Se Deus está contra nós".
- flag: "o gafanhoto se tornar pesado" is Ec 12:5 in the Bíblia Livre (KJV "the grasshopper shall be a burden"); used as "o gafanhoto se tornará pesado", the verb's tense changed (unlisted).

### 974 1Kings 11:26-40 Jeroboam's promotion.
- flag: "há muitos pensamentos no coração do homem; porém o conselho do SENHOR prevalecerá" is Pv 19:21 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted; KJV "many devices in a man's heart", Henry "whatever devices are in men's hearts").

### 975 1Kings 11:41-43 The death of Solomon.
- flag: "Vaidade e aflição de espírito" (Henry's quotation marks, Ec 1:14 etc.) translated, not checked against the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "alguém maior que Salomão" alludes to Mt 12:42 (Bíblia Livre: "quem é maior que Salomão"); translated.

### 976 1Kings 12:1-15 Rehoboam's accession, The people's petition, His rough answer.
- quoted (1Kings 12:14): “the counsel of the young men” → “conforme ao conselho dos rapazes”
- flag: "escondeu dos seus olhos aquilo que lhe traria paz" alludes to Lc 19:42 in the Bíblia Livre's words ("aquilo que lhe traria paz"; unlisted).

### 977 1Kings 12:16-24 Ten tribes revolt.
- quoted (1Kings 12:24): “to the word of the Lord” → “palavra do SENHOR”

### 979 1Kings 13:1-10 Jeroboam's sin reproved.
- translated, not quoted (1Kings 13:1; Henry's "the word of the Lord will endure for ever" quotes 1 Pe 1:25, not 1 Rs 13:1; quoted from the Bíblia Livre's 1 Pe 1:25 instead ("a palavra do Senhor permanece para sempre").): “the word of the Lord”
- flag: "a palavra do Senhor permanece para sempre" is 1 Pe 1:25 in the Bíblia Livre's words (the listed verse, 1 Rs 13:1, is the wrong one).
- flag: "as obras das trevas" echoes Ef 5:11 (Bíblia Livre: "das obras infrutíferas das trevas").

### 981 1Kings 13:23-34 The disobedient prophet is slain, Jeroboam's obstinacy.
- quoted (1Kings 13:33): “Jeroboam returned not from his evil way” → “não se converteu Jeroboão de seu mau caminho”
- flag: "mas eles a perderam": the English "they lost it" is ambiguous (the calves lost the crown); kept as in the English.

### 982 1Kings 14:1-6 Abijah being sick, his mother consults Ahijah.
- flag: "Naquele tempo" (Henry's quotation marks) is 1 Rs 14:1 in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: "todo o conselho de Deus" is At 20:27 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "para que não profetize ... o bem, mas o mal" echoes 1 Rs 22:8 (Bíblia Livre: "nunca me profetiza bem, a não ser somente mal"); translated. The English "lest it should prophesy" has "it" for the counsel declared; kept impersonal.

### 983 1Kings 14:7-20 The destruction of Jeroboam's house.
- quoted (1Kings 14:13): “in the house of Jeroboam” → “na casa de Jeroboão”
- quoted (1Kings 14:7): “the Lord God of Israel” → “SENHOR Deus de Israel”
- flag: "retirados do mal vindouro" alludes to Is 57:1 (Bíblia Livre: "o justo é levado de diante do mal"); translated.

### 984 1Kings 14:21-31 Rehoboam's wicked reign.
- quoted (1Kings 14:25): “Shishak, king of Egypt, came” → “Sisaque rei do Egito”
- flag: "O pecado faz o ouro escurecer-se, muda o bom e fino ouro" echoes Lm 4:1 (Bíblia Livre: "Como se escureceu o ouro! Como mudou o bom e fino ouro!"; unlisted, Henry's own sentence around it).

### 985 1Kings 15:1-8 Wicked reign of Abijam, king of Judah.
- quoted (1Kings 15:3): “heart was not perfect with the Lord his God” → “não foi seu coração completo com o SENHOR seu Deus”
- quoted (1Kings 15:3): “walked in all the sins of his father” → “andou em todos os pecados de seu pai”
- flag: KJV "perfect" (heart) is "completo" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's gloss "he wanted sincerity" follows it.

### 986 1Kings 15:9-24 Good reign of Asa, king of Judah.
- quoted (1Kings 15:11): “was right in the eyes of the Lord” → “o que era correto diante dos olhos do SENHOR”
- flag: "aprender a fazer o bem" echoes Is 1:17 (Bíblia Livre: "Aprendei a fazer o bem"); "confiar no Senhor de todo o coração" echoes Pv 3:5; both in Henry's own sentence, translated.

### 987 1Kings 15:25-34 The evil reigns of Nadab and Baasha in Israel.
- flag: "nenhuma palavra de Deus cairá em terra" echoes 2 Rs 10:10 (Bíblia Livre: "nada cairá em terra"); translated.

### 990 1Kings 16:29-34 Ahab's wickedness, Hiel rebuilds Jericho.
- flag: The English repeats itself ("which was devoted to God's honour: he began to build, in defiance of the curse well devoted to God's honour: he began to build, in defiance of the curse well known"), an editing slip; translated as it stands, repetition included.

### 991 1Kings 17:1-7 Elijah fed by ravens.
- flag: "um rio cujos ribeiros alegram a cidade de Deus" is Sl 46:4 and "fonte de água, que salte para vida eterna" is Jo 4:14, both in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted); KJV "the streams whereof" is in both, Henry's "a river which makes glad" shortens it.
- flag: "no seu dia" renders Henry's "in the day" (the bread of the day, day by day).

### 992 1Kings 17:8-16 Elijah sent to Zarephath.
- quoted (Luke 4:25): “Many widows were in Israel in the days of Elias” → “muitas viúvas em Israel nos dias de Elias”
- quoted (Ps 37:19): “days of famine they shall be satisfied” → “não serão envergonhados no tempo mau, e terão fartura nos dias de fome”
- flag: "Ó mulher, grande é a tua fé" is Mt 15:28 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted; Henry has "great was thy faith"); "nem mesmo em Israel" echoes Mt 8:10.
- flag: "contra a esperança, crer ... em esperança" alludes to Rm 4:18 (KJV wording); the Bíblia Livre words it quite differently, so translated.
- flag: Quote 1: Henry quotes the whole of Sl 37:19 ("not be ashamed in the evil time ... satisfied"); the Bíblia Livre's whole verse used.

### 994 1Kings 18:1-16 Elijah sends Ahab notice of his coming.
- quoted (2Chr 11:13): “The priests and the Levites” → “os sacerdotes e levitas”
- quoted (2Chr 11:14): “to Judah and Jerusalem” → “a Judá e a Jerusalém”
- flag: "o pão de angústia ... os nossos olhos veem os nossos instrutores" echoes Is 30:20 (Bíblia Livre: "pão de angústia ... teus olhos verão os teus instrutores"); Henry changes the person, so translated.

### 996 1Kings 18:21-40 Elijah's trial of the false prophets.
- quoted (John 20:29): “Blessed are they that have not seen” → “bem-aventurados aqueles que não virem”
- flag: The English has "the world of God", a slip for "word"; translated "a palavra de Deus".
- flag: "o Deus que responder por fogo, esse seja Deus" is 1 Rs 18:24, "Quem vos pediu isso de vossas mãos" is Is 1:12 and "o SENHOR é o Deus" is 1 Rs 18:39, all in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "hesitar entre" echoes 1 Rs 18:21 (KJV "halt", Bíblia Livre "hesitareis").
- flag: "a oração eficaz e fervorosa do justo, que muito pode" follows the KJV of Tg 5:16; the Bíblia Livre has "A oração de um justo muito pode efetuar" (no "effectual, fervent"), so translated.

### 997 1Kings 18:41-46 Elijah, by prayer, obtains rain.
- flag: "uma nuvem de um palmo" renders Henry's "a cloud of span long" (KJV 1 Rs 18:44 "like a man's hand", Bíblia Livre "como a palma da mão de um homem").
- flag: "o dia das pequenas coisas" is Zc 4:10 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).

### 998 1Kings 19:1-8 Elijah flees to the wilderness.
- flag: "ser desligado, e estar com Cristo" is Fp 1:23 and "no Senhor e na força do seu poder" is Ef 6:10, both in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted); KJV "to depart" is "ser desligado" there.
- flag: "não era melhor que seus pais" echoes 1 Rs 19:4 (Bíblia Livre: "não sou eu melhor que meus pais"), changed to the third person.

### 999 1Kings 19:9-13 God manifests himself to Elijah.
- quoted (1Kings 19:9): “What doest thou here, Elijah” → “Que fazes aqui, Elias”
- flag: "eu só restei" is 1 Rs 19:10 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "a voz mansa": KJV 1 Rs 19:12 "a still small voice"; Henry's "still voice" translated, not checked against the Bíblia Livre.

### 1000 1Kings 19:14-18 God's answer to Elijah.
- flag: "os seus escondidos, Sl 83:3": KJV "thy hidden ones"; the Bíblia Livre has "teus preciosos", so Henry's point rests on the KJV wording; translated.
- flag: "O Senhor conhece os que são seus" is 2 Tm 2:19 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "Que fazes aqui?" is 1 Rs 19:13 (Bíblia Livre: "Que fazes aqui, Elias?"), shortened as Henry does.

### 1001 1Kings 19:19-21 The call of Elisha.
- flag: "no dia do poder ... voluntários" alludes to Sl 110:3 (Bíblia Livre: "Teu povo será voluntário no dia do teu poder"); translated in Henry's sentence.
- flag: "consultado a carne e o sangue" follows the KJV of Gl 1:16 ("conferred not with flesh and blood"); the Bíblia Livre has "pedir conselho com pessoa alguma", so translated.
- flag: "confirmar o nosso chamamento e escolha" echoes 2 Pe 1:10 (Bíblia Livre: "confirmar vosso chamamento e escolha").

### 1002 1Kings 20:1-11 Benhadad besieges Samaria.
- flag: Ahab's saying (1 Rs 20:11, Bíblia Livre: "que não se glorie o que se cinge, como o que já se descinge") is referred to, not quoted; "não sabemos o que ele trará" echoes Pv 27:1.

### 1003 1Kings 20:12-21 Benhadad's defeat.
- quoted (1Kings 20:21): “slew the Syrians with a great slaughter” → “derrotou os sírios com grande estrago”

### 1005 1Kings 20:31-43 Ahab makes peace with Benhadad.
- quoted (Isa 26:10): “to the wicked, yet will he not learn righteousness” → “se faça favor ao perverso, nem assim ele aprende a justiça”
- quoted (1Kings 20:43): “went to his house, heavy and displeased” → “se foi a sua casa triste e irritado”
- flag: The English ends "are busy and there till the day of salvation is gone", a slip for "busy here and there" (1 Rs 20:40); translated "andam ocupados aqui e ali".
- flag: "nele há perdão" echoes Sl 130:4 (Bíblia Livre: "contigo está o perdão"), changed to the third person.
- flag: Henry's "his friend and God's" rendered "o seu amigo, que também era amigo de Deus" for clarity.

### 1007 1Kings 21:5-16 Naboth murdered by Jezebel.
- flag: "ajuda idônea" (Gn 2:18) and "filhos da desobediência" (Ef 2:2) are the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
