# To review: pt commentary batch 013

### 400 Num 3:40-51 
- quoted (Acts 15:18): “Known unto God are all his works” → “São conhecidas por Deus desde a antiguidade todas as suas obras”
- flag: At 15:18 in the Bíblia Livre has "desde a antiguidade" where Henry has "beforehand" (KJV "from the beginning of the world").

### 403 Num 4:21-33 The duties of the Gershonites and Merarites.
- flag: "estaremos com o Senhor para sempre" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Ts 4:17 (unlisted); Henry's "shall be" has no subject, read as "we".

### 405 Num 5:1-10 The unclean to be removed out of the camp, Restitution to be made for trespasses.
- quoted (James 3:17): “from above is first pure, then peaceable” → “a sabedoria do alto é primeiramente pura, depois pacífica”
- quoted (Num 5:6): “a trespass against the Lord” → “transgressão contra o SENHOR”

### 406 Num 5:11-31 The trial of jealousy.
- quoted (2Sam 2:26): “will be bitterness in the latter end” → “ao fim se segue amargura”
- quoted (Rom 2:16): “judge the secrets of men” → “julgará os segredos dos seres humanos”
- flag: The English has "2 In particular" without a full stop; kept.
- flag: Rm 2:16 in the Bíblia Livre says "dos seres humanos" for KJV "of men".

### 407 Num 6:1-21 The law concerning the Nazarites.
- quoted (Judg 13:14): “drink wine or strong drink, nor eat” → “vinho nem bebida forte”
- flag: The listed verse is Jz 13:14 (Samson's mother); Henry is paraphrasing Nm 6:3. Only "vinho nem bebida forte" is kept from the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: Henry's comparison with Roman Catholic religious orders is translated as written, without softening.
- flag: The English ends a sentence with a comma before "Let us not forget"; a full stop is used.

### 408 Num 6:22-27 The form of blessing the people.
- translated, not quoted (2Sam 6:18; Henry describes the priests' duty in his own words; not a quotation of 2 Sm 6:18.): “the people in the name of the Lord”
- quoted (Rom 16:20): “the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ” → “graça de nosso Senhor Jesus Cristo”
- quoted (2Cor 13:14): “the communion of the Holy Ghost” → “a comunhão do Espírito Santo”
- flag: "The name Jehovah is three times repeated": the Bíblia Livre writes "SENHOR" three times in Nm 6:24-26; "Jeová" is kept as Henry's name.

### 409 Num 7:1-9 The offerings of the princes at the dedication of the tabernacle.
- quoted (Num 7:5): “the service of the tabernacle” → “o serviço do tabernáculo”

### 410 Num 7:10-89 The offerings of the princes at the dedication of the altar.
- flag: "o mesmo ontem, hoje, e eternamente" follows the Bíblia Livre's Hb 13:8 (unlisted).

### 411 Num 8:1-4 The lamps of the sanctuary.
- quoted (2Pet 1:19): “in a dark place” → “uma luz que ilumina em lugar escuro”

### 412 Num 8:5-26 Consecration of the Levites, and their service.
- quoted (Isa 52:11): “clean that bear the vessels of the Lord” → “Purificai-vos vós que levais os vasos do SENHOR”
- quoted (Num 8:7): “water of purifying upon them” → “sobre eles a água da expiação”
- quoted (Num 8:15): “to do the service of the tabernacle” → “a ministrar no tabernáculo do testemunho”
- flag: Henry's "They must be clean that bear the vessels of the Lord" is given as the Bíblia Livre's imperative of Is 52:11.
- flag: Nm 8:7 in the Bíblia Livre says "a água da expiação" for KJV "water of purifying"; Nm 8:15 "ministrar no tabernáculo do testemunho" for "do the service of the tabernacle".

### 413 Num 9:1-14 Of the Passover.
- quoted (Gal 6:7): “Be not deceived: God is not mocked” → “Não vos enganeis: Deus não se deixa escarnecer”
- flag: "a sabbath" here is the Christian Lord's day in Henry's sense; kept as "um sábado" as he writes it.

### 414 Num 9:15-23 The removals of the Israelites.
- quoted (Num 9:18): “As long as the cloud” → “todos os dias que a nuvem estava sobre o tabernáculo, eles estavam parados”
- quoted (Num 9:17): “When the cloud was taken up” → “se erguia a nuvem do tabernáculo”
- quoted (Num 9:18): “At the commandment of the Lord” → “À ordem do SENHOR”
- quoted (James 1:21): “word, which is able to save your souls” → “recebei com mansidão a palavra implantada em vós … que pode salvar as vossas almas”
- flag: Henry's "As long as the cloud … so long they continued" is given with the Bíblia Livre's whole clause of Nm 9:18 ("todos os dias que a nuvem estava sobre o tabernáculo, eles estavam parados").
- flag: The English has "his counsel. Psa 73:24, even unto death" with a full stop; kept.
- flag: "a casa terrestre deste tabernáculo" follows the Bíblia Livre's 2 Co 5:1 (unlisted).

### 416 Num 10:11-28 The Israelites remove from Sinai to Paran.
- quoted (Num 10:13): “took their journey according to the commandment of the Lord” → “ao dito do SENHOR”
- quoted (Num 10:12): “out of the wilderness of Sinai” → “do deserto de Sinai”
- quoted (Num 10:12): “rested in the wilderness of Paran” → “no deserto de Parã”

### 418 Num 10:33-36 The blessing pronounced by Moses.
- quoted (Num 10:35): “when the ark set forward” → “em movendo a arca”
- quoted (Num 10:35): “let thine enemies be scattered” → “Levanta-te … e sejam dissipados teus inimigos”
- quoted (Deut 33:29): “Happy art thou, O Israel! who is like unto thee, O people” → “Bem-aventurado és tu, ó Israel! Quem é como tu, povo”
- flag: Henry's quotation of Nm 10:35 leaves out "O Lord"; the Bíblia Livre's "Levanta-te, o SENHOR, e sejam dissipados teus inimigos" is shortened with "…".
- flag: Dt 33:29 is cut after "povo", as Henry stops at "O people!" (the Bíblia Livre goes on "povo salvo pelo SENHOR").

### 422 Num 11:16-23 Elders appointed to divide the charge. Flesh meat promised.
- quoted (Num 11:23): “whether my word shall come to pass” → “verás se a minha palavra te acontecerá ou não”
- flag: The English numbers the points "I." and then "2."; kept as written.

### 423 Num 11:24-30 The Spirit rests on the elders.
- quoted (Num 11:26): “went not out unto the tabernacle” → “não haviam saído à tenda”
- translated, not quoted (Num 11:26, Num 11:27; Henry's "in the camp, and there" is his own sentence; only "no acampamento" of the Bíblia Livre fits, too short for a quotation.): “in the camp, and there”
- quoted (Num 11:29): “all the Lord's people were prophets” → “todos do povo do SENHOR fossem profetas”
- quoted (Num 11:29): “would put his Spirit upon” → “pusesse seu Espírito sobre”
- flag: Nm 11:26 in the Bíblia Livre says "tenda" for KJV "tabernacle".
- flag: "sopra onde quer" follows the Bíblia Livre's Jo 3:8 (unlisted).

### 424 Num 11:31-35 Quails are given.
- flag: "a comida que perece… a comida que permanece para vida eterna" follows the Bíblia Livre's Jo 6:27 (unlisted).

### 425 Num 12:1-9 God rebukes the murmuring of Aaron and Miriam.
- quoted (Matt 11:11, Matt 11:12): “he that is least in the kingdom of heaven, is greater than he” → “o menor no Reino dos céus é maior que ele”
- quoted (2Pet 2:10): “are not afraid to speak evil of dignities” → “medo de insultar as coisas dignas de glórias”
- flag: 2 Pe 2:10 in the Bíblia Livre: "insultar as coisas dignas de glórias" for KJV "speak evil of dignities".

### 426 Num 12:10-16 Miriam struck with leprosy, and healed at the prayer of Moses.
- flag: Henry's quotation "Father, forgive them" given in the Bíblia Livre's words of Lc 23:34 ("Pai, perdoa-lhes").
- flag: "Her foul tongue… a foul face" (Bishop Hall): "imunda" keeps the wordplay with the leprosy's uncleanness.

### 427 Num 13:1-20 Twelve men sent to search the land of Canaan, Their instructions.
- flag: "Be of good courage" given as the Bíblia Livre's "esforçai-vos" (Nm 13:20, unlisted).

### 429 Num 13:26-33 Their account of the land.
- flag: Henry's point ("not… conquer it; but… possess it") rests on the KJV's wording of Nm 13:30; the Bíblia Livre has "Subamos logo, e passemos a ela", so his contrast is translated.
- flag: "ao que crê" echoes the Bíblia Livre's Mc 9:23.

### 430 Num 14:1-4 The people murmur at the account of the spies.
- quoted (2Cor 7:10): “The sorrow of the world worketh death” → “a tristeza do mundo opera a morte”

### 431 Num 14:5-10 Joshua and Caleb labour to still the people.
- flag: "o seu amparo se afastara deles" follows the Bíblia Livre's Nm 14:9 ("seu amparo se afastou deles"; KJV "their defence is departed from them").

### 433 Num 14:20-35 The murmurers forbidden to enter the promised land.
- quoted (Num 14:34): “Ye shall know my breach of promise” → “conhecereis meu castigo”
- quoted (Num 14:31): “said should be a prey, them will I bring” → “dissestes que seriam por presa, eu os introduzirei”
- flag: Henry's comment builds on the KJV's "my breach of promise" (Nm 14:34); the Bíblia Livre has "meu castigo", so "the causes of it… the consequences of it" are applied to "castigo".

### 434 Num 14:36-39 Death of the evil spies.
- quoted (Num 14:36): “a slander upon the land” → “trazendo um mau relato daquela terra”
