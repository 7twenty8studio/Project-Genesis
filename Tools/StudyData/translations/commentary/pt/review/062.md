# To review: pt commentary batch 062

### 2821 Hos 5:8-15 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Mt 3:7: "fugir da ira futura" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: the English has a stray semicolon ("Many a nation; as well as many a person"); rendered as a comma.
- flag: "with him is plenteous redemption" alludes to Ps 130:7 (Bíblia Livre "com ele muito resgate"); translated to fit Henry's sentence.

### 2822 Hos 6:1-3 
- quoted (Hos 6:3): “as the latter and former rain unto the earth” → “como a chuva, como a chuva da primavera, que rega a terra”
- flag: the Bíblia Livre has "como a chuva, como a chuva da primavera" for the KJV's "as the latter and former rain"; Henry's next sentence ("both the latter and the former rain") is translated "a chuva serôdia como a temporã".

### 2823 Hos 6:4-11 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hos 6:6: "misericórdia, e não sacrifício" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 2825 Hos 7:8-16 
- quoted (Ps 51:10): “a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within” → “um coração puro, ó Deus; e renova um espírito firme”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hos 7:8: "um bolo que não foi virado" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: Ps 51:10 is turned by Henry into "in us ... within us"; "em nós ... dentro de nós" kept outside the quoted words.

### 2826 Hos 8:1-4 
- flag: "My God, I know thee" echoes Hos 8:2 (Bíblia Livre "Deus meu, nós, Israel, te conhecemos"); Henry's singular wording translated.

### 2827 Hos 8:5-10 
- translated, not quoted (Hos 8:7; Henry follows the KJV ("the bud shall yield no meal") and changes meal to fruit; the Bíblia Livre ("nem a produção dará farinha") words it too differently for his sentence.): “the bud shall yield no”
- quoted (Rom 6:21): “the end of those things is death” → “o fim delas é a morte”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hos 8:9: "um jumento selvagem solitário" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 2829 Hos 9:1-6 
- quoted (Hos 9:3): “dwell in the Lord's land” → “na terra do SENHOR”

### 2831 Hos 9:11-17 
- flag: the English begins with a stray full stop (". God departs"); kept as in the source.
- flag: sensitive: Henry presents "the scattered Jews" as a daily warning of God's wrath against those who neglect or abuse the gospel.

### 2832 Hos 10:1-8 
- flag: "hemlock" follows the KJV of Hos 10:4; the Bíblia Livre has "erva venenosa". "An empty vine" (Hos 10:1, KJV) is "uma vinha fértil" in the Bíblia Livre; Henry's reading kept.

### 2833 Hos 10:9-15 
- flag: the English "and be a broken and contrite spirit" is ungrammatical; rendered "e tenham um espírito quebrantado e contrito".
- flag: "reap according to mercy" follows the KJV of Hos 10:12; the Bíblia Livre has "colhei para vós bondade".

### 2834 Hos 11:1-7 
- quoted (Eccl 8:11): “fully set in them to do evil” → “está cheio de vontade neles, para fazer o mal”
- flag: "With bands of love; this word signifies stronger cords than the former" depends on the KJV of Hos 11:4 ("cords of a man, with bands of love"); the Bíblia Livre has "cordas" for both, so "laços de amor" is used here to keep Henry's contrast.

### 2835 Hos 11:8-12 
- quoted (1John 1:9): “He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins” → “ele é fiel e justo para nos perdoar os pecados”
- flag: the English has "such us despise Him", a typo for "such as"; translated as "os que o desprezam". "The religious service of Israel were" (number slip) rendered in the singular.

### 2836 Hos 12:1-6 
- quoted (Hos 12:3): “by his strength he had power with God” → “em sua força lutou com Deus”

### 2837 Hos 12:7-14 
- flag: "the word also signifies a Canaanite": Henry explains the Hebrew behind Hos 12:7 (Bíblia Livre "O mercador").

### 2838 Hos 13:1-8 
- translated, not quoted (Hos 13:2; Henry builds on the KJV's command ("Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves"); the Bíblia Livre has an exclamation ("As pessoas que sacrificam beijam bezerros!"), which cannot carry his "in token of".): “Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves”

### 2840 Hos 14:1-3 
- flag: "Take away iniquity" and "receive ... graciously" follow the KJV of Hos 14:2; the Bíblia Livre has "Tira toda a maldade, e aceita o que é bom", so Henry's words are translated.

### 2841 Hos 14:4-8 
- translated, not quoted (Hos 14:5; Henry turns God's "he shall grow" into "they shall grow" in a series of his own sentences; the Bíblia Livre ("ele florescerá como lírio") cannot stand in the plural.): “shall grow as the lily”
- quoted (Hos 14:8): “What have I to do any more with idols” → “o que mais eu tenho a ver com os ídolos”
- quoted (Hos 14:8): “tree; From me is thy fruit found” → “de mim vem o teu fruto”
- flag: "Whom God loves, he loves freely" echoes Hos 14:4 (Bíblia Livre "eu os amarei voluntariamente"); translated in Henry's words.
- flag: "as the root of a tree": the KJV's "fir tree" in Hos 14:8 is "faia verde" in the Bíblia Livre.

### 2842 Hos 14:9-9 
- quoted (1Pet 2:8): “a Stone of stumbling, and a Rock of offence” → “pedra de tropeço, e pedra que causa queda”
- flag: the Bíblia Livre's "pedra que causa queda" stands for the KJV's "Rock of offence", so Henry's capitals (Stone, Rock) are not kept inside the quotation.

### 2846 Joel 2:1-14 
- flag: "the wrath to come" (Mt 3:7) rendered "ira futura" as in the Bíblia Livre; "fasting, weeping, and mourning" uses the Bíblia Livre's "jejum, choro e pranto" (Jl 2:12).

### 2847 Joel 2:15-27 
- flag: "Spare us, good Lord" is the Book of Common Prayer's Litany, not Joel 2:17's wording; translated.

### 2850 Joel 3:9-17 
- flag: "The acceptable year of the Lord" alludes to Is 61:2 (Bíblia Livre "o ano do favor do SENHOR"); translated as Henry's own phrase.

### 2851 Joel 3:18-21 
- translated, not quoted (Joel 3:18; a common phrase in Henry's own sentence, applied to the heavenly temple ("the house of the Lord above"), not a quotation of Joel 3:18.): “the house of the Lord”

### 2853 Amos 2:1-8 Judgments against Moab and Judah.
- quoted (Amos 2:4): “despised the law of the Lord” → “rejeitaram a lei do SENHOR”
- flag: the KJV's "despised the law of the Lord" is "rejeitaram a lei do SENHOR" in the Bíblia Livre; "Os de Judá" makes Henry's "Judah" plural to fit the verse.

### 2855 Amos 3:1-8 Judgments against Israel.
- flag: Henry's distinction between natural and moral evil answers the KJV of Am 3:6 ("shall there be evil in a city"); the Bíblia Livre already reads "calamidade".

### 2856 Amos 3:9-15 The like to other nations.
- quoted (Rom 11:5): “a remnant, according to the election of grace” → “um remanescente, escolhido pela graça”
