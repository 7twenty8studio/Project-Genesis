# To review: pt commentary batch 037

### 1427 Ps 18:29-50 He gives to God the glory of all his mighty deeds.
- flag: The English is garbled here, its phrases shuffled: "Learn that we must seek release being made through Christ, shall be rejected. In David the type, we behold out of trouble through Christ. The prayer put up, without reconciliation Jesus our Redeemer…". It probably meant "Learn that we must seek release out of trouble through Christ. The prayer put up, without reconciliation being made through Christ, shall be rejected. In David the type, we behold Jesus our Redeemer…". Translated as it stands, the broken order included.

### 1428 Ps 19:1-6 The glory of God's works.
- quoted (Ps 19:1): “declare the glory of God” → “Os céus declaram a glória de Deus”
- flag: "They have no speech or language, so some read it": Henry gives a reading of Sl 19:3 other than the KJV's; the Bíblia Livre has the KJV's sense ("Não há língua, nem palavras, onde não se ouça a voz deles"), so his alternative is translated.

### 1429 Ps 19:7-10 His holiness and grace as shown in his word.
- quoted (Ps 19:8): “of the Lord are right” → “Os preceitos do SENHOR são corretos”
- quoted (Ps 19:9): “The judgments of the Lord” → “Os juízos do SENHOR”
- flag: "The statues of the Lord" in the English, a slip for "statutes" (Sl 19:8); the Bíblia Livre's "Os preceitos do SENHOR são corretos" used.
- flag: "The pleasure of sense soon surfeit" (sic, singular noun with plural verb) rendered as plural "Os prazeres dos sentidos".
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 19:9: "O temor ao SENHOR", "é limpo" and "permanece para sempre" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: "The word translated \"law\"": the Bíblia Livre also has "lei" in Sl 19:7.
- flag: "The commandments of the Lord are pure" translated (the Bíblia Livre's Sl 19:8 has the singular "o mandamento do SENHOR é puro").

### 1430 Ps 19:11-14 Prayer for the benefit of them.
- flag: "Surely in the Lord Jesus have I righteousness and strength" adapts Is 45:24 ("Certamente no SENHOR há justiça e poder" in the Bíblia Livre); Henry adds "Jesus", so translated.
- flag: "Redeemer or Divine Kinsman": Henry alludes to the Hebrew go'el (Sl 19:14); "Parente divino" for "Kinsman".

### 1431 Ps 20:1-9 This psalm is a prayer for the kings of Israel, but with relation to Christ.
- quoted (Jer 26:16): “in the name of the Lord our God” → “em nome do SENHOR nosso Deus”
- quoted (Ps 20:6): “the saving strength of his right hand” → “o poder salvador de sua mão direita”
- flag: sensitive: "the pride and power of Jewish unbelief, and pagan idolatry" translated as it stands.
- flag: "risen-from the dead" has a stray hyphen in the English; ignored.
- flag: Quotation 0 is listed from Jr 26:16, but Henry echoes Sl 20:5 ("in the name of our God we will set up our banners"); the words used are those of the listed verse, which fit his phrase exactly.

### 1432 Ps 21:1-6 Thanksgiving for victory.
- quoted (Ps 21:6): “thou hast made him exceeding glad” → “tu fazes abundante a alegria dele”
- flag: "God kingdom" in the English, a slip for "God's kingdom"; translated "reino de Deus".
- flag: Henry explains the KJV's "prevented" (Sl 21:3, "thou preventest him") as "went before"; rendered "preveniu, ou foi adiante de". The Bíblia Livre has "tu foste até ele".
- flag: Henry's "countenance" (approval) rendered "favor" after the quoted words; the Bíblia Livre's verse ends "com tua face".

### 1433 Ps 21:7-13 Confidence of further success.
- flag: A sentence begins with a lower-case "yet" after an exclamation mark in the English; kept lower case.

### 1434 Ps 22:1-10 Complaints of discouragement.
- quoted (1Pet 1:11): “the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow” → “dos sofrimentos de Cristo e das glórias seguintes”
- quoted (Ps 22:1): “Why hast thou forsaken me” → “Por que me desamparaste”
- flag: "To cry our" in the English, a slip for "To cry out"; translated "Clamar".
- flag: 1 Pe 1:11 in the Bíblia Livre has the plural "glórias".

### 1435 Ps 22:11-21 With prayer for deliverance.
- quoted (Phil 3:10): “the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings” → “o poder de sua ressurreição e a comunhão em seus sofrimentos”
- flag: Henry's "They pierced his hands and his feet" (Sl 22:16) translated in his own third person.

### 1436 Ps 22:22-31 Praises for mercies and redemption.
- quoted (Ps 22:30): “A seed shall serve him” → “A descendência o servirá”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “fugir da ira futura”
- flag: "the ungodly professor": Henry's "professor" (one who professes religion) rendered "o professo ímpio".
- flag: The English ends a question ("inquire, why … if their own doings could atone for sin?") with a question mark inside reported speech; given a full stop.

### 1437 Ps 23:1-6 Confidence in God's grace and care.
- quoted (Ps 23:1): “The Lord is my shepherd” → “O SENHOR é meu pastor”
- quoted (Ps 23:3): “in the paths of righteousness” → “pelos caminhos da justiça”
- quoted (Ps 23:4): “The valley of the shadow of death” → “O vale da sombra da morte”
- flag: "unless. God lead us" in the English has a stray full stop; kept as "a não ser que. Deus".
- flag: "The work of righteousness in peace." is a fragment in the English (cf. Is 32:17); translated as it stands.
- flag: "green pastures" and "still waters" (Sl 23:2) given as "pastos verdes" and "águas quietas", the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: "the paths of righteousness" is "caminhos da justiça" in the Bíblia Livre, so Henry's "In these paths" becomes "Nestes caminhos".

### 1438 Ps 24:1-6 Concerning the kingdom of Christ, and the subjects of that kingdom.
- quoted (Mark 10:17): “What shall I do, that I may” → “que farei para”
- quoted (Ps 24:5): “blessing from the Lord, and righteousness from the God” → “a bênção do SENHOR, e a justiça do Deus de”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 24:1: "a terra e a sua plenitude" close to the Bíblia Livre ("a terra, e sua plenitude").
- flag: Sl 24:5 in the Bíblia Livre ends "de sua salvação"; Henry's "our salvation" kept as "nossa salvação" after the quoted run.

### 1439 Ps 24:7-10 Concerning the King of that kingdom.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 24:8: "forte e poderoso; poderoso na guerra" in the Bíblia Livre's words ("guerra" for Henry's "battle").
- flag: Ap 3:20 ("Eis que eu estou à porta, e bato" in the Bíblia Livre) turned into Henry's third person; translated.

### 1441 Ps 25:8-14 Prayer for remission of sins.
- quoted (1Tim 1:15): “came into the world to save sinners” → “veio ao mundo para salvar os pecadores”
- quoted (Ps 25:10): “All the paths of the Lord” → “Todos os caminhos do SENHOR”
- quoted (Ps 25:10): “All the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth” → “Todos os caminhos do SENHOR são bondade e verdade”
- flag: Sl 25:10: the Bíblia Livre has "bondade e verdade" (quoted in the second place); in the first, where Henry's sentence breaks the verse with an explanation, his "mercy and truth" is translated "misericórdia e verdade". "paths" is "caminhos" in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: "guide in judgment" (Sl 25:9; the Bíblia Livre: "guiará os humildes ao bom juízo") translated in Henry's sentence.

### 1442 Ps 25:15-22 For help in affliction.
- quoted (Lam 3:26): “wait for the salvation of the Lord” → “tranquilo aguardar a salvação do SENHOR”
- translated, not quoted (Ps 32:8; Not a quotation of Sl 32:8 but Henry's own prayer ("appear before thee in the way which thou shalt choose"); the Bíblia Livre's "o caminho que deves seguir" would not fit.): “thee in the way which thou shalt”
- flag: "thou art the Lord our righteousness" (Jr 23:6; the Bíblia Livre: "O SENHOR é nossa justiça") translated in Henry's prayer.

### 1443 Ps 26:1-12 David, in this psalm, appeals to God touching his integrity.
- flag: "We are complete in him" (Cl 2:10; the Bíblia Livre: "vos tornais plenos nele") translated in Henry's first person.

### 1444 Ps 27:1-6 The psalmist's faith.

- flag: Sl 27:5: "tabernacle" is "tenda" in the Bíblia Livre.
- flag: Sl 27:1 ("O SENHOR é minha luz … a força da minha vida" in the Bíblia Livre) echoed in Henry's third person; translated.

### 1445 Ps 27:7-14 His desire toward God, and expectation from him.
- quoted (Ps 27:13): “see the goodness of the Lord” → “veria a bondade do SENHOR”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 27:8: "Buscai a minha face" in the Bíblia Livre's words; the answer "I will seek it" is translated ("Eu a buscarei"), since the Bíblia Livre's "Eu busco a tua face, SENHOR" is longer and present tense.

### 1446 Ps 28:1-5 A prayer in distress.
- quoted (Ps 28:5): “the works of the Lord” → “os atos do SENHOR”
- flag: Sl 28:5: "works" is "atos" in the Bíblia Livre.

### 1447 Ps 28:6-9 Thanksgiving for deliverance.
- quoted (Ps 28:7): “The Lord is my strength” → “O SENHOR é a minha força”
- quoted (Ps 28:9): “lift them up for ever” → “levanta-os para sempre”
- flag: The English is garbled: "we have the less benefit from the light of the sun, nor from the light of God's countenance, for others' sharing therein" (probably "we have not the less benefit … nor …"); translated as it stands ("temos o menos benefício … nem …").

### 1448 Ps 29:1-11 Exhortation to give glory to God.
- quoted (Ps 29:2): “in the beauty of holiness” → “na honra da santidade”
- quoted (Ps 29:2): “the Lord the glory due” → “ao SENHOR a glória de seu nome”
- flag: Sl 29:2: the Bíblia Livre has "na honra da santidade" for the KJV's "in the beauty of holiness"; Henry's own following sentence ("There is a beauty in holiness") keeps "beleza", so the link between the two is weaker.
- flag: Sl 29:2: the Bíblia Livre's "a glória de seu nome" lacks the KJV's "due".
- flag: "repentance faith, and love" (missing comma) and "the nature world" (for "natural world") in the English; the first kept as "arrependimento fé e amor", the second rendered "mundo natural".
- flag: "O Lord our God, thou art very great!" echoes Sl 104:1 ("ó SENHOR meu Deus, tu és grandioso" in the Bíblia Livre); translated in Henry's words.

### 1451 Ps 31:1-8 Confidence in God.
- translated, not quoted (Ps 31:7; Henry turns the psalmist's first person ("eu me alegrarei e ficarei cheio de alegria") into the third person.): “will be glad and rejoice”
- flag: "made his soul a free-will offering for sin" may echo Is 53:10 ("make his soul an offering for sin"); translated.

### 1452 Ps 31:9-18 Prayer in trouble.
- quoted (1Pet 2:15): “to silence the ignorance of foolish men” → “a ignorância dos homens tolos”
- flag: "his mercies's sake" in the English (sic); rendered "por amor das suas misericórdias".
- flag: "a man of sorrows … acquainted with grief" (Is 53:3; the Bíblia Livre: "homem de dores, e experiente em enfermidade"): "homem de dores" as in the Bíblia Livre, the rest translated.
