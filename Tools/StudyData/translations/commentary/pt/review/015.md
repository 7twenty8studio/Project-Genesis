# To review: pt commentary batch 015

### 468 Num 24:15-25 Balaam's prophecies.
- quoted (Num 24:16): “heard the words of God” → “ouviu os ditos do SENHOR”
- quoted (Num 24:16): “knew the knowledge of the Most High” → “o conhecimento do Altíssimo”
- flag: Nm 24:16: the Bíblia Livre has "os ditos do SENHOR" where Henry (KJV) has "the words of God".
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "todo olho o verá" (Ap 1:7), "amou o pagamento da injustiça" (2 Pe 2:15); "brands plucked out of the fire" (Zc 3:2) made plural by Henry, translated.
- flag: Henry's anti-papal reading ("Rome papal also; antichrist") kept as written.

### 476 Num 27:1-11 The daughters of Zelophehad apply for an inheritance, The law of inheritances.
- flag: Point 2 reads "And earnest desire" in the English (a slip for "An earnest desire"); translated as "E um desejo ardente".

### 478 Num 27:15-23 Joshua appointed to succeed Moses.
- quoted (Num 27:18): “a man in whom is the Spirit” → “homem no qual há espírito”
- quoted (John 1:17): “grace and truth came by Jesus Christ” → “a graça e a verdade foi feita por Jesus Cristo”
- flag: Nm 27:18: the Bíblia Livre writes "espírito" in lower case; Henry takes it as the Holy Spirit ("the Spirit of grace").
- flag: Jo 1:17: "a Lei foi dada por Moisés" (unlisted, same verse) is in the Bíblia Livre's words; its "a graça e a verdade foi feita" keeps the singular verb.

### 479 Num 28:1-8 Offerings, The daily sacrifice.
- quoted (Phil 2:17): “the sacrifice and service” → “o sacrifício e serviço”

### 480 Num 28:9-15 The offering on the sabbath and new moons.
- flag: "o Sol da justiça" is Ml 4:2 in the Bíblia Livre's words (unlisted).
- flag: Henry's "sabbath days" here are the Old Testament sabbaths, applied to Christian devotion; no Lord's-day note added since he doesn't name it.

### 481 Num 28:16-31 
- translated, not quoted (Eph 1:15; Henry's own words (repentance, faith in the Lord Jesus, and love), not a quotation of Ephesians 1:15): “faith in the Lord Jesus, and love”
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 482 Num 29:1-11 The offering at the feats of trumpets, and on the day of atonement.
- flag: The English title has "feats" (feast); the reference "Le 22:24" should be Lv 23:24 (the feast of trumpets); kept as in the source.
- flag: "para que o homem de Deus seja completo" is 2 Tm 3:17 in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "perfect"; unlisted).

### 483 Num 29:12-40 Offerings at the feast of tabernacles.
- quoted (Rev 1:5): “loved us and washed us from our sins in his own blood” → “que nos amou, e em seu sangue nos lavou de nossos pecados”

### 484 Num 30:1-2 Vows to be kept.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 485 Num 30:3-16 The cases wherein vows might be released.
- quoted (Esther 1:22): “every man should bear rule in his own house” → “senhor em sua casa”

### 487 Num 31:7-12 Balaam slain.
- quoted (Num 31:8): “slew the Kings of Midian” → “aos reis de Midiã”

### 489 Num 31:19-24 
- quoted (Num 31:19): “abide without the camp seven days” → “fora do acampamento sete dias”

### 492 Num 32:1-5 The tribes of Reuben and Gad request an inheritance on the east of Jordan.
- flag: Unlisted: "a cobiça dos olhos e a ostentação da vida" follows 1 Jo 2:16 in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 493 Num 32:6-15 Moses reproves the Reubenites and Gadites.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 494 Num 32:16-27 They explain their views, Moses consents.
- quoted (Num 32:17): “go ready armed before the children of Israel” → “diante dos filhos de Israel”
- quoted (Num 32:23): “Be sure your sin will find you out” → “sabei que vos alcançará vosso pecado”
- flag: Nm 32:23: the Bíblia Livre's "vos alcançará" (KJV "will find you out") is carried through Henry's play on "find out" ("alcançar" / "descobrir").

### 495 Num 32:28-42 They take possession of the land to the east of Jordan.
- quoted (Matt 5:16): “your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven” → “brilhe vossa luz diante das pessoas, para que vejam vossas boas obras, e glorifiquem ao vosso Pai, que está nos céus”

### 496 Num 33:1-49 Encampments of the Israelites.
- quoted (Num 33:1): “of the children of Israel” → “dos filhos de Israel”
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: "não temos aqui cidade permanente" (Hb 13:14) and "eis agora o tempo agradável, eis agora o dia da salvação" (2 Co 6:2).

### 497 Num 33:50-56 The Canaanites to be destroyed.
- flag: "pricks in their eyes, and thorns in their sides" (Nm 33:55, unlisted) is close to the Bíblia Livre ("aguilhões em vossos olhos, e por espinhos em vossos lados"); Henry's change of person kept, translated.

### 498 Num 34:1-15 The bounds of the promised land.
- flag: 160 and 50 "miles" kept as English miles (about 260 by 80 km).
- flag: Unlisted: Sl 37:16 in the Bíblia Livre's words around Henry's inserted clause ("o pouco que o justo tem" … "do que a riqueza de muitos perversos"); Sl 24:1 translated, as Henry builds it into a concessive clause.

### 500 Num 35:1-8 The cities of the Levites.
- quoted (Gal 6:6): “is taught in the word” → “aquele que é instruído na palavra”
- quoted (Gal 6:6): “in all good things” → “todas as boas coisas”

### 501 Num 35:9-34 The cities of refuge, The laws about murder.
- quoted (Num 35:25): “the death of the high priest” → “até que morra o sumo sacerdote”
- quoted (Zech 9:12): “to the strong hold, ye prisoners of hope” → “Voltai-vos à fortaleza, ó prisioneiros que tendes esperança”
- quoted (Heb 6:18): “the hope set before us” → “a esperança posta diante de nós”
- quoted (Acts 5:31): “to be a Prince and a Saviour” → “por Príncipe e Salvador, para dar … arrependimento e perdão de pecados”
- flag: Nm 35:25: the Bíblia Livre's "até que morra o sumo sacerdote" is used for Henry's "till the death of the high priest".
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: Jo 6:37, Jo 6:55 (adapted to "cuja carne"), Hb 3:12 ("coração mau e infiel") and Rm 2:11 ("não há acepção de pessoas").

### 503 Num 36:5-12 The daughters of Zelophehad are to marry in their own tribe.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 504 Num 36:13-13 Conclusion.
- quoted (Num 36:13): “in the plains of Moab” → “nos campos de Moabe”
- flag: Nm 36:13: the Bíblia Livre says "campos de Moabe" where other sections say "planícies de Moabe" for Henry's "plains of Moab".

### 505 Deut 1:1-8 The words Moses spake to Israel in the plains of Moab, The promise of Canaan.

### 507 Deut 1:19-46

- flag: "great and terrible wilderness" (Dt 1:19) follows the Bíblia Livre's "grande e temível deserto" (unlisted).

### 508 Deut 2:1-7 The Edomites to be spared.
- quoted (Isa 5:8): “be placed alone in the midst of the earth” → “no meio da terra”
- flag: Is 5:8: the Bíblia Livre's verse differs in form ("para que somente vós fiqueis como moradores no meio da terra"); only "no meio da terra" is quoted.
- flag: "Dominion is not founded in grace": Henry's maxim against claiming lands or rule on the ground of being God's people; rendered literally.

### 509 Deut 2:8-23 The Moabites and Ammonites to be spared.
- flag: Caphtorims / Avims: the Bíblia Livre's "caftoreus" / "aveus" (Dt 2:23) are used.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 510 Deut 2:24-37 The Amorites to be destroyed.
- flag: Unlisted, in the Bíblia Livre's words: Sl 24:1, Hb 11:10 ("a cidade que tem fundamentos firmes"), 1 Ts 1:10 ("da ira futura"); 2 Co 1:22 adapted to "em seus corações".
