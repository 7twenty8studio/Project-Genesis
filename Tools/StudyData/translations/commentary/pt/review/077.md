# To review: pt commentary batch 077

### 3315 John 2:1-11 The miracle at Cana.
- quoted (Luke 21:34): “overcharged with surfeiting and drunkenness” → “não venham a se encher de ressaca e embriaguez”
- flag: Source has "and make them comforts indeed" (for "makes"); translated as "e os torna".
- flag: sensitive: "the idolatry of after-ages, in giving undue honours to his mother" is aimed at the veneration of Mary (Roman Catholic practice); translated in full.

### 3316 John 2:12-22 Christ casts the buyers and sellers out of the temple.
- quoted (John 2:19): “In three days I will raise it up” → “em três dias o levantarei”
- quoted (John 2:22): “was risen from the dead, his disciples remembered” → “ressuscitou dos mortos, seus discípulos se lembraram”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jo 2:19: "Derrubai este Templo" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "Destroy ye this temple").
- flag: Source has "he has said this" (for "had said"); translated as past ("dissera").

### 3318 John 3:1-21 Christ's discourse with Nicodemus.
- quoted (John 3:8): “The wind bloweth where it listeth” → “O vento sopra onde quer”
- quoted (1Cor 2:14): “the things of the Spirit of God” → “as coisas que são do Espírito de Deus”
- quoted (John 3:16): “God so loved the world” → “Deus amou ao mundo de tal maneira”
- quoted (2Cor 5:19): “God was in Christ reconciling the world” → “Deus estava em Cristo reconciliando consigo ao mundo”
- quoted (Rom 5:5): “The love of God is shed abroad” → “O amor de Deus está derramado”
- flag: Source has "Something is wrong, whereby such a life begins" (probably for "wrought"); translated literally as "Algo está errado".
- flag: Source has "What Christ speak" (for "spake"); translated as present "fala".
- flag: "shapen in sin" / "shapen in iniquity" alludes to Sl 51:5 (Bíblia Livre "em perversidade fui formado"); rendered "formados em pecado" / "formados em perversidade".
- flag: Closing quotation "Depart from me, I know you not, ye workers of iniquity" blends Mt 7:23 and Lc 13:27; the Bíblia Livre words differ in both, so it is translated, Henry's quotation marks kept.
- flag: Explanation "The same word signifies both the wind and the Spirit" refers to the Greek pneuma, not to English; it holds in Portuguese only through the Greek.
