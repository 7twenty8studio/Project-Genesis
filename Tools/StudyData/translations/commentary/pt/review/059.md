# To review: pt commentary batch 059

### 2684 Ezek 7:16-22

### 2685 Ezek 7:23-27 
- quoted (Luke 10:42): “good part which shall not be taken away” → “parte boa, a qual não … será tirada”

### 2688 Ezek 8:13-18 
- quoted (Ezek 8:17): “lo, they put the branch to their nose” → “eis que põem o ramo em suas narinas”

### 2689 Ezek 9:1-4 
- flag: Henry plays on 'interest' (Christ 'has an interest in heaven', saints 'have an interest' in him): rendered 'tem influência no céu' / 'têm parte'.

### 2690 Ezek 9:5-11 
- flag: unlisted quotation of Jo 18:9 (Henry's 'Of all that thou hast given me, I have lost none'): Bíblia Livre's words used verbatim.

### 2692 Ezek 10:8-22 
- quoted (Isa 55:6): “the Lord while he may be found” → “ao SENHOR enquanto se pode achar … enquanto ele está perto”

### 2694 Ezek 11:14-21 
- quoted (Ezek 11:19): “will give them one heart” → “um mesmo coração”
- quoted (2Cor 5:17): “If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature” → “se alguém está em Cristo, uma nova criatura é”
- flag: 'one heart': the Bíblia Livre has 'um mesmo coração' (one and the same heart); Henry's gloss ('firmly fixed for God, and not wavering') translated as written.

### 2695 Ezek 11:22-25 
- quoted (1Sam 12:22): “the Lord will not forsake his people” → “o SENHOR não desamparará a seu povo”
- flag: Henry's concessive 'Though the Lord will not forsake his people, yet…' recast as two clauses so the Bíblia Livre's words stand verbatim.

### 2704 Ezek 15:1-8 
- quoted (Ezek 15:4): “it is cast into the fire” → “é posto no fogo”
- flag: 'é posto no fogo' (the Bíblia Livre's masculine, of the vine's wood) kept verbatim after the feminine 'videira'.

### 2709 Ezek 17:22-24 
- flag: 'which has borne so long, so large a sway': 'borne' read as 'borne sway' (held dominion), translated 'exerceu um domínio'.

### 2710 Ezek 18:1-20 
- quoted (Ezek 18:4): “The soul that sinneth it shall die” → “A alma que pecar, essa morrerá”
- flag: 'this man would not perish for his father's sins': 'this man' refers to the son; rendered 'esse filho' for clarity.

### 2711 Ezek 18:21-29 
- quoted (Ezek 18:28): “he shall surely live” → “certamente viverá”

### 2712 Ezek 18:30-32 
- quoted (Ezek 18:31): “a new heart and a new spirit” → “um novo coração e um novo espírito”

### 2715 Ezek 20:1-9 
- quoted (Ezek 20:1): “The elders of Israel” → “anciãos de Israel”
- flag: The second paragraph begins with a stray '. ' in the English; kept as given.

### 2716 Ezek 20:10-26 
- flag: The text begins with a stray '. ' in the English; kept as given.

### 2718 Ezek 20:33-44 
- flag: English has lower-case 'jews' here; translated 'judeus' (lower case is normal in Portuguese).

### 2720 Ezek 21:1-17 
- quoted (Ezek 21:19): “of the king of Babylon” → “rei da Babilônia”

### 2721 Ezek 21:18-27 
- flag: sensitive: 2721 Henry reads the 'overturnings of the Jewish nation to the present day' as the fulfilment of this judgment on the Jews; translated in full.

### 2723 Ezek 22:1-16 
- translated, not quoted (Ezek 22:2; The Bíblia Livre's wording here ('Por acaso julgarás tu… a cidade derramadora de sanguinária?') is a question with a garbled phrase; Henry's plain statement translated.): “judge the bloody city”

### 2724 Ezek 22:17-22 
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “fugir da ira futura”

### 2726 Ezek 23:1-49 
- flag: Henry's glosses of the names Aholah ('her own tabernacle') and Aholibah ('my tabernacle is in her') translated with 'tenda'; the Bíblia Livre spells the names Oolá and Oolibá.

### 2729 Ezek 25:1-7 
- flag: The second paragraph begins with a stray '. ' in the English; kept as given.

### 2730 Ezek 25:8-17 
- flag: 'Though one event seem to the righteous and wicked': the English is elliptical (Ec 9:2 'one event to the righteous'); read as 'though the same event seems to befall'.

### 2734 Ezek 27:26-36

- flag: unlisted quotation of Sl 146:5 ('the God of Jacob for their Help… whose hope is in the Lord their God'): Bíblia Livre's words used verbatim ('o Deus de Jacó como sua ajuda, cuja esperança está no SENHOR seu Deus').

### 2736 Ezek 28:20-26 
- flag: The text begins with a stray '. ' in the English; kept as given.

### 2737 Ezek 29:1-16 
- quoted (Ezek 29:15): “it shall be the basest of the kingdoms” → “será mais inferior que os outros reinos”
- flag: 'the basest of the kingdoms' (KJV): the Bíblia Livre has 'mais inferior que os outros reinos'.

### 2739 Ezek 30:1-19
