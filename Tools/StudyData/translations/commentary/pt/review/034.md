# To review: pt commentary batch 034

### 1317 Job 16:17-22 Job maintains his innocency.
- flag: "go the way whence we shall not return" follows Job 16:22 KJV; translated (the Bíblia Livre's wording there may differ).

### 1319 Job 17:10-16 His hope is not in life, but in death.
- translated, not quoted (Job 18:5; Job 18:5 in the Bíblia Livre reads "a luz dos perverso se apagará" (a slip, and the future tense): it can't stand in Henry's "when the hope of the wicked shall be put out".): “of the wicked shall be put out”
- flag: Henry's bed and the worm follow Job 17:13-14 KJV ("I have made my bed in the darkness"); the Bíblia Livre has "nas trevas estenderei minha cama".

### 1321 Job 18:5-10 Ruin attends the wicked.
- quoted (Prov 29:6): “In the transgression of an evil man there is a snare” → “Na transgressão do homem mau há uma armadilha”
- flag: "hunts for the precious life" (Prov 6:26 KJV) adapted from the Bíblia Livre's "anda à caça de uma alma preciosa".

### 1322 Job 18:11-21 The ruin of the wicked.
- quoted (Prov 10:7): “The memory of the just is blessed, but the name of the wicked shall rot” → “A lembrança do justo será uma bênção; mas o nome dos perversos apodrecerá”
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “fugir da ira futura”
- quoted (John 16:22): “shall rejoice, and your joy no man taketh” → “se alegrará, e ninguém tirará vossa alegria”
- flag: "king of terrors" (Job 18:14 KJV) rendered with the Bíblia Livre's "rei dos assombros".
- flag: source "the lord Jesus" (lower case) read as "the Lord Jesus".
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's "será uma bênção" for Henry's "is blessed".

### 1323 Job 19:1-7 Job complains of unkind usage.
- flag: Heb 12:3 KJV ("endured such contradiction of sinners against himself") translated; the Bíblia Livre has "suportou tal hostilidade dos pecadores contra si mesmo".

### 1325 Job 19:23-29 Job's belief in the resurrection.
- translated, not quoted (Job 19:28; the Bíblia Livre's Job 19:28 has "a raiz do problema", which in Henry's use (the root of the matter = a living principle of grace) would read "the root of the problem" and lose his point.): “the root of the matter”
- translated, not quoted (Job 19:28; as quote 0: "a raiz do problema" would turn the principle of grace into a problem.): “the root of the matter”
- translated, not quoted (Job 19:28; as quote 0: Henry means the essential belief they agreed in, not a problem.): “the root of the matter”
- flag: "the root of the matter" (Job 19:28 KJV) rendered "a raiz da questão" three times; the Bíblia Livre has "a raiz do problema".
- flag: Heb 11:14,16 alluded to ("declarou claramente que buscava a pátria melhor, isto é, a celestial"), adapted from the Bíblia Livre.

### 1327 Job 20:10-22 The ruin of the wicked.
- quoted (Job 20:22): “he shall be in straits” → “Estando cheio de sua fartura, ainda estará angustiado”
- flag: "the sins of his youth" follows Job 20:11 KJV; the Bíblia Livre's wording there may differ.

### 1328 Job 20:23-29 The portion of the wicked.
- quoted (Job 20:29): “This is the portion of a wicked man from God” → “Esta é a parte que Deus dá ao homem perverso”
- flag: "stand in awe and sin not" (Ps 4:4 KJV) translated; the Bíblia Livre has "Quando estiverdes perturbados, não pequeis".

### 1331 Job 21:17-26 The dealings of God's providence.
- flag: Gen 18:25, 1Sam 20:3 and Mark 9:44 alluded to, adapted from the Bíblia Livre ("O juiz de toda a terra", "há um passo entre mim e a morte", "seu verme não morre, e o fogo nunca se apaga").

### 1337 Job 23:1-7 Job complains that God has withdrawn.
- quoted (2Cor 5:19): “in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself” → “em Cristo reconciliando consigo ao mundo”

### 1338 Job 23:8-12 He asserts his own integrity.
- quoted (Job 32:2): “justified himself rather than God” → “justificava mais a si mesmo que a Deus”

### 1339 Job 23:13-17 The Divine terrors.
- flag: source typo "has began" read as "has begun".
- flag: Heb 12:5 KJV ("faint when thou art rebuked of him") alluded to and translated; the Bíblia Livre has "nem te canses de ser reprendido por ele".

### 1340 Job 24:1-12 Wickedness often unpunished.
- quoted (Job 24:12): “God layeth not folly to them” → “Deus não dá atenção ao erro”
- quoted (Jer 17:11): “riches, and not by right” → “riquezas, mas não com justiça”
- quoted (Jer 17:11): “at his end shall be a fool” → “em seu fim ele será tolo”
- flag: quote 0: Henry explains the KJV's "God layeth not folly to them" (Job 24:12) as God not yet showing their folly; the Bíblia Livre's "Deus não dá atenção ao erro" is near but has "erro" for "folly".
- flag: "ch. xxi" (Roman numerals in the source) kept as "cap. xxi".

### 1341 Job 24:13-17 The wicked shun the light.
- quoted (Rom 13:14): “provision for the flesh to fulfil the lusts” → “realizar os desejos da carne”
- flag: quote 0: the Bíblia Livre's Rom 13:14 has no "provision" ("não fiqueis pensando em realizar os desejos da carne"); Henry's "make provision for" rendered "se ocupam em".

### 1345 Job 26:5-14 Job acknowledges the power of God.
- quoted (Gen 1:2): “moved upon the face of the waters” → “se movia sobre a face das águas”
- quoted (Ps 33:6): “the breath of his mouth” → “o sopro de sua boca”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Job 38:2 ("obscurece o conselho com palavras sem conhecimento", Bíblia Livre), the verb put in the past.
- flag: Henry's "beautified" the heavens follows Job 26:13 KJV ("garnished"); the Bíblia Livre's wording there may differ.

### 1346 Job 27:1-6 Job protests his sincerity.
- flag: "taking away his judgment, and vexing his soul" (Job 27:2 KJV) adapted from the Bíblia Livre's "tirou meu direito … amargou minha alma".

### 1348 Job 27:11-23 The miserable end of the wicked.
- quoted (Matt 16:26): “what is a man profited if he” → “que proveito há para alguém, se ganhar o mundo todo”

### 1349 Job 28:1-11 Concerning wordly wealth.
- quoted (Prov 16:16): “How much better is it to get wisdom than gold” → “Obter sabedoria é tão melhor do que o ouro”
- flag: source title typo "wordly" read as "worldly".
- flag: "Go to the miners, thou sluggard" adapts Prov 6:6; shaped on the Bíblia Livre's "Vai até a formiga, preguiçoso; olha para os caminhos dela, e sê sábio".

### 1351 Job 28:20-28 Wisdom is the gift of God.
- flag: Job 28:28 KJV ("the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding") alluded to; adapted from the Bíblia Livre's "o temor ao Senhor é a sabedoria, e o desviar-se do mal".

### 1352 Job 29:1-6 Job's former comforts.
- flag: Henry's "candle" follows Job 29:3 KJV ("his candle shined upon my head"); the Bíblia Livre has "lâmpada".

### 1354 Job 29:18-25 His prospect of prosperity.
- flag: "the blessing of … ready to perish" echoes Job 29:13 (the Bíblia Livre: "A bênção do que estava a ponto de morrer").

### 1356 Job 30:15-31 Job a burden to himself.
- flag: Ps 103:13 and Heb 12:1 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's words ("assim como um pai se compadece dos filhos", "facilmente … envolve"), adapted.

### 1357 Job 31:1-8 Job declares his uprightness.
- quoted (Heb 4:12): “the thoughts and intents of the heart” → “os pensamentos e intenções do coração”
- flag: source typo "strict honestly" read as "strict honesty".

### 1360 Job 31:24-32 Job not guilty of covetousness or idolatry.
- flag: 1Tim 6:10 alluded to, adapted from the Bíblia Livre ("perfuraram a si mesmos com muitas dores").

### 1361 Job 31:33-40 Job not guilty of hypocrisy and violence.
- quoted (Prov 28:13): “his sins, shall not prosper” → “encobre suas transgressões nunca prosperará”
- quoted (Job 31:40): “The words of Job are ended” → “Aqui terminam as palavras de Jó”

### 1363 Job 32:6-14 He reproves them.
- flag: Jas 1:19 alluded to with the Bíblia Livre's "pronta para ouvir, tardia para falar", adapted to the plural.
