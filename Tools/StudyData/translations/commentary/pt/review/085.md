# To review: pt commentary batch 085

### 3570 Rom 7:7-13 The use and excellence of the law.
- quoted (Rom 7:12): “the law is holy, and the commandment holy” → “a Lei é santa, e o mandamento é santo, justo, e bom”

### 3573 Rom 7:23-25 
- quoted (Rom 7:25): “the law of God; but with the flesh” → “à Lei de Deus, mas, com a carne”
- quoted (Rom 7:23): “against the law of my mind” → “contra a lei do meu entendimento”
- flag: KJV "the law of my mind" / "with the mind" is "entendimento" in the Bíblia Livre (Rm 7:23, 25); Henry's "with my mind" rendered "com o meu entendimento" to match.

### 3574 Rom 8:1-9 The freedom of believers from condemnation.
- quoted (Rom 8:5): “the things of the Spirit” → “as coisas do Espírito”
- quoted (Rom 8:9): “Ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit” → “Vós não permaneceis na carne, mas sim, no Espírito”

### 3575 Rom 8:10-17 Their privileges as being the children of God.
- quoted (Rom 8:1): “walk, not after the flesh, but after the Spirit” → “segundo a carne, mas sim segundo o Espírito”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rm 8:15: "espírito de escravidão" and "Espírito de adoção" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: Quote 0: Henry's "to walk" (Bíblia Livre "andam", third person) is his own "andar"; the rest is the Bíblia Livre's run.

### 3576 Rom 8:18-25 Their hopeful prospects under tribulations.
- quoted (Rom 8:18): “the sufferings of this present time” → “as aflições deste tempo presente”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Hb 10:37: "aquele que vem virá" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "he that shall come will come").
- flag: KJV "sufferings" is "aflições" in the Bíblia Livre (Rm 8:18); Henry's own "sufferings" elsewhere rendered "sofrimentos".

### 3578 Rom 8:28-31 Their interest in the love of God.
- quoted (Rom 8:29): “Whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son” → “Aos que desde antes conheceu, também os predestinou para serem conformes à imagem do seu Filho”
- quoted (Rom 8:29): “be conformed to the image of his Son” → “conformes à imagem do seu Filho”
- quoted (Rom 8:30): “Whom he did predestinate, them he also called” → “Aos que predestinou, a esses também chamou”
- quoted (Rom 8:28): “called according to his purpose” → “chamados segundo o seu propósito”
- quoted (Rom 8:30): “Whom he called, them he also justified” → “Aos que chamou, a esses também justificou”
- quoted (Rom 8:30): “Whom he justified, them he also glorified” → “Aos que justificou, a esses também glorificou”
- quoted (Eph 3:19): “the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge” → “o amor de Cristo, que excede o entendimento”

### 3579 Rom 8:32-39 Their final triumph, through Christ.
- quoted (Rom 8:34): “Yea, rather that is risen again” → “Além disto é o que também ressuscitou”
- quoted (Rom 8:34): “at the right hand of God” → “à direita de Deus”
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rm 4:5: "crê naquele que torna justo o ímpio" in the Bíblia Livre's words (KJV "believe on him that justifieth the ungodly").
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rm 8:35: "Quem nos separará?" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: Source "You may even live to see and seek your parting" is obscure; translated literally ("viver para ver e buscar a vossa separação").

### 3580 Rom 9:1-5 The apostle's concern that his countrymen were strangers to the gospel.
- flag: Henry's quoted "accursed" follows the KJV of Rm 9:3; the Bíblia Livre reads "separado de Cristo"; translated as "maldito", his quotation marks kept.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Rm 9:5: "Deus bendito eternamente" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: sensitive: "the destruction about to come upon them for their obstinate unbelief" (the Jewish nation); translated in full.

### 3581 Rom 9:6-13 The promises are made good to the spiritual seed of Abraham.

- flag: sensitive: "The rejection of the Jews by the gospel dispensation"; translated in full.

### 3582 Rom 9:14-24 Answers to objections against God's sovereign conduct, in exercising mercy and justice.
- quoted (Rom 9:24): “not of the Jews only” → “não somente dentre os judeus, mas também dentre os gentios”

### 3583 Rom 9:25-29 This sovereignty is in God's dealing both with Jews and Gentiles.
- flag: sensitive: "The rejecting of the Jews"; translated in full.

### 3584 Rom 9:30-33 The falling short of the Jews is owing to their seeking justification, not by faith, but by the works of the law.

### 3589 Rom 11:1-10 The rejection of the Jews is not universal.
- translated, not quoted (Rom 11:5; The Bíblia Livre renders it "um remanescente, escolhido pela graça"; Henry's next sentences build on the noun "election", so translated "segundo a eleição da graça".): “according to the election of grace”
- translated, not quoted (2Cor 8:1; Not a quotation: "the grace of God bestowed on" is Henry's own phrase; the listed 2 Co 8:1 is a chance match.): “of the grace of God bestowed on”
- flag: sensitive: "The Jewish nation were as in a deep sleep" and "the dreadful judgments of God upon them" (the Jews) for Christ's sufferings; translated in full.

### 3590 Rom 11:11-21 God overruled their unbelief for making the Gentiles partakers of gospel privileges.
- flag: sensitive: "the righteous rejection of the unbelieving Jews" and their relation to Abraham and to God "cut off"; translated in full.

### 3591 Rom 11:22-32 The Gentiles cautioned against pride and unbelief, The Jews shall be called as a nation, and brought into God's visible covenant again.
