# To review: pt commentary batch 021

### 730 Judg 13:8-14 The angel appears to Manoah.
- flag: "Blessed are those who have not seen, and yet… have believed" (Jo 20:29) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "bem-aventurados aqueles que não virem, e crerem".

### 731 Judg 13:15-23 Manoah's sacrifice.
- quoted (Heb 9:12): “by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place” → “pelo seu próprio sangue … ele entrou de uma vez por todas no Santuário”
- flag: In Hb 9:12 the Bíblia Livre puts "pelo seu próprio sangue" at the end of the verse; both runs are quoted verbatim in Henry's order.
- flag: "Certamente morreremos" follows the Bíblia Livre's Jz 13:22 (unlisted); "ajuda idônea" its Gn 2:18 (Henry's "help meet").

### 732 Judg 13:24-25 Birth of Samson.
- quoted (Judg 13:25): “The Spirit of the Lord began to move” → “O espírito do SENHOR começou a manifestar-se”
- flag: Henry's "to move" follows the KJV; the Bíblia Livre's Jz 13:25 has "manifestar-se", quoted verbatim.
- flag: "não fiqueis bêbados com vinho, mas enchei-vos do Espírito" follows the Bíblia Livre's Ef 5:18 (unlisted; its middle clause, which Henry leaves out, is left out).

### 734 Judg 14:5-9 Samson kills a lion.
- translated, not quoted (Judg 14:6; Not a quotation of Jz 14:6 here but Henry's own phrase "in the strength of the Spirit of the Lord"; the Bíblia Livre's lower-case "espírito" would read oddly in it.): “the Spirit of the Lord”
- flag: The English runs "find honey in the carcass abundant strength" with no stop; read as "in the carcass: abundant strength" and given a colon.
- flag: "how they exposed themselves" read as present ("how they expose").

### 735 Judg 14:10-20 Samson's riddle.
- quoted (Judg 14:14): “forth meat, and out of the strong” → “do comedor saiu comida … do forte saiu doçura”
- flag: Henry's present "comes forth" is given with the Bíblia Livre's past "saiu" (Jz 14:14).
- flag: "arasse com a novilha" follows the Bíblia Livre's Jz 14:18 ("arásseis com minha novilha", unlisted).

### 736 Judg 15:1-8 Samson is denied his wife, He smites the Philistines.
- flag: The text opens with a second heading ("Samson kills a thousand of the Philistines with a jaw-bone.") and a bare "(9-17)", kept as separate paragraphs as in the source.

### 737 Judg 15:9-17 
- translated, not quoted (Judg 3:10; Listed against Jz 3:10, but Henry is speaking of Jz 15:14, where the Bíblia Livre has "o espírito do SENHOR caiu sobre ele"; those words are used verbatim.): “the Spirit of the Lord came upon”
- quoted (2Cor 3:17): “where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty” → “onde está o Espírito do Senhor, aí há liberdade”
- flag: Quotation 0 uses the Bíblia Livre's Jz 15:14 ("o espírito do SENHOR caiu sobre"), not the listed Jz 3:10.
- flag: "We can do all things through Him that strengtheneth us" (Fp 4:13) translated from Henry's wording; the Bíblia Livre has "Posso todas as coisas em Cristo que me fortalece".

### 738 Judg 15:18-20 His distress from thirst.
- flag: The English has "attend God their praises", read as "attend God with their praises".
- flag: "Lehi" means "jaw-bone"; the pun is explained by "por causa da queixada".

### 740 Judg 16:4-17 Samson enticed to declare his strength lay.
- flag: The source's title lacks a word ("to declare his strength lay"); read as "to declare where his strength lay".
- flag: "take away the heart" (Os 4:11, KJV) rendered "tiram o entendimento", after the Bíblia Livre's wording there (unlisted).

### 743 Judg 16:25-31 He destroys many of the Philistines.
- flag: The English has "even thought it is", read as "even though it is".
- flag: "cujo sangue purifica de todo pecado" follows the Bíblia Livre's 1 Jo 1:7 (unlisted).

### 744 Judg 17:1-6 The beginning of idolatry in Israel, Micah and his mother.
- quoted (Judg 17:6, Judg 17:7): “Every man did that which was right in his own eyes” → “Cada um fazia como melhor lhe parecia”
- quoted (2Kings 13:2): “did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord” → “o que era mau aos olhos do SENHOR”
- flag: Henry's "see chap. Jud 20:28" kept as "ver cap. Jz 20:28" (the point is Phinehas, Eleazar's son, still serving).
- flag: Quotation 1 is a phrase common in Judges (Jz 2:11 etc.); it was matched to 2 Rs 13:2, whose Bíblia Livre wording "o que era mau aos olhos do SENHOR" is used (Jz 2:11 there has "mal").
