# To review: pt commentary batch 035

### 1364 Job 32:15-22 He speaks without partiality.
- flag: The English reads "set the wrath Lord always before us", apparently garbled (perhaps "the Lord"); rendered "pôr sempre diante de nós a ira do Senhor", keeping both words.

### 1368 Job 33:19-28 God sends afflictions for good.
- quoted (Job 33:27): “perverted that which was right” → “perverti o que era correto”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 73:22 ("So foolish have I been and ignorant") given in the Bíblia Livre's words "me comportei como tolo, e nada sabia".
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Mt 20:28 ("gave his life a ransom for many"): "a sua vida em resgate por muitos" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 1369 Job 33:29-33 Elihu entreats Job's attention.
- flag: A word is missing in the English ("kept back from the we shall bless"); probably "the pit" (Jó 33:30): supplied as "[cova]" in square brackets.

### 1370 Job 34:1-9 Elihu accuses Job of charging God with injustice.
- flag: "I have cleansed my hands in vain" (Sl 73:13) translated: the Bíblia Livre's wording ("purifiquei meu coração e lavei minhas mãos na inocência inutilmente") is longer than Henry's phrase.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1371 Job 34:10-15 God cannot be unjust.
- quoted (Prov 24:12): “render to every man according to his works” → “retribuirá ao homem conforme sua obra”
- flag: Henry's "God cannot do wickedness, nor the Almighty commit wrong" (Jó 34:10, worded differently in the Bíblia Livre: "longe de Deus esteja a maldade") translated.
- flag: "every man" in Henry; the Bíblia Livre's Pv 24:12 says "ao homem".

### 1373 Job 34:31-37 Elihu reproves Job.
- quoted (Ps 145:17): “is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works” → “é justo em todos os seus caminhos, e bondoso em todas as suas obras”
- flag: The English begins "Let will only oblige him", apparently a slip for "Elihu will"; rendered "Eliú só o obrigará".
- flag: Sl 145:17 in the Bíblia Livre has "bondoso" (kind) where the KJV Henry quotes has "holy".

### 1374 Job 35:1-8 Elihu speaks of man's conduct.
- flag: The English mixes tenses ("reproves … and called"); kept.

### 1375 Job 35:9-13 Why those who cry out under afflictions are not regarded.
- flag: The English lacks a word ("how to reconcile the justice of God", read as "reconcile with"); rendered "conciliar com".
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jó 35:10: "dá canções na noite" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: Echo of Is 59:1 ("the Lord's hand is shortened, or his ear heavy") translated in Henry's own sentence.

### 1376 Job 35:14-16 Elihu reproves Job's impatience.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jó 35:16: "abriu sua boca em vão, e multiplicou palavras sem conhecimento" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 1377 Job 36:1-4 Elihu desires Job's attention.
- flag: English corrected from an independent copy of Henry (source_fixes.json); translation updated to match.

### 1380 Job 36:24-33 The wonders in the works of creation.
- flag: A verb is missing in the English ("cause the Lord to his face"), probably "hide"; supplied as "[esconder]" in square brackets.

### 1381 Job 37:1-13 Elihu observes the power of God.
- quoted (Matt 3:7): “to flee from the wrath to come” → “fugir da ira futura”
- flag: The English ends its exclamation ("Oh that men would …") with a full stop; given an exclamation mark.

### 1382 Job 37:14-20 Job required to explain the works of nature.
- quoted (1Cor 13:10): “when that which is perfect is come” → “quando vier o que é completo”
- flag: 1 Co 13:10 in the Bíblia Livre reads "o que é completo" (with a marginal "Ou: perfeito" run into the text); "completo" used for Henry's "perfect".

### 1383 Job 37:21-24 God is great, and is to be feared.
- quoted (Job 37:22): “With God is terrible majesty” → “Em Deus há majestade temível”

### 1386 Job 38:12-24 Concerning the light and darkness.
- quoted (Job 38:14): “as clay to the seal” → “como barro sob o selo”
- quoted (Job 38:18): “the breadth of the earth” → “as larguras da terra”
- flag: Lc 1:78-79 ("the Day-spring from on high hath visited us, to give light to them that sit in darkness") echoed in Henry's sentence; given near the Bíblia Livre's words ("o raiar da manhã … nos visitou", "iluminar os que estão sentados nas trevas") but with "do alto" added as Henry has it, so treated as translation.

### 1388 Job 39:1-30 God inquires of Job concerning several animals.
- quoted (Jer 49:16): “I will bring thee down from thence, saith the Lord” → “de lá eu te derrubarei … diz o SENHOR”
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Jr 8:6 ("Every one turneth to his course, as the horse rusheth into the battle"): "Cada um se virou ao seu percurso, tal como cavalo que corre com ímpeto para a batalha" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
- flag: "The unicorn" is the KJV's word in Jó 39:9; kept as "o unicórnio" for Henry's description (the Bíblia Livre's own word may differ).
- flag: Henry's "the ostrich, which … is without natural affection" ("it" in the English for the ostrich) kept.

### 1389 Job 40:1-5 Job humbles himself to God.
- quoted (Job 40:2): “Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him” → “Por acaso quem briga contra o Todo-Poderoso pode ensiná-lo”
- quoted (Ps 29:4): “the voice of the Lord is powerful” → “a voz do SENHOR é poderosa”
- quoted (John 16:13): “the Spirit of truth is come, he” → “quando vier aquele Espírito de verdade, ele”
- flag: "vile" is the KJV's word in Jó 40:4; the Bíblia Livre has "insignificante" there. Henry's "vile" translated "vil".

### 1393 Job 42:1-6 Job humbly submits unto God.
- quoted (2Cor 3:18): “into the same image” → “segundo a mesma imagem”

### 1394 Job 42:7-9 Job intercedes for his friends.
- quoted (Matt 25:21): “Well done, thou good and faithful servant” → “Muito bem, servo bom e fiel”
- flag: The English has a comma where a sentence ends ("reconcile them, Those who differ"); given a full stop.
- flag: "he spake of God that which was right" (Jó 42:7): "o que era correto" matches the Bíblia Livre, but Henry turns the verse round, so the sentence is translated.
- flag: Echo of Is 53:12 / Hb 7:25 ("making intercession for the transgressors") translated in Henry's sentence.

### 1395 Job 42:10-17 His renewed prosperity.
- quoted (Prov 4:18): “more and more unto the perfect day” → “ilumina até o dia ficar claro por completo”
- flag: Henry's wordplay "warm devotions … warm disputes" rendered "devoções fervorosas … disputas acaloradas".
- flag: Pv 4:18: the Bíblia Livre has "que vai, e ilumina até o dia ficar claro por completo" for the KJV's "shineth more and more unto the perfect day"; "vai crescendo" is Henry's "more and more".

### intro Ps 
- flag: "mediating on his truths" in the English, a slip for "meditating"; rendered "meditando nas suas verdades".
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Tt 2:8 ("sound speech, that cannot be condemned"): "uma palavra sã e irrepreensível" in the Bíblia Livre's words.

### 1397 Ps 1:4-6 The sinfulness and misery of a wicked man, The ground and reason of both.
- quoted (Ps 1:4): “are like the chaff which the wind” → “são como a palha que o vento dispersa”
- flag: The English title joins two headings with a comma ("…wicked man, The ground…"); given a full stop.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 1:4 ("The ungodly are not so"): "Os maus não são assim" in the Bíblia Livre's words; elsewhere Henry's "ungodly" is "ímpios".
- flag: Echoes of Sl 1:1 ("conselho dos maus", "caminho dos pecadores" as in the Bíblia Livre; "seat of the scornful" translated "assento dos escarnecedores"), Mt 3:12 and 2 Co 5:17 ("tudo se fez novo", Bíblia Livre) worked into Henry's sentences.

### 1398 Ps 2:1-6 Threatenings against the enemies of Christ's kingdom.
- quoted (Eph 1:22): “Head over all things to the church” → “cabeça sobre todas as coisas para a Igreja”
- flag: The English has "in so opposing so powerful a kingdom"; rendered "em se oporem assim a um reino tão poderoso".

### 1399 Ps 2:7-9 Promise to Christ as the Head of this kingdom.
- quoted (Heb 5:5): “said unto him, Thou art my Son” → “lhe disse: Tu és meu Filho”
- flag: The English closes "my Sovereign'" with a stray apostrophe; left out as punctuation.
- flag: "the heathen" (Sl 2:8, KJV) rendered "os pagãos"; the Bíblia Livre has "as nações" there.

### 1400 Ps 2:10-12 Counsel to all, to espouse its interests.
- flag: Henry's play on "perish in the way … from the way" (Sl 2:12; the Bíblia Livre: "pereçais no caminho") rendered "pereçais no caminho … fora do caminho".
- flag: "love him much, as she did" (Lc 7:47) translated in Henry's words.

### 1401 Ps 3:1-3 David complains to God of his enemies, and confides in God.
- flag: Unlisted quotation of Sl 3:3 ("a shield for me"): "escudo para mim" in the Bíblia Livre's words.
