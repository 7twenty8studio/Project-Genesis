# To review: pt commentary batch 076

### 3283 Luke 20:27-38 Concerning the resurrection.
- quoted (Luke 20:35): “the resurrection from the dead” → “ressurreição dos mortos”

### 3285 Luke 21:1-4 Christ commends a poor widow.
- flag: 'two mites': rendered 'duas pequenas moedas' (the Bíblia Livre at Luke 21:2 has its own word for the coins)

### 3286 Luke 21:5-28 His prophecy.
- flag: sensitive: the destruction of Jerusalem and 'utter dispersion of the Jewish nation'; 'The scattered Jews around us preach the truth of Christianity'; 'When Christ came to destroy the Jews, he came to redeem the Christians that were persecuted and oppressed by them'; 'So fully did the Divine judgements come upon the Jews' (3286)
- flag: 'thirty-eight years': written in words in the English, kept in words ('trinta e oito anos')

### 3287 Luke 21:29-38 Christ exhorts to watchfulness.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 21:36; Henry turns the verse to the first person plural ('we'); the Bíblia Livre's 'sejais considerados dignos de escaparem' is second person): “may be accounted worthy to escape all”
- flag: sensitive: 'the ruin of the Jewish nation', with Henry's note that 'this race and family of Abraham' shall survive as a nation (3287)
- flag: 'Take heed to yourselves' (Luke 21:34) and 'Watch therefore, and pray always' (Luke 21:36, the Bíblia Livre: 'vigiai sempre, orando') translated in Henry's words

### 3290 Luke 22:19-20 The Lord's supper instituted.
- flag: 'rememberance': typo in the English for 'remembrance'; translated 'em memória'

### 3291 Luke 22:21-38 Christ admonishes the disciples.
- quoted (Phil 2:7): “took upon him the form of a servant” → “tomando a forma de servo”
- quoted (1Pet 1:5): “are kept by the power of God” → “guardados no poder de Deus”
- quoted (Luke 22:36): “a purse, let him take it” → “quem tem bolsa, tome-a”

### 3292 Luke 22:39-46 Christ's agony in the garden.
- quoted (Luke 22:44): “agony, he prayed more earnestly” → “Estando em angústia, orava mais intensamente”
- quoted (Luke 22:44): “his sweat was as it were great drops of blood falling down” → “seu suor se fez como gotas de sangue, que desciam”
- flag: Henry's 'agony' is the KJV's word at Luke 22:44; the Bíblia Livre has 'angústia', kept in the quotation (point 2), while Henry's own 'agony' is 'agonia' elsewhere
- flag: Q1: the Bíblia Livre has no word for Henry's (KJV) 'great' ('great drops'), and goes on 'até o chão', which Henry leaves out; quoted only as far as he does
- flag: unlisted quotation of Luke 22:43: the Bíblia Livre's 'apareceu-lhe um anjo do céu, que o fortalecia' used verbatim (point 1)

### 3293 Luke 22:47-53 Christ betrayed.
- translated, not quoted (Luke 22:53; the Bíblia Livre's 'a vossa hora, e sob a autoridade das trevas' would not give Henry's phrase 'the hour and the power of darkness' as one subject): “hour and the power of darkness”

### 3294 Luke 22:54-62 The fall of Peter.
- quoted (Luke 22:61): “The Lord turned and looked upon Peter” → “o Senhor, virando-se, olhou para Pedro”
- flag: 'the letting forth of water' (Prov 17:14, KJV) translated in Henry's words; the Bíblia Livre has 'deixar águas rolarem'

### 3295 Luke 22:63-71 Christ confesses himself to be the Son of God.
- quoted (Heb 12:3): “endured such contradiction of sinners against himself” → “aquele que suportou tal hostilidade dos pecadores contra si mesmo”
- flag: Q0: the Bíblia Livre's 'hostilidade' stands for Henry's (KJV) 'contradiction'

### 3298 Luke 23:13-25 Barabbas preferred to Christ.
- quoted (Luke 23:25): “He delivered Jesus to their will” → “a Jesus lhes entregou à sua vontade”

### 3300 Luke 23:32-43 The crucifixion, The repentant malefactor.
- flag: unlisted quotation of Luke 23:42: the Bíblia Livre's 'Senhor, lembra-te de mim' used verbatim
- flag: 'a brand out of the burning' (Zech 3:2, KJV) rendered close to the Bíblia Livre's 'um tição tirado do fogo'

### 3302 Luke 23:50-56 The burial of Christ.
- flag: Henry moves from the women's sabbath to 'the day of sacred rest, which is the Lord's day' (o dia do Senhor); he doesn't say 'Christian sabbath' here, so no bracketed note was added

### 3303 Luke 24:1-12 The resurrection of Christ.
- quoted (Luke 24:2): “they found the stone rolled away” → “acharam a pedra já revolvida”
- flag: 'Peter now ran to the sepulchre, who so lately ran from his Master': Henry's play on 'ran to' / 'ran from' rendered 'correu ao sepulcro' / 'fugira do seu Mestre'

### 3305 Luke 24:28-35 And makes himself known to them.
- quoted (Luke 24:30): “He took bread, and blessed it, and brake, and gave to them” → “tomou o pão, o benzeu, o partiu, e o deu a eles”
- translated, not quoted (Luke 24:35; Henry turns the verse into a present statement about the Lord's supper ('is known to them in breaking of bread'); the Bíblia Livre's 'foi reconhecido por eles quando partiu o pão' is a past narrative): “them in breaking of bread”

### 3307 Luke 24:50-53 His ascension.
- quoted (Luke 24:50): “He lifted up his hands, and blessed them” → “levantando suas mãos, os abençoou”
- quoted (Luke 24:52): “returned to Jerusalem with great joy” → “voltaram para Jerusalém com grande alegria”
- flag: 'Even so, Lord Jesus, come quickly' joins Rev 22:20's 'Even so, come, Lord Jesus' with its 'I come quickly' (unlisted); translated as Henry wrote it; the Bíblia Livre has 'Sim, vem, Senhor Jesus!'

### intro John 
- flag: 'A. D. 97' rendered 'por volta do ano 97 d.C.' (Henry's 'about')

### 3308 John 1:1-5 The Divinity of Christ.
- quoted (John 1:1): “The Word was with God” → “a Palavra estava junto de Deus”
- quoted (John 1:3): “All things were made by him” → “Por esta foram feitas todas as coisas”
- quoted (John 1:3): “Without him was not any thing made that was made” → “sem ela não se fez coisa nenhuma do que foi feito”
- flag: 'the Word': the Bíblia Livre's 'a Palavra' (feminine, with 'esta' / 'ela' at John 1:3) used throughout, so 'Por esta' and 'Sem ela' stand for Henry's (KJV) 'by him' / 'without him'; Henry's own pronouns for Christ stay masculine ('ele')
- flag: 'the darkness comprehends it not' (John 1:5): Henry's present tense kept, 'as trevas não a compreendem' (the Bíblia Livre: 'não a compreenderam')

### 3309 John 1:6-14 His Divine and human nature.
- quoted (John 1:9, John 1:10): “world. He was in the world” → “No mundo estava”
- quoted (John 1:10): “the world knew him not” → “o mundo não o conheceu”
- flag: Q0: the listed run spans 'world. He was in the world' across two sentences; only the Bíblia Livre's 'No mundo estava' (John 1:10) is quoted, Henry's previous sentence ending in his own words
- flag: missing full stop after 'like the Son of God' in the English; supplied
- flag: 'full of grace ... full of truth' (John 1:14): Henry splits the phrase to explain each part; translated, close to the Bíblia Livre's 'cheio de graça e de verdade'

### 3310 John 1:15-18 John the Baptist's testimony to Christ.
- quoted (Titus 2:10): “adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour” → “a doutrina de Deus nosso Salvador”
- flag: 'we have received "even grace"': Henry's quoted words aren't the KJV's 'grace for grace' (John 1:16; the Bíblia Livre: 'graça por graça'); translated as he wrote them, 'sim, graça', in his quotation marks
- flag: Q0: Henry's verb 'adorn' rendered 'adornar' before the Bíblia Livre's words; the Bíblia Livre's own verb ('adornem') is in another person

### 3311 John 1:19-28 John's public testimony concerning Christ.
- quoted (Luke 1:17): “in the spirit and power of Elias” → “no espírito e virtude de Elias”

### 3312 John 1:29-36 Other testimonies of John concerning Christ.
- quoted (John 1:29): “away the sin of the world” → “tira o pecado do mundo”
- quoted (John 1:29): “away the sin of the world” → “tira o pecado do mundo”
- flag: 'He bore sin for us, and so bears it from us': Henry's play on 'bear' rendered 'o levou por nós, e assim o leva para longe de nós'
- flag: 'He saw and bare record that he is the Son of God' (John 1:34) translated in Henry's third person; the Bíblia Livre has 'E eu o vi, e testemunhado tenho, que este é o Filho de Deus'

### 3313 John 1:37-42 Andrew and another disciple follow Jesus.
- quoted (2Cor 6:2): “Now is the accepted time” → “eis agora o tempo agradável”
- flag: unlisted quotation of John 1:38: the Bíblia Livre's 'Que buscais?' used verbatim
- flag: 'like a stone': Henry alludes to the name Cephas / Peter (John 1:42); translated as he wrote it

### 3314 John 1:43-51 Philip and Nathanael called.
- quoted (John 1:47): “indeed, in whom is no guile” → “em quem não há engano”
- flag: Q0: the Bíblia Livre's 'engano' for Henry's (KJV) 'guile', used also in Henry's own 'In Nathanael there was no guile' ('não havia engano')
