- 33 p0: "podría contener más que dieciocho de los barcos" -> "podría contener más de dieciocho de los barcos" (grammar: Spanish takes "más de" before a number)
- 40 p0: "no hallando paz sólida de satisfacción en" -> "no hallando paz ni satisfacción sólidas en" (garbled: the source slip "peace of satisfaction" for "peace or satisfaction" had been translated literally and did not make sense; flag updated)

(grammar/quotation: Henry's own reading "God shall persuade Japheth, and being persuaded, he shall dwell" is a future statement, and the RV1909's subjunctive "habite" was ungrammatical next to "persuadirá". Quote 1 is now marked translated and its flag updated)
