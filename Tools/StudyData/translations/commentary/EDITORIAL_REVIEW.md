# Passages proposed for editorial notes (decided 19 November 2026: see NOTES.md)

Henry's text stays whole in every case; nothing is proposed for omission.
Each note would be Versely's, shown before the paragraph in its own
callout (`EditorialNoteView`), in English, Spanish and Portuguese, like the
approved Genesis 9 note (section 47). Found by a keyword scan of the
English (races, peoples, faiths, slavery) and read in context; translators
also flag such passages ("sensitive:") as they go, and new finds are added
here. Decisions are recorded in NOTES.md.

## A. Skin colour as a picture of sin (2 passages)
Proposed note: "Editorial note: Henry uses dark skin here as a picture of
sin. Versely rejects any link between skin colour and sin or worth; every
person bears the image of God."
- 2547, Jeremiah 13:18-27: "We cannot alter the natural colour of the
  skin; and so is it morally impossible to reclaim and reform these
  people. Sin is the blackness of the soul; it is the discolouring of it…
  But Almighty grace is able to change the Ethiopian's skin."
- 2307, Song of Solomon 1:2-6: "The spouse was black as the tents of the
  wandering Arabs… The believer is black, as being defiled and sinful by
  nature, but comely, as renewed by Divine grace."

## B. The Jewish people as a whole blamed and cursed (9 passages)
Proposed note: "Editorial note: Henry speaks of the Jewish people as a
whole as guilty of Christ's death and under God's curse, a view common in
his time. Versely rejects that interpretation and any use of Scripture to
blame, punish or vilify the Jewish people."
- 3102, Matthew 27:11-25: "The Jews' curse upon themselves has been
  awfully answered in the sufferings of their nation."
- 584, Deuteronomy 28:15-44: the curses "with which God would punish the
  people of the Jews… We may observe the fulfilling of these threatenings
  in their present state."
- 3085, Matthew 24:4-28: "From the time that the Jews rejected Christ… the
  sword never departed from them."
- 2939, Zechariah 5:5-11: "the condemnation of the Jews, after they filled
  the measure of their iniquities by crucifying Christ".
- 2957, Zechariah 13:7-9: "Because of the sin of the Jews in rejecting and
  crucifying Christ… the Romans would destroy the greater part."
- 3281, Luke 20:9-19: "the Jews who murdered the prophets and crucified
  Christ, in their enmity to God".
- 3923, James 5:1-6: "Above all their other crimes, the Jews had condemned
  and crucified that Just One".
- 2803, Daniel 9:20-27: "the Jews are guilty of most obstinate unbelief, in
  expecting another Messiah" and "would commit that crime by which the
  measure of their guilt would be filled up".
- 2924, Haggai 2:1-9: "enough to silence the Jews, and condemn their
  obstinate rejection of Him".
- Not proposed (Henry follows Romans 11 closely, with the promise of
  restoration): 3590, 3591.

## C. Roman Catholics, the pope and Antichrist (6 passages)
Proposed note: "Editorial note: Henry, an English Protestant writing around
1700, identifies the Roman Catholic Church and the papacy with Antichrist
or Babylon. That is his historical interpretation; Versely is
non-denominational and does not endorse it."
- 3813, 2 Thessalonians 2:5-12: "This passage exactly agrees with the
  system of popery, as it prevails in the Romish church, and under the
  Romish popes."
- 4019, Revelation 13:1-10: "introducing popish idolatry, the same in
  substance [as pagan idolatry], only in a new dress".
- 4034, Revelation 18:9-19: the merchants "who trafficked for her
  indulgences, pardons, and honours"; "the Roman antichrist".
- 468, Numbers 24:15-25: "Rome the last enemy of the church. Not Rome pagan
  only, but Rome papal also; antichrist".
- 2807, Daniel 11:31-45: the Roman empire "in its pagan, early Christian,
  and papal states" as antichrist.
- 2809, Daniel 12:5-13: "the papal usurpation" (with Islam, below).
- Not proposed (a doctrinal disagreement stated plainly): 407 (religious
  orders, Numbers 6), 3007 (clerical celibacy, Matthew 8). Possibly 4015
  (Revelation 11: "the Scriptures were locked up from the people").

## D. Islam and the Turks (3 passages)
Proposed note: "Editorial note: Henry reads this prophecy as foretelling
Islam and the Ottoman Turks, and speaks of Islam with hostility. That is
his historical interpretation, which Versely does not endorse."
- 2799, Daniel 8:1-14: "It seems that the Mohammedan delusion is here
  pointed out."
- 2809, Daniel 12:5-13: "The imposture of Mohammed, and the papal
  usurpation… a twofold attack upon the church of God." (C and D together.)
- 4010, Revelation 9:13-21: "the power of the Turks… brought a poisonous
  and ruinous religion."

## Also possible

## A suggestion as the list grows
Group B now has well over twenty candidates, and the Gospels and Acts are
still ahead. Rather than picking each, one rule may serve better: the B
note before every paragraph where Henry treats the Jewish people as a
whole as guilty, cursed or punished (the list above, kept in
editorial.json by section and paragraph), and no note where he follows
the text about particular people (Pharisees, the crowd at the trial).
- Mark, for the B note: 3184 (Mark 15:33-41, "The Jews were doing their
  utmost to extinguish the Sun of Righteousness"), 3171 (Mark 13, the Jews
  "hastened their own ruin"), 3181 (Mark 15:1-14, the kingdom "taken from
  them"). Milder (the "Jewish church" laid aside, the fig tree as its
  figure): 3160, 3161, 3163. 3102 also says Christ's blood is upon us "by
  the Jews' rejection of it".
- Read and not proposed: 3192 (Luke 1, "though we are not to pray to
  her", a doctrinal point like 407).
- 3086 (Matthew 24:29-41, the judgment "hastening upon the nation and people of the Jews"): possible B note, milder.

  prophets"), 3286 (Luke 21:5-28, "When Christ came to destroy the Jews"),
