Study.sqlite (people, places, events and map routes used by the timeline,
maps and character explorer) is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

It is adapted from:
- Theographic Bible Metadata by Robert Rouse, CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://github.com/robertrouse/theographic-bible-metadata
- Bible Geocoding Data by OpenBible.info, CC BY 4.0 (place coordinates and
  confidence scores for each identification)
  https://github.com/openbibleinfo/Bible-Geocoding-Data
- Easton's Bible Dictionary by M. G. Easton (1897), public domain
  (biographies and place descriptions)

Changes: converted to SQLite; verse references converted to numeric ids;
dictionary markup removed; eras, era summaries and map routes added;
God and the Holy Spirit omitted from the people list; each place given
OpenBible.info's current confidence score for its best identification. The app's source
code is not covered by this license.

Topics.sqlite (the topic index used by search) is adapted from
NavesTopicalDictionary.csv in BibleData by Brady Stephenson, licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://github.com/BradyStephenson/bible-data
It is based on Nave's Topical Bible by Orville J. Nave (1896), public domain.
Changes: converted to SQLite; references converted to numeric ids and checked
against the KJV; topic names set in title case.

WordStudy.sqlite (original-language words, lexicon and commentary for word
study) is built by Tools/StudyData/build_wordstudy.py from:
- STEPBible-Data: TAHOT (Translators Amalgamated Hebrew OT), TAGNT
  (Translators Amalgamated Greek NT), TBESH and TBESG (Translators Brief
  lexicons of Extended Strongs for Hebrew and Greek).
  Data created by www.STEPBible.org based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge,
  licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Source: https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data
  TBESG's definitions are from Abbott-Smith's Manual Greek Lexicon of the New
  Testament (1922) and Middle Liddell (1889), both public domain. TBESH's
  "Meaning" definitions (from Online Bible's Abridged BDB) are not used.
- Strong's Hebrew Dictionary (James Strong, 1890, public domain), as edited
  in HebrewStrong.xml by the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible project, licensed
  under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License:
  https://github.com/openscriptures/HebrewLexicon
- Matthew Henry's Concise Commentary on the Whole Bible (1706-1721), public
  domain, from the CrossWire SWORD module MHCC as converted to JSON in
  https://github.com/daiyip/interactive-bible (data/commentary).
Changes: converted to SQLite; STEPBible's references converted to numeric
verse ids in KJV versification (Psalm titles joined to verse 1; KJV
references used where TAGNT gives them); Hebrew words reconstructed from the
LXX ("X") and Greek words not in the Textus Receptus left out; prefix and
punctuation separators, paragraph marks and editorial brackets removed from
the words; repeated word forms stored once; lexicon markup removed (line
breaks kept); Hebrew definitions, derivations and KJV renderings taken from
Strong's; commentary reference links and markup removed, and section headings
taken from Henry's chapter outlines; TAGNT's word types reduced to a note on
Greek words of the Textus Receptus that the Nestle-Aland text lacks (table
editions). No English Bible text is included.
